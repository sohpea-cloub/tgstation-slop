# #58924: Heads of staff are listed as "fleeing the station" in the revolution round-end report even after winning

## Summary

In the revolution section of the round-end report, every head of staff who is alive and not on a station z-level is flagged "while fleeing the station" in red text. This includes heads who won the round by killing or exiling the head revolutionaries and then left on the escape shuttle. The heads list called `printplayer(head, 1)` with the flee check hard-coded on. The fix passes the check only when the station did not win (`GLOB.revolution_handler.result != STATION_VICTORY`), the same pattern the head-rev and rev lists already use.

## The bug

A player who wins a revolution round as a head of staff and then boards the escape shuttle sees their own entry in the round-end report read "... survived while fleeing the station", with "fleeing the station" in red, as if they had done something wrong. The issue reporter reproduced this (and another commenter confirmed it on a live round).

The check itself is simple: a surviving mind whose body is not on a station z-level gets the label. The emergency shuttle at CentCom is not a station z-level, so any head who leaves on the shuttle is flagged, regardless of how the revolution ended.

Early comments on the issue argued the behaviour was intended, because a head leaving the station while revolutionaries are still alive has arguably abandoned it. That reasoning only applies when the revolution was not quelled. After a station victory, there is nothing left to flee from.

## Reproduction

The report was exercised by calling the report proc directly with a hand-built revolution state, not by playing out a full revolution round to the shuttle. The check that matters (`printplayer`) is the same one a real round would run.

1. On a test server running master, start a round and spawn a full dummy crew with the Spawn Debug Full Crew admin verb. This fills the manifest, so `SSjob.get_all_heads()` returns the heads.
2. In the Lua editor, create a `/datum/revolution_handler` and assign it to `GLOB.revolution_handler`. Put one of the heads (the Captain) into its team's `ex_headrevs` list, so the team report does not exit early.
3. Move the Captain's body to z-level 1, which is space and not a station z-level (standing in for the escape shuttle at CentCom).
4. Set the handler's `result` to `STATION_VICTORY` (2) and call `roundend_report()` on the revolution team.

Observed on master: the heads-of-staff entry for the Captain read "... and survived while fleeing the station".

Setting `result` to `REVOLUTION_VICTORY` (1) or leaving it unset gave the same text. That is expected, since the check is unconditional.

## Cause

`/datum/team/revolution/roundend_report()` (`code/modules/antagonists/revolution/revolution.dm:500`) prints three lists. The head revolutionaries and the revolutionaries are printed with a flee check that depends on the outcome:

```dm
printplayerlist(headrevs, GLOB.revolution_handler.result != REVOLUTION_VICTORY)
printplayerlist(revs, GLOB.revolution_handler.result != REVOLUTION_VICTORY)
```

The heads of staff list, however, ignored the outcome (`revolution.dm:553`):

```dm
head_text += "[printplayer(head, 1)]</li>"
```

`printplayer()` (`code/__HELPERS/roundend.dm:701`) appends "while fleeing the station" when `fleecheck` is set and the mind's body is missing a turf or is not on a station z-level. With `fleecheck` always 1, surviving heads who were on the shuttle, at CentCom or elsewhere off-station were always flagged. The result is decided in `code/modules/antagonists/revolution/revolution_handler.dm`, which sets `STATION_VICTORY` at line 114 and `REVOLUTION_VICTORY` at line 81.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 58924: heads of staff are no longer listed as fleeing the station after the revolution is quelled". It changes only the heads list in `/datum/team/revolution/roundend_report()`:

```dm
// Heads who won by quelling the revolution did not flee by leaving on the shuttle
head_text += "[printplayer(head, GLOB.revolution_handler.result != STATION_VICTORY)]</li>"
```

The flee check is skipped after a station victory and kept otherwise. This is a local change that does not touch `printplayer()`, `considered_escaped` or `considered_exiled`, which are used elsewhere. The alternative of dropping the check from the heads list entirely was not taken, because a head who leaves while the revolution is still alive, or unresolved, can reasonably still be reported as fleeing.

## Verification

On the test server, the same hand-built state was run before and after the change, with the Captain on z-level 1:

- `result` = `STATION_VICTORY` (2): before the fix the entry read "... survived while fleeing the station"; after it, "... survived as ..." with no flee text.
- `result` = `REVOLUTION_VICTORY` (1) and `result` unset (0/null): after the fix the entry still read "while fleeing the station", as intended.

Not checked: a complete round played through to the shuttle leaving with real revolutionaries, and the rendered round-end report window (only the proc's returned text was inspected). Other maps and other head jobs were not tried.

## Notes

- A head can still be shown as fleeing when the round ends without the revolution being resolved (`result` unset). That is arguably intended, and the fix leaves it.
- Unrelated to this fix, but found while reading the code: `considered_exiled()` (`code/__HELPERS/game.dm:61`) has a misplaced parenthesis: `istype(implant_check, /obj/item/implant/exile && player_mind.current.onAwayMission())`. The `&&` is inside the `istype()` call, so the type argument is the result of the `&&` expression and the exile-implant check cannot match as written. It was not changed here. A comment on the issue mentions heads being marked as exiled after leaving the z-level, which is a separate mechanism from this report text.
