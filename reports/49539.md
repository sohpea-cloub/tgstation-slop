## Summary

Three atmospherics issues were tested together on one test server. #49539: the huge air scrubber's `wrench_act` runs the normal portable-machine connect/disconnect and then also toggles the anchoring, so wrenching it onto a connector port connects it and then unanchors it (connected but loose). The fix makes it behave like other portables when it is on a port or connected to one. #59770: `hotspot_expose()` treated any air with plasma, tritium or hydrogen as flammable even in hyper-noblium air that normal gas reactions refuse to burn in; the fix applies the same hyper-noblium check there. #71957 (flaps leaving an atmos block behind) could not be reproduced.

## The bug

### #49539

The report says a huge air scrubber cannot be connected to a connector port by wrenching it. A 2023 comment gives the more precise symptom: it does connect, but it is not anchored, so it can be pulled off the tile, breaking the connection.

On master the huge scrubber also gets the standard portable behavior (an interface with a "connected to port" state), which is why players expect it to connect.

### #59770

An igniter (the assembly or the incinerator igniter machinery) creates a fire hotspot in air that contains oxygen and plasma, tritium or hydrogen, even when 5 or more moles of hyper-noblium are present. Hyper-noblium is supposed to suppress fire reactions. The report notes the hotspot died down after a few ticks and no tritium was burnt. The report also lists numbers it thinks might be worth balancing (hotspot volume is multiplied by 25 on creation, so the exposed volume is very large and the whole turf is set to the igniter's temperature) and says it may be intentional.

### #71957

The report says that plastic flaps made by construction or placed on the map leave an airtight pocket after removal, so gas can no longer flow into or out of the tile, but that unscrewed or moved flaps do not do this.

## Reproduction

### #49539 (reproduced, with a player-style wrench click)

1. On the test server, start a round and join as a human holding a wrench. Using the Lua editor, spawn a `/obj/machinery/portable_atmospherics/scrubber/huge` and a `/obj/machinery/atmospherics/components/unary/portables_connector` on the same tile (the scrubber is anchored by default).
2. Click the scrubber with the wrench.

Observed on master: the first click printed "You unfasten the huge air scrubber from the port." followed by "You begin securing the huge air scrubber...", i.e. the disconnect ran and then it was anchored again. The next click printed "You fasten the huge air scrubber to the port." immediately followed by "You begin unsecuring the huge air scrubber...", which completes with "You unsecure the huge air scrubber.". The scrubber ended up connected to the port and unanchored.

Test artifact: a connector spawned from Lua after round start has no pipenet, and the very first `connect()` caused a runtime at `code/modules/atmospherics/machinery/portable/portable_atmospherics.dm:181` (`reconcile_air` on a null pipenet; the game logs "Component is missing a pipenet! Rebuilding"). Wrenching again after that worked. This is a consequence of spawning the connector that way, not part of the bug.

### #59770 (not run on an unfixed build)

The pre-fix behavior was not run separately; it follows from reading the code (see Cause) and from the issue's own screenshot. The test described under Verification was run on the fixed build only. Its control case (no hyper-noblium) shows that a hotspot is created from the same setup.

### #71957 (not reproduced)

1. Using the Lua editor, spawn `/obj/structure/plasticflaps` on a floor tile, put 50 oxygen and 50 plasma at 600 K on it (`atmos_spawn_air`), and call `hotspot_expose(1000, 100)` on the turf. This starts a fire under the flaps. This was done with direct proc calls, not by lighting plasma by hand or building the flaps from a plastic stack.
2. Wait for the flaps to melt, then inspect the turf.

Observed: the flaps melted away (chat: "melts away into plastic goo"); there were no plastic flaps left in the turf's contents; the open neighbouring turf's `atmos_adjacent_turfs` contained the turf and vice versa (mutual adjacency restored); the turf had an excited group; the fire spread out into the hallway.

Earlier test: spawning flaps and removing them with `qdel` restored `atmos_adjacent_turfs` from nil (fully blocked while the flaps stood) to 3 turfs. One in-between run seemed to show a plasticflaps still in the turf after deletion with the gas stuck; this was most likely a leftover from earlier failed test runs on the same tile (several runs there had errored after spawning flaps), and the clean retest on a fresh tile above did not repeat it.

## Cause

### #49539

`/obj/machinery/portable_atmospherics/scrubber/huge/wrench_act()` (`code/modules/atmospherics/machinery/portable/scrubber.dm:242`):

```dm
/obj/machinery/portable_atmospherics/scrubber/huge/wrench_act(mob/living/user, obj/item/tool)
	. = ..()
	if(default_unfasten_wrench(user, tool))
		if(!movable)
			on = FALSE
		return ITEM_INTERACT_SUCCESS
	return FALSE
```

`. = ..()` runs the parent `portable_atmospherics/wrench_act()` (`code/modules/atmospherics/machinery/portable/portable_atmospherics.dm:283`), which disconnects if connected, connects if a `portables_connector` is on the tile, or says "Nothing happens". Its result is discarded and `default_unfasten_wrench()` (`code/game/objects/objs.dm:288`) always runs on top. `connect()` (line 168) sets the machine anchored (line 183) and `disconnect()` (line 199) un-anchors it (line 207), so the unfasten toggle immediately undoes that: wrenching on gives "connected, not anchored", wrenching off gives "disconnected, anchored".

### #59770

`/turf/open/hotspot_expose()` (`code/modules/atmospherics/environmental/LINDA_fire.dm:23`) computes `plas_trit_h2_threshold` (line 35) from plasma, tritium and hydrogen above 0.5 mol, with no regard to hyper-noblium. The gas reaction code does check it: `gas_mixture.dm:523` returns `STOP_REACTIONS` when hyper-noblium is at least `REACTION_OPPRESSION_THRESHOLD` (5 mol) and the temperature exceeds `REACTION_OPPRESSION_MIN_TEMP` (20 K). So the igniter creates a hotspot and calls `SSair.add_to_active()` even though the reactions it would drive are suppressed. Igniters call `hotspot_expose` at `code/modules/assembly/igniter.dm:36` (the assembly's `activate()`) and `code/game/machinery/igniter.dm:124` and `:263`.

### #71957

No defect was found. The relevant code looks consistent: `plasticflaps` calls `air_update_turf(TRUE, TRUE)` in `Initialize()` (`code/game/objects/structures/plasticflaps.dm:53`), its `Destroy()` calls `oldloc.air_update_turf(TRUE, FALSE)` (lines 238-242), and the base `/atom/movable/Destroy()` also resets `can_atmos_pass` and calls `air_update_turf(TRUE, FALSE)` for movables that block air (`code/game/atoms_movable.dm:223-226`). The screwdriver path updates atmos too (`plasticflaps.dm:160-161`). No stale state was found after deletion.

## Fix

Two commits on the branch.

**"Fix issue 49539: huge air scrubbers can be wrenched onto a connector port without also being unanchored."** In `huge/wrench_act()`, if the scrubber is connected to a port or a `portables_connector` is on its tile, it now returns the parent `wrench_act()` result and does nothing else. Otherwise the old path (`default_unfasten_wrench()` and turning the scrubber off if it is not movable) runs unchanged. The issue's comments suggested three options: call the parent so it also connects, remove the override so it can only be wrenched onto a port, or change the anchoring tool. This takes the first, with the smallest change, keeping the anchoring behavior for a scrubber that is not on a port.

**"Fix issue 59770: igniters no longer start a hotspot in hyper-noblium air."** In `hotspot_expose()`, after computing `plas_trit_h2_threshold`, it is set to FALSE when hyper-noblium is at least `REACTION_OPPRESSION_THRESHOLD` and `exposed_temperature` is above `REACTION_OPPRESSION_MIN_TEMP`, mirroring `react()`. This covers every `hotspot_expose` caller, not only igniters. The issue's remarks about the volume multiplier and hotspot numbers were deliberately left alone; the report itself describes them as possibly intentional.

No change for #71957.

## Verification

### #49539

After a rebuild, with the same setup and a wrench:

- First click: "You unfasten the huge air scrubber from the port." Connected port nil, anchored 0. No "securing" message.
- Second click: "You fasten the huge air scrubber to the port." Connected port set, anchored 1. No "unsecure" message.
- Third click: "You unfasten the huge air scrubber from the port." again.

Not checked: wrenching a huge scrubber that is not on a connector port (the unchanged `default_unfasten_wrench` path), the `movable` and cargo scrubber variants, whether a connected scrubber scrubs the room and exchanges gas with the pipe network as expected, and `connect()` on a connector that has a proper pipenet from map load (the test connector needed an extra attempt, see above).

### #59770

Using the Lua editor on two turfs, each with 50 oxygen and 50 plasma at 300 K, an igniter assembly was spawned on the turf and `activate()` was called:

- Without hyper-noblium: `active_hotspot` was "the hotspot" (a hotspot was created).
- With 10 mol of hyper-noblium: `active_hotspot` was nil.

The first case shows the fix did not break ordinary ignition. The behavior with noblium on an unfixed build was not run.

Not checked: the incinerator igniter machinery (`code/game/machinery/igniter.dm`), tritium or hydrogen as the fuel, hyper-noblium between 0.5 and 5 mol, and an already burning hotspot in air that later gets hyper-noblium. The runtime log had no runtimes from the changed code.

### #71957

No fix, so nothing to verify. Not tested: flaps built from a plastic stack, the unscrew-then-rescrew and wirecutter paths, and plasma ignited in a way other than `hotspot_expose`.

## Notes

- **Consequence for the scrubber fix.** With the fix, disconnecting a huge scrubber from a port leaves it unanchored (`disconnect()` un-anchors it). While it stands on a connector tile, wrenching it again always connects. The unanchor-by-wrench path is therefore not reachable on that tile. A scrubber that is not `movable` also turns itself off when it is not anchored (`process_atmos()`, `scrubber.dm:225`), which the old code avoided only by accident (it re-anchored on disconnect). A maintainer may want to decide whether a disconnected, unanchored, non-movable huge scrubber is acceptable. This was not tested in game.
- **Hyper-noblium temperature check.** `react()` compares the gas mixture's own temperature with `REACTION_OPPRESSION_MIN_TEMP`, whereas the fix compares the heat source's `exposed_temperature` (igniters use 1000), which is above 20 K, so in practice the check reduces to "5 or more moles of hyper-noblium". Using the mixture's temperature would match `react()` more strictly; this was not tried.
- **Existing hotspots.** With the fix, an already existing hotspot in hyper-noblium air no longer has its temperature or volume raised by `hotspot_expose` (that branch is gated on the same variable), and falls through to the freon branch. This was not tested.
- **#59770, remaining part.** The issue's second part (the volume multiplier and the different igniter volumes, 500 for the machinery and 1000 for the assembly) is a balance question and was left as is.
- **#71957.** The report's screenshot might show flaps that were still present; this is a guess and was not confirmed. The report may be closable if nobody can reproduce it by real construction.
