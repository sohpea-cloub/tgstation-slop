## Summary

Both issues are traitor panel bugs and were handled together; they have different causes.

- **#43265:** Adding Lone Operative (or Wizard) through the traitor panel to a mind whose current mob is a ghost called living-only procs (`add_personality`, `add_mood_event`, `clear_mood_event`) on the ghost. This produced "undefined proc" runtimes and aborted the antagonist's `on_gain()` partway. The fix guards those calls with `isliving()`.
- **#65555:** The panel took the first antagonist of a category as its preference source. For Revolution that is `/datum/antagonist/rev`, whose `ROLE_REV` flag is not a selectable preference (only Head Revolutionary is), so the panel always showed "Disabled in Prefs". The fix picks the first antagonist of the category whose preference flag is selectable.

## The bug

### #43265

The 2019 report: a ghost made itself a Lone Operative from the traitor panel. The game prompted ghosts for a lone operative spot, several runtimes appeared, and the nuke operative options then disappeared from the panel. The "prompt ghosts" part is old behaviour: a lone operative is now just an antagonist datum, with no ghost poll. The part that still occurs is the runtimes when the target mind belongs to a ghost.

A normal ghost has no mind ("This mob has no mind!" from the panel), so the situation needs a mind whose `current` is an observer. Admin tools can create it. In normal play it could also come up if a body is destroyed while its mind is kept (suggested by the testers, not confirmed).

### #65555

The traitor panel's category headers say "Enabled in Prefs" or "Disabled in Prefs", based on the target's `be_special` list. For Revolution the header read "Disabled in Prefs" even when the player had Head Revolutionary enabled. A comment on the issue notes that the same happens for the Syndicate category (the first shown entry is Battlecruiser Crewmember) and Deviant Crew (Brainwashed Victim).

## Reproduction

### #43265 (on master)

1. As an observer on a running round, use the Lua editor to give the observer a mind: `SS13.new('/datum/mind', me.key):transfer_to(me)`. The mind's `current` is now the ghost.
2. Run Show Traitor Panel on that ghost and add Lone Operative (topic `add_antag=/datum/antagonist/nukeop/lone`).
3. Read the runtime log.

Observed on master:

- `runtime error: undefined proc or verb /mob/dead/observer/add personality()`, at the moment the antagonist was added.
- `runtime error: undefined proc or verb /mob/dead/observer/clear mood event()`, a few seconds later. The action that triggered it was not recorded; the likely source is `clear_antag_moodies`, which calls `clear_mood_event` (see Cause).
- The panel afterwards showed "Assigned role: Unassigned Crewmember" instead of Operative: `on_gain()` had stopped before it set the role, so the rest of it (equipment, team, uplink and discount handling) did not run.
- The stack traces in the log were empty. A body with a ghost-kept mind, as opposed to a mind whose current mob is the ghost, worked without runtimes.

The log also had unrelated runtimes about `null.air`, `null.moles` and `null.temperature` (slime stasis and atmos pipes). They are not part of this bug.

### #65555 (on master)

1. Add Head Revolutionary to the target client's `prefs.be_special` (Lua editor, `list.add`).
2. Open the traitor panel on that player's mind and read the Revolution header.

On master it read "Disabled in Prefs". Caveat: it is unclear whether this baseline was actually observed before the fix: one account records "before: Disabled in Prefs", another says no baseline was taken. The cause analysis below is code-based and matches the reporters' description.

## Cause

### #43265

`code/modules/antagonists/nukeop/datums/operative.dm:48` calls `owner.current.add_personality(/datum/personality/callous)` in `on_gain()`. `add_personality` exists only on living mobs, so for a ghost the call runtimes and the rest of `on_gain()` (from `. = ..()` on) is skipped:

```dm
/datum/antagonist/nukeop/on_gain()
	give_alias()
	forge_objectives()
	owner.current.add_personality(/datum/personality/callous)
	. = ..()
	owner.set_assigned_role(SSjob.get_job_type(job_type))
	equip_op()
```

`code/modules/antagonists/wizard/wizard.dm:85-86` does the same with two `add_personality` calls. `give_antag_moodies` and `clear_antag_moodies` (`code/modules/antagonists/_common/antag_datum.dm:374` and `:383`) call `add_mood_event` and `clear_mood_event` on `mob_override || owner.current`, typed as `/mob/living`, without checking that it is living. The `clear_mood_event` runtime came from this path (most likely when the antagonist was removed or its owner changed body, which call `clear_antag_moodies` at `antag_datum.dm:201` and `:329`).

### #65555

`code/modules/admin/antag_panel.dm:170-179` picks `pref_source` as the current antagonist, or, if the target has none in that category, the first prototype with `show_in_antagpanel`, and then calls `enabled_in_preferences()` on it (`antag_datum.dm:465`), which checks `pref_flag in client.prefs.be_special`. In the Revolution category the first prototype is `/datum/antagonist/rev` with pref flag `ROLE_REV` ("Revolutionary"), which players cannot select. Only `ROLE_REV_HEAD` is. The same shape of problem affects Syndicate and Deviant Crew.

## Fix

Two commits on `fix/43265`.

**Fix issue 43265: making a ghost an operative or wizard through the traitor panel runtimes.**

- `operative.dm`: `add_personality` is called only if `isliving(owner.current)`.
- `wizard.dm`: both `add_personality` calls are inside an `isliving(owner.current)` check.
- `antag_datum.dm`: `give_antag_moodies` and `clear_antag_moodies` return early if the target is not living.

The guards were chosen over refusing to add the antagonist to a ghost, since admins do this on purpose (the report's author wanted to prepare an antagonist and offer it to ghosts). Only the personality and moodlet parts are skipped.

**Fix issue 65555: traitor panel shows the wrong preference status for antag categories.** The panel now uses the current antagonist as the preference source only if its `pref_flag` is in `get_all_antag_flags()` (the flags players can actually choose, `code/modules/client/preferences/middleware/antags.dm:105`). Otherwise it uses the first shown prototype of the category whose flag is in that list, and falls back to the first shown prototype if none qualifies, which is the old behaviour. The comment above it explains that not every antagonist of a category has its own preference.

## Verification

On the test server after rebuilding.

#43265:

- Adding Lone Operative to a ghost-held mind printed "You are a Clandestine Chemicals agent!", the panel showed "Assigned role: Operative" and listed Lone Operative with its objective.
- Removing it printed "You are no longer Lone Operative!".
- The runtime log had no new runtimes apart from the unrelated atmos and slime ones.

#65555:

- With Head Revolutionary in `be_special`, the Revolution header read "Enabled in Prefs".
- Syndicate and Deviant Crew still read "Disabled in Prefs" after adding Battlecruiser Crew and Brainwashed Victim to `be_special`. Those flags are not in `get_all_antag_flags()` (no dynamic ruleset uses them), so they are not selectable preferences and the fallback applies.

Not checked:

- The wizard path was not run in game. It has the same `isliving()` guard as the operative.
- Both preference states for Revolution were not exercised after the fix, only the enabled case.
- Whether the ghost-held-mind state arises in ordinary play was not tested; the test created it with the Lua editor.

## Notes

- For Syndicate and Deviant Crew, #65555's comment suggests adding a flag to say which antagonist of a category represents the preference. This fix does not do that. It relies on `get_all_antag_flags()`, so those two categories still read "Disabled in Prefs" until a selectable preference exists for them, or the panel gets a way to name the representative antagonist.
- The 2019 report's lone-operative ghost poll no longer exists. #43265 was handled as the current runtime on the same action, and the fix addresses only that.
- The operative change is in `operative.dm` and may conflict textually with another change to `on_gain()` in the same file.
