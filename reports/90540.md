# #90540: Switched-off and light-eaten overlay lights keep counting in get_lumcount; directional lights are counted at the holder

## Summary

Movable lights (flashlights, lanterns, PDAs, and so on) are tracked for `get_lumcount` and related checks through the spatial grid. On master, turning such a light off, or having it eaten by a light eater, did not remove it from the grid, so turfs around it still counted as lit and a nightmare could not heal or shadow walk there. Directional lights (seclites) were also counted around their holder instead of where their cone lands, and a light with power 0 still counted as +0.5. The fix cleans the grid registration when a light turns off or changes range, counts each light by its own reach, and centres directional lights on the turf they are cast to.

## The bug

#90540 describes nightmare and light-eater play where flashlights, lanterns and PDAs that were light-eaten still seemed to affect "dark" checks, so neither the nightmare nor the players fighting it could tell which dark tiles were really dark, and a light that was still visible did not always count as light for jaunts. #94450 describes standing in pitch black next to a seclite pointed the other way and taking nightmare damage, unable to shadow walk, and notes that anything using `get_lumcount` is affected. Its comments identify two suspects: dynamic light always contributing a flat 0 or 0.5, and `update_dynamic_luminosity` ignoring directional lights.

## Reproduction

With the round started and an admin observing, using the Lua editor and reading `get_dynamic_lumcount()` over a row of 17 turfs centred on the holder:

1. Spawn a `/obj/item/flashlight` on the floor and turn it on. Nine turfs in the row read 0.5.
2. Call `set_light_on(0)` on it. On master the same nine turfs still read 0.5. They should read 0.
3. Add the `light_eaten` element to a lit flashlight (which sets its power and range to 0 and turns it off). On master, three turfs still read 0.5.
4. Give a human a seclite (a directional light, range 5), turn it on, and compare the row when the human faces east and west. On master the two rows were identical and centred on the holder, although the visible cone is cast ahead of the holder.

This checks the lumcount procs that the nightmare's abilities call; it did not run the abilities themselves.

## Cause

All in `code/datums/components/overlay_lighting.dm` and `code/modules/lighting/lighting_turf.dm` at master.

- Registration lives in `dynamic_light_sources` of spatial grid cells. `update_luminosity_cells()` (`:105-110`) starts with `if(get_turf(light.current_holder) == luminosity_turf) return`. `turn_off()` (`:208-218`) calls only that, and `set_range()` (`:151-155`) does the same. Because the holder had not moved, the early return skipped `clean_old_cells()`, so the light was never removed from the grid. `light_eaten` goes through `set_light_on(FALSE)` and hits the same path.
- `clean_old_cells()` (`:92-96`) removed cells using the current `lumcount_range`, not the range they were registered with, so a changed range also left stale cells behind.
- `turf/collect_dynamic_lightsources()` (`lighting_turf.dm:90-112`) takes the largest `lumcount_range` of any light in the cell and gathers every light in `hearers()` of that distance, with no per-light range check. It also treats every light as centred on its holder, ignoring the directional cast offset (`cast_range` in `code/modules/lighting/light_overlay.dm`).
- `set_power()` (`:158-163`) gave `lum_power` of 0.5 for any power `>= 0`, so a power-0 light counted.

The nightmare checks are `check_lumcount_below(SHADOW_SPECIES_LIGHT_THRESHOLD)` (`code/modules/antagonists/nightmare/nightmare_organs.dm:44` and `:144`, threshold 0.2), so a single stale 0.5 entry is enough to block healing and shadow walking.

## Fix

One commit, `Fix issue 90540: overlay lights keep counting for get_lumcount after being turned off, eaten or aimed`:

- `overlay_lighting.dm`: a new `registered_cell_range` records the range used when registering, and `clean_old_cells()` removes exactly that and resets `luminosity_turf`. `turn_off()` now calls `clean_old_cells()` directly. `set_range()` cleans then re-registers. New `get_cell_range()` (the range plus up to `MAX_DIRECTIONAL_CAST_RANGE` for directional lights) and `get_light_center()` (the holder's turf, or the turf the cone is cast to). `set_power()` gives `lum_power` 0 for power 0.
- `light_overlay.dm`: stores `cast_distance`, the actual forward distance after the cone stops at opaque turfs.
- `lighting_turf.dm`: `collect_dynamic_lightsources()` uses `get_cell_range()` for the search range and then, per light, requires `get_dist(src, light.get_light_center()) <= light.lumcount_range`.
- `__DEFINES/lighting.dm`: `MAX_DIRECTIONAL_CAST_RANGE` set to 3, matching the clamp on cast range for non-beam directional lights.

Alternatives are not discussed in the inputs.

## Verification

On a rebuilt server, with the same row measurements:

- Flashlight on: the same nine turfs at 0.5. Off: 0 everywhere. With the range changed from 2 to 5 the lit span went from 5 to 11 tiles. Moving it 3 tiles with `forceMove` made the lit span follow.
- Light-eaten flashlight: 0 everywhere.
- Seclite in open space: facing east the lit box shifted 2 tiles east of the holder, west 2 tiles west, south 2 tiles south, matching the cast distance of 2 of its cone.
- A holder moved 10 tiles with `forceMove` left no stale lit turfs at the old position; dropping the seclite kept it lit; deleting it or turning it off gave 0 lit turfs.
- No runtimes appeared in the server log from the changes.

Not checked:

- A real nightmare: light eating by claw, healing in the dark, shadow walk, or the nightmare's death check. These call `check_lumcount_below`, which shares `collect_dynamic_lightsources()`, and were assumed covered by the measurements above.
- How the lights look on screen. The "light still visible but not counted" symptom was not examined.
- The directional behaviour in a corridor with opaque turfs, beam lights, and diagonal facings.
- Other callers of `get_lumcount`.
- Performance, since `collect_dynamic_lightsources()` now does a `get_dist` check per light.

## Notes

- The verified defect is that turned-off or eaten lights still count as light, i.e. the opposite direction to #90540's "visible light that does not count". The issue also reports that dark-looking areas may not actually be dark, which matches the stale count; the opposite symptom was not reproduced and may need separate investigation.
- In #94450 the reporter's reason 1 is that `get_lumcount` clamps static lumcount together with flat 0/0.5 dynamic contributions. This fix leaves that design unchanged (`lum_power` is still +0.5 for any positive-power light) and only removes the wrong entries and the wrong distances. Reason 2 (that `update_dynamic_luminosity` ignores directional lights) was addressed here only through the lumcount search, not by changing the dynamic luminosity of mobs.
- The directional bound of 3 duplicates the clamp in `light_overlay.dm`; the two should stay in sync.
