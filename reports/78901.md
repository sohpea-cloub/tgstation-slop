## Summary

Several issues share one underlying problem: a buckled seat and its rider can be moved separately (a transit tube pod takes the seat but not the rider, a teleport or z-level change takes only one of them), and the next move then drags the one left behind over to the other, which looks like a teleport snap. In transit tubes, `Bumped()` on both the tube station and the pod dispenser called `forceMove(pod)` on the seat only. The branch makes stations take a seat's riders into the pod with it (#78901, #94416), makes a separated seat and rider unbuckle instead of snapping back together (#80633), and fixes three neighbouring defects: the dispenser making an empty pod on every walk into a mob standing on it (#67784), a missing rider-of-a-rider in z-level moves (#90478), and a wrong plane on unbuckle across z-levels (#85463).

## The bug

#78901 describes a paraplegic player in a wheelchair driving into a transit tube. The chair goes into the pod and is spat out at the far end, while the player stays standing at the entrance, locked in place until they move and their position updates. If another player pushes them into the tube, the reporter saw many copies of the pod created, each teleporting the chair back, which lagged the server. #94416 is the same on an emergency bed: the bed arrives, the body stays at the entrance and reappears when the bed is dragged. #80633 lists more cases of the same snap (teleporters, the Icebox climbing hook while buckled to a chair). #67784 reports that walking into a tube dispenser while another mob stands on it makes pods endlessly. #90478 reports odd behaviour with a rideable mob buckled to a wheelchair over open space, and #85463 reports a rider that becomes invisible and unclickable after climbing z-levels while buckled.

## Reproduction

All checks used the Lua editor as an observer on a multi-z test map, after the round started. A small tube rig was built on z2: a dispenser at the entry, three straight tubes, and a dispenser at the far end. After spawning each piece with `SS13.new`, its `dir` was set and `init_tube_dirs()` and `update_appearance()` were called. Boarding was from the tile north of the entry dispenser, moving south. Movement was driven by calling procs (`relaymove`, `Move`) on spawned mobs, not by a real client pushing or driving.

**#78901 / #94416 (seat enters the tube without its rider)**
1. Buckle a spawned human to a spawned wheelchair at the entry and call `relaymove(human, SOUTH)` on the chair, into the dispenser.
2. Result on master: the chair was inside the pod and the human stood on the floor at the entry, still buckled to the chair. When the pod arrived the chair was at the far end (128,128) and the human was still at the entry.
3. Calling `Move(west)` on the human then put the chair on the floor next to the human again (the chair was moved from the far end back to the human), i.e. the snap-back.
4. Repeating the same with an emergency bed and a body gave the same result: the bed went to the far end, the body stayed at the entry, still buckled.
5. The reported lag loop was not reproduced. Repeated `Move` calls from the human into the station while the chair was in the pod did not move it and made no extra pods. Only the cause (the seat taken without its rider) was reproduced.

**#67784 (empty pods)**
1. Place a mob on the entry dispenser tile and walk another mob into the dispenser three times.
2. Result on master: three pods were created (pod count on the station 1, 2, 3) and the walker stayed outside each time.

**#80633 (climbing hook)** The hook calls `forceMove` on the user. For a living mob that unbuckles the mob first, so the climbing hook case did not reproduce on master: the climber was unbuckled after the climb and its plane was correct (-4). The shared cause was confirmed for seats moved without their rider (the tube above): the chair snapped from the far tube end back to the rider on the rider's next step.

**#85463** Not reproduced through the climbing hook, for the same reason (living `forceMove` unbuckles first, plane -4). The latent defect (see Cause) was found by reading the code and was exercised on the fixed build only.

**#90478** A tamed raptor buckled to a wheelchair with a rider was driven off an open-space edge. There is no valid before-fix run: the stock-build attempt happened to be on a tile with a lattice (126,121 on z4), where nothing falls. The cause below is from reading the code.

## Cause

- `code/game/objects/structures/transit_tubes/station.dm:36-41` (`station/Bumped`) and `:252-279` (`dispenser/Bumped`) call `AM.forceMove(pod)` on whatever bumped the station. For a wheelchair or bed that is the seat only. `atom/movable` `forceMove` does not unbuckle, so the rider stays on the platform, still buckled.
- `code/game/atoms_movable.dm:1476` (`handle_buckled_mob_movement`) and `code/modules/mob/living/living.dm:1046-1052` (`/mob/living/Move`): when a seat and its rider are on different turfs, the next move of either one pulls the other over with `Move`, which is the visible snap.
- `code/modules/mob/living/living.dm:236-239` (mob swap): with a mob standing on the dispenser, the swap's own `Move` into the dispenser bumps it, and the dispenser puts the walker in a new pod. The swap then fails and its undo runs `forceMove(oldloc)`, which takes the walker back out of the pod, so the pod leaves empty. Each step repeats this, producing a new pod every time. (This is consistent with the guess in the #67784 comments.)
- `code/game/atoms_movable.dm:415-419` (`get_z_move_affected`) includes a movable's buckled mobs but not mobs buckled to those, so a raptor in a wheelchair fell and its rider stayed over open space.
- `code/datums/components/riding/riding.dm:249-254` (`restore_rider_layer_and_offsets`) set the rider's plane with `SET_PLANE_EXPLICIT(buckled_mob, initial(buckled_mob.plane), source)` where `source` is the ridden thing, so a rider unbuckled while on a different z-level than the ridden thing got the other level's plane.

## Fix

Five commits:

1. **Fix issue 78901: transit tube stations take a seat's riders into the pod with it** adds `station/proc/board_pod()`. It unbuckles all mobs from the boarder (forced), moves the boarder into the pod, then moves each former rider that is still on the boarding turf into the pod too. Both `Bumped()` procs use it. This also fixes #94416. Riders come out of the pod unbuckled; they are not re-buckled at the far end.
2. **Fix issue 80633: a seat and its rider that got separated unbuckle instead of snapping back together** adds `is_separated_from_buckled()` (a rider is together with the seat while on the same turf as the seat's old or new location). `handle_buckled_mob_movement()` now takes the old location and unbuckles a rider that is separated instead of dragging it over, and `/mob/living/Move` does the same for a mob whose seat is elsewhere (unless the mob has `TRAIT_CANNOT_BE_UNBUCKLED`). It also iterates over a copy of `buckled_mobs`.
3. **Fix issue 90478: the rider of a mob buckled to something goes through z-levels with it** makes `get_z_move_affected()` recurse into riders of buckled mobs.
4. **Fix issue 85463: an unbuckled rider gets the plane of its own z-level** replaces the explicit-source plane with `SET_PLANE_IMPLICIT`, which uses the rider's own z.
5. **Fix issue 67784: walking into a tube dispenser through a mob on it boards the pod** makes a failed mob swap only undo the parts that are still at the swap position (`if(M.loc == oldloc)`, `if(loc == oldMloc)`), and a swap that left the walker somewhere else (inside a pod) no longer continues to push the other mob.

Riders are unbuckled rather than re-buckled at the far end; the inputs do not record why that was chosen over keeping them buckled.

## Verification

Checked on the test server on the fixed build with the same rig:

- **#78901:** after driving the chair into the dispenser, the chair and the human were both in the pod (human unbuckled), then both at the far end (128,128).
- **#94416:** bed and body both entered the pod and arrived together at 128,128; the body was unbuckled.
- **#67784:** the walker boarded on the first walk and arrived at the far end.
- **#80633:** a chair `forceMove`d down a level alone, with the rider then stepping: the move succeeded, the rider was unbuckled with the right plane (-4), and the chair stayed where it was (no snap-back). Same for a chair teleported on the same z.
- **Normal driving:** a wheelchair and its rider still moved together and stayed buckled.
- **#90478:** a wheelchair, raptor and rider stack driven off open space at y=119 fell together to z3, all at the same tile and still buckled, the rider with the z3 plane (-111).
- **#85463:** a rider unbuckled across z-levels got the correct plane (-4).
- The runtime log of the fixed round had three runtimes, all from the test scripts reading `obj_flags` on non-objs; none came from the game.

Not checked: the original lag loop (never reproduced), a real client pushing or driving instead of proc calls, a before-fix run for #90478, other rideables (scooters, janicarts, skateboards, office chairs) mentioned in a #80633 comment, a full round, and the unit tests.

## Notes

- The pod boarding path unbuckles riders on purpose, so a rider no longer stays buckled to a chair while in the pod. Maintainers may prefer re-buckling at the far end.
- The `Move` and `handle_buckled_mob_movement` change in #80633 is the broadest part of the branch. It affects every seat/rider pair, so it deserves a close look and a full test run.
- On master the climbing hook case of #80633 and #85463 already behaves correctly because `/mob/living/forceMove` (`living.dm:1967`) unbuckles the mob first, unless it is z-moving. The reports probably came from builds before that, or from rider-of-rider setups.
- #85463's report is about raptors; the fix is a defence for any unbuckle across z-levels, not a confirmed fix of the original report.
