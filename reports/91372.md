## Summary

The reporter was turned into a chicken by a magic pool on Icebox, died, and then saw the dead chicken keep producing chicken messages in deadchat. On current master, dead chickens did not speak again in any variant tried: a chicken possessed by a player, a mindless one, one created by wabbajacking a human, and one whose AI had been running before it died. The chicken's speech behavior does not check whether the pawn is dead, but the AI controller is switched off on death, which cancels it. The issue dates from May 2025, before the current behavior-tree AI; the bug was likely in the older AI subtree code. No fix was made.

## The bug

As reported, a player polymorphed into a chicken died, and the corpse kept emitting chicken speech and sounds in deadchat indefinitely. The two screenshots in the issue show this; they were not available as text, so the exact messages are unknown. One later comment says only "True".

## Reproduction

Not reproduced. On master, with admin tools (Lua editor and the Observe/ghost verbs), these were tried, each followed by a wait of 30-40 seconds while reading chat for chicken messages:

1. A chicken (`/mob/living/basic/chicken`) spawned and possessed by a player, then killed with `death()`, then the player ghosted.
2. A mindless chicken killed immediately after spawning.
3. A human wabbajacked with the animal result (repeated until a chicken came out), with the mind transferred as the game does, then killed, then ghosted.
4. A chicken left alive without a client. It croons within about 10 seconds ("The chicken croons." with the clucking sound), which confirms its AI was running. It was then killed and watched for 40 seconds. Only the death message ("BWAAAAARK BWAK BWAK BWAK!") appeared. There was no further speech or floating text, and filtering chat for "chicken" showed nothing more.

In variant 3 the AI status of the dead chicken was not fully checked.

## Cause

No cause for the report was found. What the code does on master:

- The chicken uses `/datum/ai_controller/basic_controller/chicken` (`code/modules/mob/living/basic/farm_animals/chicken/chicken.dm:96`), driven by a behavior tree that includes `/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/random_speech_blackboard` (`code/datums/ai/generic_behaviors/speech.dm:146`). That behavior does not look at the pawn's stat, so on its own it would keep speaking after death.
- It is prevented from running by the controller: `get_expected_ai_status()` (`code/datums/ai/_ai_controller.dm:442`) returns off for a dead pawn that cannot act while dead. The controller re-evaluates this on stat change, and `set_ai_status()` (line 642) calls `cancel_current_plan()` (line 662) when the status becomes off, so nothing runs after death.

Not found: any "Icebox magic pool" polymorph source. A search for polymorph and wabbajack users found the generic wabbajack path (animal polymorph with mind transfer, `code/modules/mob/living/living.dm`), the polyjuice reagent, staves and wands, and the curse of polymorph.

## Fix

None. A possible hardening, not made and not needed on current evidence: return a failed/delayed result from the speech behaviors when the pawn is dead or has a client, so they are safe even if the controller is somehow on.

## Verification

Nothing to verify; no change was made. No runtimes related to this appeared in the test server's log during the runs.

Not tested: a player who disconnects while in the chicken form and then dies; reviving or re-entering the chicken's corpse; the original magic pool path.
