## Summary

Three botany issues were checked. #66920 (plant analyzer not listing chem genes of a replica pod seed) and #70015 (a 100-potency corpse flower never emitting miasma) did not reproduce on master. #76049 (a bonsai skillchip holder can trim a photosynthetic potted plant, which then turns into a normal sprite that keeps its glow) is real in code. `/obj/item/kirbyplants/photosynthetic` lacks `trimmable = FALSE`, which the other non-trimmable plants set. The fix adds that line. I did not get to test the fix in game.

## #66920: chem genes not shown for a replica pod

### The bug
The reporter right-clicked a replica pod seed with the plant analyzer after giving it extra chemical genes. The result showed only the seed's contained reagents ("blood 5") and no chem genes. A 2024 comment explained this by pointing to the old analyzer code, in which the reagent-holder check ended the scan before genes were examined.

### Reproduction
1. Spawned a replica pod seed with two random reagent genes.
2. With the plant analyzer in hand, right-clicked the seed (a real click, not a proc call).
3. The analyzer's UI opened. Its `seed_data.reagents` listed the chem genes (Sorium 0.13 and Garlic Juice 0.05), and the seed's own reagent holder contents were in a separate `plant_data` block.

Not tested: how the UI looks on a real client, and a seed that actually holds blood (the later comment says blood is irrelevant, and `plant_data` and `seed_data` are built independently).

### Cause
Not applicable on master. The analyzer now builds the two lists separately: reagents held by the item go into `plant_data` (`code/game/objects/items/devices/scanners/plant_analyzer.dm:256-262`), and chem genes into `seed_data["reagents"]` (`plant_analyzer.dm:315-320`). The tgui `PlantAnalyzer` page shows both. The code linked in the 2024 comment is the analyzer from before the UI rework, so the rework most likely fixed this.

### Fix
None needed.

## #70015: corpse flower not producing miasma

### The bug
A corpse flower with 100 potency and 10 yield reportedly produced no miasma in normal air or in vacuum. A later comment says it did produce gas at one round at 100 potency with some pressure, and that 0 potency did not help either.

### Reproduction
1. Planted a corpse flower (potency 100, yield 10, maturation 2) through the normal planting path (`propagate_plant`) in a constructed tray in standard air.
2. After about 60 seconds the plant was harvestable, the gas gene was processing, and the turf had 0.79 mol of miasma.

Not tested: vacuum or other pressures, and the failure the reporter saw.

### Cause
No bug was found. The gas gene's `process()` stops when the air pressure differs from one atmosphere by more than `potency / 10 + 10` (`code/modules/hydroponics/unique_plant_genes.dm:675-678`). Vacuum therefore produces no gas by design, and the seed's description says the odor stops "in difficult atmospheric conditions". Higher potency makes the allowed pressure band wider, not narrower.

One way the gene could fail silently: it only gets its tray through `set_home_tray`, which is called on `COMSIG_SEED_ON_PLANTED` (`unique_plant_genes.dm:636-643`). A tray whose seed was set another way, for example with `set_seed()` or a mapped-in plant, never gets a home tray, so `process()` calls `stop_gas()`. I could not show this in game, and it may not match what the reporter did.

### Fix
None.

## #76049: bonsai holders can trim photosynthetic plants

### The bug
With the bonsai skillchip (`TRAIT_BONSAI`) and a sharp tool, a player can trim any `kirbyplants` that is `trimmable`. For a photosynthetic plant this swaps its icon to an ordinary plant sprite while it keeps its blue light. Potty, the plastic plants and the fern cannot be trimmed.

### Reproduction
The in-game before/after test was not completed, so the following is read from code only:
1. Give a mob `TRAIT_BONSAI` and a sharp item such as a kitchen knife.
2. Spawn `/obj/item/kirbyplants/photosynthetic` on the floor and use the knife on it.
3. In the code, `item_interaction` starts the 3-second trim when `trimmable` is TRUE (`code/game/objects/items/kirby_plants/kirbyplants.dm:54`), and `change_visual()` then cycles `base_icon_state` (`kirbyplants.dm:72-78`).

In the test server I confirmed that `trimmable` defaulted to TRUE on a freshly spawned photosynthetic plant. I did not run the trim itself.

### Cause
`trimmable` defaults to TRUE (`kirbyplants.dm:18`). Several subtypes set it to FALSE, but `/obj/item/kirbyplants/photosynthetic` (`kirbyplants.dm:134`) does not. A later comment on the issue from the author of the sprite-subtyping change says that change did not touch the photosynthetic plant.

### Fix
One commit, "Fix issue 76049: photosynthetic potted plants can't be trimmed with the bonsai skillchip", adds `trimmable = FALSE` to the photosynthetic plant. This matches how Potty, the plastic plants and the fern already opt out. The alternative of making the trim keep the glow in step with the sprite was not pursued, since the issue and a comment question whether such a plant should be trimmable at all.

### Verification
Not verified in game. The test server was running a build without the change, and the fix was neither built nor tested. Still to check: that a trim attempt on the photosynthetic plant no longer starts, and that an ordinary random potted plant can still be trimmed.
