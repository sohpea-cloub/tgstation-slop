# #62547: Sideways firelocks interact strangely with ladders

| | |
|---|---|
| Issues | [#62547](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/62547): verified, fixed |
| Status | Verified on the test server; fix committed |
| Fix branch | [fix/62547](https://github.com/sohpea-cloub/tgstation-slop/tree/fix/62547) |
| Tested on | tgstation master `87b93338`, 2026-10-23 |

## Summary

A closed sideways (border-only) firelock on the same tile as a ladder makes the ladder impossible to climb from the side the firelock faces. The click on the ladder fails the `Adjacent()` check, because `/obj/machinery/door` overwrites `pass_flags_self` and loses the `LETPASSCLICKS` flag that other machines have. The fix adds `LETPASSCLICKS` back to `/obj/machinery/door/firedoor/border_only`.

## The bug

The reporter had a ladder and a closed sideways firelock on one tile. Clicking the ladder did nothing from one side ("I just clicked on the ladder a whole bunch"), while a ladder without a firelock worked. Nothing is wrong with the ladder itself: the click never reaches it because the game decides the user is not adjacent to it. Only the side the firelock faces is affected; the other sides work.

## Reproduction

1. On the test server, start the round and open the Lua editor as an admin.
2. Spawn a human at one tile (108,144 on the Z-level 2 station map), a ladder at 109,144, a second ladder at 109,148, and link the first ladder up to the second.
3. Spawn `/obj/machinery/door/firedoor/border_only/closed` at 109,144 and set its `dir` to WEST (8), so it faces the human.
4. Take control of the human (set its `ckey`).
5. From 108,144 (the tile the firelock faces), click the ladder. Result: nothing happened, twice.
6. Move to 109,145 (north of the ladder) and click it. Result: the "climbing up..." progress bar appeared.

So only the side the firelock faces fails. Evidence that the click itself reached the ladder: the firelock's sprite is transparent at the tile centre, and `PREVENT_CLICK_UNDER_1` is already cleared for border-only firelocks (`code/game/machinery/doors/firedoor.dm:739` and `:748`). The failure is the adjacency check, not click targeting.

## Cause

Clicking an object requires `user.Adjacent(object)`. For a neighbouring tile, that goes through `/turf/proc/ClickCross` (`code/_onclick/adjacent.dm:97`), which looks at every object on the tile:

```dm
if((mover && O.CanPass(mover, target_dir)) || (!mover && !O.density))
	continue
//If there's a dense object on the turf, only allow the click to pass if you can throw items over it or it has a special flag.
if(O == target || O == mover || (O.pass_flags_self & (LETPASSTHROW|LETPASSCLICKS)))
	continue
if( O.flags_1&ON_BORDER_1)
	if( O.dir & target_dir || ...
		return FALSE
```

A dense border object that fails `CanPass` in the click direction blocks the click unless its `pass_flags_self` contains `LETPASSTHROW` or `LETPASSCLICKS`. `/obj/machinery` sets `pass_flags_self = PASSMACHINE | LETPASSCLICKS` (`code/game/machinery/_machinery.dm:95`), but `/obj/machinery/door` replaces it with `pass_flags_self = PASSDOORS` (`code/game/machinery/doors/door.dm:17`), dropping `LETPASSCLICKS`. A closed border firelock is dense and faces its `dir`, so `ClickCross` returned FALSE, `Adjacent()` failed, and the ladder's `attack_hand` never ran. Full-tile firelocks are not on the border, so with `border_only` set that branch ignores them and they never blocked clicks.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 62547: sideways firelocks no longer stop clicks reaching a ladder on their tile". In `code/game/machinery/doors/firedoor.dm`, `/obj/machinery/door/firedoor/border_only` now has:

```dm
pass_flags_self = parent_type::pass_flags_self | LETPASSCLICKS
```

This restores the flag only on border-only firelocks, matching how full-tile firelocks already behave, and leaves movement and atmos blocking unchanged.

## Verification

After rebuilding, the same setup was repeated on the test server: human at 108,144, ladder at 109,144 linked to a ladder at 109,148, closed border-only firelock facing west. Clicking the ladder from 108,144 now produced the "climbing up..." progress bar; the click report listed the firelock as transparent at the click point and the ladder as the object hit. The runtime log showed no new runtimes.

Not checked: alt-click, other click types, windoors or other doors, a ladder under a firelock facing other directions, and the effect on combat or item use.

## Notes

- Side effect: because the firelock now lets clicks pass, anything else sharing the firelock's tile can also be reached or hit through it from the side it faces. That follows from the flag's purpose (windows keep blocking because they do not have it), but a maintainer should decide whether it is acceptable.
- The alt-click suggestion in the issue comments would not have helped: it goes through the same `Adjacent()` check.
