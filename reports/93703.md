## Summary
All three issues describe the "Ghost 2.0" rework (PR 92879, merged 2025-09-06), where Passthrough left a vulnerable grave crate behind and tethered the ghost within 7 tiles of it. PR 95779 (merged 2026-05-04) reverted that rework; its changelog says Passthrough has unlimited range and no longer leaves a grave. On current master the described mechanics do not exist, so nothing was fixed.

## The bug
- #93703: a ghost in Passthrough can be flushed through disposals far beyond the 7-tile grave limit.
- #93682: a ghost can ride the shuttle out of bounds; its grave is sold and the ghost gibs.
- #93756: using the ghost ability repeatedly inside a locker duplicates the mob.

## Reproduction
- #93703 and #93682: not reproducible. `code/modules/mob/living/carbon/human/species_types/ghost.dm` has `/datum/action/innate/toggle_passthrough` (`swap_mode`, around line 205) that only toggles `TRAIT_MOVE_PHASING`, plus a holy-turf block in `attempt_move`. No grave, range limit or grave type is referenced, and `git log -S"grave"` on the file finds nothing in this tree. These two were judged by reading the code, not by playing them.
- #93756: tested in game with the Lua editor. A human was spawned, set to `/datum/species/spirit/ghost`, moved into a `/obj/structure/closet`, and the passthrough ability's `Activate()` was called three times. The human stayed in the closet, there was one mob (no extra copy in the human list), and the action owner was the same mob.

## Cause
The old behaviours came from the grave and mob-swapping logic of the reverted rework. That logic is gone. The ghost species is now only reached through the wizard "Forsake Body" spell, polymorph or admins.

## Fix
None. No commits.

## Verification
Only the locker test above was run, by calling the ability's `Activate()` directly rather than through the player's button. Disposal flushing and shuttle riding with a ghost were not tested.

## Notes
Suggest closing all three as obsolete after PR 95779. Unlimited-range phasing is intended per that PR, so flushing a ghost through disposals is not an escape from anything.
