# #81226: Crafted food inherits its ingredients' reagents, which then react inside the dish

## Summary

When a food item is crafted, the `edible` component copies the reagents of every food ingredient into the result with reactions enabled. Reagents that are harmless in separate ingredients (potassium in a banana and water in a melon or bread, the cocoa, sugar and milk in a chocolate bar) therefore react in the dish: it "begins to bubble", explodes, or loses its contents. The fix transfers the inherited reagents without reacting and then drops every reagent that would take part in a reaction that could start in the dish. This stops the reactions, at the cost of also removing the non-offending reagents of such a reaction (for example sugar), which is a design choice described under Notes.

## The bug

#81226 lists several symptoms of reagents moving between ingredients and results during crafting:

- Foods containing a melon or water and bananas (the "banana rootwich", a melon fruit bowl) react as soon as they are crafted, so the food is destroyed or explodes.
- The trash cannon is filled with tar when crafted.
- Rice dough duplicates itself on any reagent update when made through the crafting menu.
- Carpotoxin transfers into recipes meant to neutralize it, and water gets transferred into some doughs (rootdough), making them explosive if mixed with bananas.
- Some recipes do not gain their own unique reagents.

#90321 reports that a crafted chocolate donut (a chocolate bar plus a plain donut) spawns a new chocolate bar after a short while, so chocolate can be duplicated.

On current master only one of these is still present: ingredient foods' own reagents are copied into the result with reactions enabled.

## Reproduction

All crafts were done through the crafting component on a test server, using admin tools. With the Lua editor, a human was spawned with a `personal_crafting` component, its `ignored_flags` set to `CRAFT_IGNORE_DO_AFTER` (512) so the craft does not wait on the do-after, and the recipe was looked up by name in `GLOB.cooking_recipes` and passed to `construct_item()`. This is the same call the crafting menu makes, but no one clicked the menu.

1. Spawn the ingredients on the floor next to the crafter.
2. Craft the **Melon fruit bowl** (watermelon, apple, orange, lemon, banana and ambrosia). Observed: the chat message "The solution begins to bubble."; the finished bowl contained no potassium and no water (they had reacted).
3. Craft the **Peanut butter and banana rootwich** with 5 units of water in each bread slice. Observed: "The solution begins to bubble." and the result's reagent list was empty (peanut butter and nutriment had been consumed or lost in the reaction); the beaker used for the peanut butter kept its 5 units.
4. Craft the same rootwich without water in the slices. Observed: no reaction at crafting time, but the result held banana 5, potassium 5 and peanut butter 5, a latent explosive that reacts as soon as water reaches it.
5. Craft a **Chocolate donut** (plain donut plus chocolate bar) with default reagents. Observed: the donut held nutriment 5, sugar 5, cocoa 2 and sprinkles 1. No chocolate-bar reaction can start (it needs milk or soy milk) and no bar spawned. **#90321 was therefore not reproduced as reported.**

Other bullets of #81226 were checked by reading the code at master and were not re-tested in game:

- Trash cannon tar: already fixed upstream by #86566. Only food recipes carry `CRAFT_TRANSFERS_REAGENT_COMPONENTS` (set in `code/modules/food_and_drinks/recipes/food_mixtures.dm:9`), and the trash cannon recipe is not a food recipe.
- Rice dough duplicating: the martian recipe sets `crafting_flags = CRAFT_CHECK_DENSITY` (`code/modules/food_and_drinks/recipes/tablecraft/recipes_martian.dm:78`), so it does not transfer reagent components.
- Water in rootdough: the lizard recipes at `recipes_lizard.dm:215` and `:241` already remove `CRAFT_TRANSFERS_REAGENT_COMPONENTS`, with a comment saying this prevents water from reacting immediately.
- Carpotoxin neutralizers, stepping mushrooms, and "recipes that don't gain their unique reagents" were not examined.

## Cause

`code/datums/components/food/edible.dm:343-351`, `OnCraft()`, which handles `COMSIG_ATOM_ON_CRAFT`:

```dm
for(var/obj/item/food/crafted_part in components)
	if(!crafted_part.reagents)
		continue
	this_food.reagents.maximum_volume += crafted_part.reagents.maximum_volume
	crafted_part.reagents.trans_to(this_food.reagents, crafted_part.reagents.maximum_volume)
```

`trans_to()` is called without `no_react`, so it defaults to FALSE and the transfer lets reactions run in the new holder. This copying happens whatever the recipe's `crafting_flags` are, so removing `CRAFT_TRANSFERS_REAGENT_COMPONENTS` from a recipe does not stop it for food ingredients. The recipe-level transfer in `code/datums/components/crafting/_crafting.dm:322` already uses `no_react = TRUE`, but nothing prevents the stored reagents from reacting later, on the next reagent update, which is why the rice dough symptom and the rootwich "latent bomb" occur.

Because the ingredients are copied together, any pair of reagents that react when mixed (potassium and water, flour and water, cocoa, sugar and milk in a chocolate bar, and so on) meet for the first time in the crafted dish.

## Fix

One commit on `fix/81226`, "Fix issue 81226: crafted food no longer inherits ingredient reagents that react with each other":

- `code/modules/reagents/chemistry/holder/reactions.dm`: a new proc `/datum/reagents/proc/remove_reactive_reagents()`. With the holder temporarily set to `NO_REACT`, it goes through the reactions indexed by the reagents present (`GLOB.chemical_reactions_list_reactant_index`). A reaction counts as startable when it has no `required_container`, at least two distinct required reagents and catalysts, all of them are present in at least the required (quantised) amount, and the holder's temperature satisfies the reaction's temperature condition. It removes the reaction's required reagents (not catalysts) and rescans, until nothing can start. It returns the total volume removed.
- `code/datums/components/food/edible.dm`: `OnCraft()` now transfers with `no_react = TRUE` and then calls `this_food.reagents.remove_reactive_reagents()`.
- `code/datums/components/crafting/_crafting.dm`: the same call is added after the recipe-reagent transfer, so a non-edible result of a food recipe is covered too.

The approach was chosen over the alternatives suggested in the issue thread: the inheritance is by design (as a commenter points out, foods inherit reagents from ingredients so that, for example, a botanist can deliberately make a dangerous dish), so inheritance itself was kept, and only combinations that would react are removed. Stripping ingredient reagents wholesale, or fixing recipes one by one, was not done.

## Verification

After rebuilding, the same crafts were run again with the same script:

- Melon fruit bowl: no bubbling. The bowl held nutriment 22, vitamin 12, banana juice 5 and the ambrosia's own reagents (aiuri, libital, space drugs, toxin). Potassium and water were gone.
- Rootwich with watered slices: no bubbling. Result: peanut butter 5, nutriment 9, banana 5, vitamin 2. Potassium and water were dropped.
- Chocolate donut with default reagents: unchanged from before the fix.
- Chocolate donut with 2 units of milk added to the chocolate bar (added with reactions suppressed so the bar did not react by itself): no new chocolate bar spawned (bars = 0), no bubbling, and the donut held only nutriment 5 and sprinkles 1.
- No new runtimes were logged.

Not checked:

- The milk case was only run after the fix. Before the fix the donut was expected to run the `chocolate_bar3` reaction, based on the code and the reaction definitions, but this exact combination was not run on unfixed code.
- The unit test in `code/modules/unit_tests/crafting.dm` was not run (no unit-test build was made). It does not assert on result reagents.
- Crafts were made through the crafting component's `construct_item()`, not through the crafting menu UI.
- Recipes other than the three above, the trash cannon, rice dough, rootdough, carpotoxin and mushroom bullets were not re-tested in game.

## Notes

- **Trade-off in `remove_reactive_reagents()`.** When a reaction could start, all of that reaction's required reagents are removed in full, not just the amount needed. A chocolate donut with milk present therefore loses its sugar and the bar's cocoa, and the fruit bowl loses its water and potassium while keeping banana juice, vitamins and nutriment. Non-reactive inheritance is unchanged. A maintainer may prefer something narrower (for example only removing the reagent that is not the dish's own), or to reject the design that foods inherit reagents at all.
- This removes the "evil botanist" option raised in the issue thread (making a dish deliberately explosive through the potassium in bananas) for any combination that would react on crafting. Dishes can still carry a latent reaction if reagents are added after crafting.
- Catalysts are not removed, only a reaction's required reagents, and reactions with fewer than two distinct requirements (decompositions) and reactions needing a specific container are ignored.
- Three of the bullets in #81226 appear to have been fixed upstream already (trash cannon tar via #86566; rice dough; rootdough water), which suggests the issue could be narrowed or split.
- #90321: the donut recipe on this build takes only the chocolate bar and a plain donut. With no milk or soy milk in the bar, the bug cannot occur. The reporter's bar presumably held milk or another reaction partner. A commenter on that issue said he did not see an issue; it is unclear what the reporter's setup was.
