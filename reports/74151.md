# #74151: Teslium reactions do not charge tesla coils

| | |
|---|---|
| Issues | [#74151](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/74151): verified and fixed; [#50504](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/50504): not reproduced |
| Status | fixed (#74151); #50504 not reproduced |
| Fix branch | [fix/74151](https://github.com/sohpea-cloub/tgstation-slop/tree/fix/74151) |
| Tested on | tgstation master `87b93338`, 2026-10-23 |

## Summary

The teslium + water reaction and teslium's spark reaction zap with `ZAP_LOW_POWER_GEN` but without `ZAP_GENERATES_POWER`. A tesla coil ignores any zap that lacks `ZAP_GENERATES_POWER`, so these zaps never charge it. Grounding rods store every zap, which is why the reporter saw power on the rod and none on the coils. The fix adds `ZAP_GENERATES_POWER` to both zap flag sets, so coils charge at the reduced low-power rate. #50504 (zaps ignoring targets in the dark) could not be reproduced on this build.

## The bug

### #74151

A recurring teslium/water reaction next to anchored grounding rods and tesla coils showed the rods producing up to 25 kW. The coils flashed when hit but produced no power, and an attached SMES was not charged.

### #50504

A 2020 report said tesla zaps ignore targets standing in darkness, because `oview()` was believed to account for light levels. The thread's suggested workarounds were never completed.

## Reproduction

### #74151

1. On the test server, using the Lua editor, anchor a tesla coil one tile from a bluespace beaker.
2. Put 50 units of teslium and 50 units of water in the beaker. The reaction fired ("The teslium starts to spark as electricity arcs away from it!").
3. Sample the coil's `stored_energy` every 0.3 seconds.

Before the fix the coil played its "hit" animation but `stored_energy` stayed at 0 in every sample.

This was triggered by mixing the reagents in a beaker, not through a plumbing setup. A SMES-connected coil and a grounding rod were not tested.

### #50504

1. Using SDQL2, turn every station light off (`set_light(0)` on all `/obj/machinery/light` on the station z-level).
2. Anchor a new tesla coil on a turf with `get_lumcount()` of 0 (its area has `static_lighting` and the turf has `luminosity` 1).
3. Trigger the same teslium reaction one tile away.

With the fix applied, the coil's `stored_energy` was 62.5 at the second sample, so the zap found a target in the dark. The darkness test was done only on the fixed build, since the zap cannot charge a coil without the fix. It shows the zap still reached a coil on a fully dark tile.

## Cause

`tesla_coil/zap_act()` (`code/modules/power/tesla/coil.dm:88`) returns early unless the zap carries `ZAP_GENERATES_POWER`:

```
if(!(zap_flags & ZAP_GENERATES_POWER)) //Prevent infinite recursive power
	return 0
if(zap_flags & ZAP_LOW_POWER_GEN)
	power /= 10
```

`ZAP_LOW_POWER_GEN` only divides the power by 10 once the first check passes. The teslium zap sites set only the low-power flag:

- `code/modules/reagents/chemistry/recipes/pyrotechnics.dm:606`: `zap_flags` of the `reagent_explosion/teslium_lightning` recipe.
- `code/modules/reagents/chemistry/reagents/pyrotechnic_reagents.dm:460`: `on_spark_act()` of the teslium reagent.

The grounding rod's `zap_act()` (`coil.dm:155`) adds the energy to `stored_energy` regardless of flags, which explains the report.

Other zaps also carry `ZAP_LOW_POWER_GEN` without `ZAP_GENERATES_POWER`: the tesla revolver (`projectile/energy/tesla.dm:6`), wizard lightning (`projectile/magic.dm:469`, `:489`), grilles (`grille.dm:388`) and the ethereal stomach (`stomach_ethereal.dm:140`). These were not tested.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 74151: teslium reaction and reagent zaps now charge tesla coils". It adds `ZAP_GENERATES_POWER` to the `zap_flags` of the teslium_lightning recipe and to the `tesla_zap()` call in teslium's `on_spark_act()`. `ZAP_LOW_POWER_GEN` stays, so coils get a tenth of the zap's power. A reply on the issue said teslium "should still generate some power".

Not changed: the other `ZAP_LOW_POWER_GEN`-only users listed above, which are outside this issue.

## Verification

After the fix, the build was rebuilt and the test server restarted. The same beaker test (50 teslium, 50 water, coil one tile away) gave `stored_energy` samples of 0, 62.5, 62.5, 62.5, 62.5, 0: the coil charged, and the stored energy was then drained by the coil's processing. The runtime log showed no runtimes from this.

Not checked: a SMES connected to a coil, a full plumbing setup, the teslium `on_spark_act` path on its own, larger reaction volumes, or any of the other zap sources.

## Notes

- The coil's own zaps (`coil.dm:17`) must not get `ZAP_GENERATES_POWER`: the check exists to stop coils recursively powering themselves.
- Giving the revolver, wizard lightning, grilles and the ethereal stomach the same flag is a balance decision the maintainers may want to make separately.
- #50504: the test found turfs keep `luminosity` 1 even at `get_lumcount()` 0, so `oview()` still sees them. It is marked a BYOND issue upstream; this may differ on other BYOND versions or for turfs with `luminosity` 0, which were not tested.
