## Summary

The issue lists five exploration-drone problems. Four were real on master: every "Ignore"/"Leave" choice in the exodrone adventures was wired to the failure node and cost the drone half its integrity; the cargo bay's total weight limit was the storage default, so only 4 normal-sized items fit instead of the 6 slots shown; two adventures awarded a loot category (`research`) that does not exist; and the drone log was never trimmed. The fix adds a `LEAVE` adventure node that ends the adventure with no damage and no loot, raises the cargo weight limit to 6 normal items, points the two adventures at the `trade_contract` loot category, and makes the log trimming actually remove entries. The fifth report, the tree softlock, was already fixed by an upstream PR.

## The bug

The issue reports (paraphrased):

1. The drone log "messes up" after exploring several anomalies.
2. Successful events give no supply token reward.
3. Ignoring the space yacht still damages the drone by 50%.
4. The sixth inventory slot "is a lie": drones only hold 5 items.
5. The tree event probably softlocks the drone unless it is self-destructed.

What happens on master:

- **Yacht damage.** Choosing "Ignore it, its not worth investigating." (or "Fly away, no chance in hell of getting in there..") ends the adventure as a failure, and the drone loses half its max integrity. Every pure ignore/leave choice in the five adventures does the same.
- **Cargo slots.** The UI shows 6 slots, but a player inserting normal-weight items by hand can only fit 4.
- **Missing rewards.** Winning the tree or the Britain-replica ("A Model Earth") adventure gives nothing. Only the Robot's Wingman adventure produced trade chips.
- **Log.** The drone log grows without bound instead of staying at its intended size of 15.

## Reproduction

All steps used the Lua editor on the test server, run as an admin ghost, calling the adventure and storage procs directly rather than playing the full drone-console flow.

1. Spawn an `/obj/item/exodrone`.
2. **Yacht damage.** Take the space yacht entry from `GLOB.explorer_drone_adventure_db_entries`, call `create_adventure()` on it, `start_adventure()` on the drone with the result, then `select_choice('choice 0')` ("Ignore it"). Result on master: the drone ("Rosetta") had integrity 100 of 200 and status `exploration`.
3. **Cargo slots.** Insert 7 `/obj/item/melee/baton/security/loaded` (normal weight class) with `atom_storage.attempt_insert`. On master `space_left()` was 2 afterwards, i.e. only 4 batons fit, while the UI reported 6 free slots.
4. **Loot.** Reading `strings/exoadventures` showed that `tree_in_the_middle_of_space.json` and `britain_replica.json` list `"loot_categories": ["research"]`, and `code/modules/explorer_drone/loot.dm` has no such category. The missing reward was established from the data, not by playing a win to the end on master.
5. **Log.** Not measured in game on either side (see Verification).

## Cause

- **Yacht damage.** The "Ignore"/"Leave" choices have `"exit_node": "FAIL"`. `handle_special_nodes` treats `FAIL_NODE` as a damaging result (`code/modules/explorer_drone/adventure.dm:230`), and `resolve_adventure` answers `ADVENTURE_RESULT_DAMAGE` with `damage(max_integrity*0.5)` (`code/modules/explorer_drone/exodrone.dm:267-268`). There was no node meaning "walk away".
- **Cargo slots.** `create_storage(max_slots = EXODRONE_CARGO_SLOTS, canthold = ...)` (`exodrone.dm:77`) leaves `max_total_storage` at its default of `WEIGHT_CLASS_SMALL * 7` = 14 (`code/datums/storage/storage.dm:65`). Six slots therefore only fill with small or lighter items; with normal items (weight 3) the weight cap is hit after four. Loot added by adventures bypasses this check, since it is moved in directly.
- **Loot.** The tree and Britain adventures ask for a `research` loot category that no generator provides; the existing ids include `maint`, `trade_contract`, `unique`, `cash`, `drugs`, `materials`, `weapons`, `syndicate_fish` and `pets`.
- **Log.** `drone_log()` does `drone_log.Remove()` with no arguments (`exodrone.dm:344-345`), which removes nothing, so the list is never trimmed.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 66085: exodrone cargo slots, ignore/leave damage, no loot, log cap":

- Adds `LEAVE_NODE` (`"LEAVE"`) and `ADVENTURE_RESULT_LEAVE` (`code/__DEFINES/adventure.dm`). `handle_special_nodes` ends the adventure with the leave result, and `resolve_adventure` handles it like a success without rewards: it unregisters the adventure signals, clears `current_adventure` and puts the drone back to `exploration`, with no damage.
- Changes the pure ignore/leave choices from `FAIL` to `LEAVE`: yacht (two), tree (two, including the "Leave." choice that upstream added to fix the softlock), Robot's Wingman ("Ignore other Drone"), Britain replica ("Ignore the planet."), and Quantum Fizz-ics ("Leave."). Choices that really are failures keep `FAIL`.
- Sets `max_total_storage = EXODRONE_CARGO_SLOTS * WEIGHT_CLASS_NORMAL` so six normal items fit.
- Changes the tree and Britain loot category from `research` to `trade_contract`, the category Robot's Wingman already used.
- Makes `drone_log()` trim with `Cut(EXODRONE_LOG_SIZE)` when the list has reached the limit, dropping the oldest entries (new ones are inserted at the front).

A new node was added rather than changing the `FAIL` result, so real failures still damage the drone.

## Verification

On the rebuilt test server:

- Yacht "Ignore it": drone integrity stayed 200 of 200, status `exploration` (master: 100).
- Seven normal batons: `space_left()` was 0 after insertion, so six fit (master: four).
- Tree adventure `award_adventure_loot`: one item was added to the drone (space left went from 6 to 5).

Not checked: the log cap (an attempt to call `drone_log` 25 times from the Lua editor failed, partly because of a name clash between the variable and the proc, and SDQL2 would not parse the call, so this part rests on reading the code); the Britain and Quantum Fizz-ics `LEAVE` choices and the other tree/yacht leave choices in game; playing a complete drone-console run from the cargo console; the admin adventure browser, which just prints the new `leave` result.

## Notes

- **Tree softlock:** already fixed upstream (PR #78378 added the extra "Leave" choice); that choice is present on master. This fix only changes its exit node so leaving no longer costs damage.
- **"No token reward" is ambiguous.** It may mean the tree/Britain wins giving nothing (fixed here) or the random site events in `resource.dm` giving no supply chips (not changed). A maintainer should decide whether `trade_contract` is the right loot for those two adventures; it was chosen only because it is an existing category already used by Robot's Wingman.
- Upstream PR #78378 is also mentioned as touching the log; that change was not in this tree, and the log defect described above was still present.
- Whether ignoring a site should really be free is a design choice; the issue author considers the damage a bug.
