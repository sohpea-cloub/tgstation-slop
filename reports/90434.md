# #90434: Living players can hear ghosts going through portals

| | |
|---|---|
| Issues | [#90434](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/90434): verified, fixed |
| Status | Fixed (one commit), checked in game on a test server |
| Fix branch | [fix/90434](https://github.com/sohpea-cloub/tgstation-slop/tree/fix/90434) |
| Tested on | tgstation master `87b93338`, 2026-10-23 |

## Summary

When a ghost (observer) entered a portal, the portal's travel sound, its entry animation and the teleport sparks all played for nearby living players, revealing something they cannot see. `/obj/effect/portal/proc/teleport` ran these unconditionally. The fix treats an observer as a silent teleport: no sparks, no animation, no sounds.

## The bug

Living players near a portal hear the "whoosh" whenever a ghost enters it, even though the ghost is invisible to them. The issue was reported with a hand teleporter and applies to any portal using the same code.

## Reproduction

1. On the test server, start the round and spawn a human to act as the listener.
2. Using the Lua editor, spawn an observer and a `/obj/effect/portal` whose `hard_target_override` is a turf six tiles east.
3. Make the ghost enter the portal by calling `attack_ghost` on the portal with the observer (the ghost-click path, called through Lua rather than by a player clicking).
4. Check which sounds the living human receives.

Observed on master: the living human heard `sound/effects/portal/portal_travel.ogg` at 50% volume and again from the destination turf at 10%, triggered only by the ghost.

## Cause

`code/game/objects/effects/portals.dm:145-168` (`/obj/effect/portal/proc/teleport`) has no check for who is moving. On a successful `do_teleport` it creates a `portal_animation` and plays `SFX_PORTAL_ENTER` at both the start turf and the destination (lines 164-166). `no_effect` (line 154-158) only depends on `last_effect == world.time` and `sparkless`, so `do_teleport` also made its own teleport sparks for ghosts.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 90434: ghosts going through portals no longer make portal sound, sparks or animation", in `portals.dm`: `teleport` computes `var/silent = isobserver(moving)`, treats it like `sparkless` when setting `no_effect`, and skips the `portal_animation` and both `playsound` calls when `silent`. The teleport itself still happens. Gating on `isobserver` was preferred over suppressing the sound in the sound system, since the other portal effects leak the ghost's presence in the same way.

## Verification

In game, with the same setup before and after:

- After the fix, the ghost teleported (the observer was found at the destination turf in `dead_mob_list`) and the living human heard no sound.
- As a control, calling `teleport` with a living human played both portal travel sounds as before.

Not checked: a hand teleporter used by a player, gateways, and other ghost types (any `isobserver` mob is covered by the check, but only one observer was tested). Other teleport sound sites, such as `teleporter.dm:82` and the hand teleporter code in `teleportation.dm`, were not changed; they do not accept ghosts.

## Notes

- `isobserver` is also true for admin ghosts, who now pass through portals silently. That seems desirable but was not specifically tested.
- Ghosts that are visible (for example with a revenant-style or cultist effect) are not special-cased.
