## Summary

The report (from 2021) says snow tiles placed on mined snow on icebox can't be removed with a pickaxe or crowbar, and no tile can be placed over them. On current master a snow tile can't be placed on mined icemoon snow at all, because mined snow is no longer plating. A snow tile placed on ordinary plating produces a `fake_snow` turf that a shovel removes. Crowbars, pickaxes and other tiles do nothing on it, silently. That looks deliberate, but it gives no feedback.

## The bug

As reported: on icebox, dig the snow outside with a pickaxe, make snow tiles from the snow blocks, place them on the dug tiles, then try to remove them with a pickaxe or crowbar. The reporter says nothing worked, and tiles couldn't be placed on top either.

## Reproduction

Tried on master with real clicks by a possessed human on the test server, using turfs set up with the Lua editor (the Meta Station map, not icebox).

1. In the Lua editor, change three adjacent turfs to `/turf/open/misc/asteroid/snow/icemoon` (mined icebox snow), `/turf/open/floor/plating`, and `/turf/open/floor/fake_snow`. Spawn a human with a pickaxe, crowbar, shovel, snow tile, iron tile and snow sheet, and possess it.
2. Click the snow tile on undug icemoon snow: nothing happened.
3. Dig the icemoon snow with the pickaxe (about 4 seconds; a snow item dropped). Click the snow tile on the dug turf: nothing happened and the tile stayed in hand.
4. Click the snow tile on the plating: the tile was used up (1 to 0) and the turf became `fake_snow`.
5. On the `fake_snow`: the crowbar did nothing, the pickaxe did nothing, and an iron tile (with the crowbar in the other hand) did nothing and gave no message.
6. The shovel on the `fake_snow` printed "You dig up the snow.", the turf became plating, and a snow tile dropped.

Result: the exact steps in the report can't be followed, but the turf the tile does produce (on plating) is not unremovable.

## Cause

- Tiles are placed only on plating: `place_tile` takes a `/turf/open/floor/plating` and returns otherwise (`code/game/objects/items/stacks/tiles/tile_types.dm:76`, called from `code/game/turfs/open/floor/plating.dm:93`). Mined icebox snow is `/turf/open/misc/asteroid/snow/icemoon`, which is not plating. Its `item_interaction` (`code/game/turfs/open/misc.dm:24`) handles only rods and metal tiles. So a snow tile used on dug snow does nothing. In 2021 mined snow was plating, which explains the report; that is inferred from the current code, not checked in the old code.
- `/turf/open/floor/fake_snow` (`code/game/turfs/open/floor/fancy_floor.dm:220`) sets `floor_tile = null`, adds the `diggable` element (`fancy_floor.dm:243`, shovel only, drops one snow tile), and overrides `try_replace_tile` and `crowbar_act` to return nothing (`fancy_floor.dm:248-252`). So crowbars can't pry it up, other tiles can't replace it, and a pickaxe isn't handled. Only a shovel removes it.

## Fix

None. No code was changed.

## Verification

Not applicable. Not checked: actual icebox map behaviour (the test used Meta Station with hand-set turfs), and any other tool (for example a welding tool, or deconstruction by explosion) on `fake_snow`.

## Notes

- Silent no-ops on `fake_snow` are a small usability gap. A possible change, if wanted: let `crowbar_act` pry `fake_snow` and return a snow tile, let `try_replace_tile` fall through to the parent, and/or let the pickaxe dig it. The `floor_tile = null` and the overrides look intentional, so this is a design decision.
- Using Lua to delete objects or to read a turf's `contents` crashed the test server twice during testing. That is separate from this issue and was not investigated.
