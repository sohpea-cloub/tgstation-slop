## Summary

The chain drawn between a player and an item handcuffed to them stretched to the bottom-left corner of the z-level when the player was inside a bitrunning netpod. The cause was that the chain was drawn to the player's raw location, and an atom inside a netpod has no map position of its own. This was already fixed upstream by PR #98082 ("Fixes cuffed items lagging in vents and disposals, broken chain visuals and uncuffing from the strip menu", merged 2026-10-10), which is an ancestor of the tested master. The chain is now drawn between the outermost holders of each end and hidden when the two ends share a holder, and in the netpod check below no stretched chain appeared. No fix was committed for this unit.

## The bug

### #97432

A player who had handcuffed themselves to an item and then entered a bitrunning netpod saw the chain stretch from the item to what looked like the bottom-left tile of the z-level. The reporter believed it was visual only and said the chain reconnected to their character when they climbed out of the pod. The report was made on a round with test merges, with client 516.1685. The reproduction given was "1. Handcuff yourself to an item 2. Bitrun".

### #96179

Reported earlier (client 516.1677) as "handcuff something to yourself, go in bitrunning pod domain, the chain consumes" (the screenshot shows the chain across the station). It describes the same behavior as #97432, and the same fix applies. It was treated as a duplicate.

What was happening: the cuffed-item chain (a `Beam` with the "chain" icon) was attached to the two raw atoms, the cuffed item and the cuffed player. When the player was inside the netpod (or any other container), the player's `loc` was the pod and not a turf. A beam endpoint that is not on a turf has coordinates 0,0, so the chain was drawn to the corner of the map.

## Reproduction

Not reproduced on master `87b93338`. The test checked that the case in the report no longer produces a stretched chain.

The steps were run with the Lua editor on the test server, and the netpod was closed around the player by calling `close_machine` directly. This was not the player action of dragging oneself into a pod, and no quantum server or bitrunning domain was set up (see Verification).

1. Spawned a human, a pair of handcuffs and a briefcase.
2. Applied the `/datum/status_effect/cuffed_item` status effect to the human with the briefcase and the handcuffs, then dropped the briefcase on the floor.
3. Placed a `/obj/machinery/netpod` 8 tiles away and put the human into it with `close_machine`.
4. Inspected the briefcase, the human and the chain with View Variables/Lua.

Observed on master:

- While the human was in the pod: the human's `loc` was the netpod, the briefcase's `loc` was also the netpod (the item was carried along with its owner), the briefcase had no `chained_together` component and there was no beam.
- After `open_machine()` on the pod: both were on the floor on the same tile (111,144), the component was present again, and the beam went from the briefcase to the human on that tile.
- `runtime.log` contained no runtime related to cuffs or the chain.

## Cause

Before PR #98082 (commit "Fixes cuffed items lagging in vents and disposals, broken chain visuals and uncuffing from the strip menu"), `/datum/component/chained_together` created its beam directly between the component's parent and the atom it was chained to, with `movable_parent.Beam(chained_to, "chain", animate = FALSE)`. If one end was inside a container and the other was not, such as the player in a netpod and the item on the floor, the beam end that was in the container had no turf position and the chain was drawn to the bottom-left corner of the map. The PR text describes the same symptom ("the chain got redrawn from the item to the bottom left corner of the map") for vents, disposals, closets and mechs; the netpod case is the same mechanism.

Current code on master contains the fix:

- `code/datums/status_effects/cuffed_item.dm:423` `update_beam()` resolves both ends with `get_beam_anchor()`, which returns `get_atom_on_turf(chain_end)` (`:440-444`) and null if the result is deleted or not on a turf. The beam is cleared when either anchor is null, when both anchors are the same atom, or when they are on different z-levels.
- `code/datums/status_effects/cuffed_item.dm:221` `on_owner_moved()` moves the cuffed item into the object the owner enters (or out of it), so that in most cases the item and its owner share a holder and no chain is drawn.

## Fix

No commit was made for this unit; the fix already exists on master in the upstream PR above. What it changes:

- The item goes in and out of containers together with its owner (`on_owner_moved`), so the leash no longer has to pathfind to a player inside an object.
- The chain is drawn between the outermost holders of each end (`get_atom_on_turf`) and hidden when they share a holder, when a holder is not on a turf, or when the z-levels differ.

Unit tests for this exist in `code/modules/unit_tests/cuffed_item.dm` (for example `follows_owner_through_closet`, which checks that the item goes into a closet with its owner without a tether and comes back out with them).

## Verification

Checked in game on master `87b93338` (steps under Reproduction): inside a netpod there was no chain component and no beam, the item was inside the pod with the owner, and after opening the pod the beam went between the two on the same tile. No runtime was logged.

Not checked:

- A full bitrunning run: no quantum server or domain was set up, so the avatar swap and the owner's body staying in the pod while their mind plays the avatar were not exercised. The beam logic for the owner's body in the pod is the same as in the check above.
- How the chain looks on screen: the beam was inspected as a datum (its endpoints), not rendered.
- Pre-fix behavior: it was not run on a build without the PR; the account of the old behavior comes from the PR's description and the removed code in its diff.

## Notes

- Both issues were filed before the upstream fix (#96179 on 2026-05-22, #97432 on 2026-08-09) and are still open as of the issue text read for this unit; they could be closed as fixed by #98082.
- #96179 is a duplicate of #97432.
- The fixed behavior makes the item travel inside the netpod with the player. A maintainer should be aware that the pod then contains the cuffed item; nothing in the checks suggests a problem with this, but taking the item along when the player's mind is moved to an avatar was not tested.
