## Summary

**#92182:** Catwalk's upper level sets its baseturf trait to `/turf/open/openspace/airless`, so removing plating on the upper deck exposes a vacuum tile. The fix uses plain `/turf/open/openspace`, as the other multi-z maps do. The claim in the title (lower-deck walls revealing space) was not reproduced: a lower wall scrapes to plating, and plating then scrapes to space, the same as on Nebula.

**#94257:** The food dumbwaiter shaft on Catwalk's lower deck has an intercom and a light mapped on a transport platform's tile. The platform collected them as cargo, so moving it dragged them off their wall and they fell apart into frames. The fix makes transport modules ignore wall-mounted objects. This affects every tram and elevator platform, so it deserves review.

## #92182: baseturf of Catwalk's levels

### The bug

Deconstructing plating on Catwalk's upper deck leaves airless openspace, which is a vacuum. One commenter reported this ("breaking plating there creates vacuum"). The issue title and body say that deconstructing walls (and, per a comment, crowbarring floor tiles) on the lower deck spaces the station. A later comment says that could not be reproduced on master.

### Reproduction

1. Load Catwalk (Change-Map admin verb, then start a round) and observe.
2. Using the Lua editor, call `ScrapeAway()` on an upper-deck (world z3) plating tile and read the resulting turf type. It became `/turf/open/openspace/airless`.
3. Do the same to a lower-deck (world z2) wall: the result was `/turf/open/floor/plating`. Scraping that plating again gave `/turf/open/space`.

The lower-deck result matches the other maps with no bottom baseturf (for example Nebula). Scraping the lowest plating gives space because it is the bottom of the world, so I could not reproduce a lower wall spacing the station.

### Cause

`_maps/map_jsons/catwalkstation.json:19` gives the upper level `"Baseturf": "/turf/open/openspace/airless"`. The lower level (line 15) has no `Baseturf`. Walls have baseturfs `/turf/open/floor/plating`, and plating has `/turf/baseturf_bottom`. `ChangeTurf` resolves that placeholder from the z-level trait (`code/game/turfs/change_turf.dm:60`: `SSmapping.level_trait(z, ZTRAIT_BASETURF) || /turf/open/space`). On the upper deck that trait is the airless openspace. Nebula, Tram and Multiz use `/turf/open/openspace` for their upper level.

### Fix

Commit "Fix issue 92182: Catwalk's upper deck baseturf is regular openspace" changes that one JSON value to `/turf/open/openspace`.

### Verification

After rebuilding and restarting on Catwalk, scraping upper plating gave `/turf/open/openspace`, and scraping a lower wall still gave plating. Not checked: crowbarring floor tiles by hand, atmos behavior when a hole is opened, and the map-level `baseturf_helper` instances in the .dmm (three, noted but not examined).

## #94257: kitchen intercom breaks at round start

### The bug

The reporter saw Catwalk's kitchen intercom broken at round start, with nobody else nearby, and suspected either a bad start state or the food dumbwaiter. The attached screenshot was not examined.

### Reproduction

1. Load Catwalk and start a round.
2. The map has two kitchen intercoms: one at world (107,156,3) on openspace beside a wall, and one at (111,156,2) on the elevator-shaft turf next to the food dumbwaiter's `/obj/structure/transport/linear/public` platform. The platform's `transport_contents` included the (111,156,2) intercom and the nearby east light.
3. Using the Lua editor, move the platform up with `simple_move_wrapper(16, 20)` on the transport controller (this calls the platform move directly, not the player-facing dumbwaiter control).
4. After the move, a `/obj/item/wallframe/intercom` and an `/obj/structure/light_construct` were left on the platform's old tile and the platform sat on z3.

### Cause

A transport module gathers everything on its tiles as cargo (`code/modules/transport/transport_module.dm`, `add_item_on_transport` at line 137 and `add_initial_contents` at line 154). The controller collects these before wall mounts have initialized and found their wall, so the intercom and light became platform cargo. When the platform moved, the cargo moved with it. The `atom_mounted` component treats movement as being torn off the wall and calls `drop_wallmount()` (`code/datums/components/atom_mounted.dm:84-97`), which turns the object into its frame.

### Fix

Commit "Fix issue 94257: wall mounts no longer ride transport platforms", all in `code/modules/transport/transport_module.dm`:

- `add_item_on_transport` now refuses anything with `TRAIT_WALLMOUNTED`.
- `LateInitialize` calls a new `remove_wallmounted_from_transport()` on every module of the controller, to drop wall mounts collected before they initialized.
- `reset_contents` skips wall-mounted objects when looking for foreign contents, so they are not deleted.

### Verification

On a fresh round, the platform's `transport_contents` held only the smartfridge. After sending the platform up, the intercom and light stayed at (111,156,2), and the platform and smartfridge were at z3.

Not checked: any tram or other elevator, a normal player-triggered dumbwaiter ride, and whether anything legitimately wall-mounted is meant to travel with a platform.

## Notes

- **Conflicting early evidence for #94257.** An early check (SDQL for intercoms in the kitchen area) found none among 302 intercoms, which suggested both kitchen intercoms were gone at round start. A later session found both present at round start and traced the loss to platform movement. The inputs do not explain the first result; I could not tell whether that check was flawed.
- **Which intercom?** The report says "upper kitchen intercom", but the one on the upper deck (107,156,3) was fine. The one that broke is on the lower-deck shaft. The reporter may have meant that one, or something else was also involved. The reporter's test merges were not examined.
- The #94257 change touches shared tram/elevator code, so a maintainer should confirm no map intentionally rides wall mounts on a platform.
- Lower-deck behavior in #92182 was left as is: the bottom plating becomes space. If a different bottom is wanted, the lower level would need its own `Baseturf` in the JSON.
