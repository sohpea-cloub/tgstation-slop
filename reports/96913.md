# #96913: Illusions never attack their targets, and mirage grenade illusions vanish instantly

| | |
|---|---|
| Issues | [#96913](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/96913): verified, fixed |
| Status | Fixed (one commit) |
| Fix branch | [fix/96913](https://github.com/sohpea-cloub/tgstation-slop/tree/fix/96913) |
| Tested on | tgstation master `87b93338`, 2026-10-23 |

## Summary

Two mistakes in `code/modules/mob/living/basic/illusion/illusion.dm` broke illusions. `mock_as()` tested its optional arguments with `isnull()` the wrong way round. A mirage grenade passes no health, so its illusion got `maxHealth = null` and died at once. `full_setup()` also copied the original's factions after `mock_as()` had removed `FACTION_NEUTRAL`, which put the illusion back in the same faction as its target. The fix inverts the three checks and copies the factions before calling `mock_as()`.

## The bug

Illusions made with `full_setup()` (for example the clones from the Grey Tide spear) stood still next to their target and never attacked. Illusions made by calling `mock_as()` directly (the mirage grenade) disappeared as soon as they spawned. The chat showed "<name> vanishes into thin air! It was a fake!" immediately instead of after the intended 15 seconds.

## Reproduction

On master, using admin tools only. The Grey Tide spear stab itself was not tested. The `full_setup()` call it makes was invoked directly from the Lua editor.

1. Observe, open the Lua editor and create a state.
2. Spawn two humans, A (the illusion's original) and B (the target), a few tiles apart.
3. Spawn an `/mob/living/basic/illusion` and call `full_setup(A, B, nil, 300, 100, 12, 0)` on it.
4. Inspect the illusion:
   - The `Current Target` blackboard key was not B, so the target had been cleared.
   - `melee_damage_upper` was 5, not the 12 passed in.
   - `faction_check_atom(B)` returned 1 (they shared a faction).
   - The illusion did not move toward B and B kept its full 100 health.
5. Spawn an `/obj/item/grenade/mirage`, call `arm_grenade(A, ...)` and then `detonate()`.
6. The chat immediately showed "vanishes into thin air! It was a fake!".

## Cause

All line numbers are at master.

1. `mock_as()` (`illusion.dm:67`) used inverted checks:

   ```dm
   if (isnull(hp))
   	maxHealth = hp
   	updatehealth()
   if (isnull(damage))
   	...
   if (isnull(replicate))
   	...
   ```

   The values were applied only when they were null, and never when they were provided. The mirage grenade (`code/modules/mod/modules/modules_security.dm:306`) and the hallucination wand (`code/modules/projectiles/guns/magic/wands/wand_hallucination.dm:47`) call `mock_as(user, 15 SECONDS)` with no `hp`. That set `maxHealth = null`, `updatehealth()` killed the mob, and `DEL_ON_DEATH` removed it. Callers of `full_setup()` had their `hp`, `damage` and `replicate` arguments ignored.

2. `full_setup()` (`illusion.dm:53-58`) called `mock_as()` first. `mock_as()` removes `FACTION_NEUTRAL` (`illusion.dm:86`) so that illusions can attack other neutral mobs. `full_setup()` then ran `SET_FACTION_AND_ALLIES_FROM(src, original)`, which put `neutral` back from the human original. The illusion and its (human) target then shared a faction, and the targeting strategy rejected the target. The target set by `set_target()` was cleared.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 96913: illusions now attack their targets and mirages last their lifetime", all in `illusion.dm`:

- The three `isnull()` checks in `mock_as()` became `!isnull()`.
- In `full_setup()`, `SET_FACTION_AND_ALLIES_FROM(src, original)` moved before `mock_as()`, with a comment explaining that `mock_as()` strips `FACTION_NEUTRAL`. `mock_as()` therefore removes it after the copy, and `faction_override` is still applied afterwards.

No alternatives were recorded.

## Verification

After rebuilding the test server:

- With the same setup, an illusion from `full_setup(A, B, nil, 300, 40, 12, 0)` attacked B: the chat showed "gores <B's name>!" eight times.
- An armed and detonated mirage grenade no longer vanished at once. Its illusion disappeared about 15 seconds later (grenade at 01:09:50, vanish line seen by 01:10:07).

Not checked:

- The Grey Tide spear itself, which calls `full_setup()` in the same way (`code/game/objects/items/weaponry/melee/spear.dm:372`).
- The cult, heretic and reactive armor callers, which share the `full_setup()` path.
- The hallucination wand, which shares the `mock_as()` path.
- Replication (`replicate`) behaviour and the illusion's health value were not examined in detail.

## Notes

- Changing the order in `full_setup()` also means `faction_override` callers (the cult items) still replace the factions afterwards, as before.
- The hallucination wand had the same instant-death bug as the mirage grenade and is fixed by the same change.
