## Summary

The admin "Manipulate Organs" verb (the View Variables "Modify organs" button) inserted organs with default movement flags. Replacing a brain therefore ran the old brain's identity transfer and moved the owner's mind into the dropped brain (#87698). The same verb also always inserted an arm implant in its default zone, the right arm, so a second arm implant replaced the first and the left arm could not be chosen (#91008). The fixes: the verb now inserts with `NO_ID_TRANSFER` (and `mob_insert` forwards the movement flags to the replaced organ's `Remove()`), and the verb asks which body zone to use for organs that fit several.

## The bug

### #87698

Using "Modify organs" on yourself and adding a Goliath genemod brain throws you out of your body: the player's mind stays with the old brain, which drops to the floor, while the new brain goes into the head. A comment says brains behave this way on purpose; another correctly points at the `special` handling of the organ procs. The mind-moving behaviour is intended for ordinary brain removal, but not for an admin swapping a brain in place.

### #91008

"Modify organs" (and `Insert()` from a Lua script) put an arm implant in the right arm only. Adding a toolset implant and then a surgical implant ejected the first, because both went to the right arm. Setting `zone` to `l_arm` on the implant before inserting does place it in the left arm, but still removes the right-arm one.

## Reproduction

### #87698

1. On master, in a started round, use the Lua editor to spawn a human and give your key control of it.
2. Open View Variables on that human and use the "Modify organs" link, choose "Add organ", and pick the `:brain/carp` entry.
3. Observed: the game said "You feel slightly disoriented. That's normal when you're just a brain." and the player's mob changed into a brain mob inside the old brain (named after the human), which had dropped out of the body.

This was reproduced with the real verb.

### #91008

The inputs describe the cause as plain from the code, and the issue text itself shows the behaviour; the pre-fix behaviour was not run separately on the test server. The steps from the issue are: use "Modify organs" on a humanoid, add `:cyberimp/arm/toolkit/toolset`, then `:cyberimp/arm/toolkit/surgery`. On master the verb never asks for a zone, so both go to the right arm.

## Cause

### #87698

In `code/modules/admin/verbs/manipulate_organs.dm:24`, "Add organ" calls `organ_to_grant.Insert(carbon_victim)` with no movement flags. `mob_insert` in `code/modules/surgery/organs/organ_movement.dm:69-71` removes any organ already in that slot:

```dm
var/obj/item/organ/replaced = receiver.get_organ_slot(slot)
if(replaced)
	replaced.Remove(receiver, special = TRUE)
```

The caller's `movement_flags` are not forwarded. For a brain, `on_mob_remove` (`code/modules/mob/living/brain/brain_item.dm:131-132`) calls `transfer_identity(organ_owner)` unless `NO_ID_TRANSFER` is in `movement_flags`, so the mind moves into the old brain. Other code that swaps brains removes the old brain first with `NO_ID_TRANSFER`, which is what the issue comments mean by the special handling.

### #91008

Arm implants share the abstract type `/obj/item/organ/cyberimp/arm` (`code/modules/surgery/organs/internal/cyberimp/augments_arms.dm:1-11`), whose defaults are `zone = BODY_ZONE_R_ARM` and `slot = ORGAN_SLOT_RIGHT_ARM_AUG`; `valid_zones` lists both arms. Something has to call `swap_zone()` (`code/modules/surgery/organs/organ_movement.dm:324`) to pick the left arm. Surgery does this in `pre_surgical_insertion`, and the autosurgeon does too, but neither the verb nor a raw `Insert()` does. The issue guesses that a refactor removing the left-arm subtypes made this visible; that was not checked.

## Fix

Two commits.

1. "Fix issue 87698: VV Manipulate Organs brain swap no longer ejects the owner's mind". The verb now calls `Insert(carbon_victim, movement_flags = NO_ID_TRANSFER)`. In `mob_insert`, the replaced organ's `Remove()` now receives `movement_flags = movement_flags`. Together they make a brain swapped by the verb leave the mind where it is.
2. "Fix issue 91008: Manipulate Organs asks which body zone for arm implants". If the new organ has more than one entry in `valid_zones`, the verb offers a "Select body zone" list (right arm / left arm), cancels (deleting the new organ) when the dialog is closed, and calls `swap_zone()` before inserting.

Alternatives and limits:

- `Insert()` itself was not changed for #91008. Surgery and autosurgeon call `swap_zone()` first, so the raw procedure keeps its caller-decides contract; a script that inserts an arm implant must set the zone itself (call `swap_zone()` or set `zone` and `slot`).
- Forwarding all `movement_flags` also forwards `DELETE_IF_REPLACED` to the replaced organ's `Remove()`. Nothing in that path reads it, so it is harmless, but it is a wider change than passing only `NO_ID_TRANSFER`.

## Verification

After the fix, on the test server with the same steps as the reproduction:

- #87698: adding `:brain/carp` through "Modify organs" no longer changed the player's mob. The old brain appeared on the floor, the player stayed in the body with the mind intact, and the human's brain was a `/obj/item/organ/brain/carp`.
- #91008: adding `:cyberimp/arm/toolkit/toolset` showed the new "Select body zone" list; choosing left arm placed it there. Adding `:cyberimp/arm/toolkit/surgery` and choosing right arm placed it in the right arm without ejecting the first. Using the Lua editor, the left arm device was the toolset (zone `l_arm`) and the right arm device was the surgical implant (zone `r_arm`). Both action buttons were present and the toolkit overlays for the left and right arm appeared.

Not checked: the "Add implant" and "Remove" branches of the verb; the repo's unit tests; builds with the patched BYOND features turned off; swapping other organs that run code in `Remove()`; skillchip or trauma handling on the swapped brain beyond the mind staying put.

## Notes

- The replaced brain's brain mob and mind are not transferred to the new brain; the player simply keeps their mind in the body. This appears to be the intent.
- The two commits touch the same verb file; the second applies on top of the first.
- #91008 is closely related but is a separate defect (zone choice), found in the same verb. It was not run before the fix.
