# #73887: Crusher killing blow is not counted in its damage total before death handlers run

| | |
|---|---|
| Issues | [#73887](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/73887): verified, fixed |
| Status | Fixed and checked in game (trophy drop roll itself not checked) |
| Fix branch | https://github.com/sohpea-cloub/tgstation-slop/tree/fix/73887 |
| Tested on | tgstation master `87b93338`, 2026-10-23 |

## Summary

The proto-kinetic crusher records the damage it deals in a `crusher_damage` status effect on the target, and the trophy-drop roll and the kill achievement read that total when the target dies. The effect counted melee damage from a signal sent after the damage was applied, which is after the target has already died and run its death handlers, so the killing blow was never in the total those handlers saw. The fix also counts the incoming damage from the before-damage signal and corrects it with the damage actually dealt afterwards.

## The bug

The issue reporter compared the crusher's damage total with the damage they actually dealt. A goliath (300 health) killed with only crusher melee hits showed a total about 20 short at the moment the drop odds were calculated: the last hit "didn't count until after my odds were calculated". The same shortfall made the drop chance (`total_damage / maxHealth * drop_mod`) and the megafauna "60% of health" check slightly lower than the real crusher share. The issue also lists damage lost in other paths (magmawing watcher backstabs, trophy damage on Bubblegum, a negative-sign problem); see Notes for which of those still apply at this commit.

## Reproduction

Only the killing-blow part was reproduced. It was done by calling the attack code from the Lua editor on the test server, not by a player swinging a crusher.

1. With admin tools (Lua editor), spawn a human holding a crusher with the wielded trait set and its force at 20, and spawn a goliath with its health lowered to 10.
2. Register a handler on the goliath's death signal that records the `crusher_damage` effect's `total_damage` at the moment of death.
3. Call `melee_attack_chain(human, goliath)` so the crusher kills it.

Observed on master: `total_damage` at death was 0; after the hit it was 20. The 20 damage was counted only after the death handlers had run, matching the reporter's second screenshot.

## Cause

`/datum/status_effect/crusher_damage` (`code/datums/status_effects/neutral.dm:3-34`) registers only `COMSIG_MOB_AFTER_APPLY_DAMAGE` and adds `damage_dealt` there:

```dm
/datum/status_effect/crusher_damage/on_apply()
	RegisterSignal(owner, COMSIG_MOB_AFTER_APPLY_DAMAGE, PROC_REF(damage_taken))
...
	if(istype(attacking_item, /obj/item/kinetic_crusher))
		total_damage += damage_dealt
```

That signal is sent at the end of `apply_damage` (`code/modules/mob/living/damage_procs.dm:98`), after the damage adjustment, such as `adjust_brute_loss`, has already killed the mob. Death runs `COMSIG_LIVING_DEATH` first, and its handlers `crusher_loot` (`code/datums/elements/crusher_loot.dm:46-56` is the roll, using `damage.total_damage`) and `kill_achievement` (`code/datums/elements/kill_achievement.dm:42-43`) read the total before the killing blow has been added.

## Fix

Commit "Fix issue 73887: count the killing crusher blow before death handlers run" changes `code/datums/status_effects/neutral.dm`:

- The effect also registers `COMSIG_MOB_APPLY_DAMAGE` (sent before damage lands). When the attacking item is a `/obj/item/kinetic_crusher`, it adds the incoming `damage_amount` to `total_damage` and remembers it in a new `pending_damage` variable.
- In the after-damage handler it adds `damage_dealt - pending_damage` and resets `pending_damage`, so the total ends up equal to the damage actually dealt (for example, if armour or health limits changed the amount).
- `on_remove` unregisters both signals.

This keeps the existing after-damage accounting as the authority and only moves the count earlier for handlers that run in between.

## Verification

Same Lua setup as above, after rebuilding the test server:

- Melee kill of the 10-health goliath: `total_damage` at death was 20, and 20 afterwards.
- Non-kill melee hit on a full-health goliath: total 20, health 300 to 280 (no double counting).
- Mark detonation kill, calling `detonate(crusher, human, false)` (50 damage): `total_damage` at death was 50.

Not checked: the actual trophy drop (the `prob` roll) and the megafauna 60% check with real kills; the magmawing watcher path; projectile-fired marks (no code change there); damage with armour reducing the hit (the correction term is untested beyond equal amounts).

## Notes

- The negative `damage_dealt` sign problem (damage_procs stores some damage types as negative values) is already fixed on master (upstream PR 89243).
- Mark detonation (`code/datums/status_effects/debuffs.dm:483-512`) already adds the trophy health deltas, trophy bonus and backstab bonus to the total before it calls `apply_damage`, so those counts were not lost at this commit. Its `apply_damage` applies bomb armour, while the total counts the pre-armour amount; this was left unchanged.
- Trophy damage that does not pass through the marked target's health (for example tail spike hitting other mobs) is not counted, which is correct for a per-target total.
- The reporter's remaining complaints (trophies not stacking with their upgraded versions, vortex talisman "bonus damage") are design points, not changed here.
