## Summary

The 2022 report said a light drinker stayed drunk for over 10 minutes after 30u of beer, a survival pen blinded the reporter for far too long, and earthsblood's druggy effect would not go away or be cured by an admin heal. On current master, drunkenness, temporary blindness and drugginess all decay on schedule and are removed by a full heal with the status flag. The report most likely came from two PRs that were test-merged at the time (#64449, #64448), and the status effect code has been rewritten since. No fix was made.

## The bug

As reported: status effects that should wear off stayed on far longer than expected, either because metabolism was wrong or because the effects were not being cleared. A commenter saw the same with earthsblood's druggy effect, and said an admin heal did not clear it.

## Reproduction

Attempted on master with admin tools (Lua editor, observer); the bug did not appear.

1. Drunkenness: gave a human `TRAIT_LIGHT_DRINKER` and added 30u of beer to their reagents. Sampled the drunk status effect's `drunk_value` every 10 s. It read 17.1 at the start of sampling (about 30 s after the drink), peaked at 26.0 around 110 s, and fell to 5.1 at 310 s, when the beer was fully metabolized (0.0u left). Beer metabolized at 1u per 10 s, so 30u took 300 s.
2. Temporary blindness: applied the status effect with a duration of 300 deciseconds. Samples every 5 s read 300, 250, 200, 150, 100, 50, then no effect, so it counted down in real time and was removed after 30 s.
3. Drugginess: gave 5u of earthsblood. Drugginess lasted about 29.6 s while the reagent metabolized (25 s), then counted down (242, 198, 150, 104, 58, 12, none) and was gone about 35 s after the last dose.
4. Admin heal: with drunkenness (30) and drugginess (300 s) active, called `fully_heal` with `HEAL_STATUS`. Both effects were removed (`has_status_effect` returned 0, drunk amount 0). An earlier attempt used `HEAL_ADMIN` instead, which correctly did not clear drunkenness.
5. Survival pen: could not be tested for blindness. See Notes.

## Cause

None found. Code read: the status effect base type (`code/datums/status_effects/_status_effect.dm`), including `process()`, which uses `seconds_per_tick` correctly for duration and tick interval, and `remove_effect_on_heal` (`:187`, registered on `COMSIG_LIVING_POST_FULLY_HEAL` at `:52`); `code/datums/status_effects/debuffs/drunk.dm`, `blindness.dm` and `drugginess.dm`; and `adjust_drunk_effect` in `status_procs.dm`.

The long drunk time is by design. Drunkenness decays by 4% of `drunk_value` plus 0.01 per 2-second tick (`drunk.dm`, `tick()`), and a light drinker has doubled booze power (`code/modules/reagents/chemistry/reagents/drinks/alcohol_reagents.dm:61`). From the measurements, 30u of beer left a light drinker drunk for roughly 9 minutes, close to the reported "over 10 minutes". That is a long but intended effect, not a stuck one.

## Notes

- The survival pen on master (`code/modules/reagents/reagent_containers/hypospray.dm:287`) contains epinephrine, aiuri, libital and leporazine, with no blindness source, so the reported pen blindness cannot occur on this code.
- Using the Lua editor: `apply_status_effect` needed `_text2path(...)`, and `is_blind` / `adjust_temp_blindness` were not callable from Lua. Mobs spawned before "Initializations complete" did not metabolize until after it, so tests should wait for it.
- The issue is from 2022 and mentions test-merged PRs; the report is probably stale and the issue likely closable.
