# #72571: Split personality stays behind when a slimeperson swaps or splits bodies

| | |
|---|---|
| Issues | [#72571](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/72571) (verified, with a fix), [#93992](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/93992) (fixed by the same change; not reproduced separately) |
| Status | Fixed on the branch below; one in-game check was scripted rather than done through the real buttons (see Verification) |
| Fix branch | [fix/72571](https://github.com/sohpea-cloub/tgstation-slop/tree/fix/72571) |
| Tested on | tgstation master `87b93338`, 2026-10-23 |

## Summary

A split personality brain trauma belongs to one body: its owner and the two backseat mobs live there. Slimeperson body swapping, splitting and the on-death swap move the mind to another body but never touch the trauma, so the trauma and its backseats stay behind on the abandoned body. The fix makes `can_swap()`, the swap action and the split action refuse whenever the body involved has a split personality, so the mind can no longer leave it. It also covers #93992, where a blackout-drunk personality ended up in a spare slime body.

## The bug

**#72571.** A slimeperson with a split personality could not swap properly between bodies. The original report (2023) describes being unable to switch bodies, and splitting leaving the player in the original body. A 2024 comment says behaviour had changed by then: splitting moved the player to the new body, but the split-personality backseat stayed on the original body and took control of it on the next `switch_personalities()`. After that no swapping occurred. If a player moved into a body occupied by someone else, the occupant was ghosted with no chance of return.

**#93992.** The reporter drank until the "You feel yourself about to black out!" message, then swapped to another slime body. The alcohol blackout is itself a split personality trauma (`/datum/brain_trauma/severe/split_personality/blackout`), so the same mismatch occurred. A ghost player became the blackout personality and ended up controlling the spare slime body. That player could see but could not use the slime body actions. The body still looked unoccupied to `can_swap()`, so the original player could swap into it and eject the ghost.

## Reproduction

Only one test client was available, so the multi-player parts were not reproduced. The stranger-side behaviour (a second player taking the abandoned body) is code-reasoned only. A split personality was also not obtained through the normal route, because an SDQL2 `gain_trauma` call started a ghost poll that ended at once with no candidates, and the trauma was deleted.

On master:

1. Using the Lua editor, spawn two humans next to the admin and set both to the slimeperson species.
2. Give the admin's body a split personality by hand in Lua: create a `split_personality` trauma, set its `owner` and `brain`, add it to the brain's `traumas` list, and call `make_backseats()`.
3. Check `can_swap()` on the other body, then run `swap_to_dupe`.

Observed: `can_swap()` returned true and `swap_to_dupe` succeeded. Afterwards the trauma's `owner` was still the old body, and the new body had no trauma. That is the state the 2024 comment describes: the trauma and backseats are left on the abandoned body.

## Cause

All three ways of moving a slimeperson's mind in `code/modules/mob/living/carbon/human/species_types/jellypeople.dm` call `transfer_to()` without looking at brain traumas:

- `split_body/make_dupe()` (`jellypeople.dm:367`, `H.mind.transfer_to(spare)`)
- `swap_body/swap_to_dupe()` (`jellypeople.dm:512`, `M.transfer_to(dupe)`)
- the on-death swap, `on_death_move_body()` (`jellypeople.dm:270`), which uses the same swap path after `can_swap()`

A split personality (`code/datums/brain_damage/split_personality.dm`) keeps the stranger and owner backseat mobs inside the body that owns the trauma. `switch_personalities()` (line 85) moves control through `owner`, so it only ever works on that old body. Once the mind has left, the trauma still points at the old body and its backseats.

For #93992, `drunk.dm:232` gives the blackout trauma at the start of the blackout (`attempt_to_blackout`). If the player swaps before that, the trauma is gained on a body whose mind has already moved. The blackout personality then arrives on the spare body. `can_swap()` (line 475) treats that body as free because the personality's mind is not "active" (`dupe.mind && dupe.mind.active` is false), so the owner can swap in and kick the occupant out.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 72571: slimepeople with a split personality can no longer swap or split bodies", changing only `jellypeople.dm`:

- `split_body/IsAvailable()` returns false (with a "Your mind is too fractured to split your body!" message when feedback is wanted) if the owner has a `split_personality` trauma, and `make_dupe()` refuses with the same message. The `make_dupe()` check covers calls that bypass `IsAvailable()`.
- `swap_body/Activate()` prints "Your mind is too fractured to move to another body!" and does not open the UI if the owner has the trauma.
- `swap_body/can_swap()` returns false if the owner has the trauma. It also returns false if the target body has one, so a body with a split personality can never be swapped into.

Because `on_death_move_body()` goes through `can_swap()`, the on-death swap is blocked too.

The alternative, moving the trauma and its backseats with the mind, would require changes inside `split_personality.dm`, since the trauma is tied to `owner` and the brain. That file was left untouched on purpose, to avoid conflicts with other work on it and the extra risk.

## Verification

On the fixed build, on the test server, through the Lua editor. The checks called the action procs from Lua scripts (the actions' `IsAvailable`, `Activate` and `make_dupe`, and `can_swap`) rather than clicking the real action buttons.

- No trauma: a slime split worked (a spare body appeared and the mind moved), `can_swap(other)` returned 1, and `swap_to_dupe` worked with the normal messages.
- Trauma on the owner: `IsAvailable(TRUE)` on the split action and `make_dupe()` printed "Your mind is too fractured to split your body!" and spawned no body. The swap action's `Activate` printed "Your mind is too fractured to move to another body!" and opened no UI. `can_swap()` returned 0.
- Trauma on the target body: `can_swap()` returned 0. After the trauma was removed it returned 1 again.
- The runtime log showed only the known Lua artifacts (proc calls with no ckey after a mob change).

Not checked: the real action buttons; a real alcohol blackout; the on-death swap; any second-player scenario, including #93992 end to end. #93992 was marked fixed from code reasoning plus the guard checks above.

## Notes

- Behaviour change: a slimeperson with a split personality (including a temporary blackout one) cannot split or swap at all. Maintainers may prefer to let the trauma follow the mind, which would be more invasive.
- #93992's reporter could swap only before the blackout trauma existed. After this change a swap during the blackout window is refused. A swap just before it is still possible, but the trauma is then gained on a body whose mind has already left. The inputs describe this only by reading the code, not by test.
- Two slime bodies can still be unoccupied-looking while holding a ghost-controlled personality if a trauma is gained some other way. The change handles that by refusing to swap into any body that has the trauma.
