## Summary

`/mob/living/carbon/Life()` ran `handle_process()` on every wound with no check on the mob's stat, so wounds on corpses clotted, healed, got infected and sent feeling messages as if the victim were alive. This is why cuts on corpses disappear (#81805) and why dead players get burn messages (#61849). One commit wraps the wound loop in `if(stat != DEAD)`. After it, a dead mob's wounds persisted while living controls' wounds cleared.

## The bug

#81805: a corpse with "Open Laceration" or lesser cuts loses those cuts over time, even though an earlier change (#74412, cited in the issue) was meant to stop that. #61849: a dead player with a severe burn wound still gets burn and infection feedback ("You feel awful", speech becoming harder), which makes no sense for a corpse.

## Reproduction

On master, from an observer with the Lua editor:

1. Spawned four humans at one spot and gave each a chest wound with `cause_wound_of_type_and_severity`: A and B a moderate slash ("Rough Abrasion"), C and D a severe burn ("Third Degree Burns").
2. Killed A and C with `death()`. B and D stayed alive as controls.
3. Waited about 35 to 50 seconds and read each mob's `all_wounds`.

Observed about 35 seconds after death: all four had no wounds left. At death A had blood flow 1.35, and C had flesh damage 8.5 and infection 0.7. So the dead mobs lost their wounds as fast as the living ones.

## Cause

`/mob/living/carbon/Life()` (`code/modules/mob/living/carbon/life.dm:9-12`) did:

```
for(var/datum/wound/wound as anything in all_wounds)
	if(!wound.processes) // meh
		continue
	wound.handle_process(seconds_per_tick)
```

without a stat check. The mobs subsystem calls `Life()` on dead mobs too (`code/controllers/subsystem/mobs.dm:37`), so each wound's `handle_process` ran on corpses: slash and pierce wounds clot (`code/datums/wounds/slash.dm:153`), burn wounds heal, get infected and message the victim (`code/datums/wounds/burns.dm:48`), and bone wounds regenerate. Neither the slash nor the burn `handle_process` checks whether the victim is dead (they only skip stasis). The #61849 messages come from the same loop.

## Fix

Commit "Fix issue 81805: wounds no longer process (clot, heal, infect, message) on dead mobs" wraps the wound loop in `if(stat != DEAD)`, with a comment. Nothing else changed. It was put in the carbon `Life()` loop rather than in each wound type so that every wound type and future ones are covered at once. The same commit covers #61849; no separate change was needed.

## Verification

Same setup on the rebuilt test server, about 60 seconds after death:

- Dead A still had "Rough Abrasion" with blood flow 1.75.
- Dead C still had "Third Degree Burns" with flesh damage 12.5.
- Living B and D had lost their wounds (clotted or cleared), as before.
- Chat showed the burn "body tenses up/slackens" messages only for living D and none for dead C.

Not checked: infection growth on a corpse over a longer time, bone wounds and pierce wounds on corpses, and what happens to wounds when a corpse is revived (they are no longer healed while dead, so a revived mob will have them).

## Notes

- Side effect: wounds on corpses no longer progress at all. That includes infection getting worse, which used to continue after death. It also means corpses in cold (pierce) or with bone wounds no longer heal. This seems intended but a maintainer should weigh it.
- The stasis path still runs for dead mobs on stasis: `code/modules/mob/living/carbon/human/life.dm:32` calls each wound's `on_stasis`, and the burn version can clear a burn and message a dead victim. It was left alone, as healing on stasis beds appears deliberate.
- Test runtimes were only the probes themselves plus a known scar `stack_trace` (no valid description in `_scars.dm`).
