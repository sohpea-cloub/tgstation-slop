## Summary

Three old reports describe the same symptom: after a z-level change (falling, an elevator, stairs, a slip onto a lower level) the player can no longer click or pick up anything in the world, and taking off optical meson scanners restores it. The symptom lines up with the "multi-z quality" preference (`multiz_performance`) being set to 0 or higher while wearing mesons. On the test server, every plane master's server-side state was identical before and after toggling mesons, so no stale server-side state explaining the bug was found. The cause is probably in how the real BYOND client hit-tests clicks over the multi-z plane masters, which could not be checked without a real BYOND client, so no fix was written.

## The bug

### #77475 (canonical)

On Icebox, the reporter lost the ability to interact with anything outside their inventory after slipping on bananium ore or being frozen by an ice drake. Dropped items could not be picked up again. They found that taking off the "optical meson scanner" and putting it back on restored interaction. In a comment they said their multi-z quality setting was low, and that it only happened "on rare occasions" on Icebox and some other multi-z maps, never on Metastation (which has no z-levels to look through).

### #75950 (same cause, per its comments)

On Tramstation, taking the elevator to the lower deck left the reporter unable to touch anything on the lower deck, while still able to examine things. A later comment says it also happened when simply falling under the tram, and a third reporter says the issue "stems from overlays such as mesons" and that removing the mesons fixes it. The first comment asked whether the move up/down verbs still work in that state; this was never answered.

### #76935 (related)

Holding a pickaxe and changing z-levels left the reporter unable to pick things up or interact with anything but their own gear. A commenter saw the same with mechs on lower levels. A pickaxe, like mesons in the other reports, is an item that changes what the player sees (mining tools and meson glasses are both worn or held by miners), but the report does not say mesons were worn, so whether it is the same mechanism is uncertain.

## Reproduction

Not reproduced. What was tried, on the test server running master:

1. Started the round on the MultiZ Debug test map (a small map with several stacked z-levels) and joined as Shaft Miner. Icebox itself was prepared as the next map but was never loaded for testing.
2. In the Lua editor, set the player's `multiz_performance` preference to 0 (default is -1, `MULTIZ_PERFORMANCE_DISABLE`), spawned an optical meson scanner and equipped it. The player's `sight` became `SEE_TURFS` (16).
3. Moved the player between z-levels 2 and 3 with `forceMove`, with mesons on and with mesons off, and with the preference at 0 and at -1.
4. Clicked the tile the player stood on, with a crowbar spawned on it, in each of these configurations. In every configuration, including the preference at -1 and no mesons, the click was reported as landing on a `/obj/effect/abstract/z_holder` (`code/datums/elements/turf_transparency.dm:43`, `mouse_opacity = MOUSE_OPACITY_TRANSPARENT`), and nothing happened. Because it behaved the same in the configurations that are not supposed to be broken, this shows nothing about the bug: the click tool used here is not a real BYOND client and evidently picks a transparent object in this situation. These clicks can neither confirm nor rule out the bug.
5. Using Lua, took a snapshot of every plane master in the player's main plane master group (`force_hidden`, `is_outside_bounds`, `render_target`, `alpha`, number of relays), toggled the mesons off and on, and took another snapshot. The two snapshots were identical. This was repeated for both z-levels and in both directions of the toggle (147 plane masters in each snapshot; the diff was empty).

The slip on bananium, ice drake freezing and the Tramstation elevator were not tried; neither were Icebox's weather, the pickaxe case or the mech case. #75950 and #76935 were not tested separately.

## Cause

The cause was not found. What was established from the code:

- The preference defaults to -1 (`code/modules/client/preferences/multiz_performance.dm`). With any value of 0 or more, `build_planes_offset()` (`code/_onclick/hud/rendering/plane_master_group.dm:162`) calls `plane.outside_bounds()` on every non-offset-blocking plane master that belongs to a level above the player (`visual_offset < 0`) or further below than the preference (`:225-226`). For a plane master without `PLANE_CRITICAL_DISPLAY`, `outside_bounds()` (`code/_onclick/hud/rendering/plane_masters/_plane_master.dm:203`) calls `hide_from()`, which removes the plane master and its relays from `client.screen`. Whatever is drawn on such a plane then renders with no plane master applied to it, so none of the plane master's own mouse settings apply to it. This fits the reports' "multi-z quality low" trigger, and is only a hypothesis for why clicks would fail.
- Mesons give `TRAIT_MESON_VISION`, which sets `SEE_TURFS` in `code/modules/mob/living/living.dm:2070`. `update_sightflags()` (`code/_onclick/hud/hud.dm:216`) only calls `refresh_planes_offset()` when `should_sight_scale()` changes, and `SEE_TURFS` without `SEE_OBJS` turns multi-z scaling off (`:231`). So putting on or removing mesons rebuilds the plane offsets and, through `light_mask`'s `handle_sight()` (`code/_onclick/hud/rendering/render_plate.dm:387`), its relays. That would explain why toggling mesons "fixes" things, but the snapshot comparison above showed no server-side difference, so the rebuild seems to change nothing visible in the server's state, at least on the MultiZ Debug map.
- A possible defect, which was not shown to cause anything: `outside_bounds()` returns immediately when `force_hidden` is set (`_plane_master.dm:204`), so `is_outside_bounds` is never recorded for a force-hidden plane. If the plane is later unhidden (`unhide_plane()`, called from `code/datums/components/hide_weather_planes.dm:46` and `:71` when weather starts, which matters on Icebox, and by the seethrough tree component), `show_to()` checks `check_outside_bounds()`, finds it false, and displays the plane and its relays even though it should be outside the preference's range. Whether this produces unclickable tiles was not tested.

## Fix

None. No code was changed or committed.

Nothing to verify. Not checked: the original scenarios on Icebox and Tramstation, the weather/unhide path described above, the pickaxe and mech reports, and any behavior of a real BYOND client.

## Notes

- Next step for whoever picks this up: reproduce on a real BYOND client with multi-z quality set to 0 or higher and mesons worn, on a multi-z map, then check which object the client reports for the click (the `Click` call's `object`) and whether the plane masters in `client.screen` differ before and after taking off the mesons. The client-side `screen` list could not be inspected from Lua (it is not iterable there), so only the server-side plane master datums were compared.
- No already-merged pull request fixing this was found by searching merged PRs for multi-z, mesons, z holder and plane master terms; the closest was the PR that added the preference (#71218), which is the origin of the code path above, not a fix.
- #75950's reporter also describes the bug happening without gravity trouble and without the elevator, so the elevator and no-gravity detail in its title is probably incidental; any z-change followed by a plane offset rebuild fits.
- #76935's pickaxe and mech reports are the weakest link in this group; they may be a different bug (for example the held item or mech changing the player's view) and should be re-triaged once the meson case is understood.
