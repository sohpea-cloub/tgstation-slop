## Summary

When a split personality takes over the body of an antagonist, the body keeps the owner's antagonist buttons (the info button and any abilities), but the controlling personality's mind has no antagonist datum, so the buttons are useless to it. The cause is that `switch_personalities()` swaps minds by plain assignment, so the antagonist's body-bound parts are never moved. The fix removes the owner's antagonist innate effects, info button and moodlet from the body while the other personality is in control, and puts them back when the owner returns. It does not make the other personality an antagonist; that is a design question for maintainers.

## The bug

The issue says that if a character with a split personality is a cultist or another antagonist with special powers, the other personality is treated as a normal crewmember. It still gets the buttons, but cannot use the powers. The thread discussed what the intended behavior should be. One comment (AnturK, 2018) said that the other personality not being an antagonist seemed fine and that "the real issue here is the alter ego retaining the buttons after swap". Other comments discussed whether the personality should count as a second cultist, or be convertible on its own. A later comment merged a closed changeling report (#33623) into this issue.

## Reproduction

The trauma was built by hand from the Lua editor and a single client, because the ghost poll that normally supplies the second personality could not be satisfied with one client. The swaps were done by calling `switch_personalities()` directly rather than waiting for the random switch.

1. On the test server, open the Lua editor as an admin.
2. Spawn a human and give it a mind (`mind_initialize()` or possession). Create a `/datum/brain_trauma/severe/split_personality` object with `SS13.new`, set its `owner` to the human and call `make_backseats()`. This creates the two backseat mobs.
3. Possess the stranger backseat with the admin's key.
4. Call `switch_personalities()` on the trauma. The stranger takes over the body.
5. Add the traitor antagonist datum to the owner's mind (`owner_backseat.mind:add_antag_datum(/datum/antagonist/traitor)`).
6. List `body.actions` and check `body.mind:has_antag_datum(traitor)`.

Observed on unfixed master:

- `body.actions` was `Activate,Open Traitor Information`, and "You are the Traitor." was shown in chat, to the player controlling the body (the other personality).
- `body.mind:has_antag_datum(traitor)` was nil, while the owner's mind (held by the owner backseat) had the Traitor datum.

So the controlling personality had the antagonist buttons without the antagonist status.

An earlier attempt with the cultist datum did not give a usable result: adding the cult datum threw a runtime in `code/modules/antagonists/cult/datums/cult_team.dm:83` (`Cannot read null.prefs`), which was attributed to the test setup having only one client. The body had no actions listed and the owner's mind showed a Cultist datum afterwards. Cult was not tested further.

In this reproduction the antagonist datum was added after the swap. The case where the owner is already an antagonist when the swap happens was only observed on the fixed build (see Verification).

## Cause

`switch_personalities()` in `code/datums/brain_damage/split_personality.dm` moves control by assigning minds directly: `new_backseat.mind = owner.mind` (line 117) and `owner.mind = current_backseat.mind` (line 136). It never calls `mind.transfer_to()`.

`transfer_to()` is what moves an antagonist's body-bound parts: it calls `/datum/antagonist/proc/on_body_transfer()` (`code/modules/antagonists/_common/antag_datum.dm:188`), which removes the innate effects, moves the info button, and moves the moodlet from the old body to the new one. With plain assignment none of that happens. The innate effects (abilities), the info button and the moodlet were granted to the body when the antagonist datum was added and stay on it, whoever controls it.

The other personality's mind has no antagonist datums, so those buttons do nothing for it. Cult spells, for example, check for cult membership.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 41601: split personality keeps the owner's antag buttons", in `code/datums/brain_damage/split_personality.dm`.

- A new proc, `set_owner_antag_effects(owner_mind, apply)`, loops over the owner mind's antagonist datums. When removing, it calls `remove_innate_effects(owner)`, removes the info button from the body and calls `clear_antag_moodies(owner)`. When applying, it does the reverse with `apply_innate_effects`, `Grant` and `give_antag_moodies`.
- In `switch_personalities()`, after the check that the incoming personality has a client, the proc computes `owner_leaving = (current_controller == OWNER && !reset_to_owner)`. If the owner is leaving, the proc calls `set_owner_antag_effects(owner.mind, FALSE)` before the minds are swapped. If the owner is coming back, it calls `set_owner_antag_effects(owner.mind, TRUE)` after the swap, when `owner.mind` is the owner's mind again.

The antagonist status stays with the owner's mind, and only the body-bound parts are taken off while the other personality is in control. The commit message gives the reasoning: the other personality is not an antagonist, so it should not get buttons it cannot use. The inputs do not list other approaches that were considered. Replacing the raw assignments with `mind.transfer_to()` would be a larger change and was not tried.

## Verification

Checked on the test server after rebuilding with the fix, using the manually built trauma, one client and the traitor antagonist:

- With the traitor datum on the owner's mind before the swap, the body's actions were `Activate` only after `switch_personalities()`. The "Open Traitor Information" button was gone, and the body's mind had no traitor datum. The remaining `Activate` action belongs to the uplink implant. The test notes say the uplink component checks that the user is its owner (`code/datums/components/uplink.dm`, around line 130), so it does not work for the other personality. This was read from code and not tested in game.
- After switching back to the owner, the status output showed "Open Traitor Information" again for the owner (taken from the test notes, not from output seen directly).

Not checked:

- Cult, changeling or any other antagonist with innate effects (only traitor was tested).
- The real ghost poll path (`get_ghost()` and `schism()`), and the random switch from `on_life()`.
- Curing or losing the trauma while the other personality is in control (`reset_to_owner`), and the owner dying.
- Antagonist datums added to the owner's mind while the other personality is in control. As the reproduction above shows, on unfixed master such a datum puts its buttons on the body. The fix only handles datums that exist at the time of the swap, so that case would still give the buttons to the other personality.
- Antagonist datums on the other personality's own mind.

## Notes

- **Design decision.** The issue title asks that the split personality inherit antagonist status. The fix does not do that. It follows the reading in the thread (AnturK) that the problem is the leftover buttons. Maintainers may prefer to give the other personality its own antagonist datum, or the same antagonist role as the owner. If so, this change is the wrong direction and should be dropped.
- After a swap, the mind that took control of the body still has its `current` pointing at its backseat mob, because minds are assigned directly. Antagonist datums added to that mind would apply their effects to the backseat mob. The fix does not change this.
- The thread also asked what happens to convertibility and defenses (a split personality of a chaplain or captain being converted or flashed). This was not examined.
