## Summary

The reporter called the emergency shuttle on green alert, expected the 20 minute call time, and got 10 minutes. On master, every path tested gave 20 minutes at green alert. The 10 minutes in the report matches what the code produces at blue alert, or at green alert with the engine coefficient at 0.5, so no defect was found in how the alert level is applied. The round's state at the time of the call is not available, so which of those it was could not be determined.

## The bug

As reported: on green alert, calling the shuttle produced a shuttle announcement of "It will arrive in 10 minutes." instead of the 20 minutes the reporter expected from green. The attached screenshot shows the announcement with a typed reason and no "Red Alert" text.

## Reproduction

Not reproduced. Using the Lua editor and the Shuttle Manipulator on the test server, after the round had started:

1. Set the security level to green and call the shuttle with `SSshuttle.call_evac_shuttle('test')`. `alert_coeff` was 2 and the time left was 20 minutes (the 6000 decisecond base call time times 2).
2. With the shuttle called, change the level with `SSsecurity_level.set_level` through blue, red, green, delta and green again. The remaining time followed 20, 10, 5, 20, 2.5 and 20 minutes, so mid-call alert changes scaled the timer as intended.
3. Recall, then call again at blue and at green: 10 and 20 minutes respectively. Each call re-reads the current level's modifier.
4. Using the Shuttle Manipulator, replace the emergency shuttle (Meta to Delta, engines 12/12, engine coefficient 1). A green call gave 20 minutes, blue 10, green 20.
5. Calling through `requestEvac(me, reason)` at green was refused, but only by the roundstart refuel delay (`canEvac`), not by anything to do with the timer.

## Cause

No cause for the report was found. In `code/modules/shuttle/mobile_port/variants/emergency/emergency.dm:38-42`, `request()` takes the coefficient from the current security level and computes:

```dm
set_coefficient = SSsecurity_level.current_security_level.shuttle_call_time_mod
alert_coeff = set_coefficient
var/call_time = SSshuttle.emergency_call_time * alert_coeff * engine_coeff
```

Green's modifier is `ALERT_COEFF_GREEN` (2), set in `security_level_datums.dm:62`. The announcement text is built from `timeLeft()` right after `setTimer()`, so a "10 minutes" announcement means the call time at that moment was exactly 10 minutes, which equals `10 min * alert_coeff * engine_coeff` only if either:

- the security level was blue (coefficient 1) when the call was made, or
- the engine coefficient was 0.5, for example with extra engines attached (`get_engine_coeff` clamps to `ENGINE_COEFF_MIN`).

Neither could be checked without the original round's state (the round ID is in the issue). The screenshot does not show the alert level.

None.

## Verification

Nothing to verify; no change was made. What was not checked: the original round's alert level history and shuttle engine count, and the interaction of the timer with other call paths (for example a call made by an event or by the AI rather than a console).

## Notes

- Side finding, real and unrelated to the report's 10 minutes on a fresh call: `alert_coeff_change` and `modTimer` in `code/modules/shuttle/mobile_port/mobile_port.dm:520-540` do `last_timer_length *= multiple` and then call `setTimer(time_remaining)`. `setTimer` assigns `last_timer_length = wait`, overwriting the scaled value with the remaining time. After any alert-level change during a call, `invertTimer` (used by a recall) computes `time_passed = last_timer_length - remaining`, which is 0, so a recall right after an alert change gets a 0 return timer. It was observed after green, blue, green during a call: `cancel()` left both the timer and `last_timer_length` at 0. A fix would keep `last_timer_length` scaled, for example by assigning `timer` directly instead of calling `setTimer`. This was not committed.
- If someone has the original round's logs, the alert level at the moment of the call and the shuttle's engine count would settle which explanation fits.
