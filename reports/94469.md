The two issues are unrelated and were worked in one unit. Each has its own commit.

## Summary

**#94469.** The space furnace (`/obj/item/flashlight/lamp/space_bubble`) rewrites its own `slowdown` and `drag_slowdown` whenever it is lit or closed, without looking at `TRAIT_SPEED_POTIONED`. A red slime speed potion therefore lasts only until the furnace is next switched off and on, or burns out and is relit. The fix guards both writes with `!HAS_TRAIT(src, TRAIT_SPEED_POTIONED)`.

**#94597.** A stabilized grey extract does call `befriend()` on adjacent slimes, but a slime already latched on kept feeding. `befriend()` left the friend as the slime's current target, and the feeding behavior never rechecked friendship. The fix adds a slime `befriend()` override that lets go and forgets the new friend, and makes the feeding behavior bail out on allies.

## The bug

### #94469

A space furnace is crafted from a pyroclastic anomaly core. When lit it slows its holder (`SLOWDOWN_ON`, 1). The reporter applied a red slime speed potion to a lit furnace and moved faster. After putting the furnace in a bag or on the ground and taking it back, or after it burned out and was relit, the slowdown was back. The potion could not be applied a second time because the item still carried the potion trait.

### #94597

The stabilized grey extract says it makes slimes friendly to the holder. The reporter was still attacked by slimes next to them. A comment on the issue says `befriend()` succeeds but does not cancel what the slime's AI is already doing. The reproduction below agrees. A fix was posted upstream as PR #94737. This unit did not check whether it merged, and this write-up does not depend on it.

## Reproduction

### #94469

Done with the Lua editor on the test server, not by crafting a furnace.

1. Spawn a human and a pre-activated furnace (`/obj/item/flashlight/lamp/space_bubble/preactivated`) and a speed potion (`/obj/item/slimepotion/speed`). Put the furnace in the human's hands and call `toggle_light(human)`. The furnace's `slowdown` was 1.
2. Apply the potion (`interact_with_atom(furnace, human)`). `slowdown` became 0.
3. Drop the furnace and pick it up again. `slowdown` stayed 0, so picking it up is not what resets it, contrary to the report.
4. Call `toggle_light` to turn it off, then again to turn it on. `slowdown` was back at 1, and the trait was still present, so the potion could not be reapplied.

Reads of variables in the same Lua run as a proc call returned stale values, so the values above were read in a separate run.

Switching off and on stands in for the burnout. The furnace turns itself off after its bubble duration and is then relit, and that goes through the same procs. The 15 minute burnout itself was not waited out.

### #94597

Also set up with the Lua editor.

1. Spawn a grey slime at the "hungry" level (nutrition 40, `BB_slime_hunger_level` 2) next to a human whose body is possessed by the test client. A client-controlled target at full health makes the feeding attempt fail, so the slime goes through melee and then latches on.
2. Give the human a stabilized grey extract (`/obj/item/slimecross/stabilized/grey`) in hand.
3. Chat showed "extract pulses gently as it communicates with the slime", then "slime lets go of <name>", and immediately afterwards "latches onto <name>" again. The slime kept draining the human ("slime eats away at you").

## Cause

### #94469

In `code/game/objects/items/devices/flashlight.dm`, `toggle_light()` sets the values on lighting (around line 1226):

```dm
slowdown = SLOWDOWN_ON
drag_slowdown = SLOWDOWN_ON
```

and `close_bubble()` restores them (around line 1244):

```dm
slowdown = initial(slowdown)
drag_slowdown = initial(drag_slowdown)
```

Neither checks `TRAIT_SPEED_POTIONED`. The potion (`/obj/item/slimepotion/speed/interact_with_atom`, `code/modules/research/xenobiology/xenobiology.dm`, about lines 960 to 990) sets `slowdown` and `drag_slowdown` to 0 and adds the trait. It refuses items whose `slowdown` is 0 or less, and the furnace's base slowdown is 0, so the potion can only be applied while the furnace is lit. Turning the furnace off resets the values to the initial 0 and turning it on writes 1 again, so the potion is lost either way.

### #94597

`/datum/status_effect/stabilized/grey/tick()` (`code/modules/research/xenobiology/crossbreeding/_status_effects.dm:484`) calls `befriend(owner)` on each slime in range 1 that does not already have the owner as an ally. `/mob/living/proc/befriend` (`code/modules/mob/living/living.dm:2926`) only adds the ally and inserts the friend into `BB_FRIENDS_LIST`. It does not touch the slime's current target or its feeding state.

The slime behavior tree (`code/modules/mob/living/basic/slime/ai/slime.bt.json`) keeps working on `BB_CURRENT_TARGET`/`BB_SLIME_EAT_TARGET`. `feed_on_slime_target.perform()` (`code/modules/mob/living/basic/slime/ai/slime_bt.dm:52`) never checks whether the target has become an ally. If the slime is already buckled to the target it just returns `AI_BEHAVIOR_DELAY` and keeps feeding. After the slime lets go, the same target is still set, so it latches on again. Melee attacks do recheck the target; feeding does not.

`can_feed_on()` (`code/modules/mob/living/basic/slime/feeding.dm:5`) has a `check_friendship` argument, but the check at line 24 is `has_faction(REF(meal))`. Allies are stored with `has_ally`, so this looks like the wrong test. It is not on the path used here and was not changed.

## Fix

The branch has two commits.

- **Fix issue 94469: space furnace keeps slime speed potion when relit.** In both `toggle_light()` and `close_bubble()`, the writes to `slowdown` and `drag_slowdown` are wrapped in `if(!HAS_TRAIT(src, TRAIT_SPEED_POTIONED))`. The `update_equipment_speed_mods()` calls are unchanged. This keeps the potion working without changing the potion code or blacklisting the furnace. The issue offered both options.
- **Fix issue 94597: befriended slimes stop feeding on their new friend.** Two changes:
  - A `/mob/living/basic/slime/befriend()` override at the end of `feeding.dm`. After the parent call succeeds it calls `stop_feeding()` if the slime is buckled to the new friend, clears `BB_CURRENT_TARGET`, `BB_SLIME_EAT_TARGET` and `BB_CURRENT_PET_TARGET` when they hold the friend, and removes the friend from `BB_BASIC_MOB_RETALIATE_LIST`.
  - `feed_on_slime_target.perform()` now returns early with `stop_feeding()` and a cleared target key when `slime_pawn.has_ally(REF(target))`. This covers a friend that is set as a target by some other route after the befriend.

## Verification

### #94469

On the fixed build, a lit furnace was held by a human, the potion was applied, and `toggle_light` was called to turn it off and on again. `slowdown` and `drag_slowdown` stayed 0 and `light_on` was true after the second call.

Not checked: the real burnout timer, crafting the furnace from an anomaly core, using a bag of holding, and other holders such as pulling or dropping on the floor. Items that never get the potion trait behave as before by construction, but were not re-tested.

### #94597

On the fixed build the same setup showed "extract pulses gently ..." and "slime lets go of <name>". Over about 50 seconds afterwards the slime did not latch onto the friend. It latched onto a different, non-friend human instead. Remaining "slime eats away at you" lines came from the Covered in Slime status from earlier feeding and expired. The round's runtime log showed no runtimes.

Not checked: slimes of other colors or in real xenobiology conditions, hunger-driven "unfriending" mentioned in the issue comments, or the interaction of the retaliate list with other attackers.

## Notes

- A possible alternative for #94469 would be a restriction in the potion code. That was not done, since the fix needs only the two writes in the furnace.
- For #94597 the client-possessed target was needed in the reproduction to get the slime to go through its feeding path. It was not tested with an NPC target.
- The suspicious `has_faction(REF(meal))` check in `can_feed_on()` (used by `pet_command.dm:9`) was left alone.
- The issue comments for #94597 say a fix PR (#94737) was posted. Whether it merged was not checked.
