# #80274: Cleanbots ignore messes on tiles they cannot enter

## Summary

A cleanbot never cleans a mess lying on a tile it cannot walk onto, such as a blood decal under a table. The cleanbot's behavior tree acquires cleanup targets with a reachability check that requires a path onto the mess's own tile. A blocked tile fails that check and the mess is put on the bot's ignore list, although cleaning only needs the bot to be adjacent. The fix sets `minimum_distance` to 1 on that acquire leaf, so a path ending next to the mess is enough.

## The bug

The issue says cleanbots cannot reach under things nearby. A comment from October 2024 says the problem remained after the earlier bot fixes in #80208: a player controlling a cleanbot can clean such messes, the AI does not. In practice a bot walks past decals under tables, leaving them there.

## Reproduction

1. On the test server, join as an observer and open the Lua editor.
2. Spawn a cleanbot. Three tiles east of it, spawn a `/obj/structure/table` with a blood decal on its tile. As a control, spawn a second blood decal on an ordinary floor tile on the other side of the bot (two tiles west).
3. Start the round and wait. The bot cleaned the control decal (a search for "blood" afterwards listed only the decal under the table). The decal under the table was still there 40 or more seconds later.

## Cause

- `code/modules/mob/living/basic/bots/cleanbot/cleanbot.bt.json`: the `acquire_target` leaf for the current target uses the `cleanbot_cleanables` target source with `must_be_reachable` true and `reach_distance` 15, and leaves `minimum_distance` at its default of 0.
- `can_reach_target` (`code/datums/ai/_ai_controller.dm:702`) calls `get_path_to`, which with a minimum target distance of 0 needs a path onto the target's own tile. The JPS pathfinder (`code/__HELPERS/paths/jps.dm:198-201`) rejects an end tile the requester cannot step onto, and cleanbots do not have `PASSTABLE`. The mess is then treated as unreachable and added to the ignore list.
- Nothing downstream needs that strict condition: `execute_clean` (`cleanbot_ai.dm:53`) only requires `get_dist` of at most 1, and the `move_to_target` node that follows already uses `required_dist` 1.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 80274: cleanbots ignore messes under tables and other blocked tiles". It adds `"minimum_distance": 1` to that acquire leaf in `cleanbot.bt.json`. `acquire_target` already supports the variable, and the pathfinder treats a nonzero `mintargetdist` as "within that distance", so a tile next to the mess is a valid destination. The commit also updates the tracked compiled copy, `build/behavior_trees/.../cleanbot.bt.compiled.json`, with the same one-field change.

## Verification

After rebuilding, with the same setup (bot, a decal under a table three tiles away, a control decal), both decals were gone within 30 seconds. No related runtimes were seen.

Not checked: an emagged cleanbot (that branch is unchanged), the "clean" pet command path (it sets the target directly), and tiles blocked by things other than tables.

## Notes

- The 2024 comment says the bug survived #80208. This matches the cause above, which is in the behavior tree data and not in the code that PR touched; that was not examined further.
- The change affects only the non-emagged cleanup leaf.
