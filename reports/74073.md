## Summary

Neither report could be reproduced on current master. In soft crit and in deeper crit, `*deathgasp` printed the speaker's name ("Testy McTest seizes up and falls limp, ..."). The `EMOTE_IMPORTANT` branch of `run_emote()` now formats the message with the name. A mob consumed by a legion is dead and in stasis, so it cannot emote, and reviving it gibs the legion. No fix was written.

## The bug

**#74073 (2023).** A player in crit used the deathgasp emote and the chat line, per the report's screenshot (which was not examined), did not show the player's name. In a 2023 comment, JohnFulpWillard attributed this to deathgasp being the only emote with `EMOTE_IMPORTANT`, which sent the message straight to chat without the name formatting in `run_emote`.

**#82677 (2024).** A player infested by a legion used a keybound deathgasp, and their character (a moth) played its death sound from inside the legion.

## Reproduction

I tried to reproduce both reports on master and could not.

### #74073

1. Using the Lua editor, spawn a human named "Testy McTest", deal 103 brute damage (health 14, not yet in crit), and give the mob to the admin's client.
2. Say `*deathgasp`. Chat: "Testy McTest seizes up and falls limp, his eyes dead and lifeless..."
3. Deal 60 more brute damage. The Succumb and Blind alerts appeared (crit). Say `*deathgasp` again. Same message, name included.
4. Deal 60 more brute damage. A "Choking (No O2)" alert appeared. After the 15 second emote cooldown, say `*deathgasp`. The message again included the name.

A runechat bubble also showed "Testy McTest: seizes up and falls limp...".

Limits: `stat` was not recorded in the last run, so it is not confirmed whether that state was hard crit. A second human (brute 150, oxy 60) was created for that purpose, but the test client's view stayed on the first mob and the emote could not be sent from it. The original screenshot was not visible, so a problem tied to another client's runechat or chat settings is not ruled out.

### #82677

1. Using the Lua editor, spawn a `/mob/living/basic/mining/legion`, fully heal a human, and call the legion's `consume()` on that human (the same proc real legion infestation uses).
2. The human died ("You have died"), was moved into the legion and got the stasis effect.
3. Say `*deathgasp` as that mob. Chat showed only "DEAD: Testy McTest says, "*deathgasp"". No emote ran and no sound was produced.

This used the proc directly, not a live legion attack.

## Cause

None found; the code has changed since the reports.

**#74073.** `run_emote()` in `code/datums/emotes.dm` now has an important-emote branch (`code/datums/emotes.dm:191`) that sends the name to every viewer, including the user:

```
else if(is_important)
	for(var/mob/viewer as anything in viewers(user))
		to_chat(viewer, span_emote("<b>[user]</b> [msg]"))
```

The runechat-preference path at `code/datums/emotes.dm:172` does the same. Deathgasp (`code/modules/mob/living/emote.dm:100`) is `EMOTE_VISIBLE | EMOTE_AUDIBLE | EMOTE_IMPORTANT` and usable while unconscious, in hard crit or in soft crit. I did not look into which later change fixed this.

**#82677.** `consume()` (`code/modules/mob/living/basic/lavaland/legion/legion.dm:98`) calls `death()` on the victim, applies stasis (`STASIS_LEGION_EATEN`) and moves it into the legion. Deathgasp's `can_use_flags` has no `EMOTE_CANUSE_DEAD`, so a dead mob cannot use it. Also, `on_consumed_revive` (`legion.dm:121`, registered at line 106 on `COMSIG_LIVING_REVIVE`) gibs the legion whenever the victim is revived, so a living mob cannot stay inside a legion.

## Fix

None.

## Verification

Only the checks listed above were done. Not checked: a real legion infestation by an attacking legion; a player with the keybind; the reporter's client and chat settings; hard crit confirmed by `stat`.

## Notes

- The 2024 report (#82677) may predate the revive handler. This was not checked.
- Sound from a living mob inside any container, such as a locker, is heard through `get_turf`. That is existing behaviour and not specific to legions.
- Both reports could probably be closed as no longer reproducible, though the #74073 screenshot was not seen.
