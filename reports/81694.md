# #81694: An upgraded biogenerator turns tofu into more biomass than its soy milk cost

| | |
|---|---|

| Status | Verified, fix committed and checked in game |
| Fix branch | [fix/81694](https://github.com/sohpea-cloub/tgstation-slop/tree/fix/81694) |

## Summary

Foods that a chemical reaction spawns directly from reagents (tofu from soy milk and enzyme, and similar) are worth `nutriments * productivity` biomass when recycled in a biogenerator, but the reagents that made them cost only a small amount of biomass, divided by `efficiency`. With upgraded servos the return is larger than the cost, so a loop of "buy soy milk, make tofu, recycle tofu" produces biomass from nothing. The fix adds a trait, `TRAIT_FOOD_REACTION_MADE`, to foods spawned by `/datum/chemical_reaction/food` reactions; the biogenerator refuses to accept such foods and gives no biomass for them.

## The bug

A fully upgraded biogenerator can synthesise soy milk at a low biomass price. Soy milk plus a small amount of enzyme (a catalyst, not consumed) reacts instantly into tofu, and the same biogenerator will recycle that tofu into more biomass than the soy milk cost. The reporter notes that an unupgraded machine does not do this (it loses biomass on the loop), and suspects cheese may behave the same way. A commenter pointed out that growing pumpkins or watermelons is easier, but it is still a free-biomass loop.

## Reproduction

The loop was reproduced with admin tools, with the key step done by calling the biogenerator's conversion proc directly rather than by inserting the tofu and activating the machine.

1. Started a round on master and, with the Lua editor, spawned a bluespace beaker next to the admin mob.
2. Added 5 units of enzyme and 100 units of soy milk to the beaker. The tofu reaction is instant, so the beaker immediately produced 10 tofu (the 100 units of soy milk made 10 tofu; the enzyme remained as a catalyst).
3. Spawned a biogenerator and set its `productivity` to 4 (the value of a machine with tier-4 servos; `RefreshParts()` sets `productivity` and `efficiency` to the same servo tier sum).
4. Called `convert_to_biomass()` on one tofu and compared `biomass` before and after.

Observed: one tofu gave +8 biomass (2 nutriment times productivity 4).

The cost side was computed from the code, not measured in game. The soy milk design costs 0.4 biomass per unit (`code/modules/research/designs/biogenerator_designs.dm:17-20`) and `use_biomass()` divides the cost by `efficiency` (`code/modules/hydroponics/biogenerator.dm:361`). So 10 units of soy milk, the amount for one tofu, cost `4 / efficiency` biomass. With a tier-N servo (`productivity` = `efficiency` = N) the return is `2N` against a cost of `4/N`, a ratio of `N^2 / 2`: a loss at N=1 (the reporter's unupgraded case), break-even or profit from N=2, and 8 biomass returned for 1 spent at N=4. The test machine had only `productivity` set to 4; its `efficiency` was not set or measured.

## Cause

Biomass in and out of the biogenerator is scaled by two independent variables that the servos raise together:

```dm
// code/modules/hydroponics/biogenerator.dm:336-338
/obj/machinery/biogenerator/proc/convert_to_biomass(obj/item/food/food_to_convert)
	var/nutriments = ROUND_UP(food_to_convert.reagents.get_reagent_amount(/datum/reagent/consumable/nutriment, type_check = REAGENT_PARENT_TYPE))
	biomass += nutriments * productivity
```

and `use_biomass()` charges `cost / efficiency` (`biogenerator.dm:361`). Neither side knows where the food came from. `/datum/chemical_reaction/food/on_reaction()` (`code/modules/food_and_drinks/recipes/food_mixtures.dm:95-101`) spawns `resulting_food_path` for each unit of reaction output, so tofu costs only the reagents it was synthesised from, which the biogenerator can itself produce cheaply. With both multipliers scaling together, the round trip is a net gain once the servos are upgraded.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 81694: foods made straight from reagents (tofu, cheese...) can't be recycled into biomass":

- `code/__DEFINES/traits/declarations.dm` and `code/_globalvars/traits/_traits.dm`: new `TRAIT_FOOD_REACTION_MADE`, declared and registered in the trait list.
- `food_mixtures.dm`: `/datum/chemical_reaction/food/on_reaction()` adds the trait (source: the reaction datum) to every food it spawns.
- `biogenerator.dm`:
  - `item_interaction()` with a single food item that has the trait prints "The biogenerator can't extract any biomass from the tofu!" and returns `ITEM_INTERACT_BLOCKING` without inserting it.
  - When emptying a storage bag, items with the trait are skipped and stay in the bag.
  - `convert_to_biomass()` deletes a trait-marked food and returns without adding biomass (a safety net for items that reach the machine by another route).

The alternative is to leave the foods alone and rebalance the machine, for example by not scaling both biomass gain and cost by the servo tier. That changes the biogenerator for every plant and food, and the fix author chose the narrower change. It is a design choice (see Notes).

## Verification

After rebuilding and restarting the test server, with the Lua editor:

- Spawned a beaker with enzyme and 20 units of soy milk, which made tofu, and a biogenerator with `productivity` 4.
- `item_interaction()` with a tofu returned `ITEM_INTERACT_BLOCKING` (2) and the tofu was not inside the machine.
- A forced `convert_to_biomass()` on the tofu gave 0 biomass.
- Control: a normal meat slab was accepted (`ITEM_INTERACT_SUCCESS`) and gave +32 biomass.
- No new runtimes appeared in the round's runtime log (the entries seen were the usual asset-cache and spritesheet startup errors).

Not checked: a real player run of insert, activate and recycle (the checks above call the insert and conversion procs directly); emptying a bag that holds reaction-made food; the chat message text; cheese wheel, candy corn, chocolate bar and synthmeat in game (they get the trait through the same code path, but only tofu was tested); and the `efficiency` side of the loop.

## Notes

- The fix covers all foods spawned by `/datum/chemical_reaction/food` reactions: tofu, candy corn, the chocolate bars, cheese wheel and synthmeat, plus the dough, batter, cheese and bread entries further down `food_mixtures.dm`. The inputs only name the first group as checked; some reactions in that file override `on_reaction` (for example `imitationcarpmeat` and `uncooked_rice`) and were not examined.
- Other free-biomass loops remain outside this change, for example cooked foods made from cheap flour or sugar. The fix only removes the reaction-made class that the issue is about.
- Whether to block these foods or rebalance `productivity` against `efficiency` is a maintainer decision. The block is a gameplay change: a chef can no longer put reaction-made cheese or tofu into a biogenerator.
- No existing upstream PR for this issue was found in the inputs.
