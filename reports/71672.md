## Summary

`update_succumb_action()` shows the succumb alert to any mob with `TRAIT_SUCCUMB_OVERRIDE` without looking at its stat. High-functioning zombies (HFZ) carry that trait, so they get the alert as soon as they become zombies and never lose it. The alert click and the succumb verb had the same missing crit check, so a zombie at full health could kill itself. The fix adds a `CAN_SUCCUMB_OR_OVERRIDE` macro that requires crit, uses it in all three places, and refreshes the alert when the trait is added or removed. #70903 (HFZ stuck in crit and unable to die) could not be reproduced: an HFZ in crit can already succumb and die on master.

## The bug

### #71672

A mob with `TRAIT_SUCCUMB_OVERRIDE` (in practice, the zombie species) gets the "Succumb" alert and keeps it after healing out of crit. Clicking the alert at that point kills the mob immediately, with no text prompt.

### #70903

The report says revhead high-functioning zombies sit in permanent crit and cannot die, so the revolution never ends. The issue has no reproduction steps beyond that. Comments suggest other ways to kill them (cremator, gibber, decapitation) and link the related report that zombies cannot succumb.

## Reproduction

### #71672

On unfixed master, using the Lua editor on the test server:

1. Spawn a human and call `set_species(/datum/species/zombie)` on it.
2. Look at the human's alerts. The `succumb` alert was already present at full health (stat 0, health 100). Without the bug it should show only in crit.

After the fix was built:

1. Spawn an HFZ the same way. No succumb alert at full health.
2. Deal 400 brute damage. The HFZ went into crit (health -208) and the alert appeared.
3. Heal it (brute -1000). Stat returned to 0 and the alert was removed.

This used the species change and damage/heal calls rather than a player action. The "click at full health kills you" part was not run on unfixed master. The issue author reports it.

### #70903

1. With the fixed build, spawn an HFZ and put it in crit as above.
2. Take control of it (set its `ckey` to an admin's) and click the Succumb alert. A text input modal appeared. Submitting it empty gave "You have given up life and succumbed to death." and the death message "has died at Central Primary Hallway".

This was run on the fixed build. The unfixed code allows the same click, because the old check passes through the override trait, so this test does not depend on the fix. It was not run with an actual revhead HFZ, only with a plain zombie-species human.

## Cause

`code/modules/mob/living/init_signals.dm:265-270`:

```dm
/mob/living/proc/update_succumb_action()
	if (CAN_SUCCUMB(src) || HAS_TRAIT(src, TRAIT_SUCCUMB_OVERRIDE))
		throw_alert(ALERT_SUCCUMB, /atom/movable/screen/alert/succumb)
	else
		clear_alert(ALERT_SUCCUMB)
```

`CAN_SUCCUMB` (`code/__DEFINES/mobs.dm:660`) requires soft or hard crit and no `TRAIT_NODEATH`. The `|| HAS_TRAIT(src, TRAIT_SUCCUMB_OVERRIDE)` term has no stat condition, so for a mob with the trait the `if` is always true and the `else` that clears the alert is unreachable. The zombie species applies the trait through `/datum/status_effect/zombie/uninfected` (`code/modules/mob/living/carbon/human/species_types/zombies.dm:281`), so the alert is thrown when the species is set.

The same pattern is in two other places:

- `code/_onclick/hud/screen_objects/alert.dm:601`: `if(!CAN_SUCCUMB(living_owner) && !HAS_TRAIT(living_owner, TRAIT_SUCCUMB_OVERRIDE)) return`. The click handler bails out only if the mob has neither, so the override trait alone lets the click proceed at any health.
- `code/modules/mob/living/living.dm:558-565`, the `succumb` verb. If `CAN_SUCCUMB` failed and the mob had the override trait, it returned only when `whispered` was set. A non-whispered call (the alert button) went on to the lethal code at any stat.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 71672: succumb alert only shows while in crit for mobs with TRAIT_SUCCUMB_OVERRIDE".

- `code/__DEFINES/mobs.dm`: adds `CAN_SUCCUMB_OR_OVERRIDE(target)`, true when the mob is in soft or hard crit and either lacks `TRAIT_NODEATH` or has `TRAIT_SUCCUMB_OVERRIDE`. Crit is required in both cases.
- `update_succumb_action()` and the alert's `Click()` use the macro.
- The `succumb` verb is simplified. It refuses with "You are unable to succumb to death! This life continues." unless the macro passes. It then keeps the existing whisper rule: a whispered succumb on a mob that still has `TRAIT_NODEATH` gets the "immortal body" message, and only the alert button can override it.
- `init_signals.dm`: a new `on_succumb_override()` handler, registered for adding and removing `TRAIT_SUCCUMB_OVERRIDE`, calls `update_succumb_action()`, so the alert is refreshed when the trait is gained or lost. Before this, a change in the trait alone did not update the alert.

A single shared macro was used so the three checks cannot drift apart again.

## Verification

Checked in game on the test server with a build of the fix:

- A newly made HFZ at full health has no succumb alert.
- After 400 brute it is in crit and has the alert.
- After healing it is out of crit and the alert is gone.
- A crit HFZ could click the alert, got the input modal, and died when it was submitted (this covers #70903).
- No new runtimes appeared in the server log during these checks.

Not checked:

- Clicking the alert or running the verb at full health after the fix. The macro blocks it in code, but this was not run.
- The whispered-succumb path.
- Non-zombie mobs with `TRAIT_NODEATH` or `TRAIT_SUCCUMB_OVERRIDE`.
- Adding or removing the trait on a mob already in crit (the new signal handler).
- A revhead HFZ in an actual revolution round.

## Notes

- A comment on #71672 says a zombie that can spontaneously die "is pretty funny" and may be intended. The fix keeps the zombie's ability to succumb while in crit (the intent of `TRAIT_SUCCUMB_OVERRIDE`). It removes only the behavior that is plainly unintended: the alert at full health and the instant kill from it.
- #70903: the report is old (2022-10-30), and the commenters tie it to zombies being unable to succumb. In the code at master, zombies have the override trait, and the crit HFZ test above shows that they can die. The likely explanation is that the report predates `TRAIT_SUCCUMB_OVERRIDE` being applied to zombies, but the inputs do not show when that happened, so this is an inference. No separate cause was found and no extra change was made for it.
- Not covered: whether an HFZ in crit can die by other means (damage does not kill it because of `TRAIT_NODEATH`; the succumb button is the player's way out).
