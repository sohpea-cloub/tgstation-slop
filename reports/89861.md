## Summary

The admin verb "Play Local Sound" ("Plays a sound only you can hear") called `playsound()` at the admin's turf. That plays the sound to everyone in earshot and scales it by distance and air pressure, so an admin observing over space heard it at 15% of the chosen volume, and other players nearby heard it too. The fix sends the sound straight to the admin with `SEND_SOUND`. The same commit fixes "Play Direct Mob Sound", which sent the raw sound and ignored the chosen volume.

## The bug

The issue (opened by a maintainer, two lines long) says that playing a sound from the code base with Play Local Sound did nothing, or at least could not be heard "as an observer".

## Reproduction

1. On the test server, join as an observer (ghost) with admin rights and move the ghost to a space turf. This was done with the Lua editor.
2. Run Play Local Sound. The verb's file-upload prompt could not be driven from the test client, so the verb was invoked through the Admin Verb Panel with the sound path (`sound/ambience/beach/seag2.ogg`) given as text. Enter volume 80 in the number prompt.
3. Observed: the client's sound list showed `seag2.ogg` at volume 12%, with no position. That is 80 times the 0.15 minimum pressure factor.
4. As a comparison, calling `playsound()` through Lua from a hallway turf gave 50%. Only low-pressure spots are muffled.

Caveat: the reporter probably picked a file from disk. That path could not be driven; it goes through the same code, so this is the likely cause for them, but it is not proven. Their location when they tried is unknown.

## Cause

`code/modules/admin/verbs/playsound.dm:48`:

```
playsound(get_turf(user.mob), sound, volume || 50, FALSE)
```

`playsound()` delivers to every client in range and applies per-listener falloff and, through `playsound_local`, atmosphere (`code/game/sound/sound.dm:130-148`). With `pressure_affected` on, a hearer or source in a vacuum gets a pressure factor of 0, raised to a minimum of 0.15 only when the listener is within 1 tile of the source. A ghost over space therefore hears the sound at 15% volume, or less when farther away. Players nearby hear it too, contrary to the verb's description.

`play_direct_mob_sound` (`playsound.dm:~64`) has a separate bug: it built `admin_sound` with the chosen volume but then called `SEND_SOUND(target, sound)`, sending the unmodified sound.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 89861: Play Local Sound is heard by others and muffled for ghosts":

- `play_local_sound` builds `sound(sound)`, sets its volume to the chosen value (default 50) and sends it with `SEND_SOUND(user, admin_sound)`, so only the admin hears it, at the volume entered.
- `play_direct_mob_sound` now sends `admin_sound` instead of `sound`.

No alternative was recorded; this follows the other admin sound verbs in the file, which already use `SEND_SOUND`.

## Verification

Repeating the same steps after rebuilding (ghost over space, volume 80) gave `seag2.ogg (80%)` in the client's sound list. No new runtimes appeared in the log.

Not checked: picking a file from disk through the verb's real upload prompt, Play Direct Mob Sound in game (the one-word change was not exercised), and listeners other than the admin after the fix.
