# #95670: Guns mounted in an emitter or turret lose their link to the gun (wormhole projector makes no portals, DRAGnet ignores its beacon)

## Summary

Emitters and portable turrets fire a gun's projectile type directly instead of firing the gun, so the projectile never receives the link back to the gun that the ammo casing normally gives it. The bluespace wormhole projector's beam needs that link to make portals, and the DRAGnet's snare needs it to find its beacon. The fix adds a no-op hook, `/obj/item/gun/proc/setup_turret_projectile()`, that emitters and turrets call on every projectile they create, and overrides it in the wormhole projector and the DRAGnet to restore the link. Hand-held use is unchanged.

## The bug

- **#95670.** A bluespace wormhole projector screwed into an emitter fires beams that make no portals, and a runtime is logged each time a beam hits something.
- **#96813.** A DRAGnet translocation shotgun synced to a DRAGnet beacon and put in an emitter fires snares that teleport the target to a random place nearby instead of to the beacon.
- **#84595.** The same happens when the synced DRAGnet is built into a portable turret.

## Reproduction

All on master `87b93338`, in the Lua editor as an admin observer on the Central Primary Hallway of MetaStation. The emitter and turret were set up by calling the procs the construction steps call (`integrate()` for the emitter, `setup()` for the turret), and firing was done by calling `fire_beam()` and `shootAt()`; the screwdriver-and-wrench construction by hand was not played through.

**#95670, wormhole projector in an emitter**

1. Spawn a human holding a `/obj/item/gun/energy/wormhole_projector`, and an `/obj/machinery/power/emitter` facing east.
2. Call `emitter.integrate(projector, human)`. It returned true and `emitter.gun` was the projector; `projectile_type` became `/obj/projectile/beam/wormhole/orange`.
3. Call `emitter.fire_beam()`.
4. Count `/obj/effect/portal` objects within 20 tiles of the emitter, before and after: none (0 and 0).
5. The runtime log had `runtime error: Cannot execute null.resolve().` in `/obj/projectile/beam/wormhole/on_hit`.

**#96813, DRAGnet in an emitter**

1. Spawn a human holding a `/obj/item/gun/energy/e_gun/dragnet`, and a `/obj/item/dragnet_beacon` at 90,143. Call `dragnet.link_beacon(human, beacon)`.
2. Spawn an emitter facing east at 107,145 and call `emitter.integrate(dragnet, human)`; `projectile_type` became `/obj/projectile/energy/snare`.
3. Spawn a second human ("Victim") at 112,145, in the beam's path, and call `emitter.fire_beam()`.
4. After the netting field's 3 seconds, Victim had been teleported to a random spot, not to the beacon at 90,143.

**#84595, DRAGnet in a turret**

1. Spawn a `/obj/machinery/porta_turret`, a synced DRAGnet as above, and call `turret.setup(dragnet)`. Set `raised = 1`, `mode = 1`, `last_fired = 0`.
2. Spawn a human at 112,143 and call `turret.shootAt(human)`. The returned projectile was an `/obj/projectile/energy/snare` whose `fired_from` was the turret.
3. The victim was hit ("is hit by an energy snare in the chest") and, after the field popped, was at 104,142: about 8 tiles from where it was hit and far from the beacon at 90,143.

## Cause

A hand-held gun fires through an ammo casing, and the casing hands the new projectile what it needs. Emitters and turrets skip the casing:

- `emitter.dm:254-259`, `fire_beam()`: `new projectile_type(get_turf(src))`, then `projectile.fired_from = src`.
- `portable_turret.dm:670-681`, `shootAt()`: `A = new stun_projectile(T)` or `new lethal_projectile(T)`, then `A.fired_from = src`.

So `fired_from` is the machine and nothing points at the gun.

- **Wormhole beam.** `/obj/projectile/beam/wormhole` keeps a weakref `gun` that is set only in `Initialize()` from `casing.gun` (`wormhole.dm:19-23`). Without a casing it stays null, and `on_hit()` does `gun.resolve()` (`wormhole.dm:26`) on null, which runtimes before any portal is made. As the issue comments note, portal creation lives on the projector (`create_portal()` in `code/modules/projectiles/guns/energy/special.dm`) rather than on the portals.
- **DRAGnet snare.** `/obj/projectile/energy/snare/on_hit()` takes `our_dragnet = fired_from` and reads `linked_beacon` from it (`net_snare.dm:15-17`). With `fired_from` being the emitter or turret, the cast fails, no beacon is found, and the netting portals are created without a destination, so `nettingportal/pop()` teleports the victim up to 15 tiles at random.

## Fix

Three commits, sharing one hook.

**"Fix issue 95670: wormhole projector in an emitter makes portals".** Adds `/obj/item/gun/proc/setup_turret_projectile(obj/projectile/fired_projectile)`, a no-op defined next to the turret property procs in `portable_turret.dm`, documented as called by machines that fire a gun's projectiles without the gun. The emitter calls `gun?.setup_turret_projectile(projectile)` in `fire_beam()` after setting `fired_from`. `/obj/item/gun/energy/wormhole_projector` overrides it to set the beam's `gun` weakref to itself. `wormhole_beam.on_hit()` now uses `gun?.resolve()`, so a beam with no known gun is dropped quietly instead of runtiming.

**"Fix issue 96813: DRAGnet snares fired from an emitter go to the linked beacon".** The DRAGnet overrides the hook to give the snare a new `dragnet_ref` weakref to itself, and `snare/on_hit()` uses `dragnet_ref?.resolve() || fired_from`, so the old behavior (fired directly from the gun) still works.

**"Fix issue 84595: beacon-synced DRAGnets in turrets teleport to the beacon".** `shootAt()` calls `stored_gun?.setup_turret_projectile(A)` after setting `fired_from`.

A per-gun hook was used, so each gun decides what link its own projectiles need; the emitter keeps its gun in `gun` and the turret in `stored_gun`, and both call the same proc. The issue comments suggest portals should not need the gun at all; that larger refactor was not attempted.

## Verification

On the fixed build, in the same setup:

- **Wormhole projector in an emitter.** The emitter was fired east (blue beam) and, after switching its mode so the projectile became the other color, west (orange beam). Two portals were created, at 127,144 and 85,144, linked to each other. No runtime was logged.
- **DRAGnet in an emitter.** The snared victim was teleported to the beacon at 90,143 (the beacon teleport allows an error of 1 tile).
- **DRAGnet in a turret.** The snared victim landed at 89,142, within that one-tile error of the beacon.
- **Hand-held wormhole projector.** A projector with its core inserted, fired by a human, still made its (blue) portal: at 112,146 for a target at 127,146.
- The runtime log of the fixed run showed no runtimes related to these changes.

Not checked: building the emitter or turret by hand (screwdriver and wrench) rather than through `integrate()`/`setup()`; a turret built from a frame; walking through the created portals or using the linked pair for teleporting; hand-held DRAGnet use after the change (the old path is kept by the `|| fired_from` fallback but was not re-run); other guns that rely on the casing for a link.

## Notes

- No upstream pull request was found for any of the three issues (searches for the issue numbers, "wormhole emitter", "dragnet emitter" and "dragnet turret").
- #84595's early comments question whether DRAGnets should work in turrets at all; later comments say it is fine. The fix makes the existing behavior consistent rather than adding a feature.
- The hook is generic. Any other gun whose projectiles depend on the casing (or on the gun) would be fixed the same way by overriding `setup_turret_projectile()`; none besides the two above were audited.
