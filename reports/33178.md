## Summary

#33178: a tomato thrown at a directional window is stopped by the window but leaves its smudge on the window's tile, which is on the far side of the pane from the thrower. #72191: `/obj/hitby` always dealt BRUTE damage to the object that was hit, so a thrown snowball (a stamina item) damaged windows and doors. The first fix splats a plant on its own tile when a border object stopped it. The second makes `/obj/hitby` use the thrown item's own damage type, so stamina damage no longer hurts objects. The two issues are unrelated except that both are about throwing.

## The bug

### #33178

A GIF in the issue shows a thrown tomato hitting a directional ("side-tile") window and splatting on the other side of it, as if it had passed through the glass. Commenters in 2023 and 2024 said it was still happening.

### #72191

Items thrown at objects deal damage whatever their damage type is. The report's example is a snowball, which is set to deal STAMINA damage but still damaged a blast door.

## Reproduction

All steps used the Lua editor on the test server, as an observer, with the objects created and thrown from Lua (`throw_at`) rather than by a player throwing them.

### #33178

1. Created an `/obj/structure/window` with `dir = WEST` on the tile directly east of an observer standing at the thrower's tile (call them tile A for the thrower and tile B for the window).
2. Created an `/obj/item/food/grown/tomato` at tile A and threw it east at a tile three tiles beyond B.
3. The window stopped the tomato. The chat showed "The tomato is squashed", and the "tomato smudge" decal appeared on tile B, the window's own tile, instead of on tile A. The window's blocking edge faces the thrower, so the splat appears behind the glass.

### #72191

1. With the same window (integrity 50 of 50), created an `/obj/item/toy/snowball` (damtype STAMINA, throwforce 20) at tile A and threw it at the window.
2. The window's `atom_integrity` dropped from 50 to 40. A second snowball took it to 30.

## Cause

### #33178

`squash_plant()` in `code/modules/hydroponics/plant_genes.dm:235-260` is called when a thrown plant with the Liquid Contents trait hits something. It places the plant and its smudge on the tile of whatever was hit:

```dm
var/turf/our_turf = get_turf(target)
our_plant.forceMove(our_turf)
```

A directional window blocks a throw at its border: `CanAllowThrough()` returns FALSE when `border_dir == dir` (`code/game/objects/structures/window.dm:141-142`). The plant therefore stops on the thrower's side, still on tile A, and the impact is reported with the window as `target`. `get_turf(target)` is the window's tile B, so the plant is moved onto B and the smudge is created there, past the window.

### #72191

`/obj/hitby()` in `code/game/objects/obj_defense.dm:2-8` hardcodes the damage type:

```dm
take_damage(damage_taken, BRUTE, MELEE, 1, get_dir(src, hit_by))
```

Damage armor processing already ignores any type other than brute and burn (`run_atom_armor()`, `code/game/atom/atom_defense.dm:97-98`), so passing the item's real damage type is enough to make stamina items harmless to objects.

## Fix

Two commits, one per issue.

**"Fix issue 33178: thrown plants splat on their own side of a directional window"** (`code/modules/hydroponics/plant_genes.dm`). In `squash_plant()`, if the target is an object with `ON_BORDER_1`, the plant is on a turf, and that turf differs from the target's turf, the splat turf becomes the plant's own turf instead of the target's. The border object is remembered and still gets `reagents.expose()` called on it, so the window is still hit by the plant's reagents. Non-border targets keep the old behaviour.

**"Fix issue 72191: thrown items damage objects with their own damage type"** (`code/game/objects/obj_defense.dm`). `/obj/hitby()` now starts with `damage_type = BRUTE` and, if the thrown thing is an item, uses its `damtype`. That value is passed to `take_damage()`.

## Verification

On the rebuilt test server, with a window of 50 integrity and the same setup as above:

- The tomato thrown at the window left its smudge on the thrower's tile (A) and not on the window's tile.
- A thrown snowball left the window at 50 integrity (before the fix it lost 10 per hit).
- A thrown crowbar (a brute item) took the window from 50 to 45.6, so brute damage still works.
- No runtime errors were found in the round's `runtime.log`.

Not checked:
- A real player throwing the items, as opposed to throwing from Lua.
- Windows facing other directions, windoors, and other border objects (railings, for example).
- Targets named in the issues other than a window: the report in #72191 used a blast door.
- Items with a BURN damage type, and thrown things that are not items.
- Whether the reagents of a tomato still reach the window and nearby objects as intended.

## Notes

- Other dense, non-border objects (full-tile windows, machines) still get the smudge on their own tile. That is unchanged, and it did not look wrong for them since the plant is adjacent and there is no pane edge to be on the wrong side of. This was not examined further.
- The `/obj/hitby` change also affects thrown items with a BURN damage type: they now deal burn damage (with burn armor) to the object instead of brute. This was not tested. Stamina and other non-brute, non-burn types now do nothing to objects when thrown, which is what the issue asks for.
- The first fix relies on the plant's `loc` being the turf it was thrown from when the impact signal is sent. That holds in the tested case.
