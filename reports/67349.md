# #67349: Blob is gimped on multi-z level maps

| | |
|---|---|
| Issues | [#67349](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/67349): verified (stairs and ladders destroyed by blob), fixed; the "spores explode on ladders" claim not reproduced |
| Status | Fixed |
| Fix branch | [fix/67349](https://github.com/sohpea-cloub/tgstation-slop/tree/fix/67349) |
| Tested on | tgstation master `87b93338`, 2026-10-23 |

## Summary

Blob tiles destroy any stair or ladder they grow over, because `/obj/blob_act` deals 400 brute damage to every object on the tile. On multi-z maps such as Tramstation that cuts blob mobs (spores, zombies, blobbernauts) off from other z-levels. The fix gives `/obj/structure/stairs` and `/obj/structure/ladder` a `blob_act` override that does nothing, so the blob grows over them and they stay usable. The report's claim that spores explode on ladders was not reproduced on current master.

## The bug

The issue lists several symptoms on Tramstation: the blob cannot go up stairs, spores die on ladders, and stairs the blob builds on become unclimbable for blobbernauts and blob zombies. What reproduced is the last one: the stairs or ladder under a blob tile is destroyed, so it is not "unclimbable" but gone.

## Reproduction

Done with admin tools, on Tramstation (map changed with Change-Map, then restart). A blob overmind was created by hand in the Lua editor, not through a real blob roundstart event.

1. Change the map to Tramstation, restart, start the round and observe.
2. In the Lua editor, spawn a `/mob/eye/blob` at 114,123,2 and call `place_blob_core(-1)`. That tile has a ladder.
3. Spawn normal blob tiles, including one on a stair at 119,110,2, and spawn blobbernauts with the overmind's `create_spore()`.
4. Compare `GLOB.ladders` and `GLOB.stairs` sizes, and try to walk a blobbernaut up the stairs.

Observed before the fix:
- `GLOB.ladders` went from 58 to 57 after the core was placed on the ladder at 114,123,2.
- `GLOB.stairs` went from 30 to 29 after a blob tile was spawned on the stair at 119,110,2.
- A blobbernaut on that tile moving north stayed at 119,110,2, because the stairs were gone.
- Control: on the stair at 120,110,2 with no blob, the blobbernaut ended at 120,111,3.
- Control: with the stair made indestructible in Lua before the blob was spawned on it, the blobbernaut climbed to 120,111,3. So the blob itself does not block climbing; only the destruction of the stairs does.

Not reproduced: spores exploding on ladders. A factory spore sent up a Tramstation ladder (136,123,2 to 136,123,3) survived (stat 0, alive).

## Cause

`/obj/blob_act` (`code/game/objects/obj_defense.dm:63`) is the default response of any object to being hit by a blob:

```dm
/obj/blob_act(obj/structure/blob/B)
	if (!..() || HAS_TRAIT(src, TRAIT_UNDERFLOOR))
		return
	take_damage(400, BRUTE, MELEE, 0, get_dir(src, B))
```

Blob tiles call it on everything on the tile they occupy, in `/obj/structure/blob/Initialize` and `ConsumeTile()` (`code/modules/antagonists/blob/structures/_blob.dm:52` and `:131`). Stairs and ladders do not override it, so 400 brute destroys them when blob spreads over them. Other objects that need to survive the blob already override `blob_act` (for example `/obj/structure/lattice/blob_act`, `code/game/objects/structures/lattice.dm:66`).

Spores: `on_z_changed` for blob minions goes through `is_valid_z_level` (`code/__HELPERS/levels.dm:13`), which accepts any two station levels, so a spore changing level on a ladder is not killed there. The 2022 report may have been fixed since; this was not bisected.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 67349: blob no longer eats stairs and ladders", adds two overrides that return `FALSE`, with a comment:

- `/obj/structure/stairs/blob_act` in `code/game/objects/structures/stairs.dm`
- `/obj/structure/ladder/blob_act` in `code/game/objects/structures/ladders.dm`

Blob tiles still grow over the stairs or ladder tile. The alternative would be to stop the blob from spreading onto those tiles, which would leave gaps in the blob and does not match how it treats other structures.

## Verification

After rebuilding, on Tramstation with the same setup: core placed on a ladder, blob tile on a stair, blob tile on a ladder. Stair and ladder counts were 30 and 58 before and after. A blobbernaut starting on the blob-covered stair reached 119,111,3 and was alive. A spore sent through the ladder at 136,123,2 arrived at 136,123,3 alive.

Not checked: stairs and ladders on other maps, damage from sources other than `blob_act` (the stairs and ladders can still be destroyed other ways), blob zombies specifically, and a normally played blob round.

## Notes

- Design choice: stairs and ladders are now immune to blob attacks, so the blob cannot be used to cut a stair or ladder. A maintainer may prefer to keep them destructible and have the blob mobs find another route.
- Not changed: the overmind itself cannot change z-level once placed (`/mob/eye/blob/can_z_move`, `code/modules/antagonists/blob/overmind.dm:130`, comment "The blob can't expand vertically (yet)"). That is intentional and would be a feature request (vertical blob expansion), not a bug fix. This covers the issue's complaint that you "can't climb stairs".
- One `add_points` runtime appeared in the first session; the overmind was created by hand in Lua, so it is probably an artefact of that setup and was not pursued.
