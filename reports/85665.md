## Summary

When Waldo (the fugitive hunter outfit with undroppable clothes) is monkeyized, the species change force-drops every worn item the monkey cannot wear, ignoring the nodrop trait. The sweater and shoes fell off, which the reporter saw as the costume disappearing apart from the hat. The fix makes the species-change check skip items with `TRAIT_NODROP`. This is a design choice; the issue comments record that maintainers had no agreed solution.

## The bug

A player spawned as Waldo cannot take off their costume, intentionally. After using monkification from genetics, the sweater and shoes came off and only the hat stayed, so Waldo no longer stood out.

## Reproduction

1. On the test server, using the Lua editor, spawn a human and call `equipOutfit(/datum/outfit/waldo)` on them.
2. Call `monkeyize(true)` on the human.
3. Observed on master: the "striped sweater" and "brown shoes" appeared on the floor. The hat remained because monkeys can wear it.

The monkeyize was called directly through Lua rather than through the genetics machine.

## Cause

Monkeys have `no_equip_flags = ITEM_SLOT_OCLOTHING | ITEM_SLOT_GLOVES | ITEM_SLOT_FEET | ITEM_SLOT_SUITSTORE` (`code/modules/mob/living/carbon/human/species_types/monkeys.dm:21`). `worn_items_fit_body_check` (`code/modules/mob/living/carbon/human/_species.dm:332`, called from `on_species_gain` at `:396`) goes through all worn items and, for any the new species cannot equip, calls `dropItemToGround(equipped_item, force = TRUE)` (`:336`). The force flag overrides `TRAIT_NODROP`, which Waldo's outfit applies (`code/modules/antagonists/fugitive/fugitive_outfits.dm:55`).

## Fix

Commit "Fix issue 85665: monkeyizing no longer strips nodrop worn items (Waldo's costume)": in `worn_items_fit_body_check`, items with `TRAIT_NODROP` are skipped with `continue`. It is a two-line change.

## Verification

On the test server, after rebuilding and restarting: a human with the Waldo outfit that was monkeyized kept "monkey, w_uniform=jeans, wear_suit=striped sweater, shoes=brown shoes, head=red striped bobble hat, glasses=circle glasses, ears=radio headset". No items fell to the floor.

Not checked: how a monkey wearing the sweater looks (the sprite may not render correctly), the real genetics path, and other callers of the check.

## Notes

- Any nodrop item is now kept across species changes, including cursed items, ghost-role species changes and infuser slot locks, since `worn_items_fit_body_check` is also reached from `update_no_equip_flags`. This may or may not be wanted for each case.
- The issue comments (a maintainer calling it a nodrop-and-monkeyization problem in general with no solution everyone agreed on) suggest the alternatives of removing the nodrop items or blocking the change; the simple option was chosen here.
