## Summary

When a shuttle moves, every shuttle turf is rebuilt on the destination by `copyTurf()`, which copies the icon, materials, colour and direction but not floor damage (`broken`, `burnt`) or wall dent decals, and does not refresh the appearance. Damaged floors and shot or hit walls therefore arrive looking pristine. The fix copies those values in `copyTurf()` and updates the appearance. The other two issues in the group (pizza-themed turfs losing their look, custom-material turfs losing their material) did not reproduce on master and do not share this cause.

## The bug

### #69210

A C4 charge was detonated on a shuttle, damaging floor tiles and doors. After the shuttle took off the damage was gone and the shuttle looked untouched. In the discussion the cause was identified as the damage overlays, which are tied to the turf and are not carried over by the shuttle move; a comment says the moved turfs do not copy the `burnt`/`broken` vars and do not call `update_appearance`.

### #87234

A shuttle was turned into pizza (the pizza dimension theme) and "went bland after launch": the toppings were reported to slide off at takeoff.

### #96322

Custom-material floors and walls on a shuttle were reported to lose their material when the shuttle lifted off, becoming plastic.

## Reproduction

All steps were done with the Lua editor on the test server, using the mining shuttle and moving it directly between two of its docks, not through a hyperspace trip. The move is asynchronous, so the turf state was read in a separate Lua run after the move.

### #69210

1. Get the mining shuttle's turfs with `SSshuttle.getShuttle("mining"):return_turfs()`.
2. Call `break_tile()` on one floor and `burn_tile()` on another. Call `add_dent(1)` on a wall.
3. Move the shuttle to its other dock with `SSshuttle.moveShuttle("mining", "mining_away", FALSE)`.
4. In a new Lua run, read `broken`, `burnt` and the dent decals of the shuttle's turfs.

Observed on master: before the move, one floor had `broken = 1` and another `burnt = 1`. After the move, no floor on the shuttle had `broken` or `burnt` set and the damage overlays were gone.

### #87234 and #96322

1. Turn the mining shuttle's turfs into pizza by applying the pizza dimension theme (`SSmaterials.dimensional_themes[/datum/dimension_theme/pizza]` and its `apply_theme`) to all 35 of them. This also covers the "custom material floor and wall" case, since the theme makes `/turf/open/floor/material` and `/turf/closed/wall/material` turfs. A separate check also used `set_custom_materials()` with pizza and gold directly on floor and wall material turfs.
2. Move the shuttle (two moves were done).
3. Compare each moved turf with a freshly made one.

Observed on master (and again with the fix applied, the same build): 23 material floors and 12 material walls, each with the same name, one texture filter, the material set, `appearance_flags` and colour as a fresh pizza turf. A render of the shuttle at its destination showed the textured orange floor. The custom-material floors and walls kept their `custom_materials`, filter, colour, name and appearance flags. So neither report reproduced.

## Cause

`onShuttleMove()` for turfs (`code/modules/shuttle/mobile_port/shuttle_move_callbacks.dm:58`) calls `new_turf.CopyOnTop(src, ...)`. `CopyOnTop()` (`code/game/turfs/baseturfs.dm:71`) ends by calling `copytarget.copyTurf(src, copy_air, flags)`. The old shuttle turf is then removed with `ScrapeAway()`.

`/turf/proc/copyTurf()` (`code/game/turfs/change_turf.dm:21`) makes the destination turf the same type and copies `icon_state`, `icon`, custom materials, atom colours and `dir`. `/turf/open/copyTurf()` (line 40) additionally copies wet floor state and air. Neither copies:

- `broken` and `burnt`, declared on `/turf/open` (`code/game/turfs/open/_open.dm:15` and `:18`). The damage sprite is built from these in `/turf/open/update_overlays()` (`_open.dm`, around line 69), so with the vars left at their defaults and no `update_appearance()` call, the new floor is drawn undamaged.
- `dent_decals` on `/turf/closed/wall` (`code/game/turfs/closed/walls.dm:31`), a list of overlay decals added by `add_dent()` (line 309). The new wall has no dent decals.

Materials and colour are already copied, which is consistent with those not being lost in the checks below.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 69210: shuttle moves no longer repair broken/burnt floors and wall dents", changes `code/game/turfs/change_turf.dm`:

- `/turf/open/copyTurf()` now copies `broken` and `burnt` to the destination when they differ, then calls `update_appearance()` so the damage overlay is drawn.
- A new `/turf/closed/wall/copyTurf()` calls the parent, then, if the source wall has dent decals, removes any decals already on the destination, copies the list and adds it as overlays.

Putting this in `copyTurf()` rather than in the shuttle code also covers any other caller of `copyTurf()`/`CopyOnTop()`; only shuttle moves were exercised. This matches the issue discussion, which located the bug in moved turfs not copying the `burnt`/`broken` vars or calling `update_appearance`.

## Verification

The fix was built and tested with the same steps as the reproduction, using more damage:

- Three iron floors (broken or burnt), one broken material floor and two walls with two dents each were put on the mining shuttle, which was then moved.
- After the move all of them had kept their state: iron floors still `broken = 1` / `burnt = 1`, the material floor `broken = 1` with its custom material intact, and both walls two dents with their overlays.
- The round's `runtime.log` showed no runtimes.

Not checked:

- Doors and airlocks, which the issue mentions. They are movable atoms rather than turfs and were not tested; they are expected to move intact.
- A shuttle trip through hyperspace transit. Only direct moves between docks were done. Whether the hyperspace path behaves differently was not examined.
- The specific damage sprite. Each `update_appearance()` picks a random broken or burnt state again (`pick(broken_states())`), so a damaged floor can show a different variant of the damage sprite after the move. The damage is kept, but the exact sprite may differ.
- Other damaged turf types (plating, wood and so on) were not individually tested; only iron floors, one material floor and walls were.

## Notes

- #87234 and #96322 may be real in other conditions (for example the hyperspace trip, a different shuttle, or client-side rendering of material textures, which could not be inspected from the test server). They were not caused by the missing `broken`/`burnt`/`dent_decals` copy; with the pizza theme and custom materials, `copyTurf()` already carried the material and filter over correctly on master. They are not fixed by this change.
- The change is limited to `broken`, `burnt` and `dent_decals`; no other turf state was audited for being dropped by `copyTurf()`.
