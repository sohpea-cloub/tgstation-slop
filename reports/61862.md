# #61862: A deployed food cart breaks itself when a shuttle moves it

## Summary

A food cart that is unpacked (deployed) while standing on a shuttle destroys itself as soon as the shuttle moves. The cart watches each of its four deployed parts with `COMSIG_MOVABLE_MOVED` to detect a part being dragged away, but a shuttle move relocates every part, which fires that signal and makes the cart break and delete all of them. The fix makes the parts ignore their own moves while a shuttle is carrying them, then checks afterwards that each part is still next to the cart.

## The bug

Reported symptoms (#61862): after deploying a stocked cart on the emergency shuttle and launching, the cart packs itself up and drops every food item, and a redeploy attempt fails and leaves an invisible barrier. A comment says the same happens on the tram, and another comment points out that the invisible wall is the real exploit. #90364 reports the same thing with the evacuation shuttle jumping through hyperspace: the deployed cart's parts were removed and the cart "broke".

What happens in the code is that the cart is marked broken and its parts are deleted, but the cart object itself is still dense. A broken cart refuses to unpack ("completely busted"), so what remains is a dense, unusable object, which is the "invisible barrier".

## Reproduction

Performed on the Meta station map as an observer, using the Lua editor. The shuttle was moved with a direct dock call instead of a real launch (see below).

1. Using the Lua editor, find a floor turf inside the "Emergency Shuttle" area that has free, non-dense shuttle turfs to its east and to the north-east and south-east of that (the cart needs a 3-tile column east of itself).
2. Spawn `/obj/machinery/food_cart` on that turf and call `unpack()` on it. Optionally spawn a food item, such as a plain burger, on the fridge's tile.
3. Move the shuttle: `SSshuttle:moveShuttle('emergency', 'emergency_home', 0)`. This moves the shuttle from its CentCom dock to the station dock. A plain dock move was used because starting a real launch with `enterTransit()` failed on the test server (no transit dock had been assigned); the turf-move path is the same one launching and hyperspace use.
4. Read the cart's variables.

Evidence on master: the cart was packed (`unpacked` 0), `machine_stat` was 1 (`BROKEN`), `cart_griddle` was nil because the parts were deleted, and `density` was still 1. Trying to unpack it by hand is refused by `attack_hand` ("completely busted").

Not reproduced exactly: a real launch of the emergency shuttle, the tram, or a bluespace/hyperspace transit jump. The food items dropping from the fridge was inferred, not checked separately: the fridge is deleted with the break and its contents spill onto the floor.

## Cause

`unpack()` in `code/modules/food_and_drinks/machinery/food_cart.dm` registers `lost_part` on each deployed part (line 78):

```dm
thing.forceMove(T)
RegisterSignal(thing, COMSIG_MOVABLE_MOVED, PROC_REF(lost_part))
```

The intent is that dragging the tent, fridge, table or griddle away breaks the cart. `lost_part()` (line 112) unregisters the watches and calls `atom_break()`, which runs `pack_up()` and deletes all four parts (lines 122-132).

A shuttle move runs `/atom/movable/proc/onShuttleMove` for every movable on the shuttle (`code/modules/shuttle/mobile_port/shuttle_move_callbacks.dm:116`), and that proc calls `abstract_move(newT)`. This move fires `COMSIG_MOVABLE_MOVED` on each deployed part, so the cart concludes its parts were taken away and breaks. The cart itself keeps `density = TRUE` (line 8), so a broken cart still blocks movement.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 61862: food cart no longer breaks itself when a shuttle carries it" (also fixes #90364), in `food_cart.dm`:

- `unpack()` now also registers two shuttle signals on each part: `COMSIG_ATOM_BEFORE_SHUTTLE_MOVE` -> `part_before_shuttle_move()` and `COMSIG_ATOM_AFTER_SHUTTLE_MOVE` -> `part_after_shuttle_move()`.
- `part_before_shuttle_move()` unregisters `COMSIG_MOVABLE_MOVED` from that part, so the shuttle's relocation is not treated as the part being moved away.
- `part_after_shuttle_move()` runs once everything has been moved. If the part is deleted, nothing happens. If the part ended on a different z-level or more than 3 tiles from the cart, it calls `lost_part()` (the cart breaks as before). Otherwise it re-registers the `COMSIG_MOVABLE_MOVED` watch (with `override = TRUE`).
- `pack_up()` and `lost_part()` also unregister the two new signals, so no handlers are left behind. In `lost_part()` the four unregister calls now share one static signal list.

The alternative of removing the `MOVED` watch altogether was not chosen, since the break-when-dragged-away behavior is deliberate. The 3-tile limit is a rough check; parts are placed within 2 tiles of the cart when deployed (they are placed around the tile east of it).

## Verification

Checked in game on the rebuilt fix, with the same steps as above:

- The cart arrived at the station dock intact: `unpacked` 1, `machine_stat` 0, and all four parts present beside it.
- A second shuttle move (station to CentCom) also left the cart intact.
- `pack_up()` and `unpack()` still worked: when packed, the cart held its 4 parts, and re-unpacking put them back in place.
- Manually `forceMove`-ing the tent away still broke the cart, so the original safeguard works.
- No runtime errors involving the food cart appeared in the round's runtime log.

Not checked: the tram, a real launch from the shuttle console, an actual hyperspace transit (shuttle rotation or a move to a transit dock), a rotating shuttle move, and the dropped-food symptom after the fix.

## Notes

- #90364 describes a bluespace jump with the evacuation shuttle; the code path is the same shuttle move, so no separate fix was made.
- The cart's own state while the shuttle is moving (e.g. mobs interacting mid-transit) was not examined.
- Other objects use the same before/after shuttle-move signal pair to avoid reacting to shuttle moves (for example `code/datums/components/atom_mounted.dm` and the orbiter component), which is the pattern followed here.
