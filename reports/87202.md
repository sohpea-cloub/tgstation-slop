## Summary

The Nebulastation arrivals shuttle map (`_maps/shuttles/arrival/arrival_nebula.dmm`) has no locker on any of its latejoin chairs, so the map is not the cause. What can happen, as a commenter on the issue suspected, is that a locker moved onto a chair tile is still chosen as a spawn point, because latejoin spawn points are picked at random with no obstruction check. The branch adds `SSjob.pick_latejoin_tracker()`, which prefers spawn points not covered by a closet or other blocking object and falls back to any point if all are covered. This is a hardening change, not a fix to the map.

## The bug

The reporter said one of the Nebulastation arrivals shuttle spawn points was under a locker. A paraplegic character who spawns there is knocked off their wheelchair and cannot get back on it: they cannot open a locker while resting, and cannot drag themselves onto the chair while on the locker.

## Reproduction

The map as shipped did not reproduce the report; the behaviour behind the report was reproduced by placing a locker on a spawn chair.

1. On the test server, set the map to Nebulastation, start the round and use admin tools to open the Lua editor and SDQL2.
2. Check the arrivals shuttle for lockers on chair tiles. Result: the shuttle has 11 latejoin chairs, and the only closets are at two x offsets in the map file (189 and 195), none sharing a tile with a chair. SDQL2 on closets on the shuttle's z-level confirmed this. The shuttle map has not changed since it was created.
3. With the Lua editor, spawn a `/obj/structure/closet` on a chair tile, then call `SSjob.send_to_late_join()` on 40 new humans.
4. Result on master: 4 of the 40 landed on the locker's tile.

This reproduces the mechanism by calling the spawn procedure directly, not by a player joining. The paraplegic and wheelchair specifics were not tested.

## Cause

Latejoin spawn points ("trackers") are the arrivals shuttle chairs, or latejoin landmarks, and are chosen with a plain `pick()`:
- `code/controllers/subsystem/job.dm:759`: `destination = pick(latejoin_trackers)` in `send_to_late_join()`.
- `code/modules/jobs/job_types/_job.dm:515`: `return pick(SSjob.latejoin_trackers)` in `get_latejoin_spawn_point()`.

`/atom/proc/JoinPlayerHere()` (`job.dm:735`) then moves the new mob to the chosen tile. Nothing checks whether the tile is blocked, so a locker that someone dragged onto a chair makes players spawn on top of it.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 87202: latejoin spawns avoid arrivals chairs covered by a locker". It adds `/datum/controller/subsystem/job/proc/pick_latejoin_tracker()`, which skips any tracker whose turf is blocked (`is_blocked_turf(exclude_mobs = TRUE, ignore_atoms = list(tracker))`) or contains an `/obj/structure/closet`, and picks randomly among the rest. If every tracker is blocked (or none has a turf), it falls back to `pick(latejoin_trackers)`, the old behaviour. Both call sites above now use it. The closet check is separate because lockers are not always dense in a way `is_blocked_turf` reports; the reason is not recorded in the inputs.

The change applies to all maps, and to latejoin landmark trackers as well as chairs.

## Verification

On the test server with the fix, the arrivals shuttle was docked elsewhere that round, so the locker was placed on a different chair tile than in the first run. Over 60 `send_to_late_join()` calls with the locker on that tile, none landed on it; the rest spread across the other 10 chairs. (Without a locker, 8 of the 60 landed on that chair, so the filter was what moved them.)

Not checked: players joining normally through the lobby; the `get_latejoin_spawn_point()` call site; landmark-type trackers; paraplegic characters and wheelchairs.

## Notes

- The change is not needed to fix the map. Whether it is worth adding is a maintainer's call: it makes an obstructed spawn point less likely to be used, at the cost of a loop over all trackers on each latejoin.
- A comment on the issue (from a later date) already argued this was not a map bug but the lack of an obstruction check; the investigation agrees.
- The branch includes no test.
