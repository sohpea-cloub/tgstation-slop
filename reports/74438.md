## Summary

The 2023 report said a player flashed into the revolution stays lying down after the conversion stun ends. On current master, four of four test humans converted by a head revolutionary's flash stood up on their own 13-22 seconds later. The get-up path that now runs when the flash knockdown ends was not there when the issue was filed. No fix was written.

## The bug

As reported: after being flashed into the revolution, the player is knocked down and stunned; when the stun expires they do not get up and stay lying down until shaken or until they toggle rest on and off. The reporter guessed the cause was a `Stun` applied while the player was already paralyzed from the flash.

## Reproduction

Attempted on the test server using admin tools (the Lua editor to call procs directly). The conversion was triggered by calling the flash proc, not by a player using the flash in hand.

1. Start the round and delay the round end (the round ended right after starting with no crew).
2. Spawn a human and give it the head revolutionary antagonist datum (`mind.add_antag_datum(/datum/antagonist/rev/head)`), holding an `/obj/item/assembly/flash`.
3. Spawn a fresh human and give it a player (the conversion needs a client and a mind).
4. Call `flash_mob(victim, headrev, 15, TRUE)` on the flash (`code/modules/assembly/flash.dm:145`).
5. Observed: the victim became a revolutionary ("You are the Revolutionary") and was lying down, knocked down and stunned.
6. Poll the victim's posture. After 13-22 seconds it read "standing (no longer lying down or resting)".

This was repeated on four fresh humans with the same result each time. A control run, in which the victim's stamina was zeroed right after the flash to rule out stamina crit ending as the trigger, also ended standing at 13 seconds. Two runs also logged that the timed action had been interrupted (the get-up `do_after`), but the mob still ended up standing.

## Cause

There is no defect to point at on master. The relevant sequence:

- The flash applies a `Knockdown(rand(25, 50) * ...)` to the target (`code/modules/assembly/flash.dm:207`).
- Conversion applies a separate 10 second `Stun` (`code/modules/antagonists/revolution/revolution.dm:307`, in the stunning branch of the add-revolutionary code).
- When the knockdown ends while the stun is still running, `on_floored_end()` (`code/modules/mob/living/living.dm:2738`) calls `get_up()` (`code/modules/mob/living/living.dm:714`) unless the mob is resting.
- `get_up()` uses a `do_after` (1 second by default). Timed actions are only cancelled when `TRAIT_INCAPACITATED` is added during them (`code/datums/timed_actions.dm:78`), so the get-up completes even though the stun is still active.

The 2023 report predates this get-up path, which would explain why it no longer reproduces. This was inferred from the current code, not checked against the code of that time. The flash path in this code never applies `Paralyze` to humans, so the reporter's paralyze-then-stun theory does not apply here.

## Fix

None. A speculative change to the living-mob signal handlers was written during the investigation and reverted because it was not needed.

## Verification

Nothing to verify since no change was made. What was not established:

- The exact moment of standing (during the conversion stun or after it) was not recorded. Lua reads of `body_position` returned bogus values (always 0), so the timeline comes only from posture polls on the test server.
- The player-driven route (a head revolutionary using the flash on a player in hand) was not run; the proc was called directly.
- Only humans were tried. Other mob types and a mob that actually is Paralyzed and then Stunned were not tested.

## Notes

- The issue is probably closable as already fixed by the later get-up changes, but the exact upstream PR was not identified.
- The reporter's workarounds (being shaken, toggling rest) were not needed in any run.
