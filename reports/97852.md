## Summary

A verb declared with `src = usr.loc`, such as the MMI and positronic brain "Toggle Listening", was never listed in the stat panel of the mob inside the container. `client/proc/init_verbs()` collects verbs from the client, the mob and the mob's contents, but not from the atom the mob is inside. The fix adds the verbs of `mob.loc` when it is a movable atom. #74153 (preference buttons missing from the ghost OOC tab) was not reproduced and has no fix.

## #97852: verbs with `src = usr.loc`

### The bug

The issue says that the listening toggle cannot be used as an MMI. The verb is declared in `code/modules/mob/living/brain/processor/_processor.dm:60` as `GAME_VERB_SRC_DESC(/obj/item/brain_processor, Toggle_Listening, usr.loc, "Toggle Listening", ..., "MMI")`. The verb is registered by BYOND and the client knows it, but the game's stat panel never received it, so no "MMI" tab with "Toggle Listening" appeared.

### Reproduction

1. On the test server, join the round and open the Lua editor.
2. Spawn a `/obj/item/brain_processor/positronic`, create a `/mob/living/brain`, put it in the processor with `set_brainmob()`, and set the brainmob's `ckey` to your own so you take it over.
3. List the client's verbs with a filter for "Toggle Listening". The client has it: `Toggle Listening [MMI] {src in usr.loc}` on the positronic brain.
4. Read the stat panel's `init_verbs` message (through the browser window's message log). It contained no "MMI" tab and no "Toggle Listening" entry.

### Cause

`/client/proc/init_verbs()` (`code/modules/client/client_procs.dm`, starts at line 1136 on master) builds `verbstoprocess` from three sources:

```dm
var/list/verbstoprocess = verbs.Copy()
if(mob)
	verbstoprocess += mob.verbs
	for(var/atom/movable/thing as anything in mob.contents)
		verbstoprocess += thing.verbs
```

A `src = usr.loc` verb lives on the container, which is `mob.loc`, so none of these lists contain it. The `GAME_VERB_SRC` macro itself (`code/__DEFINES/verb.dm`) is fine: BYOND does register the verb.

### Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 97852: statpanel verb list ignores verbs of the container the mob is in", adds to `init_verbs()`:

```dm
if(ismovable(mob.loc))
	var/atom/movable/container = mob.loc
	verbstoprocess += container.verbs
```

### Verification

After rebuilding and repeating the steps above, the `init_verbs` payload had an "MMI" tab and ended with `["MMI","Toggle Listening"]`.

Not checked: the rendered stat panel (the tab is built by client-side JavaScript, which could not be viewed), actually clicking the verb, unit tests, and other `src = usr.loc` verbs.

### Notes

- The list is only rebuilt when `init_verbs()` runs (login, mob transfer, the Update Verbs action). A mob moved into a container later, without one of those, does not get the new verbs until the next call. This was not addressed.
- The tgui panel list (`verbs/init`) skips `src_based` verbs by design, so this fix affects only the classic stat panel.

## #74153: preferences buttons missing from the ghost OOC tab

### The report

From 2023: as a ghost, the OOC tab lacks "Open Game Preferences" and "Open Character Preferences", while living players have them. Comments suggest it happens after a disconnect and reconnect and that the buttons are meant to be in the OOC tab.

### What was checked

The verbs are native client verbs (`GAME_VERB_DESC` in `code/modules/client/preferences_menu.dm`), so they are always in `client.verbs`. On the test server as a ghost, before and after a disconnect and reconnect (same ghost mob), the client verb list contained both, and the `init_verbs` payload sent to the stat panel contained `["OOC","Open Game Preferences"]` and `["OOC","Open Character Preferences"]`. So the server sent the buttons both times.

### Verdict

Not reproduced. The verb system has been rewritten since the 2023 report. A leftover display glitch in the client-side stat panel (`html/statbrowser.js`) after a reconnect cannot be ruled out, because the rendered tab could not be inspected. No change was made.
