## Summary
Examining Keeki, the bread cat or the bread dog printed "She is a object made of meat." The ungrammatical "a object" came from the generic `atom/examine_descriptor()`, which no basic mob overrides. The fix adds `/mob/living/basic/examine_descriptor()` returning "creature", so the line reads "She is a creature made of meat." The word "meat" is intentional (see Notes), and a "cake" material would need a new `/datum/material`, so that part was not changed.

## The bug
The issue screenshot shows examine text on the cake cat saying it is "a object made of meat". Two things are wrong from the player's view: the mob is called an "object", and its material is meat although the description says it is made of cake.

## Reproduction
1. On the test server, start a round and open the Lua editor.
2. Create `/mob/living/basic/pet/cat/cak` (Keeki) and call its `examine()` with your own mob as the user. Read the returned lines.
3. Observed on master: "She is a object made of meat." The bread cat (`breadcat`) gave identical output.
4. For comparison, a corgi and a bear were created the same way. They had no material line at all, because they have no `custom_materials`.

This was reproduced by calling `examine()` through Lua rather than by clicking Examine as a player, though that call is what produces the text.

## Cause
`atom/examine_descriptor()` at `code/game/atom/atom_examine.dm:127` returns "object". Only items, structures, machinery, robots and turfs override it, so every mob falls through to the default. The material text comes from `examine_post_descriptor()` (`code/game/atom/atom_examine.dm:131`), which appends "made of X" when the atom has `custom_materials`. Keeki (`keeki.dm:25`), the bread cat (`cat.dm:177`) and the bread dog (`dog_subtypes.dm:97`) set `custom_materials` to meat times 3, with a comment saying this is "to match crafting". Crafting (`_crafting.dm:328`) sums the materials of the three meat slabs used, so crafted ones really end up with meat.

## Fix
One commit, "Fix issue 93452: basic mobs made of materials examine as "object"", adds to `code/modules/mob/living/basic/basic.dm`:

```dm
/mob/living/basic/examine_descriptor(mob/user)
	return "creature"
```

Overriding at the basic-mob level was chosen over changing the three affected types, since any basic mob given `custom_materials` would otherwise hit the same wording.

## Verification
After rebuilding and restarting the test server, `examine()` was called through Lua again: Keeki and the bread cat returned "She is a creature made of meat."; the corgi and bear output was unchanged. Not checked: the bread dog specifically, other basic mobs, or examining by clicking as a player.

## Notes
- "Made of meat" is by design: the materials match what the crafting recipes produce. Making the text say "cake" would need a new material (none exists for cake or bread), which is a design call and was not attempted.
- The generic fallback "object" is still used by other mobs (e.g. simple animals or other mob types) if they gain `custom_materials`; only basic mobs were covered.
