## Summary

#88863 reports that dropping the desynchronizer while desynced leaves the user's camera stuck where they desynced once the timer ends. This could not be reproduced: after the timer ran out the client's eye was back on the mob. #88862 reports that a desynced user is protected from most damage but is still gibbed by an explosion centered on their tile, and still takes pressure damage. Both were reproduced on master. The desync pocket (`/obj/effect/abstract/sync_holder`) was missing `PREVENT_CONTENTS_EXPLOSION_1` and passed the turf's air through to its contents. The fix sets the flag and gives the pocket its own safe air.

## The bug

### #88863: camera stuck after dropping the desynchronizer

The reporter's account: desync with a short timer, drop the desynchronizer while desynced, wait for the timer to expire, and the camera stays at the place where the user desynced.

### #88862: desynced users are not fully protected

While desynced the user is immune to most damage, but the reporter found that an explosion centered on the user's tile gibs them (or, in a bomb suit, kills them by damage), and that pressure (and probably temperature) damage still applies. They consider this inconsistent.

## Reproduction

### #88863 (not reproduced)

On master, as an admin using the Lua editor on a running round:

1. Possess a human and give it a desynchronizer. Set its `duration` to 100 (10 s) in one run and 60 (6 s) in another.
2. Use the desynchronizer in hand to desync.
3. Drop the item while desynced (the drop key).
4. Wait for the timer to expire.

Observed: the user and the item both reappeared on the turf and movement worked. Reading the client with the Lua editor showed `client.eye` was the mob, `client.perspective` was 0 (`MOB_PERSPECTIVE`), and the eye's x and y equalled the mob's (108,144). There was no stuck camera. This was checked from the server's view of the client only; no one looked at the actual client screen.

### #88862 (reproduced)

On master, using the Lua editor:

1. Give a human a desynchronizer with a long duration (600 = 60 s) and use it, so the human's `loc` is the desynchronized pocket.
2. Call `explosion(turf, 1, 2, 4)` on the human's tile (a turf argument was needed; a call with a null origin only produced a runtime).
3. The chat showed "You have died" and the mob was gibbed.

Pressure was also checked in the second test below, since it needs an air difference between the desynced user and the surrounding turf.

## Cause

### #88863

No defect was found. When a living mob with a client is moved, `/mob/living/forceMove` calls `reset_perspective()` (`code/modules/mob/living/living.dm:1967`, the call is at the end of the proc), which puts the client's eye back on the mob (`/mob/proc/reset_perspective`, `code/modules/mob/mob.dm:507`). When the timer ends `resync()` deletes the pocket (`code/game/objects/items/devices/desynchronizer.dm:61`), and `/obj/effect/abstract/sync_holder/Destroy` (`desynchronizer.dm:94`) force-moves everything in it out, including the user. A dropped desynchronizer also ends up in the pocket, because the pocket's `AllowDrop()` returns TRUE (`desynchronizer.dm:100`), so it is released at the same time. The original cause of the reported behavior was not identified; it may be version-dependent or need a different sequence than the one tried.

### #88862

Two separate reasons.

Explosions: for the explosion's epicenter turf, `/datum/controller/subsystem/explosions/proc/explode` collects the contents of every atom on the turf unless that atom has `PREVENT_CONTENTS_EXPLOSION_1`:

```dm
if(explode == epicenter) // Ensures explosives detonating from bags trigger other explosives in that bag
	var/list/items = list()
	for(var/atom/holder as anything in explode)
		if (length(holder.contents) && !(holder.flags_1 & PREVENT_CONTENTS_EXPLOSION_1))
			items += holder.get_all_contents(ignore_flag_1 = PREVENT_CONTENTS_EXPLOSION_1)
```
(`code/controllers/subsystem/explosions.dm:434-437`)

The desync pocket sits on the user's turf and did not have the flag, so the user inside it was treated as an item on the epicenter and exploded at the full severity. Other holders that keep their contents safe, such as `/obj/effect/dummy/phased_mob`, set the flag.

Pressure and temperature: `/mob/living/Life` takes the environment from `loc.return_air()` (`code/modules/mob/living/life.dm:56`) and passes it to `handle_environment`. The pocket did not define `return_air()`, so it used the default `/atom/proc/return_air`, which returns `loc?.return_air()` (`code/game/atom/_atom.dm:403`): the air of the turf the pocket is on. A desynced user therefore felt that turf's pressure and temperature.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 88862: desynchronized users are immune to epicenter explosions and turf pressure", in `code/game/objects/items/devices/desynchronizer.dm`, on `/obj/effect/abstract/sync_holder`:

- `flags_1 = PREVENT_CONTENTS_EXPLOSION_1`, so explosions on the pocket's turf do not recurse into it.
- A `return_air()` override that returns `SSair.parse_gas_string(OPENTURF_DEFAULT_ATMOS)`: a fresh copy of standard air on each call, so that breathing inside the pocket cannot use it up. The pocket gives the user safe air, so pressure and temperature damage no longer apply.

The veil subtype of the pocket (used by the existential crisis brain trauma, `code/datums/brain_damage/special.dm`) inherits both changes. No change was made for #88863.

## Verification

After rebuilding and starting a new round on the test server:

- Pressure: two humans stood on hallway tiles whose air had been removed (the Lua editor removed air from both tiles repeatedly for a few seconds). The control human on the first tile ended at health 92.5. The human in the desync pocket on the second tile stayed at 100 health. The pocket's `return_air()` pressure was about 101.3 kPa.
- Explosion: `explosion(turf, 2, 3, 5)` on the desynced human's tile left them at health 100, `stat` 0 and still inside the pocket, with no gib.

Not checked: #88863 on the real client screen (only the server-side eye was read); explosions that are not centered on the user's tile (the reporter already says those do not harm a desynced user); temperature damage from fire; a bomb-suit case; the veil subtype in game.

## Notes

- The air override is an addition beyond the explosion fix. The reporter only says they "assume" pressure damage is a bug (and think temperature is as well), so a maintainer may want to decide whether a desynced user should be fully protected from the environment, or only from explosions. Breathing in the pocket uses the standard station mix.
- The change is shared with the veil subtype used by the existential crisis brain trauma, which now also ignores epicenter explosions and turf air.
- #88863 may be worth closing as not reproducible unless the reporter can show a stuck camera on current master.
