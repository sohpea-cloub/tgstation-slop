## Summary

The 2020 report said that a player with a voice of god source who transformed (shapeshifted) could still see their original body speak the commands. On master, the shapeshift status effect hands the caster's body-bound actions to the new form, and the original body is held in stasis with life processing disabled, so the voice of god is spoken by the shape and the original body stays silent. This was confirmed in game with a polar bear shapeshift. A related gap, unrelated to the reported bug, was seen: while shapeshifted, the Godwoken Syndrome trauma and the colossus vocal cords do nothing.

## The bug

The issue (from a local test, no round ID) says: have voice of god or Godwoken Syndrome and a way to transform, transform, command people to say their names, and the old body says its name. A screenshot is attached but its content is not available here, so only the text was followed.

## Reproduction

An attempt was made on master with admin tools on the test server, using the Lua editor from a ghost:

1. Spawned a human ("Orig Wizard"), gave it a `voice_of_god` spell targeted at the human and a polar bear shapeshift spell (`/spell/shapeshift/polar_bear`), and triggered the shapeshift on the human. The human became a "magic polar bear".
2. Checked the voice of god spell: its `owner` was now the polar bear, so the body-bound spell had been regranted to the shape.
3. Set the spell's `command` to "SAY YOUR NAME" and called its `cast` on its owner. Chat showed `The magic polar bear says, "SAY YOUR NAME"`; the original human said nothing. The game log contained `(magic polar bear) said 'SAY YOUR NAME' with Voice of God, affecting ...` and no line for the original body.
4. For the Godwoken Syndrome trauma, created two humans without clients, both with the trauma, and shapeshifted one of them. Over about five minutes the unshifted control fired three times (game log entries "forced by Godwoken Syndrome"). The shapeshifted one never fired, and neither did its hidden original body.

Notes on method: the voice of god spell was cast by calling its `cast` proc from the Lua editor, so the text prompt (`before_cast`) and normal button use were not exercised. The five-minute Godwoken check is probabilistic (see Cause), so "never fired" is consistent with, but not a proof of, a block.

## Cause

Why the reported behaviour is gone:

- `/datum/status_effect/shapechange_mob/from_spell/on_apply` (`code/modules/spells/spell_types/shapeshift/_shape_status.dm:171-191`) regrants the shapeshift spell to the new shape and then every action on the caster whose `target` is the caster (`:186-189`) to the shape, so those actions' `owner` is the shape. `restore_caster` (`:193-211`) grants them back. `/datum/action/cooldown/spell/voice_of_god/cast` (`code/modules/spells/spell_types/self/voice_of_god.dm:35-38`) speaks as `cast_on`, which is the spell's owner, so the shape speaks.
- The status effect puts `TRAIT_NO_TRANSFORM` on the hidden original body (`_shape_status.dm:40`) and `/mob/living/proc/Life` returns before processing for any mob with that trait (`code/modules/mob/living/life.dm:41`). Godwoken Syndrome's `on_life` (`code/datums/brain_damage/special.dm:16`, 2% chance per tick to speak) therefore never runs from the original body.

The shapechange status effect is believed to come from upstream PR #69091. That was not checked against history (the local clone is shallow).

The remaining gap is not the reported bug. Because Godwoken Syndrome acts from the original body's `on_life`, which is skipped, it does nothing while the player is shapeshifted. The colossus vocal cords are an organ action whose `target` is the organ, not the body, so the regrant loop does not move it and it does not work through the new form either. The test only observed the lack of effect; the organ case was concluded from reading the code.

## Fix

None. No commit was made.

## Verification

Not applicable as a fix. The negative result (the shape speaks, the original body does not) was the check on master.

## Notes

- The issue can probably be closed as already fixed. The earlier report assumed the original body acted; on master it cannot, because its Life processing is skipped for the duration of the transformation.
- Whether Godwoken Syndrome and the colossus vocal cords should work through a shapeshifted form is a separate design question; the current behaviour is "nothing happens" rather than "the original body speaks".
- Only the polar bear shapeshift was tested; other transformations that do not go through the shapechange status effect (for example a wabbajack or a mindswap) were not tried.
