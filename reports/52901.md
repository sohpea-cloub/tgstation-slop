## Summary

#52901 reports that the ash drake's lava arena does not turn its lava back to ground in time, so the last attack cannot be avoided, with a `null.copy_from()` runtime from `ChangeTurf`. Three arena runs on the test server left no lava behind and produced no runtime. By reading the code, the arena has a 1.4 s margin between one round's lava reverting and the next round's lava appearing, so a stuck turf would need server lag, which fits the issue comment that connects the problem to a lagging server. #90008 (fire breath keeps going while the drake is flying) could not be tested: the swoop did not start in the one attempt. Reading the code shows the fire-breath actions never check whether the drake is swooping. No fix was written for either issue.

## The bug

#52901 (reported in 2020, with a later comment from 2024): during the lava arena attack (the enraged ash drake, below half health, swoops over a player and encases them in a ring of fire), some arena turfs are said to stay lava instead of reverting to basalt before the next round. The player then has no safe tile, and the board shows a patchwork of lava and ground. A 2024 comment adds a runtime seen when an arena was created (`null.copy_from()` inside `/turf/open/ChangeTurf`) and says the author had also seen this under heavy server lag.

#90008: when a player dodges inside the arena, the drake is in flight. If it had started the all-directions fire breath before or around the swoop, the breath is said to keep hitting the player (one to three hits, "very consistent"), especially after the player teleports out of the arena and the drake pulls them back in. A commenter describes the same thing as the drake starting an attack and going into flight mid-cast. The reporter was unsure whether this is a bug or intended.

## Reproduction

Neither issue was reproduced. What was tried, using the Lua editor on the test server:

1. Spawned an ash drake on the mining level next to a godmode human controlled by the tester. The drake's AI stayed idle and never picked a target on its own, so its attacks were started by hand.
2. Lowered the drake's health to about 1100 of 2500 (via `adjust_brute_loss`; setting `health` directly was overwritten) so it counted as enraged, and set `lava_swoop.enraged = true`.
3. Started the arena from Lua with `drake.swooping = 0; drake.lava_swoop.enraged = true; drake.lava_swoop:Trigger(nil, 0, me)`. Calling `lava_swoop:Activate` directly skips the ability started/finished signals and leaves `swooping` stuck at 2, so it was not used for the measurements.
4. After each run, counted `/turf/open/lava` turfs in a 13x13 square around the arena, at once and again after about 30 s, and read the runtime log.

Results for #52901:

- Three arena runs. The drake swooped and "encases you in an arena of fire!" was printed. Roughly 144 turfs became lava during the runs and all of them reverted.
- The count of lava turfs in the area was the same 12 before and after each run. Those 12 (x 103-106, y 94-97) are an ordinary lava pool that was already there.
- No runtime appeared in the round's runtime log, and in particular no `null.copy_from()`.
- The runs were not made under artificial lag, and no player was walking around inside the arena, so the issue's lag scenario was not tested.

Results for #90008, which are inconclusive:

- A fire cone was started with a zero-delay timer and the swoop was triggered 0.5 s later. The fire cone ran (8 fire hotspots at 0.5 s, none from 1.0 s on), but `swooping` stayed 0, so the drake never took off. The most likely reason is that the swoop was still on its cooldown from the earlier runs, but this was not confirmed.
- The overlap between a breath and a swoop was therefore never observed.

## Cause

No defect was demonstrated. The following is from reading the code at master.

#52901, arena timing. `lava_arena()` in `code/datums/actions/mobs/lava_swoop.dm:135-189` runs three rounds. Each round creates a `/obj/effect/temp_visual/lava_warning` on every arena turf that is not a safe tile (`lava_swoop.dm:182-186`), then waits `SLEEP_CHECK_DEATH(2.4 SECONDS, owner)` (`lava_swoop.dm:188`). `lava_warning/fall()` in `code/modules/mob/living/simple_animal/hostile/megafauna/drake.dm:238-266` sleeps for the warning's `duration` of 13 ds, then turns the turf into lava and schedules the reset:

```dm
var/lava_turf = /turf/open/lava/smooth
var/reset_turf = our_turf.type
our_turf.TerraformTurf(lava_turf, flags = CHANGETURF_INHERIT_AIR)
addtimer(CALLBACK(our_turf, TYPE_PROC_REF(/turf, ChangeTurf), reset_turf, null, CHANGETURF_INHERIT_AIR), reset_time, TIMER_OVERRIDE|TIMER_UNIQUE)
```

With the default `reset_time` of 10 ds, lava appears 1.3 s after the warning, reverts at 2.3 s, and the next round's lava appears at 2.4 + 1.3 = 3.7 s: a 1.4 s gap. In the last round the revert (2.3 s) comes 0.1 s before `lava_arena()` returns (2.4 s). Under normal timing nothing overlaps.

The code has one weak point that matters only if timing slips. `fall()` computes `can_transform_turf = !isclosedturf(our_turf) && !islava(our_turf)` (`drake.dm:243`). A turf that is still lava when the next round falls is not transformed again and gets no new reset timer, only a "set ablaze" fireball effect (`drake.dm:259-261`). If the first revert were late, for example under heavy lag, that turf would stay lava until something else reverted it. This is consistent with the 2024 comment that the problem was seen under heavy server lag. It was not shown in the test.

Also, the 2020 runtime is not present in the current code in that form. `/turf/open/ChangeTurf` now contains `stashed_air.copy_from(air)` (`code/game/turfs/change_turf.dm:215`), and `ChangeTurf` returns the existing turf rather than a replaced one. The `null.copy_from()` runtime from the issue was not seen on this build.

#90008, fire breath during flight. The fire-breath actions in `code/datums/actions/mobs/fire_breath.dm` do their work asynchronously and never check whether the owner is swooping. `progressive_fire_line()` (`fire_breath.dm:43-52`) loops over a precomputed list of turfs with `sleep(fire_delay)` between them, checking only that the owner is alive. `mass_fire.attack_sequence()` (`fire_breath.dm:113-118`) queues three `fire_spin()` calls on timers at 0, 2.5 and 5 s (`breath_delay = 2.5 SECONDS`, `total_spins = 3`), and each `fire_spin()` (`fire_breath.dm:121-128`) builds its lines from the owner's position at that moment. `Activate()` returns as soon as the lines are queued (`fire_breath.dm:22-27`), so the drake's other abilities are available again while the breath is still running. In the drake's `OpenFire()` (`drake.dm:107-132`) the check `if(swooping) return` only stops a new attack from starting during a swoop. Together these mean that a breath started before a swoop would not stop when the drake takes off. For `mass_fire`, later spins would start from wherever the drake is then (the swoop moves the drake onto its target). `arena_escape_enrage()` (`drake.dm:154-166`) also calls `mass_fire.Activate(target)` directly when the target escapes the arena. This matches the issue's description, but it is a reading of the code, not an observation. Whether a swoop actually starts while spin timers are pending was not tested.

## Fix

None. No commit was made for either issue.

For #52901, no defect was found that a fix could target. A speculative hardening (a single reset timer per turf, or a longer gap between rounds) was considered and not written, because the failure was not observed and the cause is unknown. For #90008, the likely change would be to make the fire-breath loops stop, or skip burning, while the owner has `SWOOPING_TRAIT` or `swooping` set, or to make the swoop wait for an active breath. That was not written because the behavior was not confirmed and the intended design is unclear.

## Verification

Nothing was verified in game beyond the negative results above: three arena runs left no stray lava and caused no runtime. What was not checked: arena behavior under server lag or heavy CPU load; a real player moving, dying, or teleporting out of the arena during the attack; the arena started by the drake's own AI instead of by hand; combinations with the lava-pool attack (whose lava lasts 60 ds) overlapping the arena; and any case of a fire breath running while a swoop is in progress.

## Notes

- #90008 may be intended behavior or at least a design question: the reporter and a commenter both say they are not sure it is a bug. The two issues were grouped because both concern attack sequencing of the same drake.
- A better test for #90008 would wait until the swoop cooldown is over, start the all-directions breath with `mass_fire`, then let the drake's own `OpenFire()` pick a swoop (or trigger it by hand after clearing the cooldown), and watch for burns on a dodging player while `swooping` is non-zero.
- The test relied on the Lua editor to call `Trigger`. Calling the ability through SDQL (`CALL lava_swoop.Trigger(...)`) did nothing.
