## Summary

Three unrelated reagent bugs were handled together, with one commit each. (1) `/datum/reagents/proc/metabolize` only pulled the reagents toward the body's temperature and never the reverse, so hot or cold reagents could not warm or chill the mob. The fix adds the missing body-side heat exchange. (2) Concentrated barber's aid sets the head's `HEAD_HAIR` flag on hairless species but leaves their hairstyle at "Bald", so the new hair is invisible. The fix gives such mobs "Very Long Hair". (3) Teslium zaps start from the grenade, and `tesla_zap` looks for targets with `oview()`, which sees nothing from inside a box, bag or hand. The fix starts the zap from the turf when the source is in a container.

## #75896: reagent temperature does not affect the body

### The bug
Injecting or ingesting very hot or very cold reagents leaves the mob's body temperature unchanged. Only the opposite direction works: the mob's temperature changes the reagents' temperature, so temperature-dependent reagents react to the body, not the other way around.

### Reproduction
On master, using the Lua editor on the test server:
1. Spawn a human.
2. Add 30u of water at 700 K: `reagents:add_reagent(water, 30, nil, 700)`. Repeat with a second human at 100 K.
3. Call `reagents:metabolize(mob, 2, 0, false)` four times on each (the procs were called directly rather than waiting for life ticks), printing `bodytemperature` and `reagents.chem_temp` after each call.

Observed: body temperature stayed at 310.15 K in both cases, while the reagent temperature dropped from 700 to 433 K and rose from 100 to 242 K. The reagents' heat disappeared.

### Cause
`code/modules/reagents/chemistry/holder/mob_life.dm:19` is the only temperature handling in `metabolize`:

```dm
if(owner)
	expose_temperature(owner.bodytemperature, 0.25)
```

This moves the reagents 25% of the way to the body temperature each call. Nothing adjusts the body.

### Fix
Commit "Fix issue 75896: reagent temperature now exchanges heat with the body" adds two defines in `code/__DEFINES/reagents_specific_heat.dm` and changes `metabolize`. Before the existing `expose_temperature` call, if the holder is not `NO_REACT` and has reagents, the body is moved by `heat_capacity() * (chem_temp - bodytemperature) * BODY_REAGENT_EXCHANGE_COEFF / BODY_HEAT_CAPACITY` (via `adjust_bodytemperature`, with `min_temp = 0`). The existing call now uses `BODY_REAGENT_EXCHANGE_COEFF` (0.25, unchanged value). `BODY_HEAT_CAPACITY` is 20000.

### Verification
Same procedure, after the fix (body / reagent temperature after each of four calls):
- 700 K water: body 339.4, 359.3, 373.0, 382.3; chem 609, 546, 502, 472.
- 100 K water: body 285.5, 266.9, 252.4, 240.7; chem 148, 179, 199, 211.
- 310 K water (control): body 310.15 → 310.12, essentially unchanged.

Not checked: real life ticks, other ways of getting reagents into the body (patches, injection by syringe), and effects on gameplay balance. It was also not checked whether the issue's question about the Seiver is affected.

### Notes
- `BODY_HEAT_CAPACITY` is a balance guess, not derived from anything. With it, 30u of 700 K water raises the body about 29 K on the first tick, and 10u of ~350 K liquid raises it about 1 K. Maintainers should tune it.
- The issue says the reverse direction was once fixed only for Herignis (#71012); this change is general.

## #79304: concentrated barber's aid on hairless species

### The bug
Players report that 100% purity concentrated barber's aid prints the hair-growth messages on lizards and moths but no hair appears. In the issue thread, the author of the feature explained that growth is delayed and that the character must change hairstyle at a mirror; reporters confirmed it only worked after combining drinking, a shower and a mirror.

### Reproduction
On master, using the Lua editor:
1. Spawn a lizard and add 15u of 100%-purity concentrated barber's aid. Run `metabolize` 25 times.
2. With the lizard's hairstyle set to anything but "Bald" (the pref value was "Parted"), the head's flags went 124 → 125 (`HEAD_HAIR` set) and 2 hair overlays appeared. So "never works" is not accurate.
3. Repeat with a lizard whose hairstyle is "Bald" (set with `set_hairstyle`, matching what a hairless species keeps by default). Result: flags 125, hairstyle Bald, 0 hair overlays: the hair exists but is invisible.

### Cause
`code/modules/reagents/chemistry/reagents/other_reagents.dm:2549` (`concentrated_barbers_aid/on_mob_life`) sets `head.head_flags |= HEAD_HAIR` and calls `update_body_parts()`, but never touches the hairstyle. Species without hair keep "Bald", so nothing is drawn until the player picks a style, which matches the thread's mirror workaround.

### Fix
Commit "Fix issue 79304: concentrated barber's aid hair growth is visible on hairless species" sets the hairstyle to "Very Long Hair" (what the reagent's `expose_mob` already uses) when it is "Bald", just before `update_body_parts()`. It sits in the branch that is skipped for `TRAIT_BALD`, so bald-trait characters stay bald.

### Verification
Bald-hairstyle lizard after the fix: flags 125, hairstyle "Very Long Hair", 2 hair overlays. Not checked: moths, the bald quirk removal, application by shower/spray, or the delay described in the thread.

### Notes
- Side finding, not fixed: `expose_mob` in barber's aid, concentrated barber's aid and baldium compute `min(100 - (touch_protection * 100), 0, 100)` (e.g. `other_reagents.dm:2538`, `:2583`). That is always ≤ 0, so touch protection never matters; `max` was probably intended.
- The thread's other complaints (the delay, the need to drink and shower) are by design or unconfirmed here.

## #88179: teslium grenades

### The bug
A grenade with 75u teslium and 100u water prints "The teslium starts to spark..." and plays a sound but shocks no one. The reporter had the grenade in a box; a commenter guessed the container was the cause.

### Reproduction
On master, using the Lua editor:
1. Build a large chem grenade with two bluespace beakers (75u teslium, 100u water), set `stage = 3`, and call `arm_grenade(nil, 20, false)`.
2. On the floor with humans nearby: chat showed "... was shocked by the large grenade!" and chain shocks. This works.
3. Repeat with the grenade inside an `/obj/item/storage/box`, victim next to it: only the "starts to spark" line and the sound; no shock lines, victim health 100.

(Grenade priming used the proc directly, not the in-game activation.)

### Cause
`code/modules/reagents/chemistry/recipes/pyrotechnics.dm:628`, `zappy_zappy`, passes the reagent holder's atom as `tesla_zap` source. `tesla_zap` finds targets with `oview(zap_range+2, source)` (`code/modules/power/tesla/energy_ball.dm:249`), which is empty when the source's location is a container, not a turf.

### Fix
Commit "Fix issue 88179: teslium reactions zap from inside containers" uses the holder atom as the source if its `loc` is a turf, otherwise `get_turf(holder_atom)`. The sound still plays at the original atom.

### Verification
With the fix, the boxed grenade produced "was shocked by" chain lines, falls and defib-zap sounds. Evidence is the chat output; the victim's health was read back as 100 only once and is not conclusive (the same read in the unfixed run was also 100, and may have been taken before the zap timers fired). Not checked: a grenade in a bag or hand, a beaker in a grenade in a box, or the live-server report that could not be reproduced by a maintainer.

### Notes
- The change is in the teslium reaction code (`pyrotechnics.dm`); other fixes touching it may conflict.
- MrMelbert noted the zap powers may not have been rebalanced after recent power changes; not examined.
