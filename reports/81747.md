# #81747: Polymorphing into a mimic crate clones the caster when the crate opens

## Summary

When a player shapeshifts into a mimic crate and the crate opens, a copy of their original body drops out while their mind is still in the crate. Reverting later moves that body back, replacing the clone's state. The cause is that the shapechange status effect stores the original mob inside the shape's contents, and the crate dumps all of its contents when it opens. The fix stores the original mob in nullspace for the duration of the shapechange. The report's second claim, that people inside the crate are deleted on revert, is already fixed on master by an earlier change.

## The bug

From the issue: after polymorphing into a mimic crate, opening the latch makes the player's "clone" pop out. Reverting then takes the clone's place, including any damage it took, which can leave the player crippled. Separately, prone people swallowed by the crate were reported deleted from reality when the player reverted.

## Reproduction

On master, using the Lua editor as an observer:

1. Spawn a human (the caster) and a `/mob/living/basic/mimic/crate`.
2. Apply the base `/datum/status_effect/shapechange_mob` with the crate as owner and the human as caster.
3. Read the human's `loc` while shifted: it was the crate.
4. Call `toggle_open()` on the crate. The human's `loc` became the floor, so the original body popped out as a clone while the mind stayed in the crate. This matches the report.

This used the status effect directly, not the polymorph belt.

## Cause

`/datum/status_effect/shapechange_mob/on_apply()` in `code/modules/spells/spell_types/shapeshift/_shape_status.dm:39` calls `caster_mob.forceMove(owner)`, putting the original mob in the shape's contents. Any shape that dumps or consumes its contents ejects the caster. The mimic crate's `toggle_open` (`code/modules/mob/living/basic/ruin_defender/mimic/mimic.dm:174-178`) releases every atom inside it, and bileworms do something similar. When the status effect ends, `restore_caster()` (`_shape_status.dm:83`) moves that body back to where the player is.

The deletion half is a different path. `from_spell` and `restore_caster` now move all contents of the shape, except the caster, to the turf before the shape is deleted. That is the upstream change #81528, "Prevents polymorph using specific mobs for deleting items/people". It was in place on master and this was confirmed (see Verification).

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 81747: store the shapeshifted mob in nullspace instead of the shape":

- `on_apply()` calls `caster_mob.moveToNullspace()` instead of `forceMove(owner)`, so no content-dumping shape can eject the caster.
- `on_remove()` used to rely on the caster being inside the owner's contents, so that deleting the owner would delete the caster too. With the caster in nullspace that no longer holds, so if the owner was deleted before the caster was restored, `on_remove()` now deletes the stored caster (when it was not already restored and still exists). This keeps the old outcome for a shape deleted outright.
- `code/modules/unit_tests/spell_shapeshift.dm` relied on the caster's `loc` being the shape. It now finds the shape through the spell's owner using a new helper, `shapeshift_shape_of()`, and also asserts that the caster's `loc` is null while shifted and non-null after reverting.

An alternative would be to special-case each shape that ejects contents, as was done with a flag for other mimic subtypes in #78706, but that leaves every future content-dumping shape vulnerable. Nullspace storage fixes the class. A closed upstream PR (#89591, never merged) took the same approach. This fix does not include that PR's `QDEL_NULL(caster_mob.ai_controller)` change, which concerns inheriting AI for disconnected players and is separate.

## Verification

After rebuilding on the test server with the fix:

- Real spell path: with the wizard shapeshift spell (`/datum/action/cooldown/spell/shapeshift/wizard`) set to a mimic crate shape, triggering it on a human left the human's `loc` as null. Opening and closing the crate left `loc` null, and no clone appeared.
- With a prone victim moved inside the closed crate, calling `restore_caster()` on the status effect put the caster on the floor and the victim on the same floor tile. The victim was ejected, not deleted.
- Shape death (`death()` on the shape) restored and killed the caster as before.
- Unit tests were built with focus on `shapeshift_spell`, `shapeshift_holoparasites` and `shapeshift_health` and passed ("Success!"). The spell casts appeared in the game log.

Not checked: the polymorph belt's own path (its channelled scan and transform, and `on_type_change`), the full unit-test suite, other shapes that dump contents such as bileworms, a real player opening the crate through the client, and the report's claim of moving sideways after limb loss and replacement.
