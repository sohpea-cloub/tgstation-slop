## Summary
A pAI in kitten, puppy or spider form can be picked up and worn on the head, but `icons/mob/clothing/head/pai_head.dmi` had no state for those chassis, so nothing was drawn. The fix adds `kitten`, `puppy` and `spider` states to that file, and the missing `frog`, `kitten`, `puppy` and `spider` in-hand states to `pai_item_lh.dmi` and `pai_item_rh.dmi`. The new sprites were generated by script from the holoform art, not hand-drawn, so they should be reviewed by a spriter.

## The bug
Picking up a pAI that is a kitten and putting it on your head makes it invisible. A comment on the issue asked whether other holoforms lack worn icons too; they do, and the in-hand sprites for some chassis were missing as well.

## Reproduction
1. Start a round, observe, and open the Lua editor.
2. Spawn a human and a `/mob/living/silicon/pai`. Call `fold_out(true)` on the pAI, then `set_holochassis('kitten')`.
3. Call `mob_pickup(human)` on the pAI, then equip the resulting holder to the human's head slot with `equip_to_slot_if_possible(holder, 64, true, true)`.
4. Observed: the holder's worn icon was `icons/mob/clothing/head/pai_head.dmi` and its in-hand icon state was `kitten`. Rendering the human with the admin render tool reported "parts naming an icon_state their icon lacks: nothing drawn" twice (the worn state and one other part), and the hat was not drawn.

This used direct proc calls to pick up and equip rather than the player actions.

## Cause
`mob_pickup` (`code/modules/mob/living/living.dm:2022`) builds a holder using the pAI's `held_state` as the icon state. The pAI sets `held_state = "[chassis]"` (`code/modules/pai/pai.dm:267`). `possible_chassis` (`pai.dm:101`) allows kitten, puppy and spider to be picked up, but `pai_head.dmi` only contained `repairbot`, `cat`, `mouse`, `monkey`, `rabbit`, `duffel`, `crow` and `frog`. `pai_item_lh.dmi` and `pai_item_rh.dmi` also lacked `frog`, `kitten`, `puppy` and `spider`, so those forms were invisible in hands as well.

## Fix
One commit, **"Fix issue 83371: add pAI kitten, puppy and spider worn sprites"**, changes three binary icon files and no code:
- `icons/mob/clothing/head/pai_head.dmi`: new `kitten`, `puppy`, `spider` states, 4 directions each.
- `icons/mob/inhands/pai_item_lh.dmi` and `pai_item_rh.dmi`: new `frog`, `kitten`, `puppy`, `spider` states.

The sprites were derived by script from the holoform art in `icons/mob/silicon/pai.dmi`, scaled to 55% and aligned to the existing `cat` sprite's position. Adding states is the only option without code changes, since the holder looks the chassis name up directly. The DMI state lists were rewritten by script.

## Verification
After rebuilding (0 errors), the same steps drew a black kitten on the human's head. The render tool's "icon_state missing" count dropped from two to one; the remaining one is not the hat.

Not checked in game: puppy, spider and frog forms, and any in-hand state. Those were checked only by looking at the sprite sheet images. The result is not hand-drawn pixel art.

## Notes
- The sprites need review by a spriter; hand-drawn art would be nicer than the scaled copies.
- Side finding, not touched: the north frame of the `spider` state in `pai.dmi` has an opaque grey (#c0c0c0) background, an existing art bug.
