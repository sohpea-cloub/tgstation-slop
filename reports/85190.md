## Summary

Both reports say that right-clicking an ice cream vat with a beaker (the interaction the vat's screentip advertises) does not add the beaker's reagents to the vat. On current master the interaction works: a right-click with a beaker or a condiment bottle moves every reagent the vat accepts into it, with or without combat mode. The reports date from July and August 2024; upstream PR #86232, "Fixes ice cream vat refilling", merged on 2024-09-03, appears to be what fixed it. No code change was made.

## The bug

As reported, a player holding a beaker of milk or other ice cream ingredients right-clicked the vat (the original reporter also tried left, ctrl, alt and both combat mode settings) and nothing happened. The vat could only dispense the 15 or so cones its starting reagents allowed, and could not be restocked.

The second report (#86181) describes the same thing. Its only comment, from September 2024, says the commenter could not reproduce it and refilled milk and vanilla powder in the Metastation kitchen's vat without trouble. That comment fits a fix landing around that time.

## Reproduction

I could not reproduce it on master. The checks, all done with a real right-click on the vat by a player-controlled mob:

1. Started a round and, using the Lua editor, spawned a human, an `/obj/machinery/icecream_vat` next to it, and a beaker holding 30u milk placed in the human's hand. Took control of the human.
2. Right-clicked the vat. Result: floating text "refilling reagents" and the drink sound; the vat's milk went from 6u to 36u (the vat starts with 6u of each of its reagents).
3. Added milk, sugar, vanilla powder, cocoa powder, water and cream to the beaker and right-clicked again. Vanilla powder in the vat went from 6u to 16u and cream from 6u to 16u. Water, which is not a reagent the vat accepts, stayed in the beaker. Sugar and cocoa had already reacted into something else inside the beaker before the click (an artifact of mixing them with the Lua editor), so they were not a useful test.
4. Repeated with combat mode on, using a beaker with milk and berry juice: "refilling reagents" again. This matters because the reporter said they tried combat mode on and off.
5. Repeated with a condiment milk carton (space milk) in hand: "refilling reagents".

No runtimes appeared in the log. Nothing was tested on a mapped kitchen vat; the vat used was one spawned with the default starting reagents.

## Cause

The cause of the original report was not observed, since the bug is gone; this is from reading master and from the upstream PR title. Right-click with a held item is handled in `obj/machinery/icecream_vat/item_interaction_secondary()` at `code/modules/food_and_drinks/machinery/icecream_vat.dm:143-160`:

```dm
/obj/machinery/icecream_vat/item_interaction_secondary(mob/living/user, obj/item/tool, list/modifiers)
	var/obj/item/reagent_containers/beaker = tool
	if(!istype(beaker) || !beaker.reagents || (beaker.item_flags & ABSTRACT) || !beaker.is_open_container())
		return NONE
	...
	for(var/datum/reagent/beaker_reagents in beaker.reagents.reagent_list)
		if(beaker_reagents.type in icecream_vat_reagents)
			added_reagents = TRUE
			beaker.reagents.trans_to(src, beaker_reagents.volume, target_id = beaker_reagents.type)
```

Left-click (`item_interaction`, lines 117-141) inserts the beaker as the vat's custom ice cream beaker, and the screentip labels right-click "Transfer beaker reagents". Item interactions run from `base_item_interaction()` (`code/game/atom/atom_tool_acts.dm:8`) before any attack, so combat mode does not intercept the right-click.

The previous version of the vat code is not in the shallow checkout I had. The reports are consistent with the secondary interaction not being reached or not doing its work on the clicks players sent, and PR #86232 is the change that made it work; I did not diff the two versions, so I cannot say exactly which line was wrong.

## Fix

None. The behavior is already correct on master.

## Verification

Checked on master as described under Reproduction: right-click with a beaker (combat mode off and on) and with a condiment bottle all transferred the accepted reagents into the vat and showed the expected balloon text and sound. Not checked: the exact pre-fix behavior (no older build was run), a vat placed by the map rather than spawned, or an unmixed beaker with sugar and cocoa (they reacted before the click).

## Notes

- Only the reagents in `icecream_vat_reagents` (`icecream_vat.dm:33-51`) are accepted. A beaker with none of them gives "no reagents to transfer!" and blocks the interaction, and unaccepted reagents stay in the beaker. This is intended, but a player holding a mixed beaker could mistake it for the old bug. #86181's commenter asked which reagent the reporter was refilling, which is the same question.
- Both issues can probably be closed as fixed by #86232.
