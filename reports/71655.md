## Summary
The Burdened (punished god sect) trauma kept its level by adding or subtracting one per event, and granted rewards only when the level crossed 3, 6 or 9 upwards. When something changed the body without a matching event (a full heal or revive regrowing limbs and organs as `special`), the level stayed too high and the mutations stayed with it (#67837, #72192, and part of #71655). When a reward was lost without the level dropping (telekinesis is removed with the head, for example from a HARS mutation), it was never given back. The fix recounts the level from the owner's real condition a tick after any relevant change and on revival, and syncs the mutations for that level every time. One of the original claims, an ethereal revive removing the trauma itself, was not reproduced.

## The bug
- **#71655**: A burdened ethereal chaplain who had every mutation got HARS (headless), lost telekinesis when it was healed, could not regain it by re-burdening, then died and was revived with the crystal ("You no longer feel burdened"). The reporter asks for a way to regain the abilities or a check that the trauma is still there.
- **#67837**: After healing (mutadone, regrown limbs) the ascension mutations (telepathy, mind reading) stay on the chaplain. A commenter noted that telekinesis was not granted at level 6 either, while mind reading was.
- **#72192**: After a failed genetic meltdown burst an organ and the chaplain was revived, the abilities were lost. The player reached tier 9 again by surgery but got no further abilities and no psyker transformation. The altar showed 16/9 punishment.
- **#58816** (2021): when the sect tries to give a mutation that is already dormant in the owner's DNA, it fails, giving combinations such as muteness without telepathy.

## Reproduction
Reproduced on master with the Lua editor, by spawning humans, giving them the burdened trauma and driving the changes directly (damage or removal of parts, death plus `revive()`, adding HARS through `dna.add_mutation`), not through a real round of play.

**Revive keeps the level (#67837, #72192, #71655).**
1. Spawned an ethereal human with the burdened trauma at burden 8, carrying four mutations: telepathy, unintelligible, telekinesis and mind reader.
2. Killed it and called `revive()` with `HEAL_ALL & ~HEAL_REFRESH_ORGANS` (the flags the ethereal heart's revive uses), so the body was whole again.
3. It was still at burden 8 and still had all four mutations.

**HARS loses telekinesis for good (#71655).**
1. Spawned a human at burden 8 with the burdened trauma.
2. Added HARS with `dna.add_mutation(/datum/mutation/headless, "activated")`. The head came off.
3. The mutations were now telepathy, unintelligible and mind reader. Telekinesis was gone and the level was still 8.

Not reproduced: the trauma disappearing after an ethereal revive. The burdened trauma has absolute resilience, so a heal does not cure it, and the 2022 behaviour of the crystal replacing organs no longer exists in this code. Not reproduced either: #58816, see Notes.

## Cause
In `code/modules/religion/burdened/burdened_trauma.dm` at master:

- The signal handlers (`organ_added_burden` and the others, lines 206-273) call `update_burden(increase = TRUE/FALSE)` (line 88) once per signal. Limb and organ handlers ignore `special` additions (for example lines 223, 231 and `is_burdensome_organ` at 173-174). A full heal or revive regrows parts with `special = TRUE`, so the level never goes down, and the level stays above the real state.
- Mutations are given only when `update_burden()` crosses 3, 6 or 9 upwards. There is no later sync step.
- Telekinesis has `limb_req = BODY_ZONE_HEAD` (`code/datums/mutations/reach.dm:8`). Losing the head removes it, including the burden source (`code/modules/surgery/bodyparts/dismemberment.dm:109-113`), but the level stays at or above 6, so nothing grants it again.

## Fix
One commit, "Fix issue 71655: recalculate burden from the owner's actual condition", in `code/modules/religion/burdened/burdened_trauma.dm`:

- `count_burden()` counts one for each missing vital organ, missing limb, addiction, negative mutation and severe brain trauma (the same things `on_gain()` already counted at start-up).
- `queue_burden_recalculation()` defers a recount by one tick (unique and overriding, so a burst of changes costs one recount). Doing it a tick later avoids reading a half-finished change, since several signals fire before the mutation or part is actually added or removed.
- `recalculate_burden()` steps `burden_level` to the count through `update_burden()`, speaking only on the last step, and then calls `sync_burden_rewards()`.
- `sync_burden_rewards()` gives or removes telepathy and unintelligible (level 3 or more) and telekinesis and mind reader (level 6 or more), plus the outline filter, whether or not a threshold was just crossed.
- `grant_burden_mutation()` adds a mutation only if it is not already ours, the owner is alive and has the required limb, so the sync can retry on a later recount instead of failing.
- All the organ, limb, addiction, mutation and trauma handlers now only queue a recount, and `on_gain()` uses `count_burden()`. A `COMSIG_LIVING_REVIVE` hook queues one too.

The alternative of patching each handler (counting `special` additions) would not give back rewards lost without a level change, and would keep the possibility of further drift, so the level is now derived from state instead.

## Verification
Checked in game on the fixed build:
- The ethereal burden-8 case with death and revive (`HEAL_ALL & ~HEAL_REFRESH_ORGANS`): burden 0 and no mutations afterwards, instead of 8 and four mutations.

Not confirmed:
- **The telekinesis re-grant after the head returns (#71655).** The follow-up checks gave inconsistent levels. After a test spawn at burden 6 and HARS the level read 0 after a second, in another test it read 2, and the results are not trustworthy. A likely confounder was that the server was busy at round start, and one test reached burden 9 or more, which starts the slow psyker transformation and replaces the head. The final batch of checks lost its results, so the HARS case after the fix is unverified.
- **#72192**: that burden stays above 9, and that going from above 9 to below 6 removes telekinesis and mind reader.
- **#58816**: an in-game check.
- A psyker (burden 9) flow check after the change, and whether the new recount could produce runtime errors beyond what the round's runtime log showed (no runtime came from the burden code).

## Notes
- **#58816** was assessed from code only. `dna.add_mutation()` takes a list of sources (`code/datums/dna/dna.dm:113`), so an existing or dormant mutation just gains the burdened source. That looks fixed already, but the in-game test was inconclusive, so it is recorded as not reproduced.
- Possible double counting: a negative mutation such as HARS now counts one, and the missing head counts one more. This may be intended; the follow-up check of it was not finished.
- Burden is now derived from state, which is a behaviour change worth review: any future source of burden must be added to `count_burden()` instead of sending a signal and counting by itself.
- Separate pre-existing bug seen in the runtime log: `/obj/effect/decal/cleanable/proc/add_diseases` (`code/game/objects/effects/decals/cleanable.dm:53`) raised "Cannot read ... .spread_flags" six times, called from the gibspawner of the psyker transformation (`psyker.dm:67` passes `get_blood_dna_list()` as the diseases argument). It is not part of this unit.
