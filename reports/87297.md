# #87297: A Spectral Blade on the floor or in hand pulls the friendly wisp away from its owner

## Summary

The Spectral Blade (`/obj/item/melee/ghost_sword`) gathers ghosts by calling `transfer_observers_to()` on everything on its turf. That proc moves the entire orbiter component, so any non-ghost orbiter goes too, including the friendly wisp released from a wisp lantern. The fix gives `transfer_observers_to()` an optional type filter and makes the blade ask for `/mob/dead/observer` only. #75082 (wisp stops following after a mining shuttle trip) did not reproduce on current master.

## The bug

### #87297

A player with a released lantern wisp orbiting them loses the wisp when a Spectral Blade is picked up. The wisp starts orbiting the blade, along with the ghosts. The wisp can be recovered by recalling and redeploying it with the lantern.

### #75082

The 2023 report says the wisp stops following the wearer, and its light effect disappears while the thermal vision stays, after crossing z-levels on the mining shuttle.

## Reproduction

### #87297

On master `87b93338`, using admin tools. The scenario was set up through the Lua editor and a ghost observer.

1. Start a round and open the Lua editor.
2. Spawn a human, spawn a wisp lantern, and release the wisp with the lantern's `attack_self`. The wisp orbits the human.
3. As an observer, follow the human (`ManualFollow`).
4. Spawn an `/obj/item/melee/ghost_sword` on the same turf as the human.
5. Wait about 2 seconds for the blade's processing to run.

Evidence: the wisp's `orbit_target` became "the spectral blade", the human's `orbiters` became null, and the blade's `orbiters` held the wisp and the ghost.

The issue says the blade is picked up. In the test the blade was on the floor of the same turf, which is enough: `ghost_check()` scans the contents of the blade's turf, not only the holder.

### #75082

Checked on master, not reproduced:

1. With the wisp orbiting a human, `forceMove` the human to z-level 3. The wisp's `orbit_target` stayed the human, it was on the same z-level, and `vision_owner` was still the human.
2. Put the human on the mining shuttle and moved it with `moveShuttle('mining', 'mining_away', false)`. After docking, the human and the wisp were both on z-level 3, the wisp's `orbit_target` and `vision_owner` were the human, and the human had `orbiters`.

The wisp and the vision effect were kept in both cases.

## Cause

### #87297

`ghost_check()` in `code/modules/mining/lavaland/mining_loot/megafauna/ash_drake.dm` runs from `process()` and loops over `cur_turf.get_all_contents()`:

```dm
for(var/atom/random_thing in contents)
	random_thing.transfer_observers_to(src)     // :136
```

`transfer_observers_to()` (`code/datums/components/orbiter.dm:233`) does `target.TakeComponent(orbiters)`, which hands over the whole orbiter component whatever the orbiters are. It assumes all orbiters are observers. The wisp orbits its owner through the same orbiter component, so when the owner stands on the blade's turf, the wisp is taken with the ghosts. This matches the explanation in the issue thread.

### #75082

No defect was found. The orbiter shuttle handling has since been reworked (the commit "Fix orbiter runtimes (#98165)" is cited in the agents' notes), and the wisp has its own refresh and `vision_owner` handling. The original report was against client version 514.1589 in May 2023.

## Fix

One commit, **"Fix issue 87297: spectral blade only takes ghosts, not the wisp, as orbiters"**:

- `transfer_observers_to(target, observer_type)` in `code/datums/components/orbiter.dm` takes an optional type. Without it, behaviour is unchanged (the whole component is taken). With it, the proc walks a copy of the orbiter list and re-orbits only orbiters of that type onto the target with their stored orbit parameters (radius, direction, speed, segments, pre-rotation). Other orbiters keep orbiting the source.
- `ghost_check()` in `ash_drake.dm` passes `/mob/dead/observer`.

Other callers (the commit mentions mind transfer and headcrab) still use the one-argument form. An alternative of excluding the wisp specifically in the blade code was not used, because the filter also protects any other non-ghost orbiter.

During development, a first version indexed the stored parameters off by one and caused "list index out of bounds" runtimes in `orbiter.dm` on each tick. It was corrected before the commit. The stored parameters do not include the orbiter itself.

## Verification

On the rebuilt test server with the same setup:

- With the blade on the turf, the wisp's `orbit_target` stayed the human. The ghost observer moved to the blade ("Now orbiting the spectral blade") and became visible.
- After putting the blade in the human's hands, the wisp's `orbit_target` was still the human, the wisp was on the human's tile, and `vision_owner` was the human.
- The runtime log showed no runtimes from `orbiter.dm`.

Not checked:
- The real chest drop and a real megafauna kill.
- A blade picked up by a player through the normal interface (a floor blade and `put_in_hands` were used).
- Several ghosts at once, ghost orbit parameters after the move, and the blade's force scaling with ghost count.
- The other callers of `transfer_observers_to()`; they use the unchanged path.

## Notes

- #75082 is labelled "Tested/Reproduced" upstream from 2023. It could not be reproduced on current master with the checks above, but those were forced moves and one shuttle move in a test round, not a play-through with the real mining trip.
- One comment on #87297 suggests the wisp following the blade looked like an intentional spirit interaction. The orbit itself being broken for the owner, and recoverable only by redeploying the wisp, says it is not a designed feature.
