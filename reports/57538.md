## Summary

Buying the Grand Corporate Monastery shuttle through an emagged communications console worked on master: the purchase succeeded and the monastery became the emergency shuttle, and the 2021 runtime in #57538 did not occur. The monastery shuttle does move the station's emergency docking port into the station on purpose when its template loads. #65515 (a replacement shuttle landing inside the station after Shuttle Catastrophe) is already handled by master: Shuttle Catastrophe resets the displaced dock before loading the replacement, and this was checked in game.

## The bug

#57538 (2021): a player bought the Grand Corporate Monastery through an emagged console and got the standard shuttle instead. A commenter at the time saw runtimes from `load_template` ("failed to reserve an area for shuttle template loading") followed by `null.canDock()`, and a 2023 comment suggested that the docking port of the shuttle being replaced had been destroyed several times.

#65515 (2022): after a dock-moving shuttle (Grand Monastery, asteroid) was bought and Shuttle Catastrophe replaced it with another shuttle, the replacement landed in the middle of the station. A commenter explained that the dock displacement done by the monastery was not reverted when the shuttle was replaced.

## Reproduction

### #57538

1. On the test server (MetaStation, master), start a round and take a captain-level character next to a communications console. Emag the console and authenticate.
2. Open the shuttle purchase menu and buy "Grand Corporate Monastery". The credits were topped up with admin tools beforehand (the cargo account was given +100000).
3. Wait for the purchase to finish. It took about 37 seconds, because `load_template` sleeps while loading.

Observed: the purchase succeeded. `SSshuttle.emergency` was the monastery ("a monastery with engines strapped to it", shuttle id `emergency`), it was docked at the away dock, and the shuttle log contained "has purchased Grand Corporate Monastery". The 2021 runtimes did not appear. The only runtimes were about nine `Cannot read null.air` errors from `pump.dm:64`, from an air pump in the transit reservation while the template loaded. They looked like noise from loading and were not related to the reported symptom.

The runtime log in the issue could not be reproduced, so its cause remains unknown. It may have been a bug in the load path of that time that has since been fixed, but this is a guess.

### #65515 (dock displacement)

1. Before buying anything, read the position of the stationary dock with shuttle id `emergency_home` ("MetaStation emergency evac bay") with View Variables: it was at 106,58,2.
2. Buy the monastery as above. The dock was then at 106,91,2, about 33 turfs further into the station. It had already moved while the purchase was still loading, before the shuttle finally docked.
3. With the dock still displaced, trigger the event "Shuttle Catastrophe" from the admin event panel and choose Meta Station Emergency Shuttle as the replacement. A temporary trace in the dock's `Moved()` (removed afterwards, no code change left) showed `emergency_home` moved back to 106,58,2 by `code/modules/events/shuttle_catastrophe.dm:57-62`, and nothing moved it again while the Meta shuttle loaded. The shuttle log read "Shuttle Catastrophe set a new shuttle, Meta Station Emergency Shuttle".

The test server used a forced event and Lua/VV to read state; the shuttle was bought through the actual console UI.

## Cause

The dock move is intentional. `/obj/effect/station_crash` is an effect that runs from its `Initialize()` (`code/modules/admin/fun_balloon.dm:155-158`) and calls `shuttle_crash()` (`:160`), which finds the `emergency_home` dock and moves it:

```dm
var/new_dir = REVERSE_DIR(SM.dir)
SM.forceMove(get_ranged_target_turf(SM, new_dir, crash_strength))
```

The `/devastating` subtype (`:169`, "Absolute Destruction. Will crash the shuttle far into the station") uses a crash strength of 15 to 25 turfs; the base effect uses 3 to 15. `_maps/shuttles/emergency/emergency_monastery.dmm` contains two `devastating` effects (lines 322 and 2763), `emergency_lance.dmm` contains one (line 643), and `emergency_meteor.dmm` has one base-strength effect (line 35). Each time the template loads, including `SSshuttle.load_template()` (`code/controllers/subsystem/shuttle.dm:989`), every effect moves the dock further. On the test server, loading the monastery during a Shuttle Catastrophe moved the dock from y=58 to 77 and then to 102 (the two effects firing in turn); the strength is random, so the amounts vary (the earlier purchase ended at y=91). No asteroid shuttle map contains this effect at master, so the asteroid case in #65515's title could not be explained from the code.

Because the effect fires at load time, a crash shuttle that is only loaded as an admin preview also moves the dock.

For #65515, `code/modules/events/shuttle_catastrophe.dm:56-62` already carries the fix ("We need to move our docking port back in case a crashlanding shuttle has been purchased previously"), which resets the port to its `initial_x/y/z` before `action_load()`.

## Fix

None. #57538 was not reproduced, and #65515 is fixed in master (the reset in `shuttle_catastrophe.dm`, attributed to upstream PR 98187).

## Verification

Checked in game: monastery purchase through an emagged console; dock position before and after the purchase; Shuttle Catastrophe with a non-crash replacement resetting the dock and the replacement loading without a further move.

Not checked: the original runtime from 2021; the asteroid shuttle; any replacement route other than Shuttle Catastrophe; a crash shuttle as the replacement beyond what is described under Notes.

## Notes

- Shuttle Catastrophe choosing a crash shuttle (the monastery) as the replacement crashes the dock again. That follows from the design of the crash effect.
- Other ways of replacing the shuttle (for example the admin Shuttle Manipulator) do not reset the dock after a crash shuttle; only Shuttle Catastrophe does. Previewing a crash shuttle also displaces the dock. These were noted as unfixed leads, and no fix was attempted because none looked clearly small.
- The 2023 comment (dock unregistered several times, standard shuttle docked anyway) was not reproduced. It is consistent with the dock being moved repeatedly by the crash effects, but that link is unverified.
