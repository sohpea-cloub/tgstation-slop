## Summary

On KiloStation, the external airlock that leads from the disposal maintenance room out to space (below engineering, under the supermatter and gravity chamber) had no power. Its tile was mapped in `/area/space/nearstation`, which is always unpowered, so the airlock could not draw from the disposal area's APC even though a cable on the tile is connected to it. The fix moves that tile's area to `/area/station/maintenance/disposal`.

## The bug

The reporter found the external airlock unpowered and could not power it with wires. A commenter pointed to the airlock in a screenshot and guessed it "wasn't mapped to disposals properly", which is what was found.

## Reproduction

1. Loaded the unmodified KiloStation map on the test server and started the round.
2. Using the Lua editor, looked up the turf at x=152, y=55 on the station level (z2 in game) and read its area.
3. On master: the area was `/area/space/nearstation` with `always_unpowered` set to 1, and a power check on it (`powered()` for the environment channel) returned 0.
4. For comparison, the inner airlock of the same pair at x=152, y=58 is in the disposal area and was powered (1).

The door was not opened or operated; unpowered status was read from the area. The cable on the tile is continuous to the disposal APC (x=148, y=61), so the cable layout is not the problem, which matches the reporter's failed attempt to fix it with wires.

## Cause

In `_maps/map_files/KiloStation/KiloStation.dmm`, the tile key `"vTz"` (used once, at line 69627 on master) holds the external airlock with its engineering-external access helper and a broken-floor helper on plating, but ends with `/area/space/nearstation`. That area is always unpowered, so area-based power (`powered()`) fails for the door regardless of the wiring.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 97609: KiloStation outer disposal airlock sat in an unpowered space area", changes that one line of the map from `/area/space/nearstation` to `/area/station/maintenance/disposal`, the same area as the inner airlock. The tile south of it (x=152, y=54) was left as space/nearstation.

## Verification

Maps load at runtime, so after editing the .dmm the test server was restarted with no rebuild. Looking up the same tile in the Lua editor: the area was `/area/station/maintenance/disposal`, `always_unpowered` was 0, and the power check returned 1. The tile to the south stayed `/area/space/nearstation`.

Not checked: opening or cycling the airlock in game; whether the airlock now behaves correctly with its atmosphere (no pressurisation or air alarm check); other tiles or airlocks on KiloStation that might have the same area mistake.

## Notes

- Moving a tile into the disposal area changes that area's contents slightly (it now includes this tile's airlock), which also means the area's lighting, atmos and fire-alarm rules apply to it. This was not examined.
- SDQL2 queries gave "Parsing error on 2nd query" in the test session even for a trivial SELECT; this was not investigated and is unrelated to the map bug.
