## Summary
Two separate defects made the sedatives unreliable. Toxins whose remaining volume is at or below the liver's tolerance are removed without running their effects, so chloral hydrate (and two other sedatives) stopped sedating when about 3u were left, and the short `Sleeping` they had applied ran out after a few seconds. Morphine's "guarantee sleep" branch used a probability of `30 * (48 - current_cycle)`, which is never positive in the cycle range where it runs, so it never fired. The fix exempts the three sedatives from the liver tolerance skip and corrects the morphine expression. #93388 (spray tan overdose that does not wear off) is intended behavior and was not changed.

## The bug
#98240: after the metabolism change the reporter links to (PR #94621), a 15u morphine dose did not keep a target asleep, and 10u of chloral hydrate put someone to sleep for a couple of seconds, after which they woke and slept again in a cycle, often long enough to move.

## Reproduction
Done on the test server with admin tools. In the Lua editor, humans were spawned and reagents were added with `add_reagent`. A 0.5 s Lua timer logged each change in `IsSleeping()`. Times are in deciseconds (ds).

1. Spawn a human and add 10u of chloral hydrate. Before the fix it was asleep only from 230 to 270 ds, about 4 s.
2. Spawn another human and add 15u of morphine. Before the fix it was asleep from 1030 to 1130 ds and awake from 1130 to 1150 ds. That sleep came from morphine's drowsiness, never from its guaranteed-sleep branch.
3. For #93388: add 15u of spray tan to a human whose skin tone was `mixed4` and whose hairstyle was `Hime Cut 2`. After the overdose the human had orange skin and the `Spiky` hairstyle, and both persisted after the reagent was gone.

## Cause
**Chloral hydrate, B33r (`fakebeer`), sodium thiopental.** In `code/modules/reagents/chemistry/holder/mob_life.dm:44`, a toxin whose volume is at or below `liver_tolerance * toxin.liver_tolerance_multiplier` is removed and the loop `continue`s without running `on_mob_life`:

```
if(amount <= liver_tolerance * toxin.liver_tolerance_multiplier)
	owner.reagents.remove_reagent(toxin.type, ...)
	continue
```

The default liver tolerance is 3u, so the last 3u of a 10u dose were removed with no effect, and the current cycle stopped advancing. A sedative applies a short `Sleeping()` each tick (chloral hydrate at `code/modules/reagents/chemistry/reagents/toxin_reagents.dm:643`), so when the effect stopped being applied the sleep ended about 4 s later. This matches the reporter's wake and sleep cycling.

**Morphine.** At `code/modules/reagents/chemistry/reagents/medicine_reagents.dm:795`, in the `48 to INFINITY` cycle branch, the chance is `SPT_PROB(30 * (48 - current_cycle), seconds_per_tick)`. For every cycle in that branch the base is zero or negative, so the guaranteed `Sleeping(...)` never ran. The sleep length, `4 SECONDS * metabolization_ratio`, was also not scaled by `seconds_per_tick`, giving only about 2 s per 2 s tick.

## Fix
One commit, "Fix issue 98240: knockout chems (chloral hydrate, morphine) stop sedating early".
- `toxin_reagents.dm`: `liver_tolerance_multiplier = 0` on chloral hydrate, B33r (`fakebeer`) and sodium thiopental, so they keep working until they are gone. Other toxins are unaffected. The same file already uses `liver_tolerance_multiplier = 0` elsewhere.
- `medicine_reagents.dm`: the morphine chance became `min(30 * (current_cycle - 48), 100)`, and the sleep duration is multiplied by `seconds_per_tick`. The clamp is needed because `SPT_PROB` misbehaves above 100% per second: it squares a negative base. A first attempt without the clamp kept the target asleep only for cycles 49 to 54.

## Verification
After a rebuild, with the same timed Lua logging:
- Chloral hydrate 10u: asleep from 226 to 356 ds, continuously (about 13 s), until the reagent was gone.
- Morphine 15u: asleep from 986 to 1626 ds, continuously (about 64 s). An earlier sleep between 806 and 906 ds is the normal drowsiness sleep.

Not checked: B33r and sodium thiopental in game, other doses, mobs with damaged or missing livers, and re-dosing while asleep.

## Notes
- Balance review is needed. Morphine at 10u or more now gives near-guaranteed sleep. Before, it was effectively only drowsiness, so this is a large strengthening.
- Sedatives that still ignore the liver tolerance are not rare: any toxin with the default multiplier skips its effects at 3u. Pancuronium (a stun) has the same skip and was left unchanged. Sulfonal already worked and was left unchanged.
- Morphine still takes about 48 cycles (about 100 s, 9.6u) before the guaranteed branch, as its table is written.
- #93388: `/datum/reagent/spraytan/overdose_process` (`code/modules/reagents/chemistry/reagents/other_reagents.dm:655`) deliberately grants hair ("No hair? No problem!"), sets the hairstyle and sets the skin tone or mutant color to orange. It also drops the metabolization rate. A maintainer comment on the issue says the overdose has not worn off for years. The claim that a DNA reset leaves orange eyes was not reproduced: nothing in this code touches eye color, though the test humans had no eyes organ, so that was not checked directly.
