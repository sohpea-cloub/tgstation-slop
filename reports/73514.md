# #73514: Ethereal crystals revive a headless or brainless body, regrowing a new head and an empty brain

## Summary

An ethereal's heart starts crystallization whenever the owner dies, without checking that the body still has a head and brain. The crystal then revives it with `revive(HEAL_ALL & ~HEAL_REFRESH_ORGANS)`, whose full heal regrows missing limbs (including the head) and organs (the brain). A decapitated ethereal therefore comes back with a new, empty head and brain while the player stays with the old brain on the floor (#73514). The same path likely explains #88780: a recovered-crew body whose ghost poll fails is killed again by the ghost-role component, which starts crystallization and revives it without a soul. The fix adds a `can_crystalize()` check for head, brain and the ghost-role-on-revive trait.

## The bug

#73514: if an ethereal's head (or brain) is removed, the body later revives on its own with a newly grown head and brain, while the player, who is in the old brain on the floor, does not get the new body. The reporter also notes that the same happens when the head is cut off in the killing blow, but not when it is cut off after death.

#88780: a recovered-crew ethereal (lost-crew body bag) revived out of nowhere with its limbs restored and no soul.

## Reproduction

### #73514

On master (`87b93338`), using the Lua editor on the test server:

1. Spawn a human, make it an ethereal (`set_species`), give it a mind (`mind_initialize()`).
2. Dismember its head (`get_bodypart('head'):dismember()`). It dies and the message "Crystals start forming around [name]" appears.
3. Wait: a crystal forms after about 40 seconds and the body revives about 60 seconds later.
4. Observed: stat 0 (alive), a new "ethereal head", a new brain, and no mind. The original head and brain, holding the mind, lay on the floor.

### #88780

This was not reproduced as reported. A freshly opened recovered-crew body was not seen reviving spontaneously. A revive of a recovered-crew ethereal with a heart produced no "soul is struggling" message within a minute, so the failed-poll path was not seen in that attempt. The fixed behaviour was tested instead (see Verification). Reproduction on unfixed code was not run for this chain; the cause is established by reading the code (below).

## Cause

In `code/modules/surgery/organs/internal/heart/heart_ethereal.dm`:

- `on_stat_change()` (`:68`) starts crystallization on death. Its guards cover suicide, the cooldown and `TRAIT_CANNOT_CRYSTALIZE`; none looks at the head or brain.
- `crystalize()` (`:108`) likewise checks only the cooldown, the stat and whether the body is inside a mob, item or effect.
- The crystal's `heal_ethereal()` calls `regenerating.revive(HEAL_ALL & ~HEAL_REFRESH_ORGANS)` (`:239`). The full heal regenerates limbs and organs, so a missing head and brain are regrown (`code/modules/mob/living/carbon/carbon.dm`, around `:675`).

For #88780 (code reading only): in `code/datums/components/ghostrole_on_revive.dm`, when a ghost poll finds nobody and `refuse_revival_if_failed` is set (the lost-crew manager sets it), the component calls `reviving.death()` (`:154`). For an ethereal that death is a stat change to DEAD, which starts crystallization as above, and the body revives with limbs regrown but nobody inside. `TRAIT_GHOSTROLE_ON_REVIVE` stays on the mob and brain while the component waits for a soul.

## Fix

Two commits, both in `heart_ethereal.dm`.

**"Fix issue 73514: ethereal crystals no longer revive a body missing its head or brain."** A new `/obj/item/organ/heart/ethereal/proc/can_crystalize()` returns FALSE for a non-carbon, or when the body has no head bodypart or no brain. It is used in three places: `on_stat_change()` (do not start), `crystalize()` (abort with "You were unable to finish your crystallization, as there is nothing left of you to bring back." and stop the process), and the crystal's `heal_ethereal()` (delete the crystal instead of reviving, in case the head or brain was lost while crystallized). Regrowing legs and arms is unchanged, since the existing ethereal revival unit test expects it.

**"Fix issue 88780: ethereal crystals no longer revive recovered-crew bodies waiting for a soul."** `can_crystalize()` also returns FALSE if the mob or its brain has `TRAIT_GHOSTROLE_ON_REVIVE`. The examine code checks both the mob and the brain in the same way. The trait is removed when a soul takes over (the component is deleted then), so it only blocks bodies still waiting.

## Verification

After rebuilding, four fresh ethereals were killed:

- Head dismembered: dead, no "Crystals start forming" message, still dead after 2 minutes, no head, no brain, no crystal.
- Brain removed, then killed: still dead after 2 minutes, no crystal.
- Right leg dismembered, then normal death (regression check): crystals formed and revived at about 100 seconds with the leg regrown and head and brain present.
- #88780: a real `ghostrole_on_revive` component (refuse revival if failed) was added to an ethereal's brain, then the body revived with nobody signing up. The message "...soul is struggling to return!" appeared (the component's `death()`), but no "Crystals start forming"; 90+ seconds later the body was still dead, the trait still on, no crystal.

The runtime log showed only three known empty Lua lines. Not checked: the #88780 chain on unfixed code, a real lost-crew body bag with a successful ghost take-over after the fix, and the "head lost while already crystallized" path in `heal_ethereal()` (written but not exercised).

## Notes

- The issue comments call this mechanic "cursed and amazing" and "a feature"; the change disables crystal revival for bodies missing a head or brain, so a maintainer who wants the behaviour (or wants a ghost poll for the new body, as suggested in a comment) would prefer a different design.
- A body with a missing head also loses its head in `can_crystalize()` for any later head removal while crystallized: the crystal is deleted and the body stays dead.
- #73425 (zombies) was mentioned in the issue as similar; it was not related to this fix.
