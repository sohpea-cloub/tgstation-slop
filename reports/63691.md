# #63691: Burned-away ants still produce a "You step on" message

| | |
|---|---|

| Status | Not reproduced on current master; no code change |
| Fix branch | none |
| Tested on | tgstation master `87b93338`, 2026-10-23 |

## Summary

The report says that after space ants were burned (the reporter used the ash drake's lava), walking over the tile still printed a red "You step on the !" message, long after the drake was dead. On master, burning ants (directly, with lava, and with the drake's lava attack) left no caltrop listener behind on the tile. Only tiles that still held a visible ants or space fire ants decal bit the player, which is the intended behaviour since #64786. A world-wide query found no caltrop component whose parent was deleted or null. No fix was made.

## The bug

As reported: ants are burned (by drake lava), the player steps on the same tile, and a red "You step on the !" message appears, with the ants' name missing. The message persisted after the drake died. One commenter in 2021 guessed that #64786 would change this, another in 2024 suspected the ants' caltrop component survives when the decal is deleted, and added that the component "seems to get deleted eventually".

The suspected mechanism: a burned `/obj/effect/decal/cleanable/ants` spawns a space fire ants decal and deletes itself, and that interacts badly with lava (a decal cannot exist on lava) and with decal merging, leaving a caltrop component with no parent but with its signal hooks still on the turf.

## Reproduction

The bug could not be reproduced. The check was done as a barefoot Assistant (shoes deleted) using the Lua editor, on a row of iron floor tiles in the Arrival Shuttle Hallway:

1. Control tile: spawned `/obj/effect/decal/cleanable/ants`.
2. Direct burn: spawned ants and called `fire_act(1000, 1000)` on them.
3. Drake lava: spawned ants, then spawned `/obj/effect/temp_visual/lava_warning` on the tile. This is the drake's lava attack: it turns the tile into `/turf/open/lava/smooth` and changes it back to floor after a delay (`reset_time` defaults to 1 second, `code/modules/mob/living/simple_animal/hostile/megafauna/drake.dm:232-266`). A second run used a 6 second reset.
4. Manual lava: spawned ants, then `ChangeTurf` to `/turf/open/lava/smooth`, then back to iron floor.
5. Ants spawned on a lava tile, a space fire ants decal spawned and then lava'd, and two ants decals on one tile (to exercise merging) before burning.
6. Read each tile's contents and signal listeners through Lua (`_listen_lookup` for `atom_entered`), walked east and west over the row several times, and ran SDQL2 queries over every caltrop component.

Observed:

- A tile only had a caltrop `atom_entered` listener, and only bit, when it held a visible ants or space fire ants decal. Walking the row gave "You step on the space ants!" and "You step on the space fire ants!" in red, with the proper name, and the player screamed after enough bites.
- Tiles whose ants had been burned by lava or turned into lava had no ants decal and no listener, and were silent.
- SDQL2 `SELECT /datum/component/caltrop MAP [parent, parent.loc, parent.gc_destroyed]` returned 753 caltrop components; a `WHERE parent.gc_destroyed` query and a `WHERE !parent` query found none with a deleted or null parent. A query of all ants decals returned 29.
- The runtime log showed no ant, lava or caltrop runtimes. Two empty "uncaught runtime error" lines appeared at the time a Lua script of the tester's own failed ("a destructed callback or destructed userdata method was called", in the tester's script, not in game code).

## Cause

No defect was found for the reported symptom. What the code does:

- `/obj/effect/decal/cleanable/ants/fire_act` (`code/game/objects/effects/decals/cleanable/ants.dm:75-77`) creates an `/obj/effect/decal/cleanable/ants/fire` on the tile and deletes the original. The fire variant bites for 15 to 25 damage (`ants.dm:86-87`) and ignores fire (`ants.dm:89-90`). Since #64786, stepping on a burned-ants tile with messages in red is therefore expected for as long as the fire ants decal is there.
- Decals refuse to exist on lava: `NeverShouldHaveComeHere` is true for groundless turfs (`code/game/objects/effects/decals/decal.dm:29-30`, `GLOB.turfs_without_ground` in `code/__DEFINES/is_helpers.dm:30-37`). `/obj/effect/decal/Initialize` returns `INITIALIZE_HINT_QDEL` in that case (`decal.dm:14-18`), and a turf change to lava runs `sanity_check_self` and deletes the decal (`decal.dm:40-46`).
- `ants/Initialize` calls `. = ..()` and then `update_ant_damage()` unconditionally (`ants.dm:14-18`), so the caltrop component is added even when the parent returned `INITIALIZE_HINT_QDEL`, whether from the bad-turf check or from merging (`code/game/objects/effects/decals/cleanable.dm:26-30`). The component belongs to the decal, so it is deleted together with it; no listener outlived its decal in the test. This matches the 2024 commenter's remark that the component "gets deleted eventually".
- The caltrop component prints `"You step on [parent]!"` (`code/datums/components/caltrop.dm:114`). A blank name as in the report ("You step on the !") would require a parent that has lost its name or been deleted; no such component exists in the tested state.

## Fix

None.

## Verification

Checked in game, as described under Reproduction: control, direct burn, drake lava (two timings), manual lava and back, ants spawned on lava, a space fire ants decal on lava, and merged ants, followed by walking over the tiles and a world-wide query for orphaned caltrops.

Not checked:
- A real ash drake fight (the lava attack's effect object was spawned directly).
- Ants burned by an actual fire or plasma flame rather than a direct `fire_act` call.
- Rounds with a long uptime or garbage collection pressure; the reporter's persistence "long after the drake was dead" may depend on that.
- Other ways a decal could get stuck outside the burn path (the reported "!" may also have come from a different decal type with a caltrop).

## Notes

- Nothing should be changed on the basis of this check, but the unconditional `update_ant_damage()` after a `INITIALIZE_HINT_QDEL` (`ants.dm:14-18`) is wasteful: it adds a caltrop and sets up an appearance for a decal that is deleted immediately. It is harmless here and was not changed.
- #64786 (ants glow, min damage, shoe permeability) is the related change that made burned ants become space fire ants.
- The issue can be closed as not reproducible, unless a reporter can supply a sequence that leaves a nameless "!" message on current code.
