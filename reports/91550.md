## #91550: Mobs can still hit you during the Sky Bulge jump

### The bug

The Sky Bulge's jump blocks the user's own attacks mid-jump ("You're technically in mid-air", `on_attack_during_jump`, `code/game/objects/items/weaponry/melee/spear.dm:653`), but NPC mobs can still hit the jumper. The reporter lost half their health to a mob while in the air above it.

### Reproduction

1. On master, using the Lua editor, spawn a human, give them a Sky Bulge and wield it.
2. Spawn a carp next to where the human will land. Its AI was turned off first and turned on about 2.4 seconds into the jump, while the human was airborne.
3. Make the human jump with the spear, calling the jump action's effect directly (`do_effect(0)`) rather than clicking.
4. Watch the human's health.

Observed: health fell from 100 to 80 at about 4.6 s (still mid-jump) and to 60 at about 6.5 s.

Caveat: the jump was started by calling the action from Lua, not by a player's click, and the mob was a carp rather than the exact mob in the report.

### Cause

`jump_up()` (`spear.dm:575`) makes the user undense and flying, and adds a few traits:

```dm
owner.add_traits(list(TRAIT_SILENT_FOOTSTEPS, TRAIT_MOVE_FLYING), ACTION_TRAIT)
```

Nothing there makes the user untargetable or immune to damage. Basic mob AI therefore still picks them as a target and its attacks still apply damage. `land()` (`spear.dm:617`) removes the same traits at the end of the jump.

### Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 91550: mobs can no longer hurt you during the Sky Bulge jump". `jump_up()` adds `TRAIT_GODMODE` to the owner with the other jump traits, and `land()` removes it with them. The lava swoop does the same (`code/datums/actions/mobs/lava_swoop.dm:42`). The basic-mob targeting strategy skips targets that have `TRAIT_GODMODE` (`code/datums/ai/basic_mobs/targeting_strategies/basic_targeting_strategy.dm:45`), and damage procs ignore it, so the mob both stops targeting the jumper and cannot hurt them if it is already attacking.

### Verification

The same Lua test after the fix: health stayed at 100 at the mid-jump check and at about 6.5 s, and the carp bit the human again after landing (60 at about 9 s, 25 later), so damage resumed on landing.

Not checked: other mob types and non-basic-mob attackers, ranged or area damage during the jump, the jump interrupted by death or deletion (whether the trait is removed on every path out of the jump), and an actual player click.

### Notes

- `TRAIT_GODMODE` blocks all damage and is a broad choice, not only melee from mobs. A maintainer may prefer narrower handling (for example only making the user untargetable). The review point is deliberate: godmode matches the existing lava-swoop precedent.

## #92596: .357 cannot be printed from a hacked autolathe and does not work when loaded

### The report

Hacked autolathes were said to offer only .357 "casings", not bullets, and single .357 rounds were said not to work when loaded into a revolver, while a speedloader worked. A comment gave a runtime from a double-barreled shotgun (`list index out of bounds` in `load_gun`).

### What was tried

Using Lua calls to the gun's `item_interaction` with a casing on freshly spawned guns (spent state was forced by clearing the casing's projectile and processing the chamber), with the round's runtime log checked afterwards (no runtimes):

- Syndicate revolver, one round fired: the load returned success and the round went in.
- Syndicate revolver, all 7 chambers spent: two loads succeeded (live rounds 0 to 1, then 2). The chambered casing stayed spent, but firing on an empty chamber calls `chamber_round()` (`code/modules/projectiles/guns/ballistic/revolver.dm:39-41`), which rotates the cylinder, so the next click reaches the loaded round.
- Double-barrel with one shell fired: load succeeded (live 1 to 2). Both spent: load succeeded and a new shell was chambered.
- Double-barrel whose `stored_ammo` held only the spent chambered shell (the situation in the quoted runtime): load succeeded without a runtime, the spent shell dropped to the floor and the new shell was chambered live.

The autolathe entry `/datum/design/a357` is named ".357 Casing (VERY Lethal)" and builds `/obj/item/ammo_casing/c357` (`code/modules/research/designs/autolathe/security_designs.dm:162-166`), which is the live round. No separate bullet design exists, so this looks like a naming misreading, as a commenter suggested.

### Cause of the quoted runtime

It came from `load_gun` indexing `magazine.stored_ammo[1]` when the list had been emptied. Master already guards it: `if(length(magazine?.stored_ammo) && chambered != magazine.stored_ammo[1])` (`code/modules/projectiles/guns/ballistic.dm:589`).

### Conclusion

Not reproduced; no fix. The "does not work unless loaded by speedloader" report may be the cylinder needing a click to rotate to the loaded round, or the older, now-guarded runtime aborting the load. This is a guess; it was not reproduced. The unreproduced part is the exact scenario in the report (on the reporter's build, with the test merges listed there).
