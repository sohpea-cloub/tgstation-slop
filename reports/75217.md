## Summary

Two pAI bugs are real and now fixed with one small change each in `code/modules/pai/pai.dm`. Disabling the holomatrix (#76887) only flipped `can_holo`, so an already-active holochassis stayed out. Folding a pAI out (#88962) removed `TRAIT_UNDENSE`, whose handler made the pAI dense, so anyone walking into it swapped places with it. The garbled radio speech in #75217 did not reproduce: with all telecomms deleted, `;` speech from a pAI arrived intact through the radio's backup transmission, and garbling happened only on whispers, which is intended for every mob.

## #75217: pAI radio garbled with no telecomms

### The bug
The reporter said a pAI speaking on the common channel got garbled output with no telecomms present, even in space, where earlier it came through as if on a station-bounced radio. A commenter saw both a clear radio message and a partial, whisper-like one, and suspected the pAI picked up its own whisper.

### Reproduction
Not reproduced.
1. On the test server (MetaStation, round running), a pAI was spawned with the Lua editor and possessed, then folded out.
2. All `/obj/machinery/telecomms` objects were deleted with SDQL2.
3. The pAI said `;hello radio test two`. The message arrived intact on frequency 145.9 after a delay of about 2 seconds. With telecomms present, the same speech arrived intact on `[Common]`.
4. The pAI whispered `;hello whisper radio` (using the Whisper verb). It came out as `H*l*o whi**er radio`.

The pAI headset has `subspace_transmission = FALSE` (`code/game/objects/items/devices/radio/headset.dm:403`), with broadcasting and listening on. Setting the broadcast flag does not make the headset hear-sensitive (`headset.dm:150`), and no stray duplicate message was seen.

### Cause
Nothing wrong was found. The delay and the change of frequency come from `backup_transmission` (`code/game/objects/items/devices/radio/radio.dm:389-400`), which sends the message when no telecomms relay it, with compression 0. The garbling is `stars()` applied to whispers:

```dm
if(LAZYACCESS(message_mods, WHISPER_MODE))
	// Radios don't pick up whispers very well
	radio_message = stars(radio_message)
```
(`radio.dm:322-325`; the same for any mob, not just pAIs.)

### Fix
None.

### Verification
Only the checks above. The reporter's exact situation was not reproduced; the most likely explanations are that they whispered, or that an older bug has since gone. A case where a clear message and a whispered one both appear was not seen.

## #76887: disabling the holomatrix does not fold the holochassis

### The bug
The PDA/card option "toggle holomatrix" used to be available only while the chassis was folded. It can now be used while the holoform is out, but then the pAI stays out until the chassis dissolves on its own.

### Reproduction
1. Spawn a pAI with the Lua editor (`SS13.new('/mob/living/silicon/pai', loc)`), set its `ckey` to possess it, and fold it out.
2. Call `toggle_holo()` on it (this is the card UI action `toggle_holo`, `code/modules/pai/card.dm:161`).
3. On master the pAI stays in `holoform`; only `can_holo` flips.

I did not record a separate before-state value from the test server; the pre-fix behaviour is from the code and the report.

### Cause
`toggle_holo()` (`code/modules/pai/pai.dm:410`) only did `can_holo = !can_holo` and never folded an active holoform.

### Fix
Commit "Fix issue 76887: disabling a pAI's holomatrix remotely folds its holochassis" adds, after the flip:
```dm
if(!can_holo && holoform)
	fold_in()
```

### Verification
On the rebuilt test server, with the holoform out, `toggle_holo()` printed "TestPAI dematerialises!" and `can_holo` was false. Not checked: re-enabling afterwards, or using the option from a real card UI instead of a direct call.

## #88962: pAI holoform is swapped with when walked into

### The bug
A pAI in holoform swaps places with anyone who walks into it, though the pAI is small and can walk under people. The reporter wanted it to be walked over, as with medbots.

### Reproduction
1. Spawn a pAI and possess it, fold it in, then fold it out (the fold-in step matters: folding out without ever folding in does not remove the trait and leaves density at 0, which made an earlier test attempt invalid).
2. Check `density`: it was 1 on master.
3. Possess a human with run intent standing at x=106 and walk east into the pAI at x=107.
4. On master the pAI was moved to 106,144 and the human took its tile: a swap.

### Cause
`/mob/living/silicon/pai` declares `density = FALSE` (`code/modules/pai/pai.dm:3`), but `fold_in` adds `TRAIT_UNDENSE` (`code/modules/pai/shell.dm:93`) and `fold_out` removes it (`shell.dm:124`). Removing the trait runs `/mob/living/proc/update_density()` (`code/modules/mob/living/living.dm:758`), which sets density TRUE when the trait is absent. A dense mob can be swapped with in `MobBump` (`living.dm:176`, `can_mobswap_with` at `living.dm:275`).

### Fix
Commit "Fix issue 88962: pAI holoforms are no longer swapped with when walked into" overrides `update_density()` on the pAI to always `set_density(FALSE)`, keeping the pAI non-dense as declared.

### Verification
On the rebuilt server, with the same fold-in/fold-out flow, density was 0, and the possessed running human walking 106 to 107 to 108 never swapped; the pAI stayed at 107,144. Not checked: a pAI walking into a human, other pAI chassis besides the one used, bumping while the human is pulling or being pulled, or any other mob (a commenter suspected others).

## Notes
- The three issues were tested together on one server.
- Making the pAI never dense also means projectiles and thrown items no longer hit it as a body; that was not tested.
- #88962 first appeared reproduced only after the fold-in/fold-out sequence; an initial direct move with the pAI spawned straight into holoform showed no swap, which was a test artefact.
