## Summary

Of the four dullahan reports, two had real defects on master. A dullahan's head speaks the raw text because the tongue's `handle_speech` blanks the body's message and has the head say it before `treat_message()` runs, so speech was not capitalized; and the head item kept a random name from before preferences were applied. Separately, camera consoles (xenobiology and others) did not move a dullahan's view because `reset_perspective()` swallowed the console's request while the dullahan was looking through its head. The branch fixes both. The other claims (examine from the head, emotes, hearing, vending machines, a debrained or eyeless head, wrong hair) did not reproduce.

## The bug

#79355 lists several problems for a dullahan player: random head preferences (wrong hair and moustache), no examining while viewing from the head, speech not automatically capitalized, and emotes not showing as text. #79336 says that a dullahan in head mode who opens a camera console gets the camera eye and can use the console, but the view stays on their character. #71020 says dullahans cannot use `*me` emotes or vending machines (a later comment narrows this to "can use `*me` but cannot hear audible messages"). #85054 says dullahans are deaf, do not see through their head, and have a debrained and eyeless head.

## Reproduction

All checks used the Lua editor on a test server running the Meta map. A dullahan with real preferences had to be built: dullahan is not a roundstart species outside Halloween, so the guest's species preference was set to dullahan in its preference value cache, hairstyle "Twintails" and the name "Pheebs Wildspark" were written to the preferences, a human was spawned, `apply_prefs_to()` was called on it, and the admin's ckey was moved onto it. A plain `species/dullahan` spawn has no preferences and is not suitable for the name check. Lua must not be run before "Initializations complete" (it took about 3 minutes on this server).

**Speech not capitalized (#79355).** The dullahan said "hello there". Chat showed `The head of Pheebs Wildspark says, "hello there"`, lowercase.

**Head name (#79355, "head prefs are random").** After preferences were applied, the head item was named "Breanna Sullivan's head", a random name, while `real_name` was "Pheebs Wildspark". Hair (Twintails), facial hair, eye colour and `real_name` were all already copied correctly (the head's `hairstyle` was Twintails), so the "random hair and moustache" part of the report was not reproduced.

**Camera console (#79336).** The dullahan clicked a slime management console. Reading values from Lua, `client.eye` stayed on the dullahan's head (name "Pheebs Wildspark"), `remote_control` was set to the "Camera Eye (...)" mob, and the two were not equal. Moving the eye did not move the view.

**Not reproduced on master**
- *Examine from the head:* the examine verb and shift-click on a crowbar and on a mob 4 to 5 tiles from the body, while viewing from the head, both printed the examine text. The examine "dark" check measures distance from the body, but every turf on this map has non-zero luminosity, so the check never triggered here; a dark-room case was not tested.
- *Emotes:* `*cry`, `*snap`, `*scream` and `*me` printed text to the dullahan ("Pheebs Wildspark cries."), because of the self-message path in `datums/emotes.dm`. What other players see was not checked from a second client.
- *Hearing (#71020, #85054):* a bystander 5 tiles from the body, standing at the head, said a line, did an emote, and triggered `visible_message` and `audible_message`; all four lines reached the dullahan.
- *Head organs and sight (#85054):* a human set to dullahan had `brain`, `dullahan relay` and `eyes` in the head, and the head overlays included head, both eyes, hair and facial hair. The view went through the head.
- *Vending machines (#71020):* clicking a Robust Softdrinks machine opened the vending UI with products.

## Cause

**Speech.** The tongue's `handle_speech` (`code/modules/mob/living/carbon/human/species_types/dullahan.dm:197-210`) is hooked to `COMSIG_MOB_SAY`, which fires at `code/modules/mob/living/living_say.dm:195`, before `treat_message()` at `:199`. It makes the head say `speech_args[SPEECH_MESSAGE]` and then blanks the body's message, so `treat_message()` (capitalization, accents, stutter and so on) never runs on what the head says.

**Head name and pitch.** `drop_limb` names the head `"[owner.real_name]'s head"` when the head is detached, at species-gain time (`code/modules/surgery/bodyparts/dismemberment.dm:211`), when the character still has its random name. `/obj/item/dullahan_relay/proc/on_prefs_loaded` (`dullahan.dm:263-276`) updates `detached_head.real_name` and the relay's own `name`, but never the head item's `name`. It also copies `detached_head.pitch = pitch`, which is the relay's own `pitch` variable, not the owner's.

**Camera view.** `/mob/living/carbon/human/reset_perspective` (`code/modules/mob/living/carbon/human/human.dm:86`) returns early for any non-forced reset while the species has `prevent_perspective_change` set, which a dullahan looking through its head does. The camera console assigns its user in `code/modules/mob/eye/camera/remote.dm` with `new_user.reset_perspective(src)` (line 70) and, on closing, `old_user.reset_perspective(null)` (line 58). Both were swallowed, so the view neither moved to the eye nor returned afterwards.

## Fix

Two commits, all in `code/modules/mob/living/carbon/human/`.

**Fix issue 79355: dullahan head speech is capitalized and treated, head named and voiced from prefs** (`species_types/dullahan.dm`)
- `handle_speech` now runs the body's `treat_message()` on the message and hands the treated text to the head.
- `on_prefs_loaded` also sets `detached_head.name = "[headless.real_name]'s head"` and copies the pitch from the owner (`headless.pitch`) instead of the relay.

**Fix issue 79336: dullahans looking through their head can use camera consoles** (`human.dm`, `_species.dm`, `species_types/dullahan.dm`)
- A new `/datum/species/proc/get_locked_perspective()` returns null by default; the dullahan returns `my_head`.
- `reset_perspective()`, while a perspective lock is active, now lets an explicit request for a view elsewhere (a camera eye, for example) through. When the default reset arrives afterwards (null or the mob itself) and the client's eye is not the locked perspective, it puts the view back on the locked perspective, the head. Otherwise the previous behaviour (ignore the change) is kept.

The lock itself is kept, so other resets are still ignored while a dullahan looks through its head.

## Verification

On the test server after the fix:
- Speech: `The head of Pheebs Wildspark says, "Hello there"`, capitalized.
- Head name: "Pheebs Wildspark's head".
- Camera: clicking the console gave `client.eye` = "Camera Eye (Pheebs Wildspark)"; moving the eye 3 steps east moved the view; ending the camera view put the view back on the head (4 tiles from the body at the time), and also back on the relay when the head was in hand.
- No new runtimes in the log, other than errors from the test scripts themselves.

Not checked: the pitch change by ear or in the code path beyond reading it; other `treat_message` effects (accents, stutter) on a dullahan; a real client with a second client observing; unit tests and a full round.

## Notes

- Emotes originate at the body, speech at the head. If the head is far from the body, bystanders near the head do not get the emote text. This was left unchanged as a design question.
- The dullahan hears only around its head (intended). Hearing relies on an upstream change (PR #78938) and `get_listening_mob`, which is the likely reason #71020 and #85054 no longer reproduce; this is inferred from code reading, not from bisecting.
- A whisper using the `#` prefix once produced odd text (`quiet words , "Sigh"`) in a quick test. It was not investigated and may have been a mistake in the test input.
- #85054's remaining complaint is only the shared hearing design (hears only around the head).
