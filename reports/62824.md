## Summary

Three unrelated chemistry issues were examined together. The missing temperature values in the Metalgen recipe paper (#62824) could not be reproduced on master, but the recipe HTML was malformed (an unclosed `<li>`, a stray `</ul>`, and a pH check that is always true), and a commit tidies that. Eigenstasium's ingest-only teleport (#63250) works as designed, so nothing was changed. Strange reagent (#67605) never revived bees because a dying bee is replaced by an `/obj/item/trash/bee` item, which `expose_mob` never sees. A new `expose_obj` on strange reagent now revives bee corpses.

## The bug

### #62824
The report says that the "old recipe" paper for Metalgen drops values: the cold recipe reads "cooling it below 216 degrees but not below degrees", with the lower temperature missing.

### #63250
The report says eigenstasium's first teleport (to where it was made) only happens when ingested, and that making ingestion behave like other chemicals' ingestion effects would send the player to the creation point *before* the recall point is set. The author suggests removing the ingest-only restriction and calling it a feature.

### #67605
Splashing strange reagent on a dead bee does nothing, so bees cannot be revived, unlike other simple mobs.

## Reproduction

### #62824 (not reproduced)
1. In the Lua editor, spawn `/obj/item/paper/secretrecipe/metalgen` and read its `raw_text_inputs`. The text read, for example, "heating it above 284 degrees but not above 490 degrees", with no blank values.
2. Call `generate_recipe()` 400 times on the metalgen reaction. Of those, 209 were cold recipes. For these, `overheat_temp` ranged from 1 to 200 and was never 0 or at/above `required_temp`. A cold paper read "cooling it below 248 degrees but not below 55 degrees".
3. The HTML of the paper was malformed on master: the heating branch printed "…not above 490 degrees<li> keeping your pH…" without a closing `</li>`, and there was a stray `</ul>` before "Mix slowly".

The blank value in the original 2021 report cannot be seen now. It may have been fixed earlier, or it may have been a rendering artifact of the paper UI.

### #63250 (not reproduced)
1. Spawn a human with a stomach and a beaker with 10u of eigenstasium at purity 1.
2. Using admin tools, transfer the reagent into the human with `trans_to` and the INGEST method.
3. The human moved from 107,144 to 106,154. The creation point was 107,156 with a tolerance of 3 tiles.
4. A test on a human with no stomach was inconclusive: the human just vomited the reagent.

### #67605 (verified)
1. Spawn a bee and call its `death()`. A bee corpse (`/obj/item/trash/bee`) replaced it.
2. Expose the corpse to a beaker of 10u strange reagent with TOUCH. On master nothing happens, because the corpse is not a mob. The code path is a reading of the source; the unpatched run was not captured as a separate step.

## Cause

### #62824
`UpdateInfo()` in `code/modules/reagents/chemistry/recipes/special.dm` (around line 255-295) builds the paper HTML. The heating branch (around line 281) opens `<li>` and never closes it; `dat += "</ul>Mix slowly<ul>"` (line 272) emits a `</ul>` that was already closed; and

```
var/datum/chemical_reaction/randomized/random_recipe = recipe
if(random_recipe)
```

does no type check (the variable is merely assigned from a typed var), so the pH and purity lines are printed for every recipe.

### #63250
`code/modules/reagents/chemistry/reagents/unique/eigenstasium.dm:44-80`: `expose_mob` with INGEST sets `data["ingested"]` on the stomach copy of the reagent. `on_mob_add` then sets `location_return` and only afterwards teleports to `location_created` if `creation_purity > 0.9 && data["ingested"]`. The return point is therefore recorded before the teleport, in the right order. The ingest-only restriction is intentional.

### #67605
`/mob/living/basic/bee/death()` in `code/modules/mob/living/basic/farm_animals/bee/_bee.dm:103` calls `spawn_corpse()`, which creates `/obj/item/trash/bee`. Strange reagent only implements `expose_mob`, which never gets called for the corpse.

## Fix

- **Fix issue 62824: close the list items and drop the stray tag in the secret recipe paper.** Closes the `</li>` in the heating branch, removes the stray `</ul>`, and replaces the always-true typecast with `istype(recipe, /datum/chemical_reaction/randomized)`. The text of the paper after rebuilding was well-formed, for example "…not above 565 degrees</li><li> keeping your pH…". This is cosmetic; it does not change the temperatures.
- **Fix issue 67605: strange reagent revives bee corpses.** Adds `expose_obj` to strange reagent in `code/modules/reagents/chemistry/reagents/medicine_reagents.dm`. For a bee corpse (not for INGEST/INHALE, and `reac_volume >= 1`) it calls the new `/obj/item/trash/bee/strange_reagent_revive()`, which shows "starts twitching!", waits 7 seconds (like mobs; the instant variant revives immediately), and replaces the corpse with a living bee of the same type and reagent. The shared helper `spawn_revived_bee()` is also used by the lazarus injector path.

No change for #63250.

## Verification

- #62824: after a rebuild, the new paper text was well-formed.
- #67605: on the test server, `bee:death()` produced a corpse; exposing it to a beaker with 10u strange reagent (TOUCH) printed "The bee starts twitching!"; after about 8 seconds the corpse was gone and a living `/mob/living/basic/bee` (stat 0, health 10) stood on the tile.
- Not checked: the instant strange reagent variant on bees, revival of a bee with a custom reagent (beegent), the lazarus injector path after the refactor, and a splash by a real player rather than a direct expose call.

## Notes

- The blank "not below degrees" text from #62824 may have been fixed earlier; it could not be seen on master.
- #63250 is a design question. Dropping the ingest-only restriction is the issue author's own suggestion; nothing was changed.
