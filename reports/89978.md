# #89978: Pacifists (gondola heart) can kill megafauna with a resonator, which never fights back

## Summary

A gondola heart makes its owner a pacifist and also adds the `hostile` and `mining` factions, so lavaland megafauna treat the owner as a friend and never retaliate. The resonator has no pacifism check, so a pacifist can still lay resonance fields on a megafauna and wear it down at no risk. The fix makes fields created by a pacifist still crush rock but skip living mobs when they burst, and stops a pacifist from placing a field on a tile that holds another living mob.

## The bug

#89978 describes walking up to a megafauna with a gondola heart implanted and hitting it with an upgraded resonator. The megafauna ignores the player and takes the damage anyway. #75397 reports the same thing from 2023, observed in a live round with Bubblegum and the Legion, and mentions gondola "genes". Both issues describe one mechanism.

Two things combine:

- The gondola heart gives `TRAIT_PACIFISM` and adds `FACTION_HOSTILE` and `FACTION_MINING`. Hostile mobs, megafauna included, check factions when choosing a target, so they see the owner as one of their own and never take a target, even after being damaged.
- Pacifism blocks the melee swing of the resonator, but the resonator places its field before that check, and the field's damage does not look at pacifism.

The result is a boss fight with no retaliation.

## Reproduction

On MetaStation, with the round started and an admin observing:

1. Using the Lua editor, spawn a human, spawn a `/obj/item/organ/heart/gondola` and insert it into the human (`Insert(human, TRUE)`), and put an `/obj/item/resonator/upgraded` in the human's hand.
2. Spawn a `/mob/living/simple_animal/hostile/megafauna/bubblegum` four tiles east of the human, and give the human a ckey so it can be controlled.
3. Check the human with the Lua editor: factions were `neutral,hostile,mining` and `pacifism` was present.
4. Walk next to Bubblegum and click it with the resonator (default automatic mode).

Observed on unfixed master:

- The chat said "You don't want to harm other living beings!" because the melee part was blocked, but a resonance field still appeared under Bubblegum and burst about 2 seconds later.
- After two bursts Bubblegum's health went 2500, 2480, 2460.
- Bubblegum's `target` stayed nil and its AI status was idle. `faction_check_atom(player)` returned 1 and `CanAttack(player)` returned 0. The player's brute damage stayed at 0.
- That was on station pressure (20 damage per field). On lavaland the pressure check triples it.

The hug/kiss/"gondola genes" part of #75397 was not needed: the heart alone is enough.

Everything above was done through the actual click on the resonator, not by calling procs directly.

## Cause

- `code/game/machinery/dna_infuser/organ_sets/gondola_organs.dm:28` gives the heart `TRAIT_PACIFISM`. `:38-44` (`on_mob_insert`) adds `FACTION_HOSTILE` and `FACTION_MINING` to the receiver. Hostile mobs reject targets that share a faction (`code/modules/mob/living/simple_animal/hostile/hostile.dm:259`, `faction_check_atom`), so a megafauna never fights back, including when it is the one being hurt.
- `code/modules/mining/equipment/resonator.dm:52-54` (`pre_attack`) calls `create_resonance()` (`:41`) before the attack is resolved, so the pacifism block on the melee hit does not stop it. `create_resonance()` has no pacifism check.
- `/obj/effect/temp_visual/resonance/proc/burst()` (`:122`) applies `resonance_damage` to every living mob on the tile (`:134-139`) without looking at the creator's traits.

So a pacifist can use the resonator as a damage source against mobs that, because of the heart's factions, will not fight back.

## Fix

One commit on the fix branch, `Fix issue 89978: pacifists' resonator fields no longer hurt living mobs`, changing only `code/modules/mining/equipment/resonator.dm`:

- In `burst()`, `harmless` is set when the field's creator has `TRAIT_PACIFISM`. In that case the loop over living mobs on the tile does nothing: no damage, no slowdown, no combat log. Rock is still crushed, and fields still chain to neighbouring fields. Because the check is on the field and not on the click, it covers automatic mode, manual mode, matrix traps, and mobs that are pulled or walk into a field, which were cases raised in the issue comments.
- In `create_resonance()`, a pacifist who tries to place or detonate a field on a tile that has another living mob gets the balloon alert "you don't want to harm!" and no field is made. This is for feedback, so the player is not left with a field that does nothing. The wording matches the existing Legion staff check (`code/modules/mining/lavaland/mining_loot/megafauna/legion.dm:85`).

This follows the suggestion in the #89978 and #75397 discussions to block pacifists from resonating mobs. The commit message says it also fixes #75397.

Alternatives raised in the comments and not implemented:

- Passing the field's creator to the mob so it can retaliate (Hatterhat). The gondola faction would still make it ignore the attacker, so this would need further changes to targeting.
- Removing the gondola heart's neutrality for a while after the owner damages a mob, as a stable pink extract does (ArcaneDefence). This is more general but touches the heart and mob AI, not just the resonator.

## Verification

On a build with the fix, with the same setup (Bubblegum two tiles east, plus a mineral wall tile north):

- Mining still works: the pacifist used the resonator on the rock, a field appeared, and the rock became plating.
- Clicking Bubblegum with the resonator showed the balloon "you don't want to harm!" and placed no field.
- In matrix mode (the resonator's mode toggled twice), a field was placed on an empty tile and Bubblegum was then moved onto it with the Lua editor. The matrix burst (blast sound, field removed), and Bubblegum stayed at 2500 health and the player took no damage.
- Control, with the Lua editor creating fields directly and calling `burst()`: a field created by a normal human on Bubblegum's tile took it from 2500 to 2480. A field created afterwards by the pacifist and burst on the same tile left it at 2480.
- The round's `runtime.log` had no runtime errors.

Not checked:

- Lavaland itself (only the pressure multiplier was reasoned about from the code, the fix does not depend on it).
- The non-upgraded resonator, which shares the same code paths.
- Other megafauna. Only Bubblegum was used. Hierophant ignores factions and is unaffected by this change.

## Notes

- Other pacifist-usable damage sources against faction-friendly megafauna are not covered. ArcaneDefence's comment on #75397 names modsuit bombs. #89978 also mentions throwing vending machines at megafauna through disposal pipes, and Jacquerel said he would allow that. This fix is deliberately limited to the resonator.
- A pacifist's resonator fields now never hurt any living mob on the tile, hostile or not, because the check is on the field's creator and not on the target.
- Matrix traps can still be laid on empty tiles by a pacifist; they crush rock but do nothing to mobs that later enter them.
- No upstream PR that fixes #89978 or #75397 was found when searching for the issue numbers and "gondola resonator".
