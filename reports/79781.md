## Summary

On master, alt-clicking the debug glasses turns x-ray vision on and a second alt-click turns it off again. What probably looked like "still x-ray" is that the glasses permanently grant meson vision, which keeps `SEE_TURFS` set (turfs stay visible through walls) after x-ray is off. The issue is probably stale: it dates from 2023, and the current code toggles through the clothing-trait helpers.

## The bug

The report says: spawn in debug gear, alt-click the glasses for x-ray, alt-click again, and x-ray remains.

## Reproduction

On the test server (round started; alt-clicks sent before the round started did nothing):

1. Spawn a human, spawn `/obj/item/clothing/glasses/debug` with the Lua editor, equip the glasses and take control of the human.
2. Alt-click the worn glasses twice as a player would (through the client).
3. Separately, call `click_alt` on the glasses from the Lua editor three or four times, both on glasses spawned directly and on glasses from `/datum/outfit/debug` (given with `equipOutfit`).

Observed:

- Real alt-click 1: sight went from `SEE_TURFS` to `SEE_MOBS|SEE_OBJS|SEE_TURFS`. Alt-click 2: back to `SEE_TURFS`.
- Lua calls on ordinary glasses: x-ray var 0, sight 16, then x-ray 1, sight 28, then back to x-ray 0, sight 16. The mob's clothing trait count went 7, 8 (with `xray_vision`), 7.
- Lua calls on the debug outfit's glasses: sights 16, 28, 16, 28, 16; the `xray` var 0, 1, 0.
- No related runtimes.

The issue could not be reproduced, so no fix was attempted.

## Cause

There is no toggle defect. `click_alt` (`code/modules/clothing/glasses/_glasses.dm:728`) calls `attach_clothing_traits(TRAIT_XRAY_VISION)` or `detach_clothing_traits(TRAIT_XRAY_VISION)` and flips `xray`. Those helpers (`code/modules/clothing/clothing.dm:293` and `:314`) add and remove a per-item source on the trait, and the sight update follows through the trait signals (`init_signals.dm:85`).

The debug glasses also list `TRAIT_MESON_VISION` in their permanent `clothing_traits` (`_glasses.dm:716`). With x-ray off, the wearer still has `SEE_TURFS` (sight 16) and keeps seeing turfs through walls. That is meson behaviour and intended.

## Verification

## Notes

- If the intent is for the off state to show no wall visibility at all, the permanent `TRAIT_MESON_VISION` on the debug glasses would need to change; that would be a design choice, not a bug fix, and was not tried.
