## Summary

Three reports about losing a limb were tested together. Hand swapping after losing the active arm (#96824) and a jumpsuit stuck after losing a leg (#80923) did not reproduce on this master, and keeping gloves on with one arm left is intended. For #76899 (nodrop items staying held after arm loss), the godhand and the arm laser implant behaved correctly, but removing an arm with an extended vorpal scythe implant caused a runtime and left the scythe on the floor. The fix is one condition in the scythe's `Retract()`.

## The bug

### #96824
The reporter said that after losing the arm in the active hand they could not swap hands, and that their gloves did not drop. Commenters said the glove behaviour is intended and only the swap is a bug.

### #76899
Items that cannot be dropped (godhand, extended arm implants, vorpal scythe) were reported to stay held after the arm holding them was removed.

### #80923
After a leg is dismembered, the jumpsuit was reported to be half-attached: still `w_uniform`, but not removable.

## Reproduction

All steps used admin tools on the test server after the round started, mostly the Lua editor to spawn and possess a human and call `dismember()`.

### #96824 (not reproduced)
1. Possess a human with gloves, holding an energy axe in the left hand.
2. Cut the left arm with the axe: combat mode, aim at the left arm, click self. The arm was cut open, then went through the real wound path (`/datum/wound/loss`, `apply_dismember`, `code/datums/wounds/loss.dm:29`), which calls `dismember()` as an attack would. The axe dropped, the left hand showed as blocked and the gloves stayed on.
3. Press the swap-hand key (X): hands cycled right, left (the lost arm), right without problems. The HUD swap-hand button also worked.
4. Further cases also swapped correctly: empty hand, holding an energy axe, a wielded fireaxe, and losing the off-hand arm while wielding a fireaxe in the other hand (including swapping onto the lost arm and back).

The abandoned-crate explosion variant was not tried. Dismemberment via `dismember()` calls and via the real wound path were both used.

### #76899 (vorpal scythe reproduced)
1. Spawn a human with the vorpal scythe arm implant and extend it.
2. Call `dismember()` on that arm.
3. The runtime log showed `Cannot execute null.apply damage()` at `code/modules/jobs/job_types/chaplain/chaplain_vorpal_scythe.dm:32`. The scythe was left on the floor and the implant's `active_item` still pointed at it.

The godhand (`/obj/item/nullrod/godhand`) deleted itself on dismemberment and nothing stayed held; the arm laser implant, extended and then dismembered, retracted into the implant. Both were fine.

### #80923 (not reproduced)
1. Give a fresh human a grey jumpsuit and call `dismember()` on a leg.
2. `w_uniform` and the inventory slot still held the jumpsuit, which was still on the mob (correct).
3. Clicking the jumpsuit removed it into the active hand; `w_uniform` became null and the slot was empty. Clothing and inventory code has no leg-dependent logic that would explain the report.

## Cause

### #96824 and #80923
No defect found. `swap_hand` (`mob.dm:952`) and `perform_hand_swap` (`living.dm:2699`) do not block on a missing arm. The only code that can block a swap is the two-handed component's `on_swapping_hands`. Gloves are only dropped when no hands remain (`code/modules/surgery/bodyparts/dismemberment.dm:186`, `if(arm_owner.num_hands == 0)`). The reports may describe states from the testmerged builds of their rounds, or from before later changes.

### #76899, vorpal scythe
`/obj/item/organ/cyberimp/arm/toolkit/shard/scythe/Retract()` (`chaplain_vorpal_scythe.dm:23-32`) punishes an unworthy sheathing:

```dm
if(scythe.empowerment >= SCYTHE_SATED)
	return ..()
...
owner.apply_damage(25, BRUTE, bodypart_owner, wound_bonus = 10, sharpness = SHARP_EDGED)
```

When the arm is removed, the call chain is bodypart `drop_limb` → `remove_bodypart` → organ `mob_remove` → `on_mob_remove` → `Retract()`, by which time `owner` is already null. The `apply_damage` call at line 32 runtimes and aborts before the base `Retract()` moves the scythe back into the implant. The refactor "Refactors arm cyberimplants (#98176)" reintroduced a problem upstream PR #77595 had once fixed.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 76899: no runtime or stuck vorpal scythe when its arm is removed". It changes the early-return test in `Retract()` to `if(!owner || scythe.empowerment >= SCYTHE_SATED)`, with a comment that no owner means the arm is being removed and there is nobody to punish. The base `Retract()` already handles a null owner by moving the item into the implant. No other changes.

## Verification

After rebuilding, a fresh human with the scythe extended had the right arm dismembered: the scythe's location was the implant, no item was held in the other hand slot, `active_item` was null, no runtime appeared, and the sheathing sound played.

Not checked: the pre-existing nodrop cases beyond godhand, the arm laser and the scythe; other arm implants; two-handed and dual-wield items being dropped with a lost arm; the abandoned-crate path for #96824.
