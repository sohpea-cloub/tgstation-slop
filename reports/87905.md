## Summary

When a quantum server resets, `scrub_vdom()` releases `generated_domain.reservations[1]`. That list belongs to the domain datum, which is shared by all quantum servers, and it keeps every past load of that domain. The first element is the oldest load. So the first load of a domain is released correctly, but from the second load of the same domain on, the live reservation is never released: its turfs, mobs, mob spawners and GPS signals stay in the world, and each further load adds one more copy. The fix gives each quantum server its own `domain_reservation` variable, set from the return value of `lazy_load()`, and releases that one. It also unregisters the server's signal handlers from the goal turfs.

## The bug

Players and admins reported domains staying loaded after the bitrunner finished and the server reset:

- #87905: four (the reporter later said seven) boss domains still loaded after the server had been turned off. A commenter saw the Wendigo corpse's GPS signal after the bitrunner had finished and left, and domains with random loot (Meta Central, Crate Chaos) spawning loot into the domain that had not been unloaded, making the new one uncompletable.
- #96403: the Revolution Aftermath and Beach Brawl domains were still loaded, with usable spawners, while bitrunners used a new domain. A comment adds that the earlier Beach Brawl copy had all its tiles replaced by pink genturf tiles.
- #96707: the domain looked broken, with several of them visible. A maintainer comment notes that broken turfs come from more than 10 turfs stacked on top of each other.
- #87395: ghost-role islanders spawning on Battle Island saw a broken map, which a commenter attributes to domains that did not unload and overlaid each other.

## Reproduction

Everything was reproduced on a quantum server on the test server with the Lua editor, by loading and resetting domains through the server's procs (`load_domain`, `reset(fast)`) rather than as a bitrunner using the console. Counts below are the number of turf reservations held by `SSmapping` and the number of reserved turfs.

On master:
1. Load Battle Island (`island_brawl`) on the quantum server and reset it. Everything is released as expected.
2. Load Battle Island again and reset. Afterwards the server's `generated_domain` was null as expected, but `SSmapping` still held 3 reservations and 9673 reserved turfs. About 7840 island turfs were still reserved after the reset.
3. Load a third time: 4 reservations and 17513 reserved turfs, with two full islands live at once.
4. A sampled maximum `baseturfs` list length was 9 on a leaked copy against 7 on a freshly loaded one, and it grows with every extra load (see Cause for why this matters for #96707 and #87395).

On the fixed build:
1. Load the Wendigo domain once: 2 reservations, 3054 turfs. Reset (`reset(1)`): 1 reservation, 1833 turfs (only the transit reservation) and `domain_reservation` null.
2. Load and reset again, the case that leaked on master: back to 1 and 1833.
3. A non-fast reset (which scrubs after 15 seconds) also released, in about 25 seconds.
4. Island brawl loaded twice with resets in between: both resets were clean.

## Cause

`scrub_vdom()` (`code/modules/bitrunning/server/map_handling.dm:223`) frees the domain with:

```dm
if(length(generated_domain.reservations))
    var/datum/turf_reservation/res = generated_domain.reservations[1]
    res.Release()
```

`generated_domain` is one of the shared `/datum/lazy_template/virtual_domain` datums found in `SSbitrunning.all_domains` (`load_domain()`, line 97-101). `lazy_load()` appends every reservation it makes to that datum's `reservations` list (`code/datums/lazy_template.dm:127`: `reservations += reservation`), and nothing removes a reservation when it is released. After the first load and reset the list is `[released_1]`. After the second load it is `[released_1, live_2]`. `reservations[1]` is `released_1` again, so releasing it does nothing and `live_2` stays. Every later load leaks in the same way. The list is shared across all quantum servers, so it can also hold other servers' reservations of the same domain.

Two further consequences follow, and they explain the reports about stacked or broken turfs:

- Domain maps contain `/obj/effect/baseturf_helper`s (Battle Island has 8). Their `LateInitialize()` (`code/modules/mapping/mapping_helpers.dm:20-35`) walks `get_area_turfs(our_area, z)`, which includes every live turf of that area on the shared reserved z-level, including leaked copies. Each pass calls `place_on_bottom()` (`code/game/turfs/baseturfs.dm:24`) and adds a baseturf layer. Past 10 layers, `baseturfs_string_list()` (`code/__HELPERS/string_lists.dm:17-24`) fires a `stack_trace` and replaces the turf with the flashing "report this" wall. This is the symptom in #96707 ("more than 10 turfs stacked") and the broken Battle Island in #87395.
- In `load_map_items()` (`map_handling.dm:137`), `goal_turfs` was a local variable and the server's `COMSIG_ATOM_ENTERED` and `COMSIG_ATOM_EXAMINE` handlers were registered on those turfs and never unregistered. A released turf keeps its signal registrations through `ChangeTurf`, and later reservations reuse those turfs, so a server's handlers stayed attached to unrelated turfs of other domains, other servers' domains and transit. (This part has its own issue, #93639, which this branch also addresses.)

## Fix

Two commits:

- **Fix issue 93639: unregister goal turfs and release the server's own domain reservation.** In `code/modules/bitrunning/server/_parent.dm` the quantum server gets two new variables, `goal_turfs` (a list of the loaded domain's send turfs) and `domain_reservation`. In `map_handling.dm`, `load_domain()` stores the return value of `generated_domain.lazy_load()` in `domain_reservation`, and `load_map_items()` fills the member `goal_turfs` instead of a local variable. `scrub_vdom()` now unregisters the two signals from each goal turf, cuts the list, removes `domain_reservation` from `generated_domain.reservations` and then `QDEL_NULL`s it, rather than releasing `reservations[1]`. Releasing the server's own reservation fixes the leak, and removing it from the shared list keeps the list from filling with released ones.
- **Fix issue 87905: bitrunning domains are unloaded again (fixed by the previous commit).** This commit has no code changes. It only records, in its message, that the previous commit fixes #87905 and its duplicates.

An alternative would be to pick the right element of `reservations` (for example, the last). That was not used because the list is shared by servers: another server may have loaded the same domain more recently, and the last element would be their reservation. Tracking the reservation on the server avoids that.

## Verification

On the fixed build, with the same Lua method: the Wendigo and Battle Island cases listed under Reproduction (loads and resets in sequence, reservations and reserved turfs back to the baseline each time, `domain_reservation` null after reset, both fast and normal reset). Because a leaked copy no longer exists, the extra baseturf layers should not appear. This was not measured directly after the fix, other than that the repeated Battle Island loads stayed clean.

One oddity on the fixed build: reloading 15 seconds after a non-fast reset, with the server's `is_ready` forced to true (which bypasses the cool-down), gave a "baseturfs list ... is 11" runtime and a load that hung in the `InitializeAtoms` late-loader loop. Waiting 25 seconds after a fast reset was clean (twice). This is probably an artefact of forcing `is_ready`, but it was not isolated, so it is unproven.

Not checked:
- Real play through the quantum console, with an avatar connected, a ghost role or a cache goal turf being entered. Only the server procs were driven.
- Several quantum servers loading at the same time.
- Whether the pink genturf tiles from #96403's comment have the same origin.
- Other domains than the Wendigo, Battle Island, and the ones noted above.

## Notes

- **#81672 (empty or half-loaded domain): not reproduced.** One plausible mechanism was found, not confirmed or fixed: `load_map_items()` iterates `GLOB.landmarks_list` globally (`map_handling.dm:142`) and `qdel`s the landmarks it uses, so landmarks belonging to another domain or a leaked copy could be consumed by the wrong server. A separate investigation of #83618 reported that concurrent loads on several quantum servers fail for that reason, which supports this. Scoping the scan to the domain's own reservation would be a hardening step; it was not done.
- **#93245 (random domain broken after a ghost-role spawn during deletion): not reproduced.** The ghost-role path (`load_advanced_npcs`, `map_handling.dm:112-130`) is dead code at master: `mission_min_candidates` is 0 for every domain (`virtual_domain.dm:82`, with no override anywhere in the codebase), so no candidates are ever polled. `ghost_spawners` is nonetheless filled from every `/obj/effect/mob_spawn` in the world by `load_map_items()`, so leaked or other domains' spawners end up in it. The leaked-domain state fixed here is a likelier explanation for "once corrupted, it stays that way" than the ghost-role path.
- The pairing of #96403 as a duplicate is based on the reported symptoms (old domains still live with spawners) and on the same cause, not on a reproduction of the exact round.
