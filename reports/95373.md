## Summary

The report says the machine wand's drone ("bug") is slow, cannot travel diagonally, and "just keeps going and going". On master the drone reached every diagonal target tried, at about half a second per tile. The speed is a fixed tuning value (`delay = 5`) rather than a defect. A harmless typo in the shared homing movement loop was found along the way, but it did not cause the reported behavior in any test. No fix was written.

## The bug

As reported: using the machine wand on a machine diagonal to the user, the drone fails to arrive and keeps moving indefinitely. The report names no other conditions beyond a round ID, so the reporter's exact map situation is unknown.

## Reproduction

Not reproduced. What was tried, using admin tools on the test server:

1. Spawned a human holding a machine wand (`/obj/item/machine_remote`), plus machines (a vending machine and a space heater) at many offsets from the user.
2. Used the wand on the machines two ways: by calling `interact_with_atom` from the Lua editor, and by real clicks from a mob that had been given direct control of the human.
3. Diagonal offsets covered: (1,1) as an adjacent click, (4,4), (7,2), (2,7), (5,-3), (-6,-1), (-3,-4), (6,6), (9,3); a 36-point grid within ±10 tiles; and 12 long ones up to (25,10), (-25,7), (22,21), (11,-18). Also tried: a machine with both orthogonal corner turfs walled, a machine placed inside a wall turf (APC style), and a ranged click at (-2,+4).

Observed: every drone reached its machine (the wand's `moving_bug` became null and its controlled machine was set). Travel took about 0.5 s per tile; 25 tiles took under 20 s. An adjacent diagonal click gave control immediately. No runtimes appeared.

## Cause

No defect matching the report was found.

- The drone is launched in `code/game/objects/items/machine_wand.dm` (around line 170) with `GLOB.move_manager.home_onto(src, thing_moving_towards, delay = 5, flags = MOVEMENT_LOOP_NO_DIR_UPDATE)`. A delay of 5 ticks is 0.5 s per tile, so "too slow" is a design/tuning matter.
- Homing uses the `move_towards` loop in `code/controllers/subsystem/movement/movement_types.dm`. Diagonal moves are split into cardinal steps by `Move` in `atoms_movable.dm`, and the drone passes through walls, which is why walled corners did not stop it.
- Unrelated typo in that loop, at `movement_types.dm:753`:

  ```dm
  if(y_ticker >= 1)
  	y_ticker = MODULUS(x_ticker, 1)
  ```

  The second line should use `y_ticker`. The effect is that the y remainder is lost or replaced by the x remainder. It had no visible effect in the tests above.
- A theoretical way to make a drone "go forever": if the target is on another z-level, `update_slope` sees both x and y deltas as 0 and the loop never converges. A normal click cannot target another z-level, so this was not pursued or tested.

## Fix

None committed.

## Verification

Nothing to verify, as no change was made. Not checked: the reporter's actual map, round conditions or client; the z-level scenario; any effect of fixing the `y_ticker` typo.

## Notes

- The issue may have been fixed since March 2026, or may depend on the reporter's exact situation (round 2604861); more detail from the reporter would be needed.
- If a maintainer wants to address the `y_ticker = MODULUS(x_ticker, 1)` typo, it is a one-word change in a shared movement loop, and so affects every `move_towards` user, not just the wand.
- If "too slow" is a real complaint, the lever is the `delay = 5` argument in `machine_wand.dm`.
