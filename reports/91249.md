# #91249: Flying or floating mobs drain stamina and drown in deep water

| | |

| Status | Verified (reproduced, fixed, checked in game) |

## Summary

A mob that is flying or floating over deep water is treated like a swimmer: it pays the swimming stamina cost and, once prone, takes oxygen damage. The swimming effect only skipped mobs that were elevated, buckled to a vehicle or mob, or had `TRAIT_SWIMMER`, and never looked at the mob's movement type. The fix skips both the entry cost and the per-tick drain when `movement_type & MOVETYPES_NOT_TOUCHING_GROUND`. The status effect is still applied, so draining resumes if the mob lands.

## The bug

A player with wings (from a potion), a MOD suit or any other way of not touching the ground who flies over deep water, such as the rainworld's, loses stamina quickly, as if swimming. If they are prone or end up in crit, they also take oxygen damage and can die. The movement speed penalty for deep water already ignored flying mobs, so only the stamina and oxygen effects were wrong.

## Reproduction

On master, using admin tools:

1. Start a round and observe. Open the Lua editor.
2. Turn two adjacent turfs on the same row into `/turf/open/water/deep_beach` with `ChangeTurf`.
3. Spawn two humans, one on each water tile. Give one the `move_flying` trait (`add_traits({'move_flying'}, ...)`), so its `movement_type` is 3 (FLYING|FLOATING); the other is the grounded control.
4. Set both humans' stamina damage to 0 and wait 14 seconds.
5. Read `get_stamina_loss()` on both.

Observed on master: the flyer and the control both had 7.5 stamina damage. The flyer was drained exactly as the grounded mob was.

This was reproduced with the trait applied in place, not with a real wing potion, MOD suit or jetpack. Oxygen damage on a prone flyer was not reproduced; it was read from the code.

## Cause

`/datum/element/swimming_tile` (`code/datums/elements/swimming_tile.dm`) charges swimmers in two places:

- `dip_in()` (line 76) applies the entry stamina cost. It returned early only for `TRAIT_SWIMMER`, mobs buckled to a vehicle or mob, or when the exhaustion probability roll failed (line 79).
- `/datum/status_effect/swimming/tick()` (line 138) applies the periodic stamina drain and the oxygen damage. It returned early only for `TRAIT_MOB_ELEVATED` (line 139) and for being buckled to a vehicle or mob.

Neither looked at `movement_type`. The `immerse` element (`code/datums/elements/immerse.dm:72`, `on_init_or_entered`) gives `TRAIT_IMMERSED` to any non-ignored movable that enters the turf. Only the overlay is skipped for such mobs, so flying mobs got the trait, which triggers `dip_in()`, which applies the swimming status. The `swimming_deep` movespeed modifier already set `blacklisted_movetypes = MOVETYPES_NOT_TOUCHING_GROUND` (`code/__DEFINES/_flags.dm:201`), so the slowdown was handled but the stamina and oxygen effects were not.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 91249: flying or floating mobs no longer pay swimming stamina cost or drown in deep water", changes `code/datums/elements/swimming_tile.dm` in two places:

- `dip_in()` skips the stamina cost when `floater.movement_type & MOVETYPES_NOT_TOUCHING_GROUND`.
- `/datum/status_effect/swimming/tick()` returns early under the same condition.

The early-return branch in `dip_in()` still applies the swimming status, as it already did for riders. A mob that stops flying while over water therefore starts draining again without needing to re-enter the tile. The existing blacklist macro was reused so the stamina rules match the movespeed rule.

## Verification

After the change the game was rebuilt and restarted and the same setup was used, except the flyer was spawned flying on a nearby tile and moved into the water with `forceMove`.

- After 14 seconds in deep water: flyer stamina damage 0 and oxygen damage 0, grounded control stamina damage 11.
- The flying trait was then removed. After 8 more seconds the flyer had 5 stamina damage, so draining resumed on landing.

Not checked: wings, MOD suits or jetpacks as the flight source (only the `move_flying` trait); a prone or crit flyer; `FLOATING` and `UPSIDE_DOWN` mobs separately (covered by the same macro, not tested individually); ventcrawling, which is not in `MOVETYPES_NOT_TOUCHING_GROUND`.

## Notes

- Upstream PR 91274 was found for this issue but was closed unmerged. It was not used.
- Mobs that are flying or floating are skipped for all deep-water effects handled by this element, including any swimming tile with other parameters. That follows the existing movespeed rule, but a maintainer may prefer a narrower condition.
