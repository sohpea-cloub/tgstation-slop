## Summary

The runtime in #80431 comes from a radial menu slice being clicked after its menu was freed; master already guards for that, so no change was made. Two other arm-implant bugs in the group were real. An emag could not reach a toolkit implant (toolset, paperwork) once installed, because nothing routed an emag to an installed organ (#41936); the fix forwards an emag aimed at the implant's arm to the implant. Fireman carrying or riding a borg deleted any prosthetic arm made from an item, such as the chainsaw arm, because it deleted every held `HAND_ITEM` (#94157); the fix skips items that are serving as prosthetic limbs.

## #80431: runtime when quickly switching tools through the radial menu

### The bug (as reported)
Spamming "use in hand" on a toolset or surgical arm implant while quickly clicking the radial slices produces runtimes: `Cannot read null.current_user` at the old `radial.dm,53`, in `/atom/movable/screen/radial/slice/Click`, with `src.loc` null.

### Cause (in the 2023 code) and current state
A slice outlived its menu: the radial menu was deleted, the slice's `parent` became null, and `Click` dereferenced `parent.current_user`. Master handles both halves of this in `code/_onclick/hud/screen_objects/radial.dm`:
- `set_parent()` (lines 10-15) registers `COMSIG_QDELETING` on the parent, and `handle_parent_del()` (lines 17-19) sets `parent` to null.
- `slice/Click()` starts with `if(QDELETED(parent)) return` (lines 59-60). `QDELETED` is true for a null reference, so the click is dropped.

### Reproduction attempt
With a toolset implant in the right arm on the test server, the radial menu was opened from the action button and a slice was clicked (extending the screwdriver), then retract, menu open and close were repeated. The runtime log showed no runtimes. The race itself could not be exercised: the admin tool used to drive the game makes one call at a time, so it could not click while spamming the action button. The conclusion rests on reading the guard in the code, not on a stress test.

## #41936: emagging an installed arm toolset implant does nothing

### The bug
Before the implant is installed, an emag can be used on it to unlock the integrated knife (toolset) or equivalent. Once it is in an arm, that no longer works, and the thread (2018) says it used to.

### Cause
The unlock is implemented in `emag_act()` on the organ (`code/modules/surgery/organs/internal/cyberimp/augments_arms.dm:244` for the toolset, line 275 for the paperwork implant). An emag reaches `emag_act()` only through clicking the target (`/obj/item/card/emag/interact_with_atom`, `code/game/objects/items/emags.dm:148-154`), and an installed organ cannot be clicked; clicking the person only calls the mob's own `emag_act()`. Nothing connected the two. This was established by code reading; the older path that made it work no longer exists.

### Fix
One commit, **Fix issue 41936: emagging a person aiming at the arm emags their installed toolkit implant**, in `augments_arms.dm`:
- `toolkit/on_mob_insert()` registers `COMSIG_ATOM_EMAG_ACT` on the implant's owner, and `on_mob_remove()` unregisters it (together with the existing drop-key signal).
- A new `on_owner_emagged()` forwards the emag to the implant's `emag_act()` when the emagger's `zone_selected` equals the implant's `zone`; otherwise it does nothing.

To emag an installed implant, aim at the arm that holds it and emag the person (or yourself). Both toolset and paperwork implants get this because the signal is registered in the shared toolkit base type.

### Verification
With Lua and an emag card, on a target with a toolset implant in the right arm and the implant's item list at 6 items:
- `interact_with_atom` with the chest selected: still 6 items, so no change.
- With the right arm selected: 7 items (the knife was added) and the result was 1.

Not checked: the paperwork implant; a real player emagging in the game UI (the emag was applied through a direct call to its `interact_with_atom`); the case of two toolkit implants; the effect on a left-arm implant.

## #94157: fireman carrying deletes object arms

### The bug
Picking someone up in a fireman carry deletes item-based prosthetic arms such as the chainsaw arm. A comment says riding a borg does the same.

### Cause
`equip_buckle_inhands()` in `code/datums/elements/ridable.dm` (lines 92-94 at master) removes hand items so that things like slappers cannot be used to grab on:

```dm
for(var/obj/item/I as anything in user.get_held_items())
	if((I.item_flags & HAND_ITEM))
		qdel(I)
```

`item_as_prosthetic_limb` registers the item with `item_flags |= (HAND_ITEM|ABSTRACT)` and the `TRAIT_NODROP` trait from `HAND_REPLACEMENT_TRAIT` (`code/datums/components/prosthetic_item.dm`, `register_item()`), so the loop deletes the chainsaw arm along with the slappers. Riding a borg goes through the same procedure.

### Reproduction
Using the Lua editor: a carrier was given a prosthetic nullrod or chainsaw arm with `make_item_prosthetic(..., 'l_arm')`, a victim was set resting, and `carrier:buckle_mob(victim, TRUE, TRUE, 16)` (`CARRIER_NEEDS_ARM`) was called. The chat reported that the carrier "can't get a grip ... hands are full", and the saw was deleted (accessing it gave a destructed-userdata error). Riding a borg was not reproduced separately.

### Fix
One commit, **Fix issue 94157: fireman carrying / riding no longer deletes item-based prosthetic arms**: the deletion now skips items that have `TRAIT_NODROP` from `HAND_REPLACEMENT_TRAIT`:

```dm
if((I.item_flags & HAND_ITEM) && !HAS_TRAIT_FROM(I, TRAIT_NODROP, HAND_REPLACEMENT_TRAIT))
```

### Verification
Repeating the same Lua steps after the fix: `buckle_mob` returned 1, the carrier's held items were "the chainsaw hand" and "the offhand", the saw's `loc` was the carrier, and one mob was buckled. No runtimes from the changed code appeared in the post-fix round's runtime log.

Not checked: borg riding after the fix; the carry-refusal message when the other hand is genuinely full.

## Notes

- #41936 is a design choice: aiming at the arm and emagging the person is one possible interface. Alternatives such as emagging the implant's action button or surgically exposing it were not considered. Maintainers may prefer a different gesture.
- The `HAND_ITEM` deletion removes any hand item, including slappers, to keep them from replacing the offhand. A prosthetic limb is the one case that must survive because it is the arm itself, hence the trait check.
