# #33758: Latejoiners spawn in space when the arrivals shuttle has been destroyed

| | |
|---|---|
| Issues | [#33758](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/33758): verified, fixed on the branch below |
| Status | Reproduced (by simulating the destruction) and fixed; fix checked on the test server |
| Fix branch | [fix/33758](https://github.com/sohpea-cloub/tgstation-slop/tree/fix/33758) |
| Tested on | tgstation master `87b93338`, 2026-10-23 |

## Summary

When every shuttle chair of the arrivals shuttle is gone, latejoin falls back to `get_last_resort_spawn_points()`. That proc picked any unblocked turf in the arrivals shuttle area, and a space turf is not blocked, so players were placed in space where the shuttle's floor used to be. The fix makes the fallback skip space and non-open turfs, and, when nothing safe is left on the shuttle, use a free turf in the station's arrivals hallway before giving up and using the error room.

## The bug

Per the issue, after a singularity ate the arrivals shuttle, people who joined the round late appeared at random places in space and no arrival announcement was made. A later comment reports the same with a bag-of-holding bomb: players spawned on seats that had fallen into the rift. The comments also debate whether the shuttle is supposed to repair itself (it is not; it docks permanently at the station after being damaged, and the "docking for repairs" message is a prompt for the crew), which is not what this fix changes.

What really happens is a fallback problem. The chairs are the normal latejoin points (`SSjob.latejoin_trackers`). Chairs remove themselves from that list when destroyed (`code/game/objects/structures/beds_chairs/chair.dm:77`). With the list empty, `send_to_late_join()` and `/datum/job/proc/get_latejoin_spawn_point()` fall back to `get_last_resort_spawn_points()`, which looked at the shuttle's area instead of the station.

## Reproduction

Done with the Lua editor on the test server, after starting the round and observing. The destruction was simulated; no singularity was used.

1. Delete every chair in `SSjob.latejoin_trackers` (16 chairs).
2. Change every turf in the arrivals shuttle area (`/area/shuttle/arrival`, 99 turfs) to `/turf/open/space`, as a singularity eating the shuttle would leave it.
3. Ask for a latejoin spawn point for the Assistant job: `get_latejoin_spawn_point()` on the job datum.

Observed on master: the returned spawn point was a `/turf/open/space` turf at 167,38,12 in the area "Arrival Shuttle". `latejoin_trackers` was empty (0), and `SSshuttle.arrivals.damaged` was nil.

The step 3 result was obtained by calling the proc, not by an actual latejoin. A real latejoin before the fix was not done; the real latejoin was done only on the fixed build (see Verification).

## Cause

`get_last_resort_spawn_points()` in `code/controllers/subsystem/job.dm:767` loops over the turfs of the arrivals shuttle area:

```dm
for (var/turf/arrivals_turf as anything in zlevel_turfs)
	var/obj/structure/chair/shuttle_chair = locate() in arrivals_turf
	if(!isnull(shuttle_chair))
		return shuttle_chair
	if(arrivals_turf.is_blocked_turf(TRUE))
		continue
	available_turfs += arrivals_turf
```

(`job.dm:772-778`). It prefers a chair, and otherwise any turf that `is_blocked_turf()` does not reject. Space turfs left in the area after the floor was removed are not blocked, so they pass and one is picked (`job.dm:780-781`). The error room is returned only when the area has no turfs at all or the area itself is missing (`job.dm:783-784`).

A second effect, not changed by the fix: the arrivals shuttle's hull check, `CheckTurfsPressure()` (`code/modules/shuttle/mobile_port/variants/arrivals.dm:124`), only iterates over the chairs in `latejoin_trackers`. With no chairs it never reports damage, so `arrivals.damaged` stays unset and the `arrivals_shuttle_require_safe_latejoin` config option (checked in `code/modules/mob/dead/new_player/new_player.dm:173`) does not block latejoining. This is why latejoin stayed allowed in the reproduction.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 33758: latejoiners no longer spawn in space when the arrivals shuttle is destroyed", changing `get_last_resort_spawn_points()` in `code/controllers/subsystem/job.dm`:

- In the loop over the arrivals shuttle area, skip any turf that is space or is not an open turf (`isspaceturf()` or `!isopenturf()`), before looking for a chair on it. This also keeps people out of holes of other kinds.
- If no usable turf is left on the shuttle, look at the area `/area/station/hallway/secondary/entry` (the arrivals hallway on the station) and pick a random turf in it that is open, not space and not blocked (`is_blocked_turf(TRUE)`).
- If that also yields nothing, behave as before: log the `stack_trace` and return the error room.

An approach that changed the shuttle itself (a handler for a mobile dock losing all its turfs, as discussed in the issue) was not attempted: the issue comments point out that shuttles are not necessarily made of turfs and that detecting full destruction is hard. The fallback is where the bad spawn was chosen, so fixing it there is small and local.

## Verification

On a fresh round with the fix built, the same destruction was applied (16 chairs deleted, all 99 shuttle area turfs turned to space):

- The spawn point for the Assistant job was now a `/turf/open/floor/iron` at 44,122,2 in the "Arrival Shuttle Hallway".
- A real latejoin as Assistant through the latejoin menu placed the new mob at 50,145,2, also in the Arrival Shuttle Hallway (a different turf from the first call, consistent with a random pick).
- The round's runtime log showed no new runtimes beyond the usual start-up lines.

Not checked:

- A real latejoin on unmodified master with the shuttle destroyed (only the spawn-point proc was called there).
- A real singularity or bag-of-holding bomb. Destruction was simulated by deleting chairs and replacing turfs.
- The case from the comments where chairs survive on turfs that were destroyed, such as the bag-of-holding rift. The chairs stay in `latejoin_trackers`, so this fix does not apply; that case depends on the existing pressure check and the `damaged` flag.
- Maps other than the one tested, and the behaviour when `/area/station/hallway/secondary/entry` does not exist or has no free turf (the code then falls back to the error room as before).
- Whether a partially destroyed shuttle that still has some intact floor but no chairs spawns people sensibly: such floor turfs are still accepted ahead of the hallway.

## Notes

- `CheckTurfsPressure()` only looking at chairs means a shuttle with no chairs is never considered damaged. Making it also treat an empty tracker list, or a missing shuttle floor, as damaged would let the existing "require safe latejoin" setting and the docking logic react. That was left alone as a separate design decision.
- The fallback sends latejoiners into the arrivals hallway without any announcement from the arrivals shuttle. The issue also complains that no arrival announcement is made for such players; that part is not addressed.
- The issue's triage note says this is partly a design question (whether and how the shuttle should repair or be replaced). This fix only stops people from being spawned into space.
