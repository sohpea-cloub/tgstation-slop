# #46696: Removing and Adding floor tiles/plating creates air

| | |
|---|---|
| Issues | [#46696](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/46696): not reproduced |
| Status | Not reproduced; no fix needed on current master |
| Fix branch | none |
| Tested on | tgstation master `87b93338`, 2026-10-23 |

## Summary

The 2019 report says that prying a floor tile up and putting it back (or building plating over space) creates air, so a vacuumed room can be refilled this way. On current master this does not happen: every tested route kept the gas on the tile unchanged, in a full vacuum and at partial pressure. The code explains why. Tile placement, tile removal and RCD/lattice plating all pass `CHANGETURF_INHERIT_AIR`, so `ChangeTurf` copies the old turf's gas mix onto the new turf and skips `Assimilate_Air()`. No fix was written.

## The bug

As reported in 2019: a player removes a floor tile, leaving plating, and places a tile again. Each cycle supposedly adds air to the tile. Repeating it was said to partly refill a depressurised room, make a vacuum area survivable, or feed oxygen into a plasma fire. The reporter also saw it on tiles placed over holofans in space.

Two comments suggest other things were going on. The first reply points out that #45701 (which fixed `CHANGETURF_INHERIT_AIR`) had just been merged, and another commenter could not reproduce it on master shortly afterwards. A 2022 comment says that on the Ice map, removing and adding floor tiles makes the air take the planet's default temperature and gas mix. That is a separate mechanism (see Notes).

## Reproduction

I tried to reproduce on master `87b93338` (MetaStation) with admin tools. The air stayed constant in every test, so the bug did not reproduce.

1. Join the round as a Station Engineer and give the character godmode (Lua editor).
2. Using the Lua editor, build a sealed 5x5 test cell in open space: reinforced walls around the edge and a 3x3 of iron floor over plating inside. There are no vents or scrubbers in the cell. Newly built tiles read about 101.34 kPa and 104 moles each.
3. Empty the nine inner tiles with `air:remove_ratio(1)` and `garbage_collect()`. Every tile then read `p=0, n=0` (the temperature read 293.15 K at that point).
4. Stand in the cell with 50 iron floor tiles and a crowbar.
5. Player-action test on the middle tile: click it with the crowbar ("You remove the floor tile."), then click it with a floor tile. The tile went iron floor -> plating -> iron floor, and its gas stayed at `n=0` at every stage.
6. Repeat 20 times by calling `melee_attack_chain` with the crowbar and then the tile from the Lua editor. Moles stayed 0 after every step.
7. Build plating over space. With a lattice built from rods and a tile placed on it, the new tile had `n=0`. With the RCD plating design (`/turf/open/floor/plating/rcd`, applied with `rcd_act` called from the Lua editor), a space turf became plating with `n=0` and the room total stayed 0.
8. Ten rounds of remove and place on three different tiles in the vacuum cell: the room total stayed 0 throughout.
9. Partial-pressure test: copy air from a normal station tile into the nine tiles and remove 80% of it. The room started at 187.2 moles in total, 20.27 kPa and 293.15 K at the middle tile. Ten rounds of remove and place on three tiles left the total unchanged at 187.2 moles.

Evidence: all readings were of `air:total_moles()` and `air:return_pressure()` through the Lua editor. The runtime log for the round had no runtime errors. Step 5 and the rods-and-tile part of step 7 were done by clicking in game. Step 6 and the RCD part of step 7 drove the same interactions by calling procs (`melee_attack_chain`, `rcd_act`) from the Lua editor rather than by clicking, and the loops in steps 8 and 9 were also driven from the Lua editor.

Not tested: holofans (mentioned in the report), the Ice map (Icebox) planetary-atmosphere case, and the other maps named in the report. One partial-pressure run failed with a Lua error (a callback to an item that had been deleted) and was re-run; only the re-run is described above.

## Cause

There is no defect on master. The inputs do not show what the code looked like in 2019; #45701 ("Fixes ChangeTurf CHANGETURF_INHERIT_AIR flag and add it to a...", merged) is the likely fix. The current code carries the air over:

- `code/game/objects/items/stacks/tiles/tile_types.dm:85`: `place_tile()` places the tile with `place_on_top(placed_turf_path, flags = CHANGETURF_INHERIT_AIR)`.
- `code/game/turfs/open/floor.dm:110-111`: removing a tile goes through `make_plating()`, which calls `ScrapeAway(flags = CHANGETURF_INHERIT_AIR)`. `remove_tile()` (`floor.dm:174`) ends in `make_plating()`.
- `code/game/objects/structures/lattice.dm:95` (RCD) and `:190` (placing a tile on a lattice) and `code/game/turfs/open/space/space.dm:187` (RCD on space) use the same flag when they put plating on top.
- `code/game/turfs/change_turf.dm:212-224`, `/turf/open/ChangeTurf`: when the flag is set and the new path is an open turf, it copies the current gas into a stash, changes the turf, then copies the stashed gas onto the new turf together with its excited state and excited group.
- `code/game/turfs/change_turf.dm:252-253`, `/turf/open/AfterChange`: `Assimilate_Air()` only runs when neither `CHANGETURF_IGNORE_AIR` nor `CHANGETURF_INHERIT_AIR` is set, so the new turf is not given the average of its neighbours.

```dm
/turf/open/ChangeTurf(path, list/new_baseturfs, flags) //Resist the temptation to make this default to keeping air.
	if ((flags & CHANGETURF_INHERIT_AIR) && ispath(path, /turf/open))
		var/datum/gas_mixture/stashed_air = new()
		stashed_air.copy_from(air)
```

Without the flag, a changed turf gets a fresh gas mixture from `initial_gas_mix` (created in `turf/open/Initialize`), which for an ordinary station tile is a full atmosphere. That was how a vacuumed tile could regain air each time it was rebuilt.

## Fix

None. The behaviour already matches what the issue asks for.

## Verification

The bug-free behaviour was verified as described under Reproduction: no air was created in vacuum, at partial pressure, or when building plating over space with rods and tiles or an RCD. No code was changed, so there was no before/after comparison. I did not run the unfixed code to check that the old behaviour was as described.

Not checked: holofans, the Ice map case, other station maps, tile types other than iron, and RCD behaviour beyond the plating design.

## Notes

- Suggested resolution: close #46696 as fixed by #45701 (`CHANGETURF_INHERIT_AIR`), with a note that current master was tested and conserves moles on all tried paths.
- The 2022 Icebox comment is a different effect. Turfs with `planetary_atmos` (for example the Ice map's surface tiles and the asteroid and ash-planet turfs under `code/game/turfs/open/`) exchange gas with the planetary mix in `/turf/proc/process_cell()` (`code/modules/atmospherics/environmental/LINDA_turf_tile.dm:332`), and the planetary mix is built from the turf's `initial_gas_mix` (`LINDA_turf_tile.dm:60-66`). A tile re-laid on such a turf will drift toward the planet's air, which can be cold and deadly, but that is the turf exchanging with its surroundings and not the tile creating gas. This was not reproduced; it is based on reading the code only. If it is still a concern it should be a separate issue.
