## Summary

The cult's "Prepare Blood Magic" action keeps its prepared spells in `blood_magic.spells`, and the slot limit is `length(spells)`. That list could hold deleted or `null` entries, or lose the spells entirely, so the cultist saw an empty hotbar slot but was still told they had the maximum number of spells (or got orphaned spell buttons that could not be picked for removal). Two ways to reach that state were reproduced on master: the cultist's mind moving to a new body, and dropping a crimson medallion. The fix keeps the spell/action link intact across a mind transfer, makes a spell remove itself from the list when it is destroyed, prunes dead entries before the slot count is read, and rewrites the medallion's over-the-limit handling. Whether these are the paths the three reporters actually hit is inferred.

## The bug

The three reports describe the same symptom from different angles:

- #87508: with four spells prepared, the reporter removed one (Conceal/Reveal Runes). The icon disappeared, but the Prepare Blood Magic menu still said they had four spells.
- #85688: a cultist's stun hand was absorbed by a heretic's antimagic. Afterwards the blue empower rune repeatedly prompted "pick a spell to remove" and would not let the cultist make a new one. Removing and re-adding cultism from the traitor panel cleared it.
- #89017: after using up Twisted Construction, it still counted as a prepared spell. The cultist could not carve another without a rune, and could not remove Twisted Construction because it no longer existed.

In each case, the displayed spells and `blood_magic.spells` disagree.

## Reproduction

All scenarios used the Lua editor on a test server. A human was given a player and the cult antagonist datum, and the prepared spells were created by hand (`new` on `/datum/action/innate/cult/blood_spell/<type>` with the mind as target, `Grant(human, blood_magic)`, and adding to `blood_magic.spells`). The real Prepare Blood Magic prompts and the carving were not used on master.

**Not reproducible on master (plain flows)**
1. With four spells, `qdel` one: the list dropped to 3, as it should.
2. Use up Twisted Construction: it removed itself from the list.
3. Let a stun hand be absorbed by a heretic: the spell was removed and the list went from 3 to 2.

This matches the comments on #87508 that the problem could not be reproduced from the reported steps. It also means the plain flows of #85688 and #89017 are fine.

**Reproduced on master: mind transfer**
1. Give a cultist four prepared spells, then call `mind:transfer_to(<new human>)`.
2. The spell buttons moved to the new body (after one `qdel`, three of four were left), but `blood_magic.spells` was empty (length 0) and each spell's `all_magic` was null.
3. Result: the buttons are orphaned. They are not counted, deleting them does not change the list, and they cannot be selected for removal from the prepare menu.

**Reproduced on master: crimson medallion**
1. Give the cultist `magic_enhanced = TRUE` and five prepared spells (the enhanced limit). Create a `/obj/item/clothing/neck/heretic_focus/crimson_medallion` and call `dropped(<cultist>)`.
2. The list ended as `[Electromagnetic Pulse | Stun | Shadow Shackles | Summon Ritual Dagger | nil]`. The spell was gone from the hotbar, but a `null` slot stayed in the list and still counted in `length(spells)`. This is the report's "icon disappears but the slot is still counted".
3. With fewer than five spells, `dropped()` indexes `spells[5]` out of bounds and never reset `magic_enhanced`.

(In the log of this scenario the first run started from six entries, because an earlier set of spells was still in the list; the final state still contained the `nil`. How exactly the `nil` entry stays in the list after `qdel` was not traced further.)

## Cause

In `code/modules/antagonists/cult/blood_magic.dm`:

- `blood_spell/Grant()` (line 123) always ends with `all_magic = BM` (line 128). When a mind moves bodies, `/datum/action/proc/on_target_mind_swapped` (`code/datums/actions/action.dm:434`) calls `Grant(source.current)` with no blood magic argument, so `all_magic` becomes `null`. The spell no longer knows which `blood_magic` list it belongs to.
- `blood_spell/Remove()` (line 131) only takes the spell out of the list when `all_magic` is set, and does so even when the spell is just being re-granted to another mob (a re-grant calls `Remove` on the old owner first).
- `blood_magic/Remove()` (line 18) `qdel`s every spell in the list unconditionally, including when it is only being moved away from the old body.
- Nothing removes stale entries before `Activate()` (line 48) compares `length(spells)` with the limit (line 56). So a `null` or deleted entry occupies a slot permanently.

In `code/modules/antagonists/heretic/items/heretic_necks.dm:99-102`, the medallion's `dropped()` does:

```dm
if(magic_holder?.magic_enhanced)
    QDEL_NULL(magic_holder.spells[ENHANCED_BLOODCHARGE])
magic_holder?.magic_enhanced = FALSE
```

This deletes the fifth spell and writes `null` into that list slot. This leaves a `null` entry (as observed), and it runs out of bounds when fewer than five spells are prepared.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 87508: cult blood magic keeps counting spells that were removed, used up or moved with the mind", changes two files.

In `blood_magic.dm`:
- `blood_spell/Grant()` only sets `all_magic` when a `BM` is passed, so a re-grant after a mind transfer keeps the old link. The description text is built once (only when `base_desc` is still null) instead of being appended to on every grant.
- New `blood_spell/Destroy()` removes the spell from `all_magic.spells` and clears `all_magic`, whatever the owner state.
- `blood_spell/Remove(mob/remove_from)` only edits the list when the spell is really leaving its owner (`owner == remove_from`), not when it is being moved to another mob.
- `blood_magic/Remove(mob/remove_from)` only `qdel`s its spells (and then cuts the list) when it is really leaving its current owner.
- New `blood_magic/proc/prune_spells()` removes `null` and deleted entries; `Activate()` calls it first, so the count used for the limit is always real.

In `heretic_necks.dm`, the medallion's `dropped()` now clears `magic_enhanced` first (so it is always reset), prunes the list, and then deletes spells from the end until the list is at `MAX_BLOODCHARGE`. Deleting a spell removes it from the list itself, so no `null` slot is left and there is no out-of-bounds index.

The fix addresses the shared list rather than the individual items in each issue, since the list is the single place where the count is made. A second option would be to keep `null`-safe checks around every `length(spells)` use. It was not taken: the prune call plus the `Destroy()` cleanup keeps the list correct at its source.

## Verification

After rebuilding, on the test server with the same Lua scenarios:
- Mind transfer with three spells: all three stayed in `blood_magic.spells`. A `qdel` of one and a use-up of another both shrank the list correctly.
- Crimson medallion dropped with five spells and `magic_enhanced`: the list went to 4, no `null`, `magic_enhanced` 0. With two spells: list 2, `magic_enhanced` 0, no out-of-bounds.
- `runtime.log` had no runtimes from `blood_magic.dm` or `heretic_necks.dm`.

Not checked:
- The reporters' exact click sequence (the Prepare Blood Magic prompt, removal from its menu, an empower rune and a real stun hand against a heretic) on master. The prompts were also clicked through on the fixed build, but no result of that was recorded, so it is not claimed here.
- Real mind-swap situations in a round (for example a cult mind swap, a body being gibbed and the mind moved, or admin "Make Cultist" toggling), which were represented only by `transfer_to`.
- #85688's route via the traitor panel (re-adding cultism cleared the problem for the reporter).

## Notes

- Which in-game event actually produced the reporters' stale list is a guess. On master, the plain flows (deleting a spell, a used-up Twisted Construction, a heretic absorbing a stun hand) all kept the list right. Both reproduced paths need the mind to move or a medallion to be dropped. #85688 mentions a heretic, and a crimson medallion is a heretic-linked item, which is consistent with, but does not prove, the medallion route for that report.
- `Remove()`/`Grant()` for these actions now has an `owner == remove_from` condition. A call to `Remove()` with no argument on a spell with an owner would therefore no longer take it out of the list. `action/Destroy()` calls `Remove(owner)`, and the new `Destroy()` handles the other cases.
- The spells' description text previously got a second "Deals X damage" line on each re-grant. The `base_desc` guard in `Grant()` avoids that as a side effect.
