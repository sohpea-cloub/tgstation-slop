# #85339: `can_see()` passes through a closed diagonal corner

| | |
|---|---|
| Issues | [#85339](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/85339): the helper bug was reproduced and fixed; the in-game symptom was not reproduced |
| Status | Optional hardening fix on a branch; no confirmed player-visible effect |
| Fix branch | https://github.com/sohpea-cloub/tgstation-slop/tree/fix/85339 |
| Tested on | tgstation master `87b93338`, 2026-10-23 |

## Summary
The `can_see()` line-of-sight helper treats a diagonal step between two opaque turfs (two walls meeting at a corner) as clear, and returns TRUE for a target diagonally adjacent through such a corner. The branch makes `can_see()` return FALSE in that case. Real space carp could not be made to target a player through a closed corner on master, because their target acquisition already excludes those candidates, so the symptom in the issue video was not reproduced. The change affects every `can_see()` caller, so it should be treated as optional.

## The bug
The issue (labelled Tested/Reproduced; the video was not viewable for this write-up) reports that basic mobs can see, and presumably target, through diagonal gaps between walls.

## Reproduction
At the proc level, on master, using the Lua editor:
1. Place a carp at (120,134) with walls at (121,134) and (120,135), forming a closed corner to its north-east.
2. Place a human at (121,135) (diagonally adjacent), then at (122,136) and (123,137) (further along the same diagonal).
3. Call `can_see(carp, human)` for each.

Result: TRUE in all three cases, although the only route is squeezed between two walls.

In-game, as a control, carps in open space and a carp walled in with one window side acquired and attacked humans, so the test setup works. With a carp boxed in by four walls and the human behind the corner at the same three distances, the carp never got a "Current Target" and the human stayed at 100 health after about two minutes. So the real AI did not target through the corner. One attempt to test keeping a target was inconclusive: the test human died of suffocation (space wind from modified floors). Setting the target by hand and moving the carp also did not settle anything (a direct move NE between the two walls returned false, so diagonal movement is blocked).

## Cause
`can_see()` is in `code/__HELPERS/atoms.dm:61`. It repeatedly calls `get_step_towards` and tests `IS_OPAQUE_TURF` on the intermediate turfs only. A diagonal step whose two orthogonal neighbours are both opaque is never examined, and when the target is diagonally adjacent the loop is skipped entirely, so the proc returns TRUE.

Basic mob combat targeting gets its candidates from BYOND's `hearers()` (`target_source/_target_source.dm:46`), which already drops targets behind a closed corner. Candidates then go through `is_valid_target` (`basic_targeting_strategy.dm:88`), which calls `can_see()` as its last check. So the helper bug exists but is masked for initial acquisition. Other consumers of `can_see()` (keeping a target, attacking, pet commands, other target sources such as `oview()`-based ones) could be affected; none was demonstrated.

## Fix
One commit, "Fix issue 85339: can_see() no longer passes through a closed diagonal corner":
- adds `is_diagonal_corner_blocked(from_turf, to_turf)` to `atoms.dm`: for a diagonal step it looks up the two orthogonal neighbours of the starting turf and returns TRUE when both are opaque;
- `can_see()` calls it on the first step and on every later step, and returns FALSE when it fires;
- removes a duplicated, unreachable `if(current == target_turf)` check.

## Verification
After rebuilding, `can_see()` on the test server gave: closed corner at distances 1, 2 and 3: FALSE; a straight wall between: FALSE; one corner side open, distances 1 and 2: TRUE; the closed corner re-checked at distance 1: FALSE; fully open, distances 1 and 3: TRUE.

Not checked: any in-game behaviour change (there was no visible symptom to compare), the other callers listed below, and no unit test was added.

## Notes
- `can_see()` is used in roughly 78 files: basic and simple-animal targeting, pet commands, AI freelook and malf modules, bots, turret-like and projectile code, tgui status checks, kinesis, heretic influences and others. After the change they all stop seeing through a two-wall diagonal corner. That is the intended geometry, but the blast radius is larger than the report suggests, which is why the change is offered as optional.
- If the issue video shows a mob acting through a diagonal gap by some other path (an `oview()`-based source, ranged attacks, turrets), that was not reproduced here.
