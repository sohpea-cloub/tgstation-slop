# #63794: Permanent split personality is not permanent

| | |
|---|---|
| Issues | [#63794](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/63794): verified and fixed |
| Status | Fixed |

| Tested on | tgstation master `87b93338`, 2026-10-23 |

## Summary

The split personality brain trauma deletes itself when its owner dies, yet it could be given at `TRAUMA_RESILIENCE_ABSOLUTE` ("permanent"), for example by an ethereal's crystal revival. The imaginary friend trauma behaves the same way. The fix adds a `can_be_permanent` var to brain traumas and makes `can_gain_trauma()` refuse to give an ABSOLUTE-resilience trauma whose `can_be_permanent` is FALSE. Both traumas set it to FALSE. They are still obtainable at lower resilience; they just can no longer be handed out as permanent.

## The bug

An ethereal who dies and crystallizes can regain life with a random brain trauma flagged as permanent. In the report, the player was offered a split personality, was told it was permanent, and then saw "You feel alone again" after dying. The trauma was gone even though it had been described as permanent. The reporter also confirmed in the comments that imaginary friend is "fragile in the same way". They also described a "re-enter body" prompt appearing on every revival, which the comments suggest may be a separate bug; see Notes.

## Reproduction

The check was done by calling procs directly through the Lua editor, not by playing through an ethereal death and revival.

1. On the test server, spawn a human and take their brain.
2. In the Lua editor, call `can_gain_trauma()` on the brain with the split personality trauma and resilience `TRAUMA_RESILIENCE_ABSOLUTE` (6).
3. On master this returned 1 (allowed). The ethereal heart's revival code (`code/modules/surgery/organs/internal/heart/heart_ethereal.dm:242`, `gain_trauma_type(BRAIN_TRAUMA_SEVERE, TRAUMA_RESILIENCE_ABSOLUTE)`) can therefore pick it.

The deletion on death is straightforward from the source and was not run through a real death.

## Cause

`/datum/brain_trauma/severe/split_personality/on_life()` (`code/datums/brain_damage/split_personality.dm`, around lines 68-75) calls `qdel(src)` once the owner is dead (after switching back to the owner if the other personality was in control). The imaginary friend's `on_death()` (`code/datums/brain_damage/imaginary_friend.dm:36-38`) does the same ("friend goes down with the ship"). Nothing stopped such traumas from being gained at ABSOLUTE resilience, so a "permanent" trauma removed itself on death. As the comments in the issue note, the trauma bypasses the normal cure mechanism (neurine, surgery) because it deletes itself.

In `can_gain_trauma()` (`code/modules/mob/living/brain/brain_item.dm`, around line 620), the resilience was taken from the trauma's default if none was given, and there was no check about whether the trauma can survive the owner's death.

## Fix

**Fix issue 63794: split personality and imaginary friend can no longer be given as permanent traumas.**

- `code/datums/brain_damage/brain_trauma.dm`: new var `can_be_permanent` (default TRUE), documented as FALSE for traumas that remove themselves on their own.
- `code/datums/brain_damage/split_personality.dm` and `imaginary_friend.dm`: set `can_be_permanent = FALSE`. These are one-line additions with no procs changed.
- `code/modules/mob/living/brain/brain_item.dm`: `can_gain_trauma()` returns FALSE when the resilience is ABSOLUTE and `!initial(trauma.can_be_permanent)`.

The issue's maintainer comments say that the death-delete was intentional and that the plan was to take the trauma out of the permanent category. The fix follows that: it excludes these traumas from permanent gain instead of making them survive death.

## Verification

After rebuilding the test server, using Lua calls on a spawned human's brain:

- `can_gain_trauma(split_personality, ABSOLUTE)` went from 1 to 0.
- `can_gain_trauma(imaginary_friend, ABSOLUTE)` returned 0.
- `can_gain_trauma(narcolepsy, ABSOLUTE)` still returned 1.
- `can_gain_trauma(split_personality, 2)` (surgery resilience) still returned 1, so it can still be obtained normally.
- 60 calls of `gain_trauma_type(severe, ABSOLUTE)` produced 12 different trauma types and never split personality.

Not checked: an actual ethereal death and crystal revival, the trauma's behaviour on a real death, and traumas from other sources that might be given as permanent by other code.

## Notes

- The reporter's side complaint that a revived body keeps getting a "re-enter body" prompt was not reproduced or investigated. A commenter suggested it may be a separate bug (several ghosts assigned to one mob).
- The reporter asked whether the trauma should survive death; the fix instead removes it from the permanent category, which the reporter noted "sidesteps" the problem.
- `can_be_permanent` is a general var; other self-removing traumas can opt in with one line.
