## Summary

Both reports describe pre-rework zombie behaviour (a decapitated zombie rising headless; duplicate brain, ears and tongue after reattaching a zombie's head). The zombie code has since been rewritten as a status effect plus an infection organ, and on master a decapitated zombie stayed dead and reattaching its head produced no duplicate organs. The exact step sequence of #73425 (removing ears and tongue by surgery while alive, then a second head) was not completed, so #73425 is only partly tested. No fix was committed. Two unrelated defects in the new zombie code were found and are described under Notes.

## The bug

- #73425: after head-organ surgery on a living infected player, who then died and revived as a zombie, decapitating and reattaching the head reportedly gave a second brain, ears and tongue existing both outside and inside the body, broken health analyzers, a null head slot that made the zombie undecapitatable, and a second head that generated another brain. The reporter said not all of this happened every time.
- #76991: a zombie that was decapitated and died rose again without a head.

## Reproduction

All steps used the Lua editor on the test server, creating the situation by script instead of through romerol or surgery.

**Zombie decapitated after rising (#76991, and the core of #73425):**
1. Spawn a human, give it a zombie infection organ (the stand-in for romerol), kill it, and wait about 50 seconds. It rose as a zombie as expected.
2. Dismember the zombie's head.
3. Observed: the infection organ was deleted at once, the head kept its brain, eyes, ears and the zombie tongue, and the body stayed dead for more than 60 seconds. It did not rise headless.
4. Reattach the head (`try_attach_limb`) to the body. Observed: organs were the normal set (lungs, heart, stomach, liver, appendix, brain, eyes, ears, tongue), no duplicates, no second brain, and the body stayed dead.

**Infected but not yet zombie, decapitated alive (#76991 variant):**
1. A living human with an infection organ was decapitated. It died; the infection organ ended up in the head with no owner and no revival timer, and the body did not rise.
2. Reattaching the head gave normal organs and the infection was owned by the body again; it rose as a zombie after its timer, which is the designed behaviour.

**Not done:** the exact #73425 sequence (ears and tongue removed while alive, then death, revival, decapitation, reattachment, a second decapitation, attaching a second head to a body that already has one, and a health analyzer on the corpse). An attempt to run it did not complete because the Lua script never resumed after a wait, and it was not repeated. The runtime log was checked for the other findings (below).

## Cause

No cause was found for the reported symptoms; the code they came from no longer exists. The relevant code is now `/datum/status_effect/zombie` in `code/modules/mob/living/carbon/human/species_types/zombies.dm` and the infection organ in `code/modules/zombie/organs.dm`. When the head comes off, `zombie_died_somehow()` (`zombies.dm:252`) deletes the infection organ, which stops the zombie from rising.

## Fix

None.

## Verification

Not applicable. What was and was not tested is listed under Reproduction. Not tested: surgery-based removal of ears and tongue, a second head, a health analyzer, and a real romerol infection (an infection organ was inserted directly).

## Notes

Likely closable: both reports are probably fixed by the zombie rework, but the exact #73425 sequence is unconfirmed.

Two side findings in the current code, neither fixed:

1. **Zombie status lingers on a dead, decapitated zombie.** `zombie_died_somehow()` ends with `qdel(infection) // this will in turn qdel this status effect` (`zombies.dm:258`), but the handler that deletes the status, `organ_removed()` (`:247`), is only registered when the status itself created the infection (`:129`). For a romerol zombie the organ already existed, so the status effect stays. In the test the corpse kept the zombie traits, husk and regenerator, and the original tongue was left in nullspace. A candidate fix is to delete the status effect (`if(!QDELETED(src)) qdel(src)`) after deleting the infection, or to register `organ_removed` regardless of who created the organ. Whether it relates to the #73425 symptoms was not checked.
2. **The zombie stun cap is broken.** `on_spec_stun(list/amount)` (`:219`) is registered on `COMSIG_HUMAN_SPEC_STUN`, which sends the mob as its first argument and the stun list second (`status_procs.dm:36`: `SEND_SIGNAL(src, COMSIG_HUMAN_SPEC_STUN, stun_amount)`). The handler's first parameter therefore receives the mob, and the runtime log showed "cannot compare 20 to the human chest".
3. Cosmetic: reattaching a head prints "<body> saws <head> open and pulls out a brain!" when the old head stump is dropped (`drop_organs` in `head.dm`, called from `try_attach_limb` in `dismemberment.dm`).
