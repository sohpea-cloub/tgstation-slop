## Summary

A magic mirror is meant to leave a player who turns into a monkey "unusually clever", able to keep using the mirror and their other items. It did not: the mirror granted the literacy and dexterity traits before the species change, and the change itself then took them away, so the monkey ended up with neither and could not open the mirror again. The fix adds a second mirror hook that runs after `set_species()` and moves the trait-granting code into it. Of the two related issues in the group, the lesser mirror's race list (#79073) is already clean on master, and the pride mirror's organs (#91975) came out right for every species that was tested.

## The bug

### #91477 and #92263 (same bug)

A human who used the magic mirror's Change Race option and picked Monkey saw only "You feel unusually monkey-like." The "You feel quite intelligent." message that the mirror is supposed to add did not appear. The monkey could not use the mirror again to turn back: the mirror's radial menu did not reopen, and clicking the mirror did nothing useful. #92263 reports the same thing from the wizard den: the wizard also cannot open the spellbook as a monkey, which is why it was filed as an oversight. #92263 was triaged as a duplicate of #91477 and the testing confirmed it has the same cause.

Natural monkeys are not supposed to operate machinery. The Monkey species' own confirmation text (`code/modules/mob/living/carbon/human/species_types/monkeys.dm:61`) says "Natural monkeys cannot operate machinery or most tools with their paws, but unusually clever monkeys or those that were once something else can." The magic mirror has a hook that grants the two traits so that a wizard cannot soft-lock themselves, and that hook was not working.

### #79073

The report is a single screenshot of the lesser magic mirror's race list, which the reporter considered badly formatted. The screenshot is not part of the material reviewed, so what exactly looked wrong is unknown.

### #91975

The report says that after using the pride mirror to change species, the organs "aren't fully changed (if changed at all)". It does not name a species or an organ.

## Reproduction

### #91477 / #92263 on master

1. On the test server, using the Lua editor, spawn a human and a `/obj/structure/mirror/magic` next to it, and give the human to a player (set the human's `ckey`).
2. As that human, click the mirror to open its radial menu and choose Change Race.
3. In the list choose Monkey and answer Yes to the "Become a Monkey?" prompt.
4. Chat showed only "You feel unusually monkey-like." The "You feel quite intelligent." line was missing.
5. In the Lua editor, `_has_trait(human, "advancedtooluser")` and `_has_trait(human, "literate")` both returned 0 for the new monkey. The mirror's radial menu did not reopen, and the monkey could not use the mirror.

This was done through the player-facing action (the radial menu and the tgui prompts), not by calling procs directly. The traits the human had before the change were not read out in game before the species change; the statement that the human had both is inferred from the code (see Cause).

### #79073

1. Spawn a `/obj/structure/mirror/magic/lesser` and open its Change Race list (also done by reading `selectable_races` after `update_choices()` in the Lua editor).
2. The list was a plain, alphabetically sorted list of names: Cerulean, Ethereal, Felinid, Human, Lizardperson, Mothman, Plasmaman. Nothing was malformed, so the issue did not reproduce. The pride mirror's list read the same way (Abductor, Android, Ash Walker, ..., Monkey, ...).

### #91975

1. Using the Lua editor, spawn a pride's mirror and read its `selectable_races` (27 species).
2. For each species, create a fresh human, call `set_species()` with it, and compare every organ slot with what the species asks for (`get_mutant_organ_type_for_slot`). This calls `set_species()` directly; it does not go through the mirror's UI. The mirror's Change Race code ends in the same `set_species()` call.
3. Every slot matched, with three exceptions: `zombie_infection` for High-Functioning Zombie (the zombie species is supposed to carry it, the check's expected value was none), and the cosmetic external organs `frills` (Silver Scale) and `horns` (Lizardperson), which depend on the body's DNA features.
4. A second, partial pass went species to species (starting from Lizardperson, Mothman, Felinid, Plasmaman, Ethereal and others, then changing to every pride-mirror species). The only extra mismatch seen was a missing `spines` organ for Plasmaman to Silver Scale, Ethereal to Ash Walker and Ethereal to Lizardperson. That pass was not finished; some starting species did not resolve in the script and were skipped.

## Cause

### #91477 / #92263

`change_race()` in `code/game/objects/structures/mirror.dm` called the mirror's hook before changing species:

```dm
on_species_change(race_changer, newrace)                              // line 219
race_changer.set_species(new_race_path, icon_update = FALSE)          // line 220
```

The magic mirror's `on_species_change()` (`mirror.dm:425-432`) grants `TRAIT_LITERATE` and `TRAIT_ADVANCEDTOOLUSER`, but returns early if the user already has both:

```dm
if(HAS_TRAIT(race_changer, TRAIT_ADVANCEDTOOLUSER) && HAS_TRAIT(race_changer, TRAIT_LITERATE))
	return
```

A human has both traits, so at that point the hook did nothing. The human gets them from the brain organ (`organ_traits` in `code/modules/mob/living/brain/brain_item.dm:22`). `set_species()` then replaced the human brain with the Monkey species' primate brain, whose `organ_traits` are only `TRAIT_CAN_STRIP`, `TRAIT_PRIMITIVE` and `TRAIT_GUN_NATURAL` ("No literacy or advanced tool usage", `monkeys.dm:116`). The brain-sourced traits left with the old brain and nothing replaced them.

Without `TRAIT_ADVANCEDTOOLUSER`, a human-type monkey's unarmed click goes to `attack_paw()` instead of `attack_hand()` (`code/_onclick/other_mobs.dm:68-71`), so the mirror's `attack_hand()` is never reached. That is why the mirror could not be used again.

The heretic mirror's random species change in `change_something()` (`mirror.dm:491-492`) has the same ordering: hook, then `set_species()`.

### #79073

Not applicable. On master the race list is built in `update_choices()` (`mirror.dm:42-54`) as an associative list of species name to type, then sorted, and `change_race()` passes it to `tgui_input_list()`.

### #91975

No defect found in the mirror. The pride mirror has no species-change code of its own; it inherits `change_race()` and calls `set_species()`, which regenerates organs.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 91477: magic mirror species change keeps literacy and tool use", changing `code/game/objects/structures/mirror.dm` only:

- Added `/obj/structure/mirror/proc/after_species_change()`, a no-op hook documented as running once the new species' traits and organs are in place. The existing `on_species_change()` comment now says it runs before the change.
- `change_race()` and the heretic mirror's `change_something()` now call `after_species_change()` straight after `set_species()`.
- The magic mirror's trait-granting code moved from `on_species_change()` to `after_species_change()` (calling `..()` first). It keeps its early return and its "You feel quite intelligent." message, and still adds the traits with `SPECIES_TRAIT`. The heretic mirror keeps using `on_species_change()` because it compares the old species with the new one before the change.

The alternative of changing the primate brain, or the Monkey species, was not chosen: natural monkeys are meant to lack these traits, and the mirror already had a dedicated hook for exactly this, so the smallest correct change was to run it at the right time.

## Verification

After the fix the same flow was repeated on a rebuilt test server (a human on a magic mirror, Change Race, Monkey, Yes):

- Chat showed "You feel unusually monkey-like." followed by "You feel quite intelligent."
- The mirror's radial menu reopened for the monkey.
- Choosing Human from the mirror's list then worked ("You feel like your old self."), and the skin tone prompt appeared as normal.

Not checked:

- The heretic mirror's random species change (`change_something()`): the edit compiled but was never exercised in game.
- Pride's mirror and the lesser mirror with a monkey result: the same code path, but not tried.
- A wizard spellbook as a monkey (#92263's second complaint): inferred to work from the dexterity and literacy traits being back, not opened in game.
- The trait values were not read back with the Lua editor after the fix; the check was the chat message plus the mirror reopening.

## Notes

- The traits are added with the `SPECIES_TRAIT` source, and the Monkey species does not list them as inherent, so they stay until something removes them. The code comment says this is intentional ("It's magic i aint gotta explain shit"). A player who goes Monkey, then Human through the mirror, or any other species, keeps the trait source; this is harmless because humans already have both from their brain.
- The commit message says `set_species()` "removed the old species' traits". Reading the source, the human's literacy and dexterity come from the brain organ, not from the Human species' inherent traits, so the precise cause is the brain swap described above. The fix is the same either way.
- Only mirrors are affected: nothing about the Monkey species, the primate brain, or other ways of becoming a monkey was changed.
- #79073 looks already fixed upstream: the name-keyed list in `update_choices()` is what the choice prompt now shows.
- #91975 is probably a vague report. The only mismatches seen were cosmetic external organs (frills, horns, spines) that depend on the body's DNA features, plus the zombie infection organ that the zombie species is meant to have. If the reporter had a specific species in mind, a repro with the species names would be needed.
