# #91153: Changeling shields work for a pilot inside an open-cockpit mech

## Summary

Mechs refuse entry to anyone holding items, except items flagged `ABSTRACT` or `HAND_ITEM`. The changeling flesh shield is `ABSTRACT`, so a changeling can climb into an open-cockpit mech while holding it, and projectiles that reach the pilot are then checked against the shield. The fix makes the shield's `hit_reaction` do nothing while the wearer is inside a mech. Whether changelings should be able to enter mechs while holding the shield at all is left to maintainers.

## The bug

The report (which had not been tested in game by its author) says a changeling can deploy one or two flesh shields, get into an open-cockpit mech such as a Ripley, and use the shields to block projectiles, which the recent restriction on entering mechs with held items was meant to prevent for riot and ballistic shields. Comments on the issue disagree on whether this is a problem: one notes shields break after a few hits and changelings can already hide in mechs; another says the shield could seal an open cockpit.

## Reproduction

1. On the test server, using the Lua editor, spawn a human holding a changeling shield (`/obj/item/shield/changeling`, `remaining_uses` 10), and spawn a base Ripley (open cockpit, no `IS_ENCLOSED`). Possess the human.
2. Have the human enter the mech with `mob_try_enter`. Entry succeeded ("climbs into" message).
3. Call the mech's `projectile_hit` with a 9mm projectile aimed at the chest, ten times.

Observed on master: the shield's `remaining_uses` fell from 10 to 0, meaning `hit_reaction` ran for every shot (the shield has a 50% block chance). Real fired projectiles were also seen hitting the mech and reaching the pilot (the pilot is only hit when the zone is head or chest).

Entry and projectile hits were done by calling procs from Lua, not by playing a changeling. Melee was not tested separately.

## Cause

- `code/modules/vehicles/mecha/mecha_mob_interaction.dm:38` rejects held items unless `item_flags & (ABSTRACT|HAND_ITEM)`. The changeling shield is abstract, so it passes.
- For open-cockpit mechs, `projectile_hit` in `code/modules/vehicles/mecha/mecha_defense.dm:122-131` forwards head and chest hits to a random occupant (`hitmob.projectile_hit(...)`).
- The occupant's shield check then calls `/obj/item/shield/changeling/hit_reaction` (`code/modules/antagonists/changeling/powers/mutations.dm:519`), which blocks and consumes a use.

## Fix

Commit "Fix issue 91153: changeling shields no longer block attacks for a pilot inside a mech" adds an early `return FALSE` at the top of the shield's `hit_reaction` (after the overwhelming-attack check, before a use is consumed) when `ismecha(owner.loc)`.

Entry is still allowed, and other abstract items (the arm blade and similar) are unaffected. The alternative, which was not implemented, is to deny mech entry while the shield is held.

## Verification

After rebuilding and repeating the same Lua steps, `remaining_uses` stayed at 10 across ten hits (the shield was never consulted), the mech's integrity stayed at 200, and the pilot took the hits.

Not checked: melee blocking inside a mech (it goes through the same `hit_reaction`, so the guard should cover it), a real changeling using the ability, and Paddy or Bess variants.

## Notes

- The balance question (should a changeling be able to enter a mech with abstract items at all, and are flesh shields in a cockpit acceptable) is a maintainer decision; the fix only removes the shielding effect.
- Because the guard is in the shield rather than in the mech, other items that rely on `ABSTRACT` to pass the entry check are not covered by it.
