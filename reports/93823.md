## Summary

Cyborgs can climb onto tables by click-dragging themselves onto them, and a glass table breaks under any non-small mob, so a borg that climbs one gets stunned and hit by a shard. The code does this on purpose, so #93823 was not changed. #95114 (borgs unequip their modules when stunned) also follows from deliberate code, and also stays as it is. The one real bug was #69989: a cyborg's `update_icons()` called `cut_overlays()` and wiped the typing and thinking bubble. The fix re-adds those overlays after the cut.

## #93823: borgs can climb onto (glass) tables and get stunned

### The bug

The reporter wrote "idk just like walk on table i guess?" and later added that they could climb every table. Their screenshots were not viewable. The reporter saw a borg end up on a glass table, which then broke and stunned it.

### Reproduction

1. On the test server, spawn a `/mob/living/silicon/robot` and a glass table with the Lua editor, and take control of the borg.
2. Walk the borg into the table. This is blocked (the tile ahead was reported as the glass table, dense). Plain walking does not put a borg on a table.
3. Click-drag the borg onto the table. The borg got "You start climbing onto the glass table", and after the delay "The glass table breaks! You're hit by the shard! You climb onto the glass table." The borg was stunned by the climb (about 2 s) and paralysed for 100 ticks by the shattering table.

So the "walk" in the report appears to mean click-dragging.

### Cause

- Silicons have `TRAIT_FENCE_CLIMBER` (`code/modules/mob/living/silicon/silicon.dm:67`).
- The climbable element's `mousedrop_receive` (`code/datums/elements/climbable.dm:132-137`) lets any living mob with that trait (or `TRAIT_CAN_HOLD_ITEMS`) climb by click-drag.
- Climbing applies a stun (`climbable.dm:99`, `user.Stun(adjusted_climb_stun)`).
- Glass tables shatter under a living, non-small mob that is on the ground in gravity (`code/game/objects/structures/tables_racks.dm:569-573`), and `table_shatter` throws a shard at the victim and paralyses it (`tables_racks.dm:575-587`).

PR #84604, "Fixes borg climbing", restored this ability on purpose after a regression from #78887, so the behaviour is deliberate.

### Fix

None. If maintainers want borgs barred from tables, `TRAIT_FENCE_CLIMBER` would have to be removed for `/mob/living/silicon/robot`, which contradicts #84604.

## #69989: cyborg `update_icons()` removes the typing indicator

### The bug

A cyborg's typing or thinking bubble disappears whenever `update_icons()` runs (for example when it takes damage), while the mob still believes the indicator is active.

### Reproduction

1. Spawn a borg with the Lua editor and take control of it.
2. Give it the `currently_thinking_IC` trait and call `create_typing_indicator()`. The player's view showed the borg gaining the `robot0` bubble overlay.
3. Apply 5 brute damage and call `update_icons()`. The `robot0` overlay was removed, while `active_typing_indicator` was still set.

### Cause

`/mob/living/silicon/robot/update_icons()` (`code/modules/mob/living/silicon/robot/robot.dm:310`) begins with `cut_overlays()`. The indicator is added with `add_overlay` outside of the overlay-update path (`code/modules/tgui_input/say_modal/typing.dm:81-85`), so the cut removes it.

### Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 69989: cyborg update_icons no longer removes the typing and thinking indicator overlays", re-adds `active_typing_indicator` and `active_thinking_indicator` with `add_overlay` right after `cut_overlays()` in the robot's `update_icons()`.

### Verification

The server was rebuilt and a new round started. The same steps showed `robot0` added, with no removal after the damage and two `update_icons()` calls. `update_icons()` was invoked from the Lua editor, as in the reproduction. Other ways of triggering it were not tried.

## #95114: cyborgs drop all modules when incapacitated

Checked by reading code only; nothing was reproduced in game.

A stun or paralysis adds `TRAIT_HANDS_BLOCKED`, which runs `on_handsblocked_start` (`code/modules/mob/living/living.dm:2744`) and `drop_all_held_items()`. The robot version (`code/modules/mob/living/silicon/robot/inventory.dm:~194`) deactivates each held module, with the comment that none of the items are dropped, only unequipped. So an EMP, a knockdown and the table-climb stun all unequip modules, in the same way humans drop held items. The commenter on the issue saw nothing wrong except the table part, which is #93823. Not changed. Keeping modules selected while stunned would be a design change.

## Notes

- `cut_overlays()` in the robot's `update_icons()` also drops any other unmanaged overlay. That was left alone.
- Both table-related issues come down to the same intended behaviour.
