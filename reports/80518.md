# #80518: Shove into a supermatter crystal is logged after the victim is dusted

## Summary

When a player shoves someone into a supermatter crystal, the "shoved" attack-log entry was written after the victim had already been dusted. It therefore named the victim as `*no key*`, and it came after the victim's death entries. `disarm()` wrote that log at the end of the proc, after `target.Move()` had already killed the target. The fix writes the "shoved" log before the move and reduces the late log lines to entries for extra details only (knockdown, dropped item).

## The bug

The issue reporter shoved a keyed player twice toward a supermatter shard. The first shove moved the victim next to the crystal and the second shoved them into it. On the shover's attack log, the second shove appeared as `shoved *no key*/(Ivy Unlikely) (NEWHP: 100)`, while the first correctly showed the victim's key. The victim's own log showed the first shove, their death and their ghosting, but nothing for the fatal shove. A moderator investigating would have had to search the global game log to find out who shoved them.

## Reproduction

On master `87b93338`, using the Lua editor on the test server:

1. Spawned two humans, a shover and a victim, and a supermatter shard (`/obj/machinery/power/supermatter_crystal/shard`) in a row: shover, victim, crystal.
2. Gave the shover a fake key. Gave the victim a real player's key (via `PossessByPlayer`). A victim with a fake key made `ghostize` runtime during the dusting and hid the bug.
3. Called `disarm()` on the victim from the shover from Lua (`shover:disarm(victim)`). This reproduces the shove by calling the proc directly, not by a player pressing the shove action.

Evidence from the attack log before the fix:

- The victim's "died" lines were written first.
- Then came `FakeShover2[DC]/(Micah Kifer) shoved *no key*/(Cameron Reade) with knocking them down (NEWHP: 100)`.

The old line also showed a second defect: the detail string was passed as `log_combat`'s `object` argument, so the text read oddly ("with  with X" for a shove with a held item).

## Cause

In `disarm()` (`code/modules/mob/living/living_defense.dm:815`), the target is moved into the next turf with `target.Move(target_shove_turf, shove_dir)` (line 839). If that turf holds a supermatter crystal, the bump runs the crystal's dusting code (`dust_mob`, `code/datums/components/supermatter_crystal.dm:270`, and `consume`, line 286), which kills the target and removes their key from the mob. The only "shoved" log call comes later, at the end of the proc:

```dm
log_combat(src, target, "shoved", append_message)   // living_defense.dm:899
```

The same shove also has a log call on the knockdown path (line 868). By the time either runs, the victim is dead and keyless.

The victim-side "was shoved by" line cited in the issue is never written in this version. `log_message` returns at once when `log_globally` is FALSE (`code/__HELPERS/logging/_logging.dm:99`), and `log_combat` evidently reaches it that way. I did not pursue this and left it as is.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 80518: log shoves before moving the target", changes `disarm()`:

- Right after `COMSIG_LIVING_DISARM_HIT`, before the shove direction and the `Move`, it logs `shoved` once, with `with [weapon]` as the addition when a weapon was used.
- The old end-of-proc and knockdown-path "shoved" logs were removed. In their place are separate lines carrying only extra information: `knocked down ... with a shove` (skipped if the target is already dead after the move) and `disarmed <item> with a shove` when the shove made the target drop their held item.
- The dropped item is now passed as the `object` argument and the text as `addition`, fixing the garbled text.

Logging first was chosen over trying to keep the victim's key alive through the move, because the log only needs to reflect the state at the moment of the shove.

## Verification

On the test server, with the same row of mob, mob and shard:

- After the first version of the fix: `FakeShover2[DC]/(Osbert Yeskey) shoved testplayer/(Isaias Zadovsky) (NEWHP: 100)` came first, with the victim's key. The victim's death lines followed, and the shove was no longer logged as `*no key*`.
- The same run still produced `... knocked down *no key*/(...) with a shove` after the death. The final edit (`target.stat != DEAD`) removes that line. It compiled with 0 errors but was not re-run in game.
- A plain shove with no crystal produced one line: `FakeA shoved FakeB (NEWHP: 100)`.

Not checked: the final `target.stat != DEAD` edit in game; shoves into tables, lockers, disposal bins or onto other humans after the fix; shoves with a weapon; a real player-initiated shove (all tests called `disarm()` from Lua).

## Notes

- The collision handlers log their own lines: `shoved ... into <thing>` for tables (`code/datums/elements/table_smash.dm:196`), closets (`code/game/objects/structures/crates_lockers/closets.dm:1293`) and human collisions (`code/modules/mob/living/carbon/human/human_defense.dm:142`), and probably disposal bins. After this change those shoves log two lines: the early generic one and the specific one. A maintainer may want to drop the generic line when a collision handler runs, or accept the duplication. This was not tested.
- The victim-side "was shoved by" entry the issue expects is still missing; fixing it would be a separate change in the logging helpers.
