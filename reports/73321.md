## Summary

The steps from #73321 (be in shuttle hyperspace, cast Ethereal Jaunt, get pulled out of the jaunt) no longer work on master: casting is refused on hyperspace tiles because they have `NOJAUNT`, and a jaunt holder cannot be walked into hyperspace. The only way to put a jaunt holder in transit was to force it there with admin tools. In that case the transit turf gave the holder a `shuttle_cling` component, so the invisible holder drifted with the shuttle and was finally dumped into deep space on another z-level with the jaunter still inside. This is a plausible mechanism for both reports ("holder and mob end up apart from where the player is looking"). The fix makes `initialize_drifting` skip jaunt holders. Neither issue's original screenshot or video state was reproduced.

## The bug

#73321 (2023) describes a player in hyperspace casting Ethereal Jaunt, entering the jaunt holder, and then being "ripped out" of it, left as a lifeless immobilized body in view of the holder. #77384 (2023) describes a heretic using Space Phase to move around the hyperspace area, with the camera centered on the place they left instead of where they are.

On the master tested here neither can be started by a player. See Reproduction.

## Reproduction

The test server ran a human with a real client, with spell requirements removed through the Lua editor.

1. Using the Lua editor, send the cargo shuttle through transit (`SSshuttle:moveShuttle('cargo', 'cargo_home'/'cargo_away', true)`). Transit is z-level 12 and made of `/turf/open/space/transit/north` tiles.
2. Move a human onto a transit tile and click Ethereal Jaunt as a client. Result: "An otherwordly force is preventing you from jaunting here." The spell refuses because hyperspace turfs have `NOJAUNT` (`code/game/turfs/open/space/transit.dm:7`, check in `code/modules/spells/spell_types/jaunt/_jaunt.dm:50`). The #73321 steps therefore do not work on this build.
3. Bypassing that check with Lua (calling `Activate` on the spell for a human standing on a transit tile) created the jaunt holder, put the jaunter in it, and the mob lost `TRAIT_HYPERSPACED`. A client then trying to move the holder got "Some strange aura is blocking the way." (the `NOJAUNT` test in `phased_check`, `code/game/objects/effects/phased_mob.dm:111`), and the client's eye stayed on the holder. No camera mismatch occurred.
4. Forcing the holder one tile east inside transit with `forceMove` (which bypasses `phased_check`): the holder (anchored, invisible) received a `shuttle_cling` component. It moved from y 51 to y 50 to y 44 within about 1.5 seconds, and was later found at 208,147 on z-level 9, dumped into space, with the jaunter still inside it.
5. Separately: an unprotected jaunter in vacuum suffocates, goes into crit, and `phased_mob.on_stat_change` ejects it from the holder (`phased_mob.dm:123-126`). The ejected body is stunned and cannot move. This matches the report's "lifeless body ripped out of the jaunt" but is a consequence of no air in hyperspace and Ethereal Jaunt giving no breath protection, not a hyperspace-specific bug.

Space Phase for #77384 was not cast directly. Its entry is blocked on transit the same way (`NOJAUNT`, per the investigation). So the third-person camera screenshot could not be recreated.

## Cause

`/turf/open/space/transit/proc/initialize_drifting` (`transit.dm:36-40`) is registered on `COMSIG_ATOM_ENTERED` (line 14) and gives every entering movable a `shuttle_cling` component unless it already has `TRAIT_HYPERSPACED` or the turf has `TRAIT_HYPERSPACE_STOPPED`:

```dm
if(enterer && !HAS_TRAIT(enterer, TRAIT_HYPERSPACED) && !HAS_TRAIT(src, TRAIT_HYPERSPACE_STOPPED))
    enterer.AddComponent(/datum/component/shuttle_cling, REVERSE_DIR(dir))
```

There is no exception for `/obj/effect/dummy/phased_mob`. The other entry point, `initialize_drifting_but_from_initialize` (`transit.dm:42-46`), does skip anchored movables (`!enterer.anchored`); the `Entered` path does not, so an anchored holder is dragged by the drift loop. `shuttle_cling` moves its parent through the hyperspace drift loop and, when the parent leaves transit, `/turf/open/space/transit/Exited` calls `dump_in_space` (`transit.dm:48-53`), which puts the holder, and the jaunter in it, on a random space tile on another z-level.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 73321: jaunt holders no longer get hyperspace drift". `initialize_drifting` now returns early for the usual cases (no enterer, already hyperspaced, hyperspace stopped) and also when `istype(enterer, /obj/effect/dummy/phased_mob)`, with a comment that jaunt holders are incorporeal and dragging one would take its jaunter with it. Only the early-return structure and the new check changed; other movables still get `shuttle_cling` as before.

This is a guard against a state that the current `NOJAUNT` checks already keep players out of. It was chosen because that was the only mechanism found that separates a jaunter from its holder in transit. It does not change the suffocation ejection.

## Verification

After rebuilding on the test server:

- The same forced-move test as step 4: the holder stayed at 218,46,12 for 3 seconds with no drift.
- A normal human on transit still drifted (y 46 to 44) and had `TRAIT_HYPERSPACED`.
- No runtimes were seen.

Not checked:

- Whether #73321 could be reproduced in the 2023 build. The investigation suspects the original steps depended on a cast check that was added later, but this was not confirmed.
- #77384's camera behavior (it needs a real client to compare the eye and the body; the heretic spell itself was not cast).
- Any change to what a player experiences, since the player path is blocked on master.

## Notes

- A related upstream PR, #73430 ("fixes jaunting in hyperspace throwing you out of the jaunt and breaking"), was closed on 2023-02-17. It changed only `code/datums/components/shuttle_cling.dm`. Its description says the author did not know why the bug disappeared. It is not in this branch.
- If the maintainers consider the original reports resolved by the `NOJAUNT` checks, this commit is optional hardening. It could be dropped without losing a player-visible fix.
