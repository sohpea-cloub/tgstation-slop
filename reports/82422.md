# #82422: Spy bounty theft sends mobs inside the stolen item to nullspace

| | |
|---|---|
| Issues | [#82422](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/82422): verified (reproduced and fixed at the proc level; the full uplink flow was not run) |
| Status | Fixed on the fix branch |
| Fix branch | [fix/82422](https://github.com/sohpea-cloub/tgstation-slop/tree/fix/82422) |
| Tested on | tgstation master `87b93338`, 2026-10-23 |

## Summary

When a spy steals a bounty item, the item is either sent to the black market (moved to nullspace with everything inside it) or deleted, and deleting it deletes its contents too. A living mob inside the stolen item, for example a person in a bluespace body bag inside a modsuit, was therefore sent to nullspace or deleted. The fix drops living mobs inside the stolen thing onto its turf before either happens. Brain mobs in MMIs and pAIs are deliberately left in their containers.

## The bug

A spy puts a person in a bluespace body bag, puts the bag in a stealable modsuit, and uses the uplink on the suit. If the suit goes to the black market (50% by default), the person ends up in nullspace. The issue's log excerpt shows a runtime `... has somehow ended up in nullspace. WTF did you do` from the ambience subsystem, then a `Cannot read null.ambient_buzz` runtime in `/area/Entered` while the mob was moved to the "Super Secret Room" (`/area/awaymission/errorroom`), which is the game's recovery for mobs found in nullspace.

If the other branch is taken (the item is deleted), the mob is deleted with it, because `/atom/movable/Destroy` qdels its contents (`code/game/atoms_movable.dm`, the `for(var/movable_content in contents)` loop).

## Reproduction

The black market branch was reproduced by calling the bounty procs directly with the Lua editor, not by the full uplink action (the scan with its `do_after` was not run).

1. Start a round and open the Lua editor as an observer.
2. Spawn a human, a bluespace body bag (`/obj/item/bodybag/bluespace`) and a MOD control unit (`/obj/item/mod/control`) on one tile. Move the human into the bag and the bag into the suit.
3. Create a `/datum/spy_bounty_handler` and a `/datum/spy_bounty/objective_item/random_easy` with that handler. Call `send_to_black_market(suit, 0, 0)` on the bounty. This is the proc that `clean_up_stolen_item` runs in its 50% branch.
4. Read the locations. Observed on master: the human's `loc` was "the bluespace body bag", its `z` was 0, and the suit's `loc` was null. The human was in nullspace, matching the issue.

The deletion branch (`finish_cleanup`) was not run on master. Its behavior is from reading the code.

## Cause

`clean_up_stolen_item` (`code/modules/antagonists/spy/spy_bounty.dm:165`) picks one of two paths. For an item that is indestructible or passes `prob(black_market_prob)` (default 50, line 26), it calls `send_to_black_market`; otherwise `finish_cleanup`.

- `send_to_black_market` (around line 219) calls `thing.moveToNullspace()`. Everything inside the item moves with it, including living mobs.
- `finish_cleanup` (around line 194) does `qdel(stealing)`, and `/atom/movable/Destroy` (`code/game/atoms_movable.dm`, in `Destroy` starting at line 215) qdels all contents.
- The machine override of `send_to_black_market` (around line 354) has a path that does `qdel(thing)` when the machine has no circuit board, with the same effect.

Nothing checks for living contents in any of these.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 82422: spy-stolen items no longer take mobs inside to nullspace", all in `code/modules/antagonists/spy/spy_bounty.dm`:

- A new proc `/datum/spy_bounty/proc/eject_living_contents(thing)` finds the turf of the thing and moves every `/mob/living` in `thing.get_all_contents_type(/mob/living)` onto it. It does nothing if the thing has no turf. It skips brain mobs (`isbrain`) and pAIs (`ispAI`), since those are meant to live inside containers.
- It is called in `send_to_black_market` before `moveToNullspace()`, in `finish_cleanup` before `qdel`, and in the machine override's no-circuit path before `qdel`.

Only this one file changed. Alternatives such as refusing to steal an item with a mob inside were not considered in the inputs.

## Verification

After a rebuild, on the test server, with the same setup as above (human in a bluespace bag in a MOD suit):

- `send_to_black_market`: the human's `loc` was "the floor" at `z` 2, the suit's `loc` was null (suit and bag in nullspace, human out).
- `finish_cleanup` on a second identical setup: the human was on the floor at `z` 2 and alive (`stat` 0).
- The runtime log of the new round showed no spy bounty runtimes.

Not checked: the full uplink flow, including the `do_after` scan and `clean_up_stolen_item`'s random branch choice; machine bounties and the no-circuit path; brain mobs and pAIs staying contained; mobs in nested containers other than the bag and suit; what the ejected mob experiences visually.

## Notes

- Ejecting happens at the moment of cleanup, so a mob appears on the item's turf after the shrink animation. Whether that is the desired result for the spy, or whether stealing a container with a living person should be refused or give a different effect, is a design choice for maintainers.
- Mobs that are held but not in `contents` (for example buckled to a stolen machine) were not examined.
