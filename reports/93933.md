## Summary

**#93933.** The surplus prosthetic arm sprites (`icons/mob/augmentation/surplus_augments.dmi`) have one transparent pixel where the regular robotic arms have an outline pixel. The pixel is at (17,16) in `robotic_l_arm` facing west and at (14,16) in `robotic_r_arm` facing east. An organic arm covers that spot, but the surplus arm does not, so one pixel of the chest sprite shows through as a skin-coloured dot. The fix fills both pixels with the outline colour used by their neighbours.

**#95603.** Advanced limbs rendered correctly when attached through the surgery code path, so this was not reproduced. Arm implants, which the report mentions, and mixed limb sets were not tried.

The two issues were worked as one batch. They are separate bugs.

## #93933: skin-coloured pixel on a surplus prosthetic arm

### The bug

A character with the prosthetic arm quirk shows a small skin-coloured pixel on the arm when the jumpsuit is off or the sleeves are rolled up. The reporter said it could also happen with bare skin and showed a screenshot. The quirk gives surplus limbs (`code/datums/quirks/negative_quirks/prosthetic_limb.dm:17-18`: `/obj/item/bodypart/arm/left/robot/surplus` and `.../right/robot/surplus`).

### Reproduction

This was reproduced by reading rendered sprite pixels, with the limbs attached by a proc call, not through the quirk.

1. On the test server, spawn a human. With the Lua editor, replace the human's left arm with an `/obj/item/bodypart/arm/left/robot/surplus` using `del_and_replace_bodypart(<new arm>, true)`, and set the human's direction to west (`dir = 8`).
2. Render the mob at scale 1 and read the pixels of the sprite.
3. Compare the tile pixel at x 17, y 16.

Observed on master: that pixel was `451a17`, a skin colour, between `13141a` outline pixels on the rows above and below (rows 15 and 17 in the same column).

The two sprites involved were also compared directly. The surplus art (`icons/mob/augmentation/surplus_augments.dmi`) was set against the regular and advanced robot arms and the human chest sprite, with the layers composited by hand. In `robotic_l_arm` facing west there is no opaque pixel at (17,16), and in `robotic_r_arm` facing east there is none at (14,16). The regular robot and advanced arms have no such hole.

The right arm was not rendered before the fix; its pixel was found by the same comparison.

### Cause

This is a sprite art defect, not a code defect. An organic arm covers that spot over the chest. The surplus arm art leaves one pixel transparent, so the chest sprite's skin-coloured pixel underneath shows through. The two states affected are `robotic_l_arm` (west) and `robotic_r_arm` (east) in `surplus_augments.dmi`. The surplus arms use that file through `icon_static` (`code/modules/surgery/bodyparts/robot_bodyparts.dm:510`, `:525`, `:540`, `:555`).

### Fix

Commit "Fix issue 93933: surplus prosthetic arm leaves a chest pixel showing" edits `icons/mob/augmentation/surplus_augments.dmi`. It fills pixel (17,16) of `robotic_l_arm` (west) and pixel (14,16) of `robotic_r_arm` (east) with palette colour `13141a` (RGB 19,20,26), the outline colour of the neighbouring pixels. The PNG was edited with a script, and the DMI metadata was kept. The change is binary only. A maintainer who wants to review it should view the two states in a DMI editor.

### Verification

The game was rebuilt with the fix. Two humans were spawned with the Lua editor, one with a surplus left arm facing west (`dir 8`) and one with a surplus right arm facing east (`dir 4`), and each was rendered at scale 1.

- Left arm facing west: the pixel at (17,16) was `13141a`, and the outline was continuous in that column (`13141a` on rows 15 to 18). No skin pixel.
- Right arm facing east: the pixel at (14,16) was `13141a`, with `13141a` on rows 15 to 18 in that column. No skin pixel.

Not checked: the other three directions of each arm (they were not found to be affected, but only these two cells were rendered after the fix), the surplus legs, and the quirk path itself (rather than attaching the limbs by `del_and_replace_bodypart`). No side-by-side before/after screenshot is included. The right arm's pre-fix state was not rendered.

### Notes

- Only the two cells found by comparison were changed; other cells of the file were not audited for the same kind of gap.
- Whether the colour matches what the original artist intended could be reviewed by a sprite maintainer. It matches the neighbouring pixels.

## #95603: advanced limbs change sprite after augmenting

### The bug

The reporter said advanced limbs "change sprite to other cybernetic limbs upon replacing the arms on a human", and that the sprites reset once an arm implant (strong arms, a toolset) is inserted. Steps: print advanced limbs, augment a human, and the limbs "may" change sprite. The report is from client 516.1679, round 260907 (no test merges recorded).

### Reproduction (not reproduced)

- Advanced limbs are `/obj/item/bodypart/*/robot/advanced` (`robot_bodyparts.dm:569-620`; there are no advanced head or chest types). Their `icon_static` is `advanced_augments.dmi`, and their `limb_id` is `robotic`.
- The limb icon cache key includes `icon_static`. `get_cache_key` on advanced, regular and surplus left arms gave `robotic-l_arm-12-icons/mob/augmentation/advanced_augments.dmi` for the advanced arm, and different keys for the others. The keys do not collide. This matters because the limbs are cached globally by key (`code/modules/mob/living/carbon/carbon_update_icons.dm:371-401`).
- A human's four limbs were dropped and advanced arms and legs were attached through `try_attach_limb` (`code/modules/surgery/bodyparts/dismemberment.dm:257`), the call the prosthetic replacement surgery uses. The mob's overlays were `robotic_l/r_arm` with emissive `_arm_e`, `robotic_l/r_hand` with `_hand_e`, and the legs likewise. The render looked correct: black limbs with cyan emissive.
- Earlier, advanced limbs attached with `del_and_replace_bodypart` also rendered correctly.

Not tried: inserting an arm implant before or after, mixing surplus and advanced limbs on one mob, other limb-attach paths, and the exact sequence in the report (printing advanced limbs from a fabricator and augmenting through the full surgery). The report says the sprites only "may" change, so a timing or ordering trigger was not excluded.

### Notes

A reproduction with the exact surgery steps and the arm implant would be needed to go further. Suspects that were listed but not checked: something in the arm implant code that refreshes the icon, an icon key built before the limb's `limb_gender` or `draw_color` is set, and `update_body_parts` being skipped by `STOP_OVERLAY_UPDATE_BODY_PARTS`.
