## Summary

The improvised chem heater is a subtype of `/obj/machinery/space_heater` that is supposed to heat only the beaker on top. It inherited the space heater's `process_atmos()`, so while switched on it also heated or cooled the surrounding air, which is what the reporters saw ("behaves as a regular heater"), and it used up the power cell doing so. The heater's own `process()` also took the sign of the energy it applies to the beaker from `mode`, a variable that `process_atmos()` sets from the room temperature, so the beaker could be pushed the wrong way. The fix stops the inherited atmos processing for the improvised heater and chooses heating or cooling by comparing the target temperature with the beaker's temperature.

## The bug

#81332: with an improvised chem heater crafted through the crafting menu, a reagent container inserted, a target temperature set and the heater switched on in auto, heat or cool mode, the air around the player heats or cools, while the container's contents do not change temperature noticeably.

#81996 ("Ghetto chem bugs") lists three complaints: the heater cannot be crafted, it does not change the liquid's temperature, and recipes requiring a welder do not check whether it is lit. The second is the same defect as #81332. The first and third are discussed under Reproduction and Notes.

## Reproduction

1. On a test server on master, with the Lua editor, spawn an improvised chem heater with a power cell and a beaker of water at 293 K, set the target temperature to 340 K, and turn it on.
2. Read the temperature of the heater's tile air and of the beaker over the next several seconds.

Observed before the fix: the room tile air went from 293 K to 332 K within about 10 seconds, the cell drained faster than expected, and the beaker rose only slowly (293 K to 297 K in about 8 seconds). The heater's mode showed "heat".

A second run used a cold room and a hot beaker: room 270 K, target 300 K, beaker 360 K. The room was heated to 319 K, although the beaker was already above the target and should have been cooled.

The heater was also built through the real crafting path in a later run: a human with the `personal_crafting` component (with `CRAFT_IGNORE_DO_AFTER` set in the component's `ignored_flags` so the 15-second do-after was skipped), a space heater one tile away, 2 cable, 2 glass, 2 iron, a thermometer, a large beaker with water, a screwdriver, a multitool, wirecutters and a power cell. The crafted heater had the same behavior.

### #81996

- "Cannot be crafted": the heater was crafted successfully with the recipe above (only the do-after was skipped). One thing to know: the result is placed on the crafter's tile, and `CRAFT_CHECK_DENSITY` fails if a dense object, such as the space heater itself, is on that tile, but the heater is normally adjacent.
- "Does not change liquid temperature": the same defect as #81332, fixed below.
- "Welder recipes do not check that the welder is lit": confirmed by reading the code only, not changed (see Notes).

## Cause

`/obj/machinery/space_heater/process_atmos()` (`code/game/machinery/spaceheater.dm:132-175`) works out `mode` (heat, cool or standby) from the temperature of the turf's air and the target temperature, and then changes the temperature of the air in the heater's tile and adjacent tiles, using the cell's charge. The base heater starts this processing in `Initialize()` (`spaceheater.dm:57`, `SSair.start_processing_machine(src)`) and in `toggle_power()` (`spaceheater.dm:321`).

`/obj/machinery/space_heater/improvised_chem_heater` (`spaceheater.dm:324`) inherits all of this. It adds its own `process()` (`spaceheater.dm:372`), which moves energy into the beaker, and calls `begin_processing()` when turned on (`spaceheater.dm:367-370`), but it never overrides `process_atmos()`. Both therefore run when it is on: the inherited one heats or cools the room and drains the cell, and the improvised one works on the beaker (at a slow conduction rate, 0.1 by default), so the visible effect is mostly that of a regular space heater.

A second defect sits in the improvised `process()`, which decided the direction of the energy flow like this (`spaceheater.dm:397-399`):

```dm
var/delta_energy = required_energy
if(mode == HEATER_MODE_COOL)
	delta_energy *= -1
```

`mode` is the room-temperature-driven mode set by `process_atmos()`, not a property of the beaker. With a cold room and a beaker above the target, `mode` is "heat", so the beaker was heated further.

## Fix

One commit on `fix/81332`, "Fix issue 81332: improvised chem heater no longer heats the room and cools/heats by the beaker's temperature", changing `code/game/machinery/spaceheater.dm`:

- A `process_atmos()` override on the improvised heater that returns `PROCESS_KILL`, so the machine is dropped from the atmos processing list straight away and the inherited room heating never runs. This keeps the subclass's inheritance and its other behavior unchanged.
- In the improvised `process()`, the sign of `delta_energy` is now taken from `target_temperature < beaker.reagents.chem_temp` (cool when the target is below the beaker's temperature, heat otherwise) instead of from `mode`. The now-unreachable `if(delta_energy == 0) return` was removed (`required_energy` is already known to be at least 1 at that point).

## Verification

The server was rebuilt and restarted with the fix and the same crafting steps were repeated:

- Heating: the beaker went from 293 K to 305 K in about 6 seconds. The room stayed at 293.17 K, the heater's `mode` stayed "standby" and `atmos_processing` was 0.
- Cooling (room 270 K, beaker 360 K, target 300 K): the beaker went from 360 K to 340 K. The room was not heated; it only equalised with its neighbours.
- No runtimes related to the heater appeared in the log.

Not checked: the heater's icon and examine text after the change (the improvised heater's `update_icon_state()` uses the beaker versus target temperatures, not `mode`, by code reading); the dedicated "heat only" and "cool only" modes beyond the cases above; heaters left running for a long time or with a swapped beaker.

## Notes

- **Welder recipes (from #81996), not changed.** `get_surroundings()` (`code/datums/components/crafting/_crafting.dm`, around lines 181-190) treats any nearby item whose `tool_behaviour` is set as a tool of that kind, and `/obj/item/weldingtool` always has `tool_behaviour = TOOL_WELDER` (`weldingtool.dm:29`) whether lit or not, with no check on fuel or whether it is on. This is general crafting behavior that affects every welder recipe; the improvised heater recipe does not use a welder at all. It needs a maintainer decision (for instance in how the crafting component asks for tools), so it was left as is.
- Because the improvised heater no longer runs `process_atmos()`, its `mode` variable stays "standby" while it works. Nothing the improvised heater displays uses `mode` (its icon logic compares target and beaker temperatures), but any future code that reads `mode` on this subtype will see "standby".
- When the target equals the beaker's temperature exactly, `required_energy` is below 1 and `process()` returns before the sign matters.
