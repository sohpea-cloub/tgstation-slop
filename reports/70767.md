## Summary

The issue says that exporting plasma sheet stacks in a paper-wrapped crate tagged with the sales tagger paid the tagger the cut of one sheet instead of the whole stack. On master `87b93338` the payout was correct in every test: the tagger's cut was the configured share of the full stack value, and the cargo report counted every sheet. The code paths involved all use the whole stack. No change was made. Which upstream change fixed it was not determined.

## The bug (as reported)

Two 50-sheet stacks of plasma were exported in a paper-wrapped parcel crate tagged with a 50% sales tagger. The cargo console registered 200000 cm³ of plasma and a payment of 7920, while the tagger was paid 40 twice, which is 1% rather than 50%. The report notes that 40 is half of one 80-credit plasma sheet. A commenter later could not reproduce this with either the blue sales tagger or a PDA barcode printer, and the reporter did not follow up.

## Reproduction

The sale was set up with the Lua editor and admin tools, and the real tagging code was used, but the sale was started by calling the export proc directly rather than by flying the supply shuttle.

1. Create a crate containing 50 plasma sheets (custom materials updated to match the stack), and wrap it in a big delivery parcel (`/obj/item/delivery/big`).
2. Put a universal scanner in sales tagger mode (`SCAN_SALES_TAG`), registered to the cargo department account with a 50% cut, onto the parcel through its `item_interaction` (the code at `code/modules/recycling/sortingmachinery.dm:145-170`, which adds a `pricetag` component to every object inside).
3. Run `export_item_and_contents` on the parcel, once as a dry run and once for real (the same call the supply shuttle's sell routine makes).

Results:

- 50 plasma sheets: the dry run valued plasma at 4000 (50 × 80) and the crate at 200. The real export gave cargo 2000 for the plasma and 100 for the crate, the report showed 5000 cm³ (50 sheets × 100), and the tagger's account was paid 2100 (500 to 2600), which is 50% of the whole.
- A crate holding two 50-sheet plasma stacks plus 50 each of gold, diamond, iron and glass: total value 36850. Cargo's report received 18425 (half), and the tagger's account rose by 18425 (2600 to 21025). The plasma report amount was 10000 cm³, i.e. 100 sheets.

The first attempts used the price tagger mode (`scanning_mode` 3) by mistake, which attaches no pricetag; those runs were discarded.

Not reproduced: the exact setup in the report (crate sealed with a paper wrapper through the hand-played tagging action, shuttle actually selling it), and the market state of the original round.

## Cause

None found on master. The relevant code:

- `code/datums/components/pricetag.dm`: `on_parent_sold` takes the export value computed for the sold object (cost times amount) and pays each payee `round(item_value * ratio)`.
- `code/modules/cargo/exports/materials.dm:50-63`: `/datum/export/material/get_amount` computes the amount from the object's material composition divided by `SHEET_MATERIAL_AMOUNT`, so a 50-sheet stack counts as 50.

The reported numbers (a payout equal to one sheet's half-price, and a cargo credit of 7920) do not fit this code. The material export and pricetag code was different in 2022 and has since been changed; the history available for this test was shallow, so the specific upstream change was not identified.

## Fix

None.
