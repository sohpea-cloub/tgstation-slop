## Summary

The report says that after a large fuel pool has been ignited and has completely burned out, the fire sound loop keeps playing forever and the "stop sound" button does not silence it. Four different fuel pool fires on current master (5x5, 9x9, a real 300u spill from a splash, and a plasma-assisted fire) all burned out cleanly: no live `/datum/hot_group`, no live `/datum/looping_sound/fire`, and the client received no more fire clips. The code paths that could leak the loop were changed after the report, so the bug may already be fixed, but the original may have depended on timing that these tests did not hit.

## The bug

As reported (with two screenshots of a large burning pool on a station map): ignite a big enough pool, wait until all fire is gone, and the fire crackle keeps looping. The reporter later added that prying up the floor tiles under the former pool removes the sound. A commenter (grungussuss) investigated and wrote that when the hotspot is deleted the loop is not cleaned up, so it stays "forever". The reporter tried a fix for the related #87974 (flaky hard delete of `/obj/effect/hotspot`) on a local copy and the problem persisted.

How the sound is supposed to work: every `/obj/effect/hotspot` belongs to a `/datum/hot_group`. When a group has more than two hotspots (`MIN_SIZE_SOUND`, `code/modules/atmospherics/environmental/LINDA_fire.dm`) it creates a `/datum/looping_sound/fire` (`update_sound()`, around line 461). The loop is stopped only when the group is destroyed, and a group is destroyed only when its last hotspot leaves it (`remove_from_group()`, lines 404-409; `Destroy()` at 398-402 does `qdel(sound)`). So a loop that never ends means some hotspot was never removed from its group, or the group was never emptied.

## Reproduction

Not reproduced. Method, on the station level of a regular station map (in the Central Primary Hallway) with the Lua editor, SDQL2 and the in-game sound log:

1. Started a round, joined as an observer and opened the Lua editor.
2. Test A, light pools: spawned fuel pools with `spawn_unique_cleanable(/obj/effect/decal/cleanable/fuel_pool)` on a 5x5 area with `burn_amount = 1` (roughly what ~8u of fuel per tile gives) and lit the centre with `ignite()`. The centre pool burned and disappeared, no other pool lit, and there were no hotspots left. See Notes: at this size the fire never spreads at all.
3. Test B, 5x5 with `burn_amount = 5`, lit at the centre. The client heard fire puffs and two fire clips (`fireclip6`, `fireclip7`) around the start of the fire, and nothing afterwards. Within about 25 seconds of lighting, there were 0 fuel pools and `SSair.hotspots` was empty.
4. After test B, `SELECT /datum/hot_group` returned one object. Looked up by reference from Lua, that reference was by then a different datum type (the freed reference had been reused), i.e. the group no longer existed. `SELECT /datum/looping_sound/fire MAP [gc_destroyed, timer_id, parent]` after test C returned 11 objects, all with a numeric `gc_destroyed` (already deleted, waiting for garbage collection), a null `timer_id` and a null `parent`.
5. Test C, 9x9 area: `burn_amount = 10` pools on every floor tile in the area, lit at the centre. Heavy fire noise (141 sounds in the 60-second sound log, many of them fire puffs). About a minute later no live fire sound loops existed.
6. Test D, a real spill: a 300u fuel splash from a bluespace beaker with `chem_splash(...)` radius 3 produced 35 pools (burn amounts of 3, 7 and 10). Ignited with `do_sparks`; the first spark did not light the pools and the second, at the centre, did. After the fire, 2 pools remained and no hotspots did.
7. Test E, plasma-assisted: 5x5 pools of `burn_amount = 10` with 3 moles of plasma added to each turf, lit at the centre. The fire lasted several minutes (5 hotspots after about 2 minutes, 1 at a high temperature after about 4 minutes, 0 after about 6 minutes), heating the hallway and setting off the fire and air alarms.
8. After test E, `SELECT /datum/hot_group MAP [gc_destroyed, sound]` returned a single object, already deleted (numeric `gc_destroyed`) and with no sound. `SELECT /datum/looping_sound/fire` returned 0 objects. The sound log showed fire alarm sounds but no further fire clips.
9. The round's runtime log contained three `uncaught runtime error` lines with no detail, logged between test B and test C; their cause was not identified, and nothing in the log named hotspots or the fire sound.

## Cause

None found on master. The leak the commenters described, a hotspot that is deleted without being removed from its group, has been addressed in the code since the report. Per the issue's code analysis, the relevant changes are #89195 (a `QDELETED` guard), #89625 (the hotspot joins its group before `perform_exposure()`, and `Destroy` removes it from the group) and #94698 (more safeguards). On master:

- `/obj/effect/hotspot/Initialize` joins or creates its group first (`LINDA_fire.dm:107-124`), then runs `perform_exposure()` (line 127). If another hotspot is created and this one is deleted during `perform_exposure()` (the comment at line 132 mentions the fuel pool's `fire_act()`), it returns early and `Destroy()` takes it out of the group.
- `/obj/effect/hotspot/Destroy()` (line 352) removes the hotspot from `SSair.hotspots` and from `our_hot_group`.
- `remove_from_group()` qdels the group when its `spot_list` is empty, and `/datum/hot_group/Destroy()` qdels the sound.
- Fuel pools (`code/game/objects/effects/decals/cleanable/fuel.dm:61-72`) respawn a hotspot from the `COMSIG_QDELETING` signal of the previous one; this "respawn while the old one is being deleted" path did not leak a group or sound in the tests above.

The reporter's observation that prying the floor tiles under the pool stops the sound would fit a hotspot (or its group) that was stuck on those turfs. That is not what current master did in any test here.

## Fix

None. No code was changed.

## Verification

Four fuel pool fires on master (tests B to E above) each ended with no live hot group, no live fire looping sound, an empty `SSair.hotspots`, and no more fire clips reaching the client.

Not checked:
- The exact situation in the screenshots (a large pool on a particular map; other stations). Only one station map was used.
- Real player action: pools were created and lit through the Lua editor and `do_sparks`, not by throwing a lit welding tool, shooting, or being set alight, and not with live client lag. The reporter's bug may depend on timing between hotspot creation and deletion (the same timing issue that #87974 was about), which a quiet test server may not trigger.
- Large multi-group fires that merge and split (`merge_hot_groups`) while burning; the tests produced fires that grew from a single point.
- SDQL2 also returns deleted objects that are waiting for garbage collection; those were told apart from live ones by their `gc_destroyed` value, so "no live group or loop" rests on that field.

## Notes

- The "CHECK FIRST" approach suggested for this issue was to pour about 200u of fuel over 5x5 tiles and light it. About 8u per tile gives `burn_amount = 1` (`other_reagents.dm:1423`: `max(min(round(reac_volume / 5), 10), 1)`). A pool with `burn_amount = 1` is deleted by `burn_process()` (`fuel.dm:68-70`) before its 0.5-second `ignite_others` timer (line 66) can light a neighbour, so the fire does not spread. Fires need roughly 15u or more per tile to spread. Test A showed exactly that.
- About 20 `fuel_pool` objects failed to garbage collect during the tests. The cause was not investigated; the likely reason is references held by the Lua editor's state, so it is probably an artifact of the way the pools were created rather than a game bug.
- Recommended disposition: the issue can likely be closed as fixed by the later hotspot/hot-group changes, or kept open for a report with a new round ID on current master.
