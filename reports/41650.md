# #41650: Mobs inside lockers are not shuttle-gibbed when a shuttle lands on the locker

| | |
|---|---|
| Issues | [#41650](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/41650): verified (the part about mobs inside lockers; the remarks about item contents are not addressed) |
| Status | Verified; fix committed |
| Fix branch | [fix/41650](https://github.com/sohpea-cloub/tgstation-slop/tree/fix/41650) |
| Tested on | tgstation master `87b93338`, 2026-10-23 |

## Summary

When a shuttle docks on a tile, `toShuttleMove()` gibs living mobs lying on the tile, destroys anchored objects and pushes unanchored ones. A mob inside a locker (or other container) on that tile is not handled as a mob. Inside an anchored locker it is deleted along with the locker, with no gib, no log line and no statistics tally. Inside an unanchored locker it is pushed along with the locker, still alive, and can end up inside a wall. The fix gibs every living mob found inside such an object, in the same way as a loose mob, and then pushes or destroys the container as before.

## The bug

The issue's report: build a locker where a shuttle will land, climb in (anchored or not) and wait for the shuttle. If the locker is anchored it is destroyed and "leaves the contents of the locker unharmed, and in the shuttle". If it is not anchored, the shuttle "pushes you, locker and all, to the edge of the shuttle". Two later comments say that the contents of crushed items, and mecha debris, are lost, and that a syndicate bomb that is crushed loses its assembly but the core remains. This write-up only covers the mobs-in-lockers part of the report.

## Reproduction

This was done on master with the cargo shuttle, using the Lua editor to place the lockers and to call the shuttle. The shuttle was moved by calling `SSshuttle.moveShuttle()` rather than by a player calling it from the console.

1. On the test server (MetaStation), open the Lua editor as an admin. The cargo shuttle's home dock was at (63,151,2), and the cargo shuttle's id was `cargo`.
2. Send the shuttle away: `SSshuttle:moveShuttle('cargo', 'cargo_away', false)`. Wait for it to finish.
3. At the home dock, spawn two `/obj/structure/closet`: one at (63,151,2) with `anchored = TRUE`, one at (63,153,2) with `anchored = FALSE`. Spawn a human in each, move the human into the closet with `forceMove`, and put a crowbar in each closet. The humans had the `never_wounded` trait added.
4. Bring the shuttle back: `SSshuttle:moveShuttle('cargo', 'cargo_home', false)`.
5. Check where the closets, humans and crowbars are.

Observed on unfixed master:

- Anchored closet: the closet, the human and the crowbar were all deleted. The human was never gibbed: the shuttle log had no "shuttle gibbed" line and the blackbox tally was not recorded.
- Unanchored closet: pushed from (63,153) to (57,153), a wall tile ("the wall"), with the human still alive inside. This matches the issue's "pushed to the edge of the shuttle". The distance (6 tiles) was not explained further.

Early attempts of the same test failed because of mistakes in the Lua (`SS13` was not required in the first attempt, and `stat` was read from the closet, which does not have that variable); the observations above come from the corrected runs.

## Cause

In `code/modules/shuttle/mobile_port/shuttle_move_callbacks.dm`, `/turf/proc/toShuttleMove()` loops over everything on a landing turf (line 25). For each thing:

- Crush-proof things are skipped (line 26).
- Living mobs lying directly on the turf are gibbed (lines 28 to 41).
- Other non-mob things are pushed with `step(thing, shuttle_dir)` if unanchored, or `qdel`'d if anchored (lines 42 to 46).

Only the first level of the turf's contents is looked at. A mob inside a locker is not in the turf's `contents`, so it is never reached by the gib branch. When the anchored locker is `qdel`'d, `/atom/movable/Destroy()` deletes everything in its contents (`code/game/atoms_movable.dm:258`, `for(var/movable_content in contents) qdel(movable_content)`), including the human. When the unanchored locker is pushed with `step()`, its contents travel with it.

Note that this differs from the issue text, which says the occupants of an anchored locker stay unharmed in the shuttle. On current master the contents of an anchored locker are deleted together with it.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 41650: shuttles now crush mobs inside lockers and other containers", in `shuttle_move_callbacks.dm`.

- The per-mob gib code (incorporeal check, vehicle "no parking" achievement, unbuckle, stop pulling, the "slams into" message, the blackbox tally, the `log_shuttle` and `investigate_log` lines, and `gib(DROP_ALL_REMAINS)`) moved unchanged into a new proc, `/mob/living/proc/get_crushed_by_shuttle(shuttle)`. The loose-mob branch now calls it.
- In the non-mob branch, before the existing push or destroy, the loop goes through `thing.get_all_contents_type(/mob/living)` and calls `get_crushed_by_shuttle()` on each mob, skipping any with `SHUTTLE_CRUSH_PROOF`. If `thing` has been deleted by then, the loop moves on to the next thing. Otherwise the thing is pushed or destroyed as before.

The chosen approach keeps all the existing push and destroy behavior and only adds the missing gib for occupants. The inputs do not mention alternatives that were considered.

## Verification

After rebuilding and restarting the test server with the fix, the same Lua setup was repeated (shuttle away, two closets with a human and a crowbar each, shuttle home):

- Both humans were shuttle-gibbed. The shuttle log had one "was shuttle gibbed by the supply shuttle." line for each of them.
- The anchored closet was destroyed with its contents.
- The unanchored closet was still pushed (this time to (56,153)) but was empty.
- No runtimes were seen in the runtime log.

Not checked: the game's unit tests, other containers (crates, mechs, vehicles, body bags, sleepers, MMIs), shuttles other than the cargo shuttle, a player-called shuttle landing at round end, and mobs that are inside several nested containers.

## Notes

- **Brains in MMIs.** A later review of this change raised a concern: `get_all_contents_type(/mob/living)` also finds a brain mob inside an MMI (or a mob held inside another mob). Gibbing it would delete the MMI. The suggested guard is to skip mobs where `isbrain(trapped) || ismob(trapped.loc)`. This was not tested here and is not part of the fix branch, so a maintainer should consider it before merging.
- Unanchored objects are still moved with `step()`, which can leave them inside shuttle walls. This is the existing behavior, probably by design, and was not changed. The "pushed to the edge" part of the issue is therefore still there for objects without a mob inside.
- The comments about the contents of crushed items (mecha debris, bomb core) were not addressed. They are a result of `Destroy()` deleting contents when a crushed anchored object is `qdel`'d, and one could argue that this is "crushing". A separate change would be needed if a different outcome is wanted.
- A mob in a mech or another vehicle on a landing tile is now also gibbed, because those are non-mob things containing a living mob. This follows from reading the code and was not tested.
