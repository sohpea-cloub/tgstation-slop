# #96790: Water smoke does not extinguish burning carbons wearing a mask or internals

| | |
|---|---|
| Issues | [#96790](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/96790): verified, fixed |
| Status | Fixed on the branch; the fix has a balance side effect (see Notes) |
| Fix branch | [fix/96790](https://github.com/sohpea-cloub/tgstation-slop/tree/fix/96790) |

## Summary

Chemical smoke (smoke machine, water smoke) never exposed a carbon to its reagents if the carbon had internals on or wore smoke-protective gear such as a gas mask. The water reagent's extinguishing code runs on TOUCH exposure, so a burning person with a mask on was never put out, while atmos fires and unmasked people were. The fix applies a TOUCH exposure to protected carbons before returning. This also lets every other reagent in the smoke touch masked carbons, which is a balance change.

## The bug

The reporter used a water smoke machine to put out a plasma fire. The fire went out, but walking into it, getting set on fire, and standing in the smoke did not extinguish them, repeatedly, until they died. The report does not say whether they wore a mask; the cause found below depends on it. In the test, only mask or internals wearers were unaffected.

## Reproduction

All steps used admin tools on the test server, with the round started and a Lua editor state open.

1. In the Lua editor, spawn a human, call `adjust_fire_stacks(10)` and `ignite_mob()` on it.
2. Spawn an anchored `/obj/machinery/smoke_machine`, add 100 units of water to its reagents, set `on = true`, and start it with the SDQL query `CALL begin_processing() ON /obj/machinery/smoke_machine`.
3. Unprotected human: the fire was out within about 3 seconds (`fire_stacks` -1, `on_fire` 0).
4. Repeat with a human wearing `/obj/item/clothing/mask/gas` (equipped in the mask slot, 32) and ignited. After about 10 seconds the human was still burning (`on_fire` 1, `fire_stacks` 8.5, `has_smoke_protection()` true).

The smoke machine was started by calling its procs directly rather than through the normal player interaction.

## Cause

`code/game/objects/effects/effect_system/fluid_spread/effects_smoke.dm:371-383`, `smoke_mob` on chem smoke:

```dm
if(smoker.internal != null || smoker.has_smoke_protection())
	return FALSE

var/fraction = (seconds_per_tick SECONDS) / initial(lifetime)
reagents.trans_to(smoker, reagents.total_volume, fraction, methods = SMOKE_MACHINE, copy_only = TRUE)
```

Chem smoke's `process()` skips living mobs (`isliving(thing)` → `continue`, around line 379), so `smoke_mob` is the only place mobs get exposed. For protected carbons it returned before any exposure. `SMOKE_MACHINE` is `TOUCH|INHALE`, and `/datum/reagent/water/expose_mob` (`code/modules/reagents/chemistry/reagents/other_reagents.dm:197`) extinguishes on TOUCH, which was therefore never reached.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 96790: chem smoke now touches carbons that wear masks/internals". It moves the `fraction` calculation above the protection check and, for protected carbons, calls `reagents.expose(smoker, TOUCH, fraction)` before `return FALSE`. Masks and internals stop breathing the smoke, not skin contact. The inhaled transfer is still skipped for them.

A narrower alternative was considered but not implemented: only extinguish protected carbons when the smoke contains water-like reagents, or expose only a filtered set of reagents.

## Verification

On the fixed build, two ignited humans stood on one turf with a smoke machine holding 100 water. After about 15 seconds the unprotected human had `on_fire` 0 and `fire_stacks` -1; the masked human (`has_smoke_protection()` true) also had `on_fire` 0 and `fire_stacks` -1. Before the fix the masked human stayed at `on_fire` 1, `fire_stacks` 8.5. No new runtimes appeared in the runtime log.

Not checked: internals specifically (only a gas mask was tested), other reagents in smoke, other smoke sources, and non-carbon mobs.

## Notes

- Balance: gas masks and internals used to block chemical smoke completely. After this change every reagent in smoke applies its TOUCH effect to masked carbons, not only water. A maintainer may prefer the narrower approach of extinguishing only, or of water-like reagents only.
- Non-carbon living mobs (simple/basic) are not exposed by chem smoke at all; this was left alone.
- The reporter's theory (small per-tick water amounts letting fire stacks outgrow the water) was not what was observed: unmasked humans were extinguished quickly.
