## Summary

On Icebox, removing a station floor on either of the two upper levels (RCD, thermite, explosion, or anything else that scrapes the floor away) leaves a tile that is an "ice chasm": `/turf/open/openspace/icemoon/keep_below`. That turf type has planetary atmosphere with the icemoon gas mix, so the hole is continuously filled with cold, thin air and drains the pressurised room around and below it. The cause is the `Baseturf` z-trait of those two levels in `icebox.json`. The fix changes it to plain `/turf/open/openspace`, which keeps the air the tile inherited.

## The bug

#90528: RCD-deconstructing a floor on Icebox, even above a room with normal atmosphere, makes the hole spew icemoon gas. The reporter suspected the baseturf. #71995 (opened in 2022 and marked a duplicate by its own author in favour of #90528) says the same for RCD, thermite and explosions: the firealarm goes off, and examining the new tile shows its name is "Ice chasm", including over sealed rooms built by players.

## Reproduction

1. Start Icebox and observe as an admin. Open the Lua editor.
2. Find an upper-level (z4) iron floor tile in a pressurised room that has another pressurised room below it on z3. A tile in Port Primary Hallway at (65,141,4), above Lesser Port Maintenance, was used. Before any change both the tile and the room below measured 101.3 kPa at 293 K.
3. Call `ScrapeAway` twice on the tile, with `CHANGETURF_INHERIT_AIR` (what the RCD's floor deconstruction passes, `code/game/turfs/open/floor.dm:358-362`): floor to plating, then plating to the z-trait baseturf. The RCD itself was not used; calling the proc directly is the same operation without the tool.
4. Read the new turf.

Observed on unfixed master:

- The new turf was `/turf/open/openspace/icemoon/keep_below`, named "ice chasm", with `planetary_atmos` set and `initial_gas_mix` `ICEMOON_ATMOS`.
- Its air was immediately at about 31.7 to 45 kPa and 180 K, and it kept being pulled back to the icemoon mix, so neighbouring tiles were drained.
- A second sample, on a plating tile in the Cargo Bay on z4, gave the same type and name after the second scrape.

## Cause

`ChangeTurf` resolves `/turf/baseturf_bottom` to the z-level's `Baseturf` trait (`code/game/turfs/change_turf.dm:60-66`). The Icebox map config set that trait to the icemoon chasm type for both upper levels (`_maps/map_jsons/icebox.json:39` and `:48`, the second and third entries of `traits`):

```json
"Baseturf": "/turf/open/openspace/icemoon/keep_below",
```

All station plating on those levels has `/turf/baseturf_bottom` at the bottom of its baseturf list, so scraping a floor down to the bottom produces that type. `/turf/open/openspace/icemoon` (`code/game/turfs/open/openspace.dm:184-188`) sets `initial_gas_mix = ICEMOON_DEFAULT_ATMOS` and `planetary_atmos = TRUE`. In `code/modules/atmospherics/environmental/LINDA_turf_tile.dm:332`, a planetary tile shares its air with the planetary mix every cycle, which for a hole in a heated station means constantly bleeding into a ~180 K mix.

## Fix

One commit, `Fix issue 90528: holes in Icebox station floors no longer become ice chasms with planetary icemoon atmos`, changing only `_maps/map_jsons/icebox.json`: the `Baseturf` trait of the two upper levels is now `/turf/open/openspace`. Plain openspace has no planetary atmosphere, and with `CHANGETURF_INHERIT_AIR` the hole keeps the air of the tile it replaced. Chasms placed by the map itself keep the icemoon type, since they are map objects and do not use the z-trait.

Alternative not taken: making the icemoon type behave differently when scraped from a station area. Changing the z-trait is the smaller change.

## Verification

After the fix, on a new round with the patched map config loaded (the test server reads a saved copy of the map config, which had to be updated to match the edited file), and with the same tile and steps:

- The result was `/turf/open/openspace`, named "open space", `planetary_atmos` off, `initial_gas_mix` the normal 22 O2 / 82 N2 at 293.15 K.
- Pressure and temperature were 101.3 kPa and 293 K right after, and still 101.3 / 293 thirty seconds later. Neighbouring tiles at (66,141,4) and (68,141,4) and the room below were unchanged.
- A map-placed chasm at (78,144,4) was still `/turf/open/openspace/icemoon/keep_below`, planetary, at 31.7 kPa and 180 K, as intended.
- The round's `runtime.log` showed no new runtime errors.

Not checked:

- An actual RCD or thermite or explosion; only `ScrapeAway` was called. The RCD path was read in code only.
- The player-visible side of the hole (falling through to z3 and the firealarm) was not exercised.
- Other Icebox z-levels and other maps (the change touches only Icebox's two upper station levels).

## Notes

- Side effect: a hole in outdoor z3/z4 plating (not station interior) is now also plain openspace with normal-temperature air instead of a cold chasm. Neighbouring snow still exchanges gas with it. Whether outdoor holes should still be cold chasms is a design choice a maintainer may want to weigh.
- The commit message describes the icemoon mix as "-180C"; the measured temperature was 180 K.
- No upstream PR fixing this was identified in the material available.
