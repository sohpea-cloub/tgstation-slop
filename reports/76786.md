## Summary

A MULEbot that has been swallowed by a clown car (or any other container) still runs its "drive over lying humans" check, and because the check looks at everything in the MULEbot's `loc`, it hits the other occupants of the car. The fix adds an `isturf(loc)` condition so the check only runs when the MULEbot is actually standing on a turf.

## The bug

In the report, a sentient MULEbot was forced into a traitor clown's clown car and ended up killing the clown, who was inside the car's storage. Nothing in the game suggests a MULEbot can drive over someone through a vehicle's contents, and the reporter was baffled by it.

What happens is that the clown car moves the MULEbot into itself, and the MULEbot's movement handler then treats the car's contents as if they were on the floor tile beneath the bot.

## Reproduction

Reproduced on master with admin tools, by forcing the entry directly rather than by playing out the traitor-clown scenario.

1. In the Lua editor, spawn a clown car and a human on the same turf.
2. Force the human into the car (`mob_forced_enter`) and knock them down (`Knockdown(600)`), so their `body_position` is lying.
3. Spawn a MULEbot and set the emagged bit (`BOT_COVER_EMAGGED`, bit 4) in its `bot_access_flags`. Emagging is needed because `run_over()` returns early for an un-emagged MULEbot unless the avoidance wire is cut. The test MULEbot had no mind.
4. Force the MULEbot into the same car with `mob_forced_enter`.

Observed on master: the human's health went from 100 to 67, `body_position` was 1 (lying), a splat sound played and a blood pool appeared. Both the human and the MULEbot had the clown car as `loc`.

## Cause

`/mob/living/basic/bot/mulebot/on_bot_movement` (`code/modules/mob/living/basic/bots/mulebot/mulebot_movement.dm:13-21`) runs on every move of the bot:

```dm
if(isnull(buckled) && isnull(pulledby) && isnull(throwing) && has_gravity())
	for(var/mob/living/carbon/human/future_pancake in loc)
		if(future_pancake.body_position == LYING_DOWN)
			run_over(future_pancake)
```

Nothing checks that `loc` is a turf. When the clown car swallows the MULEbot (a `forceMove` into the car), the handler fires with `loc` set to the car, so `in loc` iterates the car's other occupants and any lying human among them is run over. `run_over()` (same file, line 36) only guards on the emag flag and the avoidance wire, so it does not stop this.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 76786: MULEbots no longer run over people inside the container they were swallowed by". It changes the condition in `on_bot_movement` to start with `isturf(loc)` and adds a comment explaining why. Nothing else changes: on a turf the behaviour is the same as before.

## Verification

After rebuilding with the fix, the same setup (lying human and emagged MULEbot both forced into a clown car) left the human at 100 health, unchanged.

As a control, an emagged MULEbot was `forceMove`d onto a turf where a knocked-down human lay. The game printed "The MULEbot drives over Myles Larson!" and the human's health fell from 100 to 56, so running over people on a floor tile still works.

Not checked: a sentient (player-controlled) MULEbot, the exact scenario of the report; other container types besides the clown car; a MULEbot that drives into the car on its own rather than being forced in.

## Notes

- The only commenter on the issue suggested the behaviour "sounds like it should be a feature". The fix takes the opposite view: a bot inside a vehicle's storage is not on the road. If maintainers want it kept, the issue can simply be closed instead.
- A sentient MULEbot (one with a mind) only runs people over if emagged or if its avoidance wire is cut, per `run_over()`. That is unchanged.
- The check is generic: it also stops a MULEbot inside any other container (a closet, for example) from running over lying humans in the same container. This was reasoned from the code and not tested.
