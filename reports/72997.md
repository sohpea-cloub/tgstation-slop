## Summary

Wall-mounted objects (such as light fixtures) on an elevator platform were destroyed the moment the lift moved, because `atom_mounted` treats any movement as being torn off the wall, while a platform moves its riders but not the wall behind them. The fix remounts such an object on whatever it can mount to at the destination, and only lets it fall apart if nothing is there. Dead, crit, paralysed and unconscious humans were all carried by the lift on master (#72997), and a knocked-down mob that left the lift did not keep following it (#74668); both reports look fixed by the later transport rewrite, but the reproductions were limited.

## #72997: elevators do not take dead or crit people

### The bug

The 2023 report: someone dead, in crit, lying down, stunned or unconscious on an elevator platform stays behind when the elevator ascends.

### Reproduction

Not reproduced. On a test map with an elevator (a single-tile-module lift moving between two z-levels), using the Lua editor and the transport controller:

1. Spawn a human on the platform on the lower level.
2. Make them alive, dead (`death()`), in crit (high oxygen damage), paralysed (`Paralyze(6000)`) or unconscious (`Unconscious(6000)`), one at a time.
3. Move the lift up instantly and down with a timed move (duration 20 ds).

In every case the human was carried: on the upper level after the up move, back on the lower level after the down move.

### Cause / current code

`add_item_on_transport()` (`code/modules/transport/transport_module.dm:137`) has no check of `stat` or lying state, and `group_move()` (line 492) moves everything in `transport_contents`. The transport rewrite since the report is the likely reason it works now; this was not confirmed against history.

### Fix

None. Not tested: a real trip, ascending in a live round with other players, or other platform types.

## #74668: "Elevator quantum entanglement"

### The bug

After tripping on an elevator going up, a player found that moving the elevator afterwards swapped them between floors wherever they were, and mail in their inventory broke. A maintainer's comment suggested the mob was not being removed from the lift's `locs`/contents correctly.

### Reproduction

Not reproduced:

1. With the Lua editor, put a human on the platform and apply `Knockdown(3000)`.
2. Move the lift up.
3. Move the human off the platform with `Move()`.

Result: the human was no longer in any module's `transport_contents`, and did not follow the next down move.

### Cause / current code

`uncrossed_remove_item_from_transport()` (`transport_module.dm:120`) removes a mob from the platform's contents when its location is no longer on the platform, and the movement-signal registrations set during a trip appeared correct for these flows when read.

### Fix

None. Not tested: a real unlucky or omen trip, being thrown off the lift, moving between modules during a timed move, and mail items. The original bug may need one of those paths.

## #90843: wall mounts on elevators break off when the lift moves up

### The bug

A light fixture (or other wall mount) on an elevator platform fell apart as soon as the elevator moved. AI status displays did not, according to a comment.

### Reproduction

1. On the same test map, use the Lua editor to spawn `/obj/machinery/light/directional/east` on the platform tile next to the wall, and call `find_and_mount_on_atom()` on it (objects spawned after map load are not mounted automatically).
2. Move the lift up.

Observed on master: the light was destroyed. (A later Lua access to it failed with "a destructed callback or destructed userdata method was called".) The wall tile at the destination was also a wall, so a remount would have succeeded.

AI status displays are not `atom_mounted`, which matches the comment.

### Cause

`/datum/component/atom_mounted/proc/on_move()` (`code/datums/components/atom_mounted.dm:84`) calls `drop_wallmount()` on every `COMSIG_MOVABLE_MOVED`, so any move deconstructs the mount (the intended behavior for, say, being dragged off a wall by a singularity). `group_move()` in `transport_module.dm` calls `mover.Moved(..., forced = TRUE, ...)` for each rider (line 543), but the wall the object hangs on stays behind.

### Fix

Commit "Fix issue 90843: wall mounts on moving elevators no longer fall off". In `on_move()`, after the lighting-debug check, if the move was forced and the parent is in the `transport_contents` of a `/obj/structure/transport/linear` on its turf: delete the component, then call `find_and_mount_on_atom()` on the object; if that fails, the object falls apart exactly as `drop_wallmount()` would (message and `deconstruct(FALSE)`). All other moves still call `drop_wallmount()`.

### Verification

After a rebuild: a new east-facing light mounted at the platform survived an instant move up (on the upper level, `atom_mounted` component present, not destroyed) and an instant move down (same). With the destination wall tile changed to a floor (nothing to mount on), moving up destroyed the light ("falls apart"), as intended. No new runtimes appeared in the runtime log beyond a known empty Lua line.

Not checked: timed moves, trams, other wall mount types (intercoms, which `drop_wallmount` handles differently, APCs, etc.), mounts on a platform's own tile that have no wall at the destination in a live round, and whether the 9 separate 1x1 modules vs. a larger platform matter.

## Notes

- The fix only applies to forced moves of objects listed in a linear platform's `transport_contents`; anything else behaves as before.
- The platform check requires the object to be on the same turf as a platform; objects riding on other contents (for example inside a container) are not covered.
