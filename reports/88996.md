## Summary

A living mob's gravity state is event based: it is refreshed when the mob itself moves, or when its `loc` gains or loses forced gravity. A mob inside a mech, closet or other container is not told when the container moves between turfs, so a pilot carried out of the gravity generator field keeps the high gravity state (and its brute damage every Life tick) until they leave the container. The inverse also happens: a mob carried into the field in a container never gets high gravity. The fix re-checks gravity every Life tick for any mob whose `loc` is not a turf.

## The bug

#88996 as reported: stand next to the gravity generator, get into an exosuit, walk away from the generator, and keep taking the slow brute damage of crushing gravity until you get out of the exosuit outside the field's range.

On master the same stale state happens for any container, not just mechs. The mob's `gravity_state` stays at the high value while `has_gravity()` already returns standard gravity, so `handle_gravity()` keeps applying damage. Leaving the container makes the mob move, which refreshes the state and clears it, which matches "until you exit the exosuit outside".

## Reproduction

All steps used admin tools on a Metastation round; the gravity generator was at 166,174,2 with a 25-turf field, gravity strength 6.

1. Started a round, joined as an observer, opened the Lua editor.
2. Using the Lua editor, created a human and a Ripley mech on a floor tile inside the field (167,174,2) and picked a floor tile outside the field (157,165,2). The human's `gravity_state` was 6 inside the field.
3. Put the human in the Ripley (`mob_enter`) and `forceMove`d the mech to the tile outside the field.
4. Read the values in a separate Lua run (reading in the same run directly after the proc calls gave stale values). Result: the human's `gravity_state` was still 6 while `has_gravity()` returned 1. A mob left in this state keeps being treated as under high gravity, so `handle_gravity()` keeps dealing the crushing-gravity brute damage.
5. Calling `mob_exit` on the pilot afterwards refreshed the state to 1, as the issue describes.
6. Repeated with a human inside a closet (`forceMove` into `/obj/structure/closet`, then moved the closet out of the field): `gravity_state` stayed 6, `has_gravity()` 1.
7. The inverse also reproduced: a mob placed in a mech or closet outside the field and carried into it never reached high gravity.

Controls that behaved correctly on master: a human walking across the field edge, a human `forceMove`d across it, and a gorilla (`/mob/living/basic/gorilla`) walked and `forceMove`d in and out (`gravity_state` 6 inside, 1 outside).

The mech and closet were moved with `forceMove` from the Lua editor and the pilot was put in with `mob_enter`/`moved_inside`, not by a player driving the mech by hand. This exercises the same movement hooks, but it is not the literal player action.

### Other issues in the group

Only the container path left a mob stale, so a mob inside a container is the only way found to get the reported result; the report itself says the gorilla walked in.
- #80089 (blindfold made with a cult archives and dragged out of the room): the blindfold and dragging steps were not reproduced. Walking, turf changes (`ChangeTurf`) and switching the field off all refreshed gravity correctly. The container path above is the only stale path found, and it is not confirmed to be what happened in that round.
- #85640 (crushing gravity after the generator lost power): with the breaker off or the APC without power the field stays while the generator discharges, which the issue comments describe as intended, and then `disable()` removed the field and every mob in it, including one inside a closet, refreshed to 0. No persistent stuck state was found. The issue's actual cause (delamination plus an RCD rebuild of the room) was not attempted.

## Cause

`gravity_state` is only refreshed from two places:

- `/mob/living/Moved()` (`code/modules/mob/living/living_movement.dm:1-27`) calls `refresh_gravity()` when the mob's own move changes the forced-gravity trait of its turf, the turf's `force_no_gravity`, or the area's default gravity, or when it moves to or from nullspace. `on_changed_z_level` (line 29) covers z moves.
- `code/modules/mob/living/init_signals.dm:58-63` registers a `connect_loc` element for `TRAIT_FORCED_GRAVITY` add/remove on the mob's `loc`, so a change to the turf it stands on refreshes it.

A pilot's `loc` is the mech, not a turf. When the mech moves between turfs, the pilot's `Moved()` is not called, and the field's forced-gravity trait sits on the turfs, not on the mech, so the `connect_loc` hook does not fire either. Nothing refreshes the pilot until they leave the container, when their own `Moved()` finally runs. The same applies to a closet. `has_gravity()` is evaluated from the current turf, so it is correct at the moment of asking; the cached `gravity_state` is what goes stale.

`handle_gravity()` (`code/modules/mob/living/life.dm:124-126` at master) uses the cached state: `if(gravity_state > STANDARD_GRAVITY) handle_high_gravity(...)`, which is why the damage continues.

## Fix

One commit on `fix/88996`, "Fix issue 88996: re-check gravity each life tick for mobs inside containers". In `handle_gravity()` in `code/modules/mob/living/life.dm`, the mob now calls `refresh_gravity()` first when `!isturf(loc)`.

This costs one cheap call per Life tick only for mobs inside containers, and means the state is at most one Life tick late in both directions (leaving the field and entering it). No other approach was tried. Refreshing a container's contents on every container move would be more precise but would need hooks in each container type; the per-tick check is one place.

## Verification

After rebuilding, with the same setup (all values read after about 5 seconds, i.e. a few Life ticks):

- Pilot in a Ripley moved out of the field: `gravity_state` went 6 to 1.
- Human in a closet moved out of the field: 6 to 1.
- A Ripley carrying a human from outside the field into it: 1 to 6, and the human's health started dropping (reading 85).
- No new runtimes were logged.

Not checked: a player actually driving a mech and walking away (the mech was moved with `forceMove`), other container types (lockers, sleepers, vehicles with riders, cargo crates with mobs, MMI/brain cases), pAIs and AIs in cards or cores, or the one-tick latency in practice. The fix was not tested against the non-container reports (#79091, #80089, #85640), because those did not reproduce.

## Notes

- The refresh is at most one Life tick late, and runs only for mobs whose `loc` is not a turf.
- If #79091 and #80089 were caused by something other than the container path, this fix will not help them. #79091 describes walking into the field as a gorilla, which refreshed correctly on master. In #80089 the blindfold and dragging steps did not reproduce. Both may need more detail from the reporters.
- #85640's reporter's thread contains a statement that crushing gravity while the generator is off but discharging is intended. This fix does not change that.
