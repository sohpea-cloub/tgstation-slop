## Summary

The service cyborg's beverage storage apparatus is supposed to resynthesize any drink poured out of its glass, through a `reagent_refiller` component on the glass. The glass the apparatus starts with never received that component, because `add_glass()` handed `handle_reflling()` the wrong object (`loc.loc`, which is the floor, instead of the cyborg), so pouring never refilled anything. A glass that is later put into the apparatus does get the component, but the component is never removed when the glass leaves, which is the separate cause of #77367 (a glass refilling itself in someone's pocket). Two commits fix the two causes.

## The bug

- **#95949 and #91671.** A service cyborg pours a drink (tequila sunrise, plain whiskey, other bar drinks) out of the glass in its default beverage storage apparatus into another glass. The poured amount is not replaced, although the apparatus describes itself as one that "will resynthesize any drinks (or other nutritional liquids) you pour out of glasses". #91671 reports the same thing for the default apparatus and its starting glass, and has comments confirming it was still broken into 2026. #91671 is the same report as #95949.
- **#77367.** A drink made by a service cyborg, drunk and put in a pocket, turned back into a full glass about a minute later. The glass given to other players did not refill. The report is from 2023; what follows is what the current code does.

## Reproduction

On master `87b93338`, with the Lua editor as an admin observer. Procs were called directly where noted.

**#95949 and #91671**

1. Spawn a `/mob/living/silicon/robot` and call `model.transform_to(/obj/item/robot_model/service, TRUE)` to make it a service cyborg. Possess it.
2. Activate its beverage storage apparatus (`/obj/item/borg/apparatus/beaker/service`) and add 50 units of tequila sunrise to the glass it holds.
3. Check the stored glass: `GetComponent(/datum/component/reagent_refiller)` was nil. The apparatus's `loc` was the cyborg and `loc.loc` was "the floor".
4. Select the apparatus and click an empty drinking glass on the floor to pour. The stored glass went from 50 to 40 units.
5. Wait. On the unfixed build there was no refill (the component's delay is 60 seconds).

**#77367**

1. Take a glass out of the apparatus by clicking it up (the apparatus's `Entered()` gives such a glass the component), with 10 units in it.
2. Move it out of the apparatus. The glass still had its `reagent_refiller` component while lying on the floor or held by a human.

An actual refill of a pocketed glass was not shown on the unfixed build; the attempt to put the glass into a pocket through the Lua editor failed with Lua errors. The retained component and the code in the next section explain the report. The same sequence on the fixed build is under Verification.

## Cause

**Starting glass.** `code/game/objects/items/robot/items/storage.dm:166-168`:

```dm
/obj/item/borg/apparatus/beaker/service/add_glass()
	stored = new /obj/item/reagent_containers/cup/glass/drinkingglass(src)
	handle_reflling(stored, loc.loc, force = TRUE)
```

Modules are created inside the cyborg (`new path(robot)` in `robot_model.dm:68`), so the apparatus's `loc` is the cyborg and `loc.loc` is the turf it stands on. `handle_reflling()` (`storage.dm:170`) wants the cyborg as its `bro` argument and returns early when it is not one (`if (!iscyborg(bro)) return`), so the component was never added. The notes do not explain why the `Entered()` path (`storage.dm:179`) did not cover the glass created inside the apparatus; what was observed is that the starting glass had no component on the unfixed build.

`Entered()` also returned early for non-glass items without calling the parent `..()`, which skipped the parent's handling for anything else put into the apparatus (condiment packets, coffee pots, syrup bottles).

**Component kept after leaving.** `/datum/component/reagent_refiller` registers `delete_self` on `COMSIG_ATOM_EXITED` of its parent (`code/datums/components/reagent_refiller.dm:37`). That signal fires when something leaves the glass (the glass's contents), not when the glass itself leaves its container, so the component is never removed when the glass leaves the apparatus. The refill is scheduled with a timer that checks `target.loc != oldloc` (`reagent_refiller.dm:66`), and `oldloc` is whatever held the glass at the time of the pour; a glass that is poured or drunk from while it holds the component therefore still refills as long as it stays in the same place for the delay, wherever it is. That matches the "my pocketed glass refilled" report.

## Fix

Two commits.

**"Fix issue 95949: service borg beverage apparatus never resynthesizes drinks in its starting glass".** `add_glass()` now passes `loc` (the cyborg) to `handle_reflling()`. In `Entered()` the call to the parent `..()` is moved to the top so it runs for every item, glass or not.

**"Fix issue 77367: glass taken out of the service borg apparatus keeps refilling itself".** Adds `/obj/item/borg/apparatus/beaker/service/Exited(atom/movable/gone, direction)`, which after the parent call deletes the `reagent_refiller` component of whatever left. Only glasses held by the apparatus can resynthesize; as soon as one leaves it loses the ability. The fix is in the apparatus's own `Exited()`, so the component itself is unchanged.

## Verification

On the fixed build, with a service cyborg set up as above:

- The stored glass had the `reagent_refiller` component. Pouring 10 units took it from 50 to 40 at 11:23:19 (test server clock), and at 11:24:30 it was back to 50 (the refill timer is 60 seconds).
- A glass with 10 units picked up by clicking had the component while in the apparatus, and nil after being moved out with `forceMove`.
- A human drank from that glass (10 to 5), put it in a pocket, and 70 seconds later it still held 5 units: no refill.
- No new runtimes beyond the `WrapAdminProcCall with no ckey` messages that come from calling procs through the Lua editor while possessing the cyborg.

Not checked: the secondary "drink" apparatus (see Notes), a glass handed between two cyborgs, power consumption of the refill, and the other storable items (condiment packets, coffee pots, syrup bottles) after the `Entered()` reordering.

## Notes

- `/obj/item/borg/apparatus/beaker/drink` ("secondary beverage storage apparatus", from the upgrade) says it does not resynthesize, as intended. Its glass-spawning override, however, is declared on `/obj/item/borg/apparatus/beaker/service2/add_glass()` (`storage.dm:198`), a type that does not exist, so the override is never used and the apparatus gets the default large beaker instead of a drinking glass. This was not changed.
- `reagent_refiller.refill()` computes the refill amount as the volume before the pour (`min(amount_per_transfer_from_this + total_volume, total_volume)`), so one refill tops the glass back up to its previous volume. It is capped by the glass's capacity.
- #91671's comments asked about glass types and the upgrade module; both are consistent with the cause above (the default apparatus and its starting glass were affected, not a special case).
