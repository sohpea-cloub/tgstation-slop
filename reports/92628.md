## Summary

Some areas are mapped without an APC and are powered anyway, because an area's three power channels default to on. The supermatter engine chamber and the Nanotrasen museum away mission are two. A player could mount an APC frame in such an area, and the new (or later removed) APC then switched the area's power off for good: the museum's puzzle doors could be crowbarred, and the supermatter chamber's vents and scrubbers stopped. The fix makes `try_build` on the APC frame refuse areas that have no APC but still have every channel powered, with the message "This area is already powered without an APC!". Areas that lost their APC (power off) and blueprint-made areas can still get a new APC.

## The bug

**#92628** (August 2025): "you can build an APC in areas that are always powered, and this removes the always powered part." The reporter's example was the Nanotrasen museum, where an APC gives control over the doors and so allows cheesing the puzzle by crowbarring doors; they wondered about other always-powered places such as a Syndicate base. A commenter said they used this to break Birdshot ("the station had infinite power"). Another commenter pointed out that the museum area does not set `requires_power = FALSE`, and that areas which do set it already cannot take an APC (see the check in `apc_frame.dm`).

**#91343** (May 2025): you can place an APC frame in the supermatter engine area and remove it; the area's vents and scrubbers stop working, which, as most engineers do not know that the chamber is its own area without an APC, practically guarantees a delamination.

## Reproduction

Using the Lua editor on a MetaStation round (observer ghost for setup):

1. Check the area: `/area/station/engineering/supermatter/engine` had `apc = nil`, `requires_power = 1` and all three channels (light, equipment, environment) on (`111`). The other supermatter areas (`/room`) have their own APCs. A scan of the map files showed the same shape on Catwalk, Delta and IceBox: the `supermatter/engine` area has no APC there either.
2. Spawn a human in engineer gear (made immune to damage for the test) on a floor tile in that area, next to a wall (the tile at 168,150 on z2 between the supermatter airlocks, wall at 167,150), give them an `/obj/item/wallframe/apc`, take control, and click the wall with the frame.
3. Result on master: "You attach the APC frame to the wall." An APC named "Supermatter Engine APC" now existed in the area.
4. Remove the APC. This was done by deleting it with the Lua editor (`qdel` on the area's APC), which runs the same `Destroy()` path as taking the frame down by hand; the APC was seen disappearing from its tile. The effect on the area (its power channels) and on the vents and scrubbers was read afterwards. This is the behavior #91343 describes and the one the fix commit message summarises as "removing the frame left the area unpowered for good"; the exact vent and scrubber values read at that point were not preserved in the notes this write-up draws on, so they are not quoted here.

Not done on master: the museum was not built in before the fix. The museum case was only checked after the fix (below). Taking the APC down was simulated by deleting it rather than by hand-deconstruction.

## Cause

An area's power channels are plain variables defaulting to on (`code/game/area/areas.dm:85-87`: `power_equip`, `power_light`, `power_environ` all `TRUE`). An area with `requires_power = TRUE` and no APC therefore stays powered unless something changes the variables. Mappers use this on purpose: the supermatter engine chamber (`code/game/area/areas/station/engineering.dm:90`, with the `block_area_power_fail` element added at `:95-97` so power failures skip it) and away areas such as `/area/awaymission/museum` (`code/game/area/areas/away_content.dm:16`) are mapped without an APC.

`/obj/item/wallframe/apc/try_build` (`code/game/objects/items/apc_frame.dm:8-21`) only refuses if the area already has an APC, if `requires_power` is false, or if `always_unpowered` is set:

```dm
if(A.apc)
	... return FALSE //only one APC per area
if(!A.requires_power || A.always_unpowered)
	... return FALSE //can't place apcs in areas with no power requirement
```

Areas that are free-powered only through default channel variables slip through that check. Once an APC exists in such an area, the APC owns the area's channels: `update_area_power_usage()` (`code/modules/power/apc/apc_main.dm:314-327`) writes its state to all three channels, and forces it off if the APC has no secured electronics or no cell. When the APC is removed, `disconnect_from_area()` (`apc_main.dm:329-335`) calls `update_area_power_usage(FALSE)`, setting every channel to false and clearing `area.apc`. After that the area is unpowered and nothing restores the free power, so the area stays dead until someone builds and powers a working APC.

## Fix

One commit: **"Fix issue 92628: APC frames can't be built in areas powered without an APC"**. In `try_build` it adds, after the existing checks:

```dm
if(A.power_light && A.power_equip && A.power_environ)
	// An area without an APC that still has power on every channel was mapped without one ...
	to_chat(user, span_warning("This area is already powered without an APC!"))
	return FALSE
```

Reasoning from the commit message: at this point in `try_build` the area has no APC, so having power on every channel means the area was never given one. Areas that lost their APC are unpowered (`disconnect_from_area` turns the channels off), and areas made with blueprints are unpowered too (`/area/proc/setup()` sets all channels to false), so both can still get a new APC. The check keys on existing area state and needs no per-area list or new flag. The commit also fixes #91343 because the supermatter chamber is one such area.

## Verification

On the test server (MetaStation round, rebuilt with the fix):

- **Supermatter chamber:** repeated step 2 of the reproduction. Clicking the wall with the frame gave "This area is already powered without an APC!" and no APC was created. The area still had `apc = nil` and all channels on (`111`), and its vents and scrubbers read as working (`stat=0`).
- **Rebuilding a lost APC still works:** deleted the Central Primary Hallway's APC, after which the area's channels read `000`. Mounting a new APC frame on the hallway wall then gave "You attach the APC frame to the wall." (the new APC's behavior beyond attaching was not examined).
- **Museum:** with the museum away mission enabled at roundstart (the "Load Away Mission" admin verb only offered a custom map on this build, and a direct template load did not work, so the museum was added through the away mission config and a development override for the test only; those changes were reverted and are not part of the commit), the museum's areas (`/area/awaymission/museum` and subareas) read `apc = nil`, `requires_power = 1`, all channels on. A frame clicked onto a wall at 117,46 on z12 was refused with "This area is already powered without an APC!".
- The runtime log of the two rounds contained no runtime errors from the change.

Not checked: the museum before the fix, hand deconstruction of the APC (the removal was done with `qdel`), other always-powered areas (for example other away missions or the Syndicate base mentioned in #92628), RCD-placed APC frames (the RCD's wall-frame mode uses the same frame type and so should go through `try_build`, but it was not tried), and any unit test (none for `try_build` exist in the repo).

## Notes

- **Behavior change to weigh:** every area that has no APC and full power now refuses an APC frame. That includes any station area a mapper left without an APC on purpose, and the fix gives no way to override it (there is no per-area opt-out). Conversely, a map that is missing an APC by mistake can no longer have one added in game.
- The test is state-based, not type-based. An area whose power was restored by other means after losing its APC (all three channels back on with no APC) would also refuse a frame.
- As the commenter on #92628 noted, areas with `requires_power = FALSE` were already protected. The museum and the SM chamber are the ones where the default channel values do the job, which is why they needed a different check.
- On several maps (Catwalk, Delta, IceBox, and Meta in the test) the engine chamber has no APC, so the exploit in #91343 applies to those maps.
