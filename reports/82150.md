# #82150: Docking the cargo shuttle at CentCom deletes ghosts on board and raises a "Mob with client has been deleted" runtime

| | |
|---|---|
| Issues | [#82150](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/82150): not reproduced on master (already fixed there); [#82982](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/82982): duplicate of #82150, same cause, also already fixed |
| Status | Not reproduced as filed; both reports reproduce only with the existing fix locally reverted. No new fix needed |
| Fix branch | none |
| Tested on | tgstation master `87b93338`, 2026-10-23 |

## Summary

When the supply shuttle docked at CentCom, its selling routine treated any ghost standing or orbiting on the shuttle as cargo and deleted it, which printed a "Mob with client has been deleted" stack trace and left the player in a new ghost with no link to their body (so they could not re-enter or be revived). Master already skips observers in that loop (an `isobserver()` check next to the existing `iseyemob()` check), apparently from an upstream PR titled "You can no longer runtime selling ghosts" (#95396); on master a ghost on the shuttle survives docking. Both issues still looked open on GitHub when this was tested.

## The bug

From #82150: with a ghost sitting on the supply shuttle (and another ghost orbiting it), the shuttle docking at CentCom deleted the ghost, which also dropped the orbiting ghost, and produced a runtime from `code/modules/mob/mob.dm`. The reporter suggests either not deleting ghost mobs or having the "mob with client deleted" check ignore `/mob/dead`.

From #82982: a dead player whose ghost was on the cargo shuttle when it docked lost the connection between ghost and body. The body then showed as soulless, the player could not re-enter it, and revival did not let the player take control. The reporter suspected the same cause as #82150.

## Reproduction

Not reproducible on master as is. It was reproduced only after temporarily removing the fix, in a local rebuild that was restored afterwards. The method used the real timed shuttle path, not a direct call to the selling proc.

1. Started a round on MetaStation, late-joined as an Assistant and opened the Lua editor.
2. Basic check: sent the cargo shuttle to its home dock with `SSshuttle:moveShuttle("cargo","cargo_home",0)`, then killed the mob (`death(0)`) and ghostized it (`ghostize(1)`).
3. Main check: with the ghost moved onto the Supply Shuttle (at 59,151,2), made a clientless `/mob/dead/observer` ("Orbiter Ghost") that orbited it, placed a mechanical toolbox on the shuttle, and sent the shuttle away with `SSshuttle:moveShuttle("cargo","cargo_away",1)`. That runs the normal timed docking path (`check()`, `initiate_docking()`, `sell()`).
4. After the shuttle docked at CentCom, checked the ghost, its mind, `can_reenter_corpse` and the orbiter, and used Re-enter Corpse.

Result on master as is (fix present):

- The same ghost mob survived, still a `/mob/dead/observer`, with `can_reenter_corpse` = 1, `mind.current` set to the body and the body's `mind` set.
- The orbiting ghost still existed (not queued for deletion) and kept its orbit component.
- The toolbox and its contents were sold ("+4 credits: Received 1 toolbox." in the CentCom message).
- No export runtime and no "Mob with client" runtime.
- Re-enter Corpse put the player back into the body.

Result with the `isobserver()` skip removed (rebuilt and restored afterwards):

- `runtime.log` showed "Mob with client has been deleted." from `Destroy()`, called from `qdel`, `export_item_and_contents()` on the observer, then the supply shuttle's `sell()`, `initiate_docking(CentCom)`, `check()` and the shuttle subsystem's `fire()`. This matches the stack in #82150. A second runtime followed, "Cannot read null.post_logout_callbacks" at `code/modules/mob/mob.dm:1606` in `become_uncliented()`, reached through the deleted ghost's `Logout()`.
- The cargo investigate log recorded "deleted through cargo export" three times.
- Afterwards the player was in a new observer with `mind` = null and `can_reenter_corpse` = 0. The body was still there but the ghost had no link to it, which is #82982's symptom. The orbiting ghost was gone.

## Cause

`/obj/docking_port/mobile/supply/proc/sell()` (`code/modules/shuttle/mobile_port/variants/supply.dm:307`) walks every movable on the shuttle's turfs and calls `export_item_and_contents()` for each (line 325). That proc (`code/modules/cargo/exports.dm`, around lines 56-63) deletes anything it does not sell, because `delete_unsold` defaults to `TRUE`:

```dm
if(!dry_run && (sold || delete_unsold) && sold != EXPORT_SOLD_DONT_DELETE)
	if(ismob(thing))
		thing.investigate_log("deleted through cargo export", INVESTIGATE_CARGO)
	to_delete += thing
```

Before the fix only eye mobs were skipped in `sell()`, so observers were queued for deletion. Deleting a ghost with a client trips the stack trace in `/mob/Destroy()` (`code/modules/mob/mob.dm:29`). The client is then given a new mindless ghost, so `can_reenter_corpse` is 0 and the original body cannot be re-entered. A ghost orbiting something on the shuttle is deleted by the same path.

## Fix

None added. Master already has the change in `sell()`:

```dm
if(iseyemob(exporting_atom))
	continue
if(isobserver(exporting_atom))
	continue
```

(`supply.dm:319-322`). The investigation attributed it to upstream PR #95396, "You can no longer runtime selling ghosts", merged 2026-03-14. The tested tree is a shallow clone, so the PR number and date come from the investigation notes and were not checked against history here; the two lines themselves were present and removing them reproduced both issues.

## Verification

The checks above on the unmodified tree show the ghost, its mind link and the orbiter surviving a real timed docking at CentCom with no runtime, while the toolbox was still sold. Removing the two lines and rebuilding brought back both the runtime and the broken ghost/body link. The tree was then restored and rebuilt at the tested commit.

Not checked: other ways a ghost can be exported (for example on other shuttles or through other export routes), other mob types that should be skipped (only eye mobs and observers are skipped), and revival of the original body after the fix beyond Re-enter Corpse working.

## Notes

- Both issues are probably closeable as fixed by the upstream PR; nothing else in the inputs changes that.
- The reporter of #82150 proposed ignoring `/mob/dead` in the `Destroy()` stack trace as an alternative. That would hide the symptom and leave ghosts being deleted and unlinked from their bodies, so skipping observers in `sell()` is the better fix.
- Side finding from the same test, unrelated to this issue: both rounds logged "No valid destination passed into forceMove" from a CentCom supply pod (`begin_launch` in `pod_landingzone`, ending in `code/game/atoms_movable.dm:1176`).
- Clientless observers created from the Lua editor with `SS13.new` are placed at a default spot by the observer's `Initialize()`, so they need a `forceMove` after creation when a test depends on their position.
