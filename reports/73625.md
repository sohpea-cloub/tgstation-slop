## Summary

The issue says the unanchored Bwoink mines in an abandoned crate all "detonate" without a sound as soon as the crate is opened. On current master, opening a crate filled by the real Bwoink spawner did not trigger the mines: they stayed armed and untriggered, and no click or adminhelp sound occurred. The mine code was rewritten after the report, and mines now ignore effects and wait for a foot on the mine. One related case remains (below), so the issue is only partly stale. No fix was committed.

## The bug

As reported (February 2023): open an abandoned crate that holds a Bwoink mine and every mine goes off silently. The mines are unanchored by the spawner so the player can place them. A comment suggested that all mines default to armed and trigger on each other or on the banhammer as they leave the crate.

## Reproduction

Attempted on master; the reported behaviour did not occur. The crate was built by hand with admin tools, not by finding a generated one in a round.

1. Using the Lua editor, spawn a `/obj/structure/closet/crate/secure/loot` and put the real spawner `/obj/effect/spawner/abandoned_crate/bwoink` inside it (this mirrors what `spawn_loot()` does). Set `spawned_loot` to 1 and `locked` to 0.
2. Open the crate once with `open(null, TRUE)` and once with a human adjacent calling `attack_hand`.
3. Both times, one banhammer and three Bwoink mines appeared on the crate's turf. There was no "*click*" message and no adminhelp sound. An SDQL2 query on the mines showed `anchored` 0, `armed` 1, `triggered` 0, `foot_on_mine` null, and all three still present.
4. Variant: a crate holding three unanchored Bwoink mines first and the banhammer last. This is the order the contents take if the banhammer is picked up, dropped and the crate closed again, since closing reinserts items in turf order. This was not run through those player steps; the crate was filled in that order directly. On opening, "*click*" appeared three times, because each mine registered the banhammer as its `foot_on_mine`. The mines did not explode at that point. Detonation when the banhammer leaves the tile was not tested.

## Cause (why it does not reproduce)

- The spawner's loot list puts the banhammer first, then three mines (`code/game/objects/structures/crates_lockers/crates/abandoned_crates/abandoned_crates_loot.dm:137-148`). `/obj/structure/closet/dump_contents` (`code/game/objects/structures/crates_lockers/closets.dm:507`) moves contents out in order, so the banhammer is on the turf before the mines are.
- `can_trigger` (`code/game/objects/effects/mines.dm:64`) returns FALSE for effects (`iseffect(on_who)`), so mines no longer trigger each other, and it ignores anchored things. `on_entered` (line 82) only records a "foot" (`foot_on_mine`) for the first unanchored arrival and clicks; the mine blows when that thing leaves (`on_exited`, line 107). The 2023 theory about mines triggering each other no longer applies; the notes attribute the change to a later mine rewrite but do not identify the specific commit.

## Fix

None. No commit was made.

## Verification

Only the test-server runs above. Not checked: a naturally generated abandoned crate, a player actually moving the banhammer off the tile to see the mines go off, or the Bwoink sound in that case.

## Notes

- Latent remainder: an unanchored item, such as the banhammer, that arrives on a mine's tile after the mines are there makes them click, and the mines blow when that item leaves. This can happen if the banhammer is picked up, dropped and the crate closed, then reopened. It is not the reported symptom of instant detonation on open.
- Possible hardening, not done: have the spawner's mines ignore arrivals that come from the same container.
- A maintainer can probably close the issue as no longer reproducible.
