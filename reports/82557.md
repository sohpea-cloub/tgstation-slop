## Summary

#82557 reports that after a failed "look up/down" the game keeps retrying the look on every step, spamming chat until a look finally succeeds. On current master the look state is only set after the target turf has been validated, so a failed look prints one message and sets no state. A look that later becomes impossible while walking ends itself after one message. No retry loop was found, so the report most likely described an older implementation (or a downstream fork; the report came from a Skyrat round). #88008 (camera left behind when looking up inside a mech) did not reproduce either: the look follows the mech. No fix was committed.

## The bug

**#82557.** A player presses look up (or look down) where they cannot look, gets "you can't look in that direction!", and from then on the game keeps trying to look up/down every time they move a tile, repeating the message until a look succeeds. The reporter says this cannot be switched off, and suspects it causes lag and input spam.

**#88008.** Inside a Clarke exosuit with open space overhead (the report says under the tram, with nothing above), holding look up and then moving left the camera at the spot where the look began until the look was released.

## Reproduction

Not reproduced. What was tried, on the MultiZ Debug map (z2 is the station floor, z3 has open space above part of it), using the Lua editor to call procs and set up positions:

1. **Failed look from a spot with a solid roof.** On a z2 tile with plating overhead, the look-up keybind (`L`) was pressed. No look started. (The keybind's key-down starts a look and key-up ends it, and the test client sent both at once, so this step alone is not conclusive about message output; the following steps cover that.)
2. **Successful look.** Standing at 117,117,2 (open space above), `look_up()` was called through the Lua editor. `looking_vertically` became 16 (`UP`), a looking holder was created on z3, and `client.eye` was the holder.
3. **Look that becomes impossible while walking.** The mob was moved to 112,130,2 (solid roof above), `look_up()` was called, then the mob was moved with `forceMove` through 112,131 to 112,133. The reported state was: `looking_vertically` 16 and a holder after the call (so the look was accepted at that position), and `looking_vertically` 16 after the later moves. The only chat line seen was "You can't see through the floor above you." (once). This test did not capture the intermediate states precisely enough to say exactly which move ended the look; the code (below) clears the state when the first move has no valid turf above.
4. **Mech movement for #88008.** A Ripley was spawned at 118,117,2 and a mob was put inside with `moved_inside()`, then `look_up()` was called. The holder was created at 118,117,3 and `client.eye` was the holder. Calling `vehicle_move(8)` twice (a turn, then a step) moved the mech 118 to 117, and the holder followed to 117,117,3 on each step, with `client.eye` staying on the holder and `looking_vertically` still 16. Two `vehicle_move(4)` calls moved the mech back to 118; a further step into a window at 119 was blocked and the holder stayed put. Forcing the mech to z3 while looking moved the holder to z4, and back to z2 moved it to z3.
5. **Caveat.** Real keyboard driving of the mech could not be tested: key presses sent by the test client did not move the mech (`relaymove` returned TRUE without moving, which is consistent with the occupant having been placed by script and not being among `return_drivers()`). `vehicle_move()`, the proc `relaymove` calls, was therefore called directly.
6. **Look up on foot, then enter the mech.** With a look-up already active, `moved_inside()` was called. The holder followed the mech, but `client.eye` became the mech rather than the holder, while `looking_vertically` stayed 16 and the holder still existed. See Notes.

No path was found in which a failed look leaves state behind or retries on later moves.

## Cause

There is no defect in master for #82557; this is why it does not reproduce.

`/mob/living/proc/look_up()` (`code/modules/mob/living/living.dm:2456`) asks `get_looking_turf()` for a target first and returns early if there is none:

```dm
var/turf/above_turf = get_looking_turf(UP)
if(!above_turf)
	return
looking_vertically = UP
looking_holder = new(above_turf, src, UP)
reset_perspective(looking_holder)
```

`look_down()` (`living.dm:2499`) is the same. `get_looking_turf()` (`living.dm:2473`) prints the failure message ("There's nothing interesting there." or "You can't see through the floor above/below you.") and returns null. So a failed look prints one message, and `looking_vertically` and `looking_holder` stay unset. Nothing is subscribed to the player's movement, so nothing can retry.

Once a look is active, `/atom/movable/looking_holder/proc/mirror_move` (`living.dm:2420`) runs on each move of the owner (or of the mech/container they are in). If the target turf is gone, it calls `owner.end_look()` (`living.dm:2439`), which resets the perspective, clears `looking_vertically` and deletes the holder. The failure message can therefore appear at most once per look.

The look keybinds (`code/datums/keybinding/living.dm:33-72`) call `look_up()`/`look_down()` on key-down and `end_look()` on key-up, so holding the key is the only way to keep the look alive.

For #88008, the holder tracks the outermost atom on the turf via `update_container()` (`living.dm:2404`), which re-registers on the mech and mirrors its moves, including z-moves. The mech case works.

## Fix

None. Both reports appear to predate the current `looking_holder` implementation (a holder atom that mirrors the container's moves), which replaced the older behaviour. No commit was made.

## Verification

Checked in game as described under Reproduction: failed look leaves no state; a look that becomes invalid is ended; the holder follows a mech across x moves and z moves when moved by `vehicle_move()`, `Move()` or `forceMove()`.

Not checked: the HUD button and the lookup/lookdown verbs (only the proc and keybind code were read); real keyboard driving of a mech; the repeated-message count over many real tile-by-tile moves with a failed look (step 3 used `forceMove`); the original downstream (Skyrat) code, where the bug may still exist.

## Notes

- **Enter a mech while looking up.** `/mob/living/forceMove()` calls `reset_perspective()` whenever a client mob is moved successfully (`living.dm:1977`). Entering a mech (via `moved_inside()`, which `forceMove`s the occupant) or being teleported while looking up therefore puts `client.eye` back on the mob/mech, while `looking_vertically` and `looking_holder` remain set until `end_look()` runs. Releasing the look key (or toggling the verb) calls `end_look()` and clears it. It is transient and key-hold-driven, so it was judged not worth a change, but it is a real state inconsistency and could be what some players see as "the camera stays behind". This was not shown to be the cause of #88008, and the inconsistency was only demonstrated with a scripted mech entry.
- #88008's report listed three test-merged PRs (jetpack/wing force buffs, baton armor, projectile rework). None of them is obviously related, but they were not examined, and a bug in that test-merge build is not excluded.
- Both issues were opened against builds older than the tested master (April and November 2024), and #82557 mentions a downstream codebase. No upstream PR that fixed them was identified.
