## Summary

Three jetpack reports were checked on master. Holding east, west and south at once with a tank jetpack or MOD advanced ion jets moved a mob cleanly south, so #88828 did not reproduce (its video could not be viewed). Fatness slowing a stabilised jetpack (#85594) is real but deliberate: the obesity movespeed modifier is documented as applying "even if flying and floating", so only a design change would alter it. A jetpack worked normally after unbuckling from Lia (#88382). No code was changed.

## The bug

### #88828

The reporter holds east, west and south together with advanced ion jets and sees odd movement, shown in a video (client 515.1641). The video was downloaded but could not be viewed, so what it shows is unknown; the issue text only lists the keys.

### #85594

Per the report (2024), a fat character flying with a stabilised jetpack is much slower. Maintainers in the comments say obesity "should affect acceleration but not max speed". One commenter pointed out that gravity plays no role in space.

### #88382

Per the report (2024), after buckling onto Lia (a space carp) in a MODsuit with ion jets, turning the jetpack on and unbuckling left the jetpack not working for the rest of the round.

## Reproduction

All tests were done on the test server with admin tools. Keys were pressed through the Lua editor (`client:keyDown()` / `keyUp()`), which moves the mob like real input (it logged a harmless "Division by zero" runtime in `get_loc_from_mousepos`). Buckling was done by calling `buckle_mob()`, not by dragging with the mouse.

### #88828 (not reproduced)

1. Give a human an `/obj/item/tank/jetpack` and turn it on in open space; hold east, west and south together. The client's `intended_direction` stayed at 14 (east|west|south), but the mob moved straight south at about 8 tiles per second, both stabilised and unstabilised, identical to holding south alone (sampled positions 100, 98, 95, 94, 91, 89, 87, 85...).
2. Equip a MOD with the advanced ion jets module (`/obj/item/mod/module/jetpack/advanced`), unstabilised, and hold the same three keys next to a dense object (a disposal outlet). The mob moved south 5 tiles and stopped at the dense object, with no drift handler left and no jitter or odd motion.

Not tested: diagonal key masks, a real keyboard on a real client, and the video's exact scenario.

### #85594 (not reproduced as a bug)

With a MOD stabilised jetpack on, `cached_multiplicative_slowdown` was 1.2. After adding the `fat` trait through the Lua editor it was 2.7.

### #88382 (not reproduced)

1. Tank jetpack: turned it on, buckled the human to `/mob/living/basic/carp/pet/lia` with a forced `buckle_mob`, turned the jetpack on while buckled (it returned TRUE), unbuckled, then held east. The mob moved from x=30 to x=36 with ion trails.
2. MOD advanced ion jets: buckled to Lia, activated the module while buckled (it became active), unbuckled, held north. The mob moved from 51,83 to 51,87 with ion trails. East was blocked by a grille, which is expected.

No runtime related to jetpacks was seen.

## Cause

### #88828: no defect found

`client/keyLoop`'s direction calculation (`code/modules/keybindings/bindings_atom.dm:22-25`) cancels opposing directions, so with east, west and south held only south is used: "Sanity checks in case you hold left and right and up to make sure you only go up". The raw `client.intended_direction` is not filtered this way, and the jetpack component reads it directly in two places: `code/datums/components/jetpack.dm:136` (to generate the ion trail when keys are pressed) and `:192` (in `on_pushoff`, which compares `intended_direction` to `get_dir(source, backup)` to decide whether the push-off halt is prevented). By reading the code, the 3-key mask would not equal a single direction, so it falls through to the later checks; no sign of wrong results was found in the cases tried. A video-only symptom could be client-side rendering or key handling and cannot be settled from the server.

### #85594: intended

`code/modules/movespeed/modifiers/mobs.dm:1-3`:

```dm
/datum/movespeed_modifier/obesity
	// large weight slows even if flying and floating
	multiplicative_slowdown = 1.5
```

It is added for fat mobs in `code/modules/mob/living/carbon/human/init_signals.dm:40`. Stabilised jetpack flight uses the normal move delay (`cached_multiplicative_slowdown`), so the obesity modifier applies on top of the jetpack's own bonus modifier. 2.7 versus 1.2 is a delay roughly 2.25 times longer. The modifier's comment shows this is deliberate, though maintainers in the issue thread argue it should only affect acceleration.

### #88382: no defect found

`should_trigger()` in the jetpack component (`jetpack.dm:158-163`) returns FALSE while buckled ("You don't want use jet in gravity or while buckled"), which only blocks use during the ride. No signal clears the jetpack on buckle or unbuckle, and the jetpack worked afterwards in both the tank and MOD cases.

## Fix

None. No commit was made for this unit.

## Verification

Not applicable (no change). What was not checked, per issue: for #88828, the issue's video and a real client holding the keys; for #85594, a change of design (no code change was tested); for #88382, buckling by mouse drag onto Lia rather than the forced `buckle_mob` call, and other mounts. It is possible the 2024 round had another effect or a since-fixed bug.

## Notes

- #85594 is a design question: if maintainers want fatness to affect only acceleration with a stabilised jetpack, the obesity modifier would need to exclude the floating/flying case or a jetpack-specific adjustment. The agents did not try this.
- #88828 may be a client-side visual artefact; it needs someone to watch the video and compare against a live client.
- #88382 may have been an effect of its round's state (the report says "consistent through the round"); a drag-based buckle test would close it.
- Related group: #88820, #72793, #77176 and #88454 (newtonian drift) were examined separately. As far as seen, nothing here shares a cause with those.
