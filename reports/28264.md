# #28264: Lizard tails are drawn above worn backpacks and capes

| | |
|---|---|
| Issues | [#28264](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/28264): verified (reproduced, fixed) |
| Status | Verified; fix committed |
| Fix branch | [fix/28264](https://github.com/sohpea-cloub/tgstation-slop/tree/fix/28264) |
| Tested on | tgstation master `87b93338`, 2026-10-23 |

## Summary

The tail's "front" overlay (the one drawn over the body) uses `BODY_FRONT_LAYER`, which is above the back slot and the neck slot. A lizard wearing a backpack or duffelbag therefore shows its tail on top of the bag. The fix adds a new layer define, `BODY_TAIL_FRONT_LAYER` (10.5), just below `NECK_LAYER` (10) and uses it for the tail's front layer. Tails now draw under back and neck items, but still over suits, belts and the uniform.

## The bug

The issue (opened 2017, still reported as present in 2023 and 2024) says that when a lizard faces north while wearing a duffelbag, the tail is drawn over the bag. The reporter suspects the same layering is why capes and cloaks also sit below tails.

## Reproduction

1. On the test server, start the round and observe.
2. Open the Lua editor and create a human with the lizard species, then equip a duffelbag on the back and turn the human to face north. The Lua used:
   - `SS13.new('/mob/living/carbon/human', me.loc)`
   - `H:set_species(dm.global_procs._text2path('/datum/species/lizard'))`
   - `SS13.new('/obj/item/storage/backpack/duffelbag', H.loc)`, then `H:equip_to_appropriate_slot(bag)`
   - `H:setDir(1)` (north)
3. Render the mob, facing north, enlarged (6x).

Observed on master: tail pixels were drawn over the lower part of the duffelbag. The bag was equipped with the normal equip proc and appeared worn in the render. The mob was created and equipped through Lua rather than by a player playing a lizard, but the rendering path is the same.

Not tried: a cape or other neck item, a tail under a suit, and east or west facing. The cape part of the claim comes from the layer numbers (see Cause), not from a render.

## Cause

Mob overlay layers are positive numbers in `code/__DEFINES/mobs.dm`, and the overlay datum draws them at the negative of that number (`get_all_overlays` in `code/datums/bodypart_overlays/bodypart_overlay.dm` passes `-overlay_layer`). A smaller number is therefore drawn on top.

The tail overlay at master is defined in `code/modules/surgery/organs/external/tails.dm:143-147`:

```dm
/datum/bodypart_overlay/mutant/tail
	layers = list(
		EXTERNAL_FRONT = BODY_FRONT_LAYER,
		EXTERNAL_BEHIND = BODY_BEHIND_LAYER,
	)
```

`BODY_FRONT_LAYER` is 2.5 (`code/__DEFINES/mobs.dm:818`). The back slot is `BACK_LAYER` 9 (line 838) and the neck slot is `NECK_LAYER` 10 (line 840). So the tail's front sprite (-2.5) is drawn above both worn backpacks (-9) and capes or other neck items (-10). `BODY_FRONT_LAYER` is a layer meant for things that go over nearly everything on the mob, such as horns, and every tail subtype inherits it from this parent datum.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 28264: draw tails below the back and neck slots":

- `code/__DEFINES/mobs.dm`: adds `BODY_TAIL_FRONT_LAYER` with value 10.5, directly after `NECK_LAYER`. Its comment says it is for tails drawn over the body, below the back slot and neck items and above everything beneath them.
- `code/modules/surgery/organs/external/tails.dm`: the tail parent datum's `EXTERNAL_FRONT` now uses `BODY_TAIL_FRONT_LAYER`.

10.5 puts the tail below `BACK_LAYER` and `NECK_LAYER` but above `SUIT_STORE_LAYER` (11), `BELT_LAYER` (12), `SUIT_LAYER` (14) and the uniform (19). Tails that show over supported suits (`mesh_in_suits`) should therefore still show over them. The tail spines' `ADJ` layer is unchanged. Wings and other `BODY_FRONT_LAYER` users, such as horns, were not touched.

Because the change is on the parent datum, every tail subtype inherits it (cat, monkey, xeno, fish and others), not just lizard tails.

## Verification

After rebuilding, the same steps were repeated on a fresh round: a lizard human, a duffelbag on the back, facing north, rendered at 6x. The duffelbag was fully visible over the tail, where before the tail was drawn over the lower part of the bag. The build had 0 errors and 0 warnings, and the round's runtime log had no runtime errors.

Not checked:
- A cape or other neck item (expected to behave the same, since 10 < 10.5, but not rendered).
- A lizard tail with a suit on, in particular a `mesh_in_suits` suit.
- East and west facing.
- Tail types other than lizard, and the fish or other infusion tails.
- Appearance of wagging tails and of tails alongside other overlays such as damage or gauze.

## Notes

- This is a visual change for every tail in the game, so it should be reviewed by someone who knows how tails are meant to look. The 10.5 value is a choice: it keeps tails over the belt and suit, but a maintainer may want a different order.
- `code/_globalvars/layers.dm` has a list of named mob layers used by the layer debug tooling. It lists `NECK_LAYER`, `BODY_FRONT_LAYER` and the others, and the fix did not add `BODY_TAIL_FRONT_LAYER` to it. This was not examined further, and the list may need the new entry.
- The new define is indented like the other sub-layers in the layer table in `mobs.dm`. The comment at the top of that table says new standing layers need `TOTAL_LAYERS` and `layers_to_offset` updated; this one is a sub-layer and is not drawn through `apply_overlay`, so neither should apply.
- Other `BODY_FRONT_LAYER` users, such as roach and moth parts, wings, ears, splat and the golem food status effect, were left as they are.
