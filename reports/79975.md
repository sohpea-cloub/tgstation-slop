## Summary

When a caster who is alive but past 100% damage (kept alive by tenacity, high mood or similar) shapeshifts with a spell or the polymorphic belt, `from_spell/on_apply()` scales their total damage onto the new form. The result is at least the new form's max health, and it is applied before the death handling is registered, so the form dies instantly with the caster's mind inside and never reverts (#79975, #87990). The fix caps the converted damage at the form's max health minus 1. Separately, when a shape dies and reverts, `restore_caster()` killed the caster before moving the mind back, so a lich's phylactery never saw the mind's body die (#87114). The fix moves the mind first and then kills the caster.

## The bug

- **#79975 and #87990:** a player at slightly negative health but not in crit (their mood or tenacity keeps them up; the issue suggests roughly 101-119 damage) uses the polymorphic belt, or any shapeshift that converts damage. They turn into the animal already dead, with their mind in it, and it is never turned back into their human body. #87990 reports the same for using the polymorph inverter in crit with tenacity. A comment on #87990 asks whether other damage-converting shapeshift spells behave the same: they do, as both go through the same status effect.
- **#87114:** a wizard with Bind Soul (a phylactery) who dies while in Wild Shapeshift form is reverted to their dead human body, but the phylactery does nothing and they are not revived.

## Reproduction

All on master `87b93338`, using admin tools on a test server (the Lua editor to set things up, then clicking the real action buttons).

### #79975 / #87990 (polymorphic belt)

1. Using Lua, create a human with a mind and give it a jumpsuit and `/obj/item/polymorph_belt/functioning` in the belt slot, with `stored_mob_type` set to fox and the update-transform action's channel time set to 0. Put a player in control of the human.
2. In a separate Lua run (the proc call has to be made as the possessed mob, or the admin proc wrapper runtimes on a missing ckey), give the human the `nodeath` trait and apply 105 brute damage. This stands in for tenacity or mood keeping a human with negative health alive.
3. Click the "Invert Polymorphic Field" action.
4. Result on master: the player is a fox with `stat` 3 (DEAD), the human inside is alive (`stat` 0), the mind is in the fox, and the `shapechange_mob/from_spell/polymorph_belt` status effect is still applied. It never reverts.

### #87114 (Bind Soul)

1. Using Lua, create a human with a mind and a player in control, grant it `/datum/action/cooldown/spell/shapeshift/wizard` (shapeshift type mouse, cooldown 1), and create a crowbar with the phylactery component (`/datum/component/phylactery` with the human's mind, 50 as the respawn time argument).
2. Click "Wild Shapeshift", then call `death()` on the mouse.
3. Result on master: the human body shows "has died", there is no "soul being dragged back" message, and no revival happens after the 5 second timer.

## Cause

### #79975 / #87990

`code/modules/spells/spell_types/shapeshift/_shape_status.dm:171` `/datum/status_effect/shapechange_mob/from_spell/on_apply()` converts the caster's damage onto the new form:

```dm
var/damage_to_apply = owner.maxHealth * (caster_mob.get_total_damage() / caster_mob.maxHealth)
owner.apply_damage(damage_to_apply, ...)
```

A caster who is alive with total damage at or past their max health (tenacity, mood or `nodeath` keeping them up) gives `damage_to_apply >= owner.maxHealth`. This runs before `return ..()` at line 191, and the parent `on_apply()` is what moves the mind (line 38) and registers `COMSIG_LIVING_DEATH` to `on_shape_death` (line 45). So the form dies with nothing listening, no revert is triggered, and the mind is then transferred into the dead form.

### #87114

`restore_caster()` (`_shape_status.dm:83`) ran in this order when `kill_caster_after` was set (the Wild Shapeshift spell sets `die_with_shapeshifted_form` and `revert_on_death`, so `from_spell/on_shape_death()` at line 226 calls it with `kill_caster_after = TRUE`):

1. `caster_mob.death()` (line 101-102). The mind is still in the shape at this point.
2. `owner.mind?.transfer_to(caster_mob)` (line 110).

The phylactery (`code/datums/components/phylactery.dm:119`, `check_if_lich_died`, registered on `COMSIG_GLOB_MOB_DEATH`) starts the revival timer only if `died.mind == lich_mind`. The caster's global death signal fired while the mind was still in the shape, so `died.mind` was null. The shape's own global death signal is sent after its `COMSIG_LIVING_DEATH` handlers have run (`code/modules/mob/living/death.dm:259-260`), and by then the mind had moved to the caster, so `died.mind` was null again. Neither death carried the mind, and no timer started.

## Fix

Two commits on the fix branch, both in `_shape_status.dm`.

**Fix issue 79975: converting a low-health caster into a shapeshift no longer kills the shape** (also covers #87990)
- The converted damage is capped: `min(owner.maxHealth * (caster_mob.get_total_damage() / caster_mob.maxHealth), owner.maxHealth - 1)`. The form survives at 1 health point (for example, a fox at 1/20) and the normal revert paths keep working.

**Fix issue 87114: reverting a shape that died now kills the caster with their mind back in them**
- The `caster_mob.death()` call is moved to after `owner.mind?.transfer_to(caster_mob)` and before the shape is deleted, so the mind's body dies with the mind in it and the phylactery's global-death check matches.

Choice made for #79975: capping keeps the shapeshift usable at lethal damage rather than refusing the shapeshift. An alternative would be blocking the transformation; this was not tried.

## Verification

- #79975, after the fix: using the same belt setup, the fox had `stat` 0 and health 1/20, the human inside was alive, and clicking the action again reverted to the human normally.
- #87114, after the fix: killing the mouse printed "You feel your soul being dragged back to this world... revive at your phylactery in 0.083 minutes", then "Your bones clatter and shudder as you are pulled back into this world!" and the corpse fell to pieces.
- The runtime log showed only two admin-proc-wrapper "no ckey" runtimes from the first Lua attempt at the setup (the trait and damage were set in the same run as the possession); none came from the changed code.
- The build compiled with 0 errors and 0 warnings.

Not checked:
- The DM unit tests (`code/modules/unit_tests/spell_shapeshift.dm`) were not run.
- Only the Wild Shapeshift spell (mouse) and the belt (fox) were tested, not other shapeshift spells.
- #87990's exact scenario (tenacity in crit) was approximated with the `nodeath` trait and damage, not with the real tenacity effect or mood.
- The edge case where the damage conversion in `after_unchange()` alone kills the caster before the mind transfer (which would reintroduce the lich ordering problem) was not exercised. A normal human does not die from 100% converted damage, so it was left alone.

## Notes

- After reverting from the capped form, damage converts back proportionally, so the caster can end up a little healthier than before the shapeshift. One revert showed 12.5 health on the caster; that number was not investigated further.
- A different fix to the same file (a separate work unit) touches the `on_apply()` / `after_unchange()` area, so the two may conflict textually when merged.
- #87990 is a duplicate of #79975: same code path, same fix.
