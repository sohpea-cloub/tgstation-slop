# #42412: Emitters not shooting (green emitters that do not fire on a powernet shared with SMES units)

| Status | Not reproduced on master; no fix committed. The investigation was cut short, so the conclusion rests on code reading plus the tests listed below |

## Summary

The issue says emitters on a powernet shared with SMES inputs show the green "powered" overlay but do not fire, because the SMES units take all the available power. On master, emitters are registered in `SSmachines.processing_early`, so they draw power before the SMES units' input step runs, which is exactly the priority the last comment on the issue asks for. A test powernet where an SMES took all the surplus still had its emitter powered and firing, and the station's supermatter emitters fired normally. One small, cosmetic inconsistency was found (a stale "powered" overlay for up to 2 seconds after toggling), and some unexplained 2-second power drops were seen in the engine log of the live station.

## The bug

The reports describe emitters that look powered (green overlay) but do not shoot. Comments suggest several things:

- A 2019 comment asks whether the emitter was really green or orange (orange is the low power overlay), and the reporter says it was green.
- Another commenter has seen mixed orange and green emitters in one powernet and suspects the orange ones were added to processing later.
- A 2023 comment could only get it once, on a single emitter, in testing.
- A 2024 comment says it reproduces on master when emitters share a line with SMES inputs, because "SMES consume all the available load without giving any to the emitters", so emitters do not work until the SMES are full or their input is reduced. The proposed fix is to give emitters priority on the powernet.

## Reproduction

Not reproduced. What was done, with the Lua editor and SDQL as an observer on the test server:

1. Built a small isolated powernet in a corner of the map: ten cable pieces on plating, a source SMES with a terminal so that it was operational and output level 6000, a welded emitter with `active = 1`, and a second SMES (the "consumer") with its own terminal and input level 50000 with input enabled. The consumer SMES was meant to take every watt of surplus. (A powernet had to be created manually for the cables, and cables on space turfs do not get one, hence the plating.)
2. Watched the emitter. It stayed powered and kept firing (`last_shot` kept advancing). The net's available power and load were both 12000, so the emitter's draw was being served while the SMES input absorbed the rest.
3. Sampled the real supermatter engine room emitters (three welded emitters on the main station grid, with three engineering SMES outputting 180000 each, so available power 540000 and load equal to it). With `active = 1` they fired normally.
4. Read the engine log (`engine.html`) for the round. All three supermatter emitters logged "lost power and turned OFF" and then "regained power and turned ON" about 2 seconds later, three times, at about 30-second intervals, all three emitters at the same moment. No more drops were seen in the following minutes. The cause was not found. It may relate to supermatter activity or to the test actions made through Lua. This is an open lead rather than a confirmed bug.

Not done: the follow-up variant with the source output lowered so there is not enough power for several emitters (to check the orange/green mix), and a check of the stale overlay in game (see Cause), because the run ended before those were done.

## Cause

There is no defect matching the report found in the code. The relevant behavior on master:

- `code/modules/power/singularity/emitter.dm:69` (in `Initialize`):
  ```dm
  //Add to the early process queue to prioritize power draw
  SSmachines.processing_early += src
  ```
  So the emitter's `process_early()` runs in the early part of each machine tick.
- `process_early()` (`emitter.dm:210`) draws power with `add_load()` only if `surplus()` is at least the amount it needs. `surplus()` (`code/modules/power/power.dm:86`) is `avail - load`. If there is not enough, it sets `powered = FALSE`, updates the overlay (orange) and logs "lost power and turned OFF".
- `/datum/powernet/proc/reset()` (`code/modules/power/powernet.dm:81`) moves `delayedload` into `load` and `newavail` into `avail` at the start of the tick, before the early processing, and gives unused power back to SMES units through `restore()`. SMES units take their input in their own `process()` (`code/modules/power/smes.dm:351`), which is in the normal machine processing, after the early queue.

So the SMES units cannot starve the emitters in the way the 2024 comment describes: by the time an SMES draws its input, the emitters have already added their load for the tick. An emitter that shows orange is one whose `surplus()` really was too low.

One minor effect was noticed in the code. `powered` is only updated in `process_early()` (`emitter.dm:222` and `emitter.dm:230`), and `interact()` (`emitter.dm:171`) switches `active` on and calls `update_appearance()` without touching `powered`. The overlay colour (`emitter.dm:150` to `162`) depends on `powered`. After toggling an emitter on, the overlay uses whatever `powered` was last (it starts FALSE and stays at its last value when the emitter is switched off), until the next `process_early()` call, up to about 2 seconds later. This is transient and cosmetic. It was reasoned from the code and not observed in game.

## Fix

None. A possible small cleanup, not made: reset or recompute `powered` when the emitter is toggled in `interact()`, and clear it in `process_early()` when the emitter is not active.

Not applicable, as no change was made. The checks that were made are described under Reproduction.

## Notes

- The issue is old (the report is from 2019 and the processing-early priority is in the current code). It likely describes behavior that has since been fixed, or a different cause, such as the whole powernet really being short of power or SMES units being off or deleted (as the first comment on the issue noticed in the round's logs).
- The simultaneous 2-second power drops of the three engine emitters in the test round were not explained. If someone wants to chase this further, sampling the powernet's `avail` and `load` inside `emitter.dm`'s `process_early()` when it happens would show whether it is a tick-order effect on the main grid.
- Not tested: a player-operated emitter (switching with a click rather than setting `active`), emitters in a mixed powernet with several emitters competing for too little power, and any station layout other than the one on the test server.
