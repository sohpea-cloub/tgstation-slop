## Summary

The 2021 report says a player controlling a mind-magnified monkey who enters a transit tube is separated from their body, with the "soul" carried away by the tube. On current master, a mind-magnified monkey rode transit tube pods in both directions with body, mind and view staying together. No code path that touches the mind was found. No fix was made.

## The bug

As reported (client 514.1561, round 172615): entering a transit tube as a mind-magnified monkey splits body and soul, and the soul is taken away by the tube. The reporter did not know where the body went or where the soul ended up. A comment asked where the body went; nobody answered.

## Reproduction

The map was a Meta-like station with two dispenser-type transit stations. No regular stations were present.

1. Start the round and observe. Using SDQL2 (`SELECT /obj/structure/transit_tube/station MAP [x, y, z, boarding_dir, dir]`), find the stations: one at (176,136,2) with `boarding_dir` north and one at (201,136,2) with `boarding_dir` west.
2. With the Lua editor, spawn a `/mob/living/carbon/human/species/monkey` next to the first station, and spawn a `/obj/item/clothing/head/helmet/monkey_sentience`.
3. Equip the helmet on the monkey (head slot). This starts the real ghost poll. Sign up, then take control of the monkey ("You're a mind magnified monkey!").
4. Walk the monkey into the dispenser from its boarding side (west into the station at 201,136, and north into the station at 176,136).
5. Observe during the ride and after arrival.

Results (both stations, both directions):

- On entry a temporary pod materialised around the monkey and moved with it.
- Mid-trip the monkey was inside the pod, and the camera was on the pod, which is the normal view from inside a container.
- On arrival the pod was deleted and the monkey was on the floor. Its ckey was still the player's, `mind.current` was the same monkey, the client's eye was on the monkey, the helmet was still on its head, and `stat` was 0. Its AI controller was present but off.
- The runtime log showed no runtimes except errors from the test's own Lua (a bad variable read).

## Cause

Not applicable: no defect was found. For reference:

- `code/game/objects/structures/transit_tubes/station.dm:252`: the dispenser's `Bumped` does `AM.forceMove(pod)` and `launch_pod()`.
- When the trip ends, `pod_stopped` deletes the pod, and `/obj/structure/transit_tube_pod/Destroy()` (`transit_tube_pod.dm`) calls `empty_pod()`, which moves the contents out.
- Pods move through a move loop; contents are carried with them.
- Nothing in this path touches mind or ckey. The helmet (`code/modules/clothing/head/mind_monkey_helmet.dm`) disconnects only when it is dropped or the wearer's species changes, and a pod does neither.

## Fix

None.

## Verification

Only the reproduction above. Not tested: a regular (non-dispenser) station with a manually placed pod, and a tube break or ejection mid-trip (`outside_tube`).

## Notes

- The report is from 2021 on BYOND 514, before the transit tube movement was reworked into move loops. It may have been fixed incidentally, or it may never have been a real body/mind split. A likely explanation is that the camera follows the pod while the player is inside it, which is normal. This is a guess, not something established.
- The untested cases above are the only remaining doubt about closing the issue.
