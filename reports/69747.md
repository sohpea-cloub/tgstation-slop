## Summary
In 2022, wizarditis was reported to permanently delete the helmet of a worn space suit (the carp suit) or the parts of a MOD suit once it gave the victim a wizard hat. On current master, neither case deletes anything. The disease now drops the worn item instead of deleting it, hooded helmets retract back into their suit on drop, and MOD parts are undroppable so nothing is replaced.

## The bug
As reported: a player wearing a carp space suit who caught wizarditis lost the helmet permanently when the disease spawned a wizard hat. A commenter said it fully deleted MOD suits too (with a video). The reporter expected the same for any hardsuit or MOD suit.

## Reproduction
Attempted on master; nothing was deleted. Both cases used the Lua editor as an observer on the test server.

1. Carp costume: spawned a human wearing `/obj/item/clothing/suit/hooded/carp_costume` with the hood deployed, gave them wizarditis (`ForceContractDisease`), then called `spawn_wizard_clothes(100)` on the disease. Result: the head slot became `/obj/item/clothing/head/wizard/yellow`, a wizard robe was attempted, and the carp costume was dropped to the floor. An SDQL query for `/obj/item/clothing/head/hooded/carp_hood` returned exactly one object, located inside the carp costume: the hood had retracted into the suit, not been deleted.
2. MOD suit: spawned a human with `/obj/item/mod/control/pre_equipped/standard` on the back and deployed it with quick-deploy, then ran the same wizarditis call twice at 100% chance. Result: back, head, suit and shoes were unchanged (MOD control, MOD helmet, chest and boots); only a staff was added.

The disease effect was triggered by calling its proc directly at 100% chance, not by waiting for it to progress naturally.

## Cause
Nothing is broken now. Why the report no longer applies:

- `code/datums/diseases/wizarditis.dm:114-126` calls `dropItemToGround()` on the current head, suit and shoes before `equip_to_slot_or_del()`. The 2022 code deleted the worn item to make room.
- Hooded clothing is handled by `/datum/component/toggle_attached_clothing`. When a deployed hood is dropped, its `COMSIG_ITEM_DROPPED` handler (`on_deployed_dropped`, `code/datums/components/toggle_attached_clothing.dm:180`) calls `remove_deployable()` (line 198), which moves the hood back into its suit.
- MOD parts get `TRAIT_NODROP` on deployment (`code/modules/mod/mod_activation.dm:86`), so `dropItemToGround` fails and the disease replaces nothing.
- The carp suit is now a hooded costume, so the old space suit with a separate helmet case no longer exists.

## Fix
None. No commits.

## Verification
Checked as described under Reproduction. Not checked: other hooded suits or MOD variants (they use the same component path, so they should behave the same), natural disease progression, or other species.

## Notes
Likely already fixed upstream. The issue can probably be closed. One side effect of the current behavior: a hooded suit worn when the hat spawns can be dropped to the floor if the robe is attempted, as seen in the carp costume test.
