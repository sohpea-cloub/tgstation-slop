## Summary

Colorful reagent overwrote a human's real hair and facial hair colors, and nothing in the wash code puts hair color back, so soap, showers and rags could not undo it, unlike the other recolors from the same reagent. The fix stores the reagent's color in a new `washable_hair_color` variable on the head, leaves the real hair colors alone, draws the hair with the washable color on top of them, and clears it when an exposed head is washed.

## The bug

The issue says that when a character is recolored with colorful reagent (by drinking it, or by eating a rainbow crayon), every recolor can be cleaned off except the hair. The new hair color stays after using soap, rags or showers. It is labelled as another consequence of #88201.

Played out: the character's skin, organs and clothes can be washed back, but hair and facial hair keep the reagent's color permanently, and the original color is lost, since the stored hair color itself was replaced.

## Reproduction

On master, using the Lua editor as an admin on a running round:

1. Spawn a human, set its hairstyle to Short Hair, and set both hair color and facial hair color to `#112233`.
2. Put 10 units of colorful reagent in a beaker and call `reagent.expose_mob(human, TOUCH, 5, 1, 0)` on the human.
3. Read `hair_color`, then call `human.wash(1)` and read it again.

Observed on master: `hair_color` was `#112233`, became `#ffc425` after the reagent, and was still `#ffc425` after the wash. The reagent was applied by calling `expose_mob` directly, not by drinking it or eating a crayon.

## Cause

The colorful reagent's `expose_mob` sets the human's permanent hair colors:

```dm
exposed_human.set_facial_haircolor(picked_color, update = FALSE)
exposed_human.set_haircolor(picked_color)
```
(`code/modules/reagents/chemistry/reagents/other_reagents.dm:2424-2425`)

That replaces `hair_color` and `facial_hair_color` on the head, which is the same data a character's own hair color uses. `/mob/living/carbon/human/wash` (`code/modules/mob/living/carbon/human/human.dm:621`) only cleans the mouth (lips), hands and similar things; nothing in it touches hair. The other recolors the reagent applies work through the atom color system (`WASHABLE_COLOUR_PRIORITY`), which `wash` does know how to remove, so hair was the only part left out.

The head already has two hair color overrides, `override_hair_color` (clearable) and `fixed_hair_color` (never cleared) (`code/modules/surgery/bodyparts/head.dm:61-63`), consulted in `set_overlay_hair_color` (`code/modules/surgery/bodyparts/head_hair_and_lips.dm:179`). Neither fit a color that should be washable.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 88987: colorful reagent hair color can now be washed off":

- `code/modules/surgery/bodyparts/head.dm`: a new variable `washable_hair_color` on the head, documented as a temporary hair and facial hair color that overrides the normal hair colors but not the override or fixed ones.
- `code/modules/surgery/bodyparts/head_hair_and_lips.dm`: `set_overlay_hair_color` uses `washable_hair_color` after `override_hair_color` and `fixed_hair_color` and before the normal color. A new proc `set_washable_hair_color(hex_string, update = TRUE)` (a no-op on `/mob/living`, working on humans) sets or clears it and calls `update_hair()`.
- `code/modules/reagents/chemistry/reagents/other_reagents.dm`: colorful reagent calls `set_washable_hair_color(picked_color)` instead of changing the permanent hair colors.
- `code/modules/mob/living/carbon/human/human.dm`: `wash` clears `washable_hair_color` when the head is not covered by something with `HIDEHAIR`, and counts that as `COMPONENT_CLEANED`.

This keeps the character's real hair color intact, so washing restores it exactly. The approach follows the existing `override_hair_color` pattern. Reusing `override_hair_color` was not tried; a separate variable avoids sharing state with whatever else sets that override (not examined).

## Verification

On a rebuilt test server, with the same steps as the reproduction:

- After the reagent: `hair_color` stayed `#112233`, and the head's `washable_hair_color` was `#0057e7`.
- After `human.wash(1)`: it returned 3 (`COMPONENT_CLEANED`) and `washable_hair_color` was null again.
- No new runtimes appeared in the round's runtime log. The three runtimes seen in the log came from the earlier run on unfixed code.

Not checked: the color of the rendered hair overlay (the Lua read of `get_hair_overlays` only returned a list); drinking the reagent or eating a rainbow crayon; soap, showers and rags individually (they use the same `wash()` path but were not run); a human wearing something that hides hair (`HIDEHAIR`), where the color should stay until the head is uncovered and washed; facial hair separately from hair.

## Notes

- Hair now stays dyed while the head is covered: `wash` leaves the washable color when `HIDEHAIR` covers the head. This follows how the rest of `wash` treats covered slots, but a maintainer may prefer different behaviour.
- The reagent's color applies to both hair and facial hair through the one variable; they cannot be washed separately.
- `override_hair_color` and `fixed_hair_color` still take priority over the washable color, so a character with either does not show the reagent's color in hair at all. The reagent's other recolors are unchanged.
- The issue calls this "another #88201 issue"; that change was not looked at.
