# #83481: Blackmarket uplink teleport delivery can silently lose an order (plus a broken uplink UI)

| | |
|---|---|
| Issues | [#83481](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/83481): fixed (reported symptom "nothing spawns" **not reproduced**; the one loss path that exists in the code was closed) <br> [#77356](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/77356): partly duplicate of #83481, fixed by the same commit (symptom matches the loss path exactly, but the path was never hit in game) <br> [#86885](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/86885): verified and fixed (separate bug in the uplink UI) |
| Status | Fixed (two commits); the original #83481 report is only partly explained |
| Fix branch | [fix/83481](https://github.com/sohpea-cloub/tgstation-slop/tree/fix/83481) |
| Tested on | tgstation master `87b93338`, 2026-10-23 |

## Summary

Black market purchases with the Teleport delivery method work on Meta and Icebox in current master. The only way found for such an order to vanish is when `SSmarket` cannot find a safe turf: the purchase has already been taken out of the queue, so it is skipped with no message, no delivery and no refund. That rate is very low (about 0.06% of purchases on Meta by measurement), so it probably does not explain both reporters, and the "sparks but no item" symptom of #83481 could not be reproduced at all. The fix makes teleport delivery retry across the whole station and fall back to delivering at the uplink, so an order is never dropped silently. #86885 turned out to be a separate pair of defects in `market_uplink.dm`'s `ui_data`, both fixed.

## The bug

### #83481 and #77356: teleport orders that never arrive

Players buy something through a black market uplink using the Teleport delivery method. They expect a chat message ("... the order is being teleported to <area> in 60 seconds"), and then the item appearing there with a spark effect. #77356 (Icebox) reports that no message appeared, nothing arrived, and the order was not in `SSmarket.queued_purchases` afterwards. #83481 (a downstream fork round, on Meta or Delta according to a commenter) reports that at the announced location only a spark effect appeared and the item did not; a commenter added that the target area was spaced.

A maintainer later (2024-04) could not reproduce #77356 on master across several stations and Z levels.

### #86885: uplink stuck on the Local Goods tab

Opening the "Local Goods" category in a black market uplink and trying to switch to another category did nothing until the uplink window was closed and reopened.

## Reproduction

### #83481 / #77356: not reproduced as reported

1. On the test server, on Meta and again on Icebox, an uplink was created with admin tools, a department account was given funds, and purchases using the Teleport method were made through the market datum's `purchase()` proc (not by clicking through the purchase UI). After about 66 seconds the result was counted with SDQL2.
2. Three bear traps bought this way on Meta all arrived, in three different station areas (Captain's Quarters, Starboard Primary Hallway, Circuit Workshop). The SDQL2 count of bear traps went from 2 to 5, and each purchase produced the "being teleported to ... in 60 seconds" message.
3. A stolen double-barrel shotgun (the item from the #83481 report) was produced with the spy "Fenced Goods" flow, which moves it to nullspace for the black market (`clean_up_stolen_item` with the black-market chance forced to 100). Its transform and alpha were untouched (`alpha` 255, `invisibility` 0). Bought via Teleport, it arrived on the floor of the target area after 60 seconds, so stolen items are not invisible or shrunk.
4. Not reproduced: the item missing at the target, with only sparks. Also not tested: a target area that is actually spaced (see Notes).

The loss path was measured separately. Using the Lua editor, `get_safe_random_station_turf_equal_weight()` was called 200 times per department on Meta and on Icebox. On Meta, engineering returned null 1 time in 200 and every other department 0 in 200; on Icebox, 0 in 200 everywhere. That is roughly 0.06% of purchases on Meta (about 1/200 for the engineering pick, which is chosen one time in nine).

The loss itself was shown by reading the code, not by hitting it in game: when that helper returns null, the code in Cause below skips the purchase.

### #86885: reproduced

1. A human mob was possessed on the test server, with an uplink in hand and its window open, and a `/datum/market_item/local_good` added to the market with the market's `add_item`.
2. From the UI, `set_category` to "Local Goods" and `select` on the item were sent as UI actions (`tgui_act`). On unmodified master code, the `viewing_category` value in the UI data stayed null after `set_category`, so the active tab was never reported to the client.
3. With the item selected and the buying modal open, the entry was deleted with `qdel`, which removes the "Local Goods" category (it disappears when its last item goes). After this the window stopped refreshing, and the runtime log showed repeated `runtime error: bad index` from `ui_data` at `market_uplink.dm`, line 64.

## Cause

### Teleport orders (#83481, #77356)

In `SSmarket.fire()` (`code/controllers/subsystem/market.dm:64-66`) every queued purchase is removed from `queued_purchases` before it is processed. The Teleport branch then did:

```dm
var/turf/targetturf = get_safe_random_station_turf_equal_weight() //todo: split weights
// This shouldn't happen.
if (!targetturf)
	continue
queued_purchases -= purchase
```

(`market.dm:95-99`). If no turf is returned, the loop moves on: no message is sent, no timer is set, the purchase is neither delivered nor deleted, and the money stays spent. This is the symptom in #77356 (no message, order absent from the queue). The `queued_purchases -= purchase` line was redundant, since the purchase was already cut at line 66.

The helper `get_safe_random_station_turf_equal_weight()` (`code/__HELPERS/turfs.dm:454-480`) picks one department at random and then calls `get_safe_random_station_turf(final_area_list)` once. That function (`turfs.dm:432`) only tries 5 random areas of the list, so a department with few areas with a free, non-dense, `VALID_TERRITORY`, non-groundless turf can return null. The helper never tried another department.

Another way an order is lost silently also exists and was left alone: `market_purchase`'s instance-deletion handler deletes the purchase if the uplink, the market entry or the item is deleted during the 60-second wait. That is deliberate ("we keep the money").

The cause of the "sparks but no item" report in #83481 was not found. `fake_teleport()` (`market.dm:134`) spawns the item at the target turf and then calls `do_sparks`, so a spark without an item would need `spawn_item` to return nothing or the item to be moved away. The report came from a fork (a Bubberstation round), whose code may differ.

### Uplink UI (#86885)

In `ui_data` in `code/modules/cargo/markets/market_uplink.dm`:

- Line 71: `data["viewing_category"] = market.categories[viewing_category] ? viewing_category : null`. `market.categories` is a list of category name strings, so indexing it with a string is always null. The viewed category was therefore never sent, and the tab was never highlighted. (It also dereferences `market` without a null check.)
- Line 64: `market.available_items[viewing_category][selected_item]` indexes the inner list unguarded. When `buying` is set and the viewed category no longer exists in `available_items`, as happens when the last Local Goods item is deleted or sold, this is a `bad index` runtime on every `ui_data` call, so the open window stops updating.

`/datum/market/proc/purchase` in `code/modules/cargo/markets/_market.dm:46` has the same unguarded double index.

## Fix

Branch: [fix/83481](https://github.com/sohpea-cloub/tgstation-slop/tree/fix/83481).

**"Fix issue 83481: black market teleport purchases are no longer silently lost"** changes two files.
- `code/__HELPERS/turfs.dm`: `get_safe_random_station_turf_equal_weight()` now returns `get_safe_random_station_turf(final_area_list) || get_safe_random_station_turf()`, so an unlucky department falls back to a random pick across the whole station.
- `code/controllers/subsystem/market.dm`: if there is still no turf, the order is delivered at the uplink's own turf (`get_turf(purchase.uplink)`) instead of being skipped. If even that is null, `stack_trace()` is called and the purchase is deleted, so it is at least logged. The redundant `queued_purchases -= purchase` was removed.

Delivering at the uplink was chosen so that the buyer always receives what was paid for; the inputs do not record other approaches that were considered.

**"Fix issue 86885: black market uplink UI stops updating and never highlights the viewed category"** changes two files.
- `market_uplink.dm`: `viewing_category` is now sent as `(market && (viewing_category in market.categories)) ? viewing_category : null`, and the line 64 lookup became `available_items[viewing_category]?[selected_item]`.
- `_market.dm`: `purchase()` uses `available_items[category]?[identifier]`, which returns FALSE for a missing category.

## Verification

Checked in game after the fixes:

- Teleport delivery (the three bear traps and the stolen shotgun from Reproduction) worked on the fixed build, with the message and delivery after 60 seconds.
- #86885, repeating the steps above on the fixed build: `viewing_category` followed the clicked tab ("Clothing", then "Local Goods", then "Weapons"). Deleting the selected Local Goods item updated the UI at once (`viewing_category` became null and "Local Goods" disappeared from `categories`), clicking Weapons then worked, and the runtime log had no new `bad index` entries.

Not checked:

- The new fallback branches (no safe turf found, and no turf even at the uplink) were never executed in game. The helper's null rate was too low to hit, and the branches were not forced.
- The one-line `?.` guard in `purchase()` in `_market.dm` was added after the in-game runs; that build was only compiled, and no purchase was made with it.
- A teleport target in an area that is actually spaced.
- Other maps and other Z layouts than Meta and Icebox.
- The client side of #86885: the reporter's exact "cannot leave the tab" may also involve the TSX interface. Only the server-side data was observed.

## Notes

- The 0.06% null rate cannot account for two independent reports, so other causes may exist, in particular for the fork round in #83481. Delivery in spaced areas was suggested by a commenter. The helper rejects groundless turfs and areas without `VALID_TERRITORY`, and a breached area that still has floor remains valid, with the item landing on the floor. Whether a truly spaced target can still be chosen on some map was not tested.
- #77356 is only a partial duplicate: its symptom matches the no-safe-turf path exactly, but that path was not hit in game and a maintainer failed to reproduce it in 2024.
- The Teleport fallback silently changes the destination to the uplink. Maintainers may prefer a different policy, such as refunding the purchase.
- With the fix, the buying modal (`ShipmentSelector`) in #86885 still stays open if the selected item disappears, and the user has to press cancel. This was left unchanged.
- While testing, setting a signal handler with a callback from the Lua editor (`SS13.register_signal`) crashed the test server once, in `lua_state.dm` `load_script`. This is a side finding and was not investigated.
