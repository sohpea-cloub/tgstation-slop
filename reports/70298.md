# #70298: Borgs able to use disabled modules

| | |
|---|---|
| Issues | [#70298](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/70298): not reproduced |
| Status | Not reproduced; no code defect found |
| Fix branch | none |
| Tested on | tgstation master `87b93338`, 2026-10-23 |

## Summary
The report says cyborgs whose modules were disabled by damage can keep using the active module, for example to weld themselves back up. On master, disabled modules are removed from the borg's hands and cannot be re-activated. What the reporter most likely saw is intended behaviour: slot 1 stays usable until health drops to -50%, and it comes back as soon as health rises above that line, so a borg that gets any heal can re-equip its welder and self-weld. No fix was made.

## The bug
The reporter watched borgs take enough damage to lose all their modules, run away, and then weld themselves to full health. They concluded that the active module was not being removed from the borg's "hands". A commenter replied that disabled modules have always been usable for a moment before the disable check removes them, but that this should not be long enough to self-weld. They suggested a passive healing upgrade or an upgraded recharger as the more likely explanation.

## Reproduction
Not reproduced. What was checked, on master:

1. Using the Lua editor as an observer, a cyborg was spawned and given the engineering model. The welding tool (slot 1), extinguisher (slot 2) and flash (slot 3) were activated.
2. Damage was applied with `adjust_brute_loss` / `take_overall_damage`, and after each step `held_items`, `disabled_modules` and `module_active` were read.

Observed:

| Health | Result |
|---|---|
| 100 | three modules held, `disabled_modules` = 0 |
| 40 (50% or less) | flash in slot 3 removed, flag = 1 (slot three disabled) |
| -5 (0% or less) | extinguisher in slot 2 removed, flags = 3; welder in slot 1 still held |
| -55 (-50% or less) | all three `held_items` empty, flags = 7, `module_active` null; `activate_module(welder)` refused with "All modules are disabled!" and `held_items` stayed empty |
| healed to -25 | the "all disabled" flag cleared; the welder could be activated again (flags = 3, one usable slot) |

A single hit of 110 damage with the extinguisher selected ended at -10 health, with slots 2 and 3 emptied, `module_active` null and the welder still held in slot 1. This matches the thresholds.

All of this was done by calling procs from Lua; the actual player actions (clicking module slots, using the welder on self) were not exercised.

## Cause
There is no defect that explains the report. The relevant code:

- `/mob/living/silicon/robot/updatehealth()` (`code/modules/mob/living/silicon/robot/robot.dm:618-646`). When health drops, slot 3 breaks at 50% or less of max health, slot 2 at 0% or less and slot 1 at -50% or less. When health rises, slot 3 is repaired at 50% or more, slot 2 at 0% or more and slot 1 at -50% or more.
- `break_cyborg_slot` (`code/modules/mob/living/silicon/robot/inventory.dm`, around lines 84-130) unequips the held item and sets the matching `disabled_modules` flag.
- `activate_module` (`inventory.dm`, around line 33) refuses when the all-disabled flag is set, and `is_invalid_module_number` (around line 226) blocks the other disabled slots.

So a borg between -50% and 0% health legitimately has one working module in slot 1. A borg that is healed to anything at or above -50% (by a borg recharger, a repair upgrade or another player) gets slot 1 back at once and can self-weld. This is what the commenter suspected. The only oddity is that there is no hysteresis: slot 1 breaks and is repaired at the same -50% line.

## Fix
None. No commit.

## Verification
Not applicable, since nothing was changed. The behaviour above was checked in game on master through the Lua editor. Not checked: real combat damage against a player-controlled borg, a recharger or repair upgrade actually healing the borg, and the module-slot click handlers.

## Notes
- If maintainers consider "slot 1 returns the moment health crosses -50%" undesirable, the change would be to add hysteresis (a higher repair threshold than break threshold). That would be a design change, not a bug fix.
- Minor unrelated observation: `doUnEquip` in `inventory.dm` uses `get_selected_module()` (the active slot) when deselecting, so breaking slot 3 while slot 1 or 2 is selected deselects the active module. It only deselects, and it did not affect these results.
- The issue is likely closable as working as intended.
