## Summary

Of the five issues grouped as "fireman carry quirks", four are about elevation offsets (a carried or spawned mob floating too high) or a leftover offhand after teleporting, and none of them could be reproduced on current master. The elevation code now stores offsets per source, with a guard against applying the same source twice, which the 2023 and 2024 reports predate. #48608 was a real gap: only the carrier got "riding offhand" items, so the carried mob kept both hands free. The fix gives the carried mob two offhands, like a piggyback rider, and refuses the carry if its hands are full. That refusal is a design decision for maintainers.

## #80203: mob placed on a table after a fireman carry floats higher

### The bug (as reported)
After fireman carrying someone and placing them on a table, they float noticeably higher. Grabbing them again fixes the height, and carrying them off the table leaves them at the carrier's feet until the carrier turns or moves.

### Reproduction attempted
On master, using the Lua editor and a real click:
1. Using Lua, spawn two humans. Set the victim lying (`set_body_position(1)`), buckle the victim to the carrier fireman-style (`buckle_mob(victim, true, true, 16)` with `CARRIER_NEEDS_ARM`), and place a table on the adjacent tile.
2. Take control of the carrier and click the table with the offhand active. The game printed that the carrier "begins to place" the victim onto the table and, 3.5 seconds later, "places" them there.
3. Read the victim's `pixel_z` and `pixel_y` and offsets.

Evidence: the victim on the table had `pixel_z` 12 and `pixel_y` -6, and was unbuckled. A normal lying mob on a table has the same values. While carried on the floor the victim had `pixel_z` 12 / `pixel_y` -6 as well (6 from riding and 6 from lying), and after being placed on the table the values were again 12 / -6. Three carry-and-place cycles gave identical results, and carrying the victim back off the table gave the same values as carrying from the floor. No double elevation appeared.

### Why it could not be reproduced
`/datum/element/elevation_core/proc/elevate_mob()` (`code/datums/elements/elevation.dm:187`) keys each offset by source (`ELEVATION_SOURCE(src)`) and returns early if the mob already has that offset (`has_offset`, line 192). Offsets are removed again with `remove_offsets(ELEVATION_SOURCE(src))`. This makes a double application from the same table impossible, and the issue's observations (2023) predate that offsets rework. The tester's reading is that the bug was fixed by that rework; it was not tied to a specific upstream pull request.

Not examined: the "fireman's dribble" mentioned in the comments (the carried mob dipping when the carrier turns and steps).

## #81732: table elevation stacking across z-levels

Reported: entering a table or transit tube from another z-level repeatedly stacks height and the mob keeps the extra height when leaving.

Tried: with Lua, a human was `forceMove`d from a location on another z-level onto a table (so the old location is not adjacent), four times in a row, then off. Result: `pixel_z` 12 on the table, 0 when off, and offsets back to empty. A real walk through a z-level edge was not tried, so this only covers the code path of entering from a non-adjacent location, not the real transit.

## #83536: spawned on a bed, pixel shift is never removed

Reported: spawning on the lavaland syndicate role's bed leaves the mob pixel-shifted up, and it stacks when stepping on again.

Tried: a human spawned on an `/obj/structure/bed` (elevation 8) had `pixel_z` 8. `forceMove` off and on three times alternated 0 and 8. The real `lavaland_syndicate` spawner `create()` on a bed tile followed by moving one tile south left no offsets. Result: not reproduced. The testers' notes attribute the fix to upstream pull request #85952 ("fixes elevation being permanent if you spawn on it"); that attribution comes from a pull request search and was not checked in the code.

## #62343: fireman carry into a teleporting portal leaves a phantom offhand

Reported: after carrying someone into an arena shuttle portal the victim was teleported elsewhere, but the carrier's offhand stayed occupied as if still carrying.

Tried: the carrier `forceMove`d, the victim `forceMove`d, `teleport` through a portal on either mob, and the real arena shuttle forcefield (`/obj/effect/forcefield/arena_shuttle_entrance`, `code/game/area/areas/shuttles.dm:307-322`, which is a plain `forceMove`) by walking into it as the carrier. In each case the victim was unbuckled and the carrier's offhand was deleted (the arena's replacement chainsaw hand took its place). A `riding_offhand` is removed through `Destroy` (`code/datums/elements/ridable.dm:179-184`) and the unbuckle paths whenever the victim stops being buckled, so this works today. Result: not reproduced.

## #48608: a fireman-carried mob can still use its hands

### The bug
A mob piggybacking on someone has both hands blocked, so it cannot act. A mob that is fireman carried can still use both hands, for example to shoot a gun or use items, which the thread calls an oversight (and a power-gaming trick).

### Cause
`/datum/element/ridable/proc/check_mounting()` (`code/datums/elements/ridable.dm:44`) gives hand items out in two places. For `RIDER_NEEDS_ARMS` (piggyback) the rider gets two `riding_offhand` items (line 60). For `CARRIER_NEEDS_ARM` (fireman carry) only the carrier gets one (line 74, `equip_buckle_inhands(target_living, 1, target_living, potential_rider)`); the carried mob gets none. The `TRAIT_HANDS_BLOCKED` trait would not have helped, because it is not enforced when clicking.

### Reproduction
This was established by reading the code. The pre-fix state was not captured separately in game: the post-fix checks below show the carried mob's hands filled, and the code shows no offhand being created for it on master.

### Fix
One commit, **Fix issue 48608: fireman carried mobs have their hands occupied**, adds 8 lines to `check_mounting()` in `ridable.dm`. After the carrier's offhand is created, a carbon carried mob also gets two riding offhands (`equip_buckle_inhands(potential_rider, 2, target_living)`). If that fails, both sides' offhands are cleaned up and the buckle is blocked with "can't get a grip on X because their hands are full!", the same behaviour as a piggyback rider. Dropping or stripping the victim's offhand unbuckles them, as for a piggyback rider.

### Verification
After rebuilding and checking in the test server, with the Lua editor and real actions:
- Carrying an empty-handed victim: the victim held two offhand items and the carrier one.
- The victim dropping an offhand: the victim was unbuckled and both hands were empty on both mobs.
- A victim holding a crowbar: the carry was refused (`buckle_mob` returned 0, the chat message appeared, no stray offhands). After dropping the crowbar the carry worked.
- Unbuckling cleared all offhands.
- The real table-place click still worked, with `pixel_z` 12 and the offhand overlays gone afterwards.
- No runtimes from the changed code (apart from one caused by misusing `do_teleport` in a Lua test).

Not checked: DM unit tests, and carrying cyborgs or other non-carbon mobs (the new branch applies to carbon mobs only).

### Notes
- Design tradeoff: a victim with an item in either hand now blocks being fireman carried until the item is removed, as with piggyback. A maintainer could instead make the carry proceed with only the free hands filled, but then a held gun stays usable, which defeats the purpose of the change.
- The thread suggested adding `TRAIT_IMMOBILIZED`; this was not tried, since mob hands and clicks are what matter here.
