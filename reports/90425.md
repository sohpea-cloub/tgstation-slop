## Summary

Three gaps in admin full heal (aheal, `revive(ADMIN_HEAL_ALL)`), all reproduced on master. Scarred eyes (for example from a shot to the eye) stayed scarred because the heal neither replaces same-type eyes nor touches the scar bits. Psykers got the eyes back that `psykerize()` deletes. An aghosted admin was pulled back into their body when it was healed. Each is fixed by a small separate commit.

## The bug

- #90425: after eye scarring, aheal left the person nearsighted or blind, with reduced eye max health.
- #71722: aheal on a psyker gave them eyes, although psykers are meant to have none.
- #80830: aheal on a body whose owner had used Aghost forced the owner back into that body. Defibrillators skip such bodies.

## Reproduction

All three were done with the Lua editor on a spawned human, calling procs directly rather than through the Admin Heal verb.

**#90425**
1. Spawn a human and call `apply_scar` on its eyes (sides 1 and 2, i.e. both eyes).
2. Observed: `scarring` 3, eyes `maxHealth` 20, person nearsighted.
3. Call `revive(ADMIN_HEAL_ALL)` on the human.
4. Observed on master: `scarring` still 3, `maxHealth` still 20, the same eyes object (not replaced), still nearsighted.

A human whose eyes had been deleted did get eyes back from aheal, so missing eyes were handled and scarred ones were not.

**#71722**
1. Spawn a human and call `psykerize()`.
2. Observed: 8 organs, no eyes, psyker brain and head.
3. Call `revive(ADMIN_HEAL_ALL)`.
4. Observed on master: 9 organs, eyes back.

**#80830**
1. Possess a newly spawned human, then use the Aghost verb.
2. Call `revive(ADMIN_HEAL_ALL)` on the abandoned body.
3. Observed on master: the client's mob switched back to the body (its key had been `@<ckey>`, the admin-ghost fake key).

## Cause

- #90425: `regenerate_organs` (`code/modules/surgery/organs/_organ.dm:347`) delegates to the species, which only replaces an organ when `get_replaceability` (`_organ.dm:445`) allows it; same-type eyes are never replaced. The remaining step, `organ.set_organ_damage(0)` (`_organ.dm:357`), resets organ damage but not `scarring` (`code/modules/surgery/organs/internal/eyes/_eyes.dm:57`) or the max-health penalty from `apply_scarring_effects`.
- #71722: `psykerize()` (`code/modules/religion/burdened/psyker.dm:87`) simply deletes the eyes. The species `regenerate_organs` sees an empty eye slot and fills it again. The 2022 comment in the issue (that healing makes new copies of organs) no longer applies to the brain: `get_replaceability` keeps non-species organs, and the psyker brain stayed.
- #80830: `revive()` (`code/modules/mob/living/living.dm:887`) calls `grab_ghost(force_grab_ghost)` (line 896), and `/mob/proc/grab_ghost` (`code/modules/mob/mob.dm:1016`) forwarded to `mind.grab_ghost` with no admin-ghost check. Defibrillation does check this (`DEFIB_NOGRAB_AGHOST`, `code/modules/mob/living/carbon/carbon.dm:728`).

## Fix

Three commits:

- "Fix issue 90425: admin heal clears eye scarring": in `regenerate_organs`, when `remove_hazardous` is set (the `HEAL_REFRESH_ORGANS` path), eyes get `fix_scar(RIGHT_EYE_SCAR)` and `fix_scar(LEFT_EYE_SCAR)` before `set_organ_damage(0)`. This was chosen over replacing the eyes so the organ object and any other state are kept.
- "Fix issue 71722: aheal no longer gives psykers their eyes back": adds `/obj/item/organ/eyes/get_availability`, which returns FALSE when the owner's head is a `/obj/item/bodypart/head/psyker`, so regeneration does not consider the eyes missing.
- "Fix issue 80830: reviving does not grab admin ghosts": `/mob/proc/grab_ghost` returns early when `!force && IS_FAKE_KEY(key)`, mirroring the defibrillator rule. `revive(..., force_grab_ghost = TRUE)` still grabs.

## Verification

In game on the test server, by Lua before and after rebuilding:

- #90425: after the fix, `revive(ADMIN_HEAL_ALL)` gave `scarring` 0, `maxHealth` 50, and no nearsightedness. A human with deleted eyes still got eyes back.
- #71722: after the fix, a healed psyker still had 8 organs, no eyes, and kept the psyker brain.
- #80830: after the fix, the client stayed as the ghost after the heal, and `revive(..., force_grab_ghost = TRUE)` still pulled it back.
- No new runtimes were logged beyond one the tester caused with a typo in their own Lua and one boot-time spritesheet runtime.

Not checked: shooting an eye in a real Russian roulette scenario, the Admin Heal verb itself (the proc was called directly), other healing sources, and non-human species.

## Notes

- The eye fix keys on `HEAL_REFRESH_ORGANS`, so any non-admin full heal using that flag (`HEAL_ALL`) also clears eye scars. Maintainers may or may not want that.
- #80830 is a design call: in the issue thread optimumtact said aheal is supposed to grab people regardless, while MrMelbert asked for parity with defibrillators. The commit follows the parity request and can be dropped independently of the other two.
- The `get_availability` override in the psyker fix is tied to the psyker head type. Another approach would be to stop `psykerize()` from relying on eyes being missing, for example with a trait or organ flag; this was not tried.
