# #96690: Airlock pump leaves its airlocks bolted open (or bolted shut) around a shuttle dock/undock

## Summary

Both issues involve the airlock cycling pump (`/obj/machinery/atmospherics/components/unary/airlock_pump`) misbehaving around a docked shuttle, and both end with an airlock that can no longer be used. #96690 is a race: if the shuttle undocks while the pump is still opening the airlocks for the dock, the "close" request is ignored because the doors are mid-animation, and the opening sequence then re-bolts them open, so both doors stay open to space and nothing can be cycled. #98096 is a separate bug: after docking, the pump compares the pressure of the wrong tile (the shuttle door's own tile, which keeps stale air while the door is closed) and can keep the external airlock bolted shut forever. One commit fixes both, in `airlock_pump.dm`.

## The bug

The reports are short. #96690 says: "If a shuttle un/docks, and then you cause a cycling airlock to cycle, the airlock is bricked perma and won't cycle on request or shuttle re/un/dock". #98096 says that, on the mining shuttle, after coming back from Lavaland and being told to repressurise the walkway, the airlock to the shuttle sometimes "will bolt shut and refuse to open to you again" (the reporter's steps: go to Lavaland and back, repressurise the walkway, try to re-enter the shuttle).

What was found:
- In the #96690 case, the station-side airlock pair ends up with both doors open and bolted, the shuttle gone, and the chamber venting to space. Since both doors are bolted, clicking them does nothing.
- In the #98096 case, the external (shuttle-facing) airlock stays bolted closed after docking and every click on it is denied, so a miner cannot get back into the shuttle.

## Reproduction

Reproduced on MetaStation with the mining shuttle and the airlock pair at the mining dock, using the Lua editor to move the shuttle (`SSshuttle.moveShuttle("mining", "mining_home" / "mining_away")`), to click the external airlock, and to log the state of the doors and the pump every fraction of a second.

Normal docking, undocking and clicking sequences worked. A random fuzz of dock/undock/click timings did not brick anything by itself. The specific timings below did.

### #96690 (race)

1. Dock the mining shuttle at the station (`mining_home`).
2. About 0.6 seconds later, undock it (`mining_away`), while the pump is still opening the doors for the dock.
3. Observed (door states: U = unbolted, L = bolted, O = open, C = closed; `dsp` is the pump's `docked_side_pressure`):
   ```
   0.3  EXT uC  INT uO  anim1  dsp101
   0.6  EXT LC* (undock tries to close the external door)
   1.0  EXT LO  INT LO  dspnil
   ```
   Both doors ended up open and bolted, the shuttle was gone, and the chamber vented to space (pressure at the pump went from 101 to 42 kPa within 12 seconds). Nothing was left to click, so the airlock could not be cycled.

### #98096 (stale air)

1. Send the mining shuttle away.
2. Remove 70% of the air from the shuttle's door tile (`remove_ratio(0.7)` on its air). This simulates air left in that tile when the door last closed at Lavaland. The natural way this happens was only partly tested: opening the shuttle door at Lavaland for 8 seconds produced a docked-side pressure of 96 kPa and no brick, so the drain is a stand-in for the real scenario.
3. Dock the shuttle (`mining_home`).
4. Observed: chamber and docked-side pressure both about 101 kPa, but the external airlock stayed bolted closed after docking, and every click on it was denied (the log was stable for 30 seconds). This matches "bolts the miner out of the shuttle".

## Cause

Two defects in `code/modules/atmospherics/machinery/components/unary_devices/airlock_pump.dm`.

**1. Undock closes doors that are still animating (#96690).** When a shuttle leaves, `/obj/machinery/door/airlock/beforeShuttleMove` calls the pump's `undock()` (`code/modules/shuttle/mobile_port/shuttle_move_callbacks.dm:190-195`; `undock()` is at `airlock_pump.dm:456`). `undock()` immediately runs `secure_close(TRUE)` on the external airlocks (`airlock_pump.dm:468`). `secure_close` (`code/game/machinery/airlock_control.dm:29`) unbolts the door, calls `close()`, then bolts it. But `/obj/machinery/door/airlock/close()` returns FALSE while the door is operating (`code/game/machinery/doors/airlock.dm:1411`). If `safe_dock()` is in the middle of `secure_open()` on that door (`airlock_control.dm:18`), the close does nothing, then the open finishes and sets `locked = TRUE`, leaving the door open and bolted. The internal airlock is also open from the same dock sequence, so the whole pair is open to the void and bolted. `undock()` had already set `docked_side_pressure` to null, so nothing later reopens or cycles it.

**2. The pressure check samples the wrong tile (#98096).** When a shuttle docks, `/obj/machinery/door/airlock/afterShuttleMove` samples the pressure on the tile behind the shuttle's door (`shuttle_move_callbacks.dm:216`, `get_step(src, REVERSE_DIR(pump.dir))`) and passes it to `on_dock_request()` as `docked_side_pressure`. But `safe_dock()` (`airlock_pump.dm:431`) and `airlock_act()` (`airlock_pump.dm:271`) then compare that value with the pressure of `get_step(airlock, REVERSE_DIR(dir))`, which is the shuttle door's own tile. While the shuttle door is closed, the air on that tile is whatever was left there when the door last closed and can differ from the shuttle interior. If the difference exceeds 10 times `allowed_pressure_error`, `safe_dock()` skips the external airlock (leaving it bolted) and `airlock_act()` denies every click. It never recovers because nothing changes that tile's air.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 96690: airlock pump no longer leaves its airlocks bolted open or bolted shut around a shuttle un/dock", touching only `airlock_pump.dm`:

- `undock()` now waits, up to 30 times 0.2 seconds, until `airlocks_animating` is false and none of the linked airlocks is operating (new helper `are_airlocks_operating()`), and only then bolts the external airlocks shut. After the wait it returns without doing anything if the shuttle docked again in the meantime (that new sequence owns the airlocks) or if power was lost.
- `on_dock_request()` returns early if `docked_side_pressure` is already null after its own queued wait, i.e. if the shuttle has already left again.
- New proc `get_docked_side_turf(airlock)` returns the tile on the docked vessel's side of an external airlock, skipping over a closed airlock on that tile to the tile behind it (as long as that tile is not a closed turf). `safe_dock()` and `airlock_act()` use it so they compare against the same tile that `afterShuttleMove` sampled.

Why this approach: waiting for the animations is the smallest change that preserves the existing "bolt the externals shut on undock" behaviour; the alternatives (forcing the close, or having `safe_dock()` check for undock after opening) would have needed changes in the generic airlock code or in the door animation timing. The 30 x 0.2 second limit bounds the wait so a stuck door cannot hang the proc forever. For the pressure check, sampling the same tile in both places was chosen over changing `afterShuttleMove`, which also serves the cases for escape pods and CentCom blast doors.

## Verification

On the test server, with the same Lua timeline logger:

- #96690: repeated the dock then undock race with the undock at 0.4, 0.6, 0.8 and 1.0 seconds after docking. Every time the end state was `EXT LC, INT LO` (external bolted shut, internal open, which is the normal state) with the pump at 101 kPa.
- #98096: repeated the stale-air scenario (shuttle away, drain the door tile, shuttle home). The external airlock now unbolted and opened (about 4 seconds after docking), the chamber dipped to about 94 kPa and then equalised.
- The runtime log showed no runtimes (only unrelated garbage-collection noise from vacated lighting objects).

Not checked:
- The stale-air state was produced by draining the tile, not by the natural sequence of opening and closing the shuttle door at Lavaland (an 8 second open did not reproduce it).
- Only the mining shuttle on MetaStation was tested. Other cycling airlock pairs (other docks, escape pods, the evac shuttle's CentCom blast doors) were not exercised after the change.
- The issue reporters' exact sequences are not known (#96690 gives no steps; #98096's report is from a live round), so it is not certain that these two causes are everything that produced the reports.
- A power loss in the middle of the new wait loop was not tested.

## Notes

- #98096 was grouped with #96690 as a likely duplicate, but the two failures have different mechanisms. Only the first is a race. The same pump and shuttle-dock code is involved, which is why one commit fixes both.
- `get_docked_side_turf()` only steps over one airlock and only one tile. A different dock layout with something other than a single airlock tile between the pump and the docked vessel's interior would not be handled.
- `undock()` now sleeps for up to 6 seconds; it is already called asynchronously (`INVOKE_ASYNC`) from `beforeShuttleMove`, so it should not delay the shuttle move. This was not measured.
