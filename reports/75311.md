## Summary
The 2023 report said ethereal blood (Liquid Electricity) carried no virus data: the P.A.N.D.E.M.I.C. showed nothing for it and it could not infect a monkey. On current master this works. Ethereal and slime-person blood both showed their viruses in the P.A.N.D.E.M.I.C. and infected a monkey when injected, the same as human blood. The issue appears to have been fixed by the later blood-type rework; the exact PR could not be identified, because the available git history is shallow. The issue looks closable.

## The bug
As reported: an ethereal with a disease could not have it analysed, since the P.A.N.D.E.M.I.C. showed nothing for their blood, and the blood could not pass the disease on. A 2024 comment says the same for slime-person blood.

## Reproduction
Not reproduced. On the test server (Station z-level), using the Lua editor:
1. Spawned a human, set its species to ethereal, and gave it the flu with `ForceContractDisease`. The same was done for a slime person (`/datum/species/jelly/slime`) and for a plain human as the control.
2. Drew 20 units of blood into a beaker with `transfer_blood_to`. For the ethereal, the blood reagent was `/datum/reagent/consumable/liquidelectricity`, with its blood type data set.
3. Put the beaker in a `/obj/machinery/computer/pandemic` and called its `ui_data`. In all three cases `has_blood` was 1 and the `viruses` list was non-empty (2 entries each, same as the human control).
4. Injected 5 units from each beaker into a fresh monkey (`trans_to` with `methods = INJECT`). The monkey caught the flu in all three cases.

Output: `human has_blood=1 viruses=2 monkey_injected=1`, with identical lines for ethereal and slime-person sources.

## Cause
Why it works now, in the master code:
- `get_blood_data()` (`code/modules/mob/living/blood.dm:433`) stores viruses and resistances for any blood type whose `blood_flags` include `BLOOD_TRANSFER_VIRAL_DATA` (`blood.dm:449`). The default flags (`code/modules/mob/living/blood_types.dm:21`) include it, and ethereal and slime blood use the default.
- The `blood_reagent` element (`code/datums/elements/blood_reagent.dm:41-46`) copies that data, including the blood type that the P.A.N.D.E.M.I.C. requires (`get_blood_reagent()`, `code/modules/reagents/chemistry/machinery/pandemic.dm:219`), onto any blood reagent, Liquid Electricity included. Infection on exposure is handled in the same element.
- The only blood types without the flag are xeno (`blood_types.dm:294`) and oil (`blood_types.dm:328`), which looks intentional.

## Fix
None needed.

## Verification
Only the checks above. Not checked: the old behaviour on an older commit, so the claim that the refactor fixed it rests on the code, not on a before/after test; infection by routes other than injection.

## Notes
- Transferring blood directly from a mob into a mob of a different blood type (for example ethereal to monkey) takes the plain `add_reagent` path (`blood.dm:395-398`) with no expose step, so no infection occurs there. This applies to every mismatched blood type, not just Liquid Electricity, and was left unchanged.
- The slime-person comment in the issue is covered by the same result.
