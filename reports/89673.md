## Summary

A player with the Telekinesis mutation (and possibly Hulk) could not pick up or interact with anything at range in one round in February 2025. On current master, telekinesis worked in every case tried, including with Hulk. The report is probably fixed by later refactors or specific to that round; the causes could not be confirmed.

## The bug

The reporter had telekinesis and probably the Hulk mutation and could not grab or interact with anything at range, while normal interactions worked. They noted it worked in other rounds. A commenter later reported TK not moving chairs or items. The issue gives no way to trigger it.

## Reproduction

Not reproduced. On the test server:

1. With the Lua editor, spawn a human, possess it, and add the Telekinesis and Hulk mutations with `dna:add_mutation(path, 'mutator')` (the source argument is required; without it `dna.dm:116` crashes).
2. Put a crowbar on the floor four tiles away and click it. "Telekinetic Grab" appeared in the hand: it worked with combat mode off and on, with Hulk.
3. Switch on throw mode and click a turf: the held crowbar flew three tiles.
4. Click an anchored chair: it rotated (expected, `attack_tk` on chairs), no grab (expected).
5. Put the human in crit and fully heal. Both mutations were still present afterwards.

## Cause

None found. Relevant code: `code/datums/mutations/reach.dm:27` (`on_ranged_attack`, registered for `COMSIG_MOB_ATTACK_RANGED` at line 18) calls into `code/_onclick/telekinesis.dm`. The click path calls `RangedAttack` only when the active hand is empty; with an item in hand it goes to the ranged item interaction instead (so no TK with a held item, by design).

The report predates two changes that may have fixed it: the mutation-sources refactor (2025-06-08) and the attack-chain cleanup (PR #90080, 2025-04-23). This is a guess; it was not bisected. A related issue, #94882 ("TK unable to move objects"), was closed 2026-01-18 and its reporter later said it worked via throw; it was called a possible duplicate of this one.

## Fix

None.

## Verification

Nothing to verify. Not tested: telekinesis obtained through a DNA injector or genetics (`dna_injector.dm:71`), a species change, TK on doors, machines or computers, multi-z, and a Hulk dying in crit (`hulk.dm:70-74` calls `on_losing` and may leave the mutation in `dna.sources`).
