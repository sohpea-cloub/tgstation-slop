# #33559: Planted C4 / X-4 charges stay behind when the shuttle moves

| | |
|---|---|
| Issues | [#33559](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/33559): verified, fixed |
| Status | Fix committed and checked on the test server |
| Fix branch | [fix/33559](https://github.com/sohpea-cloub/tgstation-slop/tree/fix/33559) |

## Summary

A C4 or X-4 charge planted on a shuttle wall does not travel with the shuttle. When a shuttle moves, its turfs are copied to the destination and the old turfs are scraped away, but the charge keeps pointing at the old turf and keeps its overlay there. It then explodes (or would) at the place the shuttle left, and the shuttle's wall at the destination shows no charge. The fix makes a planted charge listen for the turf's shuttle-move signal and move its target and overlay to the new turf.

## The bug

The report (2017, confirmed again in 2022 and 2023) says that planting an X-4 on the emergency shuttle's walls with a timer meant to go off after arrival at CentCom makes the charge disappear. A 2023 comment states that the charge is left behind on the x, y, z coordinates of the wall it was attached to. A 2017 comment from a maintainer noted that this is not really a shuttle bug but a turf-copy bug that does not carry over the data X-4 needs. The same code serves C4, so C4 is affected too.

## Reproduction

On the test server, using the Lua editor, on master:

1. Docked the emergency shuttle at the station (`SSshuttle.moveShuttle('emergency', 'emergency_home', FALSE)`) and picked one of its titanium walls.
2. Made an `/obj/item/grenade/c4/x4` and set it up as if planted: `target` set to the wall, `active = TRUE`, the charge's `plastic_overlay` added to the wall, and the charge moved to nullspace as the real plant code does. This step was emulated from Lua and did not use the player planting action.
3. Moved the shuttle to CentCom (`moveShuttle('emergency', 'emergency_away', FALSE)`).
4. Read the charge's `target`. It was a `/turf/open/space` turf at 89,45 on the station z-level, the coordinates the shuttle wall had left. None of the shuttle's walls at CentCom had an overlay. Detonating the charge would have exploded at that station location, not on the shuttle.

## Cause

`/turf/proc/onShuttleMove()` (`code/modules/shuttle/mobile_port/shuttle_move_callbacks.dm:49-68`) copies the shuttle turf onto the destination turf with `CopyOnTop()` and sends `COMSIG_TURF_ON_SHUTTLE_MOVE` with the new turf. `afterShuttleMove()` (line 71) then calls `oldT.ScrapeAway()` on the turf that was left behind (line 79).

`/obj/item/grenade/c4/proc/plant_c4()` (`code/game/objects/items/grenades/plastic.dm:113-155`) stores the planted-on atom in `target` (line 124) and adds the charge's overlay to it (line 152). Nothing updates either when that turf is replaced by a copy. So `target` still refers to the abandoned turf, and the overlay was added to that turf, not to the copy at the destination.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 33559: planted C4/X4 follows its turf when a shuttle moves", in `code/game/objects/items/grenades/plastic.dm`:

- In `plant_c4()`, after adding the overlay, if `target` is a turf the charge registers `COMSIG_TURF_ON_SHUTTLE_MOVE` on it.
- A new handler `on_target_shuttle_move(turf/source, turf/new_turf)` ignores in-place moves (`new_turf == source`), removes the overlay from the old turf, unregisters from it, sets `target = new_turf`, registers on the new turf (so later moves are also followed), and adds the overlay to the new turf.
- `Destroy()` unregisters the signal when the target is a turf.

This uses the signal that `onShuttleMove()` already sends, so no change to the shuttle movement code was needed. The inputs do not record any alternative that was tried (for example, copying overlays in the turf-copy code itself).

## Verification

On the rebuilt test server:

1. Docked the emergency shuttle at the station and spawned a human on the shuttle's floor next to a wall, possessed it, and gave it an X-4 charge with a long timer.
2. Used the charge on the wall (the real plant action; "You plant the bomb" was shown). The charge's target was the titanium wall and it had its overlay.
3. Moved the shuttle to CentCom. The charge's `target` was now the shuttle's titanium wall (`/turf/closed/wall/mineral/titanium`, area "Emergency Shuttle") at 211,66 on the CentCom z-level, with the overlay present on it.
4. Called `detonate()` on the charge directly. It produced an explosion and fire at that CentCom location, on the shuttle. No runtimes mentioning the plastic explosive code were logged.

Not checked:
- The timer expiring on its own after the shuttle arrived; `detonate()` was called by hand.
- C4 as opposed to X-4 (both use the same code), and X-4 directional behaviour after the move.
- More than one shuttle move with the same charge planted, and shuttle rotation in place.
- Charges planted on shuttle floors or other turfs, or on items and mobs (those are not turfs and are not affected by the change).
- Other turf replacements under a planted charge that are unrelated to shuttles.

## Notes

- The fix covers only the shuttle case. A planted charge also loses its target in other situations where a turf is replaced under it, and those were not examined.
- The issue title says "arriving at CCom", but the code path is the same for any shuttle move that goes through `onShuttleMove()`.
