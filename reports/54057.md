## Summary

The status MediHUD icon of a dead human is chosen by `/mob/living/carbon/med_hud_set_status()`, which shows the "can be defibbed" icon (`huddefib`, the wavy line) while the body's ghost is in `GLOB.player_list`. Nothing refreshed the icon when that ghost's client left, so a body whose player had disconnected kept the wavy line while its examine text already said "soul has departed". The fix refreshes the former body's MediHUD from the observer's `Logout()` and `Login()`.

## The bug

The issue reports that examining a dead, soulless body correctly says its soul has departed, but the MediHUD still shows the wavy line instead of the skull, until something forces a refresh (for example, hitting the body). Two commenters in 2022 and 2024 confirmed that it still happened.

What happens is that the HUD icon is only recomputed when something calls `med_hud_set_status()` on the body. The examine text, by contrast, is computed each time it is read, so the two disagree in between.

## Reproduction

On master, with admin tools (the Lua editor was used):

1. Start the round and open the Lua editor.
2. Create a human and give it your ckey, then, in a later run (doing it in the same run as the possession fails with a `WrapAdminProcCall` runtime because the usr changes), call `death()` on it and `ghostize()` it. The body's status HUD (`hud_list['2'].icon_state`) read `huddefib`, as expected while its ghost has a client.
3. Take the ghost out of the player list, which is what `/mob/Logout()` does when a client leaves: call `remove_from_player_list()` on the ghost.
4. Read the body's HUD icon and `can_defib_client()`.

Observed on master:

- HUD icon: `huddefib` (stale).
- `can_defib_client()`: `nil`.
- After calling `med_hud_set_status()` on the body by hand: `huddead` (the correct icon). Putting the ghost back in the list and refreshing again gave `huddefib`.

This reproduces the stale state by calling the player-list removal directly. It was not reproduced with a real client disconnect, because the server stops game time while no client is connected (see Verification). Calling `Logout()` on the ghost from Lua was also tried and was unusable: it hit a runtime in `update_z` and disconnected the client.

## Cause

`/mob/living/carbon/med_hud_set_status()` (`code/game/data_huds.dm:187`) picks the icon for dead bodies (`code/game/data_huds.dm:192-199`):

```dm
if(IS_DEAD_OR_FAKING(src))
	if(can_defib_client())
		set_hud_image_state(STATUS_HUD, hud_state = "huddefib")
	else if(HAS_TRAIT(src, TRAIT_GHOSTROLE_ON_REVIVE))
		set_hud_image_state(STATUS_HUD, hud_state = "hudghost")
	else
		set_hud_image_state(STATUS_HUD, hud_state = "huddead")
```

`can_defib_client()` (`code/modules/mob/living/carbon/carbon.dm:772`) is true if the body has a client or `get_ghost(FALSE, TRUE)` finds one. With `ghosts_with_clients` set, `/datum/mind/proc/get_ghost` (`code/datums/mind/_mind.dm:460`) searches `GLOB.player_list`, which only holds mobs that have clients.

When a ghost's client disconnects, `/mob/Logout()` (`code/modules/mob/logout.dm`) calls `remove_from_player_list()`. The ghost keeps its key and mind, so it is still the body's ghost, but `can_defib_client()` now returns false. `/mob/dead/observer/Logout()` (`code/modules/mob/dead/observer/logout.dm`) did nothing about the body's HUD, so the icon stayed at `huddefib`.

The examine text for the same body is built on demand (`code/modules/mob/living/carbon/examine.dm:339-344`): with no valid ghost client it ends with "and [t_his] soul has departed...". Hitting the body calls `med_hud_set_status()`, which is why that refreshes the icon.

Other cases already refresh the HUD: the ghost being destroyed (`code/modules/mob/dead/observer/observer.dm:169`), ghosting out of the body (`observer.dm:302`) and choosing do-not-resuscitate (`observer.dm:403`). Only the disconnect (and the matching reconnect) was missing.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 54057: refresh a dead body's MediHUD when its ghost logs out or back in", changes two files:

- `code/modules/mob/dead/observer/logout.dm`: after `..()` (which removes the ghost from the player list), if `mind?.current` is a living mob, call its `med_hud_set_status()`.
- `code/modules/mob/dead/observer/login.dm`: at the end of `Login()`, do the same, so the icon goes back to `huddefib` when the player returns.

The refresh is placed after `..()` in `Logout()` on purpose, so the ghost is already out of `GLOB.player_list` when the status is recomputed. The change reuses the existing proc and the existing refresh points; no new state is stored.

## Verification

Checked in game:

- The fixed build compiled with 0 errors and ran, with no new runtimes from the change.
- The `Login` half: with a dead body and its ghost controlled by the test client, the client was disconnected and reconnected. The game log showed the ghost's `Logout` and then `Login`, and afterwards the body's HUD read `huddefib`, which is correct for a ghost with a client.

Not checked:

- The `Logout` half with a real disconnect. The expected result is `huddead` while the ghost is offline. This could not be observed because the Master controller sets `world.sleep_offline = TRUE` after initialization (`code/controllers/master.dm:472-473`), so with the only client gone the world's time stops, and a timer meant to sample the HUD while disconnected only ran after reconnecting. One sample taken around the reconnect read `hudhealthy` with `can_defib_client()` at 1; it was probably taken at the reconnect boundary, but this was not explained. A second client would be needed to look at the HUD while the ghost is offline.
- The fix was not tested on a body whose ghost was already unable to re-enter or on other mob types.
- The stale `huddefib` icon, the `huddead` after a manual refresh and the `nil` result of `can_defib_client()` come from the manual `remove_from_player_list()` reproduction, not from a real disconnect.

## Notes

- The new `Logout` hook is a direct call to the same procedure that produced `huddead` in the manual reproduction, which is the reason for expecting it to work. It is not a replacement for observing it.
- `med_hud_set_status()` is called for every observer login and logout whose mind points at a living mob. The proc is cheap and already handles living bodies, but a maintainer may prefer to restrict the call to dead bodies.
- The `Login` refresh matters even though the body may already show `huddefib`: while the ghost was offline the icon would have been changed to `huddead` by the new `Logout` hook, and it needs to come back.
