# #70269: Multi-z ruins are placed without checking that their lower parts fit (Demonic Frost Miner often missing)

| | |
|---|---|
| Issues | [#70269](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/70269): verified, fixed on the branch; [#78870](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/78870): verified (same cause), fixed; [#93832](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/93832): verified (same cause), fixed; [#81439](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/81439): partly verified (Demonic Frost Miner part, same cause, fixed); the clockwork defender part not reproduced; [#61682](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/61682): not reproduced on master |
| Status | Reproduced and fixed (1 commit) for the multi-z placement; #61682 and part of #81439 not reproduced |

## Summary

On IceBox the only multi-z ruin, "Ice-Ruin Mining Site" (the Demonic Frost Miner's arena, with an underground part), often failed to load completely or at all. `seedRuins()` had two defects: when the z level it picked had no level below it, it gave up on the ruin instead of picking another z, and `try_to_place()` only checked the top part of a ruin, so the lower part could later fail on its turf and leave half a ruin. The fix checks the ruin and all of its parts below together before placing, reserves them together, and only picks z levels that have a level below for such ruins. After it, the Mining Site and the frost miner spawned in the test round.

## The bug

- **#70269** (2022): sometimes the Mining Site is loaded with only its first (upper) z part. The reporter traced it to the upper z being a valid location while the lower one was not, and the spawner not checking this. They also said they could not find a log message for the failure.
- **#78870** (2023) and **#93832** (2025): the Demonic Frost Miner very rarely spawns on IceBox, which makes its armor set effectively unobtainable.
- **#81439** (2024): the frost miner's ruin and the clockwork defender's ruin both often fail to spawn on IceBox.
- **#61682** (2021): ruins spawning floating over openspace on multi-z mining maps (screenshot only).

## Reproduction

On master `87b93338`, with IceBox set as the next map:

1. Boot the test server on IceBox. Mapping init logs which ruins were placed. In the unfixed round the server log said `Failed to place Ice-Ruin Mining Site ruin!`, so the frost miner did not exist (the log line for failures already exists on master, `ruins.dm:183`; the "no log" complaint in #70269 is outdated).
2. With the Lua editor, listing the Ice Ruins z levels: z4 has a level below (z3), while z5 and z6 (wilderness levels) have none (`multiz_levels[z]` down was 0).
3. Using the Lua editor to sample random centres on z4 for the Mining Site template: there were spots where the surface part's area and turfs were valid but the underground part was blocked by `/area/icemoon/underground/explored` (a mapped area). The unfixed code would have placed only the top half at such a spot.

This reproduced the failure in one real boot plus offline sampling of the placement checks. A success rate over many boots was not measured; the roughly "2 rounds in 3" figure below follows from the z-level layout, not from many boots.

## Cause

Both defects are in `code/modules/mapping/ruins.dm`. IceBox's `seedRuins` call is at `code/controllers/subsystem/mapping.dm:268` with the three Ice Ruins levels, and the Mining Site is `always_place` with its underground part linked as `PLACE_BELOW` (`code/datums/ruins/icemoon.dm:100-113`).

1. **Inverted z check (`ruins.dm:150-164`).** For a ruin with a `PLACE_BELOW` part, the placement loop picks a random z from `z_levels` and checks whether a level exists below it:

   ```dm
   if(!GET_TURF_BELOW(T))
       if(forced_z)
           continue outer
       else
           break outer
   ```

   When a z was forced the loop retried, which makes sense, but when the z was randomly picked it did `break outer`, abandoning the ruin altogether. The Mining Site is `always_place`, so its forced z is null (`forced_ruins[R] = -1`) and each try picks one of the three Ice Ruins levels at random. Two of those three (the wilderness levels) have no level below, so each attempt had about a two in three chance of killing the placement outright. In the unfixed round the Mining Site failed this way.
2. **Only the top part is checked (`try_to_place`, `ruins.dm:1-45`).** `try_to_place()` checks the turfs' `NO_RUINS` flag and the area whitelist for the top ruin only and reserves those turfs. The lower part is queued as a forced ruin on `GET_TURF_BELOW(placed_turf)` (`ruins.dm:211-212`) and is checked on its own later. If it failed (a disallowed area or `NO_RUINS` turfs below), the top half stayed alone, which is #70269. Other forced ruins queued in between could also take the space below.

The maintainer comment on #78870 stated the same: `PLACE_BELOW` does not check for enough space on the lower level, and the "can this ruin be placed here" logic should be extracted from `try_to_place()` and run on both top and bottom before placing either.

## Fix

One commit: "Fix issue 70269: check that multi-z ruins' lower parts fit before placing them", in `code/modules/mapping/ruins.dm`.

- `try_to_place()` is split into helpers. `can_place_at()` holds the old per-ruin check (`NO_RUINS` and allowed areas). `get_ruins_below()` returns the templates linked with `PLACE_BELOW`. `can_place_with_parts_below()` recursively checks the ruin and every part below it, failing if there is no turf below. `reserve_with_parts_below()` marks the turfs of the whole stack `NO_RUINS` at once, so no other forced ruin can take the space between the check and the placement.
- `seedRuins()` records the lower parts in a local `reserved_below` list as it queues them, and when it reaches one it places it on its reserved turf without checking again.
- For ruins with parts below, only z levels that have a level below are considered. The `outer:` loop with the inverted `break outer` is gone; a ruin that has no valid z simply fails after the tries, logging "Failed to place".

## Verification

On a fresh boot of the rebuilt server on IceBox:

- The log showed `Successfully placed Ice-Ruin Mining Site ruin (193,169,4 to 223,195,4)` and `Successfully placed Ice-Ruin Mining Site Underground ruin (193,169,3 to 223,195,3)`. With Lua, ruin landmarks existed at 208,182,4 and 208,182,3, and a live `demonic_frost_miner` mob was at 208,182,3.
- Sampling 300 random z4 centres in Lua with a surface-only whitelist, there were 15 spots where the top part alone fit and `can_place_with_parts_below()` accepted 0 of them. With an all-icemoon whitelist there were 15 top fits and 5 accepted; the other 10 were rejected for `NO_RUINS` turfs or blocked areas below. The new check therefore rejects exactly the half-placement case.
- No runtimes in the fixed round apart from the tester's own Lua reading `ruin_template` on landmarks that were not ruins.

Not checked: a success rate over many boots (only one fixed boot); other maps or other multi-z ruins (the Mining Site is the only one, per #70269); the full unit test suite. Mapping init took 88 s against 58 s unfixed, but the host was loaded at the time (another phase also took longer), so this is probably not due to the fix; the extra cost per attempt is small (`get_ruins_below()` only loops over `always_spawn_with`).

## Notes

- **#61682 (floating ruins)** was not reproduced. On master no openspace turfs on the surface z are in `/area/icemoon/surface/outdoors/unexplored`, the only surface area ruins may use: an SDQL query for `/turf/open/openspace` on z4 found 1211 turfs, none in that area (only nospawn, less_spawns and station areas), and the surface cave generator (`code/datums/mapgen/Cavegens/IcemoonCaves.dm`) places only snow. Ruins are also loaded after terrain generation now. Floating ruins at roundstart are therefore not expected on current master.
- **#81439, clockwork defender part:** not reproduced as a bug in this code. The clockwork defender sits in "Asteroid Site", an ordinary random surface ruin without multi-z links. It was placed in one test round and logged `Failed to place Ice-Ruin Asteroid Site ruin!` in another, which looks like normal budget and space competition. Since the Mining Site now places reliably on z4, it takes about 31x27 tiles of surface space there, so the Asteroid Site (40x40) may miss slightly more often. The commenter's suggestion to move some ruins to lower layers was not explored.
- Whether the Mining Site fails often depends on the map's Ice Ruins z-level layout (here three levels, two without a level below), so the failure rate grew when wilderness levels were added.
