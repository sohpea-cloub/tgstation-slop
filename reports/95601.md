## Summary

Field generators only placed "floor shields" when the setup was a plain box. `shield_floor()` looked for a single generator shared by two neighbouring generators, which does not exist as soon as one edge has a mid-way generator. The fix walks the chain of connected generators along each axis to find the far corner of a closed box, fills the rectangle, and remembers the placed shields so they are removed on shutdown. #95560 (off, unwrenched generators shocking on bump) did not reproduce on master.

## #95601: no floor shields with mid-way generators

### The bug

A box of field generators with a generator in the middle of one edge makes the containment fields but no floor shields. A perfect box without mid-way generators makes both.

### Reproduction

Using the Lua editor and SDQL2 on the test server, in space:

1. Create field generators for a box spanning x 10..16, y 10..14 (corners and a generator at (13,14) on the top edge). Set each anchored, `state = 2` (welded), `instantenous = TRUE`, then call `turn_on()`.
2. Count floor shields with the SDQL2 query `SELECT /obj/effect/shield`.
3. Control: a box spanning x 30..36, y 10..14 with no mid-way generator.

Observed on master: 0 shields for the box with the mid-way generator; 35 for the control box.

### Cause

`code/modules/power/singularity/field_generator.dm`, the old `shield_floor()`: for each consecutive pair in `connected_gens` it took `(A.connected_gens & B.connected_gens) ^ src` and returned if that was empty. It relied on the opposite corner being the single generator common to two neighbours. With a mid-way generator on an edge, a corner is connected to the mid-way generator, no unique opposite corner exists, and the proc returned without placing anything.

### Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 95601: field generator floor shields with mid-way generators", all in `field_generator.dm`:

- New `get_furthest_connected_gen(direction)` follows `connected_gens` along a cardinal direction through any mid-way generators (same z-level, no revisits) and returns the far end.
- `shield_floor(TRUE)` finds the far end east or west and north or south, requires the opposite corner to be reachable from both ends (a closed box), and fills the rectangle between this generator and that corner.
- New `floor_shields` list records the shields placed or found. `shield_floor(FALSE)` deletes those shields. This is used because during the shutdown cascade the generators' `connected_gens` are already pruned, so recomputing the box would not work.
- `place_floor` was simplified to add the shield to the list (reusing an existing shield on the turf).

### Verification

After rebuilding: a box with two mid-way generators ((13,14) and (10,12)) plus the control box gave 70 shields (35 each). Calling `turn_off()` on the mid-way generator at (13,14) left 35, i.e. only the control box's, so cleanup works.

Not checked: real engineering construction by a player, boxes spanning several z-levels, and overlapping or L-shaped layouts.

## #95560: off, unwrenched field generators shock on bump

The report (a screenshot and "approach field gen") says a generator that is off and not wrenched shocks, and trips and stuns an insulated player, when bumped. In the Lua editor, an unanchored, off generator was bumped by a human (`Bumped(human)`): total damage was 0. The test human was already paralyzed before the bump by the spawn process (checked before bumping), so the paralysis did not come from the generator. In the code, the overridden shock and bump-field procs only act `if(fields.len)`, so a generator with no fields does nothing. The issue was reported as already fixed upstream by PR #95561. No change was made for it.

## Notes

- #95560 is related to #95601 (both concern field generators), not the same bug.
