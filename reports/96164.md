## Summary

#96164 (drag a disposal bin by cuffing an item to yourself and putting it inside) could not be reproduced: anchored containers leash the cuffed player instead of being dragged. #62565 is real and fixed: a disposal holder only blocked client movement (`relaymove`), so a mob moved by `walk()` or `step()` stepped out mid-pipe; the holder now refuses normal moves out of it. #30977 is real and has a partial fix: disposal pipes under an intact floor tile took full explosion damage and broke while the tile survived; they now take a quarter of bomb damage, as cables do. Some pipes under intact tiles still broke in the final test.

## #96164: dragging a disposal chute via a cuffed item

### The bug

The reporter cuffed something to themselves, put it in a disposal bin, and could then drag the bin around; the comments add that morgues and crematoria behave the same.

### Reproduction

Not reproduced. On the test server:

1. Using the Lua editor, applied the `cuffed_item` status effect to a toolbox and a player, then put the item in a disposal bin, a delivery chute, a morgue and a crematorium, and moved the owner away. In every case the container stayed in place and the owner was blocked ("too far!"). The same was confirmed with a real possessed player.
2. Inserting by hand is already blocked: clicking the bin with the cuffed toolbox gave "You robust the disposal unit", because the cuffed-item logic blocks a non-forced transfer into an object.
3. Pushbroom path: `transferItemToLoc` of the cuffed item into the bin returned 0, so it cannot be swept in.
4. Throw path: throwing the cuffed toolbox at the bin put it inside ("lands in the disposal unit", `bin.dm:567`), but walking away was blocked and the bin stayed put.

### Cause (why it does not drag)

`update_link()` in `code/datums/status_effects/cuffed_item.dm:164-173` leashes the owner to the container when the container is anchored or has `move_resist` above the owner's `move_force`, and only chains the container to the owner otherwise. Bins, chutes, morgues and crematoria are anchored. The file was reworked by PR 98082 (merged 2026-10-06, "Fixes cuffed items lagging in vents and disposals ..."). The old logic had the same anchored check, so there is no proof that PR fixed this report, and the reporter's exact path is unknown.

### Fix

None.

### Verification and notes

Not tested: an unanchored container (that would be dragged by design), several owners with items in the same bin, or the reporter's actual test-merged build. A side quirk was found: a second cuffed item in a second container makes the `chained_together` component return `COMPONENT_REDUNDANT`, so the owner loses the leash from the second container. That is not a bin drag.

## #62565: disposal holders only block movement through relaymove

### The bug

A mob moved by `walk()` or `step()` inside a disposal holder walks out of it, while the holder continues down the pipes. The mob's eye follows the holder, and the screen goes black when the holder is deleted.

### Reproduction

1. Using the Lua editor, built a row of 61 `/obj/structure/disposalpipe/segment` (`dir` 4, `dpdir` 12) at y=165, x=40 to 100.
2. `forceMove`d a human into a new active `/obj/structure/disposalholder` at the first pipe and called `start_moving()`.
3. Called `_walk(human, NORTH, 1)` on the human.
4. On master the human walked out and ended in space at 40,185 while the holder carried on.

### Cause

`code/modules/recycling/disposal/holder.dm:197` overrides `relaymove`, which only handles client input. `walk()`/`step()` go through `Move`, which calls the holder's `Exit()`, and the holder did not override it.

### Fix

Commit "Fix issue 62565: disposal holders stop contents from walking out" adds `/obj/structure/disposalholder/Exit`, which returns `FALSE` while the leaving atom's `loc` is the holder. `forceMove()` does not call `Exit`, so expelling at the end of the pipe and struggling free still work.

### Verification

Same setup after the fix: the human stayed inside until the holder expelled it at the pipe end (ended on the floor at 105,169). Not checked: other ways of being inside a holder (for example other mob types) beyond this one test.

## #30977: disposal pipes break under intact floors in explosions

### The bug

After an explosion, disposal pipes can be cut while the floor tile above them is intact. Several people reported it still happening in 2022 and 2024.

### Reproduction

1. Using the Lua editor, laid a row of disposal pipes and called `explosion(locate(98,165,2), 0, 3, 3, 0, 0, false, true)` (asynchronous; checked after a couple of seconds).
2. The tile at 96,165 stayed an intact iron floor but its pipe had become `/obj/structure/disposalpipe/broken`, and a trunk was lost too.

### Cause

`/obj/ex_act` (`code/game/objects/obj_defense.dm:10`) deals `rand(100, 250)` brute on a heavy blast to anything with `max_integrity` 200, and `turf/contents_explosion` (`code/game/turfs/turf.dm:579`) hits underfloor objects too. A broken pipe is created when a pipe is deconstructed non-disassembled (`code/modules/recycling/disposal/pipe.dm:86`). A floor tile can survive the same blast, so the pipe beneath it was cut.

### Fix

Commit "Fix issue 30977: disposal pipes under intact floor take reduced bomb damage" adds `/obj/structure/disposalpipe/run_atom_armor` in `pipe.dm`, multiplying damage by 0.25 when `damage_flag == BOMB` and the pipe has `TRAIT_UNDERFLOOR`. It is modelled on the existing cable behaviour. A heavy blast then does 25 to 62 against 200 integrity; a devastating blast (`INFINITY`) still destroys the pipe, and exposed pipes are unchanged.

### Verification

With 172 pipe segments on floors (x84..100, y158..172) and an explosion of strength (0, 9, 12) at 92,165:

- `run_atom_armor(200, brute, bomb)` returned 50 for a pipe on intact floor and 200 on plating.
- Surviving pipes on intact floor had integrity of 125 to 200 (largest loss 75); exposed pipes on plating ranged from 0 to 200, i.e. full damage.
- Five pipe tiles under floor still ended broken (for example 99,168 and 99,170, where the tile cracked, and 96,165 near the centre). The cause was not investigated; possibilities are tiles reduced to plating mid-explosion (the explosion queue is asynchronous) or repeated hits.

Pipes spawned by Lua are not hidden until `levelupdate()` is called on the turf; this was done so the underfloor trait applied. Not checked: real gameplay bombs, or tuning of the 0.25 factor.

## Notes

- The #62565 fix changes `Exit()` semantics for the holder; anything that relied on stepping out of a holder with a normal move would now be refused (nothing like that was found).
- #30977's issue text links a related disposal-nullspace bug (#8484); that is a different problem and is not addressed.
