# #97626: Alt clicking is broken for terror spiders

## Summary

The report says terror spiders cannot alt-click, which makes webs hard to click through. On current master a possessed giant spider (tarantula and the other types checked) could alt-click and open the loot panel on webbed tiles, including tiles with the spider's own webs. Nothing spider-specific in the code intercepts alt-click. The report's round ran test merges (#97357, #97612) that master lacks, so the cause may be in one of those, in a selected click ability, or on the client side. No fix was made.

## The bug

As reported: webs are annoying to click through when trying to use a camera, light or ladder. Alt-click (which opens the loot panel on a tile) would help, but the reporter could not use it as a spider. The reproduction steps are: play the spider, make a web on top of what you want to use, struggle.

## Reproduction

Not reproduced. What was tried, with the Lua editor and a client on the test server:

1. Started a round, spawned a `/mob/living/basic/spider/giant/tarantula` and possessed it by setting its ckey.
2. Spawned a `/obj/structure/spider/stickyweb` and a crowbar on the adjacent tile.
3. Alt-clicked the web tile. The loot panel opened and listed the floor, the web and the crowbar. (The very first click right after possession showed no panel; the round was still initializing, and later clicks were fine.)
4. Also worked: alt-click on a plain tile; alt-click while standing on the web tile; alt-click on a hardened (sealed, opaque) web with a wrench on it; alt-click on an opaque web passage with a screwdriver on it.
5. Alt-clicking an item inside the panel re-opened the panel. A plain click on the hardened web from the panel made the spider attack it, so the panel was interactive for the spider.
6. With the Lua editor, read `mobility_flags` and `interaction_flags_atom` on the guard, hunter, nurse, midwife, viper, tangle, ambush, scout and tarantula types: all had `mobility_flags` 127 (use and pickup set) and `interaction_flags_atom` 0.

## Cause

No defect was found. For living mobs, `base_click_alt` (`code/_onclick/click_alt.dm:27`) ends in `try_open_loot_panel_on` (`click_alt.dm:121`), which only requires the mob to see the target (`viewers`) and, if blind, to be within one tile. The loot panel's `ui_status` (`code/modules/lootpanel/_lootpanel.dm:78`) gave the spiders an interactive state given the flags above. Nothing in `code/modules/mob/living/basic/space_fauna/spider` or `code/game/objects/effects/spiderwebs.dm` overrides alt-click.

One mechanism could swallow alt-clicks for a spider. The Wrap ability is a click-to-activate ability (`spider_abilities/wrap.dm:11`, `click_to_activate = TRUE`). While one is selected, the mob's click intercept consumes every click before modifiers are read (`code/_onclick/click.dm:76`, `check_click_intercept`), alt-clicks included. This applies to any click ability, was read from the code and not tested in game, and is probably not what the reporter hit.
