# #74140: Folded bluespace body bag contents lost on destruction (not reproduced); a second bag from a telekinetic grab (#77268, fixed)

## Summary

#74140 reports that a person inside a folded bluespace body bag ends up in nullspace when the bag is destroyed, for example by C4. On current master every destruction path that was tried put the occupant on the floor, so it was not reproduced; the only related oddity found is that C4 planted on an unfolded bag points at a deleted object once the bag is folded. #77268 reports that a telekinetic grab can make a second bluespace bag from one folded bag. That mechanism was reproduced: a folded bag that is already deployed (and therefore in nullspace) could be deployed again through a grab that still had it as its target, creating a second bag in nullspace. The fix makes `deploy_bodybag()` refuse to deploy a bag that is itself in nullspace, or to deploy to a null location.

## The bug

### #74140

The reporter (with a video that is not available to this write-up) says a bluespace bag dumps its contents into nullspace when it is destroyed with C4 or "something similar". They add that the C4 interaction while folding the bag is "wack", and that other ways of destroying the bag may do the same.

### #77268

The report describes a race with two players, one of whom has telekinesis: the telekinetic player is inside a bluespace bag, the other folds the bag and picks it up, the telekinetic player grabs the bag with telekinesis first, then the folder puts the bag down. The result is said to be that infinite bags can be placed, and that incinerating the original bag sends the former occupant to the debug room because their `loc` becomes null.

## Reproduction

All of this used the Lua editor and SDQL2 from an observer on a test round. The scenarios are built by calling procs directly (spawning mobs and a `/obj/structure/closet/body_bag/bluespace`, putting a human inside with `forceMove`, folding with `perform_fold()` so the folder ends up holding the item). They do not use real hands-on clicks.

### #74140: destruction paths (not reproduced)

Setup for each case: a folder human A, a victim human V forced into the bluespace bag, then `perform_fold(A)`. The location of V was checked after each action.

| Action | Where V ended up |
|---|---|
| `qdel` the folded bag item in A's hand | the floor |
| `take_damage(1000)` on the item in A's hand | the floor |
| `take_damage(1000)` on the item lying on the floor | the floor |
| `atom_destruction()` on the item on the floor | the floor |
| Item deployed (V is moved into the unfolded bag, which is then opened): `take_damage(1000)` on the unfolded bag | the floor |
| Item deployed: `ex_act(2)` on the unfolded bag | the floor |
| `ex_act(2)` on the folded item in hand | no destruction; the item survived and V stayed inside it |
| A real `/obj/item/grenade/c4` with its `target` set to the folded item (in hand, on the floor, and on a deployed bag) and `detonate()` called | no human anywhere with a null `loc` (SDQL `SELECT /mob/living/carbon/human WHERE !loc` returned none) |
| `qdel` A, the holder of the folded bag | V on the floor |
| `gib` or `dust` A | V's location was the bag, not nullspace |

The C4 `target` was set directly instead of through the 3-second planting action, so the planting interaction itself (including the timer path) was not exercised.

### #77268: telekinetic double deploy (reproduced as a mechanism)

1. Spawn a folder human A, a victim V, and a third human P; give P the telekinesis mutation (`dna:add_mutation(/datum/mutation/telekinesis, 'admin')`).
2. Put V in a bluespace bag, fold it with A, put the folded item `I` on the floor.
3. Have P grab `I` with telekinesis (`I:attack_tk(P)`): P holds a Telekinetic Grab whose focus is the folded bag.
4. Deploy the bag normally with `I:deploy_bodybag(A, turf)`. This created closet C1 on the floor, V was moved out of the bag onto the floor, and `I` moved to nullspace (`I.loc` null), as designed.
5. Call `attack_self(P)` on the grab. On unmodified master this ran through `attack_self_tk` into `I.attack_self()` and `deploy_bodybag()` a second time, with `get_turf(I)` being null. SDQL `SELECT /obj/structure/closet/body_bag/bluespace WHERE !loc` found a second bluespace bag with no location. (The SDQL output with the exact count on the unfixed build was not recorded in the notes, only that a second bag was found.)

Not reproduced: the report's claim that the original occupant ends up in nullspace or is duplicated. In these runs V was already on the floor after the first deploy because deploying a bluespace bag opens it and moves the contents out. The timing-dependent race with real clicks (the grab only lives a short time after the item leaves the floor) was not attempted; both the deploy and the grab's `attack_self` were invoked by admin calls.

## Cause

### #74140

No cause was found. The folded bluespace bag's own `Destroy()` is written to move its contents out before it goes:

```dm
/obj/item/bodybag/bluespace/Destroy()
	for(var/atom/movable/A in contents)
		A.forceMove(get_turf(src))
```
(`code/game/objects/items/tools/medical/bodybag.dm:70-75`)

This would only strand an occupant in nullspace if the folded item were itself in nullspace with contents. The item goes to nullspace only in `deploy_bodybag()`, and the bluespace override moves all contents into the unfolded bag first (`bodybag.dm:77-86`), so no code path was found that leaves a loaded folded bag in nullspace. The video from the report was presumably made on older code; whether older code behaved differently was not checked.

The C4 oddity the report calls "wack" is visible in code. `plant_c4()` sets `target = bomb_target` and moves the C4 itself to nullspace (`code/game/objects/items/grenades/plastic.dm:124` and `:140`). If the target is a deployed bag that is later folded, the unfolded bag is deleted but the C4 keeps pointing at it. In `detonate()` a target that is `QDELETED` leaves `location` unset (`plastic.dm:78-81`), so by reading, no explosion would be produced for that C4. That was not run and was not changed.

### #77268

`deploy_bodybag()` has no guard against being called on an item that is already deployed:

```dm
/obj/item/bodybag/proc/deploy_bodybag(mob/user, atom/location)
	var/obj/structure/closet/body_bag/item_bag = new unfoldedbag_path(location)
	...
	moveToNullspace()
```
(`bodybag.dm:34-41`; the bluespace override at `bodybag.dm:77-88` is the same)

An already deployed bag is in nullspace (that is how its unfolded form keeps it). `/obj/item/tk_grab/attack_self()` calls `focus.attack_self_tk(user)` without checking `check_if_focusable()` first (`code/_onclick/telekinesis.dm:143-151`). The grab only drops a focus that has become unfocusable at its next `fastprocess` tick (`process()`, `telekinesis.dm:119-121`; `check_if_focusable()` requires the target's `loc` to be a turf, `telekinesis.dm:233-239`). In between, `attack_self_tk` reaches `/obj/item/bodybag/attack_self()`, which uses `get_turf(src)` as the location, which is null for a bag in nullspace, and `new unfoldedbag_path(null)` makes a second closet in nullspace.

## Fix

One commit: "Fix issue 77268: don't deploy an already deployed bodybag again". In `code/game/objects/items/tools/medical/bodybag.dm`, both `/obj/item/bodybag/proc/deploy_bodybag()` and the bluespace override now start with:

```dm
if(isnull(loc) || isnull(location))
	return null
```

A folded bag with a null `loc` is already deployed, and a null `location` can only produce a body bag in nullspace, so neither case should create anything. The guard sits in `deploy_bodybag()` instead of in the telekinesis code or in `attack_self()` because that proc is the single place that creates the bag and sends the item to nullspace, and all callers (hand use, clicking a floor, telekinesis) go through it.

Not changed, though they could be considered: making `tk_grab/attack_self()` verify the focus is still focusable before using it; the stasis bag's own `deploy_bodybag()` override (`bodybag.dm:150`), which was not looked at; the C4 `target` handling.

## Verification

After rebuilding the test server, the same sequence was repeated: grab the folded bag with telekinesis, deploy it, then call `attack_self` on the grab.

- `I.loc` stayed null and no second bag was created. SDQL `SELECT /obj/structure/closet/body_bag/bluespace WHERE !loc` selected 3 objects by path and 0 after the `WHERE` (that is, no bluespace bag with a null location).
- Refolding the bag gave the same item back (`same` was true) and redeploying it put the bag on the floor, so normal deploy and fold still work.

Not checked:

- The race with real player clicks (two players, real timing).
- Hand deployment, floor-click deployment and the non-bluespace and stasis bags after the change, other than the regular bluespace refold/redeploy above.
- The report's claim about the original occupant going to the debug room after incineration.
- The #74140 destruction paths on the fixed build (the fix does not touch them).

## Notes

- #74140 may have been fixed upstream by a change after the report or may have depended on a different version of the bag or C4 code. Nothing in the tested code puts a loaded bag in nullspace.
- The "C4 planted on an unfolded bag, then the bag folded" interaction (C4 keeps a reference to a deleted bag) looks like a real defect by code reading but was not run, and is outside what #74140's title describes.
- Group membership: the two issues were grouped as "loses or duplicates its contents". The duplication part turned out to be a duplicated empty bag, not duplicated contents.
