## Summary

#47592 (a changeling in critical condition who uses Reviving Stasis keeps gasping) could not be reproduced: stasis stops the mob from breathing, so the suffocation gasps no longer happen. #49543 is real in general form: a changeling revived from stasis inside a mob that swallowed their body (a morph, a space dragon) stays inside it, alive, because the revive code never checks the location. The blob zombie case from the original report is already handled by the zombie itself. The fix makes the revive eject the changeling to the devourer's turf with messages to both.

## The bug

### #47592

The report: as a changeling, get into crit, use Reviving Stasis without dying, and you are forced to `*gasp` (from being in crit) while examine shows you as dead; you also do a `*deathgasp` once you succumb.

### #49543

A changeling in stasis whose body ends up inside another mob (the report: a blob spore zombifies the corpse and eats it into itself; comments: morph, legion, space dragon, demons) revives while still inside the mob. They are alive and can use abilities, but cannot control the mob and are only released when it dies. Several older issues about the same pattern are closed; laughter and slaughter demons got custom handling in the past.

## Reproduction

All steps used the Lua editor and calls to the procs that the abilities call, not the abilities used by a player.

### #47592 (not reproduced)

1. Spawn a human, give them a mind and the changeling antagonist datum, then deal 150 oxygen damage. The human went to hard crit (stat 2, health -50).
2. Baseline: without stasis the human said "gasps!" three times in about 15 seconds.
3. Call `enable_fakedeath` on the Reviving Stasis action for the human (what the ability does when used).
4. Observed: one "seizes up and falls limp" death emote at the start (expected for a non-silent fakedeath), then no gasp for more than 40 seconds. Stat stayed 2, oxygen damage rose slowly, no emotes appeared.

The `*deathgasp` on a real death after a feigned one (the second part of the report) was not exercised in game; from the code it is the normal death emote.

### #49543 (reproduced before the fix)

1. Spawn a human changeling and put them into fakedeath (the stasis state).
2. `forceMove` the human into a morph.
3. Call `revive()` on the action.
4. Result on master: stat 0, health 85, location still the morph.

## Cause

### #47592

`enable_fakedeath` adds `TRAIT_STASIS` (`code/modules/antagonists/changeling/powers/fakedeath.dm:43`). `/mob/living/Life` skips `handle_breathing` for mobs with that trait (`code/modules/mob/living/life.dm:48-51`), so the crit suffocation gasp (`code/modules/mob/living/carbon/life.dm:410`) never runs. The old behaviour in the report presumably came from before stasis applied that trait. The remaining deathgasp comes from `death()` (`code/modules/mob/living/death.dm:230`), which runs on a real death.

### #49543

`/datum/action/changeling/fakedeath/proc/revive` (`fakedeath.dm:94-119`) cures the fakedeath, calls `user.revive(...)` and then regrows limbs, but never looks at `user.loc`. Mobs that swallow a victim by moving it into themselves (morph, space dragon) have no revive handler, so the changeling revives inside them.

The blob zombie case behaves differently: when the zombie takes a corpse it registers `COMSIG_LIVING_REVIVE` on it (`code/modules/mob/living/basic/blob_minions/blob_zombie.dm:80`), and `on_corpse_revived` (lines 83-87) kills the zombie, and the zombie's `death()` drops the corpse (lines 37-39). That is why the main case of the report no longer happens.

## Fix

One commit on the branch, **Fix issue 49543: changeling revive from stasis ejects them from the creature that ate them**. After `user.revive(...)` in `fakedeath.dm`, the code checks `isliving(user.loc)`. If so, it moves the changeling to the devourer's turf, plays the splat sound, and sends "bursts out of" messages to the room, to the changeling, and "Something bursts out of you!" to the devourer. Running the check after the revive keeps the blob zombie path working: the zombie's own handler fires during `revive()` and drops the corpse first, so the changeling is no longer inside a mob when the check runs.

This follows the suggestion in the issue thread (check `isliving(user.loc)` in the revive code and eject). No change was made for #47592.

## Verification

On the rebuilt server, by calling `revive()` from Lua:

- A changeling inside a morph: "bursts out of the morph!", stat 0, location the floor, the morph still alive.
- A changeling corpse inside a blob zombie, then revived: the zombie was killed and deleted, the changeling was alive on the floor (no regression).
- A changeling not inside anything: revived on the floor as before.
- No new runtimes in the round's `runtime.log`.

Not checked: space dragon and legion (the dragon uses the same pattern as the morph); laughter and slaughter demons and other mobs that eject on their own; revive through the real ability button and the 40-second wait; whether a mob that swallows a living body via different containers (for example a mob's `contents` that is not its location) is affected; #47592's "examine shows you as dead" claim.

## Notes

- Whether being eaten by a morph or dragon should ever allow revival (as opposed to ejecting) is a design question; commenters on #49543 disagreed on whether it was a feature. The fix chooses the "burst out" behaviour suggested in the thread. A maintainer who prefers blocking revival while inside a mob, as suggested for blob spores, would need a different change.
- #47592 looks closable as no longer applicable. The unverified part is the exact version of the game in which it was reported (2019).
- Related closed issues listed in the thread for #49543 include #52432, #27757, #29213, #32069 and the unmerged attempt in #31624.
