## Summary

When a changeling headslug lays an egg in a corpse and its player then takes another role (a ghost role or midround spawn), the egg still tries to hand the planter's old mind and client to the newly hatched monkey. The egg uses the old mind's stored key, so it pulls the client out of whatever body the player is now in. The fix makes `pop()` skip the mind and client transfer when the client controls a mob that is neither a ghost nor the mind's own body. The hatch then produces a plain, uncontrolled monkey.

## The bug

#37274 (2018): as a headslug, the player puts an egg in a body and dies, accepts a ghost role as "Joe Schmuck", plays for a while, and later the egg hatches and the player is thrown out of Joe's body into the new changeling monkey. A commenter on the issue pointed out that the client was attached to a different mind and mob when this happened, and that the transfer should concern the planter's mind, not the client. Other commenters suggested this was an intentional guard (for example, against cloners denying the ling spawn) or a feature rather than a bug; the issue keeps the Bug and Oversight labels.

#56314 (2021) is a three-part report on headslugs:
1. Action buttons for the changeling's abilities stay on the HUD after Last Resort.
2. The egg's revive timer was far longer than expected (over 40 minutes in one round).
3. After taking control of another mob (the reporter became a blob), the hatching egg ghosted the reporter out of that mob and back into the changeling monkey. A 2024 comment says this still stood, that the other mob was fine afterwards, and that it is odd for the antag datum to remain on the mind after taking a new role.

## Reproduction

Reproduced on master `87b93338` with the Lua editor, calling `pop()` on the egg directly rather than waiting for the 4-minute incubation or running a real ghost-role handover. The planter's mind was a fresh `/datum/mind` created with the test client's key, not a mind produced by a real headslug/ghost-role sequence.

1. Started a round on the test server and observed.
2. Created a mind with the test client's key and transferred it into a `/mob/living/basic/headslug`.
3. Spawned a human corpse and had the headslug infect it (`slug:infect(corpse)`), then killed the headslug.
4. Gave the client a second, living human named "Joe Schmuck" (`PossessByPlayer`), which has its own mind.
5. Called `pop()` on the egg in the corpse.

Result: chat showed "You are a fresh changeling birthed from a headslug!" and the client's mob changed to the new monkey. The player was removed from Joe Schmuck.

An early attempt failed because `SS13` was not required in the script, and the egg lookup initially found nothing; the working run is described above.

## Cause

`/obj/item/organ/body_egg/changeling_egg/proc/pop()` (`code/modules/antagonists/changeling/headslug_eggs.dm:27`) builds the monkey and then, if the origin mind exists and its body is dead (or it is a ghost), runs:

```dm
if(origin && (origin.current ? (origin.current.stat == DEAD) : origin.get_ghost()))
	origin.transfer_to(spawned_monkey)
	spawned_monkey.PossessByPlayer(origin.key)
```

(lines 34-36). Once the player has moved on, `origin.key` still names their client, so `PossessByPlayer` drags that client out of its current mob. Nothing checks whether the player is currently playing something else. The condition only inspects the old mind's own body, which is the dead headslug.

Two points in the second report match this: bug 3 is this path. Bug 1 is cosmetic (the changeling power's `can_be_used_by` check in `changeling_power.dm` requires a human mob, so the abilities cannot be used from the slug). For bug 2, the code sets `hatch_time = world.time + EGG_INCUBATION_TIME` (4 minutes) the first time the egg is inserted into a body and keeps the remaining time if it is moved (`headslug_eggs.dm:14-16`), so a long timer could not be explained from the code; a 2024 comment on the issue also failed to reproduce it.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 37274: changeling egg no longer pulls the planter's client out of another mob", changing `code/modules/antagonists/changeling/headslug_eggs.dm`:

- New proc `origin_player_is_busy()`. It looks up the client for `origin.key` in `GLOB.directory`. If there is none, it returns FALSE. Otherwise it returns TRUE when that client's mob is neither an observer nor `origin.current`.
- `pop()` now requires `!origin_player_is_busy()` before the mind and client transfer.

When the player is busy, the egg still bursts and gibs the host, and the new monkey is an ordinary monkey with no mind, client or changeling datum. The planter's original mind is left alone. A ghost planter, or one still in the dead headslug body, is handled as before.

## Verification

After rebuilding and restarting, using the same direct-`pop()` method:

- Case A, client in "Joe Schmuck" when the egg popped: the client stayed in Joe (`c.mob == joe` was true, name "Joe Schmuck"), and the new monkey had no client and no mind.
- Case B, client a ghost when the egg popped (the client was ghosted with `ghostize(false)` first): the client was pulled into the monkey as a changeling ("you are now mob monkey ...", the usual birth messages). The normal hatch is unchanged.

Not checked: a real hatch driven by the incubation timer, a real ghost-role or midround-spawn handover (instead of `PossessByPlayer` from Lua), a planter whose mind is the real one from a headslug player, the case where the planter's client has disconnected, and changeling-specific checks on the monkey (profile, powers) in case B beyond the normal birth messages.

## Notes

- Design choice: in case A the egg produces a plain monkey that nobody controls. An alternative would be to offer that monkey to ghosts so the changeling is not wasted. Comments on the issue also argue that the old behaviour was a deliberate fix or consistent with other revivals that pull a ghost into its body; the fix keeps that behaviour for ghosts only.
- #56314 bug 3 is resolved by this fix. Bug 1 and bug 2 remain open: bug 1 is cosmetic and was not checked in game; bug 2 was not reproduced and the hatch time was not measured in game.
- The old antag datum staying on a mind that has taken a new role (mentioned on #56314) was not investigated.
