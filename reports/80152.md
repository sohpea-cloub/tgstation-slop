## Summary

The issue has two parts: a megafauna "ignores" a player disguised with a chameleon projector, and the player "can just avoid all projectiles". The projectile half is a real defect. A mob inside the projector's dummy has the dummy as its `loc`, so projectiles aimed at the mob never hit it and also pass through the non-dense dummy. The fix redirects a projectile's `original` and `direct_target` to the dummy when fired, so the dummy is hit and the projector breaks. The targeting half comes from `hearers()` not listing a mob inside the dummy. That looks like the disguise working as coded, so it was left unchanged.

## The bug

A player using a chameleon projector next to a megafauna is not attacked, and shots the megafauna aims at them fly straight through without harm or revealing them. The reporter calls this an exploit that makes megafauna easy to kill. Commenters remark that the projector is an antagonist item, and one notes the Wendigo did not drop its crusher trophy in such a fight. The trophy remark was not investigated.

## Reproduction

1. On the test server, join as an observer, open the Lua editor, and spawn a human and a `/mob/living/simple_animal/hostile/megafauna/colossus` in the same hallway.
2. Put the human inside a chameleon projector dummy (activate a projector on the human).
3. Targeting half: reading the colossus's `ListTargets()` result through admin tools gave 1 entry for the human while the human stood outside the dummy and 0 entries once the human was inside, although `CanAttack(human)` returned 1 for the human. So the colossus does not see a disguised mob at all.
4. Projectile half: with the human in a dummy, create a `/obj/projectile/colossus`, aim it at the human (`aim_projectile(human, firer)`) and call `fire()` from five tiles away. After three seconds the human's `loc` was still the dummy, the projector's `active_dummy` was still set, there were no sparks and the human took no bolt damage. This was a direct proc call, not a megafauna firing by itself. A first attempt at a spot where a wall was in the way (the bolt hit the wall) was inconclusive.
5. The run described in step 4 was on a different map from the later after-fix run, so it is not an exact before/after comparison.

## Cause

- `code/modules/projectiles/projectile.dm:627`, `can_hit_target`: `if(!ignore_loc && (loc != target.loc) && ...) return FALSE`. A mob in the dummy has `loc` equal to the dummy, never the turf the projectile is on, so it can never be hit directly.
- `code/modules/projectiles/projectile.dm:685-700`, `scan_moved_turf`, only loops over mobs on the turf.
- `code/game/objects/items/devices/chameleonproj.dm:119-124`: the dummy is non-dense, so it is hit only when it is the projectile's `original`, that is, the clicked target. A player clicking on the disguise hits it that way, but a megafauna or turret aims at the mob inside, so the dummy's `bullet_act` (`chameleonproj.dm:152`, which calls `disrupt()`) is never reached.
- Targeting: `ListTargets()` in `code/modules/mob/living/simple_animal/hostile/hostile.dm:170` builds its list with `hearers(vision_range, ...)`, which does not include a mob inside the dummy. The disguised mob stays out of the list until the disguise breaks or the mob leaves it.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 80152: shots aimed at a mob inside a chameleon projector dummy hit the dummy". In `/obj/projectile/proc/fire` (`projectile.dm`), if `original` or `direct_target` has a `/obj/effect/dummy/chameleon` as its `loc`, it is replaced with that dummy:

```dm
if (istype(original?.loc, /obj/effect/dummy/chameleon))
	original = original.loc
if (istype(direct_target?.loc, /obj/effect/dummy/chameleon))
	direct_target = direct_target.loc
```

A shot aimed at the hidden mob now hits the dummy, whose `bullet_act` ejects the mob and breaks the projector. Shots not aimed at the disguised mob that merely cross its tile still pass through, as before, since the dummy is non-dense. The targeting code was not changed.

## Verification

After rebuilding and restarting the test server, on a different map from the "before" run: a human inside a dummy at 111,144, the same colossus bolt aimed at the human and fired from 104,144. Results: sparks at the dummy's tile (the dummy was disrupted), the human's `loc` became a floor turf, the projector's `active_dummy` became nil, and its `can_use` was 0 (on cooldown). The build had 0 errors. No runtime log was checked beyond the results of the Lua runs.

Not checked: a real megafauna or turret firing at a disguised player end to end (the megafauna never targets a disguised mob, see Cause); other projectile types; an A/B comparison on the same map.

## Notes

- Whether megafauna should target a disguised player is a design call. The `hearers()` behaviour is not specific to this projector, so changing it could affect other hidden-mob cases. This was left for maintainers.
- A related observation, outside this issue: in this tree an idle (`AI_IDLE`) simple animal appears to wake only on damage; the test colossus stayed idle with a plain human three tiles away until its AI was toggled on. This may affect how a test like this looks.
- No upstream PR fixing this was found in a search of PR titles.
