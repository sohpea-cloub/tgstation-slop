## Summary

All three issues are about the say pipeline. #58706: expressive aphasia listens to the same `COMSIG_MOB_SAY` signal as accents, and handlers run in registration order, so a tongue accent (registered at spawn) always ran before the trauma (registered later). Aphasia therefore judged already-accented words, and allowed words such as "this" were scrambled for lizards. The fix adds `COMSIG_MOB_PRE_SAY`, sent just before `COMSIG_MOB_SAY` with the same argument list, and moves aphasia onto it. #97816: the 2-second stun applied when climbing could roll the stun status effect's forced "Ack!"; the fix lets `Stun()` suppress the forced speech and climbing does so. #76152: reproduced, but left unfixed because the right behavior is a design decision.

## The bug

### #58706

Expressive aphasia is a brain trauma that only lets the player say a short list of common words and scrambles the rest. Species accents (lizard "sss", fly and others) are applied by the tongue. Because aphasia saw the message after the accent, a lizard with the trauma had "this" and "is" altered before the check, so they no longer matched the allowed list and were scrambled too. The reporter noted that this effectively removes every `s` word for lizards and flypeople, and suspected snails would be affected as well. (Snails were not tested.)

### #76152

A player with the Soft-Spoken quirk who uses divine vocal cords (the Colossus cords) has the command whispered. The reporter's screenshot shows this; commenters disagreed on whether it is a bug or thematic, and one noted that anyone standing out of earshot is still affected by the command.

### #97816

A player who has the say window open and climbs onto a table or other climbable thing voluntarily can get their message cut off with "Ack!", as if they had been slammed onto the table.

## Reproduction

All steps used the Lua editor on a test server, with the round started.

### #58706 (reproduced)

1. Spawned a human, set its species to lizard, and gave it the `expressive_aphasia` brain trauma.
2. Made it say "this is the best station".
3. Observed: `hisses, "Hti si the ebs stsas"`. The allowed words "this" and "is" were scrambled because the lizard accent had already changed them. A plain human saying the same sentence was untouched.

This was done by making the mob speak through the Lua editor, not by typing as a player.

### #76152 (reproduced)

1. Gave a human the `softspoken` trait and inserted a `/obj/item/organ/vocal_cords/colossus` into it.
2. Called `speak_with('stop')` on the cords.
3. Observed: the human whispers "STOP". Without the trait the same call says "STOP" normally.

### #97816 (cause found; live symptom not seen on master)

1. Possessed a human with the say window open (the key that opens the say input), and dragged the human onto a table, several times. Three attempts on master produced no "Ack!".
2. From the code the chance is roughly 44% per climb (see Cause), so three misses is not strong evidence either way. The symptom was not observed on master; the diagnosis rests on the code path.

## Cause

### #58706

`/mob/living/say()` sends `COMSIG_MOB_SAY` once (`code/modules/mob/living/living_say.dm:195`). Handlers run in the order they registered. The tongue's accent registers when the tongue is created (`code/modules/surgery/organs/internal/tongue/_tongue.dm:144`), while a brain trauma registers in `on_gain` (`code/datums/brain_damage/brain_trauma.dm:54`), later. The aphasia handler (`code/datums/brain_damage/mild.dm:213`, `expressive_aphasia/handle_speech`) is a restrictive filter, not an accent, but it sat in the same ordered list, so it always saw accented text.

### #76152

`code/modules/mob/living/living_say.dm:155` forces `WHISPER_MODE` for `TRAIT_FORCE_WHISPER`, which the soft-spoken quirk grants. `voice_of_god()` (`code/datums/voice_of_god_command.dm:29`, calling `user.say` at `:35`) affects every listener from `get_hearers_in_view(8)`, but the whispered message only carries one tile (`message_range` of 1). So the command is whispered yet still obeyed by everyone within 8 tiles.

### #97816

`code/datums/elements/climbable.dm:99` calls `user.Stun(adjusted_climb_stun)` (2 seconds). That applies `/datum/status_effect/incapacitating/stun`, whose `on_apply` calls `try_force_say` (`code/datums/status_effects/debuffs/debuffs.dm`). The effect has `force_say_chance` 66, and a 2-second duration needs a `prob(66)` roll to pass. On success, `human.force_say` makes the say input append one of the "hurt" phrases, which include "Ack!". The reporter's "raised platform" is the same element.

## Fix

Branch contents, by commit subject:

**"Fix issue 58706: expressive aphasia runs before accents and speech modifiers"**
- Adds `COMSIG_MOB_PRE_SAY` ("mob_pre_say") next to `COMSIG_MOB_SAY` in `code/__DEFINES/dcs/signals/signals_mob/signals_mob_main.dm`.
- `living_say()` sends it immediately before `COMSIG_MOB_SAY`, passing the same `args` list so handlers can edit the message in place.
- `expressive_aphasia` now overrides `on_gain` to unregister from `COMSIG_MOB_SAY` and register on `COMSIG_MOB_PRE_SAY`; `on_lose` unregisters it. `handle_speech` gained `SIGNAL_HANDLER`.
- Why this approach: it follows the reporter's own suggestion of a signal that fires before ordinary modifiers, and leaves every existing `COMSIG_MOB_SAY` user untouched. The alternative of reordering registrations would have been fragile.

**"Fix issue 97816: climbing onto a table no longer makes you say "Ack!""**
- `Stun()` (`code/modules/mob/living/status_procs.dm`) gets a third argument, `force_say = TRUE`, which passes `!force_say` as a new `no_force_say` argument to the stun status effect.
- `/datum/status_effect/incapacitating/on_creation` sets `force_say_chance = 0` when `no_force_say` is true, before calling the parent.
- `climbable.dm` now calls `user.Stun(adjusted_climb_stun, force_say = FALSE)`. All other stuns keep the default behavior.

No commit for #76152.

## Verification

### #58706 (after rebuilding)

- Lizard with the trauma saying "this is the best extraordinary station" gave `hisses, "Thisss isss the bessst akssoterr ssstation"`: common words intact and then accented, the rare word scrambled and then accented.
- "this is a hippopotamus" gave "Thisss isss a phopip".
- A lizard without the trauma gave "Thisss isss the bessst ssstation". No new runtimes appeared.
- Not checked: other accents (fly, snail, masks), losing the trauma and then speaking, and multiple aphasia sources. Speech was produced through the Lua editor, not typed by a player.

### #97816

- With the Lua editor, `Stun(20)` on a human produced a stun effect with `force_say_chance` 66, and `Stun(20, false, false)` produced 0.
- Not checked: a real climb with the say window open. The random roll is only about 44% likely to show the symptom, and the three attempts on unfixed master also did not roll it. So there is no before/after in game.

### #76152

Nothing to verify; no change was made.

## Notes

- #76152 needs a maintainer decision: either soft-spoken should not whisper the divine voice (the cords cannot be whispered), or the command's area should shrink to the whisper range. A small possible implementation is a `message_mods` key that `living_say.dm:155` respects, which `voice_of_god`'s `user.say` would set. Maintainers in the thread were ambivalent and the issue is labeled Oversight.
- The `COMSIG_MOB_PRE_SAY` hook may suit other restrictive speech traits that should see the original text; none were changed.
- The branch also contains an earlier unrelated commit ("Fix lua-runtimes ...") under the two fixes; it is not part of this work.
