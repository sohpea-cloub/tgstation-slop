# #86828: Spasms keep firing, and hurt the owner, inside a silverscale statue

## Summary

A player with the Spastic effect who turns into a silverscale statue is moved inside the statue object, but the spasms status effect only checks incapacitation traits, none of which the statue sets. The effect keeps ticking, and its "hit yourself" case calls `ClickOn(owner)`, which works from inside the statue. The fix makes the tick return early unless the owner is directly on a turf. It also stops arm spasms in any other container (lockers, mechs and so on); a maintainer may want to weigh that.

## The bug

A player with spasms who uses the silverscale "Become Statue" action expects to be safe until they break free. Instead they keep getting "Your arm spasms!" messages and punching themselves, taking damage every so often. The reporter was slapping themselves roughly every 30 seconds and slowly dying.

## Reproduction

On master, with admin tools:

1. Using the Lua editor, spawn a human, insert a `/obj/item/organ/tongue/lizard/silver` (the silverscale tongue) and take control of them.
2. Use the "Become Statue" action. It has a 3 second delay. No spasms were active yet.
3. Apply 30 copies of `/datum/status_effect/spasms` through Lua. The effect is `STATUS_EFFECT_MULTIPLE`, so stacking copies makes the 15% per-tick chance fire much more often and shortens the wait.
4. Wait inside the statue (`owner.loc` is the statue).

Observed: "Your arm spasms!" and "<name> punches you! / You punch <name>!" appeared twice within 10 seconds, and the owner's health dropped (health HUD went from state 1 to 2, with a brute damage overlay). A security bot nearby arrested the test mob after one spasm punch hit it; bots were then turned off with Lua to keep the test clean.

Stacking spasms was a shortcut to make the bug show quickly. The Spastic mutation itself was not used.

## Cause

`/datum/status_effect/spasms/tick()` (`code/datums/status_effects/debuffs/debuffs.dm:749`) only returns early for `incapacitated`, unconscious, `TRAIT_HANDS_BLOCKED` or `TRAIT_IMMOBILIZED`:

```
if(owner.incapacitated || IS_UNCONSCIOUS(owner) || HAS_TRAIT(owner, TRAIT_HANDS_BLOCKED) || HAS_TRAIT(owner, TRAIT_IMMOBILIZED))
	return
```

The statue action (`code/modules/surgery/organs/internal/tongue/_tongue.dm:276`, `/datum/action/cooldown/turn_to_statue/Activate`) only calls `owner.forceMove(statue)` (line 310) and sets none of those. In the tick, case 4 (`debuffs.dm:781-786`) calls `owner.ClickOn(owner)` in combat mode, which succeeds from inside a container. Case 1, the leg spasm, already required `isturf(owner.loc)` (line 756), which is why this was only noticed for arms.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 86828: spasms no longer fire while enclosed (silverscale statue)". It adds, after the existing early return in `tick()`:

```
// Enclosed in something (a silverscale statue, a locker...): no room to flail around
if(!isturf(owner.loc))
	return
```

This follows the leg-spasm case's own existing `isturf(owner.loc)` condition and fixes it for all cases at once, rather than adding a statue-specific trait in the tongue action.

## Verification

After rebuilding and restarting the test server, the same setup (30 stacked spasm effects, owner inside the statue) was left for 20 seconds: no spasm messages and no damage. The owner then broke free of the statue (the action's 0.5 second break-free); within 10 seconds they got "Your arm spasms" 9 times, "Your leg spasms" 11 times and 4 self punches, so the effect still works outside.

Not checked: the real Spastic mutation, lockers, mechs or other containers, and holding items or guns while spasming.

## Notes

- Side effect to weigh: spasms now never trigger while the owner is in any non-turf location (lockers, mechs, closets, body bags, vehicles). That is probably intended, but it is a behaviour change beyond the statue.
- Alternative: have the statue action apply a trait the tick already checks (for example `TRAIT_HANDS_BLOCKED`) for the duration. That would scope the change to the statue but leave other containers unchanged.
