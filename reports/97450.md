# #97450: Dog growl sound is never played

## Summary

`SFX_GROWL` is defined but nothing plays it. The behavior-tree version of the dog's harassment of hated mobs (felinids) only sent the "growls at" message. The fix adds a `playsound()` call next to that message.

## The bug

Dogs that growl at felinids give the message but make no sound.

## Reproduction

Natural harassment is rare (the behavior has a 0.1 random chance and a 10% per-tick probability); nothing happened in about four minutes of watching. Instead, on the test server with the Lua editor:

1. Spawned a corgi and a felinid (which has the hated-by-dogs trait) next to each other.
2. Before the fix, there is nothing to observe: `SFX_GROWL` is defined (`code/__DEFINES/sound.dm:228`, sound key at `code/game/sound/sound_keys/sound_keys.dm:570`) and referenced nowhere else in `code/`.

## Cause

In `/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/find_hated_dog_target/perform()` (`code/datums/ai/dog/dog_bt.dm:48`), once the dog picks a hated target it only calls `audible_message()` (`dog_bt.dm:67`) and sets the blackboard keys. The playsound was presumably lost when the AI was ported to behavior trees; the report suggests this but it was not checked in history.

## Fix

**Fix issue 97450: play the dog growl SFX when a dog growls at a hated mob.** Adds `playsound(dog, SFX_GROWL, 50, TRUE, -1)` after the message. The branch where the dog is buckled ("much too comfy to move", `dog_bt.dm:63`) stays silent since the dog doesn't act there.

## Verification

After rebuilding, with the dog adjacent to the felinid, called `find_hated_dog_target`'s `perform()` from the Lua editor (five times). Each call produced "...growls at ..., seemingly annoyed by his presence." and a sound of `sound/mobs/non-humanoids/dog/growl1.ogg` or `growl2.ogg`. No runtimes. Not checked: the sound during natural, unforced harassment.

## Notes

- The volume (50), extra range (-1) and the silent buckled branch are choices a maintainer may want to adjust.
- Calling `perform()` from Lua needed the targeting strategy passed as a typepath; a string name made the call fail. That was a Lua quirk, not a game bug.
