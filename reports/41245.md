# #41245: Health analyzer "Overall" damage ignores limb damage coefficients

| | |
|---|---|
| Issues | [#41245](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/41245): verified (fixed on the branch); [#55103](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/55103): verified (same cause, same fix) |
| Status | Reproduced on master, fix committed and checked in game |
| Fix branch | [fix/41245](https://github.com/sohpea-cloub/tgstation-slop/tree/fix/41245) |
| Tested on | tgstation master `87b93338`, 2026-10-23 |

## Summary

A carbon mob's `health` weights each bodypart's damage by that part's `body_damage_coeff` (2.5 for surplus prosthetics, 0.75 for ordinary limbs, 1 for head and chest). The health analyzer's "Overall" brute and burn row, however, was built from `get_brute_loss()` / `get_fire_loss()`, which are plain unweighted sums. For anyone with prosthetic limbs the "% healthy" line and the "Overall" row therefore disagree, which is what both issues describe. The fix adds `get_weighted_brute_loss()` and `get_weighted_fire_loss()` to carbons, uses them in `updatehealth()` so there is one definition of "damage that counts against health", and uses them for the analyzer's Overall row. The per-limb rows are left raw on purpose.

## The bug

#41245 (2018) reports that prosthetic limb damage is reported inconsistently and that "tons of stuff aren't factoring in the limb's `body_damage_coeff` multiplier", also mentioning `getBrute()` in general. #55103 (2020) shows a health analyzer readout whose total health percentage does not fit its damage numbers and wonders whether the way overall health is measured is at fault. A comment on #55103 points out that health analyzers read each limb's raw damage rather than how much that damage costs the human. Neither issue's screenshot was available to us, so this write-up relies on the issue text and on what we observed in game.

What a player sees: a person with a surplus prosthetic arm takes 10 brute to that arm. The analyzer says "75% healthy", yet the Overall row shows only 10 brute, which would imply about 90%. The 25 points of health that were actually lost are not visible anywhere in the damage table.

## Reproduction

Reproduced on master. The player action (hitting a prosthetic limb and scanning with a held analyzer) was not played out. Instead the same code path was exercised from the Lua editor on the test server, calling the global `healthscan()` proc that the analyzer and the physical scanner tablet app both use.

1. Start the test server and open the Lua editor as an admin.
2. Spawn a human (`/mob/living/carbon/human`).
3. Spawn a `/obj/item/bodypart/arm/left/robot/surplus` and call `replace_limb()` on the human to replace the left arm.
4. Call `receive_damage(10, 0)` on the human's left arm bodypart, then `updatehealth()` on the human.
5. Read the arm's `body_damage_coeff`, `brute_dam`, the human's `get_brute_loss()`, `health` and `maxHealth`, and call `healthscan(human, human, 1, 0, 0)` and read its output.

Observed on master:

- `body_damage_coeff` 2.5, arm `brute_dam` 10.
- `get_brute_loss()` returned 10.
- `health` was 75 out of 100 (the arm cost 10 x 2.5 = 25).
- The scan said "Overall status: 75% healthy", while the Overall row showed 10 brute and the left-arm row showed 10.

So the "% healthy" line and the Overall row came from two different definitions of damage.

## Cause

`/mob/living/carbon/updatehealth()` (`code/modules/mob/living/carbon/carbon.dm:426`) builds health from weighted damage:

```dm
total_brute += (BP.brute_dam * BP.body_damage_coeff)
total_burn += (BP.burn_dam * BP.body_damage_coeff)
```

(`carbon.dm:432-433`). The coefficients are defined in `code/__DEFINES/bodyparts.dm:23-26` (0.5 advanced robotic, 0.75 default, 1 head/torso, 2.5 surplus prosthesis).

`get_brute_loss()` and `get_fire_loss()` on carbons (`code/modules/mob/living/carbon/damage_procs.dm:33-43`) return the unweighted sums of `brute_dam` / `burn_dam`. `healthscan()` (`code/game/objects/items/devices/scanners/health_analyzer.dm:161-162`) stores those in `fire_loss` / `brute_loss`, prints "% healthy" from `target.health` (line 163), and prints `ceil(brute_loss)` / `ceil(fire_loss)` in the Overall row (about lines 241-244). The percentage is weighted and the table is not.

The "general `getBrute()`" complaint in #41245 is the same split: only `updatehealth()` and `get_organic_health()` (`carbon_defense.dm`, about line 536-539) apply the coefficient; every other reader of `get_brute_loss()` / `get_fire_loss()` sees the raw value.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 41245: health analyzer overall damage applies limb damage coefficients":

- `damage_procs.dm`: new `/mob/living/carbon/proc/get_weighted_brute_loss()` and `get_weighted_fire_loss()`. They sum `damage * body_damage_coeff` over `get_bodyparts()` and round with `DAMAGE_PRECISION`, like the existing getters.
- `carbon.dm`: `updatehealth()` now calls these instead of its own inline loop, so health and the analyzer cannot drift apart again.
- `health_analyzer.dm`: the Overall brute and burn cells use the weighted values. Tox and oxy are unchanged.

Design choice: `get_brute_loss()` / `get_fire_loss()` themselves were not changed. Healing and the `set_*_loss` procs work in raw bodypart units (`damage_procs.dm:87,104`), and other callers (the damage overlay at `carbon.dm:554`, defib tissue-damage checks at `carbon.dm:733`) rely on raw values, so weighting the getters would change healing and defib behavior. Per-limb rows in the analyzer were also kept raw, because that is the amount of damage that repair restores; the weighted figure is shown only on the Overall row.

## Verification

After rebuilding, three fresh humans each took 10 brute / 4 burn on one limb, and the scan output was read through the Lua editor:

| Damaged part (coeff) | health | `get_brute_loss()` / `get_fire_loss()` | Overall row (brute / burn) | Limb row |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Surplus prosthetic left arm (2.5) | 65 | 10 / 4 | 25 / 10 | 10 / 4 |
| Organic left arm (0.75) | 89.5 | 10 / 4 | 8 / 3 | 10 / 4 |
| Chest (1.0) | 86 | 10 / 4 | 10 / 4 | 10 / 4 |

Checks: the prosthetic's health 65 equals 100 - 25 - 10; the organic arm's Overall row shows 8 and 3, which is `ceil` of 7.5 and 3.0; the chest, with coefficient 1, is unchanged from before. No runtimes appeared that were related to the change.

Not checked: scanning with a held health analyzer item in the hand, the tablet's physical scanner app, the verbose scan mode, cyborg or non-human carbons, advanced robotic limbs (0.5), multiple damaged limbs at once, and any gameplay that reads the other raw `get_brute_loss()` callers. The test was run once with a single damaged limb per mob.

## Notes

- #55103: its comment says analyzers do not use the coefficient; that was still true on master `87b93338`, so the analyzer rework referenced in that area (PR #86666) did not fix it. The same commit covers both issues.
- Raw readers remain: examine text, self-check, the damage overlay, defib damage limits, and the "fake death" oxy padding in `healthscan()` (which still adds the raw sums, `health_analyzer.dm:165-168`). Whether any of these should become weighted is a maintainer decision; the issue's "`getBrute()` in general" wording suggests the reporter wanted a broader change than this commit makes.
- A maintainer may prefer showing the raw per-limb numbers alongside the weighted Overall (as now), weighting per-limb rows as well, or labeling the Overall row so the difference is clear. The commit takes the narrowest option that makes the Overall row consistent with "% healthy".
- Small behavioral difference: the weighted totals are now each rounded to `DAMAGE_PRECISION` before being subtracted in `updatehealth()`, whereas the old inline loop only rounded the final health value. This was not tested separately; it could only matter in the last rounding digit.
