## Summary

In 2021 smoking pipes had their own `process()` that skipped the cigarette's drag cooldown, so a pipe inhaled every `SSobj` tick and damaged lungs much faster than a cigarette. On current master `/obj/item/cigarette/pipe` is a subtype of `/obj/item/cigarette` and has no `process()` of its own, so it uses the cigarette's cooldown-gated drag. Testing a lit pipe and a lit cigarette side by side gave identical lung damage over time. No commit was needed.

## The bug

As reported (April 2021): cigarettes only inhale when a drag timer (`nextdragtime`) has elapsed, so they can do at most one point of lung damage per 10 seconds. The pipe's `process()` lacked that check, so it dragged about every 2 seconds, one drag per `SSobj` tick. The reporter estimated a pipe could destroy lungs in about 3.7 minutes, against more than 30 minutes of chain-smoking cigarettes. A commenter confirmed it was reproducible in 2021.

## Reproduction

The original bug no longer reproduces. The comparison run on master:

1. Started a test round, joined as an observer, opened the Lua editor.
2. Spawned two humans. Created a plain `/obj/item/cigarette` with 15 units of nicotine added, and a `/obj/item/cigarette/pipe` with its `smoketime` raised to 13 minutes and 100 units of nicotine added. Equipped one human with the cigarette and the other with the pipe in the mask slot, then lit both.
3. Read each human's lungs `damage` var with the Lua editor after waiting.

Observed:

| Time since lighting | Cigarette wearer lung damage | Pipe wearer lung damage |
|---|---|---|
| about 25 s | 1 | 1 |
| about 80 s | 4.1 | 4.1 |

The pipe's `smoketime` and reagent volume went down at the same pace as expected for its size, and the runtime log showed no errors from the smoking code (the log did contain unrelated runtimes caused by the test's Lua scripts while setting up the humans).

## Cause

The original defect was the pipe having a separate `process()` without the drag-cooldown check. On master:

- `code/game/objects/items/cigarettes.dm:942`: `/obj/item/cigarette/pipe` is declared as a subtype of `/obj/item/cigarette`. The pipe subtype (lines 942-1016) overrides `Initialize`, `update_name`, `put_out`, `item_interaction` and `attack_self`, but not `process()`.
- `cigarettes.dm:581-598`: the inherited `/obj/item/cigarette/process()` only calls `handle_reagents()` when `COOLDOWN_FINISHED(src, drag_cooldown)` is true and then starts the cooldown:

  ```
  if((reagents?.total_volume) && COOLDOWN_FINISHED(src, drag_cooldown))
  	COOLDOWN_START(src, drag_cooldown, dragtime)
  	handle_reagents(seconds_per_tick)
  ```

- `cigarettes.dm:225`: `dragtime` defaults to `10 SECONDS`, and `cigarettes.dm:237` gives the pipe the default `lung_harm = 1`, same as a plain cigarette. In `handle_reagents()` each drag does `lung_harm * smoker_resistance` lung damage (line 577) and, unless `smoke_all` is set, inhales `REAGENTS_METABOLISM` of reagents (line 551), so the per-drag harm matches a cigarette's.

## Fix

None needed. The inputs show that a pull request titled along the lines of "Pipes now i..." (#58126) was found in a search by issue number and was closed, not merged. The inputs do not say which later change turned pipes into cigarette subtypes, so how the fix reached master is not established here.

## Verification

Checked in game: the side-by-side comparison above (1 vs 1 at about 25 s, 4.1 vs 4.1 at about 80 s).

Not checked:

- A long run to lung failure (the reporter's 3.7 minute vs 30 minute figures were not re-measured).
- The special pipes: the cob pipe, and the crack pipe, which sets `lung_harm = 2` (`cigarettes.dm:1034`).
- Pipes with a `TRAIT_SMOKER` smoker (the 0.5 resistance multiplier) or lungs of other kinds.
- Pipes loaded with plant material, so a different reagent volume or `smoke_all` was not covered.

## Notes

- With lungs healing naturally, the damage rate measured here is bounded by the cigarette's 10-second drag timer, which is what the report asked for.
- The issue is still open on GitHub with the Bug label. It can likely be closed as fixed.
