## Summary

Three issues report limbs and paralysis state going out of sync: a paraplegic walking after losing legs (#81651), a hemiplegic character with a prosthetic arm losing both hands after a laughter demon released them (#78477), and robotic replacement legs not working after the legs were badly wounded (#78727). None could be reproduced on current master. Targeted tests of each scenario and a 560-operation randomized limb/trait test found no case where the usable-limb counts or paralysis traits disagreed with the limbs actually present. The code now treats missing limbs as stumps that are always paralysed and not counted, which was probably what the 2024 report lacked. No fix was made.

## The bug

- **#81651** (Feb 2024): with the paraplegic quirk, removing one or both legs lets the player walk.
- **#78477** (Sep 2023): a character with hemiplegia and a prosthetic limb used only one hand, was eaten and released by a laughter demon, and then could not use either hand. Re-attaching the arm did nothing; a completely new arm replacement fixed it.
- **#78727** (Oct 2023): after both legs were wounded until "limp and non-functional", robotic replacement legs also did not work. The reporter said they did not have the paraplegic quirk.

## Reproduction

All attempts used the Lua editor on a started round, with spawned humans. Per-zone traits were read from the limbs, along with `usable_legs`, `num_legs`, `usable_hands`, `bodypart_disabled` and the mob's `floored` and `handsblocked` status traits.

1. **#81651.** Gave a human the paraplegic trauma and dismembered the left leg, then the right. `usable_legs`/`num_legs` went 0/2, 0/1, 0/0. The floored status stayed on throughout and the body stayed lying down. A healthy control went 2/2, 1/1, 0/0. The bug did not occur.
2. **Hemiplegic checks.** With left hemiplegia, removing the healthy right leg gave 0/1 (correct). Removing the paralysed left leg gave 1/1, so the right leg still worked (correct). Removing the paralysed left arm left hands at 1/1 (correct).
3. **Replacement.** Paraplegic with a leg removed and a new organic leg attached: 0/2, so the new leg was disabled as it should be.
4. **Randomized test.** 560 random operations across 14 mobs: toggling per-zone paralysis traits, dismembering, `drop_limb` (normal and special), attaching organic, robotic and surplus limbs (normal and special), `del_and_replace_bodypart`, `revive` and `regenerate_limbs`. After each operation it checked that `usable_legs` and `usable_hands` equalled the number of non-disabled limbs, and that every limb whose zone trait was set was disabled. There were 0 violations.
5. **#78477.** Hemiplegic (left) with a surplus right arm, in both orders (prosthetic first, trauma first), then `revive` with two different flag values: usable hands 1/2, left disabled, right usable. Then 1000 brute damage to the surplus arm (disabled, hands 0/2), death, and `revive(ADMIN_HEAL_ALL)`: the arm was re-enabled, hands 1/2, no `handsblocked`. This approximates what the laughter demon does to a victim. The demon itself was not used.
6. **#78727.** Gave both legs bone and critical wounds (usable legs 0/2), dismembered both and attached robotic legs: usable 2/2, not floored, not disabled.

## Cause

None found. The code read explains why the reported states do not occur now:

- Arms and legs track their owner's per-zone paralysis traits. `apply_ownership`, `clear_ownership` and the gain/loss handlers (`code/modules/surgery/bodyparts/default_parts.dm:289-318` for arms, with the same pattern for legs) add or remove `TRAIT_PARALYSIS` on the limb as the owner's trait appears or goes.
- The usable-limb counts only include non-stump limbs: `set_disabled` for legs at `default_parts.dm:417-430`, and `add_bodypart`/`remove_bodypart` in `carbon.dm` skip stumps via `IS_STUMP`. A missing limb is now a stump that always has `TRAIT_PARALYSIS` and is not counted. The paraplegic and hemiplegic traumas set these traits in `code/datums/brain_damage/severe.dm:81-105`.

The issues are from 2023-09/10 and 2024-02, and the test tree is from 2026-10. No closing PR was found, and #81651 is still open on GitHub, so which change fixed the behaviour (if one did) is unknown.

## Fix

None.

## Verification

Not applicable beyond the reproduction attempts above. Not checked: a live laughter demon eating and releasing a player; real surgery or a robotic technician's workflow (limbs were removed and attached with direct calls); #78727's original cause (a wizard's laser eyes mangling the legs; wounds were applied directly); a real client playing any scenario.

## Notes

- **#78477 original cause is unknown.** One thing seen that could produce a lasting disabled arm: a surplus robotic arm stays disabled while its damage is at or above half the disable threshold, and it is not re-enabled automatically unless the damage is healed. It was not reproduced after a full revive.
- **Unverified side suspicion.** `can_be_disabled` may not be restored on a limb moved from an owner with `TRAIT_NOLIMBDISABLE` to a normal owner (`code/modules/surgery/bodyparts/_bodyparts.dm:1037-1043`). The attempt to trigger it did not toggle it, so this is unconfirmed and may be a wrong trait guess.
- Wounding a fresh human from Lua hits a known `stack_trace` in `_scars.dm:86`. This is a side effect of the test method.
- Maintainers may want to close #81651 and #78727 as no longer reproducible; #78477 was a one-off report with no known recipe.
