## Summary

The AI could see what was on tiles that no camera covers. Shift-click was already blocked by a camera visibility check, but the Examine verb (right-click menu and chat links) goes through `mob/proc/run_examinate`, which has no such check, and the AI's `sight` flags include `SEE_OBJS`, so it worked on objects under camera static. The fix adds an AI override of `run_examinate` that refuses atoms the AI cannot see.

## The bug

The reporter, playing as the AI, shift-right-clicked a tile to see its contents and got the list even though the tile was not on cameras. A comment noted that blind mobs seem able to do the same.

## Reproduction

On the test server, with admin tools (observer, then the Lua editor):

1. Turned a human into an AI, gave the AI a player's control, and moved its eye to 107,144.
2. Spawned a pocket crowbar on 116,137, a tile in the Captain's Quarters shower that cameras do not cover (`SScameras.is_visible_by_cameras` returned 0 for it; Lua shows 0 rather than false).
3. Shift-clicked the off-camera tile: nothing appeared in chat (this path is already gated at `code/_onclick/ai.dm:46-47`: `if(!can_see(A)) return`). Shift-clicking a holopad that is visible to cameras did examine it.
4. Used the Examine verb on the crowbar on the off-camera tile.

Result on master: the Examine verb printed the crowbar's full examine text. So the shift-click path was already correct; the verb path was the bug.

## Cause

`/mob/proc/run_examinate` (`code/modules/mob/mob.dm:556`) has no camera or visibility check for non-turf atoms. The AI has `sight = SEE_TURFS | SEE_MOBS | SEE_OBJS` (`code/modules/mob/living/silicon/ai/ai_defines.dm:10`), so BYOND lets the verb and right-click menu target objects even when camera static covers them. The loot-panel alt-click path is already gated by `can_see` in the AI click handler.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 77607: AI can no longer Examine objects on tiles hidden by camera static". In `code/modules/mob/living/silicon/ai/ai.dm` it adds an override:

```dm
/mob/living/silicon/ai/run_examinate(atom/examinify, force_examinate_more = FALSE)
	if(client && examinify != src && examinify != eyeobj && !can_see(examinify))
		return
	return ..()
```

The AI itself and its eye are exempt. Otherwise it calls the normal examine only when `can_see()` passes, the same check the click path already uses. A change to `can_see_loot_turf` (taken from another commit) was considered and found unnecessary: the AI has `SEE_OBJS`, so that check would return TRUE anyway.

## Verification

After rebuilding, the Examine verb on the off-camera crowbar produced no reply, and the same verb on the holopad (visible to cameras) printed its normal text.

Not checked: other verbs in the right-click menu on off-camera objects (they may leak information in the same way), turfs and mobs hidden by static through other paths, and a real AI player's client (the test used a possessed AI through admin tools).

## Notes

- The comment that blind mobs can do the same is a separate matter and was not touched.
- The `client &&` condition leaves non-player (clientless) calls unchanged.
