## Summary

Neither report reproduced on master. A slimeperson's Swap Body UI and Split Body action kept working after a disconnect and reconnect, and Blood Magic survived a split for a cultist. The HUD stayed intact after swapping into a split body wearing SWAT gear. The reconnect and cult symptoms were probably caused by mind-transfer bugs that have since been fixed (PR #76931 is the candidate; this was inferred from the code, not confirmed). The "everything goes black" report in #73263 could not be judged with two bodies.

## The bugs as reported

- #76671: after reconnecting (or closing and reopening the game) as a slimeperson, the player can no longer open the swap UI or split. A cultist who splits also loses Blood Magic. A commenter linked the cult part to #76746.
- #73263: with a split body wearing SWAT gear and a mask, swapping bodies makes the screen go black and the buttons disappear, so the player cannot swap again. The number of bodies needed was unknown ("quite a few"). A commenter suspected a plane (rendering) problem.

## Reproduction attempts

On master, with admin tools:

1. Started the round, observed, and in the Lua editor spawned a human, set the species to slime (`/datum/species/jelly/slime`), set blood volume to 1500 and possessed it. (The first Split click did nothing until blood reached the split threshold, `BLOOD_VOLUME_SLIME_SPLIT`, 1120.)
2. Clicked the "Split Body" button. The do_after completed, the message "you're besides yourself" appeared, a new body existed, and Regenerate, Split and Swap buttons were on screen.
3. Disconnected and reconnected (the reported trigger). The player kept the same mob with its full set of screen objects. "Swap Body" opened the body swapper UI listing both bodies, swapping worked and rebuilt the HUD with its buttons, and swapping back and forth twice worked.
4. For #73263, equipped the other body with a SWAT helmet, SWAT armor, SWAT gas mask, SWAT combat boots and combat gloves, and swapped into it through the UI. Health, health doll, Swap Body and Split Body were still on screen. Split was greyed only because blood was below the threshold, which is expected.
5. For the cult part of #76671, gave the slimeperson the cult antagonist datum and called `make_dupe()`. The new body had "Prepare Blood Magic" and "Blood Sense" as well as Split and Swap.

## Cause analysis

No defect was found. For the reconnect symptom: `/mob/living/Logout()` sets `mind.active = FALSE` when the key and mind separate (`code/modules/mob/living/logout.dm:5`), and `/mob/living/Login()` restores it through `sync_mind` (`code/datums/mind/_mind.dm:530-533`). The swap UI and `can_swap` check `mind.active` (`code/modules/mob/living/carbon/human/species_types/jellypeople.dm:464` and `:495`), so a mind that failed to re-sync would break exactly as reported. It worked in testing. Each slime body gets its own swap and split actions in `on_species_gain` (`jellypeople.dm:255-260`). The failure in 2023 most likely came from mind-transfer bugs since fixed, possibly PR #76931 and the related #76746 work; this was not verified by bisecting.
