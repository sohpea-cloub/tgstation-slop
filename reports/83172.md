## Summary
Neither report could be reproduced on master. A legion spawned on the station z-level attacked and infected a human and a monkey, and baby goliaths from a goliath broodmother attacked and killed a human on the station. The old behaviour in #83172 was probably basic-mob AI going to sleep away from lavaland, but master now keeps AI running for mobs standing in station or shuttle areas. #87248 concerns a `simple_animal` mob that has no z-level logic, and no defect was found in it.

## The bug
### #83172
Reported in May 2024: legions worked on lavaland but did not attack the reporter or monkeys on the station level.

### #87248
Reported in October 2024 (labelled Performance): when a goliath broodmother is on the station and summons baby goliaths, the babies often do not attack or explode. A comment says they do explode on death but do not attack as often as they should.

## Reproduction
Both were attempted on a MetaStation round (station z-level 2) with an observer present, using the Lua editor to spawn mobs.

### #83172
1. Spawn a `/mob/living/basic/mining/legion` in a station hallway, with a human a few tiles away. An existing monkey was nearby.
2. Observed: the chat showed "The legion bites ..." repeatedly, then "legion burrows into the flesh of ...". The target seized up and a new legion stood up. Later the legion also attacked the monkey and other crew, and legion brood land objects appeared.
3. Result: legions attack and infect on the station z-level. Not reproduced.

### #87248
1. Spawn a `/mob/living/simple_animal/hostile/asteroid/elite/broodmother` and a human a few tiles away, and call `spawn_children(human)`. The babies dug tentacles under the human, grabbed them and bashed them; the human died.
2. Second run: a broodmother and a human, calling `spawn_children()` with no target. This is what `OpenFire` does for a client-controlled mother. Both babies acquired the human on their own (their AI had the human as target). The human's health later went well below zero and they were dead. The babies exploded on death, as designed.
3. Result: not reproduced.

## Cause
No defect was found. Notes on the code at master:

- #83172: the likely old cause was AI sleeping off lavaland. `get_active_ai_status()` in `code/datums/ai/_ai_controller.dm:481` now starts with "AI actually standing on the station or a shuttle always stays high priority": a pawn in an `/area/station` or `/area/shuttle` returns `AI_STATUS_ON`. Elsewhere the AI runs only with a nearby client, or for `RUN_WHILE_UNWATCHED` controllers. So a legion in space with no client nearby would still sleep; that is intended performance behaviour, not a bug. The previous behaviour was inferred from the code, not observed.
- #87248: the broodmother and its children are `simple_animal/hostile` mobs (`code/modules/mob/living/simple_animal/hostile/mining_mobs/elites/goliath_broodmother.dm`), not basic-mob AI, and have no z-level or station logic. `spawn_children()` (line 130) is called without a target from `OpenFire` (lines 90 and 101), so the babies always rely on their own target search; this worked in the test. The faction copy to the children is correct. The 2024 report used a 515 client with several test merges and a Performance label, so it may have been lag or since-changed code.

## Fix
None; there was nothing to fix.

## Verification
Not applicable (no change). The not-reproduced results above are the evidence.
