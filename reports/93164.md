## Summary

Two independent defects leave a living mob with a stale gravity state. `/mob/living/forceMove` refreshed gravity before moving, so teleports (the hand teleporter) evaluated the old location; and `/mob/living/Moved` only compared the areas' `default_gravity`, ignoring the `NO_GRAVITY` area flag that `has_gravity()` also uses, so walking between a flagged area and the station never refreshed. The visible result is a permanent Weightless alert on the station. The fixes move the refresh after the move and add the `NO_GRAVITY` flag to the area comparison. #38643, a 2018 report about simple animals, did not reproduce.

## The bug

#93164: teleporting with the hand teleporter to the ordnance bomb test site and straight back leaves the player with the Weightless status although the station has gravity. The reporter adds that they could still walk normally but could not traverse Z-levels (stairs, tram). A comment adds that on Meta the airlock above the auxiliary base does the same when stepping onto the station.

#88870: stepping onto station gravity tiles does not refresh the "no-slowdown" status (the issue is a video only; the title is the whole text).

#38643: in 2018, after the gravity generator was switched off and on again, simple animals kept their floating animation.

## Reproduction

All steps used the Lua editor on a round on Kilo Station, with a human and a corgi as test subjects. Teleporting was done by calling `forceMove` (which is what the hand teleporter uses) rather than by using a hand teleporter item.

**#93164 (teleport):**

1. Put a human at the Central Primary Hallway (107,144,2), then `forceMove` it to the Ordnance Bomb Site (136,22,2), then `forceMove` it back.
2. Observed on master: at the bomb site the mob's gravity state was still `1` (stale; it should be `0`), and after returning to the hallway it was `0` with `has_gravity()` true and the Weightless alert present. One further `forceMove` to the same tile corrected it, showing the one-move lag.

**#88870 (walking):**

1. Set the `NO_GRAVITY` area flag (16384) on the hallway area and call `refresh_gravity()` on a human standing there; its gravity state became `0`.
2. Walk the human from (100,113) to (100,112), into the Medbay Lobby (a normal station area).
3. Observed on master: the gravity state stayed `0` although the tile has gravity (`has_gravity()` true).

**#38643:** a corgi (a basic mob) went through the same sequences after the fix and refreshed gravity exactly like the human. Before the fix it was not separately tested. The simple-animal code from the 2018 report no longer exists. The report was therefore not reproduced; whether the corgi was affected by the two bugs above before the fix was not recorded.

## Cause

1. `code/modules/mob/living/living.dm:1967-1976`, `/mob/living/forceMove`:

   ```dm
   	refresh_gravity()
   	. = ..()
   ```

   `refresh_gravity()` ran before the move, so `has_gravity()` was evaluated at the old location. A teleport therefore left the state of the place the mob came from. `Moved()` did not catch it either, because it only refreshes when the old and new areas differ in `default_gravity`, and both the station areas and the bomb site have `ZERO_GRAVITY`.

2. `code/modules/mob/living/living_movement.dm:23-27`, `/mob/living/Moved`:

   ```dm
   if(old_area.default_gravity != new_area.default_gravity)
   	refresh_gravity()
   ```

   But `has_gravity()` (`code/game/atom/_atom.dm:~803`) also returns false for areas with `area_flags & NO_GRAVITY` (e.g. solars, "nearstation" areas, the ordnance bomb site). Moving between a `NO_GRAVITY`-flagged area and the station, where `default_gravity` is the same, never triggered a refresh.

The reporter's claim about not being able to traverse Z-levels was not tested; it would follow from the stale Weightless trait.

## Fix

Two commits on the fix branch:

- "Fix issue 93164: Refresh living gravity after forceMove, not before": in `/mob/living/forceMove`, `refresh_gravity()` is called after `. = ..()` instead of before, with a comment that gravity depends on where the mob is now.
- "Fix issue 88870: Refresh living gravity when walking between areas that differ in NO_GRAVITY": `Moved()` now also refreshes when `(old_area.area_flags & NO_GRAVITY) != (new_area.area_flags & NO_GRAVITY)`.

Both are minimal and match how `has_gravity()` is defined.

## Verification

After rebuilding and restarting, the same sequences were run on both a human and a corgi:

- Both start with gravity state `1`; after `forceMove` to the bomb site they had state `0`, `has_gravity()` false and the Weightless alert; after `forceMove` back they had state `1`, `has_gravity()` true and no alert.
- The human, made weightless by the flag on the hallway, walked out to the Medbay Lobby and ended with state `1`, gravity true and no Weightless alert.
- No new runtimes were seen in that session.

Not checked: using an actual hand teleporter item or portals; the Meta auxiliary-base airlock case; Z-level traversal while stale; the gravity generator being switched off and on (#38643's scenario) on a corgi or any simple/basic mob.

## Notes

- The second fix is the same one-line change as upstream PR #98054 (closed, not merged), according to the investigation.
- The Meta auxiliary-base airlock case from the comment on #93164 is very likely the second bug (a `NO_GRAVITY`-flagged area next to the station); it was not tested on Meta.
- #38643 is left as not reproduced: it is from 2018 and describes simple animals, a code path that has since been replaced by basic mobs.
