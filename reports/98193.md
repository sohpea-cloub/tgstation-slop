## Summary
The issue says that interrupting weather (with the admin verb or the weather tower) leaves the ending sound effects playing for far too long. On rain storms, the wind-down phase loop inherited the rain intro sound (12.5 s of `rain_start.ogg`) from its parent loop, so winding down first replayed the intro before the actual `rain_end.ogg` loop. The fix clears the inherited start and end sounds on the three rain phase loops. This explains part of the report. Ending the weather directly stopped the rain sound immediately, so the reported lingering was not reproduced for rain. A case for ash storms was found and left alone (see Notes).

## The bug
When a rain storm winds down, players expect the rain to fade out with the ending sound. Instead the wind-down plays the rain intro sound first, then the ending sound, and the ending sound repeats until the weather finally ends 30 seconds later.

## Reproduction
Done on the test server as an observer (ghost) in an outdoor area, using admin tools.

1. In the Lua editor, start rain on z-level 2: `dm.global_vars.SSweather:run_weather(dm.global_procs._text2path('/datum/weather/particle/rain_storm'), 2)`. The telegraph phase lasts 30 s. Indoor areas are not affected by weather and play no weather sound, so the hallway used was set to `outdoors = 1`.
2. After the rain reached its main stage, call `wind_down()` on the weather datum (the same call the weather tower makes).
3. Watch the sounds sent to the client. Before the fix: `wind_down()` at 02:05:54, `rain_start.ogg` at 02:05:57, `rain_end.ogg` at 02:06:09.
4. Separately, ending the rain in its main stage with `end()` (from Lua, `w['end'](w)`, because `end` is a Lua keyword) stopped the rain sound channel immediately. This is the same path as the admin verb "Stop All Active Weather", which calls `end()` directly. The lingering end sound described in the issue did not occur here.

A natural ash storm may already be in `SSweather.processing`; filter by name when picking the weather to interrupt.

## Cause
In `code/datums/looping_sounds/weather.dm:101`, `/datum/looping_sound/rain` defines `start_sound = rain_start.ogg` (`start_length = 12.5 SECONDS`) and `end_sound = rain_end.ogg`. Its subtypes `/start`, `/middle` and `/end` (lines 110, 114, 118) only override `mid_sounds` and `mid_length`, so they inherited both. A phase loop therefore played the parent's start sound before its own looping sound (`_looping_sound.dm:289`). For the wind-down phase that meant `rain_start.ogg` first (12.5 s), then `rain_end.ogg`, repeating every 17 s until `end()` ran 30 s later. The middle phase loop likewise opened with `rain_start.ogg` instead of `rain_mid.ogg`.

## Fix
One commit, "Fix issue 98193: rain phase loops no longer inherit start/end sounds", sets `start_sound = null` and `end_sound = null` on `/datum/looping_sound/rain/start`, `/middle` and `/end`, with a comment that the weather chooses the phase, so the parent's intro and outro would play on top of the phase's own sound. The weather system already switches loops per phase, so each phase loop only needs its own `mid_sounds`.

## Verification
After rebuilding and restarting the test server, with the same steps (observer mob):
- `wind_down()` at 02:16:28 stopped the old `rain_mid` loop. `rain_end.ogg` played at 02:16:33 and again at 02:16:50 (the 17 s loop). It stopped at 02:16:57 when `end()` ran. No `rain_start.ogg` played during the wind-down.
- No new runtimes.

Not checked: snow, sand, void or radiation storms; `wind_down()` followed immediately by `end()`; `wind_down()` during the telegraph phase; the weather tower with a living player (all tests used an observer).

## Notes
- The remaining lag after `wind_down()` is a separate behavior. `change_the_track()` (`code/datums/components/area_sound_manager.dm:58`) delays the new loop until the old loop's timer expires, so up to about 15 s of silence can pass before the ending sound starts. This was left unchanged.
- Ash storm loops use random sound channels, so `end()` cannot stop an already playing 10-14 s file. They are not stopped immediately, and no ending sound is played on `end()`. Reserving a random channel would fix this but would hard-cut overlapping tails, so it was not done.
- Snow storms play `snow_end.ogg` (about 9.4 s) through `send_alert()` on the weather channel. This was not tested.
- The reported symptom is therefore only partly explained: for rain the long ending came from the replayed intro, but no other cause of a lingering ending sound was reproduced.
