## Summary

Neither #89675 (gas mask invisible after removing a helmet that hides masks) nor #93173 (waistcoat drawn twice on a monkey) could be reproduced on current master, though only the simplest variants were tried. #95610 (every mask gets the gas mask's red pepper-spray eyes) was traced to the default `dirt_state` of `/obj/item/clothing/mask/gas`. Two masks that keep `PEPPERPROOF` but do not look like a gas mask (the security gas mask and the tactical clown mask) inherited that overlay. The fix gives those two masks no dirt overlay, and makes one condition in the dirt component consistent with its sibling handler.

## #89675: gas mask invisible after removing a helmet

### The bug
The report: wear a helmet that conceals the mask (space suit or atmos helmet), put on a gas mask, remove the helmet, and the mask is invisible. A comment adds that jumpsuits sometimes behave the same way under hardsuit-like armor (drake armor, explorer suit) until the jumpsuit is adjusted.

### Reproduction
Not reproduced. Using the Lua editor on the test server, humans were spawned and the following checked:

1. Gas mask worn, then an EVA helmet or welding helmet worn over it. `obscured_slots` included `HIDEMASK` (5104). The helmet was removed both by dropping it and by moving it to a hand. `obscured_slots` went to 4832 (no `HIDEMASK`) and the mob's overlays included the gas mask overlay (`gas_alt`) again. Hardhat and hardsuit helmets were also tried with the same outcome.
2. Explorer suit with its hood toggled up and down through the attached-clothing component, then the suit dropped: the obscured state was correct and both the jumpsuit and gas mask overlays were present.

The relevant code path is `add_item_coverage` / `refresh_obscured` / `item_coverage_changed` in `code/modules/mob/living/carbon/inventory.dm:143-168`, which calls `update_clothing`, then `update_worn_mask` in `code/modules/mob/living/carbon/human/human_update_icons.dm:388` (it returns early while `HIDEMASK` is in `obscured_slots`). The state was refreshed correctly in every case tried, so the bug may already have been fixed by that refresh logic. This was not confirmed from history.

Not tried: MOD suit sealing (`seal_part`, `code/modules/mod/mod_activation.dm:292`), chameleon clothing, a real strip of the helmet by another player through the HUD, drake armor, and the jumpsuit-under-hardsuit case from the comment. The report may still exist in one of those paths.

## #93173: waistcoat on a monkey drawn twice

### The bug
Attaching a waistcoat to a jumpsuit while a monkey reportedly draws it twice, and removing it unrenders only one copy.

### Reproduction
Not reproduced. In the Lua editor a human was given a grey jumpsuit and made a monkey with `monkeyize()`, then a waistcoat was attached with `attach_accessory`. Rendering the mob at 8x scale showed one waistcoat. `pop_accessory` removed it completely and the render was clean again.

The accessory overlay comes from `generate_accessory_overlay` (`code/modules/clothing/under/accessories/_accessories.dm:115`) via `worn_overlays` in `code/modules/clothing/under/_under.dm:116`. That code does not handle body shape, but no duplicate appeared.

Not tried: attaching the waistcoat before `monkeyize()` (the reporter noted it might need to have been worn by a human first, and that the waistcoat was found on the floor), attaching by a real item click with a monkey as the user, and species change with an accessory attached, which could cause a double update.

## #95610: pepper spray overlay on masks that are not gas masks

### The bug
After being pepper sprayed, a player's ordinary mask showed the gas mask's red-eye overlay drawn over it.

### Reproduction
Pepper spray itself was not used. Using the Lua editor, humans were spawned, each mask equipped to the mask slot, and the mask's `clothing_dirt` component set directly (`dirtiness` 2, red `dirt_color`), then the slot icon updated and the mob's overlays inspected.

1. Security gas mask (sechailer): overlays were `[sechailer]` plus `[gas_dirt]` in `#cc0000`, the gas mask's red eyes drawn on a mask of a different shape.
2. Tactical clown mask (clownops): it also had a `gas_dirt` state on its component.

Plain gas masks and atmos masks legitimately show `gas_dirt`. The issue body is only a title and a four-line complaint, so the exact masks the reporter wore are unknown. Per the investigation, most other `/obj/item/clothing/mask/gas` subtypes (clown, mime, owl and so on) set `flags_cover` without `PEPPERPROOF` and get no component, so the problem is mainly these two masks and any other masks that have `PEPPERPROOF` but a different sprite.

### Cause
`/obj/item/clothing/mask/gas` has `var/dirt_state = "gas_dirt"` and in `Initialize` adds the component for any mask with `PEPPERPROOF` while `pepper_tint` is set (`code/modules/clothing/masks/gasmask.dm:36-48`). `on_separate_worn_overlays` (`code/datums/components/clothing_dirt.dm:93-102`) then draws that state from the mask's own worn icon. Only `gas_dirt`, `plague_dirt` and `welding_dirt` exist in the mask icon file, so a mask that keeps `PEPPERPROOF` but has a different shape (the security gas mask at `code/modules/clothing/masks/hailer.dm:57-61`, the tactical clown mask at `gasmask.dm:352-358`) gets the gas mask sprite.

A second, related defect is in `on_overlays_updated` (`clothing_dirt.dm:85`):

```
if (!dirtiness || !dirt_state && !(source.flags_cover & PEPPERPROOF))
```

Because of operator precedence this only skips when there is no `dirt_state` and no `PEPPERPROOF`. With a null `dirt_state` on a pepperproof mask it would fall through and build an overlay from the item's whole icon file with a null state.

### Fix
Commit "Fix issue 95610: don't draw the gas mask pepper overlay on masks it doesn't fit":

- `dirt_state = null` on the security gas mask (`hailer.dm`) and on the tactical clown mask (`gasmask.dm`). The component and the vision tint still apply; only the mismatched overlay is gone.
- In `on_overlays_updated`, the condition became `!dirtiness || !dirt_state || !(source.flags_cover & PEPPERPROOF)`, matching `on_separate_worn_overlays`, so a null state draws nothing instead of the whole item icon.

The alternative of setting `pepper_tint = FALSE` on those masks was not chosen because it would remove the vision tint as well.

### Verification
After rebuilding, the security gas mask's overlays were `[sechailer]` only. Plain gas mask (`[gas_alt]` + `[gas_dirt]`) and atmos mask (`[gas_atmos]` + `[gas_dirt]`) were unchanged, and the plague doctor and welding masks kept their own states.

Not checked: the tactical clown mask's worn overlay after the fix (same mechanism, not inspected), the item-icon (inventory) overlay path that `on_overlays_updated` draws, and real pepper spray exposure (the component values were set directly).

## Notes

- Captain and CentCom atmos masks still use `gas_dirt`; their shape is similar so this looks intended.
- Any new `/obj/item/clothing/mask/gas` subtype with `PEPPERPROOF` and a non-gas-mask sprite needs its own `dirt_state` (or `null`).
- #89675 and #93173 are best treated as unconfirmed until someone tries the untested paths listed above; no fix was written for either.
