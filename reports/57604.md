## Summary

The three issues are about reagents that are the liquid form of a gas (plasma, oxygen, nitrogen, carbon dioxide, nitrous oxide) and when they turn into gas. #57604 (liquid plasma does not become gas when spilled or heated) did not reproduce on master: heating boils plasma and a hot spill releases gas, and at room temperature a spill releases nothing on purpose. #49605 (splashing gas reagents on people or walls releases nothing) was reproduced and fixed with a shared `release_gas()` helper. #88807 (weakened virus plasma evaporating in virology) was traced to the virus plasma reagents inheriting liquid plasma's boil-off, and a flag now disables it for them.

## The bug

- **#57604.** The reporter, in 2021, could not make liquid plasma turn into gas by spilling a beaker, throwing it at a wall, or heating it above 50 C, and found that doing both worked. A commenter pointed at plasma's temperature-change signal never being registered, and a 2024 comment said heating works on current code while spilling does not.
- **#49605.** Gas-form reagents release their gas when splashed on a floor, but not when splashed or thrown at a human or a wall.
- **#88807.** Mixing plasma, viral food and synaptizine to make weakened virus plasma sometimes destroys the mix: the plasma "evaporates" and only viral plasma stays in the flask. Commenters suspected a temperature of about 300 K or more.

## Reproduction

All steps used an observer with the Lua editor on the test server. Beakers were created and filled through Lua (`SS13.new` and `add_reagent`). Lua cannot index `air.moles[...]`, so released gas was measured as the change in `turf.air:total_moles()` around a call to `splash_reagents()`. The splashes were therefore triggered by calling the proc, not by a player throwing a beaker.

### #57604 (not reproduced)

1. Fill a beaker with 30u plasma and call `set_temperature(400)`. The beaker's plasma went from 30 to 0 (it boiled off and the gas ignited). The old explanation, that `on_new` is not called without data, no longer applies: `code/modules/reagents/chemistry/holder/holder.dm:171` calls `new_reagent.on_new(data)` for every new reagent.
2. Splash 20u plasma at `chem_temp` 330 on a floor: about +20 mol of gas.
3. Splash plasma at 300 K on a floor: +0 mol.

### #49605 (reproduced)

1. Fill a beaker with 100u nitrous oxide and splash it on a floor: about +5 mol. 
2. Splash it on a human, thrown and not thrown: 0 and 0.5 mol (the 0.5 is the part that landed on the turf under the target, not the part that hit the mob).
3. Throw it at a reinforced wall: the beaker emptied and nothing was released.

### #88807 (cause found, round conditions not reproduced)

1. Put 20u virus plasma and 20u weakened virus plasma in a beaker and call `set_temperature(400)`. Both were 0 afterwards: they boiled off as plasma gas.
2. Mix 10u + 10u of viral food and plasma with synaptizine at 300 K, and 100u + 100u + 50u in a bluespace beaker: the mix stayed at 300 K and was fine. No reaction in the tested recipe raised the temperature, so what heated the mix in the reporter's round was not identified. Any heat above 50 C destroys it, which matches the commenters' guess.

## Cause

**#57604.** Liquid plasma is meant to boil above `LIQUID_PLASMA_BP`, which is 50 C (`code/modules/reagents/chemistry/reagents/toxin_reagents.dm:85`). It does so in two places: `on_temp_change()` (`:130`) boils the reagent when the holder heats up, and `expose_turf()` (`:142`) spawns plasma gas on the turf when the reagent is spilled at or above that temperature:

```dm
/datum/reagent/toxin/plasma/expose_turf(turf/open/exposed_turf, reac_volume)
	if(!istype(exposed_turf))
		return
	var/temp = holder ? holder.chem_temp : T20C
	if(temp >= LIQUID_PLASMA_BP)
		exposed_turf.atmos_spawn_air(...)
```

Both worked in testing. A spill below the boiling point does nothing, which is the same threshold the heating path uses. The wiki wording ("spilled or heated") from the issue does not match the code: the code needs it to be hot, and spilling alone does not vaporise it.

**#49605.** Gas reagents (oxygen `other_reagents.dm:999`, nitrogen `:1032`, carbon dioxide `:1681`, nitrous oxide `:1697`, and plasma at `toxin_reagents.dm:142`) release gas only in `expose_turf()`. `splash_reagents()` (`code/modules/reagents/reagent_containers.dm:239`) sends a non-thrown splash on a mob to the mob only (`expose_mob()`, which releases no gas), and when a thrown container hits a wall the splash goes to a closed turf, which fails the `istype(exposed_turf)` check on an open turf.

**#88807.** `/datum/reagent/toxin/plasma/plasmavirusfood` and its `/weak` subtype (`other_reagents.dm:2663` and `:2671`) are subtypes of liquid plasma. They inherit `on_new()` (which registers `on_temp_change`, `toxin_reagents.dm:106-108`) and `expose_turf()`, so they boil off into plasma gas once the holder is at or above 323 K, destroying the mix.

## Fix

Two commits.

**"Fix issue 88807: virus plasma no longer boils off into plasma gas".** Adds a `boils_into_gas` variable to liquid plasma (default `TRUE`). `on_new()` registers the temperature signal only if it is set, and `expose_turf()` returns early if it is not. The two virus plasma subtypes set it to `FALSE`. An alternative would be to move the boiling into a separate subtype, but a flag keeps the type tree unchanged for anything that checks for plasma.

**"Fix issue 49605: gas reagents splashed on mobs and walls release their gas".** Adds `/datum/reagent/proc/release_gas(target, gas_id, moles)` in `code/modules/reagents/chemistry/reagents.dm`. The gas goes to the target's own turf if it is open (a floor, or whatever stands on it, like a mob). For a wall it goes to the open turf the container is in, if that is adjacent to the wall. Oxygen, nitrogen, carbon dioxide and nitrous oxide now use it in `expose_turf()` and in a new or extended `expose_mob()` for the `TOUCH` method, and liquid plasma uses it too (still only at or above the boiling point), including on mobs. The amounts stay the same as before (`reac_volume / 20` moles for the four gases, `reac_volume` for plasma).

## Verification

After rebuilding, the same Lua checks on the test server gave:

- Plasma at 330 K on a floor: +20 mol. At 300 K: 0 (unchanged).
- Virus plasma at 330 K on a floor and on a human: 0. Virus plasma and weakened virus plasma at 400 K kept their 20u each. Liquid plasma at 400 K still boiled to 0.
- Nitrous oxide, oxygen, nitrogen and carbon dioxide on a human: +5 mol each. Plasma at 330 K on a human: +20 mol. Plasma at 300 K on a human: 0.
- A container on the adjacent floor, thrown at a reinforced wall: nitrous oxide +5 mol, plasma at 330 K +20 mol, plasma at 300 K 0.
- No new runtimes, apart from two caused by the Lua script's own bad access to a wall's air.

Not checked: a real round in which a player makes weakened virus plasma and what heated it; windows and airlocks (not changed, since their gas already goes onto their turf); actual player throws, as opposed to calling `splash_reagents()` from Lua.

## Notes

- Whether plasma at room temperature should vaporise when spilled (the title of #57604) is a design decision. It was left as it is, and the fix keeps the existing 50 C threshold for spills on mobs and walls too.
- Splashing a mob now creates gas on the mob's turf only for the `TOUCH` method, so ingesting or inhaling is unaffected.
- #49605 asked that gas should not appear inside windows and airlocks. That behaviour was not changed.
- The reporter's edit in #57604 ("doing both works") fits the code: a hot spill is the only spill that releases plasma gas.
