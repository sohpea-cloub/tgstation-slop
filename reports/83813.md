# #83813: A transfer valve worn on the back survives its own explosion when the wearer has a bomb suit

## Summary

A tank transfer valve (TTV) worn on the back by someone in a bomb suit survived the explosion of its own tank: the wearer died, but the valve stayed intact with its other tank, ready to be reused. The valve is normally destroyed by receiving the explosion itself, but a human with enough bomb armor does not pass the explosion on to their contents. The fix destroys the valve directly when a tank bursts inside it.

## The bug

As the issue describes, wearing a bomb suit and a TTV as a backpack and detonating any gas mix made the TTV survive. The wearer died (and could be revived), and the valve could take new tanks and be used again.

## Reproduction

On master, using the Lua editor, View Variables and the runtime log:

1. Spawn a human and equip a bomb suit and its hood.
2. Spawn a `/obj/item/transfer_valve/fake` (tanks already attached), set `wired` to TRUE and `slot_flags` to the back slot, and equip it on the human's back. This was set up from Lua; the valve was not assembled by hand.
3. Set the temperature of both tanks to 3000 K, then call `toggle_valve()` on the valve.

Observed on master: a large explosion and the wearer dead, but the valve still existed. Its location was the wearer, its `atom_integrity` was 200, and its plasma tank was still inside it (integrity 200, 0 pressure). The other tank had burst and was gone.

## Cause

When a tank over the fragmentation pressure is destroyed, `atom_destruction` in `code/game/objects/items/tanks/tanks.dm` (around lines 391-404) calls `dyn_explosion(src, ...)`. Nothing there destroys the valve that holds the tank. The valve is normally destroyed only because it also receives the explosion's `ex_act`.

A worn valve is inside a mob, and `human/ex_act` (`code/modules/mob/living/carbon/human/human_defense.dm:257-290`) only forwards the explosion to the mob's contents when it gibs the mob, which happens when the wearer's bomb armor is below `EXPLODE_GIB_THRESHOLD`. With a bomb suit the wearer is not gibbed, their contents are protected, and the valve is never exploded. The same applies to a valve carried in hand by a wearer with bomb armor.

## Fix

Commit "Fix issue 83813: destroy a transfer valve when its tank bursts inside it": after the `dyn_explosion` call in the tank's `atom_destruction`, if the tank's `loc` is an `/obj/item/transfer_valve`, that valve is `qdel`ed, which also deletes the tanks and attached device inside it. The destruction is done by the tank that burst instead of relying on the explosion reaching the valve, so it works wherever the valve is held. An alternative (making `ex_act` pass through armor to the contents) was not considered, as it would change how bomb armor works in general.

## Verification

After the fix, with the same steps: the explosion still happened and the wearer was affected as before, the valve was deleted (Lua reported "a destructed callback or destructed userdata method was called" when the valve was used afterwards), and no new runtimes appeared in the runtime log.

Not checked: a wearer without bomb armor (they are gibbed and their contents are deleted anyway, so no change is expected); a valve held in hand; a valve on the floor or in a bag; a real assembled TTV with a signaller or timer; and no unit test was added.

## Notes

- The check only covers a tank bursting directly inside a valve. Other things that carry the valve, such as a bag, were not examined.
- The `log_bomber` line in the same function already treats a tank in a transfer valve specially, so the new check follows the existing convention.
