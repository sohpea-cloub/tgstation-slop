## Summary

A projectile fired from a mech could not enter the mech's own tile. The projectile refused to *hit* its firer's mech, but the mech is dense, so the projectile still could not *pass* it. A projectile moving at about the mech's speed therefore bumped the mech on every step and made no progress until the mech stopped, at which point all of them were released at once (#89520). Separately, the Odysseus's syringe gun marked every dart as a telekinetic shot, which switched off the firer-immunity check, so the darts really did hit the mech (#92607). The fix lets projectiles pass through their firer's mech, and stops treating mech-mounted guns as telekinetic.

## The bug

**#89520.** When a fast mech (the report's example is the Clarke) moves while firing a slow projectile in the direction it is travelling, the projectiles stay just behind it and move only as fast as the mech does. They appear to "pile up" and bounce against the mech while it keeps moving. When the mech stops they fly past it together. The reporter observed this moving right and suspected latency played a part; the reproduction below did not need latency, and nothing in the investigation explained why the direction would matter (see Notes).

**#92607.** An Odysseus driving forward and firing its syringe gun in the direction of travel repeatedly took damage from its own darts.

## Reproduction

All of this was done on the test server with the Lua editor. It was not tested with a player-driven mech, a Clarke, or a live server with latency; the mech was driven from script at a fixed rate.

**#89520**

1. Using the Lua editor, spawned an Odysseus with a pilot inside, in a straight hallway.
2. Drove the mech east by calling `vehicle_move(EAST)` on it every 0.125 s (about one tile per step; calling it directly ignores the mech's normal move delay).
3. Every third step, just before moving, fired a `/obj/projectile/plasma/adv/mech` with the mech as firer and a speed of 0.62.
4. Tracked the projectiles' offsets from the mech and counted `COMSIG_MOVABLE_BUMP` signals on them.

Observed on unfixed master: every projectile stayed two tiles behind the mech for the whole run, with `pixel_x` jumping between -16 and +15 (the visual pull-back described under Cause). The bump signal fired 21 times in 22 steps. So the projectiles were held directly behind the mech, advancing only as fast as it did, and bumping it constantly.

Varying the projectile speed gave the expected pattern. At speed 0.25 each projectile bumped once (the mech overtook it). At speed 1.0 there were no bumps (the projectile outran the mech). The bug therefore needs a projectile speed close to the mech's speed, which matches the issue's "especially fast mech or especially slow projectile".

**#92607**

1. Using the Lua editor, put a pilot in an Odysseus (120 health) and drove it east.
2. Fired the exosuit syringe gun while driving.
3. A `com_proj_prehit` hook on the mech showed `PREHIT syringe ... ign=1` for each dart, i.e. `ignore_source_check` was TRUE on the darts.

Observed on unfixed master: the Odysseus lost 24 health over 4 darts (6 each). With only the #89520 fix applied the Odysseus still lost 24 health, so the two issues are independent.

## Cause

**#89520.** `/obj/projectile/CanPassThrough` (`code/modules/projectiles/projectile.dm:714`) only let a projectile through things it had already impacted:

```dm
/obj/projectile/CanPassThrough(atom/blocker, movement_dir, blocker_opinion)
	return impacted[blocker.weak_reference] || ..()
```

Firer immunity lives somewhere else, in `can_hit_target` (`projectile.dm:639-645`): a projectile will not hit its firer, the mech the firer sits in, or what the firer is buckled to. The two checks disagree. For a projectile behind its firer's mech:

- The mech is dense, so the step into its tile fails and `Bump()` is called.
- `Bump()` (`projectile.dm:462-465`) asks `can_hit_target`, which refuses because of firer immunity, so nothing is hit.
- The projectile still did not move into the tile. In `process_movement` the pixel offset is moved back by a tile after a failed step (`pixel_x -= x_shift * ICON_SIZE_X`, `projectile.dm:1059`), so it is also pulled back visually.

If the mech moves about as fast as the projectile, this repeats every step. When the mech stops, the projectiles are no longer blocked and all move on together. The report did not explain why the reporter saw it only moving right, so the direction dependence is unexplained.

**#92607.** `ammo_casing/proc/tk_firing` (`code/modules/projectiles/ammunition/_firing.dm:38`) was:

```dm
return fired_from != user && !user.contains(fired_from)
```

The exosuit syringe gun sits inside the mech, not in the pilot's inventory, so every shot from it counted as telekinetic. `ready_proj` then set `loaded_projectile.ignore_source_check = TRUE` (`_firing.dm:71-72`), which removes firer immunity from `can_hit_target`, so the dart could hit the mech the pilot was driving into it. `tk_firing` is also used for the muzzle effect (line 87) and for `aim_projectile` (line 93), where it sends the knockback (`newtonian_move`) to the gun instead of the pilot.

## Fix

Two commits on `fix/89520`.

**"Fix issue 89520: projectiles can pass through their firer's mech instead of piling up behind it"** (`projectile.dm`):

- The firer-immunity test was moved out of `can_hit_target` into a new `/obj/projectile/proc/is_immune_firer_part(atom/target)`. It returns TRUE when `ignore_source_check` is false, there is a firer, and the target is the firer, the mecha the firer is in, one of the firer's buckled mobs, or the thing the firer is buckled to. This is the same logic as before, unchanged.
- `can_hit_target` now calls it (`if(!direct_target && is_immune_firer_part(target)) return FALSE`).
- `CanPassThrough` now returns TRUE for such a blocker, unless the blocker is the projectile's `original` target, before falling back to the old `impacted` check.

Sharing one helper keeps "cannot hit" and "cannot be blocked by" from drifting apart. `ignore_source_check` is respected: a projectile whose firer is no longer immune (a ricochet sets the flag, `projectile.dm:490`) is still blocked by the firer, as before.

**"Fix issue 92607: mech-mounted syringe gun darts no longer count as telekinetic shots that can hit their own mech"** (`_firing.dm`): `tk_firing` still returns FALSE when the firing item is the user or inside the user. It now also returns FALSE when `fired_from.loc` is a mecha and is the user's `loc`, since equipment mounted on the mech the user is piloting is not being fired telekinetically. Otherwise it returns TRUE as before.

## Verification

Checked on the test server with the same scripted setup as the reproduction:

- **#89520, fixed build, same run:** zero bump signals. The projectiles' offset from the mech grew from -1 to +4 tiles, i.e. they passed through the mech and flew ahead of it.
- **#92607, fixed build:** 6 syringes fired while driving east, no PREHIT events, the mech stayed at 120/120 health and the pilot's brute damage stayed at 0.
- **Others can still be hit:** a human standing 6 tiles east of the mech took 20 damage from a dart (firer: the pilot) and from a plasma blast (firer: the mech). The mech took no damage.
- No runtimes other than known noise from the Lua editor were seen.

Not checked: a real player-driven mech such as the Clarke, a live server with latency, mech LMG or cutter specifically (only the plasma projectile and the syringe gun were used), directions other than east, other firer-immune cases (buckled mobs, riding vehicles) beyond what the shared helper already covered, and the visual result as seen by a client.

## Notes

- Because the pass-through is decided in `CanPassThrough`, it applies to every dense thing the helper considers part of the firer, not only mechs: the firer itself, buckled mobs, and the vehicle the firer is buckled to.
- The visual pull-back in `process_movement` after a failed step (`projectile.dm:1059`) was left unchanged. It can still affect projectiles blocked by other dense things that are not hit targets.
- A comment on #92607 said mech projectiles had been made to bounce off the mech until it no longer overlapped them, and that syringes had probably been missed. The investigation found the darts were different because of `tk_firing`, not because they were missed by the bounce handling.
- The reproduction only demonstrates the mechanism. The direction dependence in the #89520 title was not investigated.
