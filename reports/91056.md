## Summary

A mob that is in an open-cockpit mech and is moved out by a normal step (a meat hook's pull, a MOD tether) leaves the mech without its client's camera being reset, so the player keeps looking at the empty mech instead of their character. The only exit paths that reset the camera are the ones that use `forceMove()`, such as the mech's own eject. The fix resets the leaving mob's perspective in `/obj/vehicle/sealed/Exited()`, which every way of leaving a sealed vehicle goes through. It is three added lines.

## The bug

Players in an open-cockpit mech (Ripley, Paddy and similar) who are hooked with a meat hook (#91056) or pulled with a MOD tether (#92087) are dragged out of the mech, and their character ends up on the floor, but the screen stays centred on the mech. Climbing back in and ejecting normally fixes the view (#91056, step 4). A comment on #91056 describes the same symptom for a player who was turned into an object that was on a player and then dropped; that case was not examined.

## Reproduction

Using the Lua editor on the test server, with a round started:

1. Spawned a human and moved the admin's client into it, spawned a mining Ripley next to it, and a second human four tiles to the east.
2. Called the Ripley's `moved_inside()` with the first human, so the client was a pilot.
3. Created the same pull the meat hook starts: a `/datum/hook_and_move` with `begin_pulling(second human, pilot, destination)`. This moves the pilot with `step()` calls, as the real hook does. A real meat hook was not fired; the pull datum was driven directly.
4. After the pull the pilot's `loc` was the floor at x=111, but `client.eye` was still the Ripley (at x=107) and `client.perspective` was `EYE_PERSPECTIVE` (1), where a normal view has `MOB_PERSPECTIVE` (0). The camera was stuck on the mech, as reported.

## Cause

Entering a mech goes through `forceMove()`; because the mob's `loc` is now a non-turf, `reset_perspective()` points the client's eye at the mech (`code/modules/mob/mob.dm:533-539`). Nothing resets it when the mob leaves in a way that is not `forceMove()`.

- The normal exit, `mob_exit()` (`code/modules/vehicles/sealed.dm:92`, and the mech's own version in `code/modules/vehicles/mecha/mecha_mob_interaction.dm:114`), moves the mob with `forceMove()`. `/mob/living/forceMove()` calls `reset_perspective()` when the mob has a client (`code/modules/mob/living/living.dm:1969-1977`), so the camera follows the mob.
- A pull that uses `step()`/`Move()` does not. The meat hook's `attempt_movement()` (`code/modules/projectiles/guns/special/meat_hook.dm:174-210`) calls `step_towards()`/`step()` on the victim, and the MOD tether's pull (`code/datums/components/tether.dm:333`) calls `movable_target.Move(...)`. Both pull the mob out of the mech's contents without touching its client's eye.

The vehicle's `Exited()` handler only dropped the occupant bookkeeping:

```dm
/obj/vehicle/sealed/Exited(atom/movable/gone, direction)
	. = ..()
	if(ismob(gone))
		remove_occupant(gone)
```

(`code/modules/vehicles/sealed.dm:33-36`.)

## Fix

One commit, **"Fix issue 91056: reset the camera of occupants removed from a sealed vehicle by force"**. After `remove_occupant()` in `/obj/vehicle/sealed/Exited()`, it calls `reset_perspective()` on the mob that left:

```dm
		// Occupants dragged out without going through mob_exit() (meat hooks, MOD tethers...) would keep their camera on us
		var/mob/leaver = gone
		leaver.reset_perspective()
```

`reset_perspective()` with no argument puts the client's eye back on the mob when it is on a turf, or on its current location otherwise, so it is also correct for a mob that leaves one container into another. `Exited()` is called for every way a mob can leave the vehicle, so this covers the meat hook, the MOD tether and anything similar, rather than patching each puller. The change applies to every sealed vehicle (mechs, cars, and so on), not only mechs. Fixing the pullers (the hook and the tether) instead would have left every other way of moving a pilot out with `Move()` still broken.

## Verification

On a build with the fix:

- **Meat hook pull (#91056):** repeated the pull from Reproduction. After it, `client.eye` was the pilot and `client.perspective` was 0; the eye's x was 111, the pilot's x.
- **Direct `Move()` out of the mech (the tether's call, #92087):** moved the pilot with `Move()` to an adjacent turf. `client.eye` was the pilot and `perspective` was 0.
- **Normal exit:** the usual `mob_exit()` path was unchanged (eye on the pilot, `perspective` 0).
- No new runtimes appeared.

Not checked:

- A real MOD tether was not fired and a real hook projectile was not shot; the pull datum and a direct `Move()` stood in for them.
- The comment on #91056 about being turned into an object and dropped.
- Other sealed vehicles, and occupants that are not humans: AIs controlling a mech, and MMI/positronic brain pilots, whose camera is deliberately set to the mech by `mmi_moved_inside()` (`mecha_mob_interaction.dm:102`). `Exited()` only runs when such an occupant actually leaves, and their own exit code already calls `reset_perspective()` there (`mecha_mob_interaction.dm:171`), but the combination was not run.

## Notes

- A comment on #92087 links an older issue (#57757) as the same "stuck to mech camera when you get forced out" problem; that issue was not read.
- A normal exit through `forceMove()` now resets the camera twice (once from `Exited()` and once at the end of `/mob/living/forceMove()`). This is harmless but redundant.
