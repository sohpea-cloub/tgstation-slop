## Summary

Three quirk-related issues were examined together. #39455 (nyctophobia triggering in smoke while holding a flashlight) could not be reproduced: the current fear handler does not treat smoke as darkness, and the later comments blame server lag, which a local test server cannot show. #63147 (light step does not protect a plasmaman's envirosuit from blood) is already fixed on master: the blood-sharing code returns early for `TRAIT_LIGHT_STEP`. #65318 was real: `SSquirks.filter_invalid_quirks` compared blacklisted quirk typepaths against a list of quirk names, so the quirk blacklist was never applied when a character is loaded from a savefile or when the preferences UI validates `give_quirk`. The fix compares quirk names instead.

## #39455: Nyctophobia and smoke

### The bug

The reporter said that a character with the Nyctophobia quirk who walks into sight-blocking smoke while holding a flashlight gets scared of "the dark" and slows down. Comments later suggested that the fear was caused by lag (the light source moving or updating a moment after the player), and a 2026 comment said that on a server where players arrive by shuttle from off-station, lighting updates can be slow enough to trigger nyctophobia (and podperson starvation).

### Reproduction

The report could not be reproduced. On the test server, using the Lua editor:

1. A turf with a static lumcount of 0 (a very dark spot) was chosen, and a human with the Nyctophobia quirk was spawned on it.
2. The nyctophobia handler's `check_condition` was called on that human. Without a light it returned 5 (fear builds up). With a lit flashlight in hand it returned 0.
3. Nine `/obj/effect/particle_effect/fluid/smoke` objects (opacity 1, lifetime 600) were spawned on the human's tile and the eight tiles around it, with the flashlight still lit. `check_condition` still returned 0, and again after about 10 seconds, with all nine smoke objects still opaque.

The handler was called directly; the quirk's real tick-driven fear buildup and the slowdown were not observed, and no lag or shuttle arrival was simulated.

### Cause

Not a defect that could be found on master. The code the reporter hit (the old quirk code linked in a 2021 comment) no longer exists; Nyctophobia is now a terror handler, `/datum/terror_handler/simple_source/nyctophobia` (`code/datums/components/fearful/sources/_sources.dm:43-69`). It counts the open turfs within range 1 of the owner that are above `LIGHTING_TILE_IS_DARK` (0.2) via `check_lumcount_above` (`code/modules/lighting/lighting_turf.dm:46`), and builds fear when lit tiles are fewer than unlit tiles. Dynamic lights are collected by `collect_dynamic_lightsources` (`lighting_turf.dm:90`) from the spatial grid and `hearers()`; in the test, a lit flashlight held by the owner counted even with opaque smoke on every tile. The code does look at light the moment it is checked, so a light source that updates late (after a shuttle arrival or under heavy load) would plausibly produce brief fear; that matches the lag explanation in the comments but could not be shown here.

### Fix

None.

### Verification

Not applicable (no change). Not tested: a light held by someone else, light sources other than a handheld flashlight, a real round with lag, or arriving by shuttle.

### Notes

A search by issue number found an upstream pull request, #90217 (merged 2025-03-27, title beginning "Terror"), that mentions this issue; it probably relates to the move of Nyctophobia into the terror handler system, but its contents were not checked. The issue can likely be closed as obsolete or as a lag-related report.

## #63147: Light step and blood

### The bug

The issue said that a plasmaman with the Light Step quirk gets their envirosuit bloodied walking over a small blood pool, that stepping on sharp objects barefoot gives no reduced effect, and (marked as possibly intended) that the exosuit slot gets bloodied regardless of the trait. Later comments pointed out that light step is meant to protect clothing from blood pools (PR #57085) and that it reduces caltrop damage and turns paralysis into a knockdown (PR #80270).

### Reproduction

On master, using the Lua editor on the test server:

1. Two plasmamen were spawned and given the plasmaman outfit; one received the Light Step quirk.
2. Each was moved onto a blood decal (`/obj/effect/decal/cleanable/blood`) with `bloodiness` 200.
3. The plasmaman with Light Step had a uniform `total_bloodiness` of 0, bloody feet of 0, and the pool still at 200. The plasmaman without the trait had uniform bloodiness 72.3 and feet 144.6.

So the envirosuit-bloodying part no longer happens. The people were moved with `forceMove` rather than walking, which fires the same step-on-blood handling.

### Cause

Already fixed on master. `/datum/component/bloodysoles/proc/on_step_blood` returns before `share_blood(pool)` when the wielder has `TRAIT_LIGHT_STEP` (`code/datums/components/bloodysoles.dm:242`; `share_blood` is at line 249). A 2022 comment in the issue suspected the blood transfer happening before the trait check; that ordering is no longer present.

For the caltrop part, `code/datums/components/caltrop.dm` (lines 104-125) already multiplies damage by 0.75 and uses `Knockdown` instead of `Paralyze` with the trait. A character whose uniform, suit or shoes cover the feet is not affected by caltrops at all, so "barefoot" damage reduction being limited is consistent with the discussion in the thread (judged to be intended). The exosuit remark was not changed.

### Fix

None.

### Verification

The blood behaviour above was checked in game. The caltrop behaviour was only read from the code, not tested in game.

## #65318: Quirk selection is not validated on save load

### The bug

Quirks loaded from a character save are supposed to be validated, but the reporter had both Mime Fan and Clown Enjoyer (which are mutually exclusive) selected on a savefile, and both were assigned every time they joined. In practice the incompatible-quirk check never removed anything when loading a save.

### Reproduction

On master, using the Lua editor on the test server, `SSquirks.filter_invalid_quirks` was called directly:

1. `filter_invalid_quirks({"Clown Enjoyer", "Mime Fan", "Nyctophobia", "Photophobia"})` was called. It returned all four names, although Clown Enjoyer/Mime Fan and Nyctophobia/Photophobia are both blacklisted pairs.

This was reproduced only by calling the proc, not by loading a real savefile.

### Cause

`load_character` calls `SSquirks.filter_invalid_quirks` on the saved quirk names (`code/modules/client/preferences_savefile.dm:405`). Inside it (`code/controllers/subsystem/processing/quirks.dm:182`), the loop that checks `GLOB.quirk_blacklist` iterates the blacklist entries, which are typepaths, and tests whether each is `in new_quirks`, but `new_quirks` is the list of accepted quirk names:

```dm
for (var/other_quirk in blacklist)
	if (other_quirk in new_quirks)
```

A typepath is never in a list of names, so `blacklisted` was never set (`quirks.dm:200-201`). The same function is used to validate the preferences UI actions `give_quirk` and `remove_quirk` (`code/modules/client/preferences/middleware/quirks.dm:116` and `:136`), which compare the filter's output with the input, so a client sending a blacklisted combination was never caught on the server. The client UI normally prevents the combination itself, using the string blacklist.

### Fix

The commit "Fix issue 65318: quirk blacklist never applied when filtering saved quirks" changes the inner loop of `filter_invalid_quirks` to iterate typed entries and compare names:

```dm
for (var/datum/quirk/other_quirk as anything in blacklist)
	// new_quirks holds quirk names, not typepaths
	if (initial(other_quirk.name) in new_quirks)
```

This is the smallest change that matches how `new_quirks` is built (`new_quirks += quirk_name`) and how `generate_quirk_string_blacklist` already turns typepaths into names.

### Verification

On the rebuilt test server, the filter was called directly with three inputs:

- `{"Clown Enjoyer", "Mime Fan", "Nyctophobia", "Photophobia"}` returned `Clown Enjoyer|Nyctophobia` (Mime Fan and Photophobia, the second of each blacklisted pair, were removed).
- `{"Mime Fan", "Clown Enjoyer"}` returned `Mime Fan`.
- `{"Clown Enjoyer", "Nyctophobia", "Alcohol Tolerance"}` returned `Nyctophobia|Alcohol Tolerance`. This input was expected to come back unchanged, but Clown Enjoyer is missing from the result. That is most likely the quirk-point balance step at the end of the function (no blacklisted pair is present; with the default of 2 free points, the positives total 6 against 3 negative points), not the blacklist, but this was not confirmed on the test server.

Not checked: loading a real character savefile, or the `give_quirk` / `remove_quirk` UI path.

### Notes

- Existing saves containing blacklisted combinations will now be trimmed on load, keeping the quirk that comes first in the saved list; players with such saves will lose the second one.
- Other code in `quirks.dm` (the random quirk assignment, lines 128, 143 and 159) tests `quirk in GLOB.quirk_blacklist`, and `code/modules/mob/dead/new_player/preferences_setup.dm:61-69` has its own blacklist loop; these were not examined or changed.
