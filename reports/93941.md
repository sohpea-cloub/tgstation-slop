## Summary

**#93941.** `/obj/item/toy/cards/proc/insert()` moved cards into a hand or deck with a bare `forceMove`. If the card was in a mob's pocket (or a hand or other slot), the mob's slot variable kept pointing at it, so the card was in the hand and still "in" the pocket. The fix unequips the card from its holder before moving it.

**#71125.** Holodeck cards did full brute damage because single cards copy `force` and `throwforce` from their deck but not `damtype`. They now copy `damtype` too. The other bullets in that old report were not changed (see below).

The two issues were grouped as related playing card bugs.

## #93941: card teleport / fake duplication

### The bug

A player puts a card in a pocket and clicks it with a hand of at least two cards. The card is added to the hand, but it also stays in the pocket, so the card shows up in both places. The reporter's screenshots (View Variables) show the card inside the hand's contents and in the pocket slot. The hand's icon can show more cards than exist. Drawing the card from the hand moves the real card to the player's hand and clears both places. That effectively teleports it from the pocket (or, per the reporter, from anywhere on the station) to the hand. No extra cards are created. The reporter did not check other z levels.

### Reproduction

This was reproduced by calling the hand's `item_interaction` (the same call a click makes) from the Lua editor, not with real mouse clicks.

1. On the test server, spawn a human with a grey jumpsuit, which provides the pocket slots, and spawn a card deck.
2. Draw some cards from the deck. Cards drawn this way stay inside the deck, so move them out onto the floor or into hands (`forceMove`).
3. Build a two-card hand by calling `insert()` on a card hand.
4. Put a third card in the human's left pocket with `equip_to_slot_if_possible(card, 16384)`.
5. Call `hand:item_interaction(human, pocketed_card, {})`, the call a click on the pocketed card makes through the single card's `item_interaction` (`code/modules/cards/singlecard.dm:189`, `return tool.item_interaction(user, src, modifiers)`).
6. Read the human's `l_store`, the card's `loc` and the hand's card count.

Observed on master: the card's `loc` was the hand and the hand's card count was 3, but `human.l_store` still pointed at the card. It was in both places.

### Cause

In `/obj/item/toy/cards/proc/insert()` (`code/modules/cards/cards.dm:74`), each card is added with a plain move (`cards.dm:94`):

```dm
for(var/obj/item/toy/singlecard/card in cards_to_add)
    card.forceMove(src)
```

`forceMove` changes where the card is, but does not tell the mob it lost the item. A mob's `l_store`, `r_store`, held item slots and so on are only cleared by the inventory procs (`temporarilyRemoveItemFromInventory`, `dropItemToGround`, ...). The card was therefore still referenced by the mob's slot. The same applies to a card taken from a hand slot or from another slot, not only a pocket.

### Fix

Commit "Fix issue 93941: cards inserted from a pocket or hand stayed in the mob's slot" adds, at the top of the loop in `insert()`:

```dm
if(ismob(card.loc))
    var/mob/holder = card.loc
    holder.temporarilyRemoveItemFromInventory(card, TRUE)
card.forceMove(src)
```

`temporarilyRemoveItemFromInventory` with `force = TRUE` clears the slot and runs the normal unequip handling. Then the existing `forceMove` puts the card in the hand or deck. This is done in `insert()` rather than at the click handlers, so every caller (single card onto hand, hand onto hand, card into deck) is covered.

### Verification

After rebuilding with the fix, with the same Lua setup:

- Card in the left pocket added to a two-card hand: `l_store` was empty, the card's `loc` was the hand, and the hand's count was 3.
- A single card in the active hand clicked with a pocketed card (right pocket): a new hand of two cards was created holding both, and `r_store` was empty.
- A pocketed card inserted into a deck left the pocket.
- No runtimes about cards appeared in the runtime log.

Not checked: a real player clicking, cards held in hand slots and other inventory slots (only the pocket and active-hand cases were run), cards worn in a non-pocket slot, and other z levels.

### Notes

The fix handles any mob holding the card, not only humans, since `temporarilyRemoveItemFromInventory` is a `/mob` proc.

## #71125: assorted playing card bugs

The report lists four things, from 2022 (round 193799):

### Holodeck cards do damage: fixed

**Cause.** `/obj/item/toy/singlecard/Initialize` copies `hitsound`, `force` and `throwforce` from the parent deck (`code/modules/cards/singlecard.dm:38-40`) but not `damtype`. The holodeck nerfs its items to stamina damage when a program loads (`code/modules/holodeck/computer.dm:331-332` and `nerf()` at `:431`), but it only touches items already in its `spawned` list at that moment. Cards are created lazily, later, when the deck is drawn from, so they keep the default `damtype` (brute).

**Fix.** Commit "Fix issue 71125: holodeck cards did full damage when the holodeck is not emagged" adds `damtype = parent_deck.damtype` to `Initialize`, so a card inherits the nerf from a (nerfed) deck.

**Verification.** A fake holodeck with a holographic deck was set up through the Lua editor. Cards drawn from it had `damtype` stamina; before the fix they were brute. Not checked: a real holodeck program, and an emagged holodeck (where `nerf` is not applied to decks, so the cards should stay brute; not tested).

### Holodeck card hands can be taken out of the holodeck: not fixed

A hand made from holodeck cards is not tracked as a hologram (it is not `HOLOGRAM_1` and is not in the holodeck's `spawned` list). When the holodeck clears its items, it derezzes the cards inside (`computer.dm:395`) and the cards are deleted, which matches the report's "disappear once you try to remove the card". This was read from the code only. It was not reproduced in game and no fix was written; the cause is unconfirmed.

### Marked cards show #00000 instead of the card colour: not reproduced

In this code, the `crayon_color` and pen colour are colour names, and examining a marked card prints the name. This was not reproduced and is likely already fixed.

### The 5th or 6th card of a hand icon does not update: not retested

`update_overlays` uses the top five cards. The report was likely fixed by upstream PR #78954. It was not retested in game.
