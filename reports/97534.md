## Summary

#97534 (lavaland fauna ignoring players in mechs) could not be reproduced: on master, goliaths, basilisks, watchers, legions, brimdemons, bileworms, lobstrosities, hivelords and goldgrubs all rate a mech with an ordinary human pilot as a valid target, on the station and on Lavaland, and a goliath was seen actually attacking one. The one "ignored" result obtained came from a godmode trait the tester had put on the pilot, which the targeting strategy rejects on purpose. #88946 (friendly legion skulls attacking mechs) was real: the legion targeting strategy treats hurt friendlies as targets, and the shared mech branch then makes any mech holding such a friendly a target. The fix makes the legion strategy skip mechs whose occupant is faction-friendly.

## The bug

### #97534

The report says Lavaland fauna ignore a player who is in a mech, and that megafauna only fights back when attacked. The report gives no further steps ("get in mech, blast through lavaland's many dangers"). It was made on a live round with test merges (#97357, #96887, #97522), so it may depend on something that was not on master at the tested commit.

### #88946

A player who was a sentient legion released skulls to heal people. The skulls instead went for an Odysseus and a Ripley and started damaging them. A maintainer replied in the issue that the skull wants to get inside the mech to reach the person, so this was working as intended. Later comments objected that a skull accidentally destroying the mech is bad, and that it should at least be able to reach the pilot of an open-cockpit mech.

## Reproduction

### #97534 (not reproduced)

Everything was done with admin tools (Lua editor) on a private server running master; the targeting strategy was called directly, and one live attack was observed.

1. Spawned a mech, put an ordinary human in it with `mob_enter`, and placed it near a wild mob (in a station hallway, then on Lavaland).
2. In the Lua editor, fetched the mob's targeting strategy from `SSai_controllers.targeting_strategies` using its `BB_TARGETING_STRATEGY` blackboard entry, and called `is_valid_target(mob, mech, 9)`.
3. Result: goliath, basilisk, watcher, legion, brimdemon, bileworm, lobstrosity, hivelord and goldgrub all returned valid (1) for the mech at a distance of 4 in the station hallway. The mook returned 0, which is expected from its faction.
4. On Lavaland (z-level 3), with a wild goliath about 5 tiles from the mech, the result was also valid, and goliaths attacked the mech ("Taking damage!" and the pilot was ejected).
5. Every mech type tried (Clarke, Vim, Phazon, Gygax, Honker, Savannah-Ivanov, Durand, Odysseus, Reticence, Ripley, Paddy, cargo Ripley, Marauder) was valid.

A first attempt returned "not valid". That was caused by the tester adding `TRAIT_GODMODE` to the occupant; removing it made the mech valid again.

Wild Lavaland mobs have their AI idle until a living player mob is on the z-level. That is the normal idle-AI behaviour and not part of the bug.

Not tested: a pilot with a real job body (shaft miner outfit) rather than a plain human; node drones from vent waves; watcher overwatch (`watcher_overwatch.dm:31` has its own `ismecha` check); megafauna (simple animals such as Bubblegum or the drake) near a mech, beyond reading the code; a pilot who is a moving client mob; and whether the mining target-priority strategy affects mechs.

### #88946 (reproduced through the targeting call)

1. In the Lua editor, spawned a `/mob/living/basic/mining/legion_brood` skull, a human with the mining faction and 30 brute damage, and a Ripley, and put the human in the Ripley.
2. Called `is_valid_target(skull, mech, 9)` on the legion targeting strategy.
3. Result before the fix: 1 (the mech is a valid target).

A live skull-versus-mech fight was not run.

## Cause

`/datum/targeting_strategy/basic/is_valid_target` in `code/datums/ai/basic_mobs/targeting_strategies/basic_targeting_strategy.dm` has a branch for mechs (lines 66-75):

```dm
else if(ismecha(the_target)) //Targeting vs mechas
	var/obj/vehicle/sealed/mecha/M = the_target
	var/valid_occupant = FALSE
	for(var/occupant in M.occupants)
		if(is_valid_target(living_mob, occupant)) //Can we attack any of the occupants?
```

A mech is a valid target if any occupant would be. For #97534 this is what makes mechs targetable at all, and it worked in the tests. Note that the recursive call passes no `vision_range` or controller, so the occupant's own range and sight checks are skipped. Godmode occupants are rejected earlier in the same proc (`HAS_TRAIT(the_target, TRAIT_GODMODE)`, around line 45).

For #88946, `/datum/targeting_strategy/basic/legion` (`code/modules/mob/living/basic/lavaland/legion/legion_ai.dm:25-37`) overrides `faction_check` so that a hurt, living friendly is *not* filtered out ("target nearby friendlies if they are hurt"). Because the mech branch above only asks whether an occupant is a valid target, a mech with a hurt friendly inside becomes valid, and the skull attacks the mech to reach them.

## Fix

One commit on the branch, "Fix issue 88946: legion skulls no longer attack mechs carrying a friendly", changes only `legion_ai.dm`. It adds an `is_valid_target` override on the legion strategy: if the target is a mech and any occupant is faction-friendly (`faction_check_atom` with the strategy's `check_factions_exactly`), it returns FALSE; otherwise it calls the parent. Hostile pilots are still targeted, and a friendly human outside a mech is still targeted when hurt.

This was chosen over changing the shared mech branch, which other mobs rely on and which is correct for them. Nothing was changed for #97534.

## Verification

After rebuilding and starting a new round, the legion strategy was called on four cases:

- friendly human in a mech: 0 (was 1 before the fix)
- friendly human on foot: 1 (healing still works)
- foreign-faction human in a mech: 1
- foreign-faction human on foot: 1

Not checked: a live fight with real skulls and a mech; open-cockpit mechs specifically; legions (rather than brood skulls) using the same strategy; any case for #97534.

## Notes

- A maintainer called the #88946 behaviour intended ("the skull wants to get inside the mech"). The fix takes the position that breaking the mech is not what a healing skull should do, but it also means skulls no longer heal a pilot sitting in a mech at all. A maintainer may prefer a different design, for example letting skulls reach pilots of open-cockpit mechs without damaging them.
- For #97534, the reporter's round had three test merges and a different build. If it still happens, the information needed is the exact mob types, whether the pilot was a normal job body, and whether the mobs were awake. A real job body, megafauna and node drones are the untested leads.
