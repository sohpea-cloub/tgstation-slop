## Summary

A cyborg's rapid service fabricator (RSF) pays for each item from its power cell, but `use_matter()` subtracts the item's matter cost (10 to 200) directly from the cell's charge in joules. With cells holding tens of kilojoules, a drinking glass cost the service cyborg's cell 0.02%, so it could fabricate effectively without limit. The fix charges each matter unit as `cyborg_energy_per_unit` joules (0.5% of a standard cell) through `cell.use()`, so a full cell pays for about 100 glasses. For #89800, the peacekeeper cookie synthesizer's examine text now reports what the cyborg's cell can pay for instead of an unused `0/10`, and clicking during the 10 second cooldown now gives feedback.

## The bug

### #84467

A service cyborg can pick an item on its RSF and fabricate it hundreds of times without running out of power or material, which the reporter says causes lag.

### #89800

A peacekeeper cyborg's cookie synthesizer always examines as holding `0/10` cookie-units. It dispenses a cookie on a 10 second cooldown regardless, and a click during the cooldown does nothing visible, which looks like the device is "spotty" or not recharging.

## Reproduction

Reproduced on master through the Lua editor on the test server. The cyborg's tool was used by calling `interact_with_atom()` directly rather than by a player clicking.

1. Start a round as an observer and open the Lua editor.
2. Spawn a `/mob/living/silicon/robot`. Transform it with `model:transform_to(<service or peacekeeper model path>, TRUE, FALSE)`; `forced` must be TRUE or the skin-selection menu blocks the transform.
3. Find the RSF (or cookie synthesizer) in `model.modules` and call `interact_with_atom(turf, borg, null)` repeatedly, reading the cell's `used_charge()` after each use.

Observed on master (service cyborg, cell of 100000 J): five glasses cost 100 J in total (20 each), the tool's `matter` stayed 0, and dispensing kept working. That is about 0.02% of the cell per glass. For #89800, the cookie synthesizer's examine reads `0/10 cookie-units` (the unused matter variable) and a click inside the cooldown returned nothing (`NONE`) with no message. The fix-side values are under Verification.

## Cause

`code/game/objects/items/rcd/RSF.dm:144` (`use_matter()`), cyborg branch:

```dm
var/end_charge = R.cell.charge - charge
if(end_charge < 0)
	...
R.cell.charge = end_charge
```

`charge` is the item's matter cost from `cost_by_item` (for example 100 for a cookie, 20 for a glass), but `cell.charge` is in joules, and cells hold tens of kilojoules, so the cost is negligible. The `matter` and `max_matter` variables are never used for cyborgs, yet `examine()` (line 70) prints `It currently holds [matter]/[max_matter] [discriptor].`, which is why a cyborg always sees `0/10`. In `interact_with_atom()` (line 131) a click during `cooldown` returns `NONE` without feedback.

## Fix

**Fix issue 84467: cyborg RSF now spends a meaningful amount of cell energy**
- Adds `cyborg_energy_per_unit = STANDARD_CELL_CHARGE * 0.005` (50 J) to `/obj/item/rsf`.
- The cyborg branch of `use_matter()` now calls `R.cell?.use(charge * cyborg_energy_per_unit)`. If that fails it shows the existing "not enough power" message and sets the spent icon; on success it calls `R.diag_hud_set_borgcell()` so the cell HUD updates.

**Fix issue 89800: cyborg cookie synthesizer shows its real charge and feedback on cooldown**
- For a cyborg with a cell, `examine()` now reports `It draws on your power cell, which can pay for N <discriptor>.` where N is the cell's charge divided by `cyborg_energy_per_unit`.
- A click during the cooldown now shows the balloon alert "still recharging!" and returns `ITEM_INTERACT_BLOCKING`.

The 10 second cookie cooldown was kept: it is the device's `cooldowndelay`, so it is intended, not a bug.

## Verification

On the test server with the fix:

- Service cyborg: five glasses used 5000 J (1000 each, which is cost 20 x 50 J). Ninety-five more glasses were then fabricated until the cell was empty; the last `use_matter()` returned 0 ("not enough power"). So about 100 glasses per full 100000 J cell.
- Peacekeeper cookie synthesizer: the cyborg's examine read "It draws on your power cell, which can pay for 2000 cookie-units." The first cookie used 5000 J (5% of the cell). A second click inside the 10 second cooldown returned `ITEM_INTERACT_BLOCKING` with the "still recharging!" balloon alert. A ghost's examine still shows `0/10` because that is the unchanged non-cyborg path.
- No runtimes appeared in `runtime.log`.

Not checked: how the new cost feels in play, and clicking with the tool as a player (the tool was driven through Lua).

## Notes

- `cyborg_energy_per_unit` (0.5% of a standard cell per matter unit) is a balance guess. With it a cookie costs 5% of a cell, paper about 0.5% and dice 10%. Maintainers may want a different value, or per-item costs.
- The cookie synthesizer is described as "self-recharging", but for cyborgs it now draws from the cell, so its description no longer matches.
- Examine shows `0/10` to non-cyborg viewers (such as ghosts), which is the existing behaviour for the unused variable.
