## Summary

Riding creatures whose riding component sets `override_allow_spacemove` (vatbeast, carp, megacarp) are allowed to step in space, but the mob itself still reports that it has nothing to push against. Every step they take therefore also starts a Newtonian drift impulse, and holding a key stacks the impulses into the "slingshot" the issue describes. The fix makes the riding component answer the space-move check itself, the way the hover skateboard already does, so these steeds no longer drift from their own steps. The syndicate pony stopping dead in space was reproduced but is the intended behaviour (it is treated like an unsupported spaceman), so it was left alone. The goliath offset report (#75893) could not be reproduced on the current sprites.

## The bug

### #87677

Two symptoms are reported:

- A tamed syndicate pony ridden out of an airlock stops dead when it reaches a space turf and cannot move in any direction.
- A tamed vatbeast ridden in space moves oddly: sometimes it snaps along the grid, sometimes it is "slingshot" across space when a movement key is held and then released.

A comment on the issue notes this is Newtonian movement and probably affects all space-capable rideables. Another comment says that, according to the pull request that added them, ponies are space-proof but not meant to gallop through space.

### #75893

A 2023 report that a ridden goliath looks offset after the lavaland resprites, with a screenshot of the rider's position.

## Reproduction

### Vatbeast drift (#87677), on master

1. In a running round, using the Lua editor, spawn a human and a `/mob/living/basic/vatbeast`, tame it, and buckle the human onto it. Move the pair onto an open space turf.
2. Make the rider take a single step east through the riding component's driver-move path, then wait.
3. Observed: the vatbeast and rider ended up seven tiles from where they began (from x=31 to x=38 on the test map) from that one step, i.e. the step itself started a drift. Holding a key adds a further impulse for each step, which stacks into the reported slingshot.

### Pony (#87677), on master

1. Spawn a `/mob/living/basic/pony/syndicate`, tame it, buckle a human on it, and place the pair in open space with no wall or lattice nearby.
2. Try to move east.
3. Observed: the move was blocked ("stops dead"). The test did not exercise pushing off a wall or lattice in game; that part is from reading code only.

These runs used proc calls and the riding component's movement path from the Lua editor, not a player holding a real movement key; the "held key" case was shown only after the fix (see Verification).

### Goliath (#75893), on master

1. Spawned a saddled, tamed goliath with a human rider.
2. Rendered the pair facing each of north, south, east and west and looked at where the rider sat on the goliath.
3. Observed: the rider sat on the saddle hump in all four directions (north and south centred, east and west on the back of the body). Nothing looked offset. One side observation: while the goliath was in its AI wind-up icon state it rendered oddly, which is unrelated and was reset.

## Cause

### Vatbeast, carp and megacarp

`/datum/component/riding/creature` has `Process_Spacemove()` (`code/datums/components/riding/riding_mob.dm:108`):

```dm
return override_allow_spacemove || living_parent.Process_Spacemove(direction, continuous_move)
```

`override_allow_spacemove` is TRUE for the carp (`:459`), megacarp (`:478`) and vatbeast (`:497`). `driver_move()` (`:112`) uses that check to decide whether the steed may move, so the steeds are allowed to `step()` in space.

But the movement code does not ask the riding component. After a step, `atom/movable/Moved()` calls `newtonian_move()` (`code/game/atoms_movable.dm:912`), and `newtonian_move()` begins with `Process_Spacemove(angle2dir(inertia_angle), continuous_move = TRUE)` (`:1334`) on the mob itself. For a ridden animal in space with nothing to hold on to that returns FALSE, so every step adds a drift impulse. A single step then carries the steed several tiles, and a held key stacks the impulses.

The hover skateboard already solves the same problem: it registers a handler on `COMSIG_MOVABLE_SPACEMOVE` that stops the drift check (`code/datums/components/riding/riding_vehicle.dm:270`). The creature riding component had no equivalent.

### Pony

The pony's riding component does not set `override_allow_spacemove`. `driver_move()` therefore falls through to the pony's own `Process_Spacemove()`, which is FALSE in space without something adjacent to push off, exactly as for an unsupported spaceman. A wall or lattice next to the pony lets it move. This matches the maintainer comment that ponies are not meant to gallop through space.

### Goliath

Not applicable (not reproduced). For reference, the goliath's rider offsets are server-side values in `/datum/component/riding/creature/goliath/get_rider_offsets_and_layers` (`riding_mob.dm:568`: north/south `0,12`, east `-4,12`, west `3,12`), and the goliath mob is a 64-pixel-wide sprite with a pixel offset of -12.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 87677: space-capable steeds no longer drift from their own steps". In `/datum/component/riding/creature/RegisterWithParent()` it registers, when `override_allow_spacemove` is set, a handler for `COMSIG_MOVABLE_SPACEMOVE` on the steed:

```dm
/datum/component/riding/creature/proc/on_spacemove(datum/source, movement_dir, continuous_move)
	SIGNAL_HANDLER
	return COMSIG_MOVABLE_STOP_SPACEMOVE
```

This makes the mob's own space-move check succeed while it is under such a riding component, so `newtonian_move()` no longer starts a drift from its steps. It follows the hover skateboard's approach, and it only affects riding components that already declare they allow space movement. No change was made for the pony, or to the goliath offsets.

## Verification

After rebuilding and restarting the test server, with a ridden vatbeast in open space:

- One step east: the vatbeast and rider stayed one tile from the start (x=31) instead of drifting to x=38.
- Six steps with the key held: they ended at x=37 and stopped; no drift afterwards.

Not checked: carp and megacarp were not ridden in game (they share the same change and the same cause); pushing off walls or lattice with the pony was not tested in game; behaviour next to space walls or with a jetpack or other drift sources while riding was not tested; no unit tests were run; a real client holding a movement key was not used.

## Notes

- Whether ponies should be able to move in space (issue comment: "they should be able to push off walls and structures like everything else, and keep their momentum") is a design decision. The code behaves as a spaceman does: with a wall or lattice adjacent a pony can move, but it cannot move away from support. The pony was left unchanged because of the maintainer comment above.
- #75893 appears to be outdated: the screenshot predates the current goliath sprites and the current offsets look right in all four directions. It could probably be closed.
