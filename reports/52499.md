## Summary

These three cyborg issues were examined together. The janitor cyborg's paint remover never removed paint from anything, because its check `!isturf(x) || !isobj(x)` is true for every atom; the fix changes `||` to `&&`. Cyborgs could not load a pulled body into a gibber because the loading code is only in `attack_hand()`, and cyborgs reach machines through `attack_robot()`, which only tries `interact()`; the fix makes the gibber's `attack_robot()` call `attack_hand()`. The auto-wash report (#73287) was not reproduced: auto-wash activated and deactivated normally, and the one way it was seen to cancel looks intentional.

## The bug

### #52499: paint remover

The janitor cyborg's module includes a paint remover (`code/modules/mob/living/silicon/robot/robot_model.dm:469`). Using it on a painted object does nothing. The issue was reported in 2020 for objects other than floor tiles. On current master the item does nothing on objects or on turfs, because its interaction handler bails out before it reaches the colour removal.

### #88633: gibber

A cyborg can grab a body (control-click gives "You grab X passively!") but cannot put it into a gibber by clicking the gibber. Nothing happens. (Comments on the issue debate whether this is deliberate. Players can already load a gibber by grabbing and clicking it, and the issue author suggests limiting borgs if the concern is abuse.)

### #73287: auto-wash

The reporter said that pressing Activate Auto-Wash with a full bucket starts a progress bar that closes quickly. A commenter could replicate it only when moving before the bar finished.

## Reproduction

All checks used a janitor cyborg that was spawned with the Lua editor and then possessed. The test server ran master `87b93338` for the "before" results.

### #52499

1. Spawn a closet and give it a colour of `#ff0000` at colour priority 3 (the priority the remover clears, `WASHABLE_COLOUR_PRIORITY`).
2. As the cyborg, select the paint remover module and click the closed closet.

Observed on master: the click did nothing useful (the closet simply opened). It never removed the paint.

### #88633

1. Spawn a bolted, powered gibber and a dead human next to it.
2. As the cyborg, control-click the human ("You grab ... passively!"), then click the gibber.

Observed on master: nothing happened on the gibber click.

### #73287

1. Fill the cyborg's bucket with water (50 units of water were added through the Lua editor).
2. Stand still and click Activate Auto-Wash once.
3. Click Activate Auto-Wash a second time during the warm-up, or move during the warm-up.

Observed on master: one click on Activate Auto-Wash gave a 4 second progress bar, then the wash sounds, and auto-wash was on; De-Activate Auto-Wash worked. A second click during the warm-up printed "activation cancelled!" and ended the progress bar, and moving also cancelled it. No runtime was seen.

## Cause

### #52499

`/obj/item/paint/paint_remover/interact_with_atom()` in `code/game/objects/items/paint.dm` begins:

```
if(!isturf(interacting_with) || !isobj(interacting_with))
	return NONE
```

(`paint.dm:136-137`.) No atom is both a turf and an object, so for any atom at least one of the two negations is true, and the proc always returned `NONE` before reaching `remove_atom_colour(WASHABLE_COLOUR_PRIORITY)`. The condition was meant to read "neither a turf nor an object". The issue's commenters link the old fix PR #43358, but that PR predates the report, and the code has since been rewritten.

### #88633

`/obj/machinery/gibber/attack_hand()` (`code/modules/food_and_drinks/machinery/gibber.dm:90-130`) holds the whole "put the pulled body in" logic (`user.pulling`, the checks for clothing and buckled mobs, the `do_after`, `forceMove` into the gibber). A cyborg's click on a machine goes to `/obj/machinery/attack_robot()` (`code/game/machinery/_machinery.dm:762`), which only calls `_try_interact()`. The gibber has no UI to open, so nothing happens, and `attack_hand()` is never called.

### #73287

Not a defect as far as it was examined. `/datum/action/toggle_buffer/Trigger()` (`robot_model.dm:532`) sets `block_buffer_change = DOING_INTERACTION(owner, "auto_wash_toggle")` (`:538`), and the warm-up `do_after` (`:565`) uses `allow_buffer_activate` as its extra check, which cancels with "activation cancelled!" when that flag is set (`:638`). Clicking the button again during the 4 second warm-up therefore cancels it, as does moving.

## Fix

Two commits.

- "Fix issue 52499: paint remover works on objects and turfs" changes `||` to `&&` in the check at `paint.dm:137`, so the proc only returns early for something that is neither a turf nor an object.
- "Fix issue 88633: cyborgs can load a pulled body into a gibber" adds `/obj/machinery/gibber/attack_robot()` which calls the parent and, if that did not handle the click, returns `attack_hand(user, modifiers)`. This reuses the existing loading logic and its checks instead of duplicating it. Cyborgs can now also start a gibbing cycle with an empty click, like other users; this was a consequence of calling `attack_hand()` whole and was not separately tested.

No change was made for #73287.

## Verification

With the fixed build:

- #52499: with the paint remover as the active module, clicking a closed closet coloured `#ff0000` changed its colour to plain. Clicking a turf coloured `#00ff00` left it with `color = nil` when read afterwards through the Lua editor.
- #88633: after control-clicking a dead human and deselecting the module, clicking the gibber printed "starts to put [name] into the gibber!", then "stuffs [name] into the gibber!". The human left its tile and the gibber showed its loaded overlay.
- No new runtimes were logged in either test.

Not checked: removing paint from item types other than the closet and turf tested; the remover on mobs; non-service cyborg models using the gibber (the issue's suggestion to restrict it to service cyborgs was not implemented); gibbing after loading as a cyborg; non-cyborg silicons (AIs); and a gibber that is unbolted, unpowered or running (only the normal case was tried).

## Notes

- With the paint remover selected, clicking an open closet printed "You can't drop your paint remover module". The closet's own item interaction runs before the remover's. This is separate from the bug fixed here, and was not changed.
- #88633: whether cyborgs should be able to use the gibber at all is a design question. The issue comments argue both ways (compare the crematorium, which cyborgs also cannot use without help), and the fix takes the "yes" side for any cyborg model.
- #73287: the report could not be reproduced and the likely explanations are a second click or movement during the warm-up, but the reporter said they did not move; a different cause on a live round (for example the `extra_checks` returning false for another reason) cannot be ruled out. The runtime pasted in the issue is from the positronic brain and character preview and is unrelated to auto-wash.
