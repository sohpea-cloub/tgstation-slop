# #91584: Walking over a closed trapdoor ignites you when a burning body lies below

## Summary

The report was that a burning corpse dropped through a trapdoor on a lower z-level later set fire to people walking over the closed trapdoor. On master this does not happen. The cause was an ordering problem when a turf is replaced, and master already fixes it by passing the old turf's signal tables into the new turf's constructor. The bug was reproduced only by temporarily undoing that ordering in a scratch edit, which was reverted.

## The bug

As reported: a burning plasmaman corpse was dropped down a trapdoor, the trapdoor closed, and walking over it set the walker on fire, although the body was on the level below.

## Reproduction

On master the bug did not occur:

1. Load a map with a z-level below the test area (the multi-z debug map was used).
2. Using the Lua editor, turn a floor tile into `/turf/open/floor/iron` and add a trapdoor component to it (`/datum/component/trapdoor`, closed).
3. Spawn a human on it, give them 20 fire stacks and ignite them, then open the trapdoor (`try_opening()`). The human fell to the lower level, burning.
4. Waited for the trapdoor to close by itself (5 seconds).
5. Spawned a second human on an adjacent tile and moved them onto the closed trapdoor.

Observed on master: fire stacks 0 before and after, not on fire.

To confirm the cause, the turf-replacement code was temporarily changed to the old ordering (see Cause) and the same steps were run: the walker's fire stacks went from 0 to 9.75 and they were on fire. The temporary change was reverted and left no diff.

The trapdoor was made by adding the component from Lua and opened by calling `try_opening()`, not through the in-game trapdoor controls.

## Cause

When the trapdoor opens, the turf is changed to openspace with `ChangeTurf`. Anything standing on the tile falls during the new turf's `Initialize` (`initialize_occupant`, `code/game/turfs/turf.dm:198`, then `zfall_if_on_turf` in `code/game/turfs/open/openspace.dm:74`).

In the old ordering the new turf was created first and the old turf's signal tables (`_listen_lookup`, `_signal_procs`) were copied onto it afterwards. The falling human's `connect_loc` element (`code/modules/mob/living/carbon/human/human.dm:35`) could therefore not unregister its `COMSIG_ATOM_ENTERED` listener from the turf, because the tables it needed were not there yet. The stale listener, `human.on_entered` (`human.dm:370`, which calls `spreadFire`), stayed on the turf and kept firing for anything that entered that turf, which is how a walker on the closed trapdoor caught fire.

Master already has the fix: `/turf/New(loc, list/inherited_listen_lookup, list/inherited_signal_procs)` (`turf.dm:119`) sets `_listen_lookup` before `Initialize` runs, and `ChangeTurf` passes the old tables in (`code/game/turfs/change_turf.dm:112`: `new path(src, old_listen_lookup, old_signal_procs)`). The unregistration now finds the tables. The old ordering matches the description in upstream PR #91897; which exact commit brought the fix into master was not checked.

## Fix

None. The issue is already fixed on master.

## Verification

Checked in game: master did not ignite the walker; the old ordering did. Not checked: other ways a burning mob can leave a stale listener (for example other turf types), a real trapdoor placed on a map rather than added from Lua, and the player-visible flow with an actual corpse.

## Notes

- The fix was identified as the turf-constructor change; the experiment confirms that the ordering is what causes the bug, not the exact upstream commit.
- Suggest closing #91584 as already fixed.
