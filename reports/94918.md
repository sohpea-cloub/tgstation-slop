## Summary

Three issues share one cause: surgery operations filter on the body part's bodytype or the patient's biotype, so some combinations of organic and robotic patient and part match no operation at all. An organic patient with a robotic head was offered neither "shock brain" nor "full system reboot". An organic patient with a robotic chest was not offered "blood filtration". The Shock Touch hand electrocuted a prepared patient instead of acting as the revival tool. The fix branch has three commits, one per issue.

## The bug

- **#94918.** A corpse of an organic species with an augmented (robotic) head had every surgical state needed for revival (skin open, bone sawed). No revival operation appeared, and the chat only told the surgeon to target the chest.
- **#82675.** An organic patient with a robotic chest could not be blood-filtered. Only "purge hydraulics" was offered.
- **#85657.** Clicking a prepared corpse's head with the Shock Touch hand did not start the revival surgery. It electrocuted the patient ("... electrocutes ...!").

## Reproduction

The setup was built with the Lua editor on the test server. The surgery availability check was called directly, apart from the Shock Touch click, which was a real click.

1. Spawn a human and kill it. Give it the surgical trait `TRAIT_READY_TO_OPERATE` and mark the head and chest as operable. Apply the surgery states on the head: skin open and bone sawed. On the chest, skin open.
2. Replace the head with `/obj/item/bodypart/head/robot` and the chest with `/obj/item/bodypart/chest/robot` (`replace_limb`).
3. Ask the surgeon what operations are available (`get_available_operations(patient, tool, zone)`) with shock paddles on the head, and with a blood filter on the chest.
4. For #85657, give the surgeon the Shock Touch mutation, trigger the action, and click the prepared patient's head in the normal, non-combat mode.

Observed on master:

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Robotic head, shock paddles or shock touch, head zone | no operations (empty list) |
| Organic head, same tool | "shock brain" |
| Robotic chest, blood filter, chest zone | only "purge hydraulics" |
| Organic head, click with Shock Touch hand | chat: "<surgeon> electrocutes <patient>!" and no surgery |

## Cause

**#94918.** `/datum/surgery_operation/basic/revival` ("shock brain", `code/modules/surgery/operations/operation_revival.dm:1`) sets `required_biotype = NONE` (line 18) but inherits `required_bodytype = ~BODYTYPE_ROBOTIC` from `/datum/surgery_operation/basic` (`code/modules/surgery/operations/_operation.dm:1239`). A robotic head therefore fails the bodytype check. The mechanical variant, `/datum/surgery_operation/basic/revival/mechanic` ("full system reboot", `operation_revival.dm:111-113`), sets `required_biotype = MOB_ROBOTIC`. An organic-biotype patient with a robotic head fails that check as well, so neither operation matched.

**#82675.** `/datum/surgery_operation/limb/filter_blood` has `required_bodytype = ~BODYTYPE_ROBOTIC` (`code/modules/surgery/operations/operation_filter.dm:8`). That excludes any robotic chest, even on an organic patient who still has blood.

**#85657.** The attached Shock Touch hand registers `on_hand_hit` on `COMSIG_ITEM_INTERACTING_WITH_ATOM` (`code/modules/spells/spell_types/touch/_touch.dm:122` and `164-172`). From reading the code, this signal fires from the item's own interaction handling in `base_item_interaction` (`atom_tool_acts.dm`), before the mob's `item_interaction`/`perform_surgery` path. So the hand cast its shock on the patient and consumed the click before surgery was considered. Whether surgery runs first for other tools was not examined.

## Fix

Three commits on the branch.

1. **"Fix issue 94918: revival surgery on patients with augmented heads."**
   - A new `revivable_state()` proc holds the shared "dead and not suicided, husked or defib-blacklisted" test, and the base `state_check` now calls it.
   - The mechanical "full system reboot" no longer filters on biotype or bodytype (`required_biotype = NONE`, `required_bodytype = NONE`).
   - It gets its own `state_check`: the patient must be revivable and have a brain, and the head must be robotic or the brain must be robotic (via `brain_check`). Limbless mobs still use `mob_check`.
   - A requirement string, "the patient's head or brain must be cybernetic", was added.
   - Rationale: the organic operation cannot be offered on a robotic head, since its body part filter excludes robotic limbs. The reboot is the existing operation that fits, so it was widened rather than adding a third revival operation.
2. **"Fix issue 82675: blood filtration on organic patients with a robotic chest."**
   - Removed `required_bodytype = ~BODYTYPE_ROBOTIC`.
   - `state_check` now also requires the chest to be organic, or the patient not to have the robotic biotype (`IS_ORGANIC_LIMB(limb) || !(limb.owner.mob_biotypes & MOB_ROBOTIC)`). Fully robotic patients are still excluded.
   - A requirement string was added.
3. **"Fix issue 85657: shock touch can be used as a tool for revival surgery."**
   - `on_hand_hit` now returns `NONE` early when the caster is not in combat mode, the target is a living mob with `TRAIT_READY_TO_OPERATE`, and `get_available_operations(target, hand, zone)` is non-empty. Surgery then handles the click.

## Verification

After the fix, in game:

- Robotic head: the available operations were `["full system reboot"]`. Organic head: `["shock brain"]`.
- Robotic chest with a blood filter: `["blood filtration", "purge hydraulics"]`. Organic chest: `["blood filtration"]`.
- A real click with the Shock Touch hand on an organic head: chat showed "You prepare to give ... brain the spark of life with the shock touch", and later "... wakes up, alive and aware!". The same worked on a robotic head via the full system reboot path, and the patient was revived.

Not checked:

- Blood filtration on a robotic chest was verified for availability only, not a full filtering run.
- The setup was built with Lua rather than through full surgery and augmentation by hand, and the surgeon's availability list was queried directly in most cases.
- Player-facing surgery UI on the radial menu was not exercised.

## Notes

- **Cooldown side effect (#85657).** Using Shock Touch as a surgery tool does not consume the spell's cooldown, and the hand stays in place. Maintainers may want to decide whether a surgical use should spend the spell.
- **Widened reboot.** "Full system reboot" now also applies to an organic head holding a robotic brain on an organic-biotype patient. Before the fix neither operation matched that case.
- **Upstream.** An earlier attempt, PR #95371, was closed without merging and used a different approach.
- **Related.** #82675 and #85657 are separate symptoms of the same filters. #82675 is from 2024, and the comments on it include an argument that toxin damage does not stop defibrillation. The reporter replied that toxin over 200 does.
