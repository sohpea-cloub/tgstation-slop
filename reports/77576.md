## Summary

#77576: a cybernetic heart that reaches the failing state (from damage or after an EMP) had no effect on its owner, because the code that stops a failing heart is gated on `can_heartattack()`, which is always false for robotic hearts, and `is_beating()` only returned the `beating` variable. `is_beating()` now also returns false while the heart is `ORGAN_FAILING`, so the owner gets cardiac arrest. #90795: a dead mob without a stomach "threw up all over" itself when fed; dead mobs now have the food spill out instead. #83512 (soulless brain examine) already gives the right text on master, so nothing was changed.

## #77576: Failing cybernetic heart does not kill the mob

### The bug
Per the report: replace a carbon's heart with a cybernetic one (tier 1 and 3 tried), shoot them with an ion rifle, and the heart takes damage until it reaches the non-functional level; the mob carries on as if nothing happened. The comments agreed it is odd even if cybernetic hearts are meant to be immune to heart attacks: a non-functioning heart is not pumping blood.

### Reproduction
On the test server, with the Lua editor:

1. Spawned a human and inserted `/obj/item/organ/heart/cybernetic` (using the special insert, replacing the natural heart).
2. Set the organ damage to its maximum with `set_organ_damage(maxHealth)`. The heart's `organ_flags` became 1042, which includes `ORGAN_FAILING`.
3. Checked `can_heartattack()` and `undergoing_cardiac_arrest()` and waited.

Result on master: `can_heartattack()` = 0 (robotic heart), `undergoing_cardiac_arrest()` = 0, and oxygen damage stayed 0 after waiting. The failing heart had no effect. The damage was applied directly through a proc; the ion rifle path was not used (an EMP calls `Stop()` on the heart directly, so it stops it briefly, see Notes).

### Cause
`/obj/item/organ/heart/on_life` stops a failing heart only if the owner can have a heart attack (`code/modules/surgery/organs/internal/heart/_heart.dm:129-130`):

```dm
if(!beating || (organ_flags & ORGAN_FAILING))
	if(owner.can_heartattack() && Stop())
```

`can_heartattack()` is false for robotic hearts (`code/modules/mob/living/carbon/life.dm:767`), so `beating` stays TRUE. `is_beating()` (`_heart.dm:107`) returned just `beating`, so `undergoing_cardiac_arrest()` (`life.dm:789`) and `handle_heart` in `human/life.dm` saw a working heart, and no oxygen loss or other consequence followed.

### Fix
Commit "Fix issue 77576: failing cybernetic hearts count as not beating": `is_beating()` now returns `beating && !(organ_flags & ORGAN_FAILING)`, with a comment explaining why. This was preferred to changing `can_heartattack()`, which would remove the robotic heart's heart-attack immunity that maintainers intend. Other callers of `is_beating()` (the stethoscope, the heretic organ and blood regeneration were looked at) are also right to treat a failing heart as not beating.

### Verification
Same setup after the fix: `undergoing_cardiac_arrest()` = 1, and about 20 seconds later the human had 65 oxygen damage (the report also lists unconsciousness and brute damage as effects seen). A healthy cybernetic heart still gave `undergoing_cardiac_arrest()` = 0.

Not checked: the actual ion rifle / EMP path with a client, the stethoscope and other `is_beating()` callers in game (read in code only), and cybernetic hearts of other tiers beyond the default `/obj/item/organ/heart/cybernetic`.

### Notes
- The EMP path already calls `Stop()` directly, so it worked temporarily. The hole was a failing heart after the 10-second restart timer, or failure from damage.
- One commenter suggested the check should simply use the `ORGAN_FAILING` flag; that is effectively what the fix does, in `is_beating()` rather than in `can_heartattack()`.

## #83512: Soulless brain says "spark of life"

### The bug
Examining a brain taken from a soulless corpse is said to show "There's still a spark of life left in this one", which should only appear for a brain with a soul.

### Reproduction
Not reproduced. With Lua, `brain_damage_examine()` returned "This one is completely devoid of life." for both (a) the brain of a mindless dead human and (b) the brain of a dead human given a mind with `mind_initialize()` after the brain was removed.

### Cause / current code
`brain_damage_examine()` (`code/modules/mob/living/brain/brain_item.dm:250-261`) shows the "spark of life" lines only when `brainmob && (decoy_override || brainmob.client || brainmob.get_ghost())`, otherwise "This one is completely devoid of life". `get_ghost()` requires that the ghost can re-enter the corpse. This appears to have been corrected since the 2024 report.

### Not checked
A real player who left the round or is DNR was not tested. No change was made.

## #90795: Beheaded, gutted corpses throw up when fed

### The bug
Force-feeding a corpse with no head and no stomach made it vomit. A commenter said that is intended since there is no stomach to hold the chemicals; the reporter's complaint is that a corpse should not react by "throwing up".

### Reproduction
With Lua on the test server: spawned a human, called `death()`, dropped the head with `drop_limb()`, removed the stomach, and called the edible component's `TakeBite(C, H)` on the corpse. Observed on master: "Unknown throws up all over himself!". The bite was run by calling the proc, not by feeding with a hand.

### Cause
`expel_ingested()` (`code/modules/mob/living/carbon/carbon.dm:385`, called from `code/modules/reagents/chemistry/holder/holder.dm:461` when the eater has no stomach) always printed the "throws up all over" message, regardless of the mob's state.

### Fix
Commit "Fix issue 90795: dead stomachless mobs no longer throw up when fed": when `stat == DEAD`, the message is "[bite] spills right out of [src], with no stomach to hold it." The vomit decal and reagent spill behaviour that followed were kept unchanged.

### Verification
After the fix, the dead case printed "The plain burger spills right out of Unknown, with no stomach to hold it." and a living, stomachless human still printed "throws up all over himself!". Not checked: a real hand-feeding action, and the decal/reagent spill after the new message (unchanged code).

### Notes
The change is only to the message; a corpse still produces the vomit decal.
