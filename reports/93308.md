# #93308: Bioscrambler resets tail and ear colours

| | |
|---|---|
| Issues | [#93308](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/93308): verified (both causes reproduced); fixed for head swaps only |
| Status | Partially fixed: head-swap half fixed and tested; chest-swap half reproduced, not fixed |
| Fix branch | [fix/93308](https://github.com/sohpea-cloub/tgstation-slop/tree/fix/93308) |
| Tested on | tgstation master `87b93338`, 2026-10-23 |

## Summary
After a bioscrambler transformation, tails and ears can lose their colour. Two separate mechanisms were found. When the new body part is a head, hair-coloured organs (cat ears, cat tail) briefly take the new head's default black hair colour; the fix recolours them once the new head has the owner's appearance. When the new part is a chest with no colour of its own, a tail that inherits its colour from the chest loses it and renders gray-white; this was reproduced, but an attempted fix worked only intermittently and was not committed.

## The bug
The report says the tail and/or ears return to their normal gray-white colour after the anomaly transformation, and suggests it depends on whether the head was changed. That matches what was found: the outcome depends on which body part the scrambler picked and on how the organ takes its colour.

## Reproduction
Both cases were reproduced on master by calling `bioscramble('test')` repeatedly on test humans from the Lua editor, not by a real anomaly detonation.
1. Lizard, chest case: spawn a human, make it a lizard, set its mutant colour to `#ff0000` and update its body. Call `bioscramble('test')` a few times per human. On every scramble that picked the chest and gave a chest with no draw colour (alien, plasmaman, fly, abductor, skeleton, monkey), the tail overlay's `draw_color` went from `#ff0000` to null (rendered gray-white). A scramble to an ethereal chest turned it `#808080`.
2. Felinid, head case: give a felinid hair colour `#00ff00` and scramble it repeatedly. On steps where the head was replaced, the cat ears and cat tail took `#000000`. About 2 of 25 scrambles showed this before the fix.

## Cause
`/mob/living/carbon/proc/bioscramble` (`code/modules/mob/living/carbon/carbon_defense.dm:629`) calls `new_part.replace_limb(src)` (line 661) and then `update_body(TRUE)` (line 668).

**Chest case.** `update_body(TRUE)` reaches the bodypart's `update_limb`, which recolours every mutant overlay with `overlay.inherit_color(src, force = TRUE)` (`code/modules/surgery/bodyparts/_bodyparts.dm:1250`, also 1322). For overlays with `ORGAN_COLOR_INHERIT`, `inherit_color` sets `draw_color = bodypart_owner.draw_color` (`code/datums/bodypart_overlays/mutant_bodypart_overlay.dm:148`). A new chest whose `draw_color` is null therefore wipes the tail colour.

**Head case.** `/obj/item/bodypart/head/update_limb` (`code/modules/surgery/bodyparts/head.dm:173`) calls the parent `update_limb` first, which runs the forced `inherit_color`. For `ORGAN_COLOR_HAIR` overlays that reads `my_head.hair_color` (`mutant_bodypart_overlay.dm:162`). Only afterwards does `copy_appearance_from(owner)` (`head.dm:183`) set the new head's hair colour, so the overlays were coloured from the head's default `COLOR_BLACK` (`head.dm:44`).

## Fix
One commit, "Fix issue 93308: keep hair-coloured tails and ears when the head is replaced". In `head.dm` `update_limb`, after `copy_appearance_from(owner)`, it loops over the owner's bodyparts and their mutant overlays and re-runs `inherit_color(part, force = TRUE)` on those with `color_source == ORGAN_COLOR_HAIR`. This re-colours them once the hair colour is known, and affects only head creation (`is_creating`).

No commit covers the chest case. An attempt in `bioscramble` snapshotted the picked part's overlay colours before `replace_limb` and restored them after `update_body(TRUE)`. A first version that restored before `update_body(TRUE)` did nothing. The later version worked when the sequence was run by hand (alien and abductor chests kept the colour) but was intermittent through the real proc: abductor, ghost and snail chests kept `#ff0000`, while alien, plasmaman and fly chests still ended null. The cause of that intermittency was not found.

## Verification
- Head fix: after building, a felinid was scrambled 3 x 30 times (90 scrambles) with zero wrong-coloured cat ears or tails, against about 2 in 25 before.
- Not verified: the chest case (no fix committed), and any real bioscrambler anomaly detonation, as opposed to direct calls to `bioscramble`.

## Notes
- A cleaner chest fix probably belongs in `update_limb`/`inherit_color`: let `ORGAN_COLOR_INHERIT` overlays keep their colour when the new bodypart has no `draw_color`. This was not tried.
- Unrelated noise during testing: a `stack_trace` at `mutant_bodypart_overlay.dm:33` when bioscramble inserts a cat tail into a non-felinid human, and a runtime in `tails.dm` (`get_base_icon_state` with a null sprite datum) for a cat tail on non-felinid DNA. Both aborted Lua runs and were pre-existing.
