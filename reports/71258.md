## Summary
Activating a colossus "theme warp" anomalous crystal that turns floors into grass places a grass floor on top of bare plating. Smuggler's satchels lying on that plating then become hidden under the new floor, the same as when a player lays a floor tile over them. The reporter considered this odd. The code path is the ordinary underfloor-object rule, which applies to every hideable object (satchels, cables, pipes), so no fix was made. Skipping plating in dimension themes would be a design change that leaves plating unthemed.

## The bug
The issue describes using a colossus crystal that changes the floor (the grass one). Smuggler's satchels that were not under floor tiles before end up under the new floor tiles ("seems weird!"). The reporter's rounds also had testmerged Caves and Heretic changes, which were not needed here.

## Reproduction
Reproduced with admin tools on a running round, by applying the crystal's terrain theme directly rather than by killing a colossus and touching its crystal.

1. Using the Lua editor, turned a floor turf into plating (`ChangeTurf` to `/turf/open/floor/plating`).
2. Spawned a `/obj/item/storage/backpack/satchel/flat/empty` (a smuggler's satchel) on that turf.
3. Took the `/datum/dimension_theme/natural` theme (grass floors) from `SSmaterials.dimensional_themes` and called `apply_theme_to_list_of_turfs()` on a list holding that one turf. This is the call the crystal makes.
4. Read the satchel's `invisibility`, `alpha` and `anchored`, and the turf name.

| | invisibility | alpha | anchored | turf |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Before | 0 | 255 | 0 | plating |
| After | 60 (`INVISIBILITY_OBSERVER`) | 128 | 1 | "grass patch" |

A power cable on the same turf, visible while the turf was plating, was likewise hidden again under the grass floor.

## Cause
The chain, all at master:

- `/obj/machinery/anomalous_crystal/theme_warp/ActivationReaction` (`code/modules/mining/lavaland/mining_loot/megafauna/colossus.dm:236`) calls `terrain_theme.apply_theme_to_list_of_turfs(current_area.get_turfs_from_all_zlevels())`, so it themes every turf of the area, once per area.
- For floors, `/datum/dimension_theme/proc/transform_floor` (`code/game/objects/effects/anomalies/anomalies_dimensional_themes.dm:129`) calls `affected_floor.replace_floor(pick_weight(replace_floors), ...)`.
- `/turf/open/proc/replace_floor` (`code/game/turfs/open/_open.dm:288`) places a new floor that needs an overfloor on top of existing plating with `place_on_top()` instead of replacing the plating. The grass floor type has that property.
- The new turf's `levelupdate()` (`code/game/turfs/turf.dm:517`) sends `COMSIG_OBJ_HIDE` to every initialized object on it with the turf's `underfloor_accessibility`, which defaults to `UNDERFLOOR_HIDDEN` (`code/game/turfs/turf.dm:22`).
- `/datum/element/undertile` (`code/datums/elements/undertile.dm:38`) responds by hiding the object and, for items such as the satchel, anchoring it.

This is the same result as a player laying a floor tile on plating. Satchels, cables and pipes under floors are intended to be found by removing the tile (a crowbar on the grass, in this case). Nothing is specific to smuggler's satchels.

## Fix
None. A change would have to skip plating in dimension themes, or reveal hideable objects afterwards. The first leaves plating unthemed in converted areas, and the second breaks the cable and pipe case. Neither was tried.

## Verification
No fix, so nothing to verify. The behaviour was confirmed with the single-turf test above.

Not checked:
- An actual colossus fight, or a real crystal activation by touch.
- Other dimension themes, or a whole-area conversion.
- Whether hidden satchels can be recovered afterwards by removing the grass (expected from the rule; not exercised).

## Notes
- Possible design directions for a maintainer: leave as is and close the issue as intended; or let `theme_warp` skip turfs that already hold hideable objects.
- Related: map-generated satchels are placed under tiles on purpose (`code/controllers/subsystem/minor_mapping.dm` hides them with the turf's `underfloor_accessibility`), so the game already treats "satchel under a floor" as the normal state.
