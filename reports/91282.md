## Summary

#91282 says the captain's antique laser gun, which calls itself indestructible, can end up permanently unusable after something such as an explosion, presumably because its inner lens (the energy ammo casing) or cell is deleted. On master, devastation-level explosions did not remove the cell or the lens from any of four antique lasers placed in different ways, and one of them fired normally afterwards. The code explains why: the gun's cell is now bomb-proof, and the gun's own flags stop an explosion from reaching its contents. Only explosions were tested, so other ways of losing the lens or cell are not ruled out. #94187 (the antique laser turning into a Type 5 laser gun when empty) was not investigated.

## The bug

### #91282

The reporter had a captain's antique laser gun end up "absolutely unusable and useless" after "something happen to it like an explosion (inconsistent but it happens)". They suspected the energy lens inside the gun was deleted. A commenter pointed to PR #91285 as a related fix. The issue does not give exact steps or a round state beyond that.

### #94187

When the captain's antique laser ran out of charge, the reporter says it "disappeared from your hand, dropped on the floor and a Type 5 laser rifle [was] in its place". The reporter could not reproduce it, there is nothing in the chat log, and a maintainer could not reproduce it either. They also noted that the gun draws its charge level with overlays, so it should not change icon or type when empty, and that no code causing the gun to drop was apparent. The issue is labelled "Needs Reproducing".

## Reproduction

### #91282: could not reproduce

Using the Lua editor on the test server (as an observer, with a round running):

1. Spawned four `/obj/item/gun/energy/laser/captain` guns:
   - one on the floor at the explosion's epicentre,
   - one inside a backpack on the epicentre tile,
   - one in the hand of a spawned human on the epicentre tile,
   - one on the floor two tiles from the epicentre, inside the devastation range.
2. Called `explosion(epicentre, 3, 5, 7)` (devastation 3, heavy 5, light 7). After a few seconds, read each gun's `cell`, `ammo_type` lens, the lens's location, and whether the gun could shoot.
3. Result for all four guns: the power cell still existed, was not deleted and was still full (charge 10000), the lens casing still existed with the gun as its location, and `can_shoot()` was true.
4. Took the gun that the blast had thrown to a nearby tile, put it into a spawned human's hand, gave that human to the admin's client and fired it at a nearby tile. It fired normally (the shot hit with a hellfire laser). The round's runtime log showed no gun, cell or casing runtimes.

The first attempt at the test script failed on a Lua error (an assignment to a read-only table) and was redone with a local table; this did not affect the result.

### #94187: not tested

No reproduction was attempted.

## Cause

There is no defect to point to for #91282 on master; the checks that protect the gun are these:

- Energy guns make their cell bomb-proof in `/obj/item/gun/energy/Initialize()`, and also indestructible when the gun itself is indestructible (`code/modules/projectiles/guns/energy.dm:103-105`):

  ```dm
  if(cell && resistance_flags & INDESTRUCTIBLE)
  	cell.resistance_flags |= INDESTRUCTIBLE
  cell.resistance_flags |= BOMB_PROOF
  ```

  The triage notes for this issue attribute this to PR #91284, merged on 2025-05-26, a few days after the report; the PR itself was not looked at.
- `/obj/ex_act()` returns without doing anything for objects that are `INDESTRUCTIBLE` or `BOMB_PROOF` (`code/game/objects/obj_defense.dm:10-12`), so neither the gun nor its cell takes damage.
- The antique laser is `INDESTRUCTIBLE` and has `PREVENT_CONTENTS_EXPLOSION_1` (`code/modules/projectiles/guns/energy/laser.dm:152-153`). The explosion subsystem skips the contents of any holder with that flag when it collects the items on the epicentre tile (`code/controllers/subsystem/explosions.dm:436-437`), so the lens casing inside the gun is not hit either.

Before those protections, the cell (and the gun's contents) could be destroyed by an explosion while the gun itself survived, which fits the report. The reporter's description was never turned into a confirmed trace for the old behaviour here; this is an inference from the code, not something that was run on an older commit.

For #94187, nothing in the inputs identifies a cause. The code that was read for #91282 does not change the gun's type, and that gun's charge level is shown through overlays; no path that replaces the gun with a Type 5 laser gun was found or looked for.

## Fix

None. No change was made or committed.

## Verification

Only the check above: four antique lasers kept their cell and lens through a devastation-level explosion and a gun fired afterwards.

Not checked:

- Anything other than explosions: for example acid, EMP, a crusher or disposal, a singularity or supermatter dusting, being deleted with its holder (a destroyed backpack or locker), cell removal by a tool, or deconstruction. The reporter does not say which event it was ("inconsistent but it happens").
- Lighter explosions (only one `explosion(3, 5, 7)` call was made) and guns that were being carried by a mob that gets gibbed.
- Whether the old behaviour existed at the time of the report; the issue is from 2025-05-21 and master was not rolled back.
- #94187 entirely.

## Notes

- If #91282's reporter still sees the problem on a current server, the missing detail is what happened to the gun. The code only guards against explosions; a different way of destroying the lens or cell would need its own look.
- #94187 is flagged "Needs Reproducing" upstream and the reporter and a maintainer both failed to reproduce it. The antique laser is `selfcharge` (`laser.dm:151`), so it normally recharges by itself, and the report may involve something other than emptying the gun (the reporter also found nothing in the chat log). Nothing in this work backs or refutes the transformation.
