## Summary

When a player is polymorphed into a cyborg (the `robot` wabbajack result), their mind is moved into the brainmob of the cyborg's MMI instead of into the cyborg. The player is left stuck as a brain inside a mindless cyborg: they can talk through the MMI but cannot move or use any interface. The next polymorph then starts from a mindless mob, so a complex mob gets no player, a basic mob (such as a blob spore) runs its AI, and the MMI's brainmob is deleted, which ghosts the player. The fix stops the MMI's `transfer_identity` from taking the mind, so the normal polymorph code moves it into the new mob. It also deletes the cyborg's default brain instead of dropping it on the floor.

## The bug

Reports on the three issues:

- #98037: after being polymorphed into a borg, the victim "has no control or interface for their new form but can talk". A brain also appeared on the ground. Polymorphing again either kicked a player out of a complex mob (monkey, human) or left a simple mob (blob spore) acting on its own AI.
- #72560: a human turned into a Syndicate assault cyborg and then a monkey by staff of change. As a monkey the character moved on its own with the monkey NPC's behaviour while the player was supposedly in control.
- #42685 (2019): after a few staff of change hits, one of them turning the victim into a standard-module borg, the next hit kept them a borg but invisible and mute. The reporter could still move and interact. A comment says that polymorphing someone into a borg and then quickly into something else left a cyborg camera on the new body.

What actually happens is that the player's mind ends up in a brainmob inside the cyborg's MMI. The cyborg itself has no mind and no key.

## Reproduction

The test server was reproduced with admin tools, by calling the polymorph proc directly, not by hitting a mob with a real staff of change or wand. The proc is the one those items use, so the code path from the polymorph onward is the same, but the projectile and spell hit were not exercised.

1. Start a round and open the Lua editor as an admin.
2. Spawn a human and put a player in it (set the human's `ckey` to the admin's ckey).
3. As that mob, call `wabbajack("robot")` on it. The random pick among robot types often gives a drone or a bot, so repeat the call, in separate Lua runs, until the result is a cyborg. The key moves with the mob between runs.
4. When the result is a cyborg, look at the client's mob and at the cyborg's mind.

Observed on master:

- The result was a Syndicate medical cyborg. The player's own mob was a `/mob/living/brain` inside a "Man-Machine Interface", not the cyborg.
- The chat showed "You feel slightly disoriented. That's normal when you're just a brain."
- The cyborg had no mind, which matches the reports (the player can speak through the brain but cannot move the cyborg or use its interface).
- The cyborg's default brain was not in the MMI. The reports of a brain appearing on the ground match the code path described below, but the floor brain was not separately examined on master.

The follow-up polymorph symptoms (a complex mob getting no player, a basic mob running its AI, the player ghosted) were derived from reading the code. They were not reproduced on master. After the fix they could not occur in the cases that were tried (see Verification).

## Cause

The cyborg is created in `mob/living/proc/wabbajack` for the `WABBAJACK_ROBOT` case. For a silicon result it copies the player's identity into the cyborg's MMI (`code/modules/mob/living/living.dm:1569`):

```dm
created_robot.mmi.transfer_identity(src) //Does not transfer key/client.
```

The comment is wrong in practice. `/obj/item/brain_processor/organic/transfer_identity` (`code/modules/mob/living/brain/processor/organic.dm:157`) takes the user's brain out with

```dm
new_brain.Remove(transferred_user, special = TRUE)
```

(`organic.dm:163`). `/obj/item/organ/brain/Remove` ends with `transfer_identity(organ_owner)` unless `NO_ID_TRANSFER` is passed (`code/modules/mob/living/brain/brain_item.dm:131`). `/obj/item/organ/brain/proc/transfer_identity` (`brain_item.dm:141`) makes a brainmob and then does

```dm
if(L.mind && L.mind.current && !decoy_override)
	L.mind.transfer_to(brainmob)
```

(`brain_item.dm:169-170`). So by the time `wabbajack` reaches `on_wabbajacked` (`living.dm:1756`), the old mob has no mind and no key. `on_wabbajacked` has `if(mind) ... else if(key)` and finds neither, so it transfers nothing. The player stays in the brainmob and the new cyborg is mindless.

The same defect exists in the other branch: when the user has no brain organ, `organic.dm:161` calls `new_brain.transfer_identity(transferred_user)` directly, which also moves the mind.

On the next polymorph the source mob has no mind and no key, so the new mob is created without a player. A basic mob then starts its AI. The old cyborg is deleted, including its MMI, which deletes the brainmob holding the player and kicks them out. When a cyborg is polymorphed into a cyborg, the same happens through the `new(src)` branch plus `brain_item.dm` `transfer_identity`.

The brain that appeared on the ground is `insert_brain` (`organic.dm:107`): it calls `remove_brain()` when the MMI already has a brain, and `remove_brain`'s default destination is `drop_location()`. The cyborg's default brain, created with the MMI, was therefore dropped when the player's brain was inserted.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 98037: polymorphing into a cyborg left the player's mind in a brainmob":

- `brain_item.dm`: `/obj/item/organ/brain/proc/transfer_identity` gained a `transfer_mind = TRUE` argument. The mind is only moved into the brainmob when it is TRUE. The default keeps every existing caller unchanged.
- `organic.dm`: the MMI's `transfer_identity` now removes the brain with `movement_flags = NO_ID_TRANSFER`, so `Remove` no longer moves the mind. It then calls `new_brain.transfer_identity(transferred_user, transfer_mind = FALSE)` for both the "has a brain" and "makes a new brain" cases, so only the name and DNA are copied. Finally it deletes whatever brain the MMI already holds (`qdel(remove_brain(null))`) before `insert_brain`, so the cyborg's default brain is replaced and not dropped.

This keeps the MMI's contract as the comment at the call site states it: identity is copied and the key/mind stays with the source mob, which lets the polymorph code move the mind to the new cyborg as it does for every other result. An alternative would have been to move the mind back out of the brainmob in `wabbajack`, but that would leave `transfer_identity` doing something its caller does not expect.

## Verification

On the test server, after the fix:

- A human with a player was polymorphed with `wabbajack("robot")` until a Syndicate medical cyborg resulted. The client's mob was the cyborg, the cyborg's mind was the player's mind, the MMI's brainmob had no mind, and the MMI held "Everett Moberly's brain". No brain item was on the floor.
- That cyborg was polymorphed into a monkey. The result was a `/mob/living/carbon/human/species/monkey`, the player's key was kept, the mind was kept, and the monkey's AI controller status was `ai_off`. This is the #72560 scenario.
- Drone, cyborg and cyborg-to-cyborg results were also tried (the last producing a Syndicate assault cyborg). In each case the client's mob was the new robot, the mind was kept and invisibility was 0. This is the #42685 scenario and no invisible or muted player was seen.
- A cyborg polymorphed into an animal (a banded snake) kept the client and the mind, with the AI off.
- No new runtimes were logged, apart from two caused by the test's own Lua probes.

Not checked:

- A hit by a real staff of change, wand or the wizard shuttle's statue. The proc was called directly.
- Mutadone on the monkey (the human-form part of #72560). It is not considered a bug: a commenter pointed out the monkey would not keep the player's appearance after mutadone.
- A polymorph into a blob spore specifically.
- Polymorphing into non-cyborg silicons, or the `WABBAJACK_ROBOT` path for a player with no brain organ.
- The situation before the fix for the "player ghosted after the second polymorph" and "AI takes over" claims; these follow from the code and the observed mindless cyborg but were not run on master.

## Notes

- #42685: this older report could not be reproduced on master. Borg-to-borg and borg-to-animal polymorphs kept the player visible, unmuted and in control (this was checked on the fixed build; on master the same polymorphs lose the mind as described above). The code behind the 2019 invisible/mute symptom may since have been rewritten, but the report's "borg then borg" symptom has the same mind loss as #98037 and is covered by this fix. The camera leak mentioned in a comment was not tested.
- #72560: the report's "I was not my human self when changed back" is not a bug: a monkey made by magic and then turned into a human by mutadone has no reason to keep the original's name and appearance. Only the active monkey AI while a player controlled it was a bug, and it is fixed.
- Design point for maintainers: `transfer_mind` is an argument on the shared brain proc. Another way to do it would be to make the cyborg code in `wabbajack` handle the MMI itself, but this keeps the change in the brain code that causes the problem.
- The comment at `living.dm:1569` ("Does not transfer key/client.") describes what the call is meant to do and is accurate after this change.
