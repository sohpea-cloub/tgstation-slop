## Summary

Player-controlled basic mobs buckled to a hoverboard drive it normally, so the reported behaviour is not a bug in the riding code. The issue's screenshot shows animals sitting on skateboards with nobody controlling them. An AI-controlled animal buckled to a board has no driver, because nothing turns AI intent into board movement. Making AI mobs steer a board would be a new feature, so no fix was written.

## The bug

The issue (title "Simplemobs don't move when buckled to hover boards", body "Tragic!" with a screenshot) says critters buckled to a hoverboard don't move it. The screenshot shows a chicken and a crab, each on a skateboard on a beach, with a player standing next to them.

## Reproduction

Using the Lua editor on the test server:

1. Spawn a `/obj/vehicle/ridden/scooter/skateboard/hoverboard`, spawn a mob on it, and call `board:buckle_mob(mob, true)`.
2. Take control of the mob (set its ckey to the admin's) and walk east.

Observed: a corgi went from 107,144 to 110,144 and the board came along. A lizard went from 70,144 to 73,144 and the board moved with it (it stopped against a mothroach).

Driver path, calling `board:relaymove(mob, EAST)` directly rather than walking, with 14 basic mob types each on its own tile: mouse, cockroach, lizard, mothroach, chicken, pug, giant spider, crab, goat, cow, sloth, frog, snake, and space carp. All buckled, and each moved the board by one tile. The space carp reported no movement only because its tile was against a wall. In an earlier run a cat and a fox also moved the board by one tile.

An unpossessed, live animal on a board was not observed directly. A hostile carp wandering the test area killed some test mobs, and the chicken tried was dead (stat 3). That part rests on reading the code.

## Cause

There is no defect in the riding code; the behaviour follows from how vehicle movement is driven:

- A buckled mob's client movement is forwarded to the vehicle: `code/modules/mob/mob_movement.dm:84` calls `mob.buckled.relaymove(mob, direct)`.
- `/obj/vehicle/relaymove` (`code/modules/vehicles/_vehicle.dm:154`) only acts when `is_driver(user)`, then calls `relaydrive`.
- The riding component's `driver_move` and `handle_ride` (`code/datums/components/riding/riding_vehicle.dm:35` and `:86`) do the movement.
- Basic mobs do get `VEHICLE_CONTROL_DRIVE` when buckled, through `post_buckle_mob` and `add_occupant` (`code/modules/vehicles/ridden.dm:34`, `_vehicle.dm:136`), and the `RIDER_NEEDS_LEGS`/`RIDER_NEEDS_ARMS` checks (`riding_vehicle.dm:58`, `:70`) do not block them.

An AI-controlled animal moves by trying to walk itself, but a buckled movable's own `Move` is refused, and nothing passes the AI's intent to the board. That matches the screenshot.

## Fix

None. Nothing is wrong in the existing code path, and supporting AI riders would add behaviour.

## Verification

The player-controlled results above are the verification that the code path works. Not checked: an unpossessed live animal staying put on a board (see above), and any AI behaviour change, since none was written.
