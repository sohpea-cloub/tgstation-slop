## Summary

None of the three reported Instant Summons failures happen on current master. A marked item sitting in a storage implant is recalled on its own and the implant and its contents stay in the caster (#42231). Trying to recall a head that is still attached to a mob does nothing, and no fake head appears (#43582). When a xenomorph or a simple mob casts the spell, the recalled item lands on the floor of its tile instead of appearing in a nonexistent hand (#45007). The only leftover is cosmetic: a failed recall of an attached head gives no feedback message.

All tests used the Lua editor and called the spell's `cast()` directly on spawned mobs, which skips the spell's cooldown. Only the spell's code path was exercised, not a player pressing the action button.

## The bugs as reported

- **#42231**: mark an item with Instant Summons, put it into a storage implant, cast again to recall it. The reporter says the storage implant vanishes along with its contents, and only the marked item arrives in hand.
- **#43582**: marking a head that is attached to a mob makes the spell summon a spriteless "head" object while the mob keeps its head. Cutting the head off later teleports the fake head to where the real one dropped.
- **#45007**: (1) a wizard turned into a xenomorph and casting Instant Summons holds the marked item as if it had human hands. (2) As a simple mob, the summoned item shows in the HUD where hands would be, a duplicate appears on the mob, and the duplicate and the "held" item interact oddly. A 2022 comment says part 1 reproduced and part 2 did not (the item was dropped on the simple mob's tile).

## Reproduction attempts

### #42231: storage implant

1. Using the Lua editor, spawn a human and grant it a `/datum/action/cooldown/spell/summonitem`. Give the human a pocket crowbar and cast once, which marks it.
2. Implant an `/obj/item/implant/storage` into the human with force, and put the crowbar into it with `atom_storage:attempt_insert`.
3. Cast again.

Observed: the message "The pocket crowbar suddenly appears in <name>'s hand!" appeared. After that the crowbar's `loc` was the human, the implant was still in the human's `implants`, its `atom_storage` was still a `/datum/storage/implant`, and the implant had not been deleted. Nothing was lost.

### #43582: attached head

1. Using the Lua editor, spawn two humans. Dismember the head of the first and have the second hold it, then mark it with the spell.
2. Reattach the head to the first human with `try_attach_limb()` (it succeeded, owner was the first human).
3. Cast the spell, twice.

Observed: nothing happened. No message was shown, the head's `loc` stayed on the first human, its owner and the human's head bodypart were unchanged, and nothing arrived in the caster's hands. No fake head object was created.

An attached head cannot be marked through normal play, since `put_in_hands()` on an attached head rips it off first. The attached-head state was reached here by marking a held head and reattaching it.

### #45007: xenomorph and simple mob

1. Using the Lua editor, mark a crowbar with a human caster, then put the crowbar on a floor tile.
2. Grant the same spell to an alien hunter and cast it. Repeat with a corgi.

Observed, xenomorph: the "suddenly disappears" and "suddenly appears!" messages appeared (the variant used when the item does not go to a hand). The crowbar was on the floor of the xenomorph's tile and its hand slots were empty. The xenomorph has two `held_items` slots but `put_in_hands()` refused the item.

Observed, corgi (no `held_items`): the crowbar was on the floor of the corgi's tile. In neither case did a duplicate item appear or an item show in a HUD hand.

Part 1 of the issue therefore does not happen on master, and part 2 matches the 2022 comment.

## Cause (why each one behaves as it does)

All three go through `try_recall_item()` in `code/modules/spells/spell_types/self/summonitem.dm:112`.

- **Storage implants (#42231).** The proc walks up the chain of containers around the marked item and brings the outermost one, except it stops when the container is an item flagged `ABSTRACT` (line 126):
  ```dm
  // Being able to summon abstract things because
  // your item happened to get placed there is a no-no
  if(mark_loc.item_flags & ABSTRACT)
  	break
  ```
  Implants are `item_flags = ABSTRACT | DROPDEL` (`code/game/objects/items/implants/implant.dm:8`), so the climb stops at the implant and only the marked item is moved. The old bug described in the issue was most likely the climb pulling in the implant and then deleting it through `DROPDEL`; the `ABSTRACT` check prevents that.
- **Attached head (#43582).** The head's `loc` is a mob, not an item. The `ismob` branch (line 131) breaks out of the loop unless the mob is a silicon, and then the final `holder.dropItemToGround(item_to_retrieve, force = TRUE)` (line 166) fails because the head is not an equipped item, so the proc returns without doing anything. No object is created.
- **Xenomorph and simple mob (#45007).** The final step is `isitem(item_to_retrieve) && caster.put_in_hands(item_to_retrieve)`, with `forceMove(caster.drop_location())` as the fallback (lines 172 to 177). When `put_in_hands()` fails the item goes to the floor of the caster's tile. This is what was observed for both mob types. (Why `put_in_hands()` refuses for a xenomorph was not investigated.)

## Notes

- Not tested: casting from a real player via the action button, cooldown behavior, the abductor baton variant, and other containers (lockers, backpacks, silicons, anchored objects, portable atmospherics) that `try_recall_item()` treats specially.
