## Summary

When a mind is linked to a creator with `enslave_mind_to_creator()` (sentience potion, golem and guardian creation, and others), it copies the creator's cult, revolution or nuclear operative membership once. It then never follows later changes, although the greeting says "If their loyalties change, so do yours." The fix makes the enslaved mind listen for `COMSIG_ANTAGONIST_REMOVED` on the creator's mind and drop the matching antag datum when the creator loses it. It was checked in game for the cult case by removing the creator's cult datum directly. The code was only read, not run, for revolution and nuclear operatives, and the original scenario (the master being polymorphed into a cyborg) was not tested. By reading the code it may not be covered (see Notes).

## The bug

In a blood cult round, a cultist scientist used a sentience potion on a magicarp, so the carp's player was told that its true master was the cultist and "if their loyalties change, so do yours". The carp (which fires bolts of change) then hit its master, who turned into a Syndicate cyborg. Cyborgs cannot stay in the cult, so the master was deconverted ("looks like he just reverted to their old faith!"). The carp stayed a blood cultist.

A commenter clarified that the bolt of change was not what deconverted the master: becoming a silicon was. They agreed the second part, the carp not following its master, is a bug. The deconversion of a cultist turned into a cyborg is intended; only the carp's missing change of loyalty is the bug treated here.

## Reproduction

The potion, the magicarp and the bolt of change were not used. The enslaving proc that the potion calls was called directly on two humans, with the master's cult datum removed directly, so this shows the loyalty-following defect and not the exact in-round sequence.

1. Build the test server with `-DTESTING`. This is needed because `is_convertable_to_cult()` normally refuses a target without a client (`code/modules/antagonists/cult/cult_other.dm:22-25`), which the test mobs do not have. Without it, `add_antag_datum(/datum/antagonist/cult)` on the test mobs did nothing (the master's cult check returned nil in the first attempts).
2. Start the round, observe, and open the Lua editor.
3. Create two humans (master and slave) and call `mind_initialize()` on each.
4. Give the master `/datum/antagonist/cult` with `mind:add_antag_datum(...)`.
5. Call `slave.mind:enslave_mind_to_creator(master)`. This is what the sentience potion does (`code/modules/research/xenobiology/xenobiology.dm:802`).
6. Check `has_antag_datum(/datum/antagonist/cult)` on both: master Cultist, slave Cultist.
7. Call `master.mind:remove_antag_datum(/datum/antagonist/cult)` and check again.

Observed on master: the master has no cult datum, and the slave still has it (the four values returned were Cultist, Cultist, nil, Cultist).

## Cause

`datum/mind/proc/enslave_mind_to_creator()` (`code/datums/mind/antag.dm:180`) checks the creator once. If the creator is a cultist it adds the cult datum, if a revolutionary it adds the slave to the revolution, and if a nuclear operative it adds a nuclear operative datum on the creator's team:

```dm
if(IS_CULTIST(creator))
	add_antag_datum(/datum/antagonist/cult)
else if(IS_REVOLUTIONARY(creator))
	...
else if(IS_NUKE_OP(creator))
	...
enslaved_to = WEAKREF(creator)
```

It then stores only a weak reference to the creator in `enslaved_to` and tells the player that their loyalties will follow their creator's. Nothing watches the creator's antag datums after that. When the creator's cult datum is removed (`antag_datum.dm:343` sends `COMSIG_ANTAGONIST_REMOVED`), nothing on the slave's side reacts. The proc's doc comment says the mind "will only switch teams when their creator does", but only the first part was implemented.

The same proc is used by the sentience potion, golems (`golem_roles.dm:108`), guardians, revived mobs (`living.dm:2920`), heretic monsters, syndicate monkeys and the harvester construct, so the missing follow-up applies to all of them. The fix only does anything for cult, revolution and nuclear operatives, because those are the datums the proc copies.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 31529: enslaved minds leave their creator's cult/rev/nukeop team when the creator does", in `code/datums/mind/antag.dm`:

- In `enslave_mind_to_creator()`, after the existing team copy, it unregisters from the previous creator's mind (if the mind was already enslaved to someone else) and registers `on_creator_antag_removed` on the new creator's mind for `COMSIG_ANTAGONIST_REMOVED`.
- New `/datum/mind/proc/on_creator_antag_removed(creator_mind, removed)` runs when the creator loses an antag datum. It returns early (and unregisters) if the creator the mind is enslaved to no longer resolves or no longer owns that mind. Otherwise it maps the removed datum to cult, revolution or nuclear operative, and ignores any other type. It does nothing if the creator still has another datum of that type, or if the slave does not have one. Otherwise it calls `remove_antag_datum()` on the slave, asynchronously.

The change is signal-based, so only cult, rev and nukeop follow; other antag types, such as traitor, are deliberately left alone (the slave does not become a traitor when it is enslaved either).

## Verification

Cult path, on the `-DTESTING` build with Lua-driven mobs, after the fix:

1. One master with cult and traitor datums, two slaves enslaved to it. Both slaves had the cult datum.
2. Removing the master's traitor datum: both slaves kept the cult (`Cultist/Cultist`).
3. Removing the master's cult datum: the master had none, and both slaves lost it (`nil/nil`), each with the cult deconversion message. The slaves' `enslaved_to` was still set afterwards.

The round's runtime log showed only boot-time messages and no runtime errors. The fix was committed from a tree where the `-DTESTING` build flag was used for testing only; the commit itself contains just the `antag.dm` change.

Not checked:
- Revolution and nuclear operative paths. They go through the same handler and were only checked by reading the code.
- The issue's own sequence: a sentience potion, a magicarp, and a bolt of change hitting the master. See Notes for why this may not be handled.
- Anything with a real client in the cult (the test used a `-DTESTING` build to bypass the client check).

## Notes

- **Possible gap for the exact case in the issue.** When a bolt of change or other transformation moves the master's mind into a new body (for example a cyborg), `mind.transfer_to()` sets the old body's `mind` to null (`code/datums/mind/_mind.dm:176`). `enslaved_to` is a weak reference to the original mob and is not updated. The cult is removed for borging when the new silicon logs in (`code/modules/mob/living/silicon/login.dm:3`, `remove_antags_for_borging`, `antag.dm:77`), after the transfer. At that point the new handler resolves `enslaved_to` to the old body (or nothing, if it was already deleted), sees that the old body's mind is not the creator's mind, and returns. This was derived by reading the code and not tested. If it is correct, the slave would still stay in the cult in the report's exact scenario, and the handler would need to look at the creator's mind instead of a body (for example by storing the mind or following mind transfers).
- Slaves still do not join a team the creator joins later, only leave one the creator leaves.
- This changes the semantics of team membership for every enslaved mind in the cult, revolution and nuclear operative teams, so it is a design change that maintainers should review.
- The bolt of change deconverting a cultist who is turned into a cyborg is intended behavior and unchanged.
- A related upstream PR (#69091, shapeshift status effect) does not touch this.
