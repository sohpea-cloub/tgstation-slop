# #49885: "Your armor has softened a hit to your chest!" printed for melee hits with zero force

## Summary

Hitting a mob wearing armor with an item that has no force still printed "Your armor has softened a hit to your chest!", although no damage could be dealt. `/mob/living/attacked_by` called `run_armor_check` (which prints that message) before it computed the final force and returned early for force 0. The fix computes the force first and only runs the armor check when it is above zero.

## The bug

Wearing an armor vest and hitting yourself with an item that does no damage (the issue uses a geiger counter) shows the attack message followed by "Your armor has softened a hit to your chest!". The armor reported blocking something that was never going to do damage. The thrown-object case from the same issue has already been fixed on master (`code/modules/mob/living/living_defense.dm:310` returns early when `thrown_item.throwforce` is 0); melee was still affected.

## Reproduction

1. On the test server, using the Lua editor, spawn a human, equip an armor vest (`/obj/item/clothing/suit/armor/vest`) and take control of that human.
2. Spawn a crowbar and set its `force` to 0. A geiger counter was not used because its own attack and interact code only scans, so a plain force-0 item was used instead.
3. Call `attack` on the human with the crowbar, with the human as both target and user.
4. Observed on master: the chat showed "You attack yourself in the chest with the pocket crowbar!" followed by "Your armor has softened a hit to your chest!".

## Cause

In `/mob/living/attacked_by` (`code/_onclick/item_attack.dm:319`), `run_armor_check` was called at line 332, with `soften_text = span_warning("Your armor has softened a hit to your [targeting_human_readable]!")`. `run_armor_check` prints the absorb, soften or penetrate message itself. Only afterwards (line 341) was `final_force` computed by `CALCULATE_FORCE`, and the `if(final_force <= 0)` early return (line 357) came after the message had already been printed. As the issue comments note, the armor-check proc does not know the weapon's force, so the check has to be skipped by the caller.

## Fix

Commit "Fix issue 49885: no armor soften message for zero-force melee hits" moves the `final_force` calculation above the armor check and wraps the check:

```dm
var/final_force = CALCULATE_FORCE(attacking_item, attack_modifiers)
// no armor message for hits that can't do any damage anyway
var/armor_block = 0
if(final_force > 0)
	armor_block = min(run_armor_check(...), ARMOR_MAX_BLOCK)
```

When the force is zero or less, `armor_block` stays 0 and no message is printed. The existing `final_force <= 0` return still runs before `armor_block` is used, and the demolition-modifier adjustment for robotic, mineral and skeletal mobs is still applied after the force calculation, as before.

## Verification

On the test server, after rebuilding, with the same setup:

- Force 0: only "You bash yourself in the chest..." was shown, with no armor line.
- Force 5: "You batter yourself..." followed by "Your armor has softened a hit to your chest!", and health dropped, so the normal armor path is intact.

Not checked: other callers of `run_armor_check` (projectiles that do 0 damage, embedding in `code/datums/embedding.dm:246`, mech melee in `code/modules/vehicles/mecha/mech_melee_attack.dm:155`), and attacks by someone other than the wearer.

## Notes

- The reproduction used a direct `attack` call from a script on a self-target, not a player clicking.
- The other `run_armor_check` callers listed above also pass soften text and may print the same message for zero damage; they were not examined.
