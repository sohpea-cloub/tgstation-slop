## Summary

A recycler with its maintenance panel open is drawn with the "panel open" sprite, which looks like a stopped grinder, but it keeps grinding items. `eat()` and `can_butcher()` never look at `panel_open`, while `update_icon_state()` does. The fix adds one `can_recycle()` proc (powered, not in safety shutdown, panel closed) and uses it in both places, so an open recycler really is off. The related report #62855 (a rebuilt recycler with upgraded parts not starting) could not be reproduced on master.

## #42080: recycler appears off with the panel open but still runs

### The bug

The reporter screwdrivered a recycler open to "disable" it, then died to it seconds later. With the panel open the recycler's icon is the open-panel grinder, which reads as switched off, yet the machine still crushes whatever walks or is moved onto it. The reporter's reproduction used an emagged recycler with a running conveyor; the same applies to an ordinary one for items.

### Reproduction

On master, using the Lua editor as an observer and calling procs directly (no conveyor or player was involved in this test):

1. Spawn a `/obj/machinery/recycler` on a floor tile. Its `icon_state` was `grinder-o1`, `is_operational` was 1, `panel_open` was 0.
2. Spawn a human and a screwdriver, and call `screwdriver_act(human, screwdriver)` on the recycler to open its panel. `panel_open` became 1 and `icon_state` became `grinder-oOpen`, the stopped-looking sprite. `is_operational` was still 1.
3. Spawn an iron sheet (`/obj/item/stack/sheet/iron`) and `forceMove()` it onto the recycler's tile, which triggers the recycler's `on_entered` handler as walking in would.
4. The sheet was recycled and the welder-like recycle sound played, with the panel open and the open-panel sprite showing.

This confirms the report for items. A mob was not run through the recycler in this test, and neither was an emagged recycler or a conveyor, so the exact emag-plus-conveyor sequence from the issue was not followed step by step.

### Cause

In `code/game/machinery/recycler.dm`:

- `update_icon_state()` (line 89) picks the open-panel sprite whenever `panel_open` is set:
  ```dm
  if(panel_open)
  	icon_state = base_icon_state + "Open"
  else
  	icon_state = base_icon_state + "[is_operational && !safety_mode]"
  ```
- `eat()` (line 128) only refuses when `machine_stat & (BROKEN|NOPOWER) || safety_mode`.
- `can_butcher()` (line 123) uses the same condition to decide whether a dead mob is left to the butchering component.

`panel_open` is not part of either condition, so the machine's behavior and its sprite disagree. Opening the panel with `screwdriver_act()` (line 76, `default_deconstruction_screwdriver()`) changes only the sprite.

### Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 42080: recyclers stop running while their maintenance panel is open". It adds

```dm
/// Whether the grinder is currently running: it needs power, no safety shutdown and a closed maintenance panel (the panel-open sprite shows it as stopped).
/obj/machinery/recycler/proc/can_recycle()
	return !(machine_stat & (BROKEN|NOPOWER)) && !safety_mode && !panel_open
```

and makes both `eat()` and `can_butcher()` use `!can_recycle()` in place of their old inline conditions. This takes the sprite as the intended behavior (open means stopped), which is what the issue title asks for, instead of changing the sprite to show a running machine. A shared proc keeps the two checks from drifting apart again. The change is 6 insertions and 2 deletions.

### Verification

On the rebuilt test server, in one Lua script on a fresh recycler:

- Panel closed, iron sheet moved onto the tile: the sheet was eaten.
- Panel opened with the screwdriver, a second sheet moved onto the tile: not eaten, and `icon_state` was `grinder-oOpen`.
- Panel closed again, a third sheet: eaten, and `icon_state` was `grinder-o1`.

Not checked in game: mobs (live or dead) entering an open or closed recycler, an emagged recycler, the conveyor path, and the butchering behavior of `can_butcher()`. Those use the same `can_recycle()` condition by construction but were not exercised.

### Notes

- `/obj/machinery/recycler/deathtrap` (the "dangerous old crusher") blocks screwdrivers and crowbars via `tool_blocker`, so its panel cannot be opened and it is unaffected.
- The check is made when something enters the recycler's tile, so the fix stops new items and mobs from being eaten; what happens to things already on the tile when the panel is opened was not examined.

## #62855: recycler not starting up after being rebuilt

### The report

A recycler was deconstructed and rebuilt with tier 4 parts in a powered area (machine frame anchored before the last screwdriver). After completion it reportedly did not start working, and toggling the APC or its machinery channel made no difference. The reporter later tried to recreate it on a local server and could not.

### Attempted reproduction

On master, with the Lua editor and direct proc calls:

1. Spawn a `/obj/structure/frame/machine/secured`.
2. Insert a `/obj/item/circuitboard/machine/recycler` board and a tier 4 servo (`/obj/item/stock_parts/servo/femto`), then call `screwdriver_act()` to finish construction, which produces a new recycler.
3. `forceMove()` an iron sheet onto the new recycler's tile.

The sheet was recycled immediately, so the rebuilt recycler worked. Only the board and the servo were supplied (the other parts the reporter may have used were not replicated), and APC switching was not tested.

Reading the construction path, a machine produced by the frame has `panel_open` false and `safety_mode` false, so neither would block it. The original report may have been a power or area problem, or already fixed since 2021. It remains not reproduced.

Note: with the #42080 fix, a rebuilt recycler whose panel was left open would not run. That is the intended behavior and is separate from this report.
