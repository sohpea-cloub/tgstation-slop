## Summary

#92975: laying a cable by clicking directly on a (reinforced) mining catwalk over lava works on current master. A change that landed before the tested commit (the agents identified it as upstream PR #93033) makes lattices hand the interaction to the turf below, so the report no longer applies.

#69607: the GPS computer in survival pods got the handheld GPS's "working" light overlay. That 7x5 pixel graphic sits two pixels below the computer's base sprite and shows as an orange box. The fix lets `/obj/item/gps/computer` opt out of GPS overlays and makes the GPS component touch overlays only when it added one.

## #92975: wires on catwalks over lava

### The report

On lavaland, clicking a catwalk over lava with a cable coil did nothing. The reporter had to alt-click to select the lava instead.

### Reproduction attempt

1. In a running round, open the Lua editor as an observer.
2. Create a `lava_land_surface` turf with an `/obj/structure/lattice/catwalk/mining` (the reinforced catwalk used over lava) on it, and a human holding a cable coil of 30.
3. Click the catwalk with the coil, as a normal click rather than an alt-click.
4. The coil went from 30 to 29, and a scan of `GLOB.cable_list` found a cable on that tile. The click printed no chat text, so the cable was found by the scan, not by what the game said.

### Why it works now

- `code/game/objects/structures/lattice.dm:69`: `item_interaction` on a lattice (catwalks inherit it) returns the underlying turf's `item_interaction`, to "hand this off to the turf instead".
- `code/game/turfs/turf.dm:417`: `/turf/item_interaction` lays the cable through `coil.place_turf` when the coil can be laid there.
- `code/game/turfs/open/lava.dm:383`: lava has `underfloor_accessibility = 2`, with a comment about routing pipes and wires over catwalks on lava, so the cable check allows it.

The tested checkout already contained the upstream change; the agents did not look at the pre-change code, so the exact version when the report was made is not confirmed here.

### Not checked

The un-reinforced catwalk, and placement through other tools or hand-built catwalks, were not tried. The reporter's round had three test-merged PRs (one of them a reagent container refactor) that could interact with this; those were not available.

## #69607: GPS overlay on survival pod computers

### The bug

In a deployed survival capsule, the GPS computer has a small orange rectangle below its sprite. The original reporter marked it on a screenshot and did not know whether it was intended. A commenter's guess was that it is the item-based GPS's overlay.

### Reproduction

The agents checked this by counting overlays and by rendering the sprites, not by looking at it in a live client:

1. A spawned `/obj/item/gps/computer` had the `working` overlay in addition to its base sprite.
2. In `pod_computer.dmi`, the `working` and `emp` states are a 7x5 pixel blob at x 13-19, y 44-48. The computer's base sprite is opaque up to row 46, so the blob pokes out two pixels beneath it. Rendering the computer with the overlay showed an orange box at the bottom; without it, none.

### Cause

- `code/game/objects/items/devices/gps.dm:21`: `/obj/item/gps` has `overlay_icon = "working"`, passed to the GPS component.
- `code/datums/components/gps.dm:60`: the component adds that state as an overlay when set. `on_emp_act`, `reboot` and `toggletracking` then hardcoded `"working"` and `"emp"` regardless.
- `/obj/item/gps/computer` (in `code/modules/mining/equipment/survival_pod.dm`) inherits all of that, but its sprite is much bigger than a handheld GPS, so the handheld's screen light lands in the wrong place.

### Fix

Commit "Fix issue 69607: pod computers no longer get the handheld GPS overlay":

- `survival_pod.dm`: `/obj/item/gps/computer` sets `overlay_icon = FALSE`. A first attempt with `null` did not work: passing `null` into the component's `Initialize` falls back to its default `"working"`, which was observed in game.
- `components/gps.dm`: the item component stores the overlay it added in a new `overlay_state` var. `on_emp_act`, `reboot` and `toggletracking` only cut or add overlays when that var is set, and use it instead of the hardcoded `"working"`. Without that guard, an EMP or toggle would still put the overlays back on the computer. The `"emp"` overlay is also skipped when there is no `overlay_state`.

The alternative of drawing a new overlay state sized for the computer was not tried. Opting out matches the existing `overlay_icon = FALSE` in the debug GPS.

### Verification

Using Lua on spawned objects, counting `overlays`:

- Computer: one overlay (base sprite) at spawn, and the same after an EMP, after the reboot, and after toggling tracking off and on. The component's `tracking` and `emped` flags still toggled correctly.
- Handheld GPS: 2 overlays at spawn, 2 after an EMP and after the reboot, 1 after toggling tracking off and 2 after toggling it back on, so its behaviour is unchanged.

The round's `runtime.log` had no new runtimes. Not checked: a rendered image of the fixed computer in a client, a real EMP from a player action, and other items that use the GPS component with an overlay.
