## Summary

#79366 (a dullahan cannot be defibrillated, and reattaching the head gibs them) is no longer true as filed: the defibrillator's brain check is already handled for dullahans, and the gib on reattaching a head is deliberate. Testing the related reports found two real bugs, both in the dullahan tongue's `handle_speech`. It blanked every message to relay it through the head, so the changeling hivemind (a say mode) never ran (#79361). And its organs were unremovable, so a dullahan changeling who transformed into another species kept the dullahan tongue, which then blanked every message of its no-longer-dullahan owner (#93690). The branch makes the tongue skip say modes and non-dullahans, and makes the dullahan organs replaceable on species loss.

## The bug

- **#79366:** the reporter said a defibrillator on a dullahan answered "Resuscitation failed - Patient's brain is missing. Further attempts futile.", and that reattaching the head gibbed the dullahan, leaving them unrevivable.
- **#79361:** a dullahan changeling could not use the hivemind channel, and pressing Regenerate gibbed them. The comments (a maintainer and another contributor) say the second is expected if you try to regrow your head.
- **#93690:** a dullahan changeling who transforms into another species can no longer speak at all.

## Reproduction

All checks used the Lua editor as an observer on the test server, spawning `/mob/living/carbon/human/species/dullahan`.

**#79366, defibrillation.** After `death()` on a spawned dullahan, `can_defib()` returned 1 (`DEFIB_POSSIBLE`) with the brain in the detached head. The "brain is missing" message therefore no longer occurs. The defibrillator's paddle `do_after` flow itself was not run (Lua cannot sleep), so only the `can_defib()` result the revive path uses was checked.

**#79366, reattaching the head.** Not run in game. The code at `code/modules/mob/living/carbon/human/species_types/dullahan.dm:69-78` (`on_gained_part`) gibs a dullahan when a head is attached on purpose ("an illegal head on shoulders"), and the maintainers' comments on #79361 say this is intended.

**#79361, Regenerate.** A dullahan with a player was made a changeling and triggered Regenerate. It printed the "limbs regrow" message and screamed, but did not gib: stat stayed 0 and the head stayed in the hand. The head is excluded in `dullahan_relay.unlist_head`. So this part is already fixed on master.

**#79361, hivemind.**
1. Make a player-controlled dullahan a changeling.
2. Have it say `:g hello hive`.
3. Result on master: chat showed `The head of <name> says, "hello hive"` aloud and nothing went to changeling chat.

**#93690.**
1. Make a player-controlled dullahan a changeling and create a profile of a normal human.
2. Call `transform()` on the changeling with that profile.
3. Result on master: the species became Human, but the organs stayed `/obj/item/organ/tongue/dullahan`, `ears/dullahan` and `eyes/dullahan`. The character could not say anything at all.

## Cause

- **Hivemind (#79361).** `handle_speech` of the dullahan tongue (`dullahan.dm:197-210`) is a `COMSIG_MOB_SAY` handler. That signal fires at `code/modules/mob/living/living_say.dm:195`. The handler makes the head say the text and then sets `SPEECH_MESSAGE` to an empty string. `living_say.dm:218` (`if(!message) return`) then aborts the say before the say mode is dispatched at `:244`, so the hivemind never ran.
- **Speech after transform (#93690).** The dullahan tongue, ears and eyes are `ORGAN_UNREMOVABLE`. When the species changes, `code/modules/mob/living/carbon/human/_species.dm:312` (`remove_existing`) skips unremovable organs, so the new species cannot replace them. `on_species_loss` (`dullahan.dm:101-117`) did not clear the flag. The leftover tongue still ran `handle_speech`, which blanked every message, because `isdullahan(owner)` was false, so nothing was relayed, but the final line that blanks the message ran unconditionally.

## Fix

Two commits, both in `code/modules/mob/living/carbon/human/species_types/dullahan.dm`.

**Fix issue 79361: dullahan changelings can use the hivemind.** At the start of `handle_speech`, return without touching the message if `SSradio.get_available_say_mode()` finds a say mode for the message's radio key. Say modes are then handled by the normal `say()` from the body.

**Fix issue 93690: changelings can speak after transforming out of dullahan.** `on_species_loss` now clears `ORGAN_UNREMOVABLE` on the dullahan tongue, ears and eyes before the new species' organs are applied, and `handle_speech` returns immediately if the owner is not a dullahan. The two changes overlap: with the organs replaceable the leftover tongue is replaced, and with the ownership check a leftover tongue does nothing. The commit message does not say why both were made; presumably the check also covers any path that does not call `on_species_loss`. The same change helps any other species change from dullahan (for example a wabbajack or an admin species change); those were not tested.

## Verification

On a build with both commits:
- **#79361:** `say(":g hello hive")` as a dullahan changeling gave `Mr. Eta: Hello hive {radio changeling}`, and normal speech still came from the head.
- **#93690:** after the transform the tongue, ears and eyes were replaced with the human ones, and the character said `Vincent Andreev says, "Can you hear me after transform"`.

Not checked: the defibrillator paddle flow with a real do_after; reattaching a head in game; speech with accents (see Notes); other radio and say modes through the head (department radio, `,` and `.` channels), which now take the early-return path; unit tests.

## Notes

- **Merge conflict expected.** The separate fix for #79355 (branch `fix/79355`) also edits the dullahan tongue's `handle_speech`, to run `treat_message()` before the head speaks. The two edits are in adjacent lines of the same proc and will conflict; they are compatible in intent.
- **Regenerate message.** For a dullahan changeling, Regenerate still prints "Your limbs regrow" and screams although nothing regrows: the head is excluded from the heal but `get_missing_limbs()` counts it (`code/modules/antagonists/changeling/powers/regenerate.dm:18`). This is cosmetic and was not changed.
- **Intended gib.** The maintainer comments on #79361 describe the head-reattach gib as won't-fix. If maintainers want a gentler behaviour, that is a design change, not a bug fix.
- The branch does not touch the defibrillator, since the "brain is missing" report no longer reproduces. The check is handled by the `COMSIG_CARBON_DEFIB_BRAIN_CHECK` signal and `defib_check()` (`dullahan.dm:97-99`).
