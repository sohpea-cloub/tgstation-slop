## Summary

Three xenobiology issues were examined together. Neither #81793 (a chilling extract that will not accept or use its 10u of plasma) nor #84692 (a polymorph belt slime form that cannot move and feeds on itself) happened on master, and no code was changed for them. #84715 (a stabilized gold extract's parrot familiar taking the extract from its owner and vanishing) was reproduced as far as the parrot stealing the extract and being deleted, and was fixed in one commit: parrots no longer steal stabilized extracts, and the gold effect makes its familiar drop what it holds before deleting it.

## #81793: Chilling extract demands more plasma and refuses more

### The bug
The reporter said that a chilling crossbreed filled with 10u of plasma still said it needed more plasma, and refused further plasma because it was "full". They suspected other plasma-activated extracts too.

### Reproduction
Not reproduced. Using the Lua editor on the test server:

1. Spawned a grey chilling extract and added 10u of plasma, both directly and by emptying a 15u syringe into it.
2. Used it in hand. It produced "a few small, grey cubes" and the barrier cubes appeared.

Pouring from a beaker into the extract does nothing, because the extract is not refillable (it only takes `INJECTABLE | DRAWABLE`). This looks intentional and is not what the report describes.

### Cause
None found. `code/modules/research/xenobiology/crossbreeding/chilling.dm:14-21` creates a 10u reagent container and checks `reagents.has_reagent(/datum/reagent/toxin/plasma, 10)`, which is consistent with the behaviour seen. The report may have involved another reagent in the extract (for example a mixed reagent that filled it), or been fixed since 2024. This is a guess.

### Fix
None.

### Verification
Only the grey chilling extract was tried, and only by syringe and direct reagent addition. Other chilling colours and other plasma-fed extract types were not tested.

## #84692: Polymorph belt slime form locks you in place and makes you eat yourself

### The bug
The reporter said that after scanning a slime and transforming with the polymorphic field inverter, the player could do nothing and could not revert, and slowly shocked themselves to death.

### Reproduction
Not reproduced. Using the Lua editor:

1. Spawned a human in a jumpsuit, equipped a polymorph belt in the belt slot and set its stored mob type to `/mob/living/basic/slime` (the belt needs the jumpsuit worn first).
2. Triggered the "Invert Polymorphic Field" action. About 3 seconds later the human became a grey baby slime.
3. The slime walked normally, had 150 health, was not buckled to anything and took no damage over about 25 seconds. It reverted normally.
4. A second transform followed by 35 seconds of idling was also fine.

### Cause
None found. The report is from July 2024; the slime form tested here is a basic mob (`/mob/living/basic/slime`). The issue text gives no slime type, so this may be a change since then, but that is unconfirmed. Slime feeding code (`start_feeding`) was read but no defect was identified.

### Fix
None.

### Verification
Only a grey baby slime was tried, once with the belt's scanned type set directly rather than by scanning a live slime. Other slime colours and the scan step itself were not tested.

## #84715: Stabilized gold parrot steals the extract and both vanish

### The bug
A stabilized gold extract set to produce a parrot spawns a "Familiar" parrot. The parrot could take the extract out of its owner's hand; the report says the parrot and the extract then disappeared completely.

### Reproduction
Partly reproduced on master:

1. Using the Lua editor, spawned a human, spawned a `/obj/item/slimecross/stabilized/gold`, set its `mob_type` to `/mob/living/basic/parrot`, and put it in the human's hand. The `/datum/status_effect/stabilized/gold` effect spawned a parrot named "Familiar".
2. Called `steal_from_mob(human)` on the parrot (the call the parrot's attack makes, not a real parrot attack). Chat said the familiar "wrestles the stabilized gold extract out of ... hand!".
3. The familiar was then gone and the extract lay on the floor.

What was not seen is the extract disappearing: in this run the extract was on the floor. The report's version (both vanish) would need the parrot to pick the extract up into its claws before the effect ends, and that was not observed. I did not test whether the parrot's own AI picks it up.

### Cause
`/mob/living/basic/parrot/proc/steal_from_mob` (`code/modules/mob/living/basic/pets/parrot/_parrot.dm:303-322`) takes any held item with `w_class <= WEIGHT_CLASS_SMALL`, including the stabilized extract that sustains the parrot itself. Removing the extract from the owner's hand ends `/datum/status_effect/stabilized/gold`, whose `on_remove` (`code/modules/research/xenobiology/crossbreeding/_status_effects.dm:1059`) does `qdel(familiar)`. The thief is deleted, and `/atom/movable/Destroy` deletes its contents, so anything it held at that moment is deleted with it. If the extract is in its claws then, it is lost too.

### Fix
Commit "Fix issue 84715: parrot familiars no longer steal their own stabilized extract":

- In `steal_from_mob`, items that are `/obj/item/slimecross/stabilized` are skipped, so the parrot cannot take the extract that keeps it alive.
- In `/datum/status_effect/stabilized/gold/on_remove`, `familiar.drop_all_held_items()` is called before `qdel(familiar)`, so anything the familiar holds (a stolen extract, or any other item) is dropped to the floor instead of being deleted with it.

Both changes were made because either alone leaves a gap: skipping the extract does not protect other items the familiar might hold when the effect ends for another reason, and dropping alone would still let the parrot remove its own extract and die.

### Verification
After rebuilding, on the test server:

- With the same setup, `steal_from_mob` returned 0 and the extract stayed in the owner's hand.
- The parrot was given a crowbar, then the owner dropped the extract. The familiar was deleted as before, and both the crowbar and the extract ended on the floor, not deleted.

Not checked: a real parrot attack through its AI, other stabilized extract colours with held-item familiars, and the original "both vanish" report.

## Notes

- The #84715 fix keeps `qdel(familiar)`; it only changes what the familiar holds at that time.
- `lastError` in the Lua editor showed a "destructed callback" message in the verification run; it came from reading the deleted parrot's name afterwards, not from the game.
