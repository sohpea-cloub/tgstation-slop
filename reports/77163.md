# #77163: Borg headlamp glow disappears on a z-level change (after a module change)

| | |

| Status | Fixed for the case that reproduced |

## Summary

A cyborg that has changed module and then moves between z-levels that use different plane offsets loses its lit-eyes (headlamp) overlay until the lamp is toggled. `cut_overlays()` clears `overlays` but leaves `managed_overlays` set, so the borg's hand-built overlay list looks fully "managed" and `update_icon()` wipes it on the z change. The fix makes `cut_overlays()` also clear `managed_overlays`. The plain case in #77163 (a fresh borg with the lamp on) did not reproduce on this master, and #71753 (module change not updating the lamp overlay) is already fine on master.

## The bug

#77163 says a borg's lights (the glowing eye overlay) turn off after changing z-level and come back when the lamp is toggled. #71753 (an older report) says that after changing module the borg keeps the standard cyborg light overlay until the lamp is toggled.

## Reproduction

All steps used admin tools on a round started on the `multiz_debug` map, with the tester observing. Cyborgs were spawned and moved with the Lua editor, and overlays were read from the live mob.

**Plain case (#77163 as written): not reproduced.**
1. Spawn `/mob/living/silicon/robot` in z2 and call `toggle_headlamp()`. Overlays: `robot_l` and an emissive blocker; two light underlays; plane -218.
2. `forceMove` it to z3 and back to z2. Plane became -111 and then -218 (the plane offset differs per level). The overlays stayed `robot_l` + emissive blocker and the light underlays stayed. The lamp stayed on.

**Module change then z move: reproduced.**
1. With a borg like the one above (lamp on), call `model:transform_to(/obj/item/robot_model/engineering)` and wait for the transform to finish. Overlays: `engineer_l` (the engineering glow) and the emissive blocker.
2. `forceMove` the borg from z2 to z3. Overlays became only the emissive blocker; `engineer_l` was gone. The light underlays remained and `lamp_enabled` was still 1, so the lamp was on but the glowing eyes were not drawn, matching the report (toggling the lamp rebuilds the icon). It was not checked in game that toggling restores it.

**#71753 (module change does not update the lamp overlay): not reproduced.** In step 1 above the glow overlay went from `robot_l` to `engineer_l` right after the transform.

This was reproduced by moving the borg with `forceMove`, not by walking up stairs or a z-transition as a player; whether the reporter's z changes go through the same code was not tested.

## Cause

- `/mob/living/silicon/robot/update_icons()` (`code/modules/mob/living/silicon/robot/robot.dm:311-327`) calls `cut_overlays()` and then `add_overlay(eye_lights)` itself. It is called at the end of the module transform (`robot_model.dm:326`, in `do_transform_delay()`), which is why #71753 is fine.
- `cut_overlays()` (`code/controllers/subsystem/overlays.dm:62`) sets `overlays = null` but did not touch `managed_overlays`, the list the overlay subsystem keeps for what `update_overlays()` produced (here the emissive blocker).
- On a z-layer change `on_changed_z_level()` (`code/game/atoms_movable.dm:1264`) calls `update_appearance()` (line 1273). In `update_icon()` (`code/game/atom/atom_appearance.dm:83-84`) "full control" is decided with `length(overlays) == length(managed_overlays)`. After the hand-built rebuild the borg has exactly one stale managed overlay and one overlay in total, so the lengths match, the code believes it owns every overlay, and sets `overlays` to null, discarding the hand-added `eye_lights`.
- The robot's own override of `on_changed_z_level()` (`robot.dm:370-377`) re-planes `eye_lights` and re-adds it, so it handles a fresh borg. After the module-change rebuild the overlay is lost anyway, as observed; the order of the two steps was not traced.

The same mechanism was found for slimes' faces and mushroom caps (#74179) in a separate fix.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 77163: cut_overlays() also forgets managed_overlays", adds `managed_overlays = null` to `/atom/proc/cut_overlays()` with a comment. Everything the subsystem was managing is gone after `cut_overlays()`, so forgetting it is correct and fixes every caller that rebuilds overlays by hand, not only borgs. The alternative of changing `robot.dm` to use `update_overlays()` instead of hand-added overlays was not tried. The change is identical to the one on the #74179 branch, so merging both is a no-op for this hunk.

## Verification

On the test server after rebuilding: spawned a borg, toggled the lamp, transformed it to engineering, waited for the transform, then moved it z2 to z3 and back to z2. At every step the overlays were `engineer_l` plus the emissive blocker (planes -111 on z3 and -218 on z2), and the runtime log showed nothing related.

Not checked: the exact path a player takes between z-levels, other cyborg models or the other lamp states (the `_e` non-lamp light), borgs that change module more than once, other `cut_overlays()` callers for regressions, and any behavior beyond the log output at the end of that run.

## Notes

- Why the reporters saw this after plain z moves is unexplained: on this master a fresh borg keeps its glow. Another path that calls `cut_overlays()` on a borg (cover toggle, damage, anything that calls `update_icons()`) would also leave the stale state and then hit the same bug on the next z change, but this was not tested.
- #71753 was reported in December 2022 and is likely fixed already; no commit that fixed it was identified.
