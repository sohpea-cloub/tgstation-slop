# #49799: Mindshield implants skip the mind checks when the mind enters an already implanted body

| | |
|---|---|
| Issues | [#49799](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/49799) verified and fixed (reproduced with a direct mind transfer; the slimeperson body swap itself was not played) |
| Status | Fixed on the branch |

## Summary

A mindshield implant only ran its antagonist checks (head revolutionaries resist it, revolutionaries are deconverted) if the host body had a mind at the moment of implanting. If the body was mindless when implanted and a mind moved in later, the body kept `TRAIT_MINDSHIELD` and nothing re-checked the new mind, so a head revolutionary could end up inside a mindshielded body without resisting. The fix moves the checks into a proc, `check_mind()`, and re-runs it whenever a mind is transferred into the implanted body.

## The bug

The reporter (2020) played a head revolutionary slimeperson, abandoned the original body after being caught and used a spare body. When the spare died they returned to the first body, and the security HUD showed it as mindshielded. They were still a head revolutionary and had neither resisted the implant nor been deconverted. Commenters noted that the same would probably work by brainswapping into an already implanted body, and that nanite mindshields might have the same problem.

## Reproduction

The slimeperson body swap itself was not played on the test server. The reproduction uses a direct mind transfer, which is the same code path (`mind.transfer_to`) that a body swap ends in. This is an inference; the original sequence of events in the report (when the first body got its implant) is not stated in the issue.

1. Using the Lua editor, spawn two humans, A and B. Give A a mind with the head revolutionary antagonist datum (`/datum/antagonist/rev/head`). Leave B mindless.
2. Implant B with a mindshield (`implant:implant(B, nil, true, true)`), so the implant is placed while B has no mind.
3. Transfer A's mind into B with `A.mind:transfer_to(B)`.
4. Observed on master: B's mind was still Head Revolutionary and the mindshield implant was still in B.

## Cause

`/obj/item/implant/mindshield/implant` (`code/game/objects/items/implants/implant_mindshield.dm:12`) sends `COMSIG_PRE_MINDSHIELD_IMPLANT` (resist) and `COMSIG_MINDSHIELD_IMPLANTED` (deconvert) only inside `if(target.mind)` (line 16). With no mind present, the implant just adds `TRAIT_MINDSHIELD` and `TRAIT_UNCONVERTABLE` (line 27). `/datum/mind/proc/transfer_to` (`code/datums/mind/_mind.dm:173`) later moves a mind into the body and sends `COMSIG_MOB_MIND_TRANSFERRED_INTO` (line 223), but nothing in the implant listens to it, so the new mind is never checked.

## Fix

Commit "Fix issue 49799: mindshield implants re-check the mind when it moves into an already implanted body":

- The two signal checks were moved out of `implant()` into a new proc, `check_mind(target, user, silent)`. It returns FALSE (after removing the implant) when the mind resisted, and TRUE otherwise. `implant()` calls it when the target has a mind, with unchanged behaviour.
- After a successful implant, the implant registers `COMSIG_MOB_MIND_TRANSFERRED_INTO` on the host. The handler `on_mind_transferred_into` runs `check_mind(source, null, TRUE)`: silent and with no implanter. The signal is unregistered in `removed()`.

This follows the suggestion in the issue comments (check on consciousness transfer whether the mind is compatible with the implant and destroy the implant if not), and it covers any way a mind can enter an implanted body, not just slimepeople.

## Verification

On the test server, with the Lua editor, after rebuilding, the same script was run for three kinds of mind transferred into a body with a mindless-time mindshield:

- Head revolutionary: stayed Head Revolutionary and the implant count dropped to 0 (it resisted and the implant was removed).
- Revolutionary: antagonist list became empty (deconverted, "remembered her real allegiance") and the implant stayed (1), as with normal implantation.
- No antagonist role (control): implant stayed (1).

Not checked: the actual slimeperson replication or body swap, brainswap and other transfer sources, and the nanite mindshield mentioned in the comments (not touched).

## Notes

- Cultists keep the implant, both here and with direct implanting, because the cult's mindshield handler only sends a message.
- The transfer re-check is silent (no "seems to resist" message), since no one is implanting.
- Nanite mindshields are a separate path and were not changed.
