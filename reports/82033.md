# #82033: Rice, flour and water only make rice dough in some orders

| Status | Fixed and checked in game; the chemistry test suite was not run |
| Fix branch | [fix/82033](https://github.com/sohpea-cloub/tgstation-slop/tree/fix/82033) |

## Summary

Mixing 10 flour, 10 rice and 10 water, the ingredients the issue says make fresh noodles, only produced rice dough if water was not added before the rice and flour had met. In other orders the rice was turned into uncooked rice, or reagents added while another reaction was running were never checked for reactions at all, so the mixture sat unreacted. The fix stops uncooked rice from forming while flour is present, and makes a reagent holder re-check its reactions when its last running reaction finishes.

## The bug

The issue reports that putting flour, water and rice in a bowl gives "10 water, 20 rice flour" and no dough. The comments narrow it down: the result depends on the order the reagents go in, and adding or removing anything afterwards can "update" the mixture into rice dough. Rice dough is the first step towards fresh noodles (rice dough is cut into raw noodles), so the player ends up with no noodles.

The first comment in the issue says rice dough can be crafted from rice flour and water. That is true, but the issue is about the three-ingredient mix, which the recipes suggest should also work: rice and flour react into rice flour, and rice flour and water react into rice dough.

## Reproduction

On master, with admin tools:

1. Join as an observer. In the Lua editor, create several large (bluespace) beakers.
2. In each beaker, `add_reagent` 10u flour, 10u rice and 10u water, each beaker in a different order, then wait about 10 seconds and read the beaker's `reagent_list`. Orders are written below as f = flour, r = rice, w = water.
3. Observed on master:
   - f, r, w and r, f, w: rice dough, with all reagents consumed.
   - f, w, r; w, f, r; r, w, f; w, r, f: the message "The rice absorbs the water." (uncooked rice) and 10 Flour left in the beaker.
4. Interleaved additions in 5u steps (f, w, r, f, w, r) stalled with Water 10, Rice Flour 10, Flour 5, Rice 5 and no reaction running. This matches the comment in the issue that adding or removing something makes it react.

The reagents were added with `add_reagent` from Lua, not by pouring or transferring by hand.

## Cause

There are two separate defects.

1. **Uncooked rice wins the race when water is present.** `/datum/chemical_reaction/food/uncooked_rice` (`code/modules/food_and_drinks/recipes/food_mixtures.dm:239`) needs 10 rice and 10 water and is `REACTION_INSTANT`. `rice_flour` (`food_mixtures.dm:162`, 5 flour + 5 rice) has no instant flag, so it runs as a timed reaction. As soon as rice and water are both in the holder, the instant reaction runs first and consumes the rice, leaving flour that has nothing to react with. Only orders where rice meets flour before water, so that rice flour is already being produced, avoid this. `rice_dough` (`food_mixtures.dm:219`) needs 20 rice flour and 10 water.
2. **Reagents added during a reaction are never rechecked.** `add_reagent` only calls `handle_reactions()` when the holder is not already reacting (`code/modules/reagents/chemistry/holder/holder.dm:156`: `if(!no_react && !is_reacting)`). The running reactions are advanced by `process()` in `code/modules/reagents/chemistry/holder/reactions.dm`. When the reaction list becomes empty, `process()` only called `finish_reacting()` (line 145) and did not look again at what was now in the holder. Anything added while the earlier reaction ran, for example the water that would make rice dough from the new rice flour, stayed unreacted until something else triggered `handle_reactions()`. This explains the stalled interleaved case above.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 82033: rice, flour and water now make rice dough regardless of order":

- `food_mixtures.dm`: `uncooked_rice` gets a `pre_reaction_other_checks` that returns `!holder.has_reagent(/datum/reagent/consumable/flour)`. With flour in the holder, rice is no longer soaked into uncooked rice, so it is free to react with the flour.
- `reactions.dm`: after `finish_reacting()` in `process()`, if the holder still has reagents and is not being deleted (`!QDELING(src)`), it calls `handle_reactions()`, so anything added during the reaction is considered.

Alternatives were not documented beyond this. The comment on the issue suggested making rice dough use rice flour and water rather than the three raw ingredients; the recipe already is rice flour and water, so the fix keeps it and removes the order dependence instead.

## Verification

After the fix, using the same beaker method:

- f, r, w; f, w, r; w, f, r; r, f, w; the 5u-step interleaved orders (f, w, r twice, and a longer pattern of 5u steps ending in rice dough): all ended with an empty beaker, i.e. everything became rice dough.
- r, w, f and w, r, f still made uncooked rice, because rice and water meet before any flour. This is intended.
- An interleaved run with 20u flour in total produced plain dough from 15 flour and water, leaving Rice Flour 10, Water 10 and Rice 15. This is a competing recipe (the plain dough recipe, flour 15 + water 10) and was judged expected.

Not checked: the unit tests (including the chemical reaction tests), real-hand mixing in a bowl or machine, and the effect of the `process()` change on other recipes. That change applies to every reagent holder, so a reviewer should run the CI chemistry tests. The fresh noodles recipe itself was not crafted after the fix.

## Notes

- The `process()` change is the broader of the two: any holder that gets reagents while reacting now re-runs `handle_reactions()` once its last reaction ends. That is probably the correct behaviour but may change results for other multi-step recipes.
- Rice before water, with no flour present, still gives uncooked rice. A player who wants noodles has to add flour before the rice meets the water.
- No upstream PR was looked up for this issue.
