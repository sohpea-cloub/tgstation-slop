## Summary

The 2020 report said that a High-Functioning Zombie becomes hungry and then cannot recover, because food no longer raises its nutrition. On current master the zombie status effect gives every zombie `TRAIT_NOHUNGER`, so zombies are outside the hunger system: food reagents do not add nutrition, nutrition changes are ignored unless forced, and the hunger HUD is hidden. The reported situation cannot arise any more, and no fix was written.

## The bug

As reported: once a high-functioning zombie got hungry, it was stuck that way, because eating gave no nutrition. The only comment suggested three options: remove the no-metabolism trait from zombies, give them `NOHUNGER`, or add a zombie-specific nutriment reaction.

## Reproduction

Not reproduced. On the test server, using the Lua editor:

1. Spawned a human and set its species to `/datum/species/zombie` (High-Functioning Zombie).
2. Checked the zombie: it had the `no_hunger` status trait, a stomach and the rotting tongue, and its nutrition was 350.
3. Called `set_nutrition(50)`. Nutrition stayed at 350, because the call is ignored for `NOHUNGER` mobs unless forced.
4. Called `set_nutrition(50, forced = TRUE)`, then `update_nutrition()`. Nutrition was now 50, and the only alert on the zombie was the "succumb" alert. There was no hungry or starving alert and no hunger HUD element (the mob had no HUD to inspect, so the HUD was not looked at directly).
5. For comparison, a normal human given the same forced low nutrition showed no alerts either, so this alert check does not by itself separate a zombie from a normal human. The hunger alerts may be produced elsewhere or on a later life tick, and that was not investigated. The stronger evidence is the code-based explanation below.

The human and zombie were driven by admin calls, not eaten from, so no real eating by a player was tested.

## Cause

There is nothing left to fix. All zombie types receive the trait through the zombie status effect in `code/modules/mob/living/carbon/human/species_types/zombies.dm`:

- `zombie_traits` (`:79-96`) includes `TRAIT_NOHUNGER` (`:90`).
- `/datum/status_effect/zombie/on_apply()` adds `unique_traits | zombie_traits` (`:115`).
- The `/datum/species/zombie` species applies the uninfected status effect when it is gained (`:13-15`), so the species variant used here gets the same traits.

With `TRAIT_NOHUNGER` set:

- `adjust_nutrition()` and `set_nutrition()` do nothing unless forced (`code/modules/mob/mob.dm:1498-1500` and `:1513-1515`).
- Consumable reagents skip adding nutrition (`code/modules/reagents/chemistry/reagents/food_reagents.dm:26-29`: `if(!ishuman(affected_mob) || HAS_TRAIT(affected_mob, TRAIT_NOHUNGER)) return`). This is the "can't process nutriment" part of the title, and it is now consistent with the zombie not needing food.
- The hunger HUD icon is hidden (`code/_onclick/hud/screen_objects/screen_objects.dm:1207`), and the mood hunger effects check the trait (`code/datums/mood.dm:118`, `:397`).
- Gaining the trait resets nutrition to the fed level (`/mob/living/carbon/human/proc/on_nohunger`, `code/modules/mob/living/carbon/human/init_signals.dm:44-53`).

The issue's own comment points at this: at the time zombies had a no-metabolism trait but not `NOHUNGER`, which left them hungry and unable to feed. The original state was not checked here, since the test checkout is shallow and has no useful history for this file.
