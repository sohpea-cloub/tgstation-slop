## Summary

While a player is under the Feral Instincts brain trauma (which Monkey Dust gives monkeys), the monkey AI takes over their body for short episodes. It runs a behaviour once a second that turns combat mode off whenever it has no enemies or no valid targets, so the player cannot stay in combat mode. The fix skips that call when the mob has a client. Separately, AI monkeys that lost their active arm never switched hands and so could not attack. `ai_interact` now swaps to a working hand. #57776 (AI monkeys walking into space) could not be reproduced: AI movement refuses to step onto space turfs.

## #91732: Monkey Dust switches you to non-combat mode

### The bug

A monkey who drinks Monkey Dust gets the Feral Instincts trauma. Players reported that combat mode switched itself off every few seconds, so they could not hit anyone. A commenter added that Jungle Arts is hard to use because the AI keeps picking up items from the floor.

### Reproduction

Reproduced on master with admin tools, using the Feral Instincts trauma directly rather than drinking Monkey Dust (Monkey Dust only calls `gain_trauma` for this trauma; `code/modules/reagents/chemistry/reagents/impure_reagents/impure_medicine_reagents.dm:979`).

1. With the Lua editor, spawn a monkey (`/mob/living/carbon/human/species/monkey`) and call `gain_trauma` on it with `/datum/brain_trauma/special/primal_instincts`.
2. Possess it by setting its `ckey` to the admin's.
3. Wait for an episode. The trauma rolls for one in `on_life`, and a chat message "You feel the urge to act on your primal instincts..." marks the start. During an episode the AI runs while the player keeps their client.
4. Toggle combat mode on with the HUD button or the `F` key.
5. Observed: the "toggle combat mode" HUD element went from `combat` to `combat_off` by itself within about a second, and `combat_mode` was 0. Outside an episode the AI is off and combat mode was left alone.

The AI's enemy list and aggression flag had to be empty or false for the cause below to apply; in the test they were cleared through the AI blackboard.

### Cause

The monkey behaviour tree (`code/datums/ai/monkey/monkey.bt.json`) runs `monkey_set_combat_target` every second. Its `perform` in `code/datums/ai/monkey/monkey_bt_nodes.dm` turns combat mode off in two places:

```dm
if(HAS_TRAIT(living_pawn, TRAIT_PACIFISM) || (!length(enemies) && !controller.blackboard[BB_MONKEY_AGGRESSIVE]))
	living_pawn.set_combat_mode(FALSE)     // :191
...
if(!length(valids))
	living_pawn.set_combat_mode(FALSE)     // :208
```

For a normal AI monkey that is harmless. Feral Instincts gives the player's body a monkey AI controller with `continue_processing_when_client = TRUE` (`code/datums/brain_damage/special.dm`, `primal_instincts/on_gain`), so the behaviour runs against a mob that still has a player. Any time the monkey has nothing to target (the docile case, or aggressive with nobody in sight), the player's combat mode is cleared.

### Fix

Commit "Fix issue 91732: Feral Instincts no longer turns off the player's combat mode": both `set_combat_mode(FALSE)` calls in `monkey_set_combat_target/perform` are wrapped in `if(!living_pawn.client)`. Mobs with no client behave as before. The alternative of making the trauma suppress the behaviour was not pursued; the guard is local to the code that overwrites the player's setting.

### Verification

On the test server after rebuilding: a monkey with the trauma, enemies cleared and aggression false, was possessed and combat mode set on. Over about 11 seconds of AI activity (the admin log showed the AI on and the client attached, and the body bit a target during that time) `combat_mode` stayed 1. Before the fix the HUD flipped to off within a second.

Not checked: drinking actual Monkey Dust end to end, the aggressive case with a real target, and the pacifism branch (the guard also covers it, so a player with TRAIT_PACIFISM under Feral Instincts would no longer have combat mode forced off by this line; that was not tested).

### Notes

- The Jungle Arts/floor-pickup complaint is the same AI controlling the body during an episode, which the trauma intends. It was not changed.
- The original comment doubted the trauma itself; the trauma worked as designed, and the fault was the behaviour tree's unconditional toggle.

## #94318: AI monkeys don't switch active hands when dismembered

### The bug

An AI monkey that loses its active arm (the left, hand index 1, by default) stops attacking. It still picks things up and drags them around.

### Reproduction

1. Spawn an `angry` monkey type, and give it a target (the test used a human it was set to retaliate against, standing adjacent).
2. Dismember its left arm.
3. Observed on master: `active_hand_index` stayed 1, `has_active_hand()` returned false, the monkey had a target but did not attack. This was a partial reproduction: the first test monkey was noisy (a second monkey with Feral Instincts was also active), and the control comparison on master was not completed.

### Cause

`/mob/living/carbon/can_unarmed_attack()` (`code/_onclick/other_mobs.dm:25`) returns FALSE when `has_active_hand()` is false. Nothing makes an AI monkey switch hands, and `ai_interact` (`code/datums/ai/_ai_controller.dm:611`) does not either, so every interaction silently fails.

### Fix

Commit "Fix issue 94318: AI mobs switch to a working hand when the active arm is gone": `ai_interact` now checks `has_active_hand()` first. If it is false it loops over `get_num_hand_slots()`, and calls `swap_hand(hand_index, silent = TRUE)` for the first index with `has_hand_for_held_index()`. The change is in the shared AI controller, so any AI carbon benefits, not only monkeys.

### Verification

After the fix, a monkey with its left arm dismembered and an adjacent retaliation target: `active_hand_index` went from 1 to 2 within 10 seconds. The target had 25 brute damage at 10 seconds and 49 at 25 seconds; an intact control monkey against its own target had 10 and 35. Not checked: right-arm loss, both arms lost (the loop then does nothing), and a disabled-but-attached arm.

## #57776: AI monkeys move freely in space

### Result: not reproduced

A monkey placed on a strip of space turfs with an angry target at the end of it walked out onto floor and stayed on floor for 25 seconds; it never entered space. In code, both AI movement types check the next step before moving: `ai_movement_basic_avoidance.dm:26` and `ai_movement_dumb.dm:24` call `can_cross_safely`, and `/turf/open/space/can_cross_safely` (`code/game/turfs/open/space/space.dm:221`) returns `HAS_TRAIT(crossing, TRAIT_SPACEWALK)`. Ordinary monkeys lack that trait, so they will not path into space.

The original report is from 2021 and predates this check, so it is likely already fixed. Not tested: a monkey pushed or thrown into space (drifting), and AI mobs using other movement types. No fix was made.
