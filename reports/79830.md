## Summary

The issue says that gibbing a human without `DROP_ITEMS` deletes all their equipment except the shoes, because the shoes get dropped when the legs are removed. On master `87b93338` this does not happen: gibbing a human wearing shoes, gloves and a jumpsuit left none of them behind. The gib path now passes `special = TRUE` to the limbs' `drop_limb`, and the arm and leg versions skip the unequip in that case. The fixing change was not identified, because the clone available for testing was shallow.

## The bug

Smite -> Gib on a human with shoes left the shoes on the floor while the rest of the equipment vanished with the mob. The reporter guessed the order of operations: the legs come off and drop the shoes, then the mob is deleted along with the remaining items. They suspected the same for gloves. Comments in the thread disagreed on whether it was a bug; the reporter's position was that a gib without `DROP_ITEMS` should leave no items. A 2025 comment traced it to `spread_bodyparts()` calling `drop_limb()`.

## Reproduction

On the test server, with the round started and an observer using the Lua editor:

1. Spawn a `/mob/living/carbon/human`, spawn black sneakers, black gloves and a grey jumpsuit, and equip each with `equip_to_appropriate_slot`.
2. Confirm before the gib that the human's `shoes` var is the sneakers and the sneakers are inside the human.
3. Call `gib(6)` on the human. 6 is `DROP_ORGANS|DROP_BODYPARTS`, the flags that Smite -> Gib passes (`code/modules/admin/smites/gib.dm:7`).
4. Search the map for shoes, gloves and jumpsuits.

Observed (four runs): no shoes, gloves or jumpsuit remained. The legs, arms, organs and gibs were present (for example "human left leg"). The search was validated first: a black shoes object spawned on the floor was found by searching "shoe".

The gib was called from the Lua editor, not through the Smite menu; it uses the same flags. Only humans were tested.

## Cause

Old behaviour, as described in the issue and read from the code: `spread_bodyparts` called `drop_limb` without `special`, and the leg and arm `drop_limb` overrides then unequipped the feet and hand items.

On master, `code/modules/mob/living/carbon/death.dm:88` calls `part.drop_limb(special = gibbed, dismembered = !gibbed)`. A gib therefore passes `special = TRUE`, and:

- `/obj/item/bodypart/leg/drop_limb` returns before dropping the leg cuffs and the shoes when `special` is set (`code/modules/surgery/bodyparts/dismemberment.dm:194-196`);
- `/obj/item/bodypart/arm/drop_limb` takes its early path for `special` (`dismemberment.dm:172`).

The remaining items are deleted with the mob. When `DROP_ITEMS` is set, `/mob/living/carbon/gib` drops every item first (`carbon/death.dm:19-22`), which is the intended opposite behaviour.

## Fix

None needed.

## Verification

The runs above showed no shoes, gloves or jumpsuit after the gib with the Smite flags. Not checked: the `DROP_ITEMS` case itself, species or mob types other than a plain human, and the history of which change fixed this (the clone was shallow, so the fixing PR is unknown).
