## Summary

A chasm lobstrosity's charge applies an "instant" melee attack on impact with `ignore_cooldown = TRUE`, which skips the call that starts the mob's 1 second melee cooldown. Its grab (from `mob_grabber`) and a cancelled amputation attempt also end the attack chain without starting any cooldown. The AI therefore attacks or grabs again on the next tick, which players see as several bites per second. The charge also re-hits its victim on every bump tick. The first commit makes the charge hit, the grab and the "already busy" cancel all start a cooldown and stops the charge's push after a hit. A second commit fixes #97866, where lobstrosities defending against an ore vent attacked the vent instead of the node drone buckled to it.

## The bug

### #98097 and #97638

Players dodging a lobstrosity or running around it after a charge were bitten many times a second. The same report appears as "it hits you the instant you get in range, even while it runs away" (#97638). Normal melee bites are one second apart; the problem was in the paths that are not normal bites: the charge impact, the grab that follows it, and attack attempts cancelled by the grab.

### #97866

During ore vent wave defence, a lobstrosity hit "the ore vent" (a loud clang) rather than the node drone that is buckled to it.

### #97898 and #97917

#97898 reports a lobstrosity attacking itself when a charge pushes it and its victim against a wall. #97917 reports a lobstrosity that "fake-charges": it charges from afar at a player standing still and stops short.

## Reproduction

All done with admin tools on the test server: the Lua editor to spawn mobs and to log the `hostile_pre_attackingtarget` / `hostile_post_attackingtarget` signals with `world.time`, and a MetaStation hallway for space.

### #98097 / #97638

1. Spawn a human with a very large maxHealth (so the test is not cut short) and a chasm lobstrosity (`/mob/living/basic/mining/lobstrosity/lava`) a tile away. Register the two attack signals on the lobstrosity from Lua and record the times.
2. Let the lobstrosity charge the human and hit it, and let it grab the human. Move the human around it.
3. Observed on master:
   - After a charge hit, the lobstrosity's own `next_move` stayed stale (it did not advance a second later even though the lobstrosity had just bitten). In one measurement (human already pulled by the lobster) the bite result was 19 and `next_move` was unchanged one second later.
   - Gaps between successful attacks of 5.5, 4 and 2.5 deciseconds were recorded in an early run, where a normal gap is about 9 to 10 deciseconds. That run partly used a limb-capped human, so it also had a test artifact (a zero-damage result gives no cooldown at all); the human was fully healed in later runs.
   - Cancelled attack chains (the grab) showed up as pre-attack signals about 1 decisecond apart with no post-attack signal (for example three pre-attacks at ticks 6591.5, 6592.5 and 6593.5). These repeated attempts with no cooldown match the "0 cooldown" report.
   - Each charge printed "charges into X" three times, i.e. the victim was bumped and hit again on later ticks of the same charge.
   - Ordinary bites (human moved around the lobstrosity with `forceMove`, or chased with `Move`) had gaps of at least 10 deciseconds, so plain circling alone did not reproduce the problem. It needs the charge hit, grab or cancelled-grab paths.

### #97866

1. Place an ore vent, buckle a node drone to it (`buckle_mob`), spawn a lobstrosity three tiles away and set its current target to the drone.
2. Observed on master: the AI attacked "the ore vent" about every 20 deciseconds (the attack obstruction behaviour has a 2 second period) and the vent's `take_damage` played the clang sound even though the vent is indestructible.

### #97898

A human was placed against a wall and charged. The lobstrosity "smashes into the wall"; no pre-attack or post-attack signal ever had the lobstrosity as its own target, and its health stayed at 150. Not reproduced.

### #97917

Not reproduced deliberately. In passing one charge stopped two tiles short (the lobstrosity went from x=148 to x=145 with the human at x=143) after being triggered from Lua; the cause was not investigated on the server.

## Cause

### #98097 / #97638

- `code/modules/mob/living/basic/lavaland/lobstrosity/lobstrosity.dm:122-131`, `hit_target` of the lobster charge, calls `basic_source.melee_attack(living_target, ignore_cooldown = TRUE)` (line 128). In `code/modules/mob/living/basic/basic.dm:229-239`, `melee_attack` only calls `changeNext_move(melee_attack_cooldown)` when `ignore_cooldown` is false, so this attack never starts the 1 second cooldown.
- The AI's melee leaf (`code/datums/ai/basic_mobs/basic_ai_behaviors/basic_attacking.dm:23-56`) is gated by `BB_BASIC_MOB_MELEE_COOLDOWN_TIMER` and `pawn.next_move`. The blackboard timer is a one-off 0.4 second delay set when it is empty (lines 31-36) and is only cleared when the target is unreachable (line 28). In practice the throttle is `next_move`, which was stale, so the AI attacks again right after the charge.
- The lobstrosity has the `mob_grabber` element (`lobstrosity.dm:46`). Its `grab_mob` (`code/datums/elements/mob_grabber.dm:23-31`) handles the pre-attack signal, starts `grabbedby` asynchronously and returns `COMPONENT_CANCEL_ATTACK_CHAIN`. `early_melee_attack` turns that into `BASIC_MOB_END_ATTACK_CHAIN`, and `melee_attack` only applies the cooldown for `BASIC_MOB_END_ATTACK_CHAIN_COOLDOWN` (basic.dm:231-234). So a grab sets no cooldown, and the AI can start another grab or attack on the next tick (`basic_melee_attack` has no `time_between_perform`). The same applies to the "already busy!" cancel in the `amputating_limbs` component (`code/datums/components/amputating_limbs.dm:53-55`, used by the lobstrosity at `lobstrosity.dm:51`).
- Separately, the charge is a `home_onto` move loop towards a fixed turf beyond the victim. After the victim is hit, the lobstrosity keeps pushing against them, so `on_bump` (`code/datums/actions/mobs/charge.dm:151-164`) fires `hit_target` again on every tick, which explains the repeated "charges into" lines.

### #97866

`attack_obstructions` (`code/datums/ai/generic_behaviors/attack_obstacles.dm:15-42`) checks whether the next step towards the target is blocked, excluding mobs (`is_blocked_turf(exclude_mobs = TRUE, ...)`). The vent is dense, so the vent tile is "blocked" even though the target (a drone buckled to the vent) is on it and can be hit directly, and the behaviour smashes the vent. Independently, the charge's `on_bump` for a dense object hits the object, and `basic_charge/can_hit_target` returns true for dense non-living objects (`charge.dm:201-206`), so a charge into the vent hit the vent rather than the drone. Hitting an indestructible vent still plays its attack sound (`code/game/atom/atom_defense.dm:24-27`).

### #97898

No path was found where the lobstrosity targets itself: `on_bump` returns immediately when `owner == target` (`charge.dm:153-154`). The report may describe something not reachable on this commit; the specific scenario ("pushed against an ore vent") was not tested with a vent.

### #97917

By code reading only: `do_charge` (`charge.dm:36-76`) aims at a fixed turf and gives the move loop a timeout of `min(get_dist, charge_distance) * charge_speed` (line 63, `charge_speed` 0.5 for the loop `delay`). With lag or slowdown the loop can time out before arrival, so the charge stops short of a victim that moved or was delayed. This was not confirmed in game and it is not clear that it explains a player who stands still.

## Fix

Two commits, in order of application:

1. "Fix issue 98097: lobstrosity charge hit and grabs now start the melee cooldown":
   - `lobstrosity.dm` `hit_target`: `melee_attack` is no longer called with `ignore_cooldown`, and `changeNext_move(melee_attack_cooldown)` is always called after it, because the instant attack may be replaced by the grab (which returns early without a cooldown). `GLOB.move_manager.stop_looping(basic_source)` is called at the end so the charge stops pushing and does not re-hit on each tick.
   - `mob_grabber.dm` `grab_mob`: `source.changeNext_move(CLICK_CD_GRABBING)` before starting the async grab, since the grab replaces the attack.
   - `amputating_limbs.dm`: the "already busy!" branch sets `CLICK_CD_MELEE`.
   Alternatives mentioned in the investigation: resetting `BB_BASIC_MOB_MELEE_COOLDOWN_TIMER` in `basic_melee_attack` after an attack; not done.
2. "Fix issue 97866: chasm lobstrosity hits the buckled node drone, not the ore vent":
   - `attack_obstacles.dm`: `attack_obstructions` returns failed (so the selector falls through to melee) when `target.IsReachableBy(basic_mob)`.
   - `charge.dm` `on_bump`: if the bumped object has mobs buckled to it, the charge hits the first of them (other than the charger) instead of the object.

## Verification

On the test server, with a rebuilt tree:

- Final build, human against a wall with the AI on and the human as target: the lobstrosity's attack times were 1380.0 (bump, cancelled grab with no post-attack), then 1391.0, 1402.5, 1414.0, 1424.5, 1435.5, i.e. gaps of 10 to 11.5 deciseconds. A second charge produced a single bump at 1459.0 and the next attack 11.5 deciseconds later at 1470.5. Targets were always the human. On the old build the same charge printed "charges into" three times and pre-attacks 1 decisecond apart.
- #97866: on an earlier build of the fix (before the `stop_looping` line was added), a vent with a buckled drone and a lobstrosity gave attacks that all targeted the drone, with gaps of 14, 13, 12 and 12.5 deciseconds and none at the vent. One pair of attacks 1 decisecond apart was seen on that build and attributed to the repeated charge bump, which `stop_looping` was added to address.
- #97898: the wall scenario described above was run; no self-attack occurred.

Not checked:

- The vent and drone test was not re-run on the final build (with the `stop_looping` change).
- The `stop_looping` effect on the repeated-bump behaviour was observed only in the wall test above.
- #97917's cause was not tested.
- No player-driven playtest, only scripted humans.
- The "already busy" amputation change was not exercised separately.
- DreamDaemon crashed twice during testing ("Crashing due to an illegal operation" while loading Lua) when Lua scripts deleted Lua-held mobs or indexed `contents[1]`; this appears unrelated to the fix.

## Notes

- #97638's complaint that the lobstrosity keeps meleeing while running away is partly by design: the AI behaviour tree (`lobstrosity.bt.json`) runs the flee behaviour in parallel with the melee leaf, so a lobstrosity can flee and bite in the same moment. After the fix the bites are at least one cooldown apart, but it may still bite while retreating.
- The grab cooldown (`CLICK_CD_GRABBING`) applies to every user of `mob_grabber`, not only lobstrosities. The `amputating_limbs` change likewise applies to all users of that component. A maintainer may prefer limiting both to lobstrosities.
- #97898 might need a different scenario (for example a charge into an ore vent, as the report mentions) to be reproduced; the code guard on self-targeting exists in `on_bump` only.
- #97917 is reported as possibly intended by a commenter on the issue; the reporter asked why a lobstrosity would not hit the player. No change was made.
