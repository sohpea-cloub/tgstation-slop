## Summary

The report says that a positronic "cube" cannot examine the pre-placed blue solar flooring on the map. On master, a possessed positronic brain mob examined solar floor tiles normally, both with the cube lying on the floor and with it held in a human's hand. No code path in the examine checks was found that would block these tiles. One untested lead remains: a brain mob that entered the cube through the normal ghost flow might have different `sight` flags than the one tested.

## The bug

As reported: a player controlling a positronic cube (a sphere) shift-clicks the blue floor tiles meant to hold solar arrays and gets no examine output. The reporter's later comments say that other blue floor tiles did examine, and that they were unsure whether the problem was specific to those tiles or to being viewed as a cube. Their screenshots, as described by the testers, show a normal human examining the same kind of blue floor and getting the usual "That's a floor. There's a small crack on the edge of it."

## Reproduction

Not reproduced. What was tried on the test server (Meta Station, round running):

1. Using the Lua editor, spawned a `/obj/item/brain_processor/positronic` (the positronic cube; the player controls its `/mob/living/brain`) at a tile of the Starboard Quarter Solar Array on z-level 2, and gave the brain mob to the admin's client by setting its `ckey`. Its `sight` was `SEE_MOBS|SEE_OBJS|SEE_TURFS`.
2. Shift-clicked four solar tiles at various distances across the array. Each time the chat printed "That's a floor. There's a small crack on the edge of it."
3. Repeated with the cube held in a human's hand: the same result. A catwalk tile also examined fine.
4. With SDQL2, queried the solar panel turfs on z-level 2. All 215 have `luminosity` 1 (the Lua scan found none with 0), so the darkness check in the examine code cannot be the cause there.

The mob was possessed by setting `ckey` directly, not by a ghost entering the cube, so the real entry path was not exercised.

## Cause

No defect was found. `/mob/proc/run_examinate` (`code/modules/mob/mob.dm:556`) has three early returns that could silently drop an examine:

- `mob.dm:560`: a turf is dropped if the mob lacks `SEE_TURFS` and the turf is not in `view(client.view, src)`. If `src` is inside an item, `view()` from there would not include outside turfs, so a brain mob without `SEE_TURFS` would fail every turf. The tested mob had `SEE_TURFS`.
- `mob.dm:565`: blind mobs go through `blind_examine_check`.
- `mob.dm:568`: a turf with no luminosity and no dynamic light, more than one tile away and without night vision, is dropped. Solar tiles all have luminosity 1.

The solar floor is `/turf/open/floor/iron/solarpanel` (`code/game/turfs/open/floor/iron_floor.dm:458`), an ordinary floor subtype with nothing examine-specific.

## Fix

None. No commits.

## Verification

Nothing to verify. Not checked:

- A brain mob that entered the cube via the real ghost flow (for example `transfer_personality`), whose `sight` was not inspected; if it lacks `SEE_TURFS`, check 1 above would drop every turf.
- A cube inside a bag or other container, or inside a cyborg or MMI.
- Whether map-loaded tiles differ from hand-built ones in some way the tested click path would not show.
- A purely client-side cause (hover or click catching), which the reporter's second comment might hint at.

## Notes

The reporter's own follow-up comments suggest the effect was inconsistent, which fits a situational cause rather than a tile defect. The lead worth testing is `sight` on a ghost-entered brain mob.
