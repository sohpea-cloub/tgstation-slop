## Summary

All three issues are in the airlock code. #39470: `/obj/machinery/door` never checked `heat_proof` in `fire_act`, so a heat-proof airlock that was open (and so could have a hotspot on its tile) lost integrity to fire like any other object; doors now ignore `fire_act` when `heat_proof` is set. #67140: an unpowered external airlock with a `space_dir` forced a 15 second "safety mechanism" delay on everybody, even from the space side or on a docked shuttle; it now opens at once in those cases and the delay remains only for opening toward an unsafe outside from the station side. #78054: external airlocks never got the one-way ("unrestricted side") overlay refreshed after `space_dir` was added to `unres_sides`, and a cycle-linked partner airlock received the `space_dir` but not the unrestricted side; both are now updated.

## #39470: Open heat-proof airlocks take heat damage

### The bug

Airlocks with `heat_proof = TRUE` (for example the incinerator airlocks, firedoors and pod doors) are unharmed by fire when closed, but are damaged once opened.

### Reproduction

Done with the Lua editor on the test server, by calling `fire_act` directly. No real fire was started, so the part about a hotspot landing on an open door's tile is from reading the code, not observed.

1. Spawn `/obj/machinery/door/airlock/public/glass/incinerator` (`heat_proof` TRUE, 300 integrity) and a normal `/obj/machinery/door/airlock/public/glass` and open both. Also spawn a closed incinerator airlock.
2. Call `fire_act(1000, 100)` on each: all stayed at 300 integrity. 1000 K is below the airlocks' `damage_deflection` (21; the fire damage is 0.02 times the temperature, so more than about 1050 K is needed).
3. Call `fire_act(3000, 100)` on an open incinerator airlock and an open normal glass airlock: both dropped from 300 to 296.

### Cause

`/obj/fire_act` (`code/game/objects/obj_defense.dm:125`) damages any object where `0.02 * exposed_temperature` exceeds `damage_deflection`, and `/obj/machinery/door` did not override it. The only place doors use `heat_proof` is `block_superconductivity()` (`code/game/machinery/doors/door.dm:694`). A closed door blocks air, so no hotspot ends up on its tile, which is why it appeared to work. An open door lets a hotspot sit on its tile, and `fire_act` then reaches it.

### Fix

The commit "Fix issue 39470: heat-proof doors no longer take heat damage from fires while open" adds, in `door.dm`:

```dm
/obj/machinery/door/fire_act(exposed_temperature, exposed_volume)
	// Heat-proof doors don't take heat damage from fires, whether they're open (so a hotspot can sit on their tile) or closed
	if(heat_proof)
		return
	return ..()
```

Placing it on `/obj/machinery/door` covers every door type that sets `heat_proof` (airlocks, firedoors, pod doors, password doors and so on) without touching `/obj/fire_act` for other objects.

### Verification

After rebuilding, the same `fire_act(3000, 100)` call left an open heat-proof airlock at 300 integrity while the normal open airlock went to 296. Not tested: a real fire with a hotspot on an open door tile, other `heat_proof` door types, and flammable-item behaviour.

### Notes

A 2018 comment in the issue said it was "pretty sure" a duplicate; which issue was meant is unknown.

## #67140: Unpowered external airlocks take 15 seconds to open

### The bug

On an unpowered station, touching an external airlock (the reporter meant the arrivals airlocks) to open it showed a 15 second "unlocking the airlock safety mechanism" wait. Worse, on the second inner airlock the shuttle could leave and the pressure difference could drag the player out again. The code's author said that unpowered airlocks should behave as if powered, and that the 15 second wait was meant for opening from the inside (the station side) toward an unsafe outside, not from the outside.

### Reproduction

Not run on master in game: the cause was found by reading the code (below), and no before-fix test was made. The reporter's steps (unpower the arrivals area, touch the external airlocks from each side, time the opening) were not followed. Which mapped doors the reporter meant was not determined; the arrival shuttle maps checked (for example `_maps/shuttles/arrival/arrival_box.dmm`) use titanium or shuttle airlocks, not `/obj/machinery/door/airlock/external`.

### Cause

`/obj/machinery/door/airlock/external/try_safety_unlock` (`code/game/machinery/doors/airlock.dm:2334-2347`): for a closed external airlock with a `space_dir`, if `!hasPower()` it always showed the message and ran `do_after(user, 15 SECONDS, target = src)` before `try_to_crowbar(null, user, TRUE)`. The access-free opening from the space side, and `should_safety_open` (shuttle docked, linked airlock's shuttle docked, or a safe turf outside), were only evaluated in the powered branch.

### Fix

The commit "Fix issue 67140: unpowered external airlocks open at once from the space side or when safe" computes `should_safety_open` before the power check. If the airlock is unpowered and either `should_safety_open` is true or the user is on the space side (`get_dir(src, user) & space_dir`), it calls `try_to_crowbar(null, user, TRUE)` immediately. Only when neither holds does the 15 second safety unlock still run. The powered branch is unchanged.

### Verification

On the rebuilt test server, with the airlock unpowered (`machine_stat` set to the no-power value) and `try_safety_unlock` called from Lua:

- A Meta escape pod door with `space_dir` north and a shuttle docked: called from the space side (north), it returned 1 and the door was open within about 2 seconds. From the station side (south) it returned 1 and the door was open within 3 seconds; the docked shuttle makes `should_safety_open` true.
- A second external airlock with `space_dir` south, no shuttle docked and a safe turf outside: called from the station side (north), it was open by 3 seconds.

Not checked: the remaining slow branch (station side, outside unsafe or vacuum, no shuttle docked), which is unchanged code; any test of the real touch/bump path by a player; the arrivals shuttle itself.

### Notes

Design points a maintainer may want to weigh: with this change the unpowered door behaves like the powered one, as the author described, but a player on the station side with an unsafe outside still has to wait 15 seconds (the author's description of unpowered inside use mentioned a crowbar instead). The issue's text also says the shuttle leaves while the player is in the airlock; that is not addressed.

## #78054: External airlocks' one-way visuals

### The bug

External airlocks that have one-way access toward space (`space_dir`) did not always show the one-way floor light overlay, though opening them and triggering an update showed it.

### Reproduction

1. On the stock Meta map at round start, a check of the 24 mapped external airlocks that have a `space_dir` found that all of them already had `unres_sides` equal to `space_dir`, and calling `update_appearance()` on each changed none of their overlay counts (so there was no missing overlay to add). Two other external airlocks had `unres_sides` set with no `space_dir` (not related to this report). So the roundstart picture in the issue was not reproduced on Meta.
2. Using the Lua editor, two external airlocks were spawned: X with `space_dir = 1`, `unres_sides = 1` and `cyclelinkeddir` toward Y, and Y with `cyclelinkeddir` toward X. Calling `X:cyclelinkairlock()` gave Y `space_dir = 1` but `unres_sides = 0`. So the partner airlock has the space direction but not the one-way behaviour or overlay.

### Cause

In `code/game/machinery/doors/airlock.dm`, `/obj/machinery/door/airlock/external/post_machine_initialize` (line 2317) adds `space_dir` to `unres_sides`, but this runs in the late initialize step, after the airlock's icon was built, and nothing refreshed the appearance. The overlay is only drawn when `hasPower() && unres_sides` (`code/game/machinery/doors/airlock.dm:626`, in the overlay update code), so it appears at the next update (a power change, opening, and so on). `external/cyclelinkairlock` (line 2327) copies `space_dir` to the cycle-linked partner (and from it) but never adds it to `unres_sides` of either door, so the partner lacked the one-way behaviour too, not only the visuals. `cyclelinkairlock` is called from the airlock mapping helper (`code/modules/mapping/mapping_helpers.dm:166`) and when `cyclelinkeddir` is edited with View Variables.

### Fix

The commit "Fix issue 78054: external airlocks show and use the one-way side from their space direction" adds `update_appearance()` after `unres_sides |= space_dir` in `post_machine_initialize`, and in `cyclelinkairlock` ORs the merged `space_dir` into `unres_sides` of both airlocks and updates the appearance of both.

### Verification

With the same two-airlock setup after the fix, both X and Y had `unres_sides` = 1 (Y was 0 before). Not checked: the overlay itself after the fix (no render or overlay comparison of the cycle-linked pair), a real round with mapped cycle-linked airlocks, and whether `post_machine_initialize` changes anything visible on any stock map (it did not on Meta at round start).

### Notes

The stock-map roundstart state looked correct on Meta (most likely because power changes during roundstart rerun the icon update; this was not confirmed), so the extra `update_appearance()` in `post_machine_initialize` is a low-risk safety net rather than a demonstrated fix for the reported pictures. The cycle-link change is the part with an observed defect before the fix. Other maps were not checked.
