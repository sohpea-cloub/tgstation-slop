# #46112: Throwing tethered defibrillator paddles gives free, repeatable thrust in space

| | |
|---|---|
| Issues | [#46112](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/46112): verified (reproduced on master, fixed on the branch) |
| Status | Verified; fix committed, awaiting a maintainer decision (see Notes) |
| Fix branch | [fix/46112](https://github.com/sohpea-cloub/tgstation-slop/tree/fix/46112) |
| Tested on | tgstation master `87b93338`, 2026-10-23 |

## Summary

Throwing the paddles of a defibrillator makes them drop out of the thrower's hand, and `shockpaddles/dropped()` immediately snaps them back into the defibrillator, so the paddles never travel anywhere. `/mob/living/throw_item()` does not notice this and still calls `newtonian_move()` on the thrower, so each "throw" pushes the thrower in space with nothing actually thrown. The paddles can be taken out again and the cycle repeated indefinitely. The fix overrides `on_thrown()` for tethered paddles so that the throw is refused with a message; paddles that do not require a defibrillator (combat and cyborg paddles) are unchanged.

## The bug

A player in space takes the paddles out of a defibrillator on their back or belt, throws them, and drifts in the opposite direction of the throw. The paddles reappear in the defibrillator straight away, so the player can pull them out and throw them again, gaining speed each time. In the game it looks like the paddles are thrown and "come back"; in reality they never left the defibrillator. The thrower receives the recoil of a throw that did not happen.

The 2019 comments on the issue mostly called this funny or intentional, and pointed out other cheap ways to move in space (cables, paper bins, credit chips). One comment (Putnam3145) noted that, physically, the thrower should be pulled back once the cord goes taut; here the thrower just keeps the momentum. Whether the behaviour should stay is a maintainer's call (see Notes).

## Reproduction

Done on the test server with admin tools. The throws were made by calling `throw_item()` on the mob through the Lua editor, not by a player pressing the throw key, so the player-facing input path (throw mode and a click) was not exercised.

1. Start a round and open the Lua editor.
2. Create a human in open space (a space turf at x=107, y=234, z=2 was used) and give it a defibrillator on its back:
   `H = SS13.new('/mob/living/carbon/human', turf)`, `D = SS13.new('/obj/item/defibrillator', H.loc)`, `H:equip_to_appropriate_slot(D)`.
3. Take the paddles out: `D:toggle_paddles(H)`. The paddles were in the human's active hand, the defibrillator on its back.
4. Throw them towards a turf 7 tiles west of the human: `H:throw_item(get_ranged_target_turf(H, WEST, 7))`.
5. Check the result.

Observed on master before the fix:

- `throw_item()` returned 1 (success).
- The paddles' location was the defibrillator (they had snapped back); the active hand was empty and the defibrillator's `on` flag was still 1 when read just after the throw.
- The human's `drift_handler` was a `/datum/drift_handler` and its x coordinate went from 107 to 121, i.e. it moved east, away from the throw direction, with no one pushing it.
- Repeating steps 3 and 4 (`toggle_paddles`, then another throw) worked every time, with the human at x=147 before the second throw was started. The human kept accelerating in the same direction. These x readings were taken a few seconds apart, so they show direction and growth, not exact speeds.

## Cause

Three pieces of code combine:

- `/obj/item/proc/on_thrown()` (`code/game/objects/items.dm:1339`) drops the item with `user.dropItemToGround(src, silent = TRUE)` and then returns `src` as the thing to be thrown (unless the user is a pacifist or cannot throw).
- `/obj/item/shockpaddles/dropped()` (`code/game/objects/items/tools/medical/defib.dm:442`) calls `snap_back()` (`defib.dm:452`) for tethered paddles (`req_defib` set), which does `forceMove(defib)`. So the item that `on_thrown()` just handed back is now inside the defibrillator.
- `/mob/living/throw_item()` (`code/modules/mob/living/living_item_handling.dm:38`) takes the returned paddles as `thrown_thing` (line 67) and carries on. It then applies the recoil to the thrower unconditionally:

  ```dm
  newtonian_move(get_angle(target, src), drift_force = drift_force)
  thrown_thing.safe_throw_at(target, ...)
  ```
  (lines 119-120). `safe_throw_at()` does nothing useful because the paddles are inside a container, but `newtonian_move()` has already pushed the thrower. The proc also ends without an explicit return value, so the `TRUE` from the parent `/mob/proc/throw_item()` is what the caller sees.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 46112: tethered defib paddles can't be thrown". It adds an override in `code/game/objects/items/tools/medical/defib.dm`, next to `snap_back()`:

```dm
/obj/item/shockpaddles/on_thrown(mob/living/carbon/user, atom/target)
	if(!req_defib)
		return ..()
	// Tethered paddles snap back into the unit the moment they leave your hands, so a "throw" would only give you free momentum
	to_chat(user, span_warning("[src] are tethered to [defib]; you can't throw them!"))
	return null
```

Returning `null` makes `throw_item()` return `FALSE` at `if(!thrown_thing)` (line 68), before the paddles are dropped and before `newtonian_move()` runs. The paddles stay in the hand and the thrower is not pushed. Paddles with `req_defib` unset (combat and cyborg paddles) take the normal `..()` path and are thrown as before.

Alternative not implemented: moving the recoil check into `throw_item()` (for example, skipping `newtonian_move()` when the thrown item is no longer in the open after the drop) would be more general and also cover other items that teleport back on drop, but it touches the shared throwing code. The override keeps the change local to the one item.

## Verification

Checked in game after rebuilding, on a fresh round, with the same setup as in Reproduction (human in space at 107,234,2, defibrillator on back, paddles taken out, throw towards a turf 7 tiles west via `throw_item()` in the Lua editor):

- `throw_item()` returned 0.
- The human stayed at 107,234 and its `drift_handler` was nil.
- The paddles were still in the active hand, with the defibrillator on.

Not checked:

- The chat message shown to the player was not read back, and the throw was not done through the normal throw key.
- Only one throw was made after the fix (there is nothing to repeat, since the throw is refused).
- Combat paddles and cyborg paddles were not tried, so "unchanged" is from reading the code, not from a test.
- Paddles held while on a station floor (no space) or thrown by a pacifist were not tried.
- No other item with a drop-time snap-back was examined (see Notes).

## Notes

- Intent: in 2019 several commenters called the behaviour intentional or "hilarious", while others called it an oversight; the issue carries the Oversight label and received a "Is this still an issue?" comment in 2023. This fix is a design change as well as a bug fix and needs a maintainer to decide. The argument for it: the paddles never actually travel, so the thrower is gaining momentum with no reaction mass; the defibrillator cord would physically stop the pair.
- If tethered throwing is ever meant to work (paddles flung to the end of a cord), it would need its own handling rather than the current drop-and-snap-back path.
- A commenter (Learner10) wondered whether other detachable-part items (firefighter backpack nozzles, for instance) share the issue. That was not investigated here.
- Detective-Google's comment noted other ways to get cheap space propulsion; those are separate behaviours and were not touched.
- Whether upstream has already changed this since master `87b93338` is unknown; it was not checked.
