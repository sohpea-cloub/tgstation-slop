## Summary

Stump limbs cannot be dismembered on master by damage, explosions or a direct `dismember()` call, so #96177 was not reproduced. The likeliest real cause is the bioscrambler picking stumps, fixed by PR 96907 on 2026-07-10, after the report (2026-05-21); this could not be verified. #84302 (blood drawn on a missing leg) could not be tested because the screenshot in the issue is too small to read.

## #96177: stump limbs can be dismembered

### The bug

The report says a stump limb was dismembered, which made surgical reattachment impossible. It gives no steps ("Unsure"); it points to one player's logs about 2 hours 10 minutes into round 262063.

### Reproduction

Not reproduced. On the test server, using the Lua editor and View Variables on spawned humans:

1. Dismembered a leg, leaving a stump.
2. Called `dismember()` on the stump: it returned 0 and the stump stayed.
3. Applied `receive_damage(60, wound_bonus = 100)` six times and `apply_damage(300)`: the stump survived and gained a bone wound.
4. Called `ex_act(2)` eight times and `ex_act(1)` eight times, then healed fully and killed the human: all four stumps on the test humans survived (stump counts stayed at 4).

### Cause (why it does not happen)

Stump types (`code/modules/surgery/bodyparts/stumps.dm`) carry `BODYPART_UNREMOVABLE | BODYPART_STUMP`. `dismember()` returns `FALSE` for unremovable parts (`code/modules/surgery/bodyparts/dismemberment.dm:9`), `can_dismember()` does too (`:2`), and wound-based loss checks `can_dismember` (`code/datums/wounds/loss.dm:30`).

A path that could remove stumps existed before PR 96907: the bioscrambler could select stumps, since `BODYPART_CAN_BE_BIOSCRAMBLED` (`code/__DEFINES/mobs.dm:171-176`) now excludes them. That PR was merged after the issue was filed and is the probable cause of the report. This is an inference; the original round's logs were not available.

### Fix

None.

### Verification

The checks above are the verification. Not tested: the bioscrambler itself, either before or after PR 96907.

### Notes

A hardening lead, not changed: the ghost species' `swap_mode` calls `drop_limb()` on every entry in the raw `bodyparts` list (`code/modules/mob/living/carbon/human/species_types/ghost.dm:221-223`), which includes stumps. `drop_limb()` is not guarded here. Other unguarded-looking callers (harvester, spread_bodyparts, flesh, butchering, jelly) use `get_bodyparts()` / `get_bodypart()`, which exclude stumps. A `if(IS_STUMP(bodypart)) continue` there would be the obvious guard, but it was not written or tested and only the rare ghost species is affected.

## #84302: blood overlay drawn on a missing leg

### The bug

A 2024 report (client 515.1639) shows a character whose right leg is missing while the leg appears bloody; the reporter added that it is hard to see in the screenshot.

### Reproduction

Not reproduced. The attached screenshot is 87x91 pixels and unreadable, so the blood overlay in question could not be identified, and no in-game test was run.

### Leads from reading the code

No per-limb "blood on legs" overlay exists. Candidates are clothing blood overlays rather than the body:

- The jumpsuit's blood overlay (`get_blood_overlay("uniform")`, `code/modules/clothing/under/_under.dm:119-124` and `code/modules/clothing/clothing.dm:656`) draws the whole `uniformblood` icon regardless of missing legs.
- Shoes: `update_worn_shoes` returns early if there are fewer than two legs (`human_update_icons.dm:278`).
- The `bloodysoles` component (blood on feet).
- Limb damage blood overlays (`_bodyparts.dm:1157-1163`, `:1376`) and wound overlays (`carbon_update_icons.dm:313`).

The suspected fix would be to skip or mask the jumpsuit blood overlay for missing legs (`handle_masking`, `carbon_update_icons.dm:499-540`, already crops some layers), but this was not tried. A reproduction would be: equip a grey jumpsuit on a human, add blood DNA to it, dismember the right leg, and render the mob. That was planned but not done.
