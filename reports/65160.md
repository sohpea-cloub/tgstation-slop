## Summary

A blob overmind (a camera eye that is allowed to ride shuttles) hovering over the arrivals dock is counted as a living passenger when the arrivals shuttle docks. The shuttle then refuses to leave, and if it does leave, it carries the overmind to its home dock, far from any blob, where the overmind cannot walk back. The fix makes the arrivals shuttle ignore eye mobs: they are not counted by `PersonCheck()` and are not moved by the arrivals shuttle. Other shuttles are unchanged.

## The bug

The reporter, playing blob, moved the overmind view over the arrivals shuttle area just as the shuttle arrived. The overmind was then stuck on the shuttle for over a minute until an admin teleported it out. A commenter noted that other cameras able to see onto a shuttle (AI eyes, the aux base console) should behave the same way.

What happens: the overmind is carried along with the shuttle and the shuttle's "is anyone aboard" check counts it, so the shuttle stays docked. The overmind can only move one tile away from existing blob, and the shuttle's walls and engines are dense, so it cannot get off.

## Reproduction

On master, using the Lua editor and admin tools:

1. Create a blob overmind with a real core (call `place_blob_core(-1)` at the blob start turf). An overmind without a core deletes itself in its `process()`, so the core is required.
2. Move the overmind to the arrivals station dock turf (51,138 on z2 on the test map) with `forceMove`, and possess it.
3. Call `SSshuttle.arrivals:SendToStation()` so the shuttle arrives.

Observed before the fix:
- The shuttle docked and the overmind rode with it ("in Arrival Shuttle", still reported at 51,138,2).
- After 80+ seconds the shuttle was still docked: mode `idle`, `arrivals:PersonCheck()` returned `TRUE`.
- The overmind could not leave on its own.
- In an earlier run, where the overmind had no core, the shuttle did depart and the overmind's ghost was carried to the shuttle's home dock at 107,41 on z12.

The shuttle was driven by calling `SendToStation()` directly rather than through a normal round flow; the overmind was placed by admin tools rather than by a player moving it there.

## Cause

`code/modules/antagonists/blob/overmind.dm:23` sets `move_on_shuttle = TRUE`. In `/mob/eye/Initialize` (`code/modules/mob/eye/eye.dm:19-21`), eyes only get `TRAIT_BLOCK_SHUTTLE_MOVEMENT` when `move_on_shuttle` is false, so the overmind lacks the trait. Two things follow:

- `/mob/onShuttleMove` (`code/modules/shuttle/mobile_port/shuttle_move_callbacks.dm:316`) only skips mobs with that trait, so the overmind is moved with the shuttle.
- `PersonCheck()` (`code/modules/shuttle/mobile_port/variants/arrivals.dm:132`) counts any non-dead player in the shuttle's areas without the trait. The overmind counts, so the departure check fails with "lifeform dectected on board" (`arrivals.dm:159`).

The aux base console eye and the imaginary friend also set `move_on_shuttle = TRUE` and go through the same path.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 65160: blob overmind over the arrivals dock holds the shuttle and gets carried off":

- `/mob/onShuttleMove` returns early when the mob is an eye (`iseyemob`) and the moving shuttle is `/obj/docking_port/mobile/arrivals`.
- `PersonCheck()` adds `&& !iseyemob(player)`.

The fix is limited to the arrivals shuttle on purpose: a blob riding the emergency shuttle is intentional, so `move_on_shuttle` itself was left alone. It applies to every eye type, not just blob.

## Verification

After rebuilding the test server and repeating the same steps (core, overmind on the dock turf, `SendToStation()`):
- The shuttle arrived and left on its own.
- The overmind stayed at 51,138,2 (the space dock turf).
- `PersonCheck()` returned `FALSE`, and the shuttle returned to z12 (176,36).

Not tested: the aux base console eye, imaginary friend or AI eye (same code path, not exercised), and other shuttles, which were not touched.

## Notes

- `afterShuttleMove` still runs on an eye that was not moved; it only updates lights and applies a shake, which is harmless but unverified.
- Eyes inside the arrivals area now never block departure, which also covers admin or ghost-like eyes.
