## Summary

The issue says a bee given Cryogelidia stings a victim, who "turns blue" instead of being frozen in an ice block. On current master, a bee carrying Cryogelidia freezes its victim in an ice block as intended. The route in the report, injecting a queen bee with the reagent, is refused on master because Cryogelidia is not a synthesizable reagent. No fix was made.

## The bug

As reported: inject a queen bee with Cryogelidia, confirm on yourself that the reagent works, get stung by one of the resulting bees, and the victim turns blue rather than being encased in ice. A bystander in the report blamed "impurity-based reagents". In the comments the reporter confirmed the bees were named "Bee (Cryogelidia)", but the commenters were unsure of the cause and one said the case was stranger than it first looked. The report gives no round ID or build.

## Reproduction

Cryogelidia was given to the bee with the Lua editor, not by injecting a queen. Injecting a queen is refused on master (see Cause).

1. On the test server, spawn a human and a `/mob/living/basic/bee`.
2. In the Lua editor, call `assign_reagent` on the bee with the Cryogelidia reagent (`/datum/reagent/inverse/cryostylane`, shown in game as "Cryogelidia").
3. Make the bee attack the human with `melee_attack`.

Observed on master:

- Chat: "The bee (Cryogelidia) stings ...!".
- A `/obj/structure/ice_stasis` ice block appeared, the human's location was inside it, and the human had the `/datum/status_effect/reagent_effect/freeze` status effect. Cryogelidia was in the human's reagents at 0.88 to 5 units.
- The same result occurred with a second human.
- The first bee kept stinging, breaking the blocks and creating new ones. The test showed glass-break sounds and fresh ice blocks. This follows from bee AI and the ice block design, and is not the reported bug.

## Cause

Nothing is broken on this path:

- The bee's venom goes through the venomous element (`code/datums/element/venomous.dm`), which moves the reagent to the victim by injection and exposes them to it.
- `/datum/reagent/inverse/cryostylane/expose_mob` (`code/modules/reagents/chemistry/reagents/impure_reagents.dm:100`) applies the freeze status effect to humans, unless the method is ingestion or the target resists cold.
- The reagent is at full purity here, so the purity-splitting code in mob life does not turn it into something else on the way.

Why the reporter's route no longer works: Cryogelidia has no `REAGENT_CAN_BE_SYNTHESIZED` flag (`impure_reagents.dm:97`, `chemical_flags = REAGENT_DEAD_PROCESS | REAGENT_IGNORE_STASIS | REAGENT_UNAFFECTED_BY_METABOLISM`). `/obj/item/queen_bee/proc/handle_needle` (`code/modules/mob/living/basic/farm_animals/bee/_bee.dm:287`) checks the flag and refuses with "[chemical] cannot be inserted into a bee's genome!". So a "Bee (Cryogelidia)" can only come from admin tools today.

## Fix

None. There was nothing to change for the reported behaviour.

## Verification

Tested in game as described above. Not checked:

- Injecting a queen bee with the reagents that do work (for example Cryostylane at low purity, the inverse reaction), and whether bees carrying such a mix freeze victims.
- The 2023 build the reporter used. The "turn blue" outcome may have come from another reagent, or from code that has since changed.
- Bees carrying other inverse reagents.

## Notes

- The commenter's theory that only carbon mobs process reagents does not matter here, because the victim is a human carrying injected reagent and exposure happens immediately on injection.
- The issue is likely stale. A maintainer could close it, or keep it open if there is a known way to get Cryogelidia into a queen on current master.
