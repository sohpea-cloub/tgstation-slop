## Summary

On master, a custom sandwich could be started on a plain bread slice but never finished, because the ingredients holder component had no way to close a holder and refused any other bread slice as an ingredient. A metal rod could not take ingredients at all, so there were no custom kebabs. The fix adds an optional "closing" mode to the component (a plain slice with ingredients becomes a sandwich when a second bread slice is used on it) and gives metal rods a holder that turns one rod of a stack into a custom kebab. Custom cakes and pies, also named in the issues, already work on master and were not touched.

## The bug

Player-visible behaviour per the three issues, and what was observed on master:

- **Sandwiches** (#81480, #59312, #62728): ingredients can be put on a plain bread slice, but a second slice of bread cannot be used to finish the sandwich. It is refused, or (per #59312) added as another filling.
- **Kebabs** (#81480, #59312): ingredients used on a metal rod do nothing.
- **Cakes and pies** (#81480, #59312): reported as not accepting ingredients. Not the case on master `87b93338`: both worked in game. The likely reason is an earlier upstream change (PR #86013); that attribution was not independently checked.
- **Soup** (#59312): not tested. A comment on #59312 says soups are now made by heating.

## Reproduction

Done with the Lua editor (an admin tool) on a test server with the tester observing. The item interactions were performed by calling `attackby` on the target with the item and a spawned human as the user, not by clicking in a client.

1. Spawn a plain bread slice and a piece of cheese and use the cheese on the slice. Result on master: the slice became a "small cheese wedge bread slice" (the holder works).
2. Use a second plain bread slice on it. Result on master: the second slice was not accepted and stayed in the hand. No sandwich was produced.
3. Spawn a plain cake and a plain pie and use cheese on each. Result: "small cheese wedge cake" and "small cheese wedge pie" (both work on master).
4. Spawn an iron rod and use cheese on it. Result on master: the cheese stayed on the floor and nothing happened.

## Cause

- Sandwiches: the plain bread slice has a holder (`code/modules/food_and_drinks/food/bread.dm:71`, `AddComponent(/datum/component/ingredients_holder, null, CUSTOM_INGREDIENT_ICON_STACK)`), but the component (`code/datums/components/ingredients_holder.dm`) has no step where a holder is finished. In addition, `valid_ingredient()` (line 108) returns false for anything carrying `TRAIT_INGREDIENTS_HOLDER`, a trait the component adds to its parent (line 77), and every plain bread slice has that trait, so a second plain slice is rejected as an ingredient. This matches the comment on #81480 that the sandwich needs a rewrite of the ingredients holder component.
- Kebabs: no customizable kebab exists. `/obj/item/stack/rods` (`code/game/objects/items/stacks/rods.dm:54`, `Initialize`) never adds an ingredients holder, so the component's attack handler (`ingredients_holder.dm:121`, `customizable_attack`) is never reached for rods. A rod sprite and filling sprites exist in `icons/obj/food/meat.dmi` but were not used.
- Cakes and pies already have the component (`cake.dm:54`, `pie.dm:43`) and work.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 81480: custom sandwiches can be finished with a second slice, rods can be made into custom kebabs". It touches five files.

- `ingredients_holder.dm`: three new optional component arguments, `closing_type`, `closed_type` and `quiet_rejection`.
  - When `closing_type` is set and the holder already contains ingredients, using an item of that type on it closes the holder (new proc `close_holder()`): it creates the `closed_type` object, moves the component, reagents, contents and materials into it, adds the lid item, renames it, gives the finished item the lid's weight class if that is larger, puts it in the user's hand if the holder was held, and deletes the old object. An item that is itself a holder with ingredients cannot be used as a lid (`can_close_with()`).
  - For stacks (rods), only one unit is split off and turned into the custom food, the rest of the stack stays. The split unit is kept out of the world until it becomes the kebab, because putting it on the floor would merge it straight back into the stack.
  - `quiet_rejection` makes non-ingredients get ignored silently, so ordinary uses of a rod (for example with a crowbar or welder) do not show "doesn't go on that!".
  - `add_ingredient()` takes an optional location for the replacement object.
- `bread.dm`: the plain slice's holder closes with any `/obj/item/food/breadslice` into a new `/obj/item/food/sandwich/empty`.
- `sandwichtoast.dm`: the new `/obj/item/food/sandwich/empty` ("A custom sandwich, made by a chef with imagination and a lot of bread").
- `rods.dm`: non-cyborg rods whose merge type is the base rod stack get a holder (up to 6 ingredients, screentip verb "Skewer") that produces the new kebab.
- `meatdish.dm`: new `/obj/item/food/kebab/empty` (icon state `rod`).

The closing logic was put in the shared component rather than in the bread, so any other holder can opt in with two arguments.

## Verification

After the fix, on the test server, same method as above:

- A plain slice with ingredients (cheese and steak) held in hand, plus a second plain slice: became "small cheese wedge sandwich" (`/obj/item/food/sandwich/empty`) with 3 contents and 18 reagents, and ended up in the hand. A further ingredient used on the closed sandwich was rejected.
- A slice with no ingredients plus a second slice still added the slice as filling, as before.
- A stack of 10 rods plus cheese: 9 rods remained, and a "small cheese wedge kebab" was made. A second ingredient (steak) could be added to that kebab (2 contents). A single rod also worked.
- A crowbar used on rods was unaffected (no balloon alert).
- The runtime log had no runtimes.

Not checked or known limitations:

- Appearance of any result: the sprites were not looked at. The finished sandwich uses a static "sandwich" sprite, without per-ingredient overlays.
- Only plain bread slices start a sandwich; other slice types do not have a holder (they lack the filling icon states). Any bread slice can close it.
- The kebab appeared on the floor and not in the hand when made from a stack.
- Soup (#59312) was not tested; unit tests were not run.
- Everything was done via direct `attackby` calls, not mouse clicks in a client.

## Notes

- The issues' claim that sandwiches should be finished with "another slice of bread (any type)" is implemented as that, but starting a sandwich on a non-plain slice (toast, tofu bread and so on) is still not possible; this was a decision based on missing sprites, not a bug in the fix.
- Because the closing item is merged into the finished food, an unrelated bread slice that happens to be edible cannot be recovered afterwards.
- The kebab holder is added only when `merge_type == /obj/item/stack/rods`, which excludes the cyborg rods and subtypes with their own merge types; the tested subtypes are the ordinary iron rods and stacks of them.
- Soup in #59312 and the "multiple fillings show only one" remark about sandwiches were not investigated further.
