## Summary

The reports say the AI cannot see medical, security or diagnostic HUD images (health bars, shocked-door markers) for things on another z-level. On master, on Nebula Station with the AI core on the lower deck and the camera eye moved to the upper deck, the AI client received the HUD images normally, including for an electrified airlock, and so did a health-scanner user working through an advanced camera console. So the basic case did not reproduce. Looking for why it might, two real defects in the HUD code were found and fixed: (1) `show_to()`, `hide_from()` and `reload_huds()` filed the viewer under the z of its body (the AI core), while the look-z handler files an AI under the z of its eye, so toggling the AI's sensors while the eye was on another deck did the wrong thing; (2) the "basic" medical HUD checked the suit-sensor requirement in only one of four code paths, so after a z change it showed exact health and status for humans whose sensors were off (#93303).

## The bug

What the reports describe:

- #84993: as an AI on a multi-z map (Nebula), medical/security/diagnostic HUDs and shocked-door markers are not visible when the camera is on another z-level. A later comment narrows it to the upper level only, seen on Nebula, the only map where the AI spawns on a lower floor.
- #76042: the shocked-door indicator does not show on other z-levels.
- #93197: the AI cannot see shocked doors or sensors on other floors; a comment says it is only floors above the AI's own.
- #90914: AI and camera consoles show sporadic or no HUD info for mobs on other z-levels, including HUDs vanishing when going back to the original z.
- #93303: the AI sees exact health bars of people whose suit sensors are not set to maximum (even "alive/dead" only). A maintainer's comment says a separate change fixes half of this and the other half comes from HUD handling on z transitions, which also affects ghosts.

What was found is different from the headline symptom, so the two defects are described separately.

## Reproduction

All checks used the Lua editor and other admin tools on a Nebula Station round: the AI core is at z2 (the lower deck of the two station levels) and the camera eye was moved to z3 (upper). The test server's AI was made by creating a human, `AIize()`-ing it and moving it into the core. What the AI client actually held was read from the images list of each test mob and door as seen by the AI's client.

### Headline symptom (#84993, #76042, #93197, #90914): not reproduced

1. Move the AI's eye to z3 (`eyeobj:setLoc`, and also `zMove`). The AI is filed in the HUD user list of z3 for all its HUDs, and its client has the images.
2. A human at z3 showed the health and status images and the ID marker (`hudhealthy`, `hudhealth100`, `hudno_id`).
3. A second human spawned at z3 after the eye had moved up showed `hudno_id`, and the health/status images appeared after it was given a jumpsuit with sensors at the maximum mode.
4. An electrified airlock at z3 showed its `electrified` image to the AI.
5. Logging in again does not lose the images: `mob/login.dm` clears the client's images and the AI login calls `view_core()`, which returns the eye to the core's z.
6. An advanced camera console (`/obj/machinery/computer/camera_advanced`) used by a human wearing health-scanner HUD glasses, with the remote eye on z3, delivered the images for a mob on z3 (`huddead`, `hudhealth-100`).
7. The security camera console is a map view and does not use client images, so no HUD images are expected from it.

The screenshots in #90914 and the "this still happens" comment may reflect a state before a later fix, camera visibility/static rather than HUDs, or something this test did not exercise. This was not determined. No upstream fix was found in the changelogs for 2026-01 to 2026-08 (searched for the terms; this is not a proof that none exists).

### Defect 1 (eye z versus body z): observed through the sensor toggle

1. With the AI core on z2 and the eye on z3, call `toggle_sensors()` to turn the sensors off.
2. On master the AI stays registered in the z3 user list and the z3 images stay on its screen; toggling the sensors back on loads the images of the core's z, not the eye's z.
3. With the fix, turning the sensors off removed all images of the z3 test human, and turning them on restored them (`huddead`, `hudhealth-100`, `hudno_id`).

The defect was first found by reading the code and then confirmed through this toggle. The symptom (stale images after turning the sensors off) is mild and does not match the reports, which describe images never appearing.

### Defect 2 (#93303): reproduced

1. Spawn four humans with suit sensor modes 0, 1, 2 and 3 (40 brute damage each), and an AI with its eye at z2.
2. On the same z, only the human at sensor mode 3 showed `hudhealthy` and the health value. This is correct.
3. Move each human z2 to z3 and back to z2 (`forceMove`). Afterwards all four showed health and status images, including the three with sensors off or low. `TRAIT_BASIC_HEALTH_HUD_VISIBLE` was still only on the sensor-3 human.
4. Humans that never changed z were correct, also after the AI eye changed z (on a map like Meta the eye change returns early with `same_z_layer`).

## Cause

### HUD bookkeeping

`code/datums/hud.dm` files each HUD atom and each viewer by z-level (`hud_atoms[z]`, `hud_users[z]`). A viewer at z sees the atoms at z and at linked lower z-levels (`get_hud_atoms_for_z_level`, line 110; `get_hud_users_for_z_level`, line 135). So an AI whose eye is on the lower deck cannot see upper-deck atoms, which is intentional; when the eye is on the upper deck it sees both. When an AI eye, a remote camera eye or someone looking up changes z, `on_look_z_level_changed()` (line 332) moves the viewer between the lists.

### Defect 1

`on_look_z_level_changed()` files the viewer under the z of the eye. But the other entry points take the z from the body:

- `show_to()` (line 158): `var/turf/their_turf = get_turf(new_viewer)` (line 169).
- `hide_from()` (line 188): `get_turf(former_viewer)` (line 206).
- `show_hud_images_after_cooldown()` (line 450): `get_turf(queued_hud_user)` (line 457).
- `/mob/proc/reload_huds()` (line 465): `get_turf(src)` (line 466).

For an AI (body in the core at z2, eye at z3) these use z2. Removing the AI from a HUD while the eye is up looked in the wrong list and left the AI registered on z3, so the images stayed; adding it again populated the core's z. This is a latent defect: it is not the reported "never see upper HUDs" symptom, and no visible breakage other than the sensors-off case above was seen.

### Defect 2 (#93303)

Humans are atoms of every human data HUD. The basic medical HUD (`code/game/data_huds.dm:20-28`) is supposed to show a human only if `TRAIT_BASIC_HEALTH_HUD_VISIBLE` is set (suit sensors at the right level), and it implements that by overriding one procedure:

```dm
/datum/atom_hud/data/human/medical/basic/add_atom_to_single_mob_hud(mob/requesting_mob, atom/hud_atom)
	if(HAS_TRAIT(hud_atom, TRAIT_BASIC_HEALTH_HUD_VISIBLE))
		return ..()
```

But three other paths in `hud.dm` add images without going through it:

- `add_all_atoms_to_single_mob_hud()` (line 358): used when a viewer changes z (AI eye, looking up), through `on_look_z_level_changed()`.
- `add_atom_to_all_mob_huds()` (line 377): used when an atom changes z.
- `add_single_hud_category_on_atom()`: used when one category of an atom's HUD is refreshed.

So after any z change by the human or the viewer, the sensor check was bypassed and the basic medical HUD showed exact health and status regardless of sensors. This fits the second half of the explanation in the #93303 comments (HUD handling on z transitions).

## Fix

Two commits.

**"Fix issue 84993: data huds follow the viewer's eye z level when shown, hidden or reloaded"** (`code/datums/hud.dm`):

- `show_to()`, `show_hud_images_after_cooldown()` and `reload_huds()` take the z of `get_turf(client?.eye || mob)`, i.e. the z the viewer is looking from, falling back to the body when there is no client.
- `hide_from()` no longer computes a z from the viewer's position. It looks through `hud_users` for the z-list the viewer is actually registered in, removes it there, and removes the images for that z. This works whatever z the eye is on now.

An alternative was to track the registered z per user in a new variable; scanning the lists was simpler and does not add state that could go stale.

**"Fix issue 93303: basic medical HUD bulk updates honor the suit sensor check"** (`code/game/data_huds.dm`): overrides on the basic medical HUD for `add_all_atoms_to_single_mob_hud()` (filters the atoms list by the trait), `add_atom_to_all_mob_huds()` and `add_single_hud_category_on_atom()` (both skip the atom without the trait). The alternative would have been to keep humans out of the basic HUD's atom list unless they have the trait, which would also touch the sensor update code; the overrides keep the check in the HUD that owns it.

## Verification

Same Nebula setup, with the fix built:

- Spawn path: a human at z3 with sensors 0 showed only `hudno_id`; with sensors 3, `hudno_id`, `hudhealthy` and a health value.
- Atom z-change path: a sensors-0 human moved z2 to z3 showed only `hudno_id` (before the fix it showed health and status); a sensors-3 human showed `hudno_id`, `hudhealthy` and its health value.
- Viewer z-change path: eye moved z3, z2, z3 again: the sensors-0 human still showed only `hudno_id`.
- Eye-z registration: with the eye on z3, `toggle_sensors()` off left the sensors-3 human with an empty image list (on master the images stayed); on again showed `huddead`, `hudhealth-100`, `hudno_id` for it.
- Advanced camera console: delivered HUD images for a z3 mob (described under reproduction).
- The runtime log had 21 runtimes, all from the test scripts themselves (a procedure call without a ckey after a ckey swap, `give_eye_control` before `CreateEye`); none in `hud.dm` or `data_huds.dm`.

Not checked: ghost HUDs and "looking up" (mentioned in a maintainer's comment on #93303) other than through the shared code path; a map other than Nebula; the advanced medical and security HUDs through the new code paths beyond what is above; AI disconnect and reconnect while the eye is up beyond the code reading (the login path resets the eye, see above). Only the medical HUD images and the diagnostic (electrified airlock) and ID markers were looked at.

## Notes

- The headline symptom of the four z-level reports did not reproduce. If it still happens in a real round, the missing ingredient is unknown: it may depend on a state not tried here (a particular camera network, sensor mode, AI moved between decks by other means, or a time-limited HUD queue).
- There is a rate limit (`ADD_HUD_TO_COOLDOWN`) on bulk re-adding, which `show_to()` queues; this was not tested for races and could plausibly cause the "sporadic" part of #90914. This is speculation and was not checked.
- A group of related issues, including the canonical #98024 (suit sensor levels not respected), is linked from #93303 in the original triage. The fix for #93303 covers the basic medical HUD's z-change paths only, not other consumers of sensor levels such as the crew monitor.
- The maintainer comment on #93303 says an upstream PR fixes half of this. The branch was cut from master `87b93338`, which includes whatever that PR changed; only the z-transition half remained in this tree.
- Testing note: creating `/mob/living/silicon/ai` directly with the Lua editor gives a broken AI (no laws, runtimes); `AIize()` on a human works. Security turrets near the core attack nearby test humans and were deleted for the test.
