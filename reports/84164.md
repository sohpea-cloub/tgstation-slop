# #84164: Mailed positronic brains can be possessed in transit

| | |
|---|---|
| Issues | [#84164](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/84164): verified, fixed |
| Status | Fixed on the branch and checked in game; whether the behaviour is intended is open (see Notes) |
| Fix branch | [fix/84164](https://github.com/sohpea-cloub/tgstation-slop/tree/fix/84164) |
| Tested on | tgstation master `87b93338`, 2026-10-23 |

## Summary

A positronic brain that arrives as a mail goodie is created inside the mail envelope, which pings ghosts as soon as it is created, and ghosts can take it while it is still sealed in the envelope. The fix skips the creation ping and refuses possession while the brain's location is a mail item. After the envelope is opened, ghosts get no automatic ping but can still click the brain to take it.

## The bug

A ghost sees the "positronic brain created" popup, follows it to a brain inside a piece of mail, possesses it, and the brain behaves normally (the reporter could talk to the mailman) although it is inside an envelope. The reporter filed it with the Oversight label. One commenter replied "It's a feature!"; that is the only reaction, and no maintainer decision is recorded.

## Reproduction

1. Start a round on the test server, observe, and open the Lua editor.
2. Using Lua, spawn a mail envelope (`/obj/item/mail/envelope`), then spawn a positronic brain (`/obj/item/brain_processor/positronic`) with the envelope as its location. This imitates what the mail system does with goodies.
3. As a ghost, open the "positronic brain created" popup's Play link and answer Yes to "Become a positronic brain?".

Observed on master: the ghost alert "positronic brain created ... (Play) (View)" appeared at creation, and after Yes the observer's mob changed to the brain inside the envelope, with the usual "ping" sound. The brain was playable while inside the mail.

The sequence was produced by spawning the brain in the envelope directly, not by running a mail delivery.

## Cause

Mail goodies are created with the envelope as their location: `code/game/objects/items/mail.dm:212` (`new target_good(src)`). The positronic brain's initializer pings ghosts whenever `autoping` is set (`code/modules/mob/living/brain/processor/positronic.dm:35-36`), and `attack_ghost()` (`positronic.dm:104`) only checks whether the brain is occupied and whether the ghost already entered it. Neither looks at what contains the brain.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 84164: Posibrains sealed in mail can no longer be possessed or pinged", in `positronic.dm`:

- A new proc `is_sealed_in_mail()` returns true when the brain's location is an `/obj/item/mail`.
- The creation ping is skipped when sealed in mail.
- `attack_ghost()` refuses with a warning ("... is sealed inside [the envelope] and can't be activated.") when sealed in mail.

The check is on the immediate location, so it works for the case the mail system creates and costs nothing otherwise.

## Verification

After rebuilding the test server and repeating the same script: no ghost alert appeared when the brain was created inside the envelope, and the Play link produced "The positronic brain is sealed inside the envelope and can't be activated." with the observer staying a ghost.

Not checked: the brain after the envelope is opened (by the code, no ping is sent at that point; ghosts must click the brain themselves); an actual mail delivery; possession of a loose, non-mailed brain (that path was not changed).

## Notes

- The commenter's "It's a feature!" may be a joke or a design position. If mailed brains are meant to be takeable, this fix is unwanted; if they are not, the fix is complete for the sealed case.
- A brain that is later unwrapped is not re-announced to ghosts. Pinging when the mail is opened would be a possible follow-up; it was not done.
- Other mail goodies that ghosts can take while sealed were not examined.
