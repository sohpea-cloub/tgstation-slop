## Summary

These three movement issues were examined together. #51110 (injury slowdown in zero gravity) no longer happens on master, because the injury slowdown modifier is ignored while the mob is floating. #91551 is real: a mob wearing active magboots who steps from a floor onto space gets no momentum and is stuck, because `/mob/living/Moved()` computes the drift before it clears the gravity-negation trait. Moving that update ahead of the parent call fixes it, and it was verified in game. #94230 (lying mobs cannot cross z-level map edges) could not be reproduced: lying mobs drifted into every map edge tested and changed z-level.

## #51110: being injured in zero gravity slows you down

### The bug

The 2020 report said that injuries slow a player down even in null gravity, where they remembered moving quickly. A 2024 comment says it still happened.

### Reproduction

Not reproduced. On the test server, using the Lua editor on a human:

1. Spawned a human and dealt 80 brute damage (health 33.3). Its movement slowdown (`cached_multiplicative_slowdown`) was 2.389 and `movement_type` was 1 (ground).
2. Set the area's no-gravity flag on the human's area and called `refresh_gravity()`. The slowdown dropped to 1.5 (the human's base value), `movement_type` became 9 (ground plus floating) and `gravity_state` was 0.
3. In the now weightless area, spawned a second human and applied 80 brute damage, then 90 stamina damage. The slowdown stayed at 1.5 each time.
4. Added 100 oxygen damage. The slowdown rose to 5.5 (+4).

### Cause

There is nothing to fix for the reported case. `code/modules/movespeed/modifiers/mobs.dm:26-28` defines

```dm
/datum/movespeed_modifier/damage_slowdown
	blacklisted_movetypes = FLOATING
	variable = TRUE
```

and `/mob/living/carbon/human/updatehealth()` applies it (`code/modules/mob/living/carbon/human/human.dm:960-966`). A modifier whose `blacklisted_movetypes` matches the mob's `movement_type` is skipped (`code/modules/movespeed/_movespeed_modifier.dm`), and being in no gravity sets the `FLOATING` movement type.

The extra slowdown at 100 oxygen damage comes from a different modifier, `carbon_softcrit` (`code/modules/movespeed/modifiers/mobs.dm:122`, added in `code/modules/mob/living/carbon/carbon.dm:445` when the mob is in soft crit, slowdown `SOFTCRIT_ADD_SLOWDOWN` = 2). It is not blacklisted for floating and appears to be intentional. The +4 observed in step 4 was not broken down further, so it may combine this with other effects; it is not the damage modifier.

### Fix

None needed. Which upstream change added the floating blacklist was not determined.

### Verification and notes

Only the Lua checks above were done. The test used an admin-set no-gravity flag on an area, not a real space or gravity-generator-off scenario, and it checked the computed slowdown value, not the movement speed a player experiences. Stamina damage is covered by the same modifier, since its input is the larger of the health deficiency and the stamina loss (`human.dm:962-964`).

## #91551: space move jank (stepping off a floor onto space with magboots)

### The bug

Per the report: wearing magboots and stepping off plating onto a no-gravity tile does not transfer any velocity, and the player gets stuck. The video attached to the issue was not viewable in the inputs, so the description rests on the issue's text and the reproduction below.

### Reproduction

1. On the test server, in the Lua editor, spawned a human in a station hallway, gave it `/obj/item/clothing/shoes/magboots`, equipped them and activated them (`attack_self`).
2. Changed seven turfs directly east of the human to `/turf/open/space/basic` (`ChangeTurf`).
3. Possessed the human and walked east.

Result on unmodified master: the human moved one step onto the first space tile, then further movement was refused ("blocked") and there was no drift. The mob was stuck. This step was done in an earlier session on the same unit and recorded in the notes; the later session repeated the same setup after the fix.

### Cause

`code/modules/mob/living/living_movement.dm:1-8`, `/mob/living/Moved()`, on master:

```dm
/mob/living/Moved(atom/old_loc, movement_dir, forced, list/old_locs, momentum_change = TRUE)
	. = ..()
	update_turf_movespeed(loc)
	if(HAS_TRAIT(src, TRAIT_NEGATES_GRAVITY))
		if(!isgroundlessturf(loc))
			ADD_TRAIT(src, TRAIT_IGNORING_GRAVITY, IGNORING_GRAVITY_NEGATION)
		else
			REMOVE_TRAIT(src, TRAIT_IGNORING_GRAVITY, IGNORING_GRAVITY_NEGATION)
```

Magboots grant `TRAIT_NEGATES_GRAVITY`, and while the mob is on a solid turf `TRAIT_IGNORING_GRAVITY` (source `IGNORING_GRAVITY_NEGATION`) is set, which makes `mob_negates_gravity()` true (`living_movement.dm:55-56`). `. = ..()` calls `/atom/movable/Moved()`, which starts the drift with `newtonian_move()` (`code/game/atoms_movable.dm:911-912`). `newtonian_move()` returns without drifting if `Process_Spacemove()` returns true (`:1334`), and `Process_Spacemove()` returns true when `has_gravity()` is true (`:1305-1307`).

When the mob steps from a floor onto space, the ignoring-gravity trait from the previous floor is still set at the moment `..()` runs. So the drift is evaluated with gravity "present", and no momentum is given. The trait is removed only afterwards, in the block below `..()`, and the mob is then in space with no drift loop, unable to move by itself. This was derived from reading the code and is consistent with the reproduction, but the intermediate values were not logged.

### Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 91551: update gravity negation before drift is computed on Moved". It moves the `TRAIT_NEGATES_GRAVITY` / `isgroundlessturf()` block above `. = ..()` in `/mob/living/Moved()` and adds a comment saying why the order matters. `update_turf_movespeed(loc)` stays after the parent call. Nothing else changes.

Alternatives were not tried. Updating the trait first is the smallest change, and it keeps the existing logic intact.

### Verification

After rebuilding, with the same setup (human with active magboots, seven space tiles east of a hallway tile), walking east six steps moved the human from x=107 to x=113 (on space), a later call showed it at 115, then 118 (floor again). It crossed all seven space tiles and drifted, a "Weightless" alert appeared, and it was no longer stuck at the first space tile.

Not checked: walking off a floor onto space without magboots (not an affected case), magboots being toggled off or on while on space, moving from space back onto a floor, or other sources of `TRAIT_NEGATES_GRAVITY` besides magboots. The test human died of lack of air and cold during the later checks and took no further part, which does not affect the result.

## #94230: laying down prevents z-level travel (trapping you at map edges)

### The bug

Per the report, a lying or prone mob with momentum toward the map edge does not transition to another z-level and instead ends up against the edge. The report is from client version 516.1659 and its comments add nothing further.

### Reproduction

Not reproduced on master. Using the Lua editor on godmode humans in open space:

- Lying mobs (`body_position` 1, set with `set_body_position(1)`) were pushed with `newtonian_move()` in north, south, north-east, south-west, west and east directions. All of them reached the edge, transferred to another z-level (for example z2 to z6, z7, z9, z10) and kept drifting.
- A mob set lying mid-drift (knockdown plus `set_body_position`) also transferred.
- A resting player-controlled mob at 250,128 on z2, given a push, went from 250,128,2 to 10,128,6.

False leads along the way: several lying mobs appeared to stop short of the edge. In every such case they were next to a station structure (a railing at 230,132, a wall at 220,128, plating at 200,140), which gives `Process_Spacemove()` a backup to push off, so they stopped for reasons unrelated to lying. Also, a resting mob in open space cannot crawl (nothing to push against), the same as a standing one.

### Cause

No defect found. The edge transfer is `turf/open/proc/transfer_occupant()` (`code/game/turfs/open/_open.dm:241-`), called from `Entered()` (`:226-234`). It does not look at body position. Neither do the drift code in `code/datums/drift_handler.dm` or `Process_Spacemove()` (`code/game/atoms_movable.dm:1305`).

One possible explanation was found but not tested: `transfer_occupant()` returns early when `occupant.pulledby` is set (`_open.dm:244`). If the reporter was being dragged while lying, transfer would be refused. This was not tried with a real player pulling or being pulled.

### Fix

None.

### Verification

Not applicable. The reported behavior was probably fixed after the report or depends on a condition that was not found. What was not tested: a real client lying down by key press while drifting, the pull case above, or the exact client version.
