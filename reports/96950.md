## Summary

The stabilized pink extract put its holder in the peace group through the `allies` list, but marked the pacified mobs through the `faction` list. `faction_check_atom()` only compares faction with faction and allies with allies, so the two never matched and hostile mobs kept attacking the holder. The fix uses the faction list for the holder too. The Lazarus injector report could not be reproduced. #78840 describes a ~1 second window caused by the once-per-second tick, and #93868 describes behaviour that appears intended. Neither was changed.

## The bug

### #96950, stabilized pink extract

A player holding a stabilized pink extract is supposed to be left alone by hostile mobs in sight. Instead, mobs "pacified" by the extract bit the holder.

### #96950, Lazarus injector

The report says a mob revived with a Lazarus injector still hunts and kills the reviver. This could not be reproduced (see below).

### #78840

The pink aura is applied on a status-effect tick, so mobs entering view are not pacified at once.

### #93868

The report says mega arachnids keep attacking light sources after being pacified or revived, and do not cloak when spawned through cytology.

## Reproduction

### #96950, stabilized pink extract (reproduced)

1. Observe on the test server, open the Lua editor and create a state.
2. Spawn a human and put a `/obj/item/slimecross/stabilized/pink` in its active hand. Wait a few seconds for the effect to tick.
3. Spawn a space carp about six tiles from the human.
4. The chat showed "The space carp bites <human>!" 11 times and the human dropped to -6 health. Inspecting the human showed `allies` containing its own ref and `pink_<name>`, while the carp's `faction` list did not contain that string.

This was done by spawning the extract and mob with admin tools, not by crafting the extract through xenobiology.

### #96950, Lazarus injector (not reproduced)

1. Killed a space carp and called `lazarus_revive(human, FALSE)` on it with a human standing on its tile. The carp stayed beside the reviver and a second human for 20 seconds without attacking.
2. Repeated with 16 basic mobs (bear, goliath, watcher, basilisk, brimdemon, mook, lobstrosity, raptor, hunter and guard spiders, mega arachnid, leaper, seedling/meanie, migo, creature, ant), each killed and then revived with a human on its tile. No attack lines appeared. (All humans showed 83.3 health from something environmental, not from attacks.)
3. A suspected cause was a mob that retaliates against a past attacker (`target_retaliate` sets `BB_TEMPORARILY_IGNORE_FACTION`). The carp was hit twice, killed and revived. It did not bite over the next 15 seconds, the reviver stayed at 100 health, and the carp had no retaliate list.

The malfunctioning injector was not tested; there the mob is not befriended, as designed.

### #78840 (code read only)

Not run in game. See the Cause section.

### #93868 (partly tested)

A spawned mega arachnid revived with `lazarus_revive` did not attack humans. Its attack on lights was not observed in game. The cytology cloaking claim was not tested.

## Cause

Line numbers are at master.

### #96950, stabilized pink extract

In `code/modules/research/xenobiology/crossbreeding/_status_effects.dm`, `/datum/status_effect/stabilized/pink`:

- `on_apply()` (line 866-868) and `tick()` (872-881) used `owner.add_ally()`, `has_ally()` and `remove_ally()` with the pink faction string, so the string went into the holder's `allies` list.
- `update_nearby_mobs()` gave nearby mobs the same string with `beast.add_faction()` (line 913), so it went into their `faction` list.
- `faction_check()` (`code/game/atoms_movable.dm:1874`) returns `LAZYLEN(faction_A & faction_B) || LAZYLEN(allies_A & allies_B)`. The holder's string was in `allies` and the mob's in `faction`, so neither intersection was non-empty.
- Mobs that left sight were unpacified with `beast.remove_ally()` (line 900) on a string that had been added as a faction, so it never removed anything. `on_remove()` already used `remove_faction`.

### #96950, Lazarus injector

`lazarus_revive()` (`code/modules/mob/living/living.dm:2911`) adds `FACTION_NEUTRAL` and calls `befriend(reviver)`. `befriend()` sends `COMSIG_LIVING_BEFRIENDED`. The test notes say the AI controller's handler (`on_tamed` in the basic controller) clears `BB_CURRENT_TARGET` and the retaliate list, so a reviver is not retaliated against. No defect was found.

### #78840

The effect's `tick()` runs on the default `tick_interval` of 1 second (`code/datums/status_effects/_status_effect.dm:13`). `update_nearby_mobs()` only pacifies mobs in `view(7, get_turf(owner))` (line 886). A mob that comes into range can therefore act for up to about a second before it gets the faction, and hostile mobs often notice targets from further than 7 tiles. This matches the report, but it is how the effect is written, not a regression.

### #93868

The mega arachnid's light attack is its surveillance behaviour (`BB_SURVEILLANCE_TARGET`, `code/modules/mob/living/basic/jungle/mega_arachnid/mega_arachnid_ai.dm:12`, a typecache of cameras and lights, using `ai_interact`). It does not check factions, so pacification and revival do not affect it. This is probably intended.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 96950: stabilized pink extract now puts its holder in the peace faction", in `_status_effects.dm`:

- `on_apply()` and `tick()` now use `add_faction`, `has_faction` and `remove_faction` for the holder instead of the ally procs (the local variable `has_ally` was renamed `has_peace`).
- `update_nearby_mobs()` now unpacifies with `beast.remove_faction()` instead of `remove_ally()`.

The alternative of moving the mobs to the `allies` list was not recorded; the faction list was kept because `on_remove()` and the mob side already used it.

No change was made for #78840, #93868 or the Lazarus injector.

## Verification

After a rebuild, a human holding a pink extract stood on the same tile as a second human, and a fresh carp was spawned 5 tiles away. The carp had the faction `pink_<holder>`. It bit the non-holder 5 times, and the holder stayed at 100 health over 20 seconds. In a first run another human was bitten 6 times and the holder was untouched.

Not checked after the fix:

- Peace breaking (`brokenpeace`) when a pacified mob is damaged, and the holder losing the faction on the next tick.
- Dropping the extract (`on_remove`).
- Unpacifying a mob that leaves sight.

An earlier plan to check these was not carried out in the recorded steps.

## Notes

- #78840: pacifying a wider radius than `view(7)` and unpacifying only beyond it would shrink the window. Any damage to a pacified mob triggers `brokenpeace` on the holder for two minutes, which is intended.
- The Lazarus report may come from a malfunctioning injector or from mobs hostile to others, but this is unverified. The reporter's screenshots were not available.
- #93868: "cytology-spawned mega arachnids don't cloak" was not tested and looks like a separate issue.
