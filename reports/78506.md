# #78506: Blood hitsplatters keep flying through the wall they hit and leave uncleanable splatters inside it

| | |

## Summary

A flying blood hitsplatter that hits a wall is meant to stop and leave one splatter drawn on the wall. Instead its move loop keeps running, it bumps the walls behind the first one, and each bump creates another splatter inside a wall turf, drawn a tile further into the wall than where it actually is. Players see a splatter on the wall but cannot clean it from any tile next to it. The fix makes `Bump` and `post_move` do nothing once the hitsplatter has already hit an endpoint.

## The bug

The reporter saw a blood splatter on a wall and could clean it only from an odd tile, not from any tile next to it or with a view of it. A commenter said splatters "phase through walls", as if travelling a fixed distance whatever is in the way. Another commenter said cleanbots can't clean wall splatters and just spray acid at them.

What is happening: after the first wall hit, extra splatters are created in wall turfs further along the flight path. They are drawn with a 32 pixel offset, so they appear one tile deeper in the wall than the turf they are in. To clean one you have to stand next to the wall turf it is in, not next to where it is drawn.

## Reproduction

On master, using admin tools:

1. Join as a Janitor on a map where the wall is more than one tile thick (a wall row with another wall behind it).
2. With the Lua editor, spawn an `/obj/effect/decal/cleanable/blood/hitsplatter` on a floor tile next to a wall (a tile with a table), and call `fly_towards` on it aimed at the adjacent wall with range 3. This calls the procs directly instead of causing a real wound that sprays blood; the bug is in the hitsplatter's own movement code.
3. Observed: two "pool of blood" `over_window` splatters.
   - One on the floor tile, with `pixel_x` -32. This is correct: it is drawn over the wall.
   - One on the wall turf itself, also with `pixel_x` -32, so it is drawn over the wall turf beyond. This is the "phasing" splatter.
4. Clicking a splatter that appears drawn on the wall from the floor tile next to it cleans the first one but not the second, which is not where it looks to be.

## Cause

In `/obj/effect/decal/cleanable/blood/hitsplatter/Bump` (`code/game/objects/effects/decals/cleanable/blood.dm:892`), the first wall hit sets `hit_endpoint = TRUE`, moves the hitsplatter into the wall with `abstract_move(bumped_atom)`, and creates the final splatter on `prev_loc` with a 32 pixel offset. The move loop keeps running (it only ends on timeout via `loop_done`). The next step bumps the wall behind, and `Bump` has no `hit_endpoint` check, so it runs again: it creates another `over_window` splatter at `prev_loc`, which is now the wall turf, with the offset.

`post_move` (`blood.dm:850`) also has no early return for `hit_endpoint`. Its only check is `if(loc == prev_loc || !isturf(loc))`, so it can also add blood DNA and leave trail decals inside the wall. (The `hit_endpoint` check inside its loop over the turf's contents only covers atoms in that turf, not the trail code after the loop.)

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 78506: blood hitsplatters no longer leave splatters inside the walls they hit":

- `Bump` returns immediately if `hit_endpoint` is set.
- `post_move`'s early-return condition becomes `hit_endpoint || loc == prev_loc || !isturf(loc)`, so a hitsplatter that has landed no longer paints or trails inside the wall while waiting for the move loop to time out.

This keeps the existing flow and adds guards. No alternatives are recorded.

## Verification

After rebuilding, the same setup produced only one splatter, on the floor tile. It was drawn over the wall. Clicking it from the floor tile next to it with a wet mop showed a progress bar and then removed it. No runtimes appeared in the runtime log.

Not checked: windows, diagonal or longer flights, and real bleeding (a wounded mob spraying a hitsplatter) instead of the Lua spawn.

## Notes

- By design, a correct wall splatter lives on the floor tile next to the wall with a 32 pixel offset, and it is not removed by mopping that floor tile (`is_mopped` is FALSE). It has to be clicked directly while standing adjacent to the floor tile. This is unchanged, and may still be confusing to players.
- The reporter's case may include something else: a fire alarm was on the tile and could intercept the click. This was not tested.
- Cleanbot comment: the cleanbot only acid-sprays trash or emagged targets (`cleanbot.dm:332`), and blood subtypes are in `cleanable_blood`. Whether it reaches a wall splatter in game was not tested, so that part of the issue is unconfirmed.
