# #95713: Abductor agent vest leaves disguise overlays on the wearer after stealth ends

| | |
|---|---|
| Issues | [#95713](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/95713): fixed. [#71176](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/71176): not reproduced |
| Status | Fixed (one-line change); verified by overlay counts only, not visually |
| Fix branch | [fix/95713](https://github.com/sohpea-cloub/tgstation-slop/tree/fix/95713) |
| Tested on | tgstation master `87b93338`, 2026-10-23 |

## Summary

When an abductor agent turns off the vest's stealth disguise, every overlay of the disguise that does not coincide with the agent's real body stays on the mob. `DeactivateStealth()` only calls `regenerate_icons()`, which rebuilds the wearer's own overlays but never removes the disguise overlays that `ActivateStealth()` added. The fix calls `wearer.cut_overlays()` before `regenerate_icons()`. #71176 (disguise lost on z-level change) could not be reproduced: the code already reasserts the disguise after a z change.

## The bug

### #95713

A disguised agent looks like the copied person. After undisguising, the real body comes back, but anything from the disguise that is larger than the real body (the issue shows a large-sprite disguise) remains drawn around it.

On the test server, with the disguise copied from a human in a wizard hat and robe, the wearer had 15 extra overlays after deactivation.

### #71176

The report says the disguise is lost when going up or down stairs or ladders, or when dropped to a lower level on Tram. A 2024 comment on the issue says the disguise is not lost, and that the wearer's clothes are drawn over it.

## Reproduction

### #95713

1. Spawn two humans, A and B, on the test server. Give A an abductor agent vest. Dress B in a wizard hat and wizard robe.
2. In the Lua editor (as an observer), build a `/datum/icon_snapshot` from B: copy its name, icon, icon_state and `overlays` (from `get_overlays_copy()`).
3. Call `SetDisguise` on A's vest with that snapshot, then `ActivateStealth()`, then `DeactivateStealth()`, counting `#A.overlays` at each point.

This was done by calling the vest procs directly, not through the vest's action button. The path is the same one the button takes (`ui_action_click()` calls these two procs).

Observed overlay counts on A:

| Point | Overlays |
|---|---|
| Before disguise | 29 |
| Stealth active | 30 |
| After `DeactivateStealth()` | 45 |

15 overlays from the disguise stayed on top of A's own 29 plus one.

### #71176

1. Run the same setup as above with stealth active.
2. Move the wearer to another z-level with `forceMove`.

The wearer kept 30 overlays before and after the move, and `stealth_active` was still 1. The multi-z case the issue describes (stairs, where `same_z_layer` is false) could not be tested, because the test map is single-z.

## Cause

`ActivateStealth()` (`code/modules/antagonists/abductor/equipment/gear/abductor_clothing.dm:86`) sets the wearer's icon and icon_state from the snapshot, then removes all overlays and adds the snapshot's:

```dm
wearer.cut_overlays()
wearer.add_overlay(disguise.overlays)
```

`DeactivateStealth()` (`abductor_clothing.dm:111`) only calls `wearer.regenerate_icons()` (line 121). That rebuilds the human's tracked overlay layers, and each `remove_overlay()` removes only the overlays that this mob had stored for that layer. The disguise overlays were added directly with `add_overlay()` and are not tracked in any layer. Any of them that is not identical to one of the wearer's real overlays therefore stays. Where the two sets coincide (same sprite at the same place), the leftover is invisible. Where the disguise is larger than the body, the excess is visible.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 95713: clear abductor disguise overlays when stealth ends". It adds `wearer.cut_overlays()` immediately before `wearer.regenerate_icons()` in `DeactivateStealth()`, with a comment. This mirrors what `ActivateStealth()` and `reassert_disguise()` already do when applying the disguise. The wearer's overlays are then rebuilt from scratch by `regenerate_icons()`.

No other approach was tried.

## Verification

After the fix, the same Lua procedure gave these counts: 29 before the disguise, 30 with stealth active, 30 after a move to another z-level with stealth active, and 29 after `DeactivateStealth()`. The 15 leftover overlays are gone and the count returns to the starting value.

Not checked: the result was not looked at visually (no render or screenshot), so only counts support the fix. The vest's action button and the other callers of `DeactivateStealth()` (`hit_reaction()`, `IsReflect()`, `flip_mode()`) were not exercised in game. A real large-sprite disguise taken through the abductor console was not tried. Only the wizard-outfit disguise was used.

## Notes

- `cut_overlays()` on the wearer removes every overlay on the mob, including any that are not tracked by the human icon layers (for example overlays another system added directly). `regenerate_icons()` does not re-add those. `ActivateStealth()` and `reassert_disguise()` already have the same property, so this was judged consistent, but a maintainer may prefer to remove only the disguise overlays (for example by remembering what was added).
- #71176: `on_z_changed()` and `reassert_disguise()` (`abductor_clothing.dm:125`, `:131`) already exist on master and reapply the disguise after a z change, through a timer. This matches the 2024 comment that the disguise is not lost. The stairs case with a plane-offset change was not tested. If clothes are drawn over the disguise there, that would be a separate defect from the one fixed here.
