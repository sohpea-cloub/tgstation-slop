## Summary

#92814: after lungs are removed and put back, `failed_last_breath` stays set, so the "CAN'T... BREATHE..." mood stays forever. The lungs remember the gas pressures of their last breath, so on reinsertion `breathe_oxygen` sees a healthy old oxygen pressure and never clears the flag. The fix resets that memory when the lungs are removed.

#93721: Spirit and Ghost species were told by the generic Undead perk that they need no food or air, but they do. The fix gives the species an accurate perk text; their behaviour is unchanged.

#93130: no fix. Plasmamen take toxin damage from energy crossbow bolts and cannot be healed by most medicine for reasons that are consistent with the current design; any change is a balance call.

## #92814: stuck suffocation moodlet after lung surgery

### The bug

A mob whose lungs are removed and then reinserted keeps a negative suffocation mood for the rest of the round, though they breathe normally. The report was about a flyperson, but nothing in this is flyperson-specific.

### Reproduction

1. In a running round, open the Lua editor as an observer.
2. Spawn a human and change their species to `/datum/species/fly`. Their lungs are `/obj/item/organ/lungs/fly`.
3. Remove the lungs (`lungs:Remove(human)`). After a moment the human had `failed_last_breath` = 1, some oxygen loss, and a `suffocation` mood event.
4. Insert the same lungs again (`lungs:Insert(human)`).
5. A few seconds later the lungs were in place, `losebreath` was 0 and oxygen loss had gone back to 0, but `failed_last_breath` was still 1 and the `suffocation` mood event was still there. It was still there when checked again after some further seconds.

This was done by calling the organ procs from Lua, not through an actual surgery. The agents did not test whether it is specific to flypeople; from the code it is not (see Notes).

### Cause

- `code/modules/mob/living/carbon/human/life.dm:91`: while a human has no lungs, `check_breath` sets `failed_last_breath = TRUE`.
- `code/modules/mob/living/carbon/life.dm:56-59`: while `failed_last_breath` is set, `handle_breathing` adds the `suffocation` mood event on every breath.
- `code/modules/surgery/organs/internal/lungs/_lungs.dm:255-256`: `breathe_oxygen` only clears the flag when `old_o2_pp < safe_oxygen_min`, that is, when the previous breath was bad.
- Lungs keep `last_partial_pressures` (`_lungs.dm:31`) when removed. Used lungs therefore still hold the healthy oxygen pressure from before removal, so after reinsertion the condition is never true and the flag is never cleared.

New lungs are not affected, since their `last_partial_pressures` is empty.

### Fix

Commit "Fix issue 92814: reinserted lungs leave a stuck suffocation moodlet": in `on_mob_remove` (`_lungs.dm:172`), after the `on_loss` callbacks have run, `last_partial_pressures` is reset to an empty `alist()`. After reinsertion the first breath compares against a pressure of 0, takes the "was bad before" branch and clears the flag.

This was preferred over changing `breathe_oxygen` to clear the flag every good breath, because stale gas memory on removed lungs also affects other gas reactions and the next owner of the organ.

### Verification

After the fix, with the same Lua steps (flyperson, remove lungs, insert them): `failed_last_breath` was 0 right after `Insert`, oxygen loss was 0, and the only mood event about 10 seconds later was `nutrition`. Not checked: a real surgery on a table, other species, lungs of a different type, or the stasis-bed question raised in the issue.

## #93721: Ghost and Spirit species perk text

### The bug

The Ghost species (and its parent, Spirit) show the Undead perk "The undead do not have the need to eat or breathe", but they suffocate and get hungry.

### Cause

`code/modules/mob/living/carbon/human/_species.dm:1785` (`create_pref_biotypes_perks`) gives that text to every species with `MOB_UNDEAD`. `/datum/species/spirit` (`species_types/ghost.dm`) has its own lungs and stomach, and its physical description says that spirits still eat, breathe, hear and see. Suffocation inside walls while phasing is expected: ghost lungs stay in the body, and wall turfs have no air. Dullahans already override the proc for the same kind of reason.

### Fix

Commit "Fix issue 93721: spirit and ghost species perk claimed they need no food or air": an override of `create_pref_biotypes_perks` on `/datum/species/spirit` (inherited by Ghost), giving an Undead perk that says most viruses can't infect them and that, unlike other undead, they still need to eat and breathe.

### Verification

The change compiled in the build, but the species selection screen was not opened in game.

## #93130: energy crossbow and plasmamen

Not reproduced in game; analysed from the code only.

- Plasmamen have no `TRAIT_TOXIMMUNE` in `inherent_traits` (`species_types/plasmamen.dm`). The energy crossbow bolt (`projectiles/projectile/energy/ebow.dm:5`) deals `TOX` with no biotype check, so it hurts them.
- Most medicine cannot heal them: reagents default to `affected_biotype = MOB_ORGANIC` (`reagents/chemistry/reagents.dm:77`) and `adjust_tox_loss(..., required_biotype = affected_biotype)` returns 0 for non-organic mobs (`damage_procs.dm:348`). Forced healing, such as from medbots, works, which matches the report.
- Giving plasmamen `TRAIT_TOXIMMUNE` was rejected: plasmaman lungs deal their suffocation damage as toxin (`oxy_damage_type = TOX`, `_lungs.dm:960`), so it would make them immune to suffocation.

A real fix is a balance decision (let medicine affect `MOB_MINERAL`, or give plasmamen a separate toxin-healing path), so no change was made. The issue thread already says the same; one commenter expects a wider rework of plasmamen.

## Notes

- #92814 is not flyperson-specific: any mob with previously used lungs that are removed and put back should have the stuck mood.
- The three issues were looked at together because they all concern species/organs; the fixes are independent.
