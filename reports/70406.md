## Summary
#70406 reports that frozen or radioactive overlays stay on a mob after the status or mutation is cured. #92750 reports a telekinesis aura stuck on the head after a species change followed by nullifying the mutation. Neither could be reproduced on master: mutation and status overlays were added and removed correctly across species changes, a real mutation-toxin transformation, the DNA-console nullify path and a 50-iteration stress loop. No fix was made.

## The bug
- **#70406** (2022): after being frozen or irradiated and then cured, the player keeps the overlay (radiation, ice cube). Comments say it also happens with the fire overlay (ash drakes, lava) and with cryogelidia, and that it is very inconsistent; a linked video showed it appearing and later disappearing.
- **#92750** (2025): apply telekinesis, change species with a mutation toxin (lizard, human or slime) and wait for the change, then nullify telekinesis. The head aura reportedly stays, and re-adding and re-nullifying telekinesis does not remove it.

## Reproduction
Not reproduced. Using the Lua editor on a test human, with the mob's overlay list inspected after each step:

1. **#92750 path:** added the telekinesis mutation (sources "activated" and "mutator"), then called `set_species` for lizard, human, jelly slime, moth, skeleton, monkey and ashwalker, then called `dna.remove_mutation`. The `telekinesishead` overlay appeared and was removed each time; the final overlay list was clean.
2. **#70406 freeze:** applied the freon status effect (600, and the `lasting` subtype). The frozen overlay was added, and removed when the status was deleted, also with `set_species`, `update_body` and `regenerate_icons` in between.
3. **Real mutation toxin:** added telekinesis and radioactive through the "mutator" source, then added 30 units of lizard mutation toxin. After about 45 seconds the human turned lizard. The telekinesis and radiation overlays stayed alongside the lizard parts, which is correct since the mutations persist. Then `remove_mutation(type, {"mutator"})` for both (what the DNA console's "nullify" does, `code/game/machinery/computer/dna_console.dm:986-1006`) removed both overlays; the overlay list was clean and no `radioactive_emitter` component was left on the mob.
4. **Stress loop:** 50 iterations of telekinesis plus radioactive with mixed sources, species cycling (including slime) and `update_body` / `regenerate_icons`. It finished with no mutations left and no stuck overlays. (An earlier attempt of this loop printed no result and was not counted.)

## Cause
No defect was found. The paths examined:

- Mutation overlays: `on_acquiring` registers `COMSIG_ATOM_UPDATE_OVERLAYS` and updates the owner's overlays (`code/datums/mutations/_mutations.dm:145-147`); `on_losing` unregisters it and updates again (lines 169-170); `on_update_overlay` supplies the overlay (lines 181-188).
- Managed overlays: the diff logic in `code/game/atom/atom_appearance.dm` (around lines 60-102).
- Freeze: `/datum/status_effect/freon` adds its overlay in `on_apply` and calls `cut_overlay(cube)` in `on_remove` (`code/datums/status_effects/gas.dm:19-27, 46`).

A suspected leak in `code/datums/mutations/radioactive.dm:39` (`QDEL_NULL(radioactivity_source_ref)` on a weakref) was ruled out: `/datum/weakref/Destroy` qdels the referenced target, and the emitter component count was 0 after removal.

## Fix
None. No commit.

## Verification
Not applicable. Not tried: the real fire overlay, ice drakes, cryogelidia, moving between z-levels with status overlays active (`carbon_update_icons.dm` warns that overlays can corrupt there), and the DNA scanner machine UI itself (only the `remove_mutation` call it makes).

## Notes
- The 2022 report may come from an engine-level overlay corruption or a path since changed; this is a guess, not something shown.
- The fire and cryogelidia cases in the comments are the most likely untested route to a stuck overlay.
