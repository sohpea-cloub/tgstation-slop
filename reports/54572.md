# #54572: AI sees radio runechat over speakers it has no camera on

| Status | Verified; fix committed and checked in game |

## Summary

When someone speaks on the radio, an AI gets runechat floating over the speaker's real location, wherever that is on the map. The runechat plane is drawn above the camera static plane, so an AI whose eye is over an area with no camera coverage still sees the text and learns where the speaker is. The fix makes the AI skip that runechat when the speaker is not on camera (or, for a carded AI, not within view range). The chat-log line for the radio message is unchanged.

## The bug

The issue consists of a title and one screenshot, with no further text: "Runechat able to be seen where AI cannot see, providing meta information". The screenshot could not be interpreted beyond the title, so the reproduction below follows the title.

As an AI, a player sees runechat bubbles above radio speakers even when the AI has no camera on that speaker and the area under the AI eye is covered by camera static. This is meta information: the AI can tell that someone is in an unwatched spot, and who it is, from where the bubble appears.

## Reproduction

On master, in a running round (the test server, with an admin observing):

1. Using the Lua editor, spawn a human and call `AIize()` on it, then give the resulting AI to the admin's ckey so the admin controls the AI.
2. Spawn a second human named "Far Speaker" on a floor turf that no camera covers. At the tested map's Z-level 2, turf 38,149 qualified: `SScameras.is_visible_by_cameras(turf)` returned 1 for covered turfs and 0 for this one (the Lua call returns DM's 0/1, and `false` from DM arrives in Lua as 0, which is truthy, so compare against 1).
3. Move the AI eye onto that turf with `eyeobj:setLoc(turf)`.
4. Make the speaker talk on the Common radio: spawn a headset next to the speaker and call `headset:talk_into(speaker, "secret far radio message", nil)` from the Lua editor.

Observed on master: the AI's client received the chat line `[Common] Far Speaker (Unknown) says, "secret far radio message"` and also a floating-text (runechat) element for the speaker's mob carrying "secret far radio message", at the uncovered turf. The runechat element is reported against the mob's `name`, which was still the random name it spawned with, because only `real_name` was set.

A natural case was also seen: while the AI eye was over that spot, a radio message from Poly (the parrot) also produced a runechat element for the AI.

Note that the radio message was produced by calling `talk_into` on a headset, not by a player typing into a radio. The same code path handles any radio message.

## Cause

`/mob/proc/create_chat_message` in `code/datums/chatmessage.dm:327` handles messages whose speaker is a `/atom/movable/virtualspeaker`, which is what radio broadcasts use. At `code/datums/chatmessage.dm:339-340`:

```dm
var/atom/movable/runechat_anchor = LAZYACCESS(virtual_speaker.runechat_anchors, src) // radio runechat
speaker = runechat_anchor || virtual_speaker.source
```

A mob normally has a runechat anchor, such as the radio it hears the message from. When there is none, the message is attached to `virtual_speaker.source`, which is the real speaker at their real location. Nothing checks whether the receiving mob can see that location.

For an AI the check matters because `RUNECHAT_PLANE` is 34 and `CAMERA_STATIC_PLANE` is 31 (`code/__DEFINES/layers.dm:105` and `:115`). Runechat is drawn above the static that hides unseen areas, so the AI eye sees it.

Other AI speech paths already check cameras. `/mob/eye/camera/ai/Hear` at `code/modules/mob/living/silicon/ai/freelook/eye.dm:221-224` only relays speech when `SScameras.is_visible_by_cameras(speaker)` is true. The runechat path above had no equivalent check.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 54572: AI no longer sees runechat over off-camera radio speakers":

- `code/datums/chatmessage.dm`: adds `/mob/proc/can_see_runechat_source(atom/movable/source)`, which returns TRUE by default. In `create_chat_message`, the virtual-speaker branch now returns early when there is no runechat anchor and `can_see_runechat_source(virtual_speaker.source)` is false.
- `code/modules/mob/living/silicon/ai/ai.dm`: the AI override returns `can_see(source)`. The existing `/mob/living/silicon/ai/proc/can_see` (`ai.dm:825`) checks camera visibility for an AI in its core, and distance within the client's view range for a carded or shunted AI.

A mob proc with an AI override was chosen so other mobs keep their behaviour (default TRUE) and the AI's existing visibility definition is reused rather than duplicated. Only runechat without an anchor is affected, so radio runechat that has an anchor, and the chat-log line, are unchanged.

## Verification

After rebuilding and restarting the test server, the same steps were repeated with a fresh AI and speaker:

- Speaker on the uncovered turf 38,149 with the AI eye there: the chat line `[Common] Far Speaker (Unknown) says, "secret far radio message"` arrived, and no floating-text element was received for the speaker.
- Speaker and AI eye moved to a camera-covered turf (38,119, `is_visible_by_cameras` 1): the chat line arrived and the AI client received the floating text for "visible radio message" for the speaker's mob.

Not checked: holopad relay (`relay_speech` called from `code/game/machinery/hologram.dm:586`), emote runechat, other AI runechat paths, a carded or shunted AI, and the surveillance-upgrade eye relay trait. Only the player-visible result was inspected in the AI client's received floating-text elements; no screenshot of the rendered view was compared. No unit test was added or run.

## Notes

- Only the path with no runechat anchor was changed. Holopad and other AI runechat paths were left alone because they are tied to a camera or holopad view; this was not verified in game.
- The AI still receives the text of the radio message in chat, as before. If the intent of the issue is that the AI should not learn who spoke at all, that is a separate decision (radio chat lines show "(Unknown)" and a track link).
- The Lua gotcha above (DM FALSE returns 0, which is truthy in Lua) affects anyone checking `is_visible_by_cameras` from the Lua editor.
