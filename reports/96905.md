# #96905: Two-handed spear damage ignores the tip material when wielded

| | |
|---|---|
| Issues | [#96905](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/96905): verified, fixed |
| Status | Fixed on the branch |
| Fix branch | [fix/96905](https://github.com/sohpea-cloub/tgstation-slop/tree/fix/96905) |

## Summary

A crafted spear's one-handed damage scales with its glass tip (10/12/13/14), but its wielded damage stayed at 18 for every tip. The tip material changes `force` directly in `change_material_strength()` without sending a signal, and the `two_handed` component only scaled its other state's force from signals that tip materials never fire. The fix makes `change_material_strength()` send a new signal with the multipliers it applied, and the component scales both `force_wielded` and `force_unwielded` by them.

## The bug

Spears crafted with plain, plasma, titanium and plastitanium glass shards do 10, 12, 13 and 14 damage one-handed but all do 18 when wielded in both hands, so a better tip gives no benefit in the two-handed mode.

## Reproduction

1. On the test server, as an admin, spawn a human and the spear ingredients: cable cuffs, rods, and glass shards of several types (plain, plasma, titanium, plastitanium).
2. Craft spears through the real crafting recipe (cuffs + shard + rod), which sets the tip material the way a player's crafting does. (An earlier attempt to imitate the crafting with direct material calls in the Lua editor did not reproduce faithfully, so the real recipe was used.)
3. Read the spear's `force` and its `/datum/component/two_handed` values in the Lua editor.
4. Observed on master: a plasma-tip spear held in hand had `force` 12, `force_unwielded` 12, `force_wielded` 18, matching the issue's 10/12/13/14 versus 18/18/18/18.

Only the plasma-tip case was read on unfixed master; the other tips are inferred from the issue's numbers and the code.

## Cause

Crafting sets the tip material through a material slot (`set_material_slot`, `code/game/objects/items/weaponry/melee/spear.dm`). `/datum/material_slot/weapon_head/on_applied` (`code/datums/materials/material_slots/generic.dm:24`) calls `/obj/item/change_material_strength()` (`code/game/objects/items.dm:2064`), which multiplies `force` and `throwforce` in place and sends no signal.

The `two_handed` component (`code/datums/components/twohanded.dm`, `on_materials_updated` and `on_material_apply`/`on_material_remove`, registered at lines 161-164) kept the current state's force in sync at `COMSIG_ATOM_FINALIZE_MATERIAL_EFFECTS` and scaled the opposite state's force from the single-material signals. A slot material fires neither for the strength change, so `force_wielded` was never scaled. The commenter on the issue guessed this area correctly.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 96905: scale wielded force of two-handed items with material strength":

- `code/__DEFINES/dcs/signals/signals_object.dm`: adds `COMSIG_ITEM_MATERIAL_STRENGTH_CHANGED` with arguments `(force_change, throwforce_change)`.
- `code/game/objects/items.dm`: `change_material_strength()` now computes the force and throwforce multipliers (inverted when removing), applies them, and sends the signal.
- `code/datums/components/twohanded.dm`: the four old material handlers and their registrations (including those in `UnregisterFromParent`) are replaced by one `on_material_strength_changed` that multiplies both `force_wielded` and `force_unwielded` by `force_change`.

Hooking the one place that edits `force` means the component sees every strength change, whichever material path caused it.

## Verification

On the fixed build, four spears were crafted through the real recipe and each was wielded and unwielded with the component, reading `force_unwielded` / `force_wielded` / `force` when wielded / `force` when unwielded:

| Tip | unwielded | wielded | `force` wielded | `force` unwielded |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| plasma | 12 | 21.6 | 21.6 | 12 |
| titanium | 13 | 23.4 | 23.4 | 13 |
| plastitanium | 14 | 25.2 | 25.2 | 14 |
| plain glass | 10 | 18 | 18 | 10 |

A spear spawned without crafting stayed at 10 / 18. The runtime log of the new round showed no runtimes.

Not checked: material removal (the inverse path), the interaction with fantasy bonuses, other two-handed items with materials, and damage dealt in an actual attack (values were read from the item).

## Notes

- `change_material_strength()` is used by every item with materials. The change only adds a signal send there, but it is a core proc.
- `/datum/component/transforming` (`code/datums/components/transforming.dm`) uses the same pattern for its alternate-state force and probably has the same bug for slot materials. It was not changed or tested.
- The old handlers also set the current state's force from `source.force` when material effects finalized. That is now covered by the multipliers; items whose force is set through other paths were not examined.
