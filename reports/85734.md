# #85734: Moving over lattices in zero-g produces footsteps

| | |
|---|---|
| Issues | [#85734](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/85734): verified, fixed |
| Status | Fixed (one commit), checked in game |

## Summary

A weightless mob walking over a lattice or catwalk in zero-g still played footstep sounds, because the check that suppresses footsteps for flying and ventcrawling movement did not include the floating movement type that zero-g sets. The fix adds `FLOATING` to that check. Walking while gravity is merely negated (for example with a trait that behaves like magboots) still plays footsteps.

## The bug

The report shows a player moving over lattices in zero-g with a jetpack and hearing footsteps. One commenter noted that code in the footstep element lowers the step rate in no gravity (`steps % 6 != 0 && !source.has_gravity()`) and asked whether the sounds were intentional. That code only makes steps rarer, which is why the sound is intermittent rather than constant. A step still gets through about one in three times.

## Reproduction

On master, as an admin with the Lua editor:

1. Start the round and observe, then open the Lua editor.
2. Spawn a human in a hallway, put lattices on a row of turfs along its path, and put an oxygen jetpack on the human's back. Possess the human.
3. Set the area's flags to include `NO_GRAV` (16384), then call `refresh_gravity()` on the human. Changing the area flag from Lua does not refresh gravity by itself. After this, `has_gravity()` returned 0 and `movement_type` was 9 (`GROUND | FLOATING`).
4. Walk along the lattices and record the sounds sent (the footstep sounds must be included explicitly; they are hidden from the default sound output).

Observed on unmodified master: footsteps (`hardbarefoot*.ogg` at 80% volume) were played, 2 of 7 steps in the hallway. One step over a lattice in vacuum also played one, at 12% volume because vacuum muffles it.

The jetpack was equipped but the notes do not record whether it was switched on during the test. The mob's movement type was `GROUND | FLOATING` from the missing gravity, not from the jetpack; I found no evidence that the jetpack adds a movement type of its own.

## Cause

`SHOULD_ATOM_DISABLE_FOOTSTEPS` (`code/__DEFINES/footsteps.dm:220`) lists the movement types that never make footsteps, and it only contained `VENTCRAWLING | FLYING` (`:224`). A mob in zero-g gets `TRAIT_MOVE_FLOATING` from `update_gravity()` (`code/modules/mob/living/living.dm:1283`), which sets the `FLOATING` movement type, not `FLYING`, so the check let it through.

Lattices add `/datum/element/footstep_override` with `FOOTSTEP_CATWALK` (`code/game/objects/structures/lattice.dm:26`). In `prepare_step()` (`code/datums/elements/footstep.dm:76`), that override makes the turf return footstep data even over space. The existing zero-g test at `:95` only skips steps that are not a multiple of 6, so some steps always got through.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 85734: no footsteps while weightless": `FLOATING` is added to the movement-type mask in `SHOULD_ATOM_DISABLE_FOOTSTEPS`. There is precedent for treating `FLYING | FLOATING` together in `code/datums/status_effects/wound_effects.dm:102`. Changing the shared macro was preferred over special-casing lattices or jetpacks, because any floating mob should be silent.

## Verification

After rebuilding and restarting, the same setup was repeated:

- Zero-g over lattices: 7 steps north, 0 footsteps.
- Control: the `negates_gravity` trait was added to the human (removing the `FLOATING` type, as magboots do), 7 steps south on the same lattices: 3 footsteps (`hardbarefoot3`, `1`, `5`). So walking is still audible when the mob is not floating.

Not checked: other users of the macro that are not mobs. It is also used from `play_simplestep_machine` (`footstep.dm:249`) and in `shoe_footstep.dm:71`, so floating mechs and machines now also skip their steps. This was not tested. Jetpack-on versus jetpack-off was not compared.

## Notes

- The macro change is broader than the issue: anything with `FLOATING` movement type goes silent, not just mobs over lattices. That seems intended, but a maintainer should confirm for mechs.
- The reproduction set the `NO_GRAV` flag by hand; real zero-g areas (for example space near the station) were not used.
