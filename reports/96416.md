## Summary

Food lying on tables on the Luxury escape shuttle became "dirty and not safe to consume" a few seconds after the shuttle was loaded or docked. The germ-sensitivity component checks for an elevated structure (a table) under the food only at the moment the food moves. During a shuttle move the food can reach its new turf before the table does, so the component sees no table and starts its countdown, and it never rechecks when the countdown ends. The fix rechecks the location when the timer fires.

## The bug

As reported: after buying the Luxury shuttle, all the food on it is dirty and makes the player sick. The examine text reads "looks dirty and not safe to consume".

## Reproduction

1. On the test server, start the round and wait for it to run.
2. Open the Shuttle Manipulator (admin tgui), select the template `emergency_luxury` and replace the emergency shuttle with it.
3. With the Lua editor and SDQL2, look at a khachapuri on a fancy table on the shuttle (it was at 222,82,1). Its location was a carpet turf with a table on it.
4. Before the fix, about 12 seconds after loading and docking, its `germ_sensitive` component had `infective = 1` and `timer_id` had been set.

## Cause

`handle_movement()` in `code/datums/components/food/germ_sensitive.dm` runs on `COMSIG_MOVABLE_MOVED`. It looks through the contents of the item's turf for types in `GLOB.typecache_elevated_structures`, and only if none is found does it start a `GERM_EXPOSURE_DELAY` (5 second) timer calling `expose_to_germs()`. `expose_to_germs()` then infected the item without checking the location again.

When a shuttle moves, the food appears to move onto its new turf before the table does, so at the time of the check there is no table there. This ordering is inferred from the code and the before/after results; the move order was not traced. The mapload protection does not help, because a shuttle move is an ordinary movement.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 96416: food on tables is no longer made dirty after a shuttle moves it". In `germ_sensitive.dm`:

- The location checks (open turf, not lava or asteroid, has gravity, no elevated structure) moved out of `handle_movement()` into a new `is_exposed_location()`. `handle_movement()` uses it with the same behaviour as before.
- `expose_to_germs()` now clears `timer_id` and calls `is_exposed_location()` first. If the parent is no longer exposed it sends `COMSIG_ATOM_GERM_UNEXPOSED` and returns instead of infecting.

The recheck at timer time handles any case where the surroundings change after the countdown started, not only shuttles.

## Verification

On the test server:

- Same steps as in the reproduction, after the fix: the khachapuri on the table had `infective = 0`, `timer_id` null, and no infective component.
- Control: a khachapuri spawned from the Lua editor on a bare floor tile of the shuttle still became infective after 5 seconds, so exposure still works where it should.
- No runtimes were seen.

Not checked: other food items beyond the khachapuri, other shuttles, and no unit test was added.

## Notes

The fix is not shuttle-specific: any case where the surroundings change between the start of the countdown and the timer firing is now covered.
