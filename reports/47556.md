# #47556: Runtime when rewrenching a multi-deck pipe adapter next to a pipe whose pipenet is still waiting for a rebuild

## Summary

Unwrenching a multi-deck pipe adapter destroys the pipenet(s) it belonged to and puts every pipe of them into `SSair.rebuild_queue`; until the subsystem gets to a pipe, that pipe has `parent == null`. If a device is wrenched down next to such a pipe in that window, `on_construction()` calls `A.add_member(src)` on the neighbour, and `pipe/add_member()` dereferences the null `parent`, giving `Cannot execute null.add_member()`, the runtime quoted in the issue. The fix makes `pipe/add_member()` queue both devices for a rebuild when the pipe's pipenet is gone, and makes the pipe air procs and `wrench_act()` tolerate a missing pipenet. The old report's other runtimes (`null.return_pressure()`, `null.air`) were not reproduced on master.

## The bug

The issue (opened 2019) says that when a multi-deck pipe adapter is unwrenched or destroyed it "breaks" the pipes and adapters connected to it: their air disappears, they cannot be unwrenched, and after reconnecting, the log fills with `Cannot execute null.addMember()` (`datum_pipeline.dm`), `Cannot execute null.return pressure()` (`atmosmachinery.dm`) and `Cannot read null.air` (`pipes.dm`).

Atmospherics has been reworked since 2019 (pipenets are now rebuilt lazily through `SSair.rebuild_queue`), and on current master the damage is not permanent. The pipes lose their pipenet only until the air subsystem rebuilds them. During that window a newly wrenched device next to such a pipe runtimes. On a large network the window is not short: on the test map the supply network held about 3000 queued machines after a single unwrench, and the queue drained at roughly 250 entries per 30 seconds in one of the observations.

## Reproduction

Tested on IceBoxStation, which has a pair of supply multi-deck adapters on layer 4 stacked on z 4 (upper) and z 3 (lower) at x=123, y=90, connected to the large supply network (a 4-way manifold at 122,90,4 and a pipe at 124,90,3). The admin tools used were the Lua editor, from an observer, calling the same procs the player action would run.

1. Start a round on IceBoxStation and open the Lua editor.
2. In the same Lua call (so the air subsystem cannot rebuild in between), call `deconstruct(TRUE)` on the upper adapter and on the lower adapter. This is what `wrench_act()` does when a player unwrenches them. Each leaves a pipe-fitting item on its turf. The supply pipenet is destroyed and about 3000 machines go into `SSair.rebuild_queue`; the neighbouring manifold's `parent` is now null.
3. Still in the same call, call `wrench_act()` with a wrench on both pipe-fitting items, which re-places the adapters.
4. Read the runtime log.

Observed on master: the log has `Cannot execute null.add member()` in proc `/obj/machinery/atmospherics/pipe/add_member` at `datum_pipeline.dm:194`, with `src` the supply 4-way manifold whose `parent` was null, called from `/obj/machinery/atmospherics/on_construction()` (`atmosmachinery.dm:553`) via the pipe item's `wrench_act`. It appeared twice, once per rewrench. This is the first runtime in the issue.

Notes on what was and was not seen:

- A plain unwrench followed by a rewrench of the upper adapter alone, done after the rebuild queue had drained, produced no runtime and the pipenets rejoined. An earlier attempt (unwrench the upper adapter, wait, rewrench it while the queue was still draining) also produced no runtime in the log. The inputs do not say why that case was clean (presumably the neighbour it touched had already been rebuilt, or the timing differed), so the exact conditions for the runtime are only established for the same-call sequence above.
- The reproduction calls the procs from the Lua editor rather than going through a player wrenching with a tool; both go through `deconstruct()` and the pipe item's `wrench_act`, but a hand-played reproduction was not attempted.
- The second and third runtimes in the issue (`return_pressure()` on null, `null.air`) were not observed before the fix.

## Cause

When a pipe is disconnected from a neighbour, `disconnect()` destroys the removed machine's pipenet (`atmosmachinery.dm:407-411`, `P.destroy_network()`). `/datum/pipeline/Destroy()` (`datum_pipeline.dm:32-44`) sets each member's `parent` to null and calls `SSair.add_to_rebuild_queue()` on it. `process_rebuilds()` (`air.dm:405`) then takes queued machines from the end of the list and rebuilds their pipenets over later ticks, so for a big network many pipes have no `parent` for some time.

When a device is wrenched down, `on_construction()` (`atmosmachinery.dm:544-554`) asks each neighbour to register it:

```dm
var/list/nodes = pipeline_expansion()
for(var/obj/machinery/atmospherics/A in nodes)
	A.atmos_init()
	A.add_member(src)
```

For a pipe neighbour, `add_member()` is:

```dm
/obj/machinery/atmospherics/pipe/add_member(obj/machinery/atmospherics/considered_device)
	parent.add_member(considered_device, src)   // datum_pipeline.dm:193-194
```

with no check on `parent`. For a neighbour still waiting in the queue, `parent` is null and this is the runtime.

Related unguarded reads of the same null `parent`: `pipe/return_air()`, `return_analyzable_air()` and `remove_air()` return or use `parent.air` (`pipes/pipes.dm:80`, `:85`, `:90`), and `wrench_act()` calls `int_air.return_pressure()` on the result of `return_air()` (`atmosmachinery.dm:429-434`). These can only fail when the pipe has no `air_temporary` either; `Destroy()` stores one on each member only if the destroyed pipenet had volume (`datum_pipeline.dm:35-36`, `:207-211`). That reading of the code is why they were left as defensive guards; no runtime from them was reproduced.

The multi-deck adapter makes this easy to hit because its `pipeline_expansion()` (`pipes/multiz.dm`) links itself directly to the adapters above and below, so unwrenching one adapter tears down the networks on both decks, and rewrenching it touches pipes on several decks.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 47556: no runtime when rewrenching next to a pipe whose pipenet is awaiting rebuild":

- `datum_pipeline.dm`, `pipe/add_member()`: if `QDELETED(parent)`, add both the pipe and the new device to `SSair.rebuild_queue` and return, instead of dereferencing `parent`. The rebuild expands over the real node links, so it picks up the new device without needing the explicit registration. (`QDELETED(null)` is true, so a null `parent` is covered. Already-queued machines are not queued twice because `add_to_rebuild_queue()` checks `rebuilding`.)
- `pipes/pipes.dm`: `return_air()` and `return_analyzable_air()` use `parent?.air`, `remove_air()` uses `parent?.air?.remove(amount)`, so a pipe with no pipenet returns null air instead of runtiming.
- `atmosmachinery.dm`, `wrench_act()`: `int_air = return_air() || new /datum/gas_mixture`, so unwrenching such a pipe works with an empty mixture instead of runtiming on `return_pressure()`.

The alternative of calling `get_rebuild_target()` on the spot to give the pipe a pipenet immediately was not tried; queueing reuses the existing rebuild path.

## Verification

After rebuilding with the change and restarting the test server (IceBoxStation, same adapters), the same-call sequence from the reproduction (deconstruct both adapters, wrench both items back) was repeated:

- No runtime in the runtime log (it previously showed two).
- Both adapters relinked: the lower adapter's `nodes[2]` was the new upper adapter and the upper's `nodes[3]` the new lower adapter.
- Read while the queue still held 3074 entries, the lower adapter, the manifold, the pipe at 124,90,3 and the upper adapter all reported the same pipenet (volume 83875 at that moment).
- Deconstructing the upper adapter and then unwrenching the manifold next to it (whose `parent` was null at that point) also worked: "You unfasten the blue air supply pipe", no runtime.

Not checked:

- The queue was not allowed to drain fully after the fix; the pipenet volume was still growing when read, so the final merged network (volume, gas contents, that nothing is orphaned) was not compared with the original.
- Nothing was done by a player with a real wrench and hands; all actions went through the Lua editor.
- The `null.air` / `return_pressure()` guards were never reached in testing, so they are unverified beyond not causing new runtimes.
- Only the supply network on one map was tried; other layers, other adapter types, destroyed (rather than unwrenched) adapters and mapped (rather than player-built) adapters were not.

## Notes

- The "never rebuilt, can't be unwrenched" symptom in the report was not seen on master; unwrench/rewrench after the queue drained was clean. The reported symptoms were probably a combination of the older, non-queued rebuild code and the same null-parent window.
- Upstream PR #79924 (closed, not merged) touched similar code (its title is about atmospherics components' air and unsafe pressure releases). It was not compared with this change.
- The patch silently tolerates a missing pipenet in `return_air()`: callers that previously crashed now get null and must cope. Only `wrench_act()` was adapted here; other callers of `return_air()` on pipes were not audited.
- `new /datum/gas_mixture` in `wrench_act()` means unwrenching a pipe that has no pipenet yet is treated as an empty pipe, so any gas it should have released is not released (its gas may still be held by the pending rebuild or stored on `air_temporary`). That seems acceptable for a transient state, but a maintainer may prefer to defer or block the unwrench until the pipe is rebuilt.
