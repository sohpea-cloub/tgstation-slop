## Summary

The issue reported that after the embedding rework (#88511) thrown spears, combat knives and throwing stars no longer embedded, that jagged rods fired from the rebar crossbow sometimes vanished, and that instant summons lost its pairing with an embedded jagged rod. On master the thrown-embed regression is gone: stars, knives and sticky/pointy tape embedded in every test. The investigation believes this is the fix from PR #88893, which makes `get_embed()` run before impact; that PR number was taken from the investigation's notes and was not checked against history here. The rebar crossbow, instant summons and "picking up an unembedded item drops it" parts were not tested. A freshly spawned spear still never embedded when thrown, because of a low `throw_speed`; this was not changed.

## The bug

From the issue and its comments:

- Spears, combat knives, throwing stars and (per a comment) sticky tape and super sticky tape could not be embedded by throwing them at a human. A comment says the "pointy" tape versions did embed.
- Jagged rods from the syndicate rebar crossbow embed but sometimes vanish. SmArtKar says the element that spawns the rod when a projectile hits something creates a new one, and that this jank predates the rework.
- Instant summons forgets its paired rod once the rod embeds.
- SmArtKar's diagnosis of the thrown-item part: (A) `embed_data` is not initialized on throw impact, and (B) an unembedded item is quickly dropped to the ground when picked up.

## Reproduction

On master, using the Lua editor as an admin on a running round:

1. Spawn a target human and a thrower human (also tried with a ghost as the thrower), with the target made unable to die.
2. Give the thrower the items to test and call `throw_at(target, 7, 4, thrower)` on each, several times.
3. Read the chat for the result.

Observed:

- Throwing star: "The throwing star embeds itself in [target]'s chest!" every time, for every thrower. A fresh star embedded on its first throw, before anything had called `get_embed()` on it.
- Combat knife: embedded 5 of 5 throws.
- Super sticky tape (a pocket crowbar given the `/datum/embedding/sticky_tape/super` embedding): "The pocket crowbar sticks itself to [target]'s left arm!". Super pointy tape: "embeds itself in [target]'s chest!" 5 of 5.
- Spear: "[target] is hit by the spear!" 6 of 6 throws, never embedded (see Cause).
- Plain rods did not embed either; the investigation notes this as expected, since they have `throw_speed` 3.

One thing to know about the method: the first Lua detector counted items whose `loc` was a body part, which gave "0/N" because an embedded item's `loc` is the victim mob (`embed_into` moves it into the owner). The chat lines are the evidence, not those counts.

## Cause

The part of the issue that was reproducible on the report's version is no longer present:

- `item.pre_impact` now calls `get_embed()` before the item hits its target ("Ensure that embedding is lazyloaded before we impact the target", `code/game/objects/items.dm:864`). This matches SmArtKar's point (A), "embed_data is not initialized upon throw impact", which the investigation took to be fixed by #88893.

Spears are a different matter. A spear's base `throw_speed` is 4 (`code/game/objects/items/weaponry/melee/spear.dm:18`), and embedding on throw needs a speed of at least `EMBED_THROWSPEED_THRESHOLD`, defined as 4 (`code/__DEFINES/combat.dm:199`), unless the embedding sets `ignore_throwspeed_threshold` (the check is at `code/datums/embedding.dm:119`). The spear's handle material slot changes the stats when applied:

```dm
target.throw_range += (hardness - 4) - (density - 4) * 2
target.throw_speed += floor((hardness - 4) / 2) - (density - 4) * 2
```
(`/datum/material_slot/handle/spear/on_applied`, `code/game/objects/items/weaponry/melee/spear.dm:221`)

With the iron handle a freshly spawned spear read `throw_speed` 1 in the Lua editor (a throwing star read 4), so thrown spears fall under the threshold and never embed. This looks like a side effect of the material rework rather than the original issue, but whether it is intended was not determined.

## Fix

None. No code was changed.

## Verification

Checked on the test server: the throws listed under Reproduction, on a master build that already contains the `get_embed()` call in `pre_impact`.

Not checked:

- The rebar crossbow and jagged rods vanishing. An attempt to fire the rod casing with `fire_casing` without a gun returned 0 and left a jagged iron rod on the floor, so it proves nothing.
- Instant summons and whether it keeps its pairing with an embedded rod.
- SmArtKar's point (B): picking up an unembedded item and having it drop.
- Whether the spear's `throw_speed` 1 is intended, and whether thrown spears embedded at any point after the rework.
- Atlas spine implants (step 4 of the report).

## Notes

- Possible follow-up: thrown spears do not embed on a normal iron-handled spear. If that is not intended, the handle material formula in `/datum/material_slot/handle/spear/on_applied` (iron gives a net -3 to `throw_speed`) is the place to look; setting `ignore_throwspeed_threshold` on `/datum/embedding/spear` would be another option. Neither was tried.
- The comment that sticky tape and super sticky tape did not embed was not reproducible on master; both stuck/embedded.
- The jagged rod vanishing and the instant summons pairing are described by SmArtKar as older behavior of how the rod is respawned on hit; they may need their own issue if they still happen.
