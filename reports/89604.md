## Summary

Four open issues report that a mob thrown, launched or grappled into lava, liquid plasma or water keeps the liquid ("immerse") overlay after leaving it, or (#89784) that a rider thrown over lava while buckled to a raptor is burned. None of this reproduced on master `87b93338`: in every variant tried, the overlay was removed when the mob walked out of the liquid, and the thrown raptor rider took no lava damage. The immerse element on master already renders its overlay with an alpha-mask filter; the issues were filed in February-May 2025, and the most likely explanation is that a later refactor of the immerse element fixed them (the investigation believed this was PR #93038, merged 2025-09-18, and could not confirm that from the repository it had). No fix was written.

## The bug

As reported:

- **#89604** (grapple hook) and **#89498** (jump boots into the edge of a lava river): after landing in lava or plasma by being thrown rather than walking in, the character keeps the half-covered "in the liquid" look permanently. #89498 also says no damage was taken.
- **#91406** (jump boots and water or other liquid tiles): the character becomes "one with the liquid" after jumping in and out. A commenter said the same happens with lava and with anything that launches a player over a fluid tile and lands them in it.
- **#89784**: a player riding a tamed raptor (which is immune to lava while ridden) was launched over lava by a bluespace capsule expanding. They reported lava damage and burning as if they had walked on it, though they never unbuckled, and a leftover lava overlay. The reporter described the overlay as covering things near them, or as a large square a little below them. The reporter also mentioned a hair rendering problem afterwards, which was not looked into.

## Reproduction

None of the following reproduced the bug. All of it was done on the test server with admin tools (the Lua editor for spawning and reading state, and normal movement and item use for the actual actions). In each case the check was whether the mob's filter list (`filter_data`) and its `vis_contents` returned to the clean state after leaving the liquid. A mob standing in lava showed one filter (`immerse_mask`) and an extra `vis_contents` entry for the mask. A clean mob after leaving had zero filters and its two normal `vis_contents` entries.

Setup for the lava tests: a row of tiles in a hallway turned into `/turf/open/lava/smooth` (or later water or `/turf/open/lava/plasma`), a human spawned and possessed, with godmode so lava would not kill it.

**#89604 and #89498 (lava)**

1. Equipped jump boots (`/obj/item/clothing/shoes/bhop`), activated them from a distance so the jump landed in the lava, then walked out: overlay removed.
2. Same, but a wall blocked the jump so that the mob stopped inside the lava: overlay removed after walking out.
3. Used a real grapple gun (`/obj/item/grapple_gun`) at a closet placed on the far side so the user landed in the lava; walked out: overlay removed. (The grapple gun target has to be a dense object, not the lava tile itself.)
4. Started already inside the lava and jumped out over the far side with jump boots: overlay removed, mob clean.

**#91406 (water, liquid plasma)**

5. Replaced the tiles with `/turf/open/water`, jumped in with jump boots (the overlay was added on landing), and also threw the mob into the water with `throw_at`; moved out: filters 0, no mask in `vis_contents`.
6. Replaced the tiles with `/turf/open/lava/plasma` and threw the mob in; moved out: filters 0, no mask. (A particle holder stayed in `vis_contents` afterwards, from fire particles of an unrelated burning corpse.)

**#89784 (raptor and bluespace capsule)**

7. Placed lava on x=109..116 of the hallway row. Spawned a human and a `/mob/living/basic/raptor`, buckled the human to the raptor, placed a survival capsule behind them and called its `get_template()` and `yote_nearby()` so both were thrown across the lava.
8. Result: the human and raptor landed in the lava still buckled. After 15 s standing in lava the human had health 100 and `fire_stacks` 0. The immerse overlay was present while in the lava, as expected, and was gone after leaving it.
9. Forcibly unbuckling the rider in the lava (via `forceMove`) did burn her. That is expected because `forceMove` of the mob out of the mount unbuckles it, and is not the reported behaviour.

Caveats on the method: the raptor rider's riding minigame logged a `null.images` runtime because no client owned the rider (a test artifact). Throws were done from script and bluespace capsule code was called directly rather than using a capsule in a player's pocket. Several scripted throws were followed by drift and collisions with corpses left in the row, which made some readings unreliable; the readings listed above were taken from fresh state after a real move out of the liquid.

## Cause

No defect was found, so there is no cause for the reported behaviour on master. For context, the relevant code on master is:

- `code/datums/elements/immerse.dm`: `add_immerse_overlay` (line 245) adds an `immerse_mask` alpha-mask filter to the mob; `remove_immerse_overlay` (line 276) removes it. `try_immerse` (line 111) and `try_unimmerse` (line 124) only add or remove the overlay when the mob (or what it is buckled to) is not floating or flying and is not mid-throw. `on_atom_exited` (line 180) calls `remove_from_element` (line 189) when the mob leaves the element's tiles.
- Jump boots add `TRAIT_MOVE_FLOATING` for the length of the throw, and `code/controllers/subsystem/throwing.dm` clears `throwing`, runs the callback (which removes the trait), and then sends `COMSIG_MOVABLE_THROW_LANDED`.

A paper trace of this code looked for a path where the overlay would be missed. The suspicion was that `try_unimmerse` skips `remove_immerse_overlay` while the mob is floating or throwing but still unregisters the signals that would add or remove it later. That case did not show up in the in-game tests above, and the in-game results above are what the verdict rests on.

## Fix

None. No code was changed and nothing was committed.

## Verification

Verified only in the sense described under Reproduction: the bug was looked for and not found in each variant. Not tested:

- Cancelling a grapple mid-flight.
- Jumping while lying down or knocked down.
- A mob already standing in lava being thrown or grappled out and back in.
- Anything on a live server, with real players, with latency, or on the older code the reporters used.
- The hair rendering problem mentioned at the end of #89784.
- Whether the real item (a capsule activated in a player's pocket) behaves differently from calling the capsule procs directly.

## Notes

- **Likely explanation.** The reports date from before the immerse refactor to alpha filters. The investigation identified PR #93038 (merged 2025-09-18) as that change, and also saw later immerse runtime fixes (#93229, #97046) in the PR history. The PR numbers and dates came from the investigation's reading of PR metadata, not from anything inspected in the repository, so treat them as unconfirmed. The four issues are all still open upstream and are probably stale.
- **Possible separate bug, paper only.** In `code/modules/mining/equipment/grapple_gun.dm`, `post_land` removes `traits_on_zipline` (floating and immobilized) from the user only if `zipliner` still resolves. `cancel_hook` (line 158) sets `zipliner = null`, and it is called from `determine_distance` (line 103) when the user gets farther from the zipline target than its maximum distance. If that happens before the throw lands, the traits seem to be left on the user. This was not tested in game.
- **Relation between the issues.** Maintainer comments on #89604 and #91406 treat all of them as one known problem (anything that launches a mob over liquid tiles). #89784's damage claim was a separate question and did not reproduce in this test.
- **Suggested action.** Ask the reporters, or anyone with a current-master round, whether the issues still occur; if not, close them against the immerse refactor.
