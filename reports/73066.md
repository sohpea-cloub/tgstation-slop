## Summary

`is_buckle_possible` refuses to buckle a mob that already has riders, but not to buckle something onto a mount that is itself buckled. In a stack such as human on cow on cow, the top rider's moves are relayed to the bottom mob, which moves; the top rider then fails to follow because the dense bottom mob blocks its tile. The rider stays behind while the stack moves. The fix refuses to buckle anything to a mob that is itself buckled, as the reporter of #73066 suggested.

## The bug

#73066: with a tamed cow riding another tamed cow and a player riding the top cow, movement keys behave strangely. Up and down teleport the cow, left and right put it on either side of the player, and diagonals move the cow stack but not the player.

#76835: a player buckled to a mulebot that is itself riding a scooter is moved in the same way. When the mulebot places them down or throws them, they appear at the destination. The title calls this a teleport on unbuckle.

## Reproduction

The nested buckle was set up with the Lua editor calling `buckle_mob` directly, not by dragging mobs in game. The stack's movement was driven by calling `Move` on the top cow, which is what the rider's movement does.

1. On the test server, spawn a human and two cows (bottom cow B, top cow T).
2. Lua: `B:buckle_mob(T, true)` returned success. Then `T:buckle_mob(human, true)` returned success, so the human was buckled to T and T to B.
3. Call `T:Move(south)`.

Observed on master: the call returned `move 2 0 104,139 | 104,138 | 104,138`. The human stayed at 104,139 while both cows moved to 104,138. Further moves left the human further behind (103,138). The human stays buckled and cannot walk away. A first run gave the same kind of result: human at 107,144, cows at 108,144 and 108,145 after one move attempt.

The #76835 setup (mulebot buckled to a scooter with a mob buckled to the mulebot) was not run before the fix. It has the same structure: a mob on a mount that is itself buckled.

## Cause

`is_buckle_possible` (`code/game/objects/buckling.dm:263`) refuses when the target already has riders:

```dm
// Stacking buckling leads to lots of jank and issues, better to just nix it entirely
if(target.has_buckled_mobs())
    return FALSE
```

It does not check whether the mount (`src`) is itself buckled, so the stack in the issue can be built the other way round.

Movement then goes wrong in three steps:

1. `Move` on the top rider is relayed to its mount (`code/modules/mob/living/living.dm:1052`, `buckled.Move(...)`).
2. The mount has its own rider, so `handle_buckled_mob_movement` (`code/game/atoms_movable.dm:1476`) moves the riders along with it.
3. The top rider's move into the new tile is blocked by the dense bottom mob. `CanPass` in `code/modules/mob/living/living_movement.dm` lets only a mover in that mob's own `buckled_mobs` through, and the top rider is not in the bottom mob's list. The rider stays where it was while the stack goes on.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 73066: Can't buckle to a mob that is itself buckled", adds a second check to `is_buckle_possible`, after the existing one:

```dm
if(ismob(src))
    var/mob/src_mob = src
    if(src_mob.buckled)
        return FALSE
```

This was chosen over making movement work through the stack, since the issue itself proposes blocking it and the code already refuses the stack in the other order. A second consequence is that a mob cannot be buckled to a mulebot that is riding a scooter, which covers #76835.

## Verification

After the fix, on a rebuilt test server (Lua editor):

- A cow can still be buckled onto a cow (the first buckle returned success).
- A human buckled onto the top cow was refused (returned 0 and the human's `buckled` stayed null).
- A mulebot buckled to a scooter still worked (returned 1), but buckling a human to that mulebot returned 0 (refused).
- A single ride, a human on one cow, still buckled. The follow-up `Move` returned 0 and both stayed at 107,137. The target tile was probably blocked, so this does not show that single riding moves correctly. It shows only that the buckle is unaffected.
- No new runtimes were seen in game.

Not checked: the actual drag-and-drop interaction by a player, the #76835 throw and place-down teleport before or after the fix, and a sentient bottom mob.

## Notes

- The new rule also blocks piggyback and fireman carry onto a mob that is buckled to a chair or similar. This follows from the symmetry with the existing rule and was seen when testing. A maintainer may want to weigh it.
- Movement through nested mounts (the "piggyback a player on a borg" case the reporter mentions) is not made to work; it is refused.
- Anything already in a nested stack, for example one created by spawning or by an admin with force, is not repaired.
