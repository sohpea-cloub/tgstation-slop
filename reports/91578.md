## Summary

Crafting a crazy hamburger through the crafting menu consumed every ingredient in every variant that was tried on master, so the bug in #91578 and its duplicate #92095 could not be reproduced. While checking the recipe, a different bug turned up in the same code: when the 15 units of oil the recipe asks for come from a reagent that is a subtype of oil (for example olive oil, sold as "quality oil"), the crafting menu counted it as valid but never took it, leaving the oil container full and logging a runtime. The fix makes crafting take every contained reagent that is a subtype of the required one.

## The bug

### Reported

Both issues say that crafting a crazy hamburger produces the burger but leaves the ingredients in place, so the burger can be made repeatedly. #91578 gives the steps "collect ingredients (possibly need twice the necessary amount), craft from the cooking menu with either button, continue doing so" and was filed with test merge #91290 active. Its only comment is an image. #92095's reproduction steps are not usable (a joke list of ingredients), and its author later noted it duplicates #91578.

### Found while testing

With only olive oil available as the oil ingredient, the recipe appeared craftable and produced a burger, but the olive oil was never consumed and a runtime was logged. The rest of the ingredients were consumed normally. This is probably not what the reporters saw (they say the ingredients as a whole are kept), but it is the one "reagent ingredient not consumed" path found in this recipe.

## Reproduction

### Reported symptom (does not reproduce)

1. On the test server, as an observer, using the Lua editor, spawn a human and give it to the player.
2. Spawn on the human's tile the crazy hamburger ingredients (`code/modules/food_and_drinks/recipes/tablecraft/recipes_burger.dm:381-393`): 2 plain patties, 1 bun, 2 cheese wedges, 1 chili, 1 cabbage, 1 green crayon, 1 flare, and 15 units of oil (a bottle of oil, or a cooking oil condiment bottle).
3. Open the crafting menu, switch to the cooking tab, and craft "Crazy hamburger" (the `make` action; `make_mass` for several).
4. Count the items left on the tile with the Lua editor.

Result in every variant: all ingredients were consumed, the oil dropped by 15 units, and no runtime was logged. The variants run were:

- exactly one set of ingredients, then two sets, then three sets with the mass-craft button (chat said "You made 3 items")
- oil from a glass bottle with 30 units, from a cooking oil bottle with 50 units, and with two oil bottles on the tile
- chili and cabbage grown from real seeds, a lit flare, and the crayon in the crafter's hand
- ingredients held in the crafter's hand

The ingredients were spawned directly, not cooked or grown in a real round. Cooked patties or cheese from the kitchen machines, for example, were not tried.

### Olive oil case (reproduces)

1. Same setup as above, but the only oil on the tile is a bottle of olive oil with 50 units.
2. Craft the crazy hamburger.
3. The burger was crafted, but the olive oil bottle still held 50 units, and the round's runtime log had `couldn't fulfill the required amount for /datum/reagent/consumable/nutriment/fat/oil. Dangit` (a `stack_trace()`). The log held two such entries, from the two olive oil crafts run.

## Cause

### Reported symptom

The crafting code path for ingredient removal is straightforward: `construct_item()` (`code/datums/components/crafting/_crafting.dm:241`) calls `get_used_reqs()` (`_crafting.dm:418`) to collect the ingredients, creates the result, and deletes everything that was not stored inside the result (`_crafting.dm:329-331`). A burger could be produced with the ingredients left behind only if something runtimed between creating the result and that deletion loop (for example in `on_craft_completion()` or `set_custom_materials()`). No such runtime occurred in any attempt, so the original cause is not identified. It may have been fixed by a later change, or it may need a particular real-round ingredient state that was not recreated. The images attached to the issues did not give more information.

### Olive oil

`get_surroundings()` counts each reagent in a nearby container under its exact type (`_crafting.dm:197-200`), and `check_contents()` accepts any content whose type is a subtype of the required path (`ispath(content_item_path, requirement_path)`, `_crafting.dm:108`). So olive oil (`/datum/reagent/consumable/nutriment/fat/oil/olive`, a subtype of the required `/datum/reagent/consumable/nutriment/fat/oil`) counts toward the 15 units.

`get_used_reqs()` then took the reagent with strict type matching: `get_reagent_amount(path_key)` and `trans_to(..., target_id = path_key)` (`_crafting.dm:448-451`) only see the exact required type. With olive oil only, `reagent_volume` was 0, nothing was transferred, the `while(amount > 0)` loop ran through every container, and ended in the `stack_trace()` at `_crafting.dm:443`. The craft still went ahead because the earlier check had already passed.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 91578: crafting takes subtype reagents (e.g. olive oil) it counted as ingredients", changing the reagent branch of `get_used_reqs()` in `code/datums/components/crafting/_crafting.dm`. For each container, it now walks a copy of the container's `reagent_list`, skips reagents that are not a subtype of the required path, and transfers each matching reagent by its own type until the required amount is reached. The old code's exact-type lookup was replaced; the surrounding loop, the `checked` list and the appearance update are unchanged.

An alternative would have been to make `check_contents()` stricter and refuse olive oil for the recipe. That would remove a working feature (any oil type counts) instead of fixing the removal, so the removal side was changed to match what the check already accepted.

The reported symptom has no fix, since it could not be reproduced.

## Verification

After the fix, on a rebuilt test server, with the same setup as the olive oil case (one full set of ingredients and a 50 unit olive oil bottle):

- The burger was crafted normally.
- The olive oil bottle dropped from 50 to 35 units (15 units taken).
- The new round's runtime log had no `Dangit` runtime.
- The crafted burger contained olive oil among its reagents.

Not checked:

- The fix with a mix of oil types across several containers (for example plain oil in one bottle and olive oil in another). The loop is written to handle it, but only the single-bottle case was run after the change.
- Other recipes that use reagent requirements (the change is in shared crafting code and applies to all of them). It was not run against them in game.
- Any case matching the reporters' symptom, which never reproduced.

## Notes

- The change is limited to the reagent loop in `get_used_reqs()`. It sits close to code touched by other crafting work (the `OnCraft` reagent inheritance for food, `_crafting.dm` near line 322), so a trivial merge conflict is possible but not expected.
- If #91578 or #92095 can still be reproduced on a current build, a round ID or the exact ingredient states (how the patties and cheese were produced) would help, as would any runtime in the log at the time of the craft.
