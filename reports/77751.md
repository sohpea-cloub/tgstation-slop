# #77751: Medisim shuttle arrows do full damage when thrown

| | |

| Status | Fixed on a branch and checked on plain humans; not tested in a real medisim match |

## Summary

In the medieval simulation (CTF) shuttle, any damage other than stamina or oxygen dusts a player. A regular arrow has `throwforce = 1`, so an archer could throw an arrow by hand and kill a player as if they had shot it. The fix gives the archers a medisim arrow type with `throwforce = 0` and a matching quiver. Arrows shot from a bow still do the projectile's damage.

## The bug

The issue (found downstream, with no round ID) says that on the medieval sim shuttle you can pick up an arrow and throw it to subvert the intended play. A thrown arrow should be harmless in the sim, since only bows are meant to kill.

## Reproduction

1. On the test server, start the round and observe. Using the Lua editor, spawn two humans.
2. Throw an `/obj/item/ammo_casing/arrow` at one of them.
3. Result: the target was "hit by the arrow" and took 1 brute damage, which is the arrow's `throwforce`.

The step that kills in the sim was not run: no medisim shuttle or CTF player was set up. That the damage dusts a CTF player is read from the code only (see Cause).

## Cause

- `code/modules/projectiles/guns/ballistic/bows/bow_arrows.dm:11` sets `throwforce = 1` on the arrow casing.
- A thrown item applies `apply_damage(thrown_item.throwforce, ...)` on impact (`code/modules/mob/living/living_defense.dm:324`).
- A CTF player has `damage_type_check` registered on `COMSIG_MOB_AFTER_APPLY_DAMAGE` (`code/modules/capture_the_flag/ctf_player_component.dm:41`). It calls `ctf_dust()` for any damage type except `STAMINA` and `OXY`, so 1 brute from a thrown arrow dusts the player.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 77751: Medisim archers' arrows no longer hurt when thrown", in `code/modules/capture_the_flag/medieval_sim/medisim_classes.dm`:

- New `/obj/item/ammo_casing/arrow/medisim` with `throwforce = 0`.
- New `/obj/item/storage/bag/quiver/full/medisim` whose `arrow_path` is the medisim arrow.
- The archer outfit's belt now uses the medisim quiver.

Setting `throwforce = 0` on the regular arrow was not chosen, so regular arrows keep their behaviour everywhere else. Fired arrows are unchanged: the projectile damage of 50 is kept, and because the casing is caseless and reusable, a fired arrow respawns as the same medisim type.

## Verification

On plain humans on the test server:

- Throwing `/obj/item/ammo_casing/arrow/medisim` gave the throw-tap sound and 0 brute damage on the target.
- The medisim quiver's `arrow_path` is the medisim arrow.

Not checked: a real medisim shuttle or CTF player, the archer outfit's actual spawn, the dust itself, and a bow shot with a medisim arrow in game.

## Notes

- Arrows that do not come from the archer quiver (for example crafted ones, or regular arrows brought onto the shuttle) would still hurt when thrown. That was judged acceptable inside the sim.
- Making the damage rule more general (for example a throw that does not count in CTF) was not attempted.
