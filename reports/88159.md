# #88159: Cyborg wire panel reveals when a malf AI has shunted

| | |
|---|---|

| Status | Fixed on a branch; two related leads left open (see Notes) |

| Tested on | tgstation master `87b93338`, 2026-10-23 |

## Summary

When a malf AI shunts into an APC its `name` is changed to "<name> APC Copy". The cyborg wire panel prints the linked AI's `name`, so a cyborg's wires tell anyone who opens them that the AI has shunted. The same `name` was also used in the cyborg's stat panel and in its law-sync message. The fix prints `real_name` in all three places.

## The bug

Opening a cyborg's wire panel shows a status line "The intelligence link display shows <AI name>." If the linked AI is a malf AI that has shunted, the line reads "<AI name> APC Copy". That reveals the shunt, though not the APC it went into, as the issue's commenters confirmed.

## Reproduction

1. On the test server, as an observer or admin using the Lua editor, turn a human into an AI (`AIize()`), spawn a `/mob/living/silicon/robot` and link it with `set_connected_ai(ai)`.
2. Possess the AI and have it shunt into an APC with `apc:malfoccupy(ai)`, answering "Yes" to the shunt confirmation.
3. Read the cyborg's `wires:get_status()`.

Before the fix the AI's `name` was "AI APC Copy" (its `real_name` was "AI") and the wire status line read "The intelligence link display shows AI APC Copy." The shunt was done through the proc and the alert, not by a player acquiring the malf module.

## Cause

`code/modules/power/apc/apc_malf.dm:50-51` renames the shunting AI:

```dm
if(!findtext(occupier.name, "APC Copy"))
	occupier.name = "[malf.name] APC Copy"
```

Three places show the linked AI's `name` to the cyborg or to whoever inspects it:

- `code/datums/wires/robot.dm:26`, the wire panel status line.
- `code/modules/mob/living/silicon/robot/robot.dm:270`, the "Master AI" line in the stat panel.
- `code/modules/mob/living/silicon/robot/laws.dm:25`, the "Remember, ... is your master" message when laws are synced.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 88159: cyborg wires, stat panel and lawsync no longer reveal a shunted malf AI". It changes `connected_ai.name` to `connected_ai.real_name` in those three places. The rename in `apc_malf.dm` is left alone, since the "APC Copy" name is used elsewhere for the shunted AI, and `real_name` keeps the AI's actual name.

## Verification

The same steps were repeated after rebuilding. The AI's `name` was still "AI APC Copy", but the wire status line read "The intelligence link display shows AI."

Not checked: the stat panel line and the law-sync message were not run in game; they use the same change and were verified only by reading the code. Other places that show an AI's `name` were not audited.

## Notes

- After the AI leaves the APC, nothing restores the name: `malfvacate` (`code/modules/power/apc/apc_malf.dm:61-83`) does not rename it, so the AI keeps the "APC Copy" name afterwards. This was read in the code, not tested, and was not fixed here.
- Other displays of an AI's `name` (the exhaustive search was not done) may still show "APC Copy" to players.
