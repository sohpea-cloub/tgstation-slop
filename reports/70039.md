# #70039: Air alarm sprites do not match their alarm status

## Summary

Air alarms only learned about the area's atmos alarm at the moment their own turf was exposed, so when one air alarm in an area set off or cleared the area alarm, the other alarms in that area kept a stale sprite, light colour and UI state. Two further defects sat in the same code: `check_danger()` returned early after choosing a hazard warning message, so the sprite never updated in a breached room, and resetting the alarm from an air alarm that was not itself the source did nothing, leaving the area alarm up. The fix has three commits: alarms now listen for the global atmos-alarm fire/clear signals and refresh themselves; `check_danger()` no longer returns before `update_appearance()`; and a new `reset_area_alarm()` clears the alarm from every air alarm in the area.

## The bug

Players saw air alarms whose sprite did not match the situation:

- **#70039, #59043, #11956**: after an atmos alarm was set or reset on one air alarm, other air alarms in the same area kept the flashing amber or red sprite (or stayed green/teal while an alarm was active), even though the UI of that alarm showed the opposite. It mostly appeared in areas with several air alarms, and sometimes fixed itself and sometimes did not. In #11956 (2015) only the alarm that had the button pressed blinked; the others needed to be unlocked and set manually.
- **#75957**: in a breached room (an AI satellite hit by an immovable rod, per the screenshots) the air alarm stayed green.
- **#59042**: with several air alarms in an area, the area's atmos alarm did not clear in the alarm app even when every alarm in the area looked nominal. Resetting by hand at the right air alarm did clear it.

## Reproduction

Using the Lua editor on master `87b93338`, on the area "Central Primary Hallway", which has 8 air alarms. `mine[n]` below are the area's alarms, `dl` is `danger_level`, `ad` is `area_danger`, and light colours are amber `#FFAA00`, red `#FF0022` and teal `#00FFCC`. This was done by calling the same procs the game uses, not through the real tgui buttons (the test observer was view-only; the Lua calls are what `ui_act` "alarm"/"reset" do).

1. **Stale area state (#70039, #59043, #11956).** `mine[1].alarm_manager:send_alarm("Atmosphere")`, set `danger_level = 2` and `update_appearance()`. Result: alarm 1 had `dl=2` but the others had `ad=nil` and kept the teal light, although the area had one active alarm. Then `check_enviroment()` on alarms 2 and 3 (what a turf expose triggers) gave them `ad=1`. Resetting on alarm 1 cleared the area alarm (area active alarms 0), but alarms 2 and 3 stayed at `ad=1`, amber with the UI reporting `atmosAlarm` true, until each ran its own `check_danger`.
2. **Sprite not updated in hazardous air (#75957).** Removed the air from alarm 4's turf (`loc.air:remove_ratio(1)`) and called `check_enviroment()` on it. Result: `dl=2` and the area alarm was sent, but the light stayed `#00FFCC`; the sprite was never updated.
3. **Reset from a non-source alarm (#59042).** With the area alarm sent by alarm 1, `mine[2].alarm_manager:clear_alarm(...)` returned 0 and the area alarm stayed at 1 active (of 2 in the test).

## Cause

All in `code/modules/atmospherics/machinery/air_alarm/_air_alarm.dm` unless noted.

1. **Area state is read in only one place.** `area_danger` is assigned from `my_area.active_alarms[ALARM_ATMOS]` inside `check_danger()` (line 584), which is only called from the alarm's own `COMSIG_TURF_EXPOSE` registration (line 41). Nothing refreshed the other alarms in the area when one of them sent or cleared the area alarm. `update_appearance()` (line 523) shows the amber/red state when `danger_level == AIR_ALARM_ALERT_WARNING || area_danger`, so a stale `area_danger` gives a stale sprite and a stale `atmosAlarm` (line 256).
2. **Early returns in `check_danger()`.** After the alarm is sent, a chain of `if(...) warning_message = ...` followed by `return` (lines 605-628) exited the proc for every hazard case, so the `update_appearance()` at line 637 and `selected_mode.replace()` at line 640 never ran when the air was dangerous.
3. **Reset only clears the caller's own alarm handler.** The "reset" action of `ui_act` (line 507), the alarm wire pulse (`code/datums/wires/airalarm.dm:50`) and the circuit component (`air_alarm_circuit.dm`) called `alarm_manager.clear_alarm(ALARM_ATMOS)` on that one air alarm. An alarm that had not sent the alarm itself had nothing to clear, so the area alarm remained if another alarm in the area had sent it.

## Fix

Three commits on the fix branch.

- **"Fix issue 75957: air alarms update their sprite when the air turns hazardous"**: the `return`s in `check_danger()` become an `else if` chain, so execution continues to the existing `update_appearance()` and `selected_mode.replace()`.
- **"Fix issue 70039: every air alarm in an area follows the area's atmos alarm"**: each air alarm registers on `SSdcs` for `COMSIG_GLOB_ALARM_FIRE(ALARM_ATMOS)` and `COMSIG_GLOB_ALARM_CLEAR(ALARM_ATMOS)`. The handler `on_area_atmos_alarm_changed()` ignores signals for other areas, re-reads `my_area.active_alarms[ALARM_ATMOS]` into `area_danger` and calls `update_appearance()` if the boolean state changed. This also covers #59043 and #11956.
- **"Fix issue 59042: resetting an air alarm clears the area's atmos alarm from every air alarm in it"**: a new `reset_area_alarm()` walks `GLOB.air_alarms`, and for the calling alarm and every alarm in the same area clears the atmos alarm, sets `danger_level` to none and updates the sprite. The UI "reset", the alarm-wire pulse and the circuit component's clear path now call it.

An alternative would be to share one area-based alarm datum, as was suggested in a 2016 comment on #11956; that was not attempted since it is a much larger refactor.

## Verification

After rebuilding and starting a fresh round on the test server, again with Lua calls rather than the tgui buttons:

- Sending the alarm from alarm 1: the four alarms sampled read `dl=2 ad=1 #FF0022 | dl=0 ad=1 #FFAA00 | dl=0 ad=1 #FFAA00 | dl=0 ad=1 #FFAA00` (the source red, the others amber). Resetting at alarm 1: all `ad=0 #00FFCC`.
- Vacuum at alarm 4: `dl=2 ad=1 #FF0022` with `msg=Danger! Low pressure detected.`, others amber.
- Two alarms (1 and 2) as sources, then `reset_area_alarm()` on non-source alarm 3: all `ad=0`, teal, and the area had 0 active alarms.
- No runtimes appeared in the runtime log of the new rounds.

Not checked: the real tgui "reset" button and the alarm-wire pulse and circuit paths (they were not exercised by themselves; they call the same helper); a real breach caused by a rod or explosion rather than removing the air with Lua; and the alarm console/Canary app display itself.

## Notes

- Side effect of fixing the early returns: `selected_mode.replace()` (the Replace-air mode, which only acts below 0.05 atm) now actually runs in vacuum. Before the change it was unreachable there. Maintainers should confirm that is intended.
- The `WIRE_ALARM` mend branch (posting an alarm, `code/datums/wires/airalarm.dm:70`) was not changed; it still sends the alarm from the one alarm, and the new signal handler updates the others.
- #11956 is from 2015 and reported that locked alarms did not blink; on master the UI already reflected the area alarm for other alarms (as a later comment noted), and the remaining visible symptom was the stale sprite fixed here.
- #59043's reporter said the sprite could be reset by anything that updates the light; this matches the cause above (a stale `area_danger` that is only corrected on the alarm's own next `check_danger`).
