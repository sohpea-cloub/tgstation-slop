| | |
|---|---|
| Issues | [#91465](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/91465): fixed |
| Status | Fixed (map-only change); checked in game by simulating RCD deconstruction, not with a real RCD |
| Fix branch | [fix/91465](https://github.com/sohpea-cloub/tgstation-slop/tree/fix/91465) |
| Tested on | tgstation master `87b93338`, 2026-10-23 |

## Summary

On NebulaStation the turfs above the AI satellite (notably the Zen Garden roof) have no reinforced plating beneath them, so RCD deconstruction can strip them down to openspace and open a hole into the AI chamber. The fix adds a reinforced-plating ceiling baseturf helper to each of the eight satellite areas and makes the 13 Zen Garden window plating tiles reinforced plating, which the helper skips.

## The bug

The issue (labelled Mapping) says the satellite needs reinforced plating "above it, to stop RCDs and such". Other stations protect the AI with RCD-proof floors above it. Nebula did not, so the roof could be removed layer by layer from the level above.

## Reproduction

1. On master, make NebulaStation the next map and restart the test server. In world coordinates the map's first z-level was z1 and its roof level was z3 on the test server (y offset of -92 from the map file).
2. Using the Lua editor, find the grass turf at (192, 82, 3), above `/area/station/ai/satellite/chamber` (circuit floor below). Its baseturfs were the bottom baseturf and plating.
3. Call `ScrapeAway()` on it, which is what the RCD's `RCD_DECONSTRUCT` branch does after its `rcd_proof` check (`code/game/turfs/open/floor.dm:358-364`).
4. Call it a second time.

Observed: the first call turned the grass into `/turf/open/floor/plating` (`rcd_proof` 0), the second turned that into `/turf/open/openspace`, a hole into the AI chamber.

Caveat: a real RCD item was not used. Only `ScrapeAway()` was called; no RCD charges, delays or tool use were exercised.

## Cause

Nothing beneath the roof turfs was `rcd_proof`. The map tooling already has a mechanism for this: `/obj/effect/baseturf_helper/reinforced_plating/ceiling` (`code/modules/mapping/mapping_helpers.dm:98`) stacks `/turf/open/floor/plating/reinforced` under the turf one z-level above every tile of the area it is placed in. NebulaStation never used it over the satellite.

Even with the helper, one case is skipped. `reinforced_plating/replace_baseturf` (`mapping_helpers.dm:93-95`) returns early if the ceiling turf is itself plating ("Plates should not be placed under other plates"). The Zen Garden roof has 13 plain-plating window tiles (with poddoors), which the helper would therefore leave unprotected.

Reading the map file, the roof above the satellite is mostly hull, engine and reinforced turfs already, plus reinforced walls, glass and the Zen Garden (grass, stone, bamboo, wood, herringbone floor, plating tiles).

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 91465: Nebula AI sat roof is RCD-proof", changing only `_maps/map_files/NebulaStation/NebulaStation.dmm`:

- One `/obj/effect/baseturf_helper/reinforced_plating/ceiling` added in each of the eight `/area/station/ai/satellite/*` areas on the lower level (atmos, chamber, equipment, foyer, interior, maintenance, service, teleporter), each placed on an existing floor-tile definition that is used once in the map.
- The tile definition used by the 13 Zen Garden window plating tiles was changed from `/turf/open/floor/plating` to `/turf/open/floor/plating/reinforced`, since the helper skips plating.

Using the existing helper was preferred over adding new roof turfs. The choice of how to protect the roof is a mapping decision and could be done differently.

## Verification

After the change, on a fresh round, a Lua sweep covered all 307 roof turfs above satellite tiles. Each was `ScrapeAway()`ed repeatedly (at most 4 times) until it stopped changing or was `rcd_proof`:

- 24 grass, 41 + 6 reinforced wall (reported as two counts), 17 glass, 8 herringbone, 5 stone, 5 bamboo, 3 wood and 1 dirt all ended as `/turf/open/floor/plating/reinforced`.
- 164 hull turfs and the 13 garden plating tiles were already RCD-proof.
- 13 asteroid, 4 water, 2 sand and 1 beach water turf were unchanged (`ScrapeAway()` does not alter these).
- No tile ended as openspace.

An earlier sweep with a faulty script modified the world, so the server was restarted and the sweep rerun clean. Maps load at runtime, so no code rebuild was involved.

Not checked: a real RCD, other ways into the chamber, and play-testing the roof level otherwise.

## Notes

- Reinforced plating can still be removed with tools (welding and prying steps); the change only stops RCD deconstruction.
- The sweep covered the 307 roof turfs above tiles of the eight satellite areas, not other parts of the station.
