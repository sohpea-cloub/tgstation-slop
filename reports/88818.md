## Summary

Deltastation has no `JoinLate` landmarks, so latejoin spawns come entirely from the chairs on the arrivals shuttle. The arrivals shuttle registers every chair in its area as a latejoin spawn point, including an unanchored `/obj/structure/chair/office` that Deltastation's shuttle contains. If that chair is rolled or dragged off the shuttle, latejoiners can spawn wherever it ended up, such as the engineering security post. The fix registers only anchored chairs, and applies the same rule in the last-resort spawn lookup.

## The bug

The reporter (a round on Deltastation with an engineering security officer latejoin) was spawned in the engineering security post, and an Assistant in the same round had the same thing happen. The issue gives no reproduction steps ("late join on deltastation?") and no cause.

What is happening: latejoin destinations are picked at random from `SSjob.latejoin_trackers`. On Deltastation that list is built from the chairs in the arrivals shuttle area when the shuttle initializes. One of those chairs is an office chair, which is unanchored and can be pushed around. Once it has left the shuttle, it is still in the list, and `JoinPlayerHere` moves the new mob to the chair's current turf.

## Reproduction

A plain latejoin on unmodified master worked normally, so the report was reproduced by displacing the chair rather than by a natural round.

1. Start a Deltastation round on master.
2. In the Lua editor, list `SSjob.latejoin_trackers`. It held 17 entries: 16 comfy shuttle chairs and one `/obj/structure/chair/office` at 153,35 on the shuttle's z-level (next to the console), which is unanchored.
3. A real latejoin as Assistant buckled the player onto the shuttle, and 30 calls to `SSjob.send_to_late_join()` with new humans all landed in "Arrival Shuttle". So with the chair in place there was no problem.
4. In the Lua editor, `forceMove` that office chair to 106,130 on the station level, a turf in the "Security Post - Engineering" area (which has office chairs of its own and is far from the arrivals dock). This stands in for a player rolling or dragging the chair away.
5. Call `SSjob.send_to_late_join()` on 60 new humans. 4 of 60 landed at 106,130 in the engineering checkpoint; the other 56 landed on the shuttle.

The rate matches picking uniformly from 17 trackers (1/17 per spawn; 4 of 60 is a sample of that).

Not reproduced: an actual player pushing the chair off the shuttle during a round. The first attempt at the forced move failed on a Lua scripting mistake and was redone; the numbers above come from the later runs.

## Cause

`code/modules/shuttle/mobile_port/variants/arrivals.dm:33-58`, `/obj/docking_port/mobile/arrivals/LateInitialize()`, adds every chair in the arrivals area, anchored or not:

```dm
for(var/obj/structure/chair/shuttle_chair in arrival_turf)
	new_latejoin += shuttle_chair
```

and then sets `SSjob.latejoin_trackers = new_latejoin` (line 58). `SSjob.send_to_late_join()` (`code/controllers/subsystem/job.dm:752-764`) picks from that list with `pick(latejoin_trackers)` and never checks that the chosen chair is still in the arrivals area.

`/obj/structure/chair/office` is defined with `anchored = FALSE` (`code/game/objects/structures/beds_chairs/chair.dm:297-299`). Deltastation's arrival shuttle (`_maps/shuttles/arrival/arrival_delta.dmm`) contains one such chair in `area/shuttle/arrival`, so it becomes a tracker.

The fallback `get_last_resort_spawn_points()` (`job.dm:767`, used when `latejoin_trackers` is empty) has the same flaw: at line 773-775 it returns the first chair it finds on an arrivals turf without considering whether it is anchored.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 88818: arrivals latejoin skips unanchored chairs":

- In `arrivals.dm` `LateInitialize()`, chairs with `anchored` false are skipped (`continue`) when building the tracker list.
- In `job.dm` `get_last_resort_spawn_points()`, the early return for a chair now requires `shuttle_chair.anchored`; unanchored chairs fall through to the normal blocked-turf checks.

Alternative considered: re-checking in `send_to_late_join` that the chosen tracker is still in the arrivals area. The notes do not record a decision between the two; filtering at registration was the smaller change. It does not help if an anchored chair is unanchored later, or if a chair is moved after registration by other means.

## Verification

After a rebuild and a new round on Deltastation, in the Lua editor: `SSjob.latejoin_trackers` held 16 entries and none was an office chair. With the same forced move applied to the office chair (still reachable on the shuttle), 60 of 60 `send_to_late_join()` calls landed on the shuttle.

Not checked: a real latejoin round after the fix, other maps' arrivals shuttles (other shuttle templates with unanchored chairs would also be affected, but none were inspected), and the last-resort path was not exercised.

## Notes

By the agents' reading it does not touch the lines changed here and the two compose: this keeps displaced chairs out of the list, that one avoids blocked ones. A merge check was not run.
- The office chair remains unanchored and movable; the fix only stops it from being a spawn point. Latejoiners now spawn in the 16 comfy shuttle chairs only.
- The issue's cause was inferred, not observed: the reporter's round data does not say the chair had been moved. The displaced-chair theory fits the reported symptom (two latejoiners in the same non-arrivals area, one of them in a security-post area that has office chairs), but it is not proven that this is what happened in that round.
