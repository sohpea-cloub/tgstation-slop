## Summary

The report says a medical cyborg's hypospray started printing "-6.10352e-005 units injected. 30 units remaining." and injected nothing, soon after the cyborg had been repaired from death. On master the hypospray injected exactly the selected amount in every check, before death and after a kill, repair and revive, for all seven reagents of the medical hypospray at both transfer settings. The code that produced that message no longer exists. While testing, a separate, minor bug was found in the same hypospray (see Notes).

## The bug

From the 2021 report (client 514, round with several testmerges): after being repaired as a dead medical cyborg, the cyborg hypospray "would start injecting a negative amount of reagent", showing a message such as "-6.10352e-005 units injected. 30 units remaining". The reporter did not know the trigger. One comment suggested a link with #61807. A later comment (2022) first said a fix for a runtime in the borg hypospray's examine text had resolved it, then retracted that.

## Reproduction

Attempted on master by following the report. It did not reproduce.

1. Started the round, observed, and opened the Lua editor.
2. Using the Lua editor, spawned a `/mob/living/silicon/robot/model/medical` and a monkey next to it, gave the monkey the `immobilized` trait so it stayed put, and took control of the cyborg.
3. Before death: equipped the hypospray, opened its interface, selected Epinephrine and clicked the monkey. Result: "You inject the monkey with the injector (Epinephrine)." and a balloon reading "5 units injected".
4. Killed the cyborg with `adjust_brute_loss(150)` and `adjust_fire_loss(100)`; it died and its modules went offline. Repaired it with `adjust_brute_loss(-150)` and `adjust_fire_loss(-100)`, then called `revive()`. `revive()` is the same step the cyborg reboot upgrade uses (`code/game/objects/items/robot/robot_upgrades.dm`, in `borgo.revive()`). The messages "ERROR CLEARED: Module N back online" appeared.
5. Re-equipped the hypospray and clicked the monkey repeatedly with real clicks. Each click gave "5 units injected" and the interface's Epinephrine count went 25, 20, 15, 10, 5, 0. The next click gave "not enough Epinephrine!". The monkey's Epinephrine rose by the injected amounts, and no balloon showed a negative or tiny number.
6. With the Lua editor, called `interact_with_atom` directly on the monkey for each of the 7 reagents at transfer amounts 5 and 2. Each stored amount dropped by exactly the set amount and the monkey's amount rose by the same. No runtimes appeared in the game log.

Not tried: other ways of repairing a cyborg (welder and cable repair, a robotics console or real round conditions such as the testmerges listed in the report), the other hypospray variants, and the upgraded (expanded reagents) hypospray. The reporter's exact sequence is unknown, so a cause outside what was tried cannot be ruled out.

## Cause

Not applicable as a bug on master. The message in the report came from an older implementation that computed the injected amount from a per-reagent container. The current hypospray (`code/game/objects/items/robot/items/hypo.dm`) keeps all reagents in one `stored_reagents` holder, and its injection path never computes an amount:

- `interact_with_atom` (line 181) refuses unless `stored_reagents.has_reagent(selected_reagent.type, amount_per_transfer_from_this)` (line 190).
- It then transfers exactly that amount with `stored_reagents.trans_to(injectee, amount_per_transfer_from_this, target_id = selected_reagent.type, ...)` (line 205).
- The balloon text prints the configured `amount_per_transfer_from_this` (line 206), not a measured value.

So a negative or fractional figure cannot come from this path unless `amount_per_transfer_from_this` itself becomes negative or fractional, and `possible_transfer_amounts` is `list(2,5)` (line 110).

## Fix

None. The triage notes for this issue attribute the removal of the old arithmetic to the later rewrite and say that the earlier fix cited in the thread only addressed an examine runtime. That history was not independently checked here.

## Verification

Nothing was fixed, so nothing was verified beyond the negative result above: injecting exact amounts before and after death, repair and revive, through real clicks (Epinephrine at 5 units) and direct calls (7 reagents at 5 and 2 units).

## Notes

The issue could probably be closed as fixed by the rewrite, with the caveat that the original trigger was never identified.

A separate defect was seen in the same hypospray and has no issue yet:

- Each reagent is stored with one hidden extra unit (`max_volume_per_reagent + 1`, line 168), so the entry is never removed from `stored_reagents`; the interface subtracts that unit when it shows the volume (line 221).
- With the transfer amount at 2, an entry with an odd displayed amount passes `has_reagent(type, 2)` at its last shot and `trans_to` then takes the whole entry (hidden unit included), which deletes the reagent datum. Observed on Epinephrine: the stored amount went from 2 to nothing (the datum was gone), and the deleted datum had a `gc_destroyed` timestamp.
- `selected_reagent` (line 138) still points at that deleted datum. Further clicks gave "not enough Epinephrine!". When the cyborg's recharge later added Epinephrine back (`regenerate_reagents`, line 179, which adds 5 units with no hidden unit), the new entry was one unit lower than a fresh one: shots of 2 went 5 to 3 to 1 and then failed, and the interface would show one unit less than is really stored (it subtracts a unit that is not there).
- The held reference to a deleted datum could also be a hard-delete risk. This was not measured.

A possible fix would be to re-point `selected_reagent` at the live entry for its type (or clear it) after an injection that empties the entry, and to re-add the hidden unit when an entry is recreated. This was not written or tested.
