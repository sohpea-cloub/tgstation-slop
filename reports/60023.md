## Summary

Three storage issues were looked at together. #60023 (paradox bags ignoring the shift+E belt hotkey) is an old report written when the bags were not real storage items; on master they use `datum/storage`, and the hotkey's proc takes items out of and puts items into the belt-worn (blue) bag. #82798 (quick-storing into a full backpack drops the item on the floor) did not reproduce: the item stayed in hand in every case tried. #83642 (a trash bag refuses a wallet clicked directly but gathers it when another item on the tile is clicked) was reproduced and fixed in `datum/storage`.

## The bug

### #60023: paradox bags and shift+E

The issue says that with a paradox bag worn on the belt, shift+E (the "quick equip belt" hotkey) neither takes an item out of it nor puts an item into it. A comment pointed out that the blue bag was only an access point to the red "master" bag, that the bags were not subtypes of `/obj/item/storage`, and that changing the type let items go in with the hotkey but not come out.

### #82798: quick-storing into a full backpack

The reporter said that quick-storing an item while the backpack was full dropped the item on the floor instead of leaving it in the hand, so a hotkey press could lose an energy sword. In a comment they added that the item "just sits" on the floor rather than falling, and that normally the item would stay in hand if it did not fit.

### #83642: trash bag and wallets

With a trash bag in hand (default gathering mode, "everything"), clicking a wallet lying on the floor shows a floating "too big!" and picks nothing up. Clicking a different item on the same tile gathers that item and the wallet together. A wallet in hand clicked onto the trash bag also goes in, so the wallet does fit. The "too big!" is the game trying to put the trash bag into the wallet, which is why it is the wrong message.

## Reproduction

All three were tried with the Lua editor on the test server (round started, then admin Lua used to spawn and equip things).

### #60023 (not reproduced)

1. Using the Lua editor, spawned a human, a red paradox bag (the red bag's `Initialize` creates the blue bag on its tile), a grey jumpsuit, a pocket crowbar and other tools, and gave the human the jumpsuit (the bag needs one to be worn on the belt) and the blue bag on the belt slot. The red bag stayed on the floor.
2. Pressed shift+E through the test client with a crowbar in hand. Nothing happened and no chat message appeared, so it is unknown whether the key reached the keybinding at all. This is a limitation of the test client, not evidence about the game.
3. Called `smart_equip_targeted(ITEM_SLOT_BELT, "belt")` on the human directly from Lua, which is what the shift+E keybinding does (`code/datums/keybinding/human.dm:34-40`). With the crowbar in hand the chat said "You put the pocket crowbar into the paradox bag." and the crowbar left the hand. Calling it again with an empty hand put the crowbar back in the left hand.

So the put-in and take-out paths work on the blue belt bag when the proc is called; the actual key press was not confirmed.

### #82798 (not reproduced)

1. Using the Lua editor, spawned a human, gave the human a backpack on the back slot filled with 21 pens.
2. With a pocket crowbar in hand, called `smart_equip_targeted(ITEM_SLOT_BACK, "backpack")` (the shift+B hotkey's proc). A "no room!" balloon appeared, chat said "You can't fit the pocket crowbar into your backpack!", and the crowbar stayed in hand (the inventory showed no change).
3. Repeated with a wielded fire axe: "too big!" balloon and "You can't fit the fire axe (Wielded) into your backpack!"; the axe stayed in hand.
4. Called `execute_quick_equip()` (the proc behind the plain E "quick-equip" hotkey) with the held items. In that run it put the crowbar in a pocket when one was free, or printed "You are unable to equip that!" when nothing fit. In no case was the item dropped.

Only proc calls were used; real key presses were not tried (see the first reproduction).

### #83642 (reproduced)

1. On the test server, spawned a human holding a trash bag (`/obj/item/storage/bag/trash`, gathering mode "everything" by default), and on one floor tile a wallet and a pen.
2. Clicked the wallet with the trash bag. Result: a floating "too big!" and nothing picked up.
3. Clicked the pen with the trash bag. Result: the pen was picked up and the wallet went into the bag with it.

## Cause

### #60023

Not applicable on master. The paradox bags are plain items with real storage: `/obj/item/shared_storage/red` calls `create_storage(max_total_storage = 15, max_slots = 21)` and creates the blue bag (`code/modules/mining/lavaland/mining_loot/equipment.dm:239-248`); the blue bag creates its own storage and calls `atom_storage.set_real_location(master)` (`equipment.dm:252-254`) so that it shows the red bag's contents. `smart_equip_targeted` (`code/modules/mob/living/carbon/human/inventory.dm:349`) uses the equipped item's `atom_storage`, inserting into it and taking the last item from `storage.real_location.contents`, so the blue bag works. The 2021 report was about an older implementation.

### #82798

No defect found. The relevant paths are `smart_equip_targeted` (`code/modules/mob/living/carbon/human/inventory.dm:349-392`, which only prints "You can't fit ..." when `attempt_insert` fails) and `equip_to_best_slot` (`code/modules/mob/inventory.dm:697-730`, which only prints "You are unable to equip that!" when nothing accepts the item). Neither calls a drop. It is likely that a later storage rework fixed whatever the reporter saw, but no specific change was identified and history was not checked (the checkout used was shallow).

### #83642

A left click with an item runs `melee_attack_chain` (`code/_onclick/item_attack.dm:24`). It calls the target's `base_item_interaction` first (`item_attack.dm:32`) and only afterwards `pre_attack` (`item_attack.dm:44,52`). Two things then go wrong when the target is a wallet and the tool a trash bag:

1. At the end of `base_item_interaction`, a target that has storage and `insert_on_attack` gets `atom_storage.item_interact_insert(user, tool)` called (`code/game/atom/atom_tool_acts.dm:53-56`). That tries to insert the tool, here the trash bag, into the wallet (`code/datums/storage/storage.dm:906-908`: `attempt_insert(thing, user)` and `return ITEM_INTERACT_SUCCESS`). The bag is too big, so "too big!" appears, and the click counts as handled, so `pre_attack` never runs.
2. Even if it had run, the bag's `on_preattack` handler, which implements gathering (`storage.dm:748`), returned early for any clicked item that has its own storage:

```dm
if(!istype(thing) || thing == parent.loc || !allow_quick_gather || thing.atom_storage)
	return
```

Clicking a different item on the tile avoids both, because that item has no storage, so gathering runs and `collect_on_turf` collects every item on the turf, including the wallet (small enough for a trash bag: `max_specific_storage = WEIGHT_CLASS_SMALL`, wallet `w_class = WEIGHT_CLASS_SMALL`).

## Fix

One commit on `fix/60023`, "Fix issue 83642: gathering bags pick up storage items they can hold when clicked directly", changes `code/datums/storage/storage.dm`:

- `on_preattack` no longer returns early for every clicked storage item. It now returns early only if the clicked item has storage and this storage cannot hold it (`thing.atom_storage && !can_insert(thing, user, messages = FALSE)`). Items that can be held fall through to the existing collect logic.
- `item_interact_insert` returns `NONE` (so the click continues to `pre_attack`) when the held tool is a quick-gather bag, the storage's parent is an item lying on a turf, and the bag can hold that item (`thing.atom_storage?.allow_quick_gather && isitem(parent) && isturf(parent.loc) && thing.atom_storage.can_insert(parent, user, messages = FALSE)`).

This keeps the rest of the click behaviour: a bag that cannot hold the clicked storage item (for example a backpack on the floor, too big for a trash bag) still gets the old "put the bag into it" attempt and is left on the tile.

No fix was written for #60023 and #82798 because neither reproduced.

## Verification

- #83642: after rebuilding and restarting the test server, spawned a human with a trash bag and on one tile a wallet, a pen and a backpack. Clicked the wallet with the bag. Result: "picked up"; the wallet and the pen were gathered and the backpack (too big for the bag) stayed on the tile, as before.
- Not checked: other gathering bags (ore bag, plant bag, mining satchel and so on), gather modes other than "everything" (the fix touches the `COLLECT_ONE` path through `on_preattack`, which was not exercised), storage items other than wallets (such as pen-holding boxes or pill bottles), right clicks, and the bag being worn rather than held. No code audit of other callers of `item_interact_insert` beyond reading the one in `code/game/objects/items/storage/boxes/food_boxes.dm:286`, which passes a food item and is unaffected by the new check.
- #60023 and #82798: results above come from direct proc calls, not real key presses.

## Notes

- #60023 looks stale and could probably be closed: the paradox bags were rewritten onto `datum/storage` since the 2021 comments. The one thing not confirmed here is a real shift+E key press; a maintainer with a normal client could check that in under a minute.
- #82798 could not be reproduced with a full backpack, whether the held item was small or a wielded two-handed item. The reporter's round was in April 2024, and the cause of the original behaviour is unknown. A maintainer may want to ask the reporter whether they still see it.
- The #83642 fix makes direct clicks on a storage item behave like the click on a neighbouring item. A deliberate consequence: a trash bag now gathers small storage items such as wallets (and anything else it can hold) from the floor when they are clicked directly, matching what it already did when they were picked up as part of a pile. If maintainers would rather never gather storage items, the opposite fix (making the click-on-wallet case consistent by excluding storage items from the collect loop) is also possible; it was not tried.
- The behaviour in the issue's last point (wallet in hand onto the trash bag works) is unchanged.
