## Summary

Two reports say that a Luminescent slimeperson's slime extract ability does nothing: the orange core's major ability should create a smoke cloud (#48253, 2019) and the adamantine core's minor ability should reduce incoming damage (#79087, 2023). On master both work. The orange ability's reagents react at once into a smoke cloud, and the adamantine ability adds a flat 25% damage resistance that visibly reduces damage taken. Nothing was changed.

## The bug

- #48253: the orange core's major ability prints "a cloud of smoke bursts out of your skin!" but, according to the reporter, no smoke appeared.
- #79087: the reporter used the adamantine core as a Luminescent to "stack armor", took the same damage as before, and suspected the armor refactor. They remembered a 30 to 50 percent reduction.

## Reproduction

Both were tested through the Lua editor on a spawned human set to the Luminescent species with the extract placed in the species' `current_extract`. The extract's `activate()` was called directly, and the real action datums (`luminescent_actions[2]` for the minor ability and `[3]` for the major one) were run with `Activate()`. The player-facing action button and the "shove the extract in" step were not used.

### #48253 (orange core, major)

1. Using the Lua editor, spawn a human, call `set_species` with `/datum/species/jelly/luminescent`, spawn an `/obj/item/slime_extract/orange` and assign it to `species.current_extract`.
2. Run `activate(human, species, 2)` on the extract, and separately run `luminescent_actions[3]:Activate()`.
3. Look at the user's tile.

Observed, in both variants: the chat message "starts violently coughing up smoke!" and `smoke.ogg` played, and 6 to 7 smoke objects (the number the test notes recorded) appeared around the user. A later run with the real action counted a smoke effect on the user's tile as well.

### #79087 (adamantine core, minor)

1. Same setup, with an `/obj/item/slime_extract/adamantine`.
2. Apply 20 brute to the chest with `apply_damage(20, 'brute', chest)` and note the increase in the chest's brute damage.
3. Run `activate(human, species, 1)` (or `luminescent_actions[2]:Activate()`), then apply 20 brute again.
4. For melee, have another human hit the Luminescent with a fire axe (force set to 20, wounds suppressed) once with the buff active and once after setting `damage_resistance` to 0.

Observed:

- `activate(..., 1)` returned 450 (the cooldown) and `damage_resistance` went from 0 to 25. The real action datum gave 25 as well.
- Chest brute: +20 without the buff, +15 with it. The fire axe gave 15 with the buff and 20 with `damage_resistance = 0`.
- `apply_damage(20, 'brute', chest, 90)` (90 extra blocked) did 0 damage with the buff, since resistance and blocked are added together and the result is not positive.

Two caveats from the testing. The first activate-then-damage run was confounded: after hand-zeroing `damage_resistance` the trait was still held and the ability replied "already hardened", so a clean retry was done (which gave 25). Also, the first chest readings were cumulative (20, then 35), which is where the 15 increment comes from. The fire-axe script also hit one Lua-side runtime from the test script's own setup, unrelated to the game code.

## Cause

There is no defect; the relevant master code:

- `code/modules/research/xenobiology/xenobiology.dm:311-316` (orange, major): adds 5 u phosphorus, 5 u potassium and 5 u sugar to the user's reagents and prints the "cloud of smoke" message.
- `code/modules/reagents/chemistry/recipes/pyrotechnics.dm:385-405`: that mix is the `smoke_powder` recipe (`REACTION_INSTANT`), so it reacts immediately inside the user. `on_reaction()` calls `do_chem_smoke()` at the user's turf with the holder's reagents as the carry, plays `smoke.ogg`, clears the holder, and for a living carbon shows "starts violently coughing up smoke!" and applies lung damage. The smoke is created by the chemical reaction rather than by the extract itself.
- `xenobiology.dm:521-531` (adamantine, minor): adds `TRAIT_ADAMANTINE_EXTRACT_ARMOR` and `user.damage_resistance += 25`, with `reset_armor()` undoing both after 120 seconds (`:541-543`).
- `code/modules/mob/living/damage_procs.dm:41-42`: `apply_damage()` adds `damage_resistance` to `blocked` for non-forced damage and scales damage by `(100 - blocked) / 100`. That is where the 25% takes effect.
- `TRAIT_ADAMANTINE_EXTRACT_ARMOR` is read only by the "already hardened" check in the same ability; it has no armor effect of its own.

The reporter of #79087 guessed that the armor refactor broke the buff by looking up limbs rather than the ability. The buff does not go through limb armor values at all (it uses `damage_resistance`), so a limb-armor change would not affect it.

## Fix

None.

## Verification

Checked on master only, as described above: orange major produced smoke, and adamantine minor reduced chest brute damage from 20 to 15 both for direct damage and for a melee hit. Not checked: projectile or burn damage (all damage types go through the same `apply_damage()` line, so the same resistance is expected), the player-facing action button, the extract being inserted through the normal in-game route, the adamantine major ability (a 12-second golem transformation), and the buff's 120-second expiry.

## Notes

- The reporter's "50-30%" figure does not match the code: the resistance is a flat 25%, lasting 120 seconds, with a 450-tick (45 second) cooldown.
- The orange ability also clears the user's reagents and damages their lungs through the smoke powder reaction (`pyrotechnics.dm:399,405`). That is intended by the reaction's code, but players may not expect it; the ability's own code comment only mentions the reagents in the user's body becoming part of the smoke.
- Both issues can probably be closed as already working (or fixed since they were opened).
