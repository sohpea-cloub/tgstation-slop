## Summary

When a mech or other sealed vehicle falls into a chasm that has no level below it, its occupants stay inside, alive and able to act, while the vehicle is moved into the invisible chasm storage. The pilot can also drive out during the fall animation and still be pulled in afterwards. The fix ejects the occupants onto the chasm just before the vehicle falls, so they fall, die and can be fished out like any other mob, and the empty vehicle goes into the storage as an ordinary object. Chasms with a level below are unchanged.

## The bug

All three issues describe the same family of symptoms around a mech and a chasm:

- #71395 (2022): driving a Clarke into a chasm and back out leaves the mech visually offset by a tile from its real position, causing hit registration problems; another mech (a Ripley) disappeared while the pilot could still move and eject.
- #70993 (2022): a posibrain in a Ripley fell into a chasm; the brain later ended up on the floor while its owner could still control the (invisible) mech.
- #93441 (2025): a mech in a chasm "enters a state of limbo": still on the chasm tile, able to fire and use modules, the pilot alive and talking over the radio, and not recoverable by fishing, so admins have to intervene.

On current master, the visible effect is the limbo state: a mech pilot that has "fallen" is not dead and cannot be reached by a rescue hook.

## Reproduction

On master, using the Lua editor on a running round. The station level was used. A room with no level below the chasm tiles is needed so the chasm takes the "oblivion" path.

### #71395 and #93441 (drive in, and drive in and straight out)

1. Using the Lua editor, put your mob in an empty room, spawn a Clarke (`/obj/vehicle/sealed/mecha/clarke`) next to you, turn the tiles on one side of the mech into chasm (`ChangeTurf(/turf/open/chasm)`), and enter the mech with `mob_enter` (calling `moved_inside` alone did not add an occupant, so the mech would not move). Face the mech toward the chasm.
2. Drive one tile into the chasm. The game prints "The Clarke falls into the chasm!". Three seconds later the mech's location is the chasm storage ("chasm depths"). The pilot is not dead (stat 0) and not stunned, and stays in control inside the storage, but driving does nothing there. This is the limbo state from #93441.
3. In a second run, drive into the chasm and immediately drive back out. The mech did drive out onto the floor tile during the fall animation. About a second later it was moved into the chasm storage from that floor tile anyway, with its appearance restored and its location set to the storage. This is the "drive out of the chasm" of #71395, as the current code behaves.
4. The pilot of a mech in the storage was not in `GLOB.chasm_fallen_mobs` (only living mobs that enter the storage directly are added to it), so a rescue hook cannot find them. This matches the "can't be fished" part of #93441.

The one-tile sprite offset from the 2022 reports did not reproduce; the chasm code is different today, and the same race now ends in the limbo state instead.

### #70993 (posibrain in a mech)

1. Spawn a Ripley, put a positronic brain processor (`/obj/item/brain_processor/positronic`) in it and make its brain mob an occupant (`add_occupant`), then move the Ripley onto a chasm tile with `forceMove` (it was placed there, not driven).

This case was only run after the fix was in place (see Verification). The behavior on master described in the issue (the brain separated from a mech that keeps moving) was not separately captured; the evidence on master for this issue is the same-code-path limbo state of the Clarke above, where the occupant stays alive and in control inside the chasm storage.

## Cause

`/datum/component/chasm/proc/drop` (`code/datums/components/chasm.dm:135`) handles anything that falls into a chasm. Mobs buckled to the dropped thing are unbuckled and dropped separately (lines 153-156). Living mobs that fall into a chasm without a level below (the "send to oblivion" part, from line 174) are stunned for 20 seconds (line 179), and for them there is a check that they are still on the chasm tile before the fall completes (line 187). Both are inside the `isliving` block.

A sealed vehicle (`/obj/vehicle/sealed`: mechs and cars) is not a mob; it takes the generic object path, and its occupants are not buckled to it. As a result:

- nothing stops the occupants from driving during the one-second animation (five 0.2 s sleeps, lines 201-206);
- there is no check that the vehicle is still on the chasm before it is moved into the storage (`dropped_thing.forceMove(storage)`, line 228), so a vehicle that drove out is pulled in from wherever it went;
- the occupants stay alive inside the vehicle in the storage. `/obj/effect/abstract/chasm_storage/Entered` (line 273) only registers living mobs that enter the storage directly, so these occupants are not in `GLOB.chasm_fallen_mobs`.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 71395: eject sealed vehicle occupants when the vehicle falls into a chasm", adds seven lines at the start of the "send to oblivion" branch of `drop`:

```dm
if (istype(dropped_thing, /obj/vehicle/sealed))
	var/obj/vehicle/sealed/doomed_vehicle = dropped_thing
	if (LAZYLEN(doomed_vehicle.occupants))
		doomed_vehicle.dump_mobs(randomstep = FALSE)
```

`dump_mobs` (`code/modules/vehicles/sealed.dm:144`) removes every occupant through the normal exit path. With `randomstep = FALSE` they land on the chasm tile and drop through the normal mob path (stun, death, entry into `GLOB.chasm_fallen_mobs` so rescue hooks work). The empty vehicle cannot be driven, so it falls into the storage and can be fished out like any object. Because ejection happens through the normal exit, effects that react to a mob being on a chasm, such as a jaunter or raptor save via `COMSIG_MOVABLE_CHASM_DROPPED`, now apply to the pilot.

The multi-level path (a chasm with a level below) was left unchanged: the vehicle still falls to the level below with its occupants inside.

This approach was chosen over the alternative suggested in #93441 (destroying the mech on contact) because it reuses existing behavior: pilots die like anyone else, and the vehicle stays recoverable. Making fall damage apply to the vehicle itself would be a design change.

## Verification

After the fix, on the test server, using the same setup:

- Human pilot in a Clarke, driving in and straight out: the chat showed "You stumble and stare into the abyss...", "<pilot> drops out of the Clarke!", "The Clarke falls into the chasm!", then "<pilot> has died". The storage contained the Clarke and the pilot, with the pilot dead and in `GLOB.chasm_fallen_mobs`. A following `move west` command moved only the observer ghost.
- Posibrain (#70993): the Ripley with the brain was placed on the chasm. The game printed "The positronic brain falls into the chasm!", then "The APLU MK-I Ripley falls into the chasm!". The storage then held the positronic brain (with its brain mob inside, no remote control) and the Ripley, which had no occupants and no driver.
- The runtime log showed no new runtimes (only two known empty-line messages from the Lua editor).

Not checked: an AI pilot, a clown car or other sealed vehicles (the code path is shared but it was not exercised), the multi-level chasm path, and fishing the pilot or the vehicle out with a rescue hook (only list membership was checked). The original sprite-offset symptom could not be verified either way.

## Notes

- Objects other than sealed vehicles that leave the chasm tile during the fall animation (for example on a conveyor) are still pulled into the storage from wherever they are. This was left alone because a "still on the chasm" check is awkward when the object moves onto another chasm tile (the code de-duplicates by `falling_atoms`).
- Living mobs in other containers (a locker, for example) that fall into the chasm stay alive in the storage in the same way. This is the same family of bug and was not addressed.
- A search for upstream pull requests mentioning these three issues found none.
