## Summary

On IceBoxStation, the "Ice-Ruin Asteroid Site" surface ruin contains a lavaland-style cave pocket with necropolis tendrils. When a tendril initializes, it turns nearby asteroid and snow turfs into `/turf/open/indestructible/necropolis`, which has lavaland air. Where those turfs border icemoon snow, which has icemoon air, the two atmospheres differ and the air subsystem reports 8 to 79 active turfs at roundstart. The fix makes each converted turf keep the air of the turf it replaced. Neither the lavaland-ruin scenario in #74566 nor the Listening Post scenario in #90027 produced an active turf in testing.

## The bug

On some IceBox rounds the mapping log reports "There are N active turfs at roundstart caused by a difference of the air between the adjacent turfs." Nothing is visible to players, but the air subsystem has to simulate those turfs from the start of the round, and mappers see the warning on every affected round. #74566 attributes it to the `/datum/map_template/ruin/icemoon/underground/lavaland` ruin being exposed when chasms or open turfs are generated above it. #90027 reports active turfs between an open turf and the turf below it in the Listening Post ruin.

In the test rounds the active turfs were not in either of those ruins. They were on the surface level, around the necropolis turfs created by the tendrils of the Ice-Ruin Asteroid Site (`_maps/RandomRuins/IceRuins/icemoon_surface_asteroid.dmm`). That ruin is a lavaland cave-generator pocket (area `/area/lavaland/surface/outdoors/unexplored`) with basalt floor and tendrils, placed inside an icemoon snow surface.

## Reproduction

The Asteroid Site is placed randomly, so not every round shows the bug.

1. Run IceBoxStation on master with an admin-enabled test server and restart until the Ice-Ruin Asteroid Site is placed. A baseline round without forced ruins had no roundstart active turfs. In every round where the bug appeared, the active turfs were in the Asteroid Site. The Asteroid Site was forced with a temporary `always_place = TRUE` edit in `code/datums/ruins/icemoon.dm` to make this quicker. That edit is not part of the fix.
2. After initialization, read the mapping log (`debug.log` of the round). It contains a line such as "There are 79 active turfs at roundstart ...", followed by one "Active turf" entry per turf with coordinates, area and turf type.
3. In the rounds seen, the listed turfs were pairs of `/turf/open/indestructible/necropolis` and an adjacent turf of another type. Depending on the round, the neighbour was basalt (`.../basalt/.../lava_land_surface`), icemoon snow, or lava. Counts in the rounds on stock code ranged from 8 to 79 (for example 8, 13, 18, 37, 70 and 79).
4. Using SDQL2 (`select /mob/living/basic/mining/tendril`) in an affected round, tendrils were found inside the Asteroid Site on the surface level (for example at 188,181,4 and 206,203,4 in one round).
5. In one round, the air of an active turf pair was read directly. A necropolis turf at 217,171,4 had temperature 339 K and 34.3 mol. The adjacent snow turf at 217,170,4 (`/turf/open/misc/asteroid/snow/icemoon`, planetary atmos, `ICEMOON_ATMOS`) had 180 K and 77.9 mol. That round reported 18 active turfs.

In all of these rounds the active turfs were discovered by reading the log and the live air values, as above. The bug was reproduced by the normal round-start path, not by calling procs by hand.

The scenarios in the issues were tried as well and did not reproduce:

- Lavaland Incursion and Listening Post were both forced into every round with the same temporary `always_place` edit, for about 8 rounds. Neither ruin produced an active turf nearby. A Lua scan of the baseline round found open-space turfs over turfs with different air only where the map itself puts open space over walls (harmless).

## Cause

`code/modules/mob/living/basic/lavaland/tendril/tendril.dm:88` (master), in `/mob/living/basic/mining/tendril/Initialize`:

```dm
if (istype(rock, /turf/open/misc/asteroid) && prob(100 / sqrt(max(1, dist))))
	infected_turfs[rock] = rock.type
	rock.ChangeTurf(/turf/open/indestructible/necropolis, null, CHANGETURF_IGNORE_AIR)
```

The tendril converts asteroid turfs within 4.5 tiles. `/turf/open/indestructible/necropolis` has `initial_gas_mix = LAVALAND_DEFAULT_ATMOS` (`code/game/turfs/open/_open.dm:395`), has no planetary atmosphere, and `CHANGETURF_IGNORE_AIR` stops `ChangeTurf` from copying the old turf's air. So a converted turf always starts with lavaland air, regardless of what surrounds it. On the lavaland map that is the same atmosphere as its neighbours. In the Asteroid Site, where snow with icemoon air borders the tendril's radius, the air differs at the edge, and the air subsystem's roundstart check (`code/controllers/subsystem/air.dm:586`) marks those turfs active.

Using `CHANGETURF_INHERIT_AIR` instead (the obvious fix) does not work. Tendrils run during atom initialization, and the neighbouring turfs, including the turf being replaced, may not be initialized yet, so their `air` is null. With that flag, active turfs remained in the test (44 to 79 in a round where the tendril was in the basalt pocket), and a later attempt logged the runtime "Cannot read null.moles" at `code/modules/atmospherics/gasmixtures/gas_mixture.dm:324`.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 74566: necropolis tendril turfs keep the air of the turf they replace", touching only `tendril.dm`.

Before converting a turf, it saves the turf's `initial_gas_mix`. After `ChangeTurf` (still with `CHANGETURF_IGNORE_AIR`), if the new necropolis turf's `initial_gas_mix` differs from the saved one, it sets it to the saved value and rebuilds the turf's air from it with `air.copy_from(create_gas_mixture())`.

This uses only the turf type's `initial_gas_mix`, which is available before the turf is initialized, so it does not depend on initialization order. The converted turf then has the air the old terrain would have had: icemoon air over snow, lavaland air over basalt. On a lavaland map, where the replaced turf already has lavaland air, the air is left as it was.

## Verification

After the fix, four test rounds with tendrils present were run, with the Asteroid Site forced. All four reported 0 "active turfs at roundstart" in the log and 0 runtime errors in the checked round's `runtime.log`. Tendrils were at different places in these rounds, and only one round had a tendril inside the Asteroid Site (at 195,185,4). The others were elsewhere on the map, including one round with tendrils on a different z-level (z5), so only one of the four exercises the specific case that had produced active turfs. The same tendril setup, before the fix, gave 8 to 79.

Not checked:
- The fix was not tested over many rounds. A single clean round with an Asteroid Site tendril is weak evidence against random placement.
- Behaviour when a tendril is killed (it reverts the converted turfs) was not tested, and neither was player interaction with the converted turfs.
- Neither Lavaland Incursion nor Listening Post was tested with the fix applied, since they were not shown to be involved.

One internal note says a bug-present round was seen once after the fix. The final report does not mention one and states four clean rounds. This write-up follows the report, but that note is ambiguous and cannot be ruled out from the available information.

## Notes

- #90027: the Listening Post was forced into about 8 rounds and produced no active turfs. The suggestion in that issue's comments that modular ruin parts lack a roof (from #89135) was not confirmed either: the Listening Post's modular map root lies inside the ruin footprint. This issue is probably a different cause from the tendril bug and remains open.
- A possible latent gap was found but not shown to cause any active turf. The `NO_RUINS` flag that `try_to_place` (`code/modules/mapping/ruins.dm:34`) sets on the ruin area is lost when the ruin map loads, because `ChangeTurf` only carries over reservation flags (`code/game/turfs/change_turf.dm:110`). In a Lua tally of about 3000 ruin turfs on the underground levels in the baseline round, none had the flag. The guard in `code/game/turfs/open/openspace.dm` (around lines 196-210), which replaces open space with snow when the turf below has `NO_RUINS`, therefore does not apply to ruin interiors. Ruins are only protected by the ceiling pass in `code/modules/mapping/map_template.dm` (`generate_ceiling`, around lines 185-197), which applies only to ruins with `has_ceiling`. Carrying `NO_RUINS` in `ChangeTurf` would change that, but it would also affect player-dug holes above ruins, so it should be restricted to mapload if attempted. This was not done.
- #74566 describes the problem as "spontaneous" and says earlier fixes did not help. This is consistent with the tendril cause, because the Asteroid Site is placed randomly, so the active turfs only appear on rounds that roll it. The original round's active turf report was not available in the inputs, so it cannot be checked whether it was this cause.
