## Summary

**#92402.** Alarm handlers announce an alarm once, and an alarm listener only knows about alarms announced after it was created. A cyborg built after an alarm fired, or revived after missing alarms and clears while dead, therefore showed "Systems Nominal" while an older station alert console listed the alarms. The fix keeps a global list of handlers that have active alarms and lets a listener sync with it when it is created and when it is revived.

**#86869.** The List Law Changes admin secret stamped entries with round time, while `silicon.log` uses the wall clock. The fix shows the UTC time first, with the round time in brackets.

**#91031.** Removing laws through the law panel did reach linked cyborgs on master for the law types tested, so this was not reproduced and nothing was changed for it.

## #92402: cyborg alert panel out of date

### The bug

An engineering cyborg's alert panel did not show the alarms that a real station alert console showed. The reporter's screenshot has the cyborg's panel on the left and a console on the right. The reporter's steps were to become an engineering cyborg, set off an air alarm, and compare the two.

What happens underneath is that each listener keeps its own list of alarms, and that list is only ever built from alarm signals received while the listener exists and is accepting changes.

### Reproduction

The alarm was triggered by calling procs from admin tools, not by the player action of setting off an air alarm in a live round.

1. On the test server, open the Lua editor and spawn a cyborg (`/mob/living/silicon/robot`). Spawning it also creates its alert listener.
2. Send an atmospherics alarm from the air alarms on z-level 2 through `alarm_manager.send_alarm("Atmosphere")`. 198 alarms were sent, covering 140 distinct areas.
3. Spawn a station alert console (`/obj/machinery/computer/station_alert`) after the alarms were sent, and read its alarm list.
4. Read the listener of a cyborg that was created after the alarms were sent.

Observed on master:

- A cyborg whose listener was created after the alarms had 0 alarms and would display "Systems Nominal", while a console listing them showed 140 areas.
- For the revive case, `death()` on a cyborg sets `accepting_alarm_changes` to 0 and `revive()` sets it back to 1, but alarms sent or cleared in between are never replayed, so the cyborg's list stays out of date.

### Cause

`code/datums/alarm.dm` (`/datum/alarm_handler`, around line 57) records the area of a sent alarm with `existing_alarms += our_area` and then only broadcasts a global signal. A listener learns about an alarm solely from that broadcast, in `add_alarm` (`alarm.dm:121`). `/datum/alarm_listener/New` (`alarm.dm:110`) only registers for the signals; it never asks the handlers what is currently active.

Cyborgs also stop accepting changes on death: `code/modules/mob/living/silicon/robot/robot.dm:102` registers `prevent_alarm_changes` on `COMSIG_LIVING_DEATH` and line 103 registers `allow_alarm_changes` on `COMSIG_LIVING_REVIVE`. Alarms and clears that happen while the cyborg is dead are dropped, and nothing re-syncs on revive (`alarm.dm:191`).

### Fix

Commit "Fix issue 92402: alarm listeners created late or revived now catch up on active alarms", in `code/datums/alarm.dm`:

- Adds `GLOB.active_alarm_handlers`, the handlers that currently hold at least one alarm. A handler is added when it sends an alarm and removed when its last alarm is cleared.
- Changes `sent_alarms[alarm_type]` from a list of areas to an associative list of area to z-level, so a late listener can apply its z-level filter.
- Adds `sync_alarms()` to the listener. It drops what the listener knows (releasing the camera deletion signals it registered), then re-adds every active alarm of the types it listens for, with no notification to the owner.
- Splits the body of `add_alarm` into `register_alarm(..., notify = TRUE)`, so `sync_alarms()` can add alarms silently.
- Calls `sync_alarms()` from `New` and from `allow_alarm_changes`.

### Verification

With the fix, in the Lua editor, using the same 198 air alarms in 140 areas:

- A newly created cyborg listener had 0 alarms before the fix and 140 after.
- Clearing one alarm left the live cyborg at 139.
- After `death()`, 29 more alarms were cleared. The dead cyborg stayed at 139, as expected, and after `revive()` it matched the handler's 118.
- No runtimes appeared from the change.

Not checked: a real air alarm triggered by gameplay rather than `send_alarm`; the TGUI panel itself on a cyborg, AI or other listener owners (the checks read the listener's data); the camera tracking part of a synced alarm.

### Notes

- Handlers left over from earlier test runs hold the same alarms, so they must be deleted between test runs or the counts will be inflated.
- `sync_alarms()` rebuilds from scratch rather than merging, which keeps it correct after missed clears, at the cost of re-walking every active handler on each revive.

## #86869: List Law Changes shows the wrong time

### The bug

Times in the List Law Changes admin secret did not match times in `silicon.log`. The reporter's example is one law change at 00:08:33 in `silicon.log` and 00:08:57 in the secret. The reporter also suggested showing seconds.

### Reproduction

1. Change a law on an AI.
2. Open the admin secret List Law Changes and compare its time with the matching `silicon.log` line.

On master the secret shows time since the round started, not a clock time, so the two cannot be matched.

### Cause

`log_law_change` in `code/_globalvars/logging.dm:86` builds the entry with `round_timestamp()`, which is time since round start. `silicon.log` uses the wall clock. (The discussion on the issue guessed at `world.realtime` rounding; the code on master does not use `world.realtime` here.)

### Fix

Commit "Fix issue 86869: law change list shows the same wall clock time as silicon.log". The entry is now `"[server_timestamp()] (round [round_timestamp()])"`: the UTC time first, with the round time kept in brackets. The wall clock time includes seconds.

### Verification

After a law change the list showed `18:06:08 (round 00:00:41)` and `silicon.log` showed `2026-10-23 18:06:08.022`. Not checked: a round that crosses midnight.

## #91031: law panel removals and linked cyborgs

### Reproduction attempt

Using the Lua editor, an AI (a human passed through `AIize()`) was set up with a linked cyborg (`connected_ai` set and `lawupdate` on). Through the law panel's `remove_law` action, an inherent law, the zeroth law and a hacked law were removed from the AI. Each time, the cyborg's law list dropped the same law.

### Cause of the expected behavior

`code/modules/admin/verbs/lawpanel.dm:187` calls `borgo.try_sync_laws()` for linked cyborgs after a successful change.

### Result

Not reproduced on master for inherent, zeroth and hacked laws. Supplied laws were not tested, so a regression limited to supplied laws is not ruled out. No fix was made.
