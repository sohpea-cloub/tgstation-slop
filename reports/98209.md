## Summary
The emergency welding tool (`/obj/item/weldingtool/mini`) uses the `miniwelder` and `miniwelder1` states of `icons/mob/inhands/equipment/tools_righthand.dmi`. In that file the east-facing and west-facing cells of both states were swapped, so the sprite is drawn on the wrong side of the character. The fix swaps those cells back, leaving every other cell unchanged.

## The bug
Held in the right hand, the emergency welding tool looks wrong when the character faces east or west: the east-facing sprite is drawn where the west-facing one belongs, and the other way around. The issue text says the same: "Right hand left facing inhands are right, and right hand right facing inhands are left."

## Reproduction
The bug was established from the icon data, not by rendering the item before the fix.

1. The item is `/obj/item/weldingtool/mini` (`code/game/objects/items/tools/engineering/weldingtool.dm:362`), with `inhand_icon_state = "miniwelder"` and the right-hand file `tools_righthand.dmi`.
2. Read the DMI metadata of `tools_lefthand.dmi` and `tools_righthand.dmi` and measure the bounding box of each east and west cell. States hold four directions in the order south, north, east, west.
3. For the other tools in the file (crowbar, wrench, screwdriver, wirecutters), the right-hand file has the east cell close to the body (x about 14) and the west cell further out (x about 8). This is the mirror of the left-hand file (east x about 20, west x about 13).
4. In the right-hand file, `miniwelder` had its east cell at x=8 (4x4 pixels) and its west cell at x=15 (5x5), which is the reverse of the rule. The lit `miniwelder1` (3 frames) was the same: east 6x6 at x=3..7, west at x=15.

## Cause
The east and west cells of `miniwelder` and `miniwelder1` in `icons/mob/inhands/equipment/tools_righthand.dmi` were stored in each other's positions. There is no code defect.

## Fix
One commit, "Fix issue 98209: swap east and west right-hand inhands of the emergency welding tool", swaps the east and west cells of `miniwelder` (cells 34 and 35) and of `miniwelder1` (cells 38/39, 42/43 and 46/47 for its three frames). The edit was made on the raw RGBA pixels and the DMI metadata was written back unchanged. Every other cell is identical to before and the file still decodes. The commit contains only the binary change to the dmi.

## Verification
- On the test server after a rebuild, two humans were spawned with the emergency welding tool in the right hand, one facing east and one facing west, and rendered. Facing east the tool sits at the hand position on the body, tip pointing east. Facing west the tool is out in front of the character, tip pointing west. This matches the other tools.
- Not checked: a render before the fix (the cause rests on the cell measurements); the lit state `miniwelder1` in game; the left hand.

## Notes
Two similar suspects in the same file were measured but not fixed or checked visually:
- `cercd`: the right-hand east and west cells look swapped compared with `rcd` and `ircd` (east 7x12 at +6, west 8x12 at +13).
- `welder` and `indwelder`: the right-hand east and west boxes equal the left-hand file's (the lit and `exwelder` ones are mirrored), so the plain welder's right-hand inhands may be a copy of the left-hand ones.
