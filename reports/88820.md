## Summary

Four reports about newtonian drift (momentum in zero gravity) were examined together. Only #88454 reproduced: when a thrown item's throw ends, `datum/thrownthing` gave it momentum along an angle computed from absolute, axis-swapped distances, so the angle always pointed into the north-east quadrant and a wall hit from any side produced an eastward push. The fix computes the real throw angle. The jetpack-and-lattice report (#88820), the z-move report (#72793) and the hard-crit report (#77176) did not reproduce on this build, though two of those checks have caveats listed below.

## The bug

### #88454: thrown items get an eastward push on hitting a wall

"Seems like it gives some force directed east on every collide." In zero gravity, an item thrown at a wall picked up drift in the wrong direction.

### #88820: stepping on a lattice stops jetpack momentum (not reproduced)

The report says that with a jetpack, crossing a lattice in space stopped momentum completely, and a maintainer commented that this is not intended.

### #72793: moving up/down a z-level kills drift (not reproduced)

Per the 2023 report, helplessly drifting in zero gravity and moving to the z-level above or below stopped the drift.

### #77176: hard crit stops inertial drift (not reproduced)

Per the 2023 report, a mob moving from inertia in space stopped when it went into hard crit.

## Reproduction

### #88454 (reproduced)

1. On the test server, in a no-gravity area with walls to the north, west and south of an open spot, using the Lua editor spawn three crowbars (the agents used `/obj/item/crowbar`).
2. Throw them at the walls with `throw_at()` (one at each wall; this is the throw code path but not a player throwing by hand).
3. Read each item's `drift_handler.drifting_loop.angle` (DM angles: clockwise from north, so 0 is north, 90 east, 180 south, 270 west) at the moment of impact.

Observed on unpatched master: the drift angle after the throw ended was 90 (east) for every throw. The north throw happened to be correct by accident; the west throw should have been 270 and the south throw 180.

### #88820 (not reproduced)

1. With the Lua editor, give a human an `/obj/item/tank/jetpack`, turn it on with stabilisation off, and place lattices at three adjacent turfs in space.
2. Call `newtonian_move(90)` (east) on the human from a few tiles west of the lattices.
3. The human drifted through the lattices and on to x=118 and beyond (181 on a longer run).
4. Moving east by input from a start tile next to the lattice row also ended past the lattices, still drifting.
5. Control: the same human without a jetpack stopped one tile short of the first lattice, adjacent to it. This is the lattice acting as something to push off, and appears intended: it applies when drifting adjacent (range 1) to the lattice, not only when stepping onto it.

Not tried: the MOD suit jetpack module, a stabilised jetpack, and switching stabilisation off mid-drift.

### #72793 (not reproduced, with a caveat)

With a drifting human (`newtonian_move(90)`), `zMove(1, <turf on another z-level>)` was called with an explicit target turf. The human moved from z 2 to z 10 and kept drifting east (x 166 -> 169 -> 171). Caveat: this called `zMove` directly; the "move up" verb itself was not used because the test map had no open space above.

### #77176 (not reproduced, with a caveat)

A drifting human took 130 brute damage (health -151; the mob's stat stayed 0 because the test mob had no-crit traits), then `set_stat(2)` (hard crit) was forced. The human, lying and blind, kept drifting from x=127 to x=144 over 3 seconds. Caveat: the crit state was forced, not a natural hard crit with normal incapacitation.

## Cause

### #88454

`code/controllers/subsystem/throwing.dm:224` and `:226`, in `/datum/thrownthing/proc/finalize()`:

```dm
thrownthing.newtonian_move(delta_to_angle(dist_x, dist_y), drift_force = drift_force)
```

`dist_x` and `dist_y` are set up in `throw_at()` (`code/game/atoms_movable.dm:1439-1459`) as `abs(...)` of the target offset, and when `dist_x <= dist_y` the two are swapped so x holds the longer axis. They carry no sign, so `delta_to_angle(dist_x, dist_y)` can only yield angles from 0 to 90 (north-east quadrant) whatever direction the throw went. A throw west, south or north therefore pushed the item east or north-east. In the wall case, `hitby_react`'s bounce (`code/game/atom/atom_act.dm`, about 0.2 s later) then overrides the drift with the reverse direction, so the visible symptom is a brief wrong push.

### #88820 (why it did not reproduce)

`mob/Process_Spacemove` (`code/modules/mob/mob_movement.dm:275-276`) returns FALSE early for `TRAIT_NOGRAV_ALWAYS_DRIFT`, before it looks for a "backup" to push against (`get_spacemove_backup()`, where a lattice counts). `code/game/objects/items/tanks/jetpack.dm:59` and `:118` add that trait when a non-stabilised jetpack is on. A stabilised jetpack or no jetpack takes the other path: `drift_handler.after_move` (`code/datums/drift_handler.dm:143`) calls `Process_Spacemove(continuous)`, which returns TRUE next to a lattice and the mob halts. That is by design. The MOD jetpack module also adds this trait (`code/modules/mod/modules/modules_general.dm:147,170`) but was not tested.

### #72793 and #77176 (why they did not reproduce)

`up()`/`down()` use `do_after`, which allows drifting (`code/datums/timed_actions.dm:175`). `zMove` is a `forceMove`, and the drift handler's move handling only deletes the handler when `Process_Spacemove(continuous)` returns TRUE, so a z-move alone does not cancel drift. Nothing in `drift_handler` or `Process_Spacemove` reads the mob's stat.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 88454: thrown items kept momentum in the wrong direction after hitting something":

- Adds `/datum/thrownthing/proc/get_drift_angle()` in `throwing.dm`: `get_angle(starting_turf, target_turf)` when the throw started and ended on different turfs of the same z-level, otherwise `dir2angle(init_dir)` (or 0).
- Both `newtonian_move()` calls in `finalize()` now use it instead of `delta_to_angle(dist_x, dist_y)`.

The throw's real start and target are what the item was travelling along, so the sign problem disappears without changing how `dist_x`/`dist_y` are used for the throw movement itself. No other fix was made: the other three issues showed no defect.

## Verification

After a rebuild, on a fresh round in a no-gravity area, three crowbars were thrown at once at walls three tiles to the north, west and south, and `drifting_loop.angle` was sampled every 0.05 s:

| Throw | Angles seen |
|---|---|
| North | 0, 0, 0, 0, 0 (north at impact, correct), then the drift handler was gone |
| West | 270, 270 (west at impact), then 90 repeatedly (the bounce reverses to east) |
| South | 180, 180 (south at impact), then 0 repeatedly (the bounce reverses to north) |

The impact angles are now 0, 270 and 180 instead of 90.

Not checked: throws from a player's hand with a thrown item hitting mobs rather than walls, diagonal throws, throws across z-levels, non-item thrown objects, and the effect on thrown items that end without hitting anything (the `!hit` branch changed the same way but was not separately sampled).

## Notes

- The build that was tested also contained an unrelated earlier change to Lua runtime logging, which is not part of this fix.
- The canonical report (#88820) came from a build before the `TRAIT_NOGRAV_ALWAYS_DRIFT` check; the jetpack path appears already fixed upstream. The lattice-grab behaviour for stabilised or absent jetpacks remains, and it is by design.
- #72793 and #77176 are open-ended: neither was ruled out by a natural-play test. A multi-z map test of the real "move up" verb and a natural hard-crit test would settle them.
