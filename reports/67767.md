## Summary

The Obsessed antagonist is a `/datum/antagonist/obsessed` that lives alongside a `/datum/brain_trauma/special/obsessed` brain trauma, and the two have to stay together. The three issues describe three ways they got separated. #67767 (the envy knife's species change curing the obsession) no longer happens on master: traumas are now carried over to the replacement brain. #97873 (removing Obsessed from the antagonist panel left the trauma behind, which blocked adding it again) and #84926 (reviving as a podperson dropped the obsession, because the trauma stayed on the old corpse) were both reproduced and fixed with one commit each.

## #67767: envy knife un-obsesses the user (not reproduced)

### The bug

The 2022 report: an Obsessed player who hits someone with the envy knife stops being Obsessed. A maintainer's comment explained why at the time: the knife calls `set_species`, which replaced the brain (even when the species was the same) and with it all brain traumas.

### Reproduction attempt

On master, using the Lua editor on the test server:

1. Spawn a human A, give them `/datum/brain_trauma/special/obsessed` with the obsession set to another human B (both with minds initialised).
2. Call `set_species` on A repeatedly: human, lizard, pod, ethereal, golem, android, abductor, human.
3. After each call, read A's traumas and mind antagonists.

Result: after every species change A still had the trauma ("Psychotic Schizophrenia"), the Obsessed antagonist datum, and exactly one trauma. The brain type really did change for the pod, golem, android and abductor species, so the replacement brain was exercised. The envy knife was not used directly; it reaches the same code via `copy_dna(user.dna, COPY_DNA_SE|COPY_DNA_SPECIES)` (`code/modules/mapfluff/ruins/objects_and_mobs/sin_ruins.dm:49`), which calls `set_species` (`code/datums/dna/dna.dm:98`), so calling `set_species` directly is equivalent for this purpose.

### Cause of the old behaviour, and why it is gone

On master, `regenerate_organs` calls `before_organ_replacement` on the old organ (`code/modules/mob/living/carbon/human/_species.dm:317`), and the brain's version (`code/modules/mob/living/brain/brain_item.dm:446` onward) moves every trauma to the replacement brain:

```dm
for(var/datum/brain_trauma/trauma as anything in traumas)
	remove_trauma_from_traumas(trauma)
	replacement_brain.add_trauma_to_traumas(trauma)
```

So the premise of the 2022 comment no longer holds. The upstream PR #69091 was noted as a different change (shapeshifting), so the fix that removed this behaviour was not identified; it came from elsewhere.

The second reply in the thread (a species change curing every trauma of an ethereal) is also covered by the same transfer.

## #97873: removing Obsessed from the antagonist panel leaves the trauma (fixed)

### The bug

Removing Obsessed from a mob through the traitor panel only removes the antagonist datum. The brain trauma stays. It keeps applying its effects, and re-adding Obsessed to the mob silently fails (the admin log says it was added). The reporter found that curing all traumas on the mob let Obsessed be added again.

### Reproduction

On master, Lua editor on the test server:

1. Give a human the obsessed trauma (with an obsession preset), so they have the trauma and the Obsessed antagonist.
2. Call the antagonist's `admin_remove`.
3. Read traumas and antagonists: the trauma was still there ("Psychotic Schizophrenia") while the antagonist was nil.
4. Try `admin_add` again: the trauma remained and the antagonist was still nil.

### Cause

`/datum/antagonist/proc/admin_remove` (`code/modules/antagonists/_common/antag_datum.dm:449`) only logs and calls `on_removal()`. The obsession is implemented in the trauma (`code/datums/brain_damage/creepy_trauma.dm`), and nothing cures it. Adding Obsessed again is refused because the mob already has a trauma of that type (`can_gain_trauma` in `code/modules/mob/living/brain/brain_item.dm:620` rejects a duplicate).

### Fix

Commit "Fix issue 97873: removing Obsessed from the antag panel also cures its brain trauma": `code/modules/antagonists/obsessed/obsessed.dm` gets an `admin_remove` override that remembers the datum's `trauma`, calls the parent, and then `qdel`s the trauma if it still exists. The trauma's own `on_lose` handles the rest of the cleanup.

### Verification

On the fixed build: add Obsessed, `admin_remove`: trauma nil, antagonist nil, zero traumas. Repeated twice, re-adding after removal worked each time (trauma and antagonist back, 4 objectives each time). Note: `admin_add` itself needs a crew member with a client as its target, so the re-add was done with `gain_trauma` with an obsession preset, which is the same path the antagonist uses.

## #84926: reviving as a podperson drops the obsession (fixed)

### The bug

An Obsessed player who dies and is revived as a podperson no longer gets the mood effects of their obsession (the positive or negative mood from being near or far from the target). The reporter did not check the trauma, and a commenter guessed that the trauma was lost while the antagonist stayed. It was initially thought to share a cause with #67767, but it has a different one.

### Reproduction

On master, Lua editor on the test server (proc-level, not the full pod flow):

1. Make human C Obsessed (trauma plus antagonist).
2. Call `C.mind:transfer_to(D)` for a new human D, which is what the replica pod does.
3. Result: D had no antagonist and no trauma; C's corpse kept the trauma.

### Cause

The obsession is a brain trauma attached to the body. When the mind leaves a body, `on_mind_lost` (`code/datums/brain_damage/creepy_trauma.dm`, about line 201) only stores the antagonist datum in the old trauma; nothing gives the new body a trauma. The replica pod's harvest (`code/modules/hydroponics/grown/replicapod.dm:193`) creates a brand new human and calls `mind.transfer_to(podman)`, so the trauma stays on the corpse and the new podperson has neither the trauma nor the antagonist. Without the trauma, nothing runs the obsession's per-tick mood logic (`on_life`).

### Fix

Commit "Fix issue 84926: obsession carries over when revived as a podperson":

- `creepy_trauma.dm`: new proc `transfer_to_new_body(new_body)`. It creates a new obsessed trauma on the new body with the same obsession, creeping time and "witnessed death" state, hands it the existing antagonist datum (so the objectives are kept) through a new `carried_antagonist` variable, then cures the old trauma silently (`lose_text` emptied, antagonist link cleared first so the datum is not cleaned up).
- `on_gain` uses the carried datum when there is one and skips `forge_objectives`, `greet` and the "developed an obsession" log line in that case.
- `on_lose` now uses `owner.mind?.remove_antag_datum(...)`, because it runtimed when the owner had no mind (found while testing).
- `replicapod.dm`: before `mind.transfer_to(podman)`, remember the old body's obsessed trauma; after `hardset_dna`, call `old_obsession?.transfer_to_new_body(podman)`.

The explicit transfer was chosen over the suggestion in the issue ("apply the trauma to the new mob with the obsessed datum") in that form: it keeps the same antagonist datum and its objectives instead of generating a new set. It is only wired into the replica pod; other paths that move a mind into a fresh body were not looked at.

### Verification

Using the real path on the fixed build: the test character was made Obsessed (target a named crewmember), died, its blood data was put into a replica pod seed in a tray, and the seed was harvested. Afterwards the character was a podperson (`/datum/species/pod`) with:

- one obsessed trauma on the new body, and the Obsessed antagonist with the same datum (`trauma.antagonist == antag` true) and its 4 objectives;
- the same obsession target;
- the old corpse's trauma nil;
- the creeping mood event applied by `on_life` when the target was in view;
- no new objectives screen greeted.

The runtime log showed one runtime from the test setup (an empty tray), none from the obsessed code.

## Not checked

- The unit tests were not run (the trauma-granting test blacklists obsessed).
- The full admin panel UI for #97873 (the Lua call to `admin_remove` was used).
- Other mind-to-new-body transfers (cloning, other revival routes, shapeshifting) were not examined for the same trauma loss.

## Notes

- #84926 is not a duplicate of #67767; they share a theme ("trauma and antagonist desync") but they have different causes and fixes. #97873 is a third, admin-side desync.
- A commenter on #67767 proposed moving Obsessed fully into the antagonist system, since anything that cures brain traumas cures it. This was not attempted. The fixes here only keep the existing two-part design from drifting apart. The airborne mind restoration virus mentioned in that comment was not tested.
