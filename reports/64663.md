## Summary

The beam between a lightning guardian and its summoner is deleted when either end is not on a turf, for example inside a bluespace body bag, and nothing ever recreated it. On master the chain simply stays gone, as a 2023 comment on the issue said; the 2022 symptom of a chain stretching to the end of the z-level did not appear. The fix recreates the chain when the guardian or summoner moves onto a turf on the same z-level while the guardian is deployed and alive.

## The bug

The issue says that putting a lightning guardian in a bluespace body bag made its lightning go "off into the horizon". On current master the guardian's chain to its summoner disappears when the guardian is bagged and does not come back when it is let out. The same happens if the summoner is bagged. Recalling and re-manifesting the guardian restores the chain, which is the only workaround.

## Reproduction

On master, using the Lua editor (the guardian and bag were set up by admin calls, not by a player using the guardian creator and bag):

1. Start the round and spawn a human and a `/mob/living/basic/guardian/lightning`.
2. Call `set_summoner(human, TRUE)` and `manifest(TRUE)` on the guardian. It got a `summoner_chain`.
3. Spawn a bluespace body bag, open it, and `forceMove` the guardian into it. Inside the bag the guardian's position read x=0, z=0 and `summoner_chain` was a nil chain.
4. `forceMove` the guardian back onto a turf. The chain component was marked destroyed, `summoner_chain` stayed nil and no chain was recreated.
5. The same applies when the summoner is moved into the bag.

The "extends to the end of the z-level" symptom was not observed.

## Cause

`datum/beam/proc/redrawing` deletes the beam when the endpoints are on different z-levels (`code/datums/beam.dm:140`: `... || origin.z != target.z`). A movable inside a container has z 0, so the beam is deleted. The damage chain component listens for its beam's deletion (`code/datums/components/damage_chain.dm:55`, `end_beam` at `:73`) and deletes itself.

The lightning guardian only created its summoner chain in `manifest_effects` (`code/modules/mob/living/basic/guardian/guardian_types/lightning.dm:33`, `summoner_chain = chain_to(summoner, max_range = INFINITY)`), so after the chain died nothing made a new one. The stale `summoner_chain` variable also kept pointing at the deleted component.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 64663: restore lightning guardian's summoner chain after leaving a container", changes `lightning.dm` only:

- Moves chain creation into a new `create_summoner_chain()`, called from `manifest_effects`, which also registers `COMSIG_QDELETING` so `summoner_chain` is nulled when the chain goes away.
- In `manifest_effects`, registers `COMSIG_MOVABLE_MOVED` on the guardian and on the summoner. The handler `on_position_changed` returns early if a chain exists, there is no summoner, the guardian is dead or not deployed, or either end is not on a turf or the two are on different z-levels. Otherwise it recreates the chain.
- `clear_chains()` unregisters those movement signals.

The fix was chosen over changing the beam code so that other beam users keep their behavior. It recreates only the summoner chain, not enemy chains.

## Verification

In one run on the test server, checked through the Lua editor:

- manifested: chain present
- guardian into bag: chain nil
- guardian out of bag: chain present
- summoner into bag: nil; summoner out: present
- recall: nil; manifest: present
- bag, recall, then manifest: chain present

The runtime log showed no runtimes. A later `death()` test on the guardian also killed the summoner, which is normal guardian behavior.

Not checked: enemy chains (not restored, by design), and what a real client sees.

## Notes

- Enemy chains are not restored after a container trip; a maintainer may or may not want that.
- The 2022 "to the end of the z-level" symptom was not reproduced; the beam code has changed since, and the 2023 comment agrees with what was seen here.
