## Summary

The issue says an active Perceptomatrix helm, which claims immunity to "ear, eye, or even brain damage", did not stop nearsightedness from eye damage. On master the helm does block the nearsighted and blind effects of eye damage: while it is worn and active, the nearsighted severity is 0 and no blindness is applied. The issue was filed in December 2024, so it may have been fixed since; the shallow clone had no history to confirm that. One leftover difference remains: the underlying status effects and `is_blind()` still report true, so the "Blind" HUD alert still shows. No fix was committed.

## The bug

As reported: a player wearing an activated helm drank Aiuri (which damages eyes), or had their eyes damaged otherwise, and still became nearsighted. A comment on the issue says the helm should prevent all forms of eye damage. The reporter gave no client version or test merge information.

## Reproduction

The reporter's steps (wear the activated helm, damage the eyes, get nearsightedness) did not reproduce on master.

1. Start a round on master and observe.
2. Using the Lua editor, spawn humans. Equip one with `/obj/item/clothing/head/helmet/perceptomatrix/functioning` (the activated helm) and keep one without as a control.
3. Call `apply_organ_damage(35)`, then `apply_organ_damage(60)`, on the eyes (`ORGAN_SLOT_EYES`), with and without the helm. Also test the helm put on after the damage, and removed again.
4. Read `has_status_effect` for nearsighted and blindness, `is_nearsighted_currently()`, `is_blind()` and the nearsighted effect's `get_severity()`.

Observed:

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Control, no helm, damage 35 | nearsighted effect present, current severity 1 (`is_nearsighted_currently()`), `get_severity()` 3, not blind |
| Control, no helm, damage 60 | blind, severity 3 |
| Helm worn first, damage 35 | effect present but current 0, severity 0, not blind |
| Helm worn first, damage 60 | effect present but current 0, severity 0, `is_blind()` true (status effect present, overlay path disabled) |
| Damage first, then helm | helm on: current 0, severity 0; helm removed: current 1, severity 3 (restored) |

So the helm suppresses the visible nearsighted effect, whether worn before or after the damage.

The on-screen overlay itself was not looked at (the screen listing did not show fullscreen overlays even in the control case); this conclusion rests on the severity values and on the code.

## Cause

The helm grants `TRAIT_SIGHT_BYPASS` when its core is installed (`code/modules/clothing/head/perceptomatrix.dm:45`). Three eye-damage effects check that trait:

- Nearsightedness: `should_be_nearsighted()` returns `NEARSIGHTED_DISABLED` when the owner has the trait (`code/datums/status_effects/debuffs/vision/nearsighted.dm:61`).
- Blindness: `update_blindness()` only calls `make_blind()` when the owner lacks the trait (`code/datums/status_effects/debuffs/vision/blindness.dm:41`).
- Eye blur: `code/datums/status_effects/debuffs/screen_blur.dm:48` checks it as well.

The helm's description (`perceptomatrix.dm:4-5`) promises immunity to "ear, eye, or even brain damage", and for the vision effects above that holds on master.

## Fix

None. Nothing was changed.

## Verification

Only the checks listed under Reproduction. Not checked: the Aiuri path specifically (eye damage was applied directly with `apply_organ_damage`); the rendered overlay and the HUD alert in a client; eyes being stabbed out; ear and brain damage.

## Notes

- Residual behaviour, not changed because it looks like a design choice: with `TRAIT_SIGHT_BYPASS` the status effects stay, so `is_nearsighted()` and `is_blind()` still return true. The "Blind" HUD alert still shows (seen in the screen listing for the helm wearer), and gameplay checks that use `is_blind()` (emotes, stripping, food, phobias, riding and similar) still treat the wearer as blind. The same applies to other sources of `TRAIT_SIGHT_BYPASS`, such as echolocation and the fugitive hunter goggles. A maintainer could decide whether the helm should also clear those.
- The report is probably out of date; the history needed to find the change that fixed it was not available.
