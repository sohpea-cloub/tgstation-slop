# #47021 / #57140: Explosions and gun internals (not reproduced), and grilles under windows (fixed)

| | |
|---|---|
| Issues | [#47021](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/47021): not reproduced. [#57140](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/issues/57140): verified and fixed |
| Status | #47021 needs no change; #57140 fixed on a branch with one commit |
| Fix branch | [fix/47021](https://github.com/sohpea-cloub/tgstation-slop/tree/fix/47021) (contains only the #57140 fix) |
| Tested on | tgstation master `87b93338`, 2026-10-23 |

## Summary

#47021 (2019) said explosions deleted the cell, firing pin and attachments inside a kinetic accelerator or other gun while the gun survived. On master no case of a surviving gun losing an internal part was found in 30 loaded accelerators, plus energy guns, across light and heavy blasts. Internals are lost only when the gun itself is destroyed, which is by design. #57140 was reproduced: every object on a turf is hit by an explosion separately, so a grille under an intact full-tile window takes the same damage as a bare one, and the fix reduces the bomb damage a grille takes to a quarter while such a window is on its tile.

## The bug

**#47021.** The reporter lost PKA mods, attachments and sometimes the power cell to gibtonite blasts, and comments said the same happened to cells in energy guns and to firing pins. An early commenter could not reproduce it, and later comments (2019, 2022) said the cell, and the firing pin, could still be destroyed.

**#57140.** Explosions near windows break the grilles under them. A comment says acid does the same to a grille under a window. A reply points out that the glass is hit as well; it just does not protect the grille.

## Reproduction

All steps used the Lua editor and SDQL2 on the test server. Explosions were created by calling `explosion()` from Lua, not by detonating real bombs or gibtonite.

**#47021 (not reproduced).**

1. Spawned 30 kinetic accelerators, each loaded with a firing pin, power cell, lens, seclite, combat knife, and damage and range modkits. Energy guns were also spawned.
2. Set off a light explosion on the epicenter turf: 12 of 12 guns, with their cells, were intact.
3. Set off a heavy explosion:
   - at the epicenter, all 30 guns were destroyed, and the cell, pin and modkits went with them (the seclite and knife dropped to the floor);
   - one tile away, 9 of 30 guns were destroyed, and their internals were destroyed with them;
   - no case was seen of a living gun that had lost its cell, pin or modkits.
4. Had humans holding an accelerator and an energy gun at the epicenter and 1 to 4 tiles away take a heavy blast. The guns were dropped but the guns and their cells were intact.

**#57140 (reproduced).**

1. Placed a `/obj/structure/window/reinforced/fulltile` and an `/obj/structure/grille` on each of 12 turfs, 1 to 3 tiles from the epicenter.
2. Called `explosion(epicenter, 0, 0, 6)` (light impact only).
3. Master result: windows were at 84 to 150 of about 150 integrity. Grilles were at 20 to 50 of 50; 8 of 12 were at or below 32, and 6 were at 20 to 24, which is at or below the break threshold (`integrity_failure` 0.4 of 50 is 20), so they were broken. (In Lua `g.broken` is 0 or 1 and 0 is truthy, so it has to be compared with `== 1`.)

## Cause

**#47021.** Nothing in the gun code makes the internals vulnerable. An explosion only reaches the contents of the objects on the epicenter turf, as `code/controllers/subsystem/explosions.dm:433-446` shows:

```dm
if(explode == epicenter)
	var/list/items = list()
	for(var/atom/holder as anything in explode)
		if (length(holder.contents) && !(holder.flags_1 & PREVENT_CONTENTS_EXPLOSION_1))
			items += holder.get_all_contents(ignore_flag_1 = PREVENT_CONTENTS_EXPLOSION_1)
```

Humans have `PREVENT_CONTENTS_EXPLOSION_1` (`code/modules/mob/living/carbon/human/human_defines.dm:15`), so what they hold or carry is not exploded separately. No gun overrides `contents_explosion`. On a heavy blast a gun on the floor at the epicenter is hit twice, once as part of the turf contents and once through the `items` list. Two rolls of `rand(100, 250)` (`code/game/objects/obj_defense.dm:24`) exceed the gun's integrity every time in the test (30 of 30). When the gun is destroyed, `/obj/item/gun/Destroy()` (`code/modules/projectiles/gun.dm:130-134`) deletes the pin and the chambered round, and the other parts go with the gun. The loss is therefore tied to the gun's destruction. A cell separately only gets `corrupt()` on a heavy or light blast (`code/modules/power/power_store.dm:242-254`): it loses half its charge and capacity, and with some chance becomes corrupted, but is not deleted. That path was not part of the tests.

**#57140.** `/obj/ex_act` (`code/game/objects/obj_defense.dm:10-28`) is called separately for each object on the turf, and a light blast deals `take_damage(rand(10, 90), BRUTE, BOMB, 0)`. A grille has `max_integrity = 50` and a bomb armor of 10 (`code/game/objects/structures/grille.dm:16, 24-29`). Having a window on top changes nothing, so the grille takes damage as if it were bare. A light blast does enough to bring it to or past its break threshold.

## Fix

**#47021:** none.

**#57140:** one commit, "Fix issue 57140: grilles under an intact fulltile window take reduced explosion damage", adding an override of `run_atom_armor` in `code/game/objects/structures/grille.dm`. When `damage_flag == BOMB`, and a non-deleted `/obj/structure/window` with `fulltile` set is on the grille's turf, the damage is multiplied by 0.25. Anything else, including bare grilles and non-bomb damage, goes through the normal path.

The quarter factor copies what cables do under floor tiles (`/obj/structure/cable/run_atom_armor` in `code/modules/power/cable.dm:167-170`, which uses 0.25 when the cable has `TRAIT_UNDERFLOOR`). It was chosen over full immunity so that a big blast, or one that destroys the window first, still breaks the grille, which matches the reporter's remark that the grille still breaks when the window does.

## Verification

#57140, after rebuilding and starting a fresh round: 27 window and grille pairs and 27 bare grilles, `explosion(epicenter, 0, 0, 8)`:

- windows averaged 136 of 150 and none were destroyed;
- grilles under windows: none broken, average integrity 44.2 of 50;
- bare grilles: 8 broken, average integrity 30.3.

An earlier run with 7 pairs also showed grilles at 30 to 41, none broken. No grille runtimes were logged.

Not checked:

- Heavy and devastating blasts on shielded grilles. By the numbers in the code a heavy blast can still destroy one (25 to 62 damage was recorded at heavy impact). This is intended but was not retested after the fix.
- Acid (the comment on #57140). `acid_act` was not changed and is a separate path.
- Directional (non-fulltile) windows. Only `fulltile` windows count.
- Real in-game bomb sources such as welding tanks or gibtonite.
- For #47021: a gun at the epicenter of a *devastate*-severity blast, a gun being thrown or fired, and other attachment types besides the ones listed. The PKA "mod capacity not shown" remark in the issue was not looked at.

## Notes

- The shield test iterates objects on the grille's turf each time a grille takes bomb damage. This is cheap, but it is the only cost added.
- The result depends on explosion processing order: the objects on a turf are handled in list order, so if the window is destroyed first in the same blast, the grille is then hit with full damage.
- #47021 can probably be closed. The behaviour reported in 2019 (cell deleted, gun alive) is gone; destroying a gun deletes its parts. If anything remains to look into, it is that a heavy blast reaches a floor gun twice, and that cells can be corrupted by blasts without being destroyed. Neither was changed.
