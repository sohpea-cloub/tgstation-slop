# #74678: Buildmode "Restore Roundstart Air Contents" gives space turfs real gas mixtures

| | |

## Summary

The mapgen buildmode block "Restore Roundstart Air Contents" replaced the air of every open turf in the selected region with a freshly built regular gas mixture, space turfs included. Space normally holds an immutable, empty mixture. After the restore it held real finite gas at 2.7K, so it exchanged gas and heat with neighbouring turfs, which is the likely source of the bogus temperatures and breaches in the report. The fix makes the module skip space and air-less turfs and reset the other turfs' existing mixtures in place.

## The bug

An admin refilled a station with the buildmode "Restore Roundstart Air Contents" block. Afterwards the arrivals hallway lost its windows and its floors melted, the shuttle docks did the same, other areas had small breaches, and an air alarm was involved. Centcom was unaffected. In the comments, one person who tried a very large region got division-by-zero errors with a gas mixture at temperature 0 and volume 200, and described other errors they thought came from space turfs being made active. A maintainer commented that giving space non-space gas mixtures looked wrong.

## Reproduction

I did not reproduce the player-visible breaches. What I reproduced is the wrong mixture on space turfs, by running the same map generator module that the buildmode block uses, not by clicking through buildmode.

1. On the test server, as an observer with the Lua editor, turn the turf at (107,146,2) into `/turf/open/space/basic`.
2. In Lua, create a `/datum/map_generator/repressurize`, define its region as (104,143) to (110,149), and call `generate()`. This runs the same repressurize module that the buildmode block runs.
3. Inspect the space turf's `air` with View Variables.

Observed on master:
- Before the run the space turf's air was `/datum/gas_mixture/immutable/space` with 0 moles.
- After the run it was a `/datum/gas_mixture/turf` at 2.7K holding 104 moles, at about 0.93 kPa pressure (the figure reported was P=0.93).
- A normal floor in the region came out correct, at 293.15K and 101.3 kPa.

The NaN and zero-temperature division errors from the issue were not reproduced. The agent noted that this probably needs a very large region.

## Cause

`code/modules/procedural_mapping/mapGeneratorModules/helpers.dm:15-18`, in `repressurize/generate()`:

```dm
for(var/turf/open/T in map)
	if(T.air)
		T.air = T.create_gas_mixture()
	SSair.add_to_active(T, TRUE)
```

Space is a `/turf/open`, and it has an `air` datum: the shared immutable `space_gas` (`code/game/turfs/open/space/space.dm:58`). `create_gas_mixture()` (`code/modules/atmospherics/environmental/LINDA_turf_tile.dm:81`) builds a regular `/datum/gas_mixture/turf` from the turf's `initial_gas_mix`, using the turf's own temperature when that differs from the default. For space the temperature is `TCMB` (2.7K), so the result was a real O2/N2 mixture at 2.7K. Space turfs were then also made active. Space therefore became a finite source and sink of real gas and heat that it shares with neighbouring turfs. This matches the comment on the issue that space should not get non-space mixtures.

I did not trace how this leads to temperature 0 and the division by zero. That link is inferred from the comments, not shown.

## Fix

One commit: "Fix issue 74678: buildmode "Restore Roundstart Air Contents" gave space turfs real gas mixtures". It changes `repressurize/generate()` so that it:

- skips turfs with `blocks_air`, space turfs and turfs with no `air` (`continue`);
- for every other turf, parses the roundstart mix from `initial_gas_mix`, copies it into the turf's existing air with `copy_air`, resets `temperature` to its initial value and calls `update_visuals()`;
- still calls `SSair.add_to_active(T, TRUE)` for these turfs.

This follows what the Fix Air verb does. Resetting in place, rather than replacing the air datum, leaves immutable mixtures alone. Skipping on `isspaceturf` and on `!T.air` was the narrowest way to exclude the turfs that must keep their own air. Other approaches were not tried.

## Verification

After rebuilding, the same Lua test ran again. Before running it, a floor in the region was drained to 10.4 moles at 900K.

- The floor came back to 104 moles, 293.15K and 101.3 kPa.
- The space turf kept `/datum/gas_mixture/immutable/space`, with 0 moles and 2.7K.
- The runtime log showed no runtimes.

Not checked: a large region such as the whole station, the issue's original errors, the arrivals and docks breaches, the buildmode UI itself (the generator was driven from Lua), and behaviour on other maps.

## Notes

- Turfs that are skipped no longer go through `add_to_active`, since the `continue` comes first. That is intended for space, but it also applies to `blocks_air` and air-less turfs.
- An upstream PR, #74682, tried the same idea and was closed without merging.
- A comment on the issue suggests making gas mixture variables protected and using setters and getters outside atmospherics code. That is a broader change and was not attempted.
