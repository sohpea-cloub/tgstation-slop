## Summary

Cyborgs received taste messages from reagents they were exposed to (pepper spray smoke), because only carbons carry the "no tongue" `TRAIT_AGEUSIA` and silicons never had it. The fix gives all silicons `TRAIT_AGEUSIA`, as they already have `TRAIT_ANOSMIA`. Separately, a mining cyborg's ore satchel stopped collecting ore once it was deactivated into the model, because `dropped()` unregistered its pickup signals. The fix skips that unregistering while a cyborg's satchel sits in its model. A third report, that a dead borg can be revived with an electrically charged arm, could not be reproduced on this master.

## #90918: Borgs can taste Scorching Agony

### The bug

A cyborg standing in smoke made from pepper spray got a taste message ("You can taste scorching agony.") despite having no tongue. The reporter noted it might work with other reagents.

### Reproduction

1. On the test server, spawn a cyborg (`/mob/living/silicon/robot`) with the Lua editor and give it a mining model (`model:transform_to`).
2. Create a reagent holder: `SS13.new('/datum/reagents', 100)`, add 10 units of `/datum/reagent/consumable/condensedcapsaicin`.
3. Expose the borg to it: `holder:expose(borg, 64)` (the INHALE method).

Observed on master: the chat showed "You can taste scorching agony." This was done by calling the exposure directly, not by actually releasing smoke.

### Cause

`/mob/living/expose_reagents()` calls `taste_list(reagents)` for any exposure with INGEST or INHALE (`code/modules/mob/living/living_defense.dm:795`). `taste_list()` returns early only if `check_tasting_blocks()` is true, and that proc blocks when the mob has `TRAIT_AGEUSIA` (`code/modules/mob/living/taste.dm:56-58`). Humans without a tongue get that trait (`code/modules/mob/living/carbon/human/human.dm:61`), but nothing gave it to silicons. `/mob/living` has a default `get_taste_sensitivity()`, so a borg tasted normally. `silicon.dm` already sets `TRAIT_ANOSMIA` (`code/modules/mob/living/silicon/silicon.dm:81`) but no taste counterpart.

### Fix

Commit "Fix issue 90918: silicons can no longer taste reagents" adds `ADD_TRAIT(src, TRAIT_AGEUSIA, INNATE_TRAIT)` directly after the `TRAIT_ANOSMIA` line in `silicon.dm`. This covers every silicon (cyborgs, AIs, pAIs) and every path through `check_tasting_blocks()`. No other approach was considered.

### Verification

On a fresh round with the fix built, a new cyborg's `check_tasting_blocks()` returned true. The borg had no client, so no chat output could be read; the check was of the trait's effect, not a repeat of the capsaicin exposure. Not checked: the exposure repro after the fix, real smoke, AIs and pAIs.

## #91704: Borg satchels now need holding to work

### The bug

A mining cyborg's mining satchel only collects ore while it is in the active module slots. Activating it once and storing it back used to keep it working. A commenter confirmed it used to work that way, and another said it was still occurring in October 2025. The reporter suspected [PR #90927](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/pull/90927).

### Reproduction

Not reproduced on master before the fix. The first attempt did not find the satchel in the Lua editor, and the cause was then established from the code. After the fix the behaviour was checked (see Verification).

### Cause

`/obj/item/storage/bag/ore/equipped()` registers a movement signal on the holder and entry signals on its turf (`code/game/objects/items/storage/bags.dm`, around line 124). `dropped()` (`bags.dm:133`) unregisters them and clears `listening_to`. A cyborg deactivating a module calls `deactivate_module()`, which does `transferItemToLoc(item_module, newloc = model)` (`code/modules/mob/living/silicon/robot/inventory.dm:54`). That moves the satchel into the model and calls `dropped()`, so the satchel stops listening. Per the tracker analysis, this follows PR #90927 (borg inventory moved to storage); that attribution is from reading the code, not from checking the PR.

### Fix

Commit "Fix issue 91704: cyborg ore satchel keeps collecting while stored" adds an early return at the top of `dropped()`, after the `listening_to` check:

```dm
if(iscyborg(listening_to) && istype(loc, /obj/item/robot_model))
	return
```

Signals are therefore kept while a cyborg's satchel is in its model. Other holders are unaffected.

### Verification

On a post-fix build with the cyborg as a miner: the satchel was activated, deactivated, then reactivated, and a diamond ore spawned on the borg's tile was picked up ("puts the diamond ore into the cyborg mining satchel"). The satchel was then deactivated again (back in the model) and plasma ore spawned; it was also picked up. Not checked: the unfixed build in game (no failure was observed there), walking onto ore, and how the satchel behaves when the cyborg changes model or is deleted.

## #92139: Reviving cyborgs with a charged arm

### The report

The reporter said a dead borg could be revived by repairing it and hitting it with the borg baton (electrically charged arm). A comment attributed it to EMP forcing a status update. The reporter also described a revived borg getting ghost vision and, later, being ghosted out of their body; they could not reproduce those.

### Attempt

On the test server a borg was killed with `death()`, healed to 100, then hit with `emp_act(1)` and `emp_act(2)`, and given 60 stamina loss (what the borg stun item does). Its `stat` stayed DEAD (3), including after the 16-second unconscious period ended.

### Code reading

`/mob/living/silicon/robot/update_stat()` (`code/modules/mob/living/silicon/robot/robot.dm:648`) only handles dying and never revives. `emp_act()` (`code/modules/mob/living/silicon/robot/robot_defense.dm:382`) only applies `Unconscious()`. The issue was probably fixed after July 2025, or depends on something these checks did not cover. A check with the real charged arm on a dead borg, in a round, was not done.
