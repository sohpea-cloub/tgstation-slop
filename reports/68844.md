# #68844: Mobs cannot move away from a ladder in zero gravity

| | |
|---|---|

| Status | Fix committed and checked in game |

## Summary

A mob standing on a ladder in a weightless tile (for example the top of a jacob's ladder that ends in space) could not move at all, because `get_spacemove_backup` skipped the non-dense ladder and found nothing to push off. The fix adds ladders next to lattices as objects that count as a push-off point. The "pulled back to the ladder and ascend" part of the report was not reproduced.

## The bug

The report describes climbing a jacob's ladder into space, trying to move away, and being "pulled back to the ladder" and ascending another z level. A comment adds that closing the radial menu with the X counts as a left click and climbs.

What happened on master: a mob that climbed to a ladder in space was stuck in place. Movement attempts were refused, with no drifting and no climbing.

## Reproduction

1. On the test server, as an admin, use the Lua editor to give the character the traits `no_breath`, `resist_cold` and `resist_low_pressure` (godmode was avoided: with `TRAIT_GODMODE` set, healing and revival did nothing).
2. Teleport to a space turf on the station z-level, spawn an `/obj/item/jacobs_ladder` there and call `attack_self` on it. This produced ladders on the three z-levels reached, with a mid ladder (up and down) on the lavaland level and an end ladder in space on the top level.
3. Left-click the ladder to climb: station level, then lavaland, then the space level (a radial menu appears on the middle ladder; "Up" climbs).
4. On the space level, try to move west.

Observed on master: the move was refused ("0 of 1 steps, blocked"). The mob could not leave the ladder tile.

Not reproduced: being pulled back to the ladder, and the comment's claim that the radial's close button climbs. Choosing "Close Menu" on the middle ladder (tried on the lavaland and space levels) closed the menu and did not climb. That comment may be about older radial code.

## Cause

`/mob/proc/get_spacemove_backup` in `code/modules/mob/mob_movement.dm:306` looks for something a weightless mob can push off. For objects it skips anything that is non-dense and passable, except lattices (`:336` on master):

```dm
var/pass_allowed = rebound.CanPass(src, get_dir(rebound, src))
if(!rebound.density && pass_allowed && !istype(rebound, /obj/structure/lattice))
	continue
```

A ladder is non-dense, so it was skipped. The ladder tile in space has no gravity and no lattice, so `Process_Spacemove` found no backup and refused the move.

## Fix

**Fix issue 68844: let mobs push off ladders in zero gravity.** The condition gains `&& !istype(rebound, /obj/structure/ladder)`, with a comment that lattices and ladders can be held onto. This treats a ladder the way a lattice is already treated, and does not alter gravity handling or the ladder's climbing code.

## Verification

Same steps as above, with the server rebuilt with the fix, and the radial menu closed with "Close Menu" first: moving west on the space-level ladder succeeded ("1 of 1 steps", from 34,130 to 33,130 on the space level).

Not checked: normal lattice and gravity movement after the change (it was expected to be unchanged but not run); a ladder that is not part of a jacob's ladder; drifting behaviour after the push-off beyond the first step.

## Notes

- Because the fix is in the shared spacemove check, every ladder now counts as a handhold in zero gravity, including mapped ladders on space tiles. That seems intended, but a maintainer may prefer to restrict it.
- The "pulled back" symptom and the comment about the close button were not reproduced, so the issue may have two causes that have since been partly fixed or changed; only the stuck-in-place part is accounted for.
