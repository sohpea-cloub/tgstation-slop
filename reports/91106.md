## Summary

Three mech issues were looked at together. #91106: when a rigged power cell explodes inside a mech, the cell is deleted but the mech keeps a reference to it, so the mech behaves as if it still had a cell. The fix makes the mech drop its references to stock parts (cell, scanning module, capacitor, servo) whenever one leaves it. #91586: a strafing mech could be turned to face a diagonal direction, which gives it a roughly 270-degree firing arc; the fix restricts strafing mechs to cardinal facings. #91276 (DNA lock lost after toggling maintenance) could not be reproduced.

## #91106: phantom cell after a rigged cell explodes

### The bug

A mech that has a plasma-rigged power cell installed blows the cell up when it draws power. The mech survives, but afterwards the crowbar cannot take a cell out of it, because the mech still lists the destroyed cell as installed. The issue reporter noted that removing the scanning module made the "cell" disappear, because the part list is rebuilt after a crowbar removal. They also suspected the same applies to other stock parts.

### Reproduction

1. On the test server, using the Lua editor, spawn a Durand and put it on the floor.
2. Add 5 units of plasma to the mech's installed cell's reagents (View Variables or Lua).
3. Call the mech's `use_energy(100)` from Lua (this stands in for the pilot moving, which is what draws power; the player action itself was not performed).

Observed on master: the message "The solution violently explodes!" appeared, the cell was queued for deletion (its `gc_destroyed` was set), yet the mech's `cell` variable still pointed at it. The mech itself survived (integrity 400).

### Cause

`/obj/item/stock_parts/power_store/proc/try_explode` ends in `qdel(src)` (`code/modules/power/power_store.dm:214`). The mech holds its parts in plain variables (`cell` at `code/modules/vehicles/mecha/_mecha.dm:51`, `scanmod` at line 53, and similarly `capacitor` and `servo`) and has no `Exited` handler to clear them when a part is deleted or moved out. `crowbar_act` builds its list of removable stock parts from those variables (`code/modules/vehicles/mecha/mecha_defense.dm`, around line 415), so it saw the stale cell. The variables are only refreshed (`locate_parts`) after a crowbar removal, which is why removing the scanner made the phantom cell vanish.

### Fix

Commit "Fix issue 91106: mechs clear their stock part references when a part leaves them" adds `/obj/vehicle/sealed/mecha/Exited` in `_mecha.dm`. When the departing object is the mech's `cell`, `scanmod`, `capacitor` or `servo`, it sets that variable to null and calls `update_part_values()`; for the cell it also calls `diag_hud_set_mechcell()`. Handling `Exited` covers deletion and any other way a part leaves the mech, rather than special-casing the cell explosion.

### Verification

After rebuilding and repeating the same Lua steps, the mech's `cell` was nil after the explosion.

Not checked: the crowbar interaction itself after the fix (the `cell` variable was read directly), the other stock parts leaving the mech, the diagnostic HUD update, and the full player flow with a syringe.

## #91586: strafing mechs can face diagonally

### The bug

With strafe on, holding two direction keys (or using the forced-rotate path) turned the mech to a diagonal facing. The firing check accepts a target when `get_dir` to it shares a bit with the mech's `dir`, so a diagonal facing accepts the north, east and north-east sectors, a firing arc of about 270 degrees.

### Reproduction

1. Spawn a Durand, enable strafe, and face it south.
2. From Lua, call `vehicle_move(5, 1)` (north-east with `forcerotate` set; the Alt-held path goes through the same branch).

Observed on master: the mech's `dir` became 5 (north-east). A plain strafing move without forced rotation kept the mech's existing facing. This was reproduced by calling the proc, not by pressing keys.

### Cause

In `vehicle_move` (`code/modules/vehicles/mecha/mecha_movement.dm:115`), `if(dir != direction && (!strafe || forcerotate || keyheld)) setDir(direction)` is passed a diagonal `direction` since diagonal movement is allowed (only the Clarke disallows it). The firing check is at `code/modules/vehicles/mecha/_mecha.dm:693` (`dir_to_target & dir`).

### Fix

Commit "Fix issue 91586: strafing mechs can only turn to cardinal directions":

- In `vehicle_move`, when strafing with a diagonal `direction`, the turn direction is the mech's current facing if that is one of the two components, otherwise the north/south component.
- In `toggle_strafe` (`mecha_actions.dm:115`), turning strafe on while facing diagonally snaps `dir` to its north/south component.

The north/south preference when neither component matches the current facing is an arbitrary choice.

### Verification

With Lua `vehicle_move` calls on a strafing Durand, starting facing south: NE gave north, SE gave south, SW gave south, NW gave north; facing north, a north-type diagonal kept north; facing west, NE gave west (kept the cardinal component). A mech left with `dir` 5, then strafe toggled off and on, ended at north (1). A plain strafing move kept the facing.

Not checked: real key input (including Alt-hold), the firing arc in game, and non-strafing mechs (the change is gated on `strafe`).

## #91276: DNA lock lost after toggling maintenance (not reproduced)

The report says that after DNA-locking a Clarke and toggling maintenance mode (with upgrades), the owner could no longer enter.

Tried on master with a Durand via Lua: set `dna_lock` to a human's `unique_enzymes`, opened the panel with `screwdriver_act`, used `crowbar_act`, closed the panel with `screwdriver_act`. `dna_lock` was unchanged after each step, and `mob_try_enter` by the human succeeded ("climbs into the Durand!").

Code reading agrees: `dna_lock` is only written in `code/modules/vehicles/mecha/mecha_ui.dm` (around lines 204-212, the `dna_lock`/`reset_dna` actions) and `work_tools.dm` (around line 388, mech combine); the panel and crowbar handlers in `mecha_defense.dm` never touch it.

Not tried: a Clarke, the maintenance toggle through the UI, upgrades being installed, a non-occupant using the DNA buttons (the Mecha tgui shows them in the maintenance view), and the reporter's drag-into-mech entry method. A cause in one of those paths remains possible.

## Notes

- The `Exited` approach also covers other ways a stock part can leave a mech (for example, being moved by admin or other code), but nothing besides the cell explosion was exercised.
- #91586: whether strafing mechs should be limited to four directions is a design call; the issue's commenter suggests a near-270-degree arc is unintended.
