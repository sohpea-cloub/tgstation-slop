# #78436: Items thrown by a lava-immune golem standing in lava catch fire

## Summary

Throwing an item first drops it onto the thrower's own turf, and the throwing datum that lava checks for ("this item is flying over me") only exists later. A golem standing in lava therefore sets everything it throws on fire at the moment of the drop, even though the lava code is meant to ignore thrown items. The fix marks the item with a new `TRAIT_BEING_THROWN` during that drop and makes lava ignore items that have it.

## The bug

A golem is immune to lava. As described in the issue, it can stand in lava, smelt volcanic ash into glass with its internal forge, and throw the glass onto the ground nearby. The stack catches fire as it leaves, burns the golem's hands if picked up, and turns to ash. A maintainer's comment points out that a comment in the lava code says a thrown item should not burn.

## Reproduction

On master, using the Lua editor as an admin observer:

1. Change a turf (here 108,144 on z-level 2) to `/turf/open/lava/smooth/lava_land_surface`.
2. Spawn a human, set its species to `/datum/species/golem`, put a player in control of it, and place it on the lava tile.
3. Give it 10 glass sheets and make it throw them (`throw_item`) at a floor turf 4 tiles west.

Observed: the glass landed on the floor with `resistance_flags` 44 (`ON_FIRE | FLAMMABLE | ACID_PROOF`), i.e. burning, and the golem took burn damage.

Caveat: that golem had been spawned on the lava as a plain human and caught fire before its species was set, so the golem's damage may be partly from that. The glass flags are the clearer evidence, and the post-fix run (below) used a stricter order and showed the golem not on fire. The throw was made through `throw_item` from admin tools, not by a player clicking.

## Cause

`/obj/item/proc/on_thrown` (`code/game/objects/items.dm:1339`) calls `user.dropItemToGround(src, silent = TRUE)` before the throw starts. The `throwing` datum is only assigned later, in `throw_at` (`code/game/atoms_movable.dm:1465`). Dropping the item onto lava triggers the turf's `Entered`, which calls `apply_lava_effects` and then `burn_stuff`, and `can_burn_stuff` (`code/game/turfs/open/lava.dm:283-286`) decides whether the item is "flying over" the lava:

```dm
if((burn_target.movement_type & MOVETYPES_NOT_TOUCHING_GROUND) || burn_target.throwing || !burn_target.has_gravity()) //you're flying over it.
	return LAVA_BE_IGNORING
```

At that moment `throwing` is still null, so the item is treated as lying in lava: `do_burn` marks it flammable and calls `fire_act`. The golem is lavaproof, but the item it throws is not.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 78436: items thrown from lava no longer ignite as they are dropped", changes four files:

- `code/__DEFINES/traits/declarations.dm` and `code/_globalvars/traits/_traits.dm`: a new `TRAIT_BEING_THROWN`, with a comment saying it marks an item for the instant it is dropped in order to be thrown.
- `code/game/objects/items.dm`: `on_thrown` computes whether the throw is gentle (pacifist with a throwforce item, or `TRAIT_NO_THROWING`; this reuses the condition that was already there), adds the trait (source `TRAIT_GENERIC`) around `dropItemToGround` unless gentle, removes it right after the drop, and uses the same `gentle` value for the "You set it down gently" branch.
- `code/game/turfs/open/lava.dm`: `can_burn_stuff` also ignores items with the trait.

A trait was chosen because the throwing datum cannot be created before the drop without restructuring the throw code. A gentle "throw" is just setting the item down, so it is still treated as lying on the turf.

## Verification

On the test server with the fix, in the Lua editor:

- A golem was spawned off lava, its species set, and then moved onto the lava tile. It was given 10 glass and threw it west: the golem was not on fire, the glass had `resistance_flags` 32 before and after (no `ON_FIRE`, no `FLAMMABLE`), and it landed on the iron floor 4 tiles away.
- Control: a glass stack dropped (not thrown) on the lava tile was destroyed by the lava.
- Control: glass thrown from the lava tile onto another lava tile landed there and caught fire (a fire overlay was seen on it).
- Runtime log: no new runtimes, except one test artifact: when a second stack was dropped onto the lava tile while the first was still there, it merged into the first and was deleted mid-throw ("Qdeleted thing being thrown around", `atoms_movable.dm:1392`). A clean re-run with a single stack produced no runtime.

Not checked: the pacifist / no-throwing path in game (its code path is the original condition, now stored in a variable); real players throwing by clicking; picking up the glass; and the icebox plasma river mentioned in the issue.

## Notes

- Any other lava-immune or lava-standing thrower (not just golems) benefits, since the check is on the item. The trait is also available to other turfs that test `throwing`.
- The issue suggests golems could instead get heat protection on their hands; that would not stop the item from igniting on the lava tile and was not tried.
