# #59961: Syndicate shuttle turrets keep shooting a mech that is mid-Skyfall

| | |
|---|---|

| Status | Fixed, checked on the test server |
| Fix branch | [fix/59961](https://github.com/sohpea-cloub/tgstation-slop/tree/fix/59961) |

## Summary

Turrets scan every mech in `GLOB.mechas_list` without checking whether the mech can currently be hit. A Savannah-Ivanov using Skyfall becomes non-dense and invisible but stays in that list, so syndicate infiltrator turrets keep targeting and shooting it. The syndicate shuttle turret also queues three follow-up shots 0.5, 1 and 1.5 seconds after the first and never rechecks the target before firing them. The fix skips non-dense mechs when scanning and makes the queued follow-up shots recheck the target before firing.

## The bug

The report describes a Savannah-Ivanov that gets into line of sight of a syndicate infiltrator turret, uses Skyfall to jump into the shuttle's interior, and is still shot by the turret, which turns to track it. The mech dies, and the other turrets do not fire at the pilot once they are inside the shuttle. A comment on the issue says syndicate turrets seem to like targeting mechs.

What happens in the code: the mech is in the air during Skyfall (not dense, alpha 0), but the turret's targeting does not take that into account. The turret keeps shooting at it, and shots already queued are fired even after the mech has moved out of the turret's sight.

## Reproduction

The Skyfall jump itself could not be steered reliably enough on the test server to land inside a hull on demand (the jump landed in place on three attempts), so the reproduction sets the state Skyfall creates by hand rather than playing the jump through.

1. On the test server, build a test area: a flat plating area with a ring of wall tiles (hull walls, interior 5x5) to stand in for the shuttle.
2. Spawn a `/obj/machinery/porta_turret/syndicate/shuttle` at about 3 tiles from a `/obj/vehicle/sealed/mecha/savannah_ivanov` on the open plating, in the turret's view, outside the ring. Put a godmode human in the mech so it is not killed during the test (the mech's integrity was also raised to roughly 990000 for the repro).
3. With the Lua editor, confirm the mech is dense: the turret fires and the mech's `atom_integrity` falls.
4. Test B (the bug): using the Lua editor, call `set_density(0)` on the mech and set `alpha = 0`, which is what Skyfall does at launch. Sample `atom_integrity` once a second for 5 seconds. Before any fix, it kept dropping at about 62 per second (987755, 987693, 987631, 987600, 987507, 987445). Restore density and alpha afterwards.
5. Test A (control): force-move the mech to a tile inside the wall ring. The damage stopped after about 2 seconds, once `can_see` failed. The three follow-up shots queued by the last targeting still fired without a recheck. This part was read from the code and from the test; the number of follow-up shots that landed was not counted.

Not reproduced as described: the issue's wording that the turret "twists its neck" and the other turrets then do not fire. That part was not tested.

## Cause

`code/game/machinery/porta_turret/portable_turret.dm:523` scans mechs with

```dm
for(var/A in GLOB.mechas_list)
	if((get_dist(A, base) < scan_range) && can_see(base, A, scan_range))
		var/obj/vehicle/sealed/mecha/mech = A
```

It only checks distance and `can_see()`. `can_see()` (`code/__HELPERS/atoms.dm:61`) is a rough step-towards walk that its own comment calls "horribly inaccurate"; it only checks for opaque turfs. Nothing checks density.

Skyfall (`code/modules/vehicles/mecha/combat/savannah_ivanov.dm:167-171`) sets `flying = TRUE`, `set_density(FALSE)` and animates `alpha` to 0, but the mech remains in `GLOB.mechas_list`, so it stays a valid target.

The syndicate shuttle turret override of `target()` (`portable_turret.dm:879-885`) calls `shootAt(target)` immediately and queues three more `shootAt(target)` timers at 0.5, 1 and 1.5 seconds. These do not recheck whether the target still exists, is still visible or is still hittable.

Separately, the shuttle's penetrator rounds (`code/modules/projectiles/projectile/bullets/sniper.dm:80`, `projectile_phasing = ~(PASSMOB|PASSVEHICLE)`) pass through walls by design. So a hull does not stop a shot that has been fired; only the turret's decision to fire matters. This was left unchanged.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 59961: syndicate shuttle turrets ignore non-dense mechs", changes `portable_turret.dm`:

- The mech scan loop now iterates typed `mech`s and skips any mech with `!mech.density`, so a mech in Skyfall is not selected as a target.
- The three delayed shots in the shuttle turret's `target()` now call a new `follow_up_shot(target)` proc instead of `shootAt`. It returns without firing if the target is deleted, is no longer dense, or `can_see(base, target, scan_range)` fails; otherwise it calls `shootAt(target)`.

Using density as the test covers Skyfall without special-casing the Savannah-Ivanov type, and also covers any other state in which a mech is not hittable. Making the penetrator round stop at walls was not tried, because the phasing is deliberate.

## Verification

On the test server, with a shuttle turret, a Savannah-Ivanov 3 tiles away and a godmode pilot in it, after the fix:

- Mech dense: the turret fired and the mech's integrity fell from 450 to 78 ("The Savannah-Ivanov is hit by .50 BMG aggression dissuasion round").
- Test B: `set_density(false)` and `alpha = 0`: integrity was 78 at the start and still 78 after about 11 seconds, with no new hit lines. Before the fix it fell about 62 per second.
- Control: `set_density(true)` and `alpha = 255`: hits resumed within 3 seconds, the mech was destroyed, and the pilot dropped out and was shot.

No runtimes came from the change; the only error seen was a Lua editor error about a destructed callback from reading the destroyed mech.

Not checked: a real Skyfall played end to end into a hull; other turret types; the follow-up-shot recheck in isolation (the density check was exercised, the deleted-target and `can_see` branches were not individually tested).

## Notes

- Skipping non-dense mechs applies to all turret types that use this scan, not only syndicate ones.
- `can_see()` remains crude, so a turret with a window or a doorway in line can still see through; it was not changed.
- Because the penetrator phases through walls, a dense mech that is inside the hull but visible to `can_see()` can still be shot through the hull. That was left as designed.
