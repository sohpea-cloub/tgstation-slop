## Summary

The three issues are three separate defects in the projectile code, all fixed on one branch.

- #43324: the "point blank" shortcut in `/obj/projectile/proc/fire()` impacted the clicked target without checking for a directional window between shooter and target.
- #95061: `/turf/Enter()` returned `PHASING` after `Bump()` had already teleported a projectile through a portal, so the interrupted step dragged the projectile back onto the portal's tile.
- #92900: a non-dense floor decal such as stabilized plasma or blood became the projectile's original target when clicked, and clicked non-dense objects are always hit, so the decal absorbed the shot.

Each fix is small and local; the #95061 fix is in the generic `/turf/Enter()`.

## The bug

### #43324

A mob standing on the same tile as a directional (non-fulltile) window can be hit by a taser fired from the tile on the window's blocking side. The reporter describes a taser stunning someone through a window on the same tile, and a commenter describes being chain-stunned after jaunting onto a protected table behind windows. The issue's video was not viewed; the behaviour below was reproduced from the text.

### #95061

A projectile that hits a portal does not come out of the other end. It is teleported, then ends up back at the portal it entered and flies on from there along its original line. A commenter in the issue gave the same call chain that we found.

### #92900

Shooting at a tile that holds stabilized plasma (or blood) spends the shot on the decal ("The stabilized plasma is hit by a laser, without leaving a mark!") instead of on the object or mob standing on the same tile, for example glowing resin. The issue also complains that the stabilized plasma sprite is made of tiny, nearly invisible pixels, which makes it hard to click. That second complaint is an art or mouse-opacity matter and was not touched.

## Reproduction

All shots were fired through the Lua editor, by creating a projectile, calling `aim_projectile()` and then `fire()`. They were not fired by a player clicking with a gun. For #43324, `fire(null, target)` was used because that is the call the gun firing code makes (`code/modules/projectiles/ammunition/_firing.dm:96`, `loaded_projectile_cache.fire(null, direct_target)`).

### #43324

1. Using the Lua editor, spawn a human (the target) and an `/obj/structure/window` on the same tile with `dir = NORTH` (not fulltile, anchored, dense).
2. Spawn a second human (the shooter) on the tile directly north of the target, next to the window's blocking side.
3. Create an `/obj/projectile/energy/electrode` with the shooter as `firer` and `fired_from`, call `aim_projectile(target, shooter)` and then `fire(null, target)`.
4. Result on master: "Brenna Schmidt is hit by an electrode in the left leg!" The target was hit through the window.
5. For comparison, with the shooter three tiles north, the shot ended with "The window is hit by an electrode, without leaving a mark!", so the window blocked it correctly. Only adjacent shots were affected.

### #95061

1. In the Lua editor, call `create_portal_pair()` with the entrance at (102,143) and the exit at (110,145), on a clear row of the map.
2. Spawn a laser projectile at (98,143) aimed east, and register a `movable_moved` signal handler on it to record its movement.
3. Fire it. Result on master: `... 100,143->101,143 | 101,143->110,145 | 110,145->102,143 | 102,143->103,143 ...`. The projectile arrived at the exit tile, was immediately moved back to the entrance tile, and continued east along row 143 as if it had never been teleported.

### #92900

1. In the Lua editor, spawn a glowing resin node and a `/obj/effect/decal/cleanable/plasma` (stabilized plasma) on the same tile, and a shooter three tiles away.
2. Create a laser, call `aim_projectile(decal, shooter)` (this is what clicking the decal does) and fire it.
3. Result on master: the glowing resin was untouched (integrity still 15) and the decal absorbed the shot.

## Cause

### #43324

`/obj/projectile/proc/fire()` has a shortcut for point-blank shots (`code/modules/projectiles/projectile.dm:786`):

```dm
if (direct_target && (get_dist(direct_target, get_turf(src)) <= 1)) // point blank shots
	impact(direct_target)
```

`impact()` is called directly, so nothing that normally blocks a projectile is consulted. A directional window blocks by way of `CanAllowThrough()`/`on_exit()` during movement, and that movement never happens here. A shooter next to a target on a directional window's tile therefore hits the target through the window. When the shooter was three tiles away the projectile moved normally and the window stopped it, which matches the observation above.

### #95061

When a projectile enters a turf, `/turf/Enter()` (`code/game/turfs/turf.dm:434-466`) calls `mover.Bump(first_bump)` for the first blocking object and then returns `(mover.movement_type & PHASING)`, i.e. "succeed only if the mover is phasing". For a portal, the sequence is:

- `/obj/effect/portal/projectile_hit()` (`code/game/objects/effects/portals.dm:140`) calls `teleport()` and returns `BULLET_ACT_FORCE_PIERCE`.
- `process_hit_loop()` in `projectile.dm` (around line 569) responds to a force pierce by setting `PHASING` on the projectile.
- `Bump()` returns to `/turf/Enter()`, which now sees `PHASING` and returns TRUE. BYOND completes the move onto the portal's tile, even though the projectile has already been teleported to the exit.

The projectile is therefore pulled back to the portal tile after being teleported, which is the "rubberbanding" described in the issue comments. The phasing loop earlier in the same proc (`turf.dm:452`, `mover.Bump(thing)` for phasing movers) has the same flaw.

### #92900

Floor decals use `CLEANABLE_FLOOR_OBJECT_LAYER` (`code/__DEFINES/layers.dm:195`, `18 + TOPDOWN_LAYER`), and they are mouse-opaque, so a click on such a tile selects the decal as the topmost clickable thing. `aim_projectile()` stores it as `original` (`projectile.dm:1257`). In `can_hit_target()` (`projectile.dm:624`) a non-dense, non-living target is only hit when it is the directly clicked target (`projectile.dm:655-657`), so the decal is hit and the projectile is spent. The layer check just above it (`target.layer < hit_threshhold`, with `PROJECTILE_HIT_THRESHHOLD_LAYER` at 2.75, `layers.dm:236`) does not exclude decals, because floor-plane objects have layers of 10000 and above. Stabilized plasma is defined at `code/game/objects/effects/decals/cleanable/mess.dm:317`; blood and other cleanable decals behave the same way.

## Fix

The branch has three commits, one per issue.

**"Fix issue 43324: point blank projectiles ignore border objects like directional windows".** The point-blank shortcut now also requires `get_turf(src).Adjacent(direct_target, direct_target, src)`. `Adjacent()` checks dense border objects with the projectile as the mover. When it fails, the projectile skips the shortcut and moves and bumps normally, so the window absorbs the shot. Shots from the open side still take the shortcut.

**"Fix issue 95061: projectiles bounced back through portals instead of teleporting".** `/turf/Enter()` now remembers `mover.loc` at entry. After `Bump()` (both the phasing-loop call and the `first_bump` call) it returns FALSE if the mover was deleted or its location changed, so the move ends where the portal put the projectile. The change is in a generic turf proc but only affects movers whose `Bump()` relocates them; a projectile piercing a portal is the case that was found.

**"Fix issue 92900: lasers and bullets aimed at floor clutter were absorbed by it".** Two changes in `projectile.dm`:

- `can_hit_target()` returns FALSE for `/obj/effect/decal/cleanable`, after the density check, even when the decal is the clicked target.
- `aim_projectile()` retargets `original` when the target is such a decal: to the first living mob on that tile, otherwise the topmost non-effect object that is not under the floor. This keeps the shot aimed at the clicked tile, so the object under the decal is hit instead of the shot sailing past. If nothing else is on the tile, `original` stays the decal, which can no longer be hit.

The retargeting keeps the shot heading for the clicked tile; making the decal unhittable alone would leave `original` pointing at a decal that can no longer be hit.

## Verification

All checks used the Lua editor on the test server, with the three fixes built in.

- #43324: five shots were fired. With the window on the target's tile facing north, a shooter adjacent on the north side and one three tiles north both got "The window is hit by an electrode" (blocked). A shooter adjacent on the south (open) side, one adjacent to the east, and one diagonally adjacent (north-east) all hit the mob, which is correct.
- #95061: the same laser through the same portal pair now moves `... 101,143->110,145->111,145->112,145->113,145`, and the target at (113,145) took 25 burn damage.
- #92900, with a laser: resin plus stabilized plasma on one tile, aimed at the decal, destroyed the resin and left the decals. A mob standing on the plasma was hit and became `original`. Plasma alone with a mob three tiles behind it let the shot through and hit the mob. Resin plus plasma plus blood retargeted `original` to the glowing resin (pipes under the floor were not chosen) and destroyed it.

Not checked:

- No shot was fired by a player with a real weapon by clicking; all were fired with the Lua editor, so the click-to-`original` step and the gun firing path were not exercised for the three bugs directly.
- #43324 was checked with a taser projectile only (stamina damage was not informative, so results were read from chat lines), not with other projectile types, and not with diagonal or multi-window layouts beyond the NE case above.
- #95061 was checked with a laser and a `create_portal_pair()` portal; hand teleporter or portal gun portals, other projectile types and moving portals were not tried. Other kinds of movers that relocate themselves in `Bump()` were not tested.
- #92900 was checked with lasers only; bullets use the same code but were not fired. The sprite and click-target complaint was not addressed.
- No runtimes related to these changes were seen. No unit tests were run.

## Notes

- The `/turf/Enter()` change is the one most worth a maintainer's attention, since it is a core turf proc that every movement goes through. It only changes behaviour when `Bump()` has moved or deleted the mover, but that reasoning was not tested for non-projectile movers.
- The #92900 fix applies to every `/obj/effect/decal/cleanable` (blood, glass shards, oil, and so on), not only stabilized plasma. Projectiles can no longer hit such decals at all, so effects that rely on a projectile hitting a cleanable decal would change. Whether any exist was not checked.
- Issue #95061 notes that a projectile "technically works for one tile", for example when someone is shot as they step through a portal. This matches the cause: the projectile is teleported and then moved back.
- The #92900 issue's second complaint (tiny, near-invisible sprite pixels making the decal hard to click) remains open.
