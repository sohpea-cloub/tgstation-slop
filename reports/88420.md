## Summary

Liquid dark matter (LDM) and sorium are meant to be used up by their own vortex when made without a stabilizing agent, but the full amount stayed in the beaker. Their `on_reaction()` removes the product, but for a normal (non-instant) reaction it runs once at the start of the reaction, before any product exists, so the removal removes nothing. The fix does the removal and the vortex in `reaction_finish()` instead, using the amount actually produced. The same branch also fixes #89568 (spawned warm donk pockets had omnizine at purity 0.5, halving its overdose threshold). #95008 (ghoul powder plus zombie powder causing permanent unconsciousness) could not be reproduced.

## #88420: LDM does not deplete itself

### The bug

Mixing stable plasma, radium and carbon without a stabilizing agent pulls everything nearby toward the container (the vortex), as intended, but the LDM that was just made is still in the beaker afterwards. The reporter expected it to be removed. Sorium, defined next to it, has the same flaw.

### Reproduction

1. On the test server, as an admin, spawn a large beaker (`/obj/item/reagent_containers/cup/beaker/large`).
2. Using the Lua editor, add 30u each of stable plasma, radium and carbon to its reagents.
3. Wait about 15 seconds and read the beaker's `reagent_list` (type and volume of each reagent).

Observed on master: the vortex fired, and the beaker held 90u of liquid dark matter afterwards.

Sorium (mercury, oxygen, nitrogen, carbon) uses the same code path. It was checked only after the fix (see Verification).

### Cause

The LDM recipe (`code/modules/reagents/chemistry/recipes/pyrotechnics.dm:320`) and the sorium recipe (`:297`) are ordinary equilibrium reactions, not `REACTION_INSTANT`. For those, `code/modules/reagents/chemistry/holder/reactions.dm:98` calls

```
reaction.on_reaction(src, equilibrium, equilibrium.multiplier)
equilibrium.react_timestep(1)
```

so `on_reaction()` runs first, and the products are only created by the `react_timestep()` calls that follow. Both recipes' overrides (`pyrotechnics.dm:302` and `:325`) do

```
holder.remove_reagent(/datum/reagent/liquid_dark_matter, created_volume * 3)
```

at that point, when no LDM is in the holder yet, so nothing is removed. Instant reactions are not affected, because `instant_react()` (`reactions.dm:261`) calls `on_reaction()` after the products are added; that is why similar recipes such as flash powder and RDX work.

### Fix

Commit "Fix issue 88420: liquid dark matter and sorium reactions now consume the product they create" replaces both `on_reaction()` overrides with `reaction_finish()` overrides. `reaction_finish()` is called from `end_reaction()` (`reactions.dm:160`) with `reacted_vol`, the amount actually produced. The override calls the parent, returns early if the holder contains a stabilizing agent, removes `react_vol` of the product, and starts the vortex with range `clamp(sqrt(react_vol), 1, 6)`.

The alternative, setting `REACTION_INSTANT` on both recipes, was written first but was not the committed approach, because it would change the reaction kinetics. The reactions stay non-instant.

### Verification

After rebuilding, the same 30/30/30 test left the beaker empty after about 25 seconds, the vortex still fired (the "The solution begins to bubble" message appeared), and no runtimes were logged. A sorium test with 20u each of mercury, oxygen, nitrogen and carbon also ended with an empty beaker.

Not checked: the stabilizing-agent case (that LDM or sorium then stays and no vortex fires), and the vortex's pull range or strength compared with before.

### Notes

- Behavior change to weigh: the vortex used to fire at the start of the reaction; it now fires when the reaction finishes, a few seconds after mixing, and the product is in the container in the meantime. A stabilizing agent added mid-reaction now also prevents the vortex and keeps the product, which it did not before.
- The removed amount and range were `created_volume * 3` (LDM) and `created_volume * 4` (sorium) before; they now use the volume actually reacted, which is the amount of product made.
- The separate `ldm_vortex` and `sorium_vortex` recipes (which act on LDM and sorium later) were not changed.

## #89568: spawned warm donk pockets overdose omnizine at 15u

### The bug

A player overdosed on omnizine at 15.1u (the log line in the report), half the normal threshold of 30. The report's attack log shows the omnizine came from eating warm donk pockets, listed with 0.5 purity.

### Reproduction

1. Using the Lua editor, spawn `/obj/item/food/donkpocket/warm` on the test server.
2. Read its reagents.

Observed on master: omnizine 6u at purity 0.5 (nutriment and protein also at 0.5). The reduced purity is what halves the threshold (see Cause). An actual overdose in a mob was not run.

### Cause

The edible component defaults `reagent_purity` to 0.5 (`code/datums/components/food/edible.dm:63` and `:142`), and a food's `starting_reagent_purity` is `null`, which means "use the default" (`code/modules/food_and_drinks/food/_food.dm:62`, passed through at `:143`). Reagents created through the food's reagent list therefore get purity 0.5. When a reagent enters a mob, `code/modules/reagents/chemistry/reagents.dm:219` adjusts its threshold: `overdose_threshold += (creation_purity - initial(purity)) * threshold`, which takes omnizine's 30 down to 15 at purity 0.5.

Donk pockets that are baked or microwaved get their omnizine from `add_reagent_list` at purity 1 (`bakeable.dm:97`, `microwavable.dm:72`), so they were not affected. Warm pockets created directly (for example from the survival pod vendor in `survival_pod.dm:264`, and pinatas) were.

### Fix

Commit "Fix issue 89568: spawned warm donk pockets have full purity omnizine" sets `starting_reagent_purity = 1` on `/obj/item/food/donkpocket/warm` (`code/modules/food_and_drinks/food/donkpocket.dm`). Other foods, such as eggs and honeycomb, already set it the same way.

### Verification

After the rebuild, a spawned warm donk pocket had nutriment 3u, protein 2u and omnizine 6u, all at purity 1. Not checked: an actual omnizine overdose in a mob; that subtypes of the warm pocket inherit the value (they were not enumerated); other foods that carry medicine and use the default purity.

### Notes

The reporter said the overdose was not trivially reproducible; this identifies the purity mechanism and a source of the 0.5-purity pockets, but the report's round may have involved a different source that was not examined.

## #95008: ghoul powder plus zombie powder, unconscious forever

### The bug

The report says drinking ghoul powder and then injecting zombie powder (or putting 50u of each in a syringe) leaves the player unconscious and unrevivable indefinitely.

### Reproduction attempts

All on the test server with a spawned human, using the Lua editor, in this order:

1. Added 10u ghoul powder then 10u zombie powder directly to the mob: it recovered after about 60 seconds.
2. Transferred 10u ghoul powder by ingestion, then 10u zombie powder by injection, from a beaker: the mob fell limp after about 20 seconds and fully recovered after about 60 seconds (normal state, no fake-death traits left).
3. Injected 50u ghoul powder and 50u zombie powder in one transfer: the mob fell limp and was still faking death and drooling two minutes later, with about 39.6u of each left, draining roughly 2.4u per minute each, so about 16 more minutes were needed. The reagents were draining, so the effect is not infinite on this path.

Not tried: drinking powder at the 50u level, defibrillation or other attempts to revive a stuck mob, and a long wait for the 50u case to finish.

### Code reading

Zombie powder applies a fake-death status effect that is tied to the reagent in the blood (`code/datums/status_effects/reagent_effect.dm:36` checks only the mob's blood holder); ghoul powder applies the fake-death trait through `metabolized_traits`. Both are removed when the reagent leaves the system. The ingest-versus-blood detail (a zombie powder dose that stays in the stomach would not keep the effect alive) was noted from the code but not tested.

### Verdict

Not reproduced. The reporter may have hit a long duration (large doses metabolize slowly) or another factor from that round; the issue remains labeled "Needs Reproducing".
