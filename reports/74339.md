## Summary

An AI core that is swallowed by a clown car can never get out again. `/obj/vehicle/sealed/mob_exit()` skips the `forceMove` for every AI, a rule meant for mechs, so the AI stops being an occupant but stays inside the car, and the car is then deleted around it (#83622). Separately, a dead clown car driver cannot be removed, because the trunk only dumps `KIDNAPPED` occupants and the dead cannot use the climb-out or dump actions (#90257). Two commits fix this: sealed vehicles now move an AI out unless it is already outside, and opening the trunk also dumps dead occupants.

## The bug

**#74339.** When a clown car runs over an AI core, the AI is put inside it. Its "Climb Out" action then does nothing useful: after the exit attempt the action disappears and the AI is still inside the car. The clown stopping, hitting a wall, or opening the trunk does not free it. The issue's original steps involved a shell, but a commenter noted that no shell is needed.

**#83622.** Destroying a clown car that holds an AI kills or deletes the AI along with the car. The reporter described the AI-controlling-a-shell case as "shell is now a regular borg and original ai body is dead", and added that AI cores cannot leave clown cars.

**#90257.** If the driver of a clown car dies, nobody can get them out. The horn and lights still work, but the body stays in the car.

## Reproduction

All steps were done on the test server with admin tools (the Lua editor to call procs directly; the player actions themselves, such as being run over, were not performed).

**#74339 and #83622 (before the fix)**

1. Create an AI, spawn a clown car, and put the AI in the car with `mob_forced_enter(ai)`. The AI's occupant flag was 2 (`VEHICLE_CONTROL_KIDNAPPED`) and its actions were "Deploy to AI Shell", "Thank the Clown Car Driver" and "Climb Out".
2. Call `mob_try_exit(ai, ai)` (the climb-out path; it has a 6 second `do_after`).
3. Observed: the car's `occupants` list was empty and the "Climb Out" action was gone (actions: "Deploy to AI Shell" only), but `ai.loc` was still the clown car. Nothing could free it, since `dump_mobs()` iterates `occupants`.
4. Repeating the setup and calling `take_damage(1000, "brute", "melee", FALSE)` on the car deleted the AI together with the car (a Lua call on `ai.loc` afterwards failed with "a destructed userdata method was called").

**#90257 (by code only before the fix)**

The pre-fix behaviour was established by reading the code, not reproduced: the driver has `VEHICLE_CONTROL_DRIVE`, the climb-out and dump actions require `AB_CHECK_CONSCIOUS`, and the trunk only dumps `KIDNAPPED` occupants (see Cause). The fixed build was exercised directly (see Verification).

## Cause

`code/modules/vehicles/sealed.dm:92-100`, `mob_exit()`:

```dm
remove_occupant(M)
if(!isAI(M))//This is the ONE mob we don't want to be moved to the vehicle that should be handled when used
	M.forceMove(exit_location(M))
else
	return TRUE
```

The exception exists for mechs: `mecha`'s own `mob_exit()` handles an AI itself (sending it back to its core or shunting it) and then calls `..()`, so the generic code must not pull the AI out of wherever it was sent. For any other sealed vehicle, including the clown car, the AI is removed from `occupants` and its actions are removed, but `forceMove` is skipped and the proc returns, so the AI's `loc` stays the car. Because it is no longer an occupant, `dump_mobs()` and `dump_specific_mobs()` cannot find it, and when the car is destroyed the AI is deleted with its contents (#83622). (The issue thread had a similar diagnosis: the AI-in-vehicle code assumed the AI always arrived by a malf mech jaunt.)

#90257: `code/modules/vehicles/cars/car.dm:48-57`, `attack_hand()`, only dumps kidnapped occupants:

```dm
if(return_amount_of_controllers_with_flag(VEHICLE_CONTROL_KIDNAPPED))
	to_chat(user, span_notice("The people stuck in [src]'s trunk all come tumbling out."))
	dump_specific_mobs(VEHICLE_CONTROL_KIDNAPPED)
```

The driver has `VEHICLE_CONTROL_DRIVE`, so opening the trunk skips them. The climb-out action (`code/modules/vehicles/vehicle_actions.dm:200-207`) and the driver's dump action require `AB_CHECK_CONSCIOUS`, so a dead driver cannot use either. Only a crash (`dump_mobs`) would remove them.

## Fix

- **Fix issue 74339: AIs swallowed by a clown car can climb out again** (`sealed.dm`). `mob_exit()` now returns early for an AI only when it is no longer inside the vehicle (`if(isAI(M) && M.loc != src) return TRUE`); otherwise it moves the AI to `exit_location()` like any other occupant. This keeps the mech behaviour, where the mech's own `mob_exit()` has already moved the AI, and fixes every other sealed vehicle. It also covers #83622 for the plain case: a destroyed car dumps the AI out through the normal path.
- **Fix issue 90257: dead clown car drivers can be pulled out of the trunk** (`car.dm`). Opening the trunk now collects every occupant that is `KIDNAPPED` or has `stat == DEAD`, and removes each with `mob_exit(occupant, randomstep = TRUE)`, paralyzing carbon occupants for 4 seconds (as the existing dump does). The "all come tumbling out" message is shown when anything was dumped. Making the climb-out action usable while dead was not considered, since dead mobs should not act; pulling the body out from the outside matches how other dead occupants are handled.

## Verification

After rebuilding:

- **#74339:** a new car and an AI, forced in, then `mob_try_exit`. Chat showed "AI drops out of the clown car!", the AI's location was a floor tile, the car's `occupants` was empty, and `ai.loc == car` was false.
- **#83622:** a car destroyed with an AI inside printed "AI drops out of the clown car!" and the AI was alive (not qdeleted, stat 0) on the floor next to the car.
- **#90257:** a human driver (occupant flag 1, `DRIVE`) was killed (stat 3). Another human called `attack_hand` on the car; after 3 seconds "<name> drops out of the clown car!" appeared and the body left the car.
- The server log showed no runtimes except one caused by a misuse of the `QDELETED` macro in the test's own Lua code.

Not checked: the real player route (running over an AI core with the car, then pressing "Climb Out" in the UI) was replaced by direct proc calls; the #83622 case of an AI deployed into a shell when the car is destroyed (shell deployment was not exercised, so whether the shell survives or stays under control is untested); mechs were not retested after the `sealed.dm` change (the code path is unchanged for AIs already outside the mech, but this was argued from the code, not tested); other sealed vehicles.

## Notes

- The `M.loc != src` test is the narrowest change that keeps the mech exception. A cleaner alternative would be to move the AI handling entirely into the mech's `mob_exit()` and drop the special case in `sealed.dm`; this was not attempted.
- #90257 dumps dead occupants of any role, not only the driver, and takes 3 seconds (the existing trunk `do_after`).
- The reporter's original suggestion that the AI should take over the car with a clown lawset (comment on #74339) was not addressed.
