## Summary

The three issues are unrelated and were handled as one batch; each has its own commit.

- **#50524**: the deep fryer accepts a held mob (`/obj/item/mob_holder`) as an item to fry. When the holder is taken out of the fryer it lands on a turf, which releases the mob unharmed and deletes the holder, so a pet can be put through the fryer and freed indefinitely. The fix blacklists `mob_holder` in the fryer and prints "You can't deep fry a living creature!".
- **#72798**: the collision check that regenerates randomized recipes (the secret sauce) compares reagents only with reagents and catalysts only with catalysts, so a random recipe that uses a reagent which is another recipe's catalyst (for example enzyme) is not seen as clashing, and the other recipe fires first. The fix adds a second check that treats reagents and catalysts alike.
- **#76649**: food created by food chemical reactions, by processing (cutting, slicing), and by replacing a base item when customizing (bun to burger and so on) is always created on the floor under the source. The fix adds a helper that moves the result into a free hand of the mob holding the source, or into the storage (such as a serving tray) containing it.

## The bug

### #50524

Players pick up a pet (Ian, any corgi, and so on), put it in a deep fryer with oil, and take it out. In 2020 the fried "holder" item could be bitten, which released the animal each time. Since then deep frying was refactored (#71551 replaced the deep-fried holder object with the `fried_item` element, which makes the fried item itself edible), and a 2023 comment on the issue says the problem remained. On current master the held-mob item is still accepted by the fryer, and the animal always comes out alive and unharmed, so the loop is endless.

### #72798

The secret sauce recipe is generated randomly each round (from a list of reagents and catalysts). The reporter found that grape juice and universal enzyme produced grappa before the sauce could be made, so the secret recipe could not be completed with the stated ingredients. The generator is supposed to reroll a random recipe whenever it collides with a normal one; for this kind of collision it does not.

### #76649

Chemical reaction foods (tofu, cheeses, bread, and so on) appear on the floor under the beaker and go dirty if the player is not quick. Some custom foods (burger, pizza, sushi, bread, onigiri) are also created on the floor when crafted instead of in the maker's hand, and slicing or flattening food that is sitting on a serving tray drops the result on the floor instead of leaving it on the tray.

## Reproduction

### #50524

The unpatched behaviour was shown by calling the fryer's procs from the Lua editor, not by clicking through the player action.

1. Spawn a deep fryer and add 50 units of cooking oil to it.
2. Spawn a human, create a mob holder for Ian (`/obj/item/mob_holder` with Ian as the held mob), and put it in the human's active hand.
3. Call `item_interaction` on the fryer with the human and the holder as arguments.
4. The holder is accepted: the fryer plays its immerse sound and the frying animation, and `fryer.frying` is the holder.
5. Set the fryer's `cook_time` high enough and take the holder out with `attack_hand`. According to the investigation notes, the holder is moved to the turf, `mob_holder/Moved` releases Ian ("Ian uncurls!") and the holder is deleted. Ian is intact. The Lua return values for this step could not be read (the holder was destroyed during the call), so the release is established from the notes and the code in `inhand_holder.dm`, not from a clean captured readout. The investigation also did not establish whether the holder itself had picked up the fried look or the edible component before being released.
6. Pick Ian up again and repeat from step 2 as often as wanted.

### #72798

A faithful reproduction of the report's ingredients was not possible, so the mechanism was reproduced instead.

1. On master, `grappa` requires wine 10 with enzyme 10 as a catalyst (`code/modules/food_and_drinks/recipes/drinks/drinks_alcoholic.dm:338-341`). Grape juice with enzyme as a catalyst gives vinegar (`food_mixtures.dm`, the `grape_vinegar` recipe). So the combination in the issue (grape juice plus enzyme gives grappa) does not occur on this build, and the secret sauce on a given round is random, so its real recipe was not known.
2. Using the Lua editor, the live secret sauce recipe datum was edited to reagents wine 10, enzyme 10, ice 2 (no catalysts), the way a random roll could produce it.
3. `chem_recipes_do_conflict(grappa, sauce)` returned 0 (no conflict), so the generator would have accepted that sauce.
4. A bucket with wine 10 and enzyme 10 bubbled and produced grappa ("The solution begins to bubble"), i.e. the other recipe fires before the secret one can be completed.

### #76649

Not reproduced on unpatched master. The cause was found from the code (results are created at `drop_location()`), and the fix was then exercised (see Verification). The reports in the issue (floor placement, dirty food) were not independently confirmed in game before the change.

## Cause

### #50524

`code/modules/food_and_drinks/machinery/deep_fryer.dm:108` (`/obj/machinery/deepfryer/item_interaction`) refuses indestructible items (lines 128-130), then checks `deepfry_blacklisted_items`, `GLOB.oilfry_blacklisted_items` (defined at line 2), storage items and items with `TRAIT_NODROP`, `ABSTRACT`, `DROPDEL` or `HAND_ITEM` (lines 132-136), and otherwise calls `start_fry` (line 138-139). `/obj/item/mob_holder` is on none of those lists, so it is fried like any other item.

When the fryer's `attack_hand` ejects the item with `forceMove(drop_location())`, `mob_holder/Moved` (`code/modules/mob/living/inhand_holder.dm:72-75`) sees `isturf(loc)` and calls `release()` (line 77-95), which puts the mob back on the floor, says "uncurls!" and deletes the holder. `mob_holder/Destroy` (line 31-35) also calls `release()`. Nothing ever damages or consumes the pet, so the cycle can be repeated without limit.

### #72798

`chem_recipes_do_conflict` (`code/__HELPERS/reagents.dm:5`) decides whether two recipes clash by checking that the shorter recipe's `required_reagents` is a subset of the longer one's `required_reagents`, and then that its `required_catalysts` is a subset of the longer one's `required_catalysts` (lines 47-59). Reagents are therefore only compared with reagents and catalysts with catalysts. A catalyst only has to be present in the container (it is not consumed), so an ingredient that a random recipe lists as a reagent satisfies another recipe's catalyst requirement just as well. In the example, grappa needs wine as a reagent and enzyme as a catalyst; a sauce with both as reagents has no catalysts, so the catalyst subset test fails and `FALSE` is returned.

`check_recipe_for_conflicts` (`code/_globalvars/lists/reagents.dm:84`) only looks up competitors under the new recipe's `required_reagents`, and the retry loop at line 143 uses it to decide whether to reroll a random recipe.

### #76649

All three code paths create the result at `drop_location()` and never place it anywhere else:

- `/datum/chemical_reaction/food/on_reaction` (`code/modules/food_and_drinks/recipes/food_mixtures.dm:95-99`) does `var/atom/location = holder.my_atom.drop_location()` and creates each result there.
- `/atom/proc/StartProcessingAtom` (`code/game/atom/_atom.dm:692`) does `new atom_to_create(drop_location())`. For a food on a serving tray, `drop_location()` is the tile under the tray, not the tray.
- `add_ingredient` in `code/datums/components/ingredients_holder.dm:204` creates the replacement item (for example the burger that replaces the bun) with `new replacement(atom_parent.drop_location())`, then deletes the original, so the replacement never takes the original's place in the hand.

## Fix

Three commits on the fix branch, one per issue.

**"Fix issue 50524: held mobs can no longer be deep fried"** (`deep_fryer.dm`). `/obj/item/mob_holder` is added to `GLOB.oilfry_blacklisted_items`, and `item_interaction` gets an explicit early check that prints "You can't deep fry a living creature!" and returns `ITEM_INTERACT_BLOCKING`. The explicit check gives the player a message; the blacklist alone returns `NONE` silently. Refusing the item is the smallest change that stops the loop; no alternative (for example actually harming the animal) was tried.

**"Fix issue 72798: randomized recipes also conflict with recipes that their reagents satisfy as catalysts"** (`code/_globalvars/lists/reagents.dm`). `check_recipe_for_conflicts` now looks up competitors under the union of the recipe's reagents and catalysts (skipping the recipe itself), and also accepts a competitor flagged by a new `chem_recipe_ingredients_trigger(competitor, reaction)`. The new proc returns TRUE when every reagent and every catalyst of the competitor is present in the other recipe, as either a reagent or a catalyst, with at least the required amount, unless the competitor is `REACTION_COMPETITIVE` or has a different required container. The old `chem_recipes_do_conflict` is unchanged, so the existing unit test `reagent_recipe_collisions` (which only calls it) is unaffected. `chem_recipes_do_conflict` was left as it is and a separate check added, so the existing comparison between normal recipes is unchanged.

**"Fix issue 76649: food made by reactions, processing and customizing goes to the hands or storage of the origin"** (`code/__HELPERS/atoms.dm`, `food_mixtures.dm`, `_atom.dm`, `ingredients_holder.dm`). A new helper `put_made_item_near(new_item, origin, replace_origin = FALSE)`:

- if the origin is held by a mob, the new item goes into a free hand (`put_in_hands`, which drops it on the mob's tile if there is no free hand); with `replace_origin` it takes the very hand that held the origin, because the origin is about to be deleted;
- if the origin is inside something with `atom_storage` (a serving tray), it is inserted there if it fits;
- otherwise nothing happens and the item stays where it was created.

It is called from the food reaction's `on_reaction`, from `StartProcessingAtom`, and from `add_ingredient` for the replacement item.

## Verification

All checks were done on the test server with the Lua editor, on the patched build.

**#50524.** A human picked up a corgi with `mob_pickup` and `fryer:item_interaction(human, holder, {})` was called. It returned 2 (`ITEM_INTERACT_BLOCKING`), `fryer.frying` stayed nil, and the corgi remained in the holder. Not checked: the chat message was not read back, and the refusal was not tried through a real click.

**#72798.**
- With a live secret sauce datum edited to specific reagents, a recipe of vodka (potato juice 10 with an enzyme catalyst of 5) was used as the competitor. The old check returned 0, and `chem_recipe_ingredients_trigger` returned 1.
- `check_recipe_for_conflicts` returned 1 for a sauce of potato juice 10 and enzyme 5, 0 for potato juice 10 and enzyme 2 (too little enzyme), and 0 for enzyme 5 and sprinkles 3 (a competitor reagent missing).
- The real generated sauce for the round passed (returned 0), and the game log had no "Couldn't regenerate" line, so the secret recipe still generates.
- Not checked: the exact reagents from the report (the wine plus enzyme case was shown only for the old build); the full unit test run (`reagent_recipe_collisions` was not built or run); repeated rerolls across many seeds. The new check ignores temperature (it is conservative), so it may reroll slightly more often than strictly necessary.

**#76649.**
- Soymilk plus enzyme in a held beaker: the tofu appeared in the human's other hand.
- The same beaker on the floor: the tofu stayed on the floor.
- The beaker inside a serving tray: the tofu appeared in the tray.
- Processing bread (`StartProcessingAtom` with time 0): on a tray, both slices went in the tray; held, one slice went to the free hand and the second fell to the floor, since there was no second free hand; on the floor, both slices stayed on the floor.
- Customizing: a held bun with meat added through `attackby` produced a burger in the same hand.
- Not checked: real clicks and do_after timing (time 0 was used); a hand-held tray; the other custom foods named in the issue (onigiri, pizza, sushi, salad, kebabs, soups, cakes, pies) were not each tried, only the bun to burger path; whether food stays clean on the tray was not observed.

A runtime appeared during the customizing test: "materials with non-positive values found in /obj/item/food/bun: meat = -400". It comes from `add_ingredient` moving the ingredient into the bun and then into the replacement item, which makes `food_exited` remove the ingredient's materials from the old bun. It is a separate bug from the change here, but the replacement flow is touched by the fix; it was not run on unpatched master to confirm it exists there.

## Notes

- Upstream: a pull request titled "Removes deep frying held mobs" (#75745) was found referencing #50524; it was closed, not merged, so the issue is still open on master.
- #72798: the report's screenshot suggests grape juice plus enzyme produced grappa, which does not match master's recipes (grape juice plus enzyme gives vinegar and grappa needs wine). Either the recipes changed since January 2023 or the secret sauce on that round used different reagents. The fix addresses the general reagent-as-catalyst class of collision, not that specific combination.
- #72798: the new `chem_recipe_ingredients_trigger` compares amounts exactly as listed, ignores temperature and pH, and can reroll a recipe that would not really have clashed in practice. The retry limit is unchanged (`MAX_RANDOMIZED_REACTION_RETRY_ATTEMPTS`); if no non-clashing recipe is found the random reaction is removed, as before.
- #76649: `put_made_item_near` also puts reaction results into a bag or any other storage that holds the beaker, if they fit. If the origin is carried but the holder has no free hand, the item still drops on the holder's tile as before. Results of food reactions inside machines (where `my_atom` is a machine) are unchanged.
- #76649: the issue says the problem got worse after #76018 (not examined here).
- #76649: where several results are created at once (one per unit of `created_volume`), the first fills a free hand and the rest end up on the floor.
- #50524: other ways to fry a living thing are not touched (for example dunking a pulled carbon into the fryer in `attack_hand`).
