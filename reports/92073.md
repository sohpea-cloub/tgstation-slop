## Summary

A player turned into a mining mob (a brimdemon through the polymorph belt, or a goliath, watcher and so on through the cursed spring) is treated by megafauna as one of their own, so megafauna never target or retaliate against them. For the belt the cause is a status-effect lookup that matches by ID: `hostile.dm` tries to exempt shapeshifted mobs from the faction check, but the belt's effect has a different ID than the one being looked for, so the exemption never applies. The fix adds a lookup that matches by type and uses it there and in `give_uplink()`, which had the same flaw. A second commit handles the cursed spring (and any other `wabbajack()`), which produces a new mob with no shapechange effect at all, by tagging the result with a new `TRAIT_POLYMORPHED`. That second commit is a design choice and is flagged for review.

## The bug

**#92073:** wear a polymorph belt that has scanned a brimdemon, transform, and spawn a colossus. The player can chew the colossus to death and it never fights back. The reporter says the same applies to other megafauna.

**#91507:** a player turned into an ancient goliath by the cursed spring on the ice moon could not get the wendigo to attack them. The reporter also guessed it might be exploitable with a polymorph belt, which turned out to be right (that is #92073).

Underneath both: megafauna (`/mob/living/simple_animal/hostile` subtypes) refuse to target anything that shares one of their factions. Mining mobs share the mining faction with megafauna, so a player in a mining mob's body inherits its factions and is ignored. Code meant to prevent this exists for shapeshifted mobs (added by the earlier fix #89592 for the same exploit), but it did not work for the belt.

## Reproduction

Done on Lavaland, using admin tools (the Lua editor for setup and for reading values).

**Cause 1: polymorph belt (#92073, and the belt steps of #91507)**

1. Spawn a dead brimdemon and a `/obj/item/polymorph_belt/functioning`. Scan the brimdemon with the belt by clicking it with the belt, as a player would, then wear the belt.
2. Click "Invert Polymorphic Field". The player became `/mob/living/basic/mining/brimdemon`.
3. Spawn `/mob/living/simple_animal/hostile/megafauna/colossus` four tiles away (the Lua editor was also used to give the player the traits needed to survive on Lavaland).
4. Bite the colossus several times.

Observed on master: five bites landed; the colossus's `target` stayed null and its AI stayed idle; it never attacked. Reading values through the Lua editor, `faction_check_atom(player)` was 1 (shared faction) and `CanAttack(player)` was 0. As a control, the same player as a plain human who punched the colossus was immediately attacked with "Judgement." and death bolts.

**Cause 2: cursed spring (#91507)**

1. Turn a turf into a cursed spring (`/turf/open/water/hot_spring/cursed`) and walk into it as a human with a mind. The log read "Your form morphs into that of a animal!" and the player was teleported to the station. In this run the result was a `/mob/living/basic/mushroom` with no status effects (`status_effects` null), which shows the spring does a plain `wabbajack()` without any shapechange effect. (It was not a mining mob, so the test continued another way.)
2. Call `wabbajack("animal")` on NPC humans until one became a `/mob/living/basic/mining/watcher/icewing` (it took five tries), then move the player's key into it. Reading values: `faction_check` was 1 and `CanAttack` was 0.
3. Hit the colossus ("You buffet the colossus!", health 2500 to 2485). Its `target` stayed null, its AI stayed idle, and it never retaliated.

The cursed spring turf itself was not shown to produce a mining mob in the sampled runs, so step 2 of this case reproduces the mechanism with a direct `wabbajack()` call on an NPC rather than the exact player action.

## Cause

**Belt and spell shapeshifts.** In `code/modules/mob/living/simple_animal/hostile/hostile.dm:260`, `CanAttack` has:

```dm
var/faction_check = faction_check_atom(L)
if(L.has_status_effect(/datum/status_effect/shapechange_mob))
	faction_check = FALSE
```

`has_status_effect` (`code/datums/status_effects/_status_effect_helpers.dm:79-85`) compares IDs, not types:

```dm
if(present_effect.id == initial(checked_effect.id))
```

The base effect has `id = "shapechange_mob"` (`code/modules/spells/spell_types/shapeshift/_shape_status.dm:5`), but its `/from_spell` subtype sets `id = "shapechange_from_spell"` (`_shape_status.dm:158`), and the belt's effect `/datum/status_effect/shapechange_mob/from_spell/polymorph_belt` (`code/modules/clothing/belts/polymorph_belt.dm:179`) inherits that. A lookup for the base type therefore never finds a belt or spell shapeshift. The exemption never fires, `faction_check` stays true, `CanAttack` returns false, and `FindTarget` finds nothing, both when the player is merely in sight and when they attack the megafauna. It might be assumed that the lookup also matches subtypes; it does not.

The same lookup appears in `code/datums/mind/antag.dm:122` (`give_uplink`), where a shapeshifted traitor would fail to find their caster mob and therefore get no uplink. No other callers of `has_status_effect(/datum/status_effect/shapechange_mob)` exist. Callers that pass `/from_spell` (`polymorph_belt.dm:128` and others) match IDs correctly. Hierophant's `CanAttack` override (`megafauna/hierophant.dm:453`) calls the parent proc first, so it inherits the fix.

**Cursed spring and other wabbajacks.** `cursed_spring.dm:29` calls `living.wabbajack(...)`. `wabbajack()` (`code/modules/mob/living/living.dm:1488`) creates a brand-new mob of a random type (the animal list includes goliaths, ancient goliaths, brimdemons and watchers). That mob has its own native factions and no status effect, so there is nothing for any shapechange check to find, and no change to the status-effect lookup could help.

## Fix

Two commits on the branch.

**"Fix issue 92073: megafauna ignore players shapeshifted by the polymorph belt"**
- Adds `has_status_effect_type()` next to `has_status_effect` in `_status_effect_helpers.dm`, as a `/mob` stub returning null and a `/mob/living` version that loops over `status_effects` and matches with `istype`, so subtypes count regardless of ID.
- Uses it in `hostile.dm` `CanAttack` and in `give_uplink()` in `antag.dm`.
- A new helper was added instead of changing `has_status_effect` globally, to keep the change local: `has_status_effect` is used throughout the codebase with ID semantics (for example `polymorph_belt.dm:128` looks up `/from_spell` and relies on it matching only that ID), so altering it would be a wider behavior change.

**"Fix issue 91507: megafauna ignore players wabbajacked into mining fauna"**
- Adds `TRAIT_POLYMORPHED` (`"polymorphed"`) to `declarations.dm` and to `traits_by_type` in `_traits.dm`.
- `wabbajack()` adds the trait to the new mob (source `MAGIC_TRAIT`).
- `CanAttack` in `hostile.dm` now also drops the faction check when the target has `TRAIT_POLYMORPHED`.

Both commits built with 0 errors and 0 warnings.

## Verification

Checked on the test server with the first commit only, then with both.

First commit only:
- Belt case: scanned a dead brimdemon with a functioning belt (real click scan), wore it, transformed to a brimdemon, spawned a colossus four tiles away. Reading values: the status effect found by the new helper was the polymorph belt effect; `faction_check_atom` was still 1, but `CanAttack(player)` was now 1. Walking up to it produced "The colossus roars, "Retribution."", "The colossus judges you!" and "You're hit by a death bolt!" four times. The brimdemon form was killed and the caster died. (Before the fix on master: `CanAttack` 0, five bites, no `target`, no retaliation.)
- Control: an NPC `/mob/living/basic/mining/goliath` six tiles from the colossus: `CanAttack` was 0, its health stayed 300/300 and it was never targeted.

With both commits:
- A wabbajacked NPC human became a `/mob/living/basic/mining/watcher`; it had `TRAIT_POLYMORPHED`, `faction_check` 1 and `CanAttack` 1. After taking control and hitting the colossus: "Retribution.", seven death bolts, the mob died.
- Control goliath (NPC, not polymorphed): `CanAttack` 0, `target` null. It lost 25 health to stray death bolts aimed at the player but was never itself targeted.
- Belt regression: a human with the belt (stored form set from Lua) transformed by the HUD button into a brimdemon; `CanAttack` was 1, and the colossus attacked at once ("Judgement.", death bolts) and killed the player.

Not checked: any megafauna other than the colossus (they share `CanAttack`, so the fix should apply, including Hierophant's override, but it was not tried), the `give_uplink()` change in a real traitor round, and the real cursed spring producing a mining mob in the exact player flow (the mechanism was tested through a direct `wabbajack()` call). Runtime log: nothing from the changed code. It contained empty `uncaught runtime error:` lines from the Lua editor, and one `No valid destination passed into forceMove` (`atoms_movable.dm:1176`) from `brim_burst.bang()`, which appeared after an admin revive of a dead brimdemon form while its death burst was pending and is believed to be an artifact of that test step.

## Notes

- **Review the second commit.** The `TRAIT_POLYMORPHED` change applies to every `wabbajack()` result (staff of change, cursed spring and others), NPC or player, and only affects `simple_animal/hostile` targeting (megafauna and similar). If maintainers think a wabbajacked mining mob should count as kin, that commit can be dropped and only the first kept.
- Basic-mob fauna (normal goliaths, watchers and so on, which use `basic_targeting_strategy`'s faction check) still treat shapeshifted or polymorphed players as kin. This was the behavior before and is unchanged. A comment on #91507 raises ice moon mobs, which is this case; it was not addressed.
- Earlier related work: #89592 ("Fixes cheesing megafauna with a polybelt", merged 2025-02-21) introduced the `has_status_effect(/datum/status_effect/shapechange_mob)` check that never matched the belt. #87908 (closed) reported the same exploit earlier. No open PR for #92073 or #91507 was found.
