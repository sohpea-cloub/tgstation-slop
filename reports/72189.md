## Summary

The build-your-own-shuttle (BYOS) chassis docks at the station in the same instant it is bought, with no landing marks, so anyone standing in the docking area is hit without warning. The map descriptions say it docks upon purchase, so the instant dock is by design; the missing warning is the oversight. The fix shows the usual landing ripples for `SHUTTLE_RIPPLE_TIME` (10 seconds) and then docks. A second, related commit stops the BYOS call time from depending on its engines: removing them doubled the call time even though the chassis is already docked, which is the complaint in #90962.

## The bugs

- **#72189:** other shuttles arrive after about 10 seconds of landing marks. The BYOS chassis appears at once, which can crush or displace people in its docking area.
- **#90962:** after buying BYOS and removing all its engines, calling the shuttle gives an arrival time of 40 minutes, although the chassis is already at the station and does not fly in.

## Reproduction

On Metastation, green alert, as an admin observer. The BYOS purchase was simulated with the Shuttle Manipulator: select the construction template and use "replace", which is the same operation the purchase performs (`action_load` with replace on the emergency shuttle). A real communications console purchase was not tried.

### #72189

1. Using the Shuttle Manipulator, load the "Build your own shuttle kit" (`emergency_construction`) as the emergency shuttle with replace.
2. Observe the chassis. It was at the `emergency_home` dock at once, with the "Docking complete" message and no ripples. No crushing of a person was tested.

### #90962

1. Load BYOS as above, then call the shuttle (`call_evac_shuttle`). On green alert the arrival estimate was 20 minutes (the green coefficient of 2 times the 10 minute base).
2. Call `alter_engines(-10)` on the emergency shuttle to simulate removing the engines (the map gives the chassis `initial_engine_power` 10; this took current power from 10 to 0 and `engine_coeff` from 1 to 2). The running call's timer was doubled.
3. With the engines already removed before the call, the call time was 40 minutes, as in the issue.
4. Calling `setTimer(100)` made ripples appear on the shuttle's own spot and the shuttle "docked" with an announcement though it was already there. This is a side effect of the test, not part of the issue.

The engine removal was simulated by calling procs, not by deconstructing the engine machines.

## Cause

### #72189

`/obj/docking_port/mobile/emergency/shuttle_build/postregister()` (`code/modules/shuttle/mobile_port/variants/emergency/emergency_types.dm:36-38`) calls `initiate_docking(SSshuttle.getDock("emergency_home"))` as soon as the chassis is registered. The template descriptions in `code/datums/shuttles/emergency.dm:51` and `:60` say "The chassis will dock upon purchase", so docking at purchase is intended. Nothing creates ripples first.

### #90962

`/obj/docking_port/mobile/emergency/request()` computes `var/call_time = SSshuttle.emergency_call_time * alert_coeff * engine_coeff` (`code/modules/shuttle/mobile_port/variants/emergency/emergency.dm:42`). `alter_engines()` (`code/modules/shuttle/mobile_port/mobile_port.dm:707`) also rescales a running call with `modTimer` when `in_flight()`. BYOS has engine power from its map, and removing it gives a coefficient of 2, so the call time doubles. For a chassis already docked at the station there is no flight to slow, so the engines should only matter for the escape flight (`setTimer(SSshuttle.emergency_escape_time * engine_coeff)`, `emergency.dm:233-237`), which was left as is.

## Fix

Two commits, in this order on the branch.

1. **"Fix issue 90962: BYOS call time ignores its engine count"**
   - `emergency.dm`: new `get_call_engine_coeff()` (returns `engine_coeff`); `request()` uses it for the call time.
   - `emergency_types.dm`: `shuttle_build` overrides `get_call_engine_coeff()` to return 1, and overrides `alter_engines()` so that, during a call or recall, the engine coefficient and power are still updated but the running timer is not rescaled. Outside a call it falls back to the normal behaviour.
2. **"Fix issue 72189: BYOS shuttle shows landing marks before docking"**
   - `postregister()` on `shuttle_build` now takes the `replace` argument, creates ripples at `emergency_home` for `SHUTTLE_RIPPLE_TIME`, and schedules `dock_at_station()` after that time.
   - `dock_at_station()` removes the ripples and docks, unless the shuttle was deleted, is already docked there, or has left idle mode (for example a call or an admin moved it first).

An alternative for #90962 was to treat engines as irrelevant to calls for every emergency shuttle; the change is limited to BYOS so other shuttles keep the existing behaviour.

## Verification

All checks used the Shuttle Manipulator on the test server.

- **#72189, after the fix:** replacing with BYOS put the chassis at its away dock (`emergency_away`) with 354 ripples at `emergency_home`, and about 10 seconds later it was docked at `emergency_home`.
- **#90962, after the fix:** with the engines removed (`engine_coeff` 2, current engine power 0), `call_evac_shuttle` gave 12000 deciseconds (20 minutes on green). Adding engines mid-call (`alter_engines(+10)`) and removing them again left the time left at 12000: `engine_coeff` changed, the timer did not.
- The runtime log showed no runtimes from these changes (only one from the tester's own script).

Not checked: the real purchase from the communications console, the cruiser kit (`constructionbig`), a person standing in the docking area when the shuttle lands, and the escape flight time with changed engines.

## Notes

- **#90962 is a design call.** A maintainer commented that BYOS having a call time at all is odd, but removing it might not be better, and suggested it may be best left as is. The fix keeps the call time and only decouples it from the engines; a maintainer may prefer to close the issue instead.
- **#72189 keeps the instant-dock design.** The chassis still docks at purchase, a short delay (10 seconds) later. The `mode != SHUTTLE_IDLE` guard means a shuttle call made during the ripple window cancels the scheduled dock rather than being interfered with.
- The setup used the manipulator's replace action, which shares its code path with the purchase, but purchase-specific handling (such as credits or the announcement) was not exercised.
