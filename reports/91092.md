## Summary

Alt-clicking (or alt-shift-clicking) a tile that meson goggles reveal through a wall opened the loot panel and listed the objects on that tile, even though the player could not see them. The loot panel checked visibility with `src in viewers(turf)`, and BYOND's `viewers()` counts a mob as a viewer of a turf when the mob has `SEE_TURFS`, whether or not there is line of sight. The fix adds a helper that also requires real line of sight unless the mob has `SEE_OBJS` (x-ray), and uses it at both places that checked.

## The bug

Meson goggles are meant to show the station's turfs through walls, not what lies on them. With mesons on, alt-clicking a revealed tile behind a wall nevertheless opened the loot panel with that tile's floor, items, structures and mobs, which gave the wearer a view of hidden objects.

## Reproduction

1. On the test server, start a round and use the Lua editor to spawn a human next to a wall. Give it meson goggles (their `sight` was `SEE_TURFS` only) and possess it.
2. Place a crowbar and a wrench on a tile directly behind the wall, and a screwdriver on a tile in open line of sight.
3. Alt-click the tile behind the wall.

Observed on master: the loot panel opened and listed the floor, the crowbar and the wrench. The mob's own look output reported the same objects as out of sight ("behind walls").

This was reproduced with the actual alt-click on the player's screen, not by calling procs directly. The map coordinates used were from the test map and are not important.

## Cause

Two places decided "can this mob see this turf" with `viewers()`:

- `code/_onclick/click_alt.dm:121`, in `/mob/living/proc/try_open_loot_panel_on`: `if(!(src in viewers(target)) || ...)`.
- `code/modules/lootpanel/_lootpanel.dm:79`, in `/datum/lootpanel/ui_status`: `if(!(user in viewers(source_turf)) || ...)`.

`viewers(turf)` includes a mob whose `sight` has `SEE_TURFS` even when the turf is not in its line of sight, and meson goggles set exactly that. The loot panel's content gathering (`code/modules/lootpanel/contents.dm`, `add_new_searchable`) only filters on invisibility and obscuring, not line of sight, so once the panel opened it listed everything on the turf.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 91092: loot panel (alt-click) no longer lists objects on tiles only shown by mesons".

- Adds `/mob/proc/can_see_loot_turf(turf/target)` in `code/modules/lootpanel/misc.dm`. It returns FALSE for a null turf or when the mob is not in `viewers(target)`. If the mob has `SEE_OBJS` it returns TRUE (x-ray keeps working). Otherwise it also requires `src in view(range, target)`, where `range` is the larger side of the mob's view size. Because `view()` is centred on the target turf rather than on the mob, the mob's own sight flags do not make it pass.
- `try_open_loot_panel_on` and `ui_status` now call this helper, so an already open panel also closes when line of sight is lost.

## Verification

After rebuilding and restarting the test server, with the same setup:

- Alt-click on the tile behind the wall: nothing happened, no panel opened.
- Alt-click on the screwdriver's tile in open line of sight: the loot panel opened and listed the floor and the screwdriver.

In both cases the test client reported the panel as view-only, which was also the case before the fix, so this is unrelated to the change.

Not checked: x-ray vision, ghosts and AI eyes. Ghosts and AI call `open()` directly, and `ui_status` now applies the line-of-sight test to them unless their `sight` includes `SEE_OBJS`. Whether their sight flags always include it was not tested, so a regression there is possible.

## Notes

- The helper treats `SEE_OBJS` as "may see through walls". Other sources of out-of-LOS vision (for example camera or other remote views) were not examined.
- The range passed to `view()` is derived from the client's view size (or `world.view` with no client), which is a design choice a maintainer may want to simplify.
