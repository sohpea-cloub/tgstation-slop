# #61402: Lattice replacement after an explosion digs through space into the z-level's baseturf (asteroid rock on Tramstation)

## Summary

`attempt_lattice_replacement()` scraped a fixed two layers off a floor. A plating built over a lattice over space has only one layer above the space turf, so the second scrape went through space into the z-level's `baseturf_bottom`, which is asteroid rock on Tramstation's lower level. The proc then placed a lattice on that rock. The fix scrapes one layer at a time and stops at the first turf that is not a floor.

## The bug

On Tramstation's lower level, near the toxins launch area, space turfs became asteroid rock with a lattice on top. The reporter blamed placing rods on space. That did not reproduce (see below). The cause found was floor damage by explosions, which call this proc.

## Reproduction

On master, on Tramstation (the lower level is z-level 2), with admin tools:

1. Using the Lua editor, call `build_with_rods` on a space turf of z2. Result: the turf stayed space with a lattice. So rods on space are not the trigger, and the issue text's description could not be reproduced.
2. On another z2 space turf, place a lattice and an iron floor tile. The turf became `/turf/open/floor/plating` with `baseturfs` of `[/turf/baseturf_bottom, /turf/open/space]`.
3. Call `attempt_lattice_replacement()` on that turf. This is the proc explosions call; it was called directly, and no real explosion was run.
4. Observed on master: the turf became `/turf/open/misc/asteroid/airless`, with a lattice on it.

## Cause

`attempt_lattice_replacement(amount = 2)` at `code/game/turfs/change_turf.dm:291`:

```dm
var/turf/new_turf = ScrapeAway(amount, flags = CHANGETURF_INHERIT_AIR)
if (istype(new_turf, /turf/open/floor))
	return
var/new_lattice = new /obj/structure/lattice(src)
```

(line 298). With one plating layer over space, the first scrape reached space and the second continued through space to `/turf/baseturf_bottom`. `ChangeTurf` resolves that to the z-level's `ZTRAIT_BASETURF` (`change_turf.dm:60`). On Tramstation's lower level that trait is `/turf/open/misc/asteroid/airless`. The result is not a `/turf/open/floor`, so the early return was skipped and a lattice was placed on the asteroid.

Callers are the heavy-explosion branches of `code/game/turfs/open/floor.dm:67` and `code/game/turfs/open/floor/reinforced_floor.dm:66`. The report also lists a third caller in the tram floors file, which does not exist at master `87b93338`; that reference is unverified. The `floor.dm` call is made only when `baseturf_at_depth(2)` is not a floor.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 61402: lattice replacement digs past space into the z-level's baseturf (asteroid rock on Tramstation)". In `attempt_lattice_replacement`, a loop runs `ScrapeAway(1, flags = CHANGETURF_INHERIT_AIR)` up to `amount` times and breaks as soon as the result is not a `/turf/open/floor`. The existing check and lattice placement are unchanged. Scraping a plating plus a floor on top of it still takes both layers.

## Verification

On the test server with the fix, again calling `attempt_lattice_replacement()` directly:

- Plating over space on z2: the turf became space with a lattice.
- Iron floor over plating over space on z2: both layers scraped, resulting in space with a lattice (unchanged behaviour).

Not checked: a real explosion through `ex_act`, other maps, and the unit tests.

## Notes

- This changes core turf code shared by all maps.
- Devastating explosions use `ScrapeAway(2)` directly (`floor.dm:62`) and do not go through this proc. The report says they can still reach asteroid on such a turf and considers that arguably intended; this was not tested.
- The reporter's trigger (rods on space) was not reproduced, and the scientist's turf may have had plating or been hit by a bomb. This is a guess; the issue does not say.
