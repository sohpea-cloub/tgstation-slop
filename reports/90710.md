## Summary

The issue says a maintenance drone can ventcrawl into Atmospherics but cannot ventcrawl out, because "Touching anything in Atmospherics could break your laws." On master a drone entered and left a vent inside Atmospherics without trouble. Nothing in the ventcrawl code checks the area, and the quoted sentence does not appear anywhere in the code. The restriction is a server-rules interpretation, not something the game enforces. No fix was written.

## The bug

As reported (April 2025, client 516.1659): a drone ventcrawls into Atmospherics to repair damage, gives up, and then cannot ventcrawl out again because Atmospherics is treated as off limits under the drone laws. The reporter appears to describe the constraint as being imposed on the player, not as a game error message. They do not quote a message from the game.

## Reproduction

Not reproduced. Using admin tools on the test server:

1. Started the round and, as an admin observer, used the Lua editor to spawn a `/mob/living/basic/drone` (with `shy` left at its default of TRUE) next to the omni nitrogen tank vent in Atmospherics.
2. Possessed the drone, picked the Maintenance Drone type and the blue colour.
3. Alt-clicked the vent to enter, then alt-clicked it again to leave.

Observed chat messages, in order:

- "You climb into the ventilation ducts."
- "You begin climbing out from the ventilation system..."
- "You scramble out from the ventilation ducts."

The drone ended up on the vent's tile in Atmospherics, not inside the pipe. No runtimes were logged.

Only the single exit from the vent where the drone entered was tried. Crawling through the pipe network and leaving at a different vent in Atmospherics was not tested, nor was entering Atmospherics from outside by pipe.

## Cause

There is no defect on master.

- `can_enter_vent()` (`code/modules/mob/living/ventcrawling.dm:17-65`) checks adjacency, the ventcrawl traits, consciousness, immobilisation, blocked hands, buckling, carried mobs, nudity for carbon mobs, a welded vent and the vent's own `VENTCRAWL_ENTRANCE_ALLOWED` flag. It has no area check.
- `handle_ventcrawl()` (`code/modules/mob/living/ventcrawling.dm:68`) handles the exit branch with a one second `do_after` and a welded-vent re-check, then calls `forceMove` onto the vent's tile. It also has no area check.
- `shy_update()` (`code/modules/mob/living/basic/drone/_drone.dm:294`) only toggles `TRAIT_PACIFISM` and `TRAIT_CAN_STRIP` for shy drones.
- The drone laws (`_drone.dm:57-60`) and the flavour text (`_drone.dm:92-101`) tell drones not to involve themselves with other beings and not to interfere with round-critical objects. Neither text names Atmospherics, and a search of the code found no occurrence of "Touching anything in Atmospherics".
- The only code path that removes ventcrawling from a drone is the hacked-drone lockdown, which does `REMOVE_TRAIT(src, TRAIT_VENTCRAWLER_ALWAYS, INNATE_TRAIT)` (`code/modules/mob/living/basic/drone/interaction.dm`, near line 158) and tells the player "ventilation access is denied". That is intended and applies everywhere, not only in Atmospherics.

## Fix

None. No commits were made.

## Verification

Nothing to verify, since no change was made. The test above shows a drone leaving a vent in Atmospherics.

## Notes

- The quoted message is probably the reporter's paraphrase of how Atmospherics is treated under server rules or by admins. If the intent is to let drones leave Atmospherics, that is a rules and wording question, and no code change is needed. If the intent is to forbid drones from Atmospherics, that would be a new feature, not a bug fix.
- It is also possible that the reporter was on a different build; the report is from April 2025 and the test used the current master, so this write-up cannot rule out a difference between the two. Nothing in the current code suggests one.
- A drone that was hacked loses ventcrawling by design (see Cause); a reporter who was hacked, or who mixed up the hacked-drone lockdown message with a rule, could see something like this.
