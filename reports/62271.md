# #62271: Sparring sect punishes the chaplain for throwing items at their own opponent

## Summary

During a sparring match, the thrown-item handler `thrown_interference` treats any human who throws an item at a fighter as an outside interferer and flubs them. It never checks whether the thrower is one of the two fighters, so a chaplain who throws a spear at their own opponent is punished for "interfering" in their own match. The fix returns early from the handler when the thrower is the chaplain or the opponent. Outsiders who throw things at a fighter are still flubbed.

## The bug

A chaplain of the sparring sect starts a match with weapons allowed. They throw a spear at their opponent and are punished as if a bystander had interfered. The reporter's screenshot shows the end result: a locked-up chest and death from infection. The first comment asks whether any thrown weapon does this; the code says yes, for any item thrown by either fighter at a fighter. The second comment guesses the handler "isn't checking for thrower", which matches the cause.

## Reproduction

Reproduced on master by calling the thrower's `throw_item` proc directly from the Lua editor, not by a player throwing with the throw intent. The match was created by hand rather than through the sparring contract.

1. Start a round and open the Lua editor.
2. Spawn two humans, a "chaplain" C and an "opponent" O, a few tiles apart. Give both the `never_wounded` trait so a hit can't confuse the result.
3. Create the match directly: `new /datum/sparring_match(4, area, 1, C, O)`. The arguments are any-weapon (`CONDITION_ANY_WEAPON`), the arena area, no stakes (`STAKES_NONE`), the chaplain and the opponent.
4. Read `M.flubs`. It starts at 2.
5. Spawn a spear, put it in C's hands, and call `C:throw_item(O)`.
6. Read `M.flubs` again.

Observed on master: `flubs` dropped from 2 to 1. The chat showed "chucks the spear" followed by the thrower screaming, which is the punishment effect. The spear did hit O (health 80), so the throw itself was ordinary.

## Cause

`code/modules/religion/sparring/sparring_datum.dm:51` registers `thrown_interference` on `COMSIG_ATOM_HITBY` for each fighter. The handler (`sparring_datum.dm:119`) is:

```dm
var/mob/thrown_by = throwingdatum?.get_thrower()
if(thrown_item.throwforce < honorbound.health && ishuman(thrown_by))
	INVOKE_ASYNC(src, PROC_REF(flub), thrown_by)
```

Any human thrower whose item does less damage than the target's remaining health is flubbed. Neither fighter is excluded. The neighbouring handlers do exclude them: the paw-attack handler just above returns early when `attacker == chaplain || attacker == opponent`, and `projectile_interference` does the same for `proj.firer`.

## Fix

One commit, "Fix issue 62271: sparring participants no longer flub their own match by throwing items". It adds an early return in `thrown_interference` when `thrown_by` is the chaplain or the opponent, matching the pattern in the sibling handlers:

```dm
if(thrown_by == chaplain || thrown_by == opponent)
	//participants may throw things at each other, any weapon restrictions are handled by the violation procs
	return
```

Only the thrower check was added. The handler's other conditions are unchanged.

## Verification

After rebuilding and restarting the test server, a new round was started and the setup was repeated with a third human X (an outsider) as well:

- C threw a spear at O: `flubs` stayed at 2 and O's health was 80.
- X (not in the match) threw a spear at O: `flubs` dropped to 1 and O's health was 65. Outsiders are still punished.

Not checked: a real player throw (the throw intent and the throw keybinding) rather than `throw_item`; a match started through the sparring contract UI; thrown items other than spears; non-human throwers.

## Notes

- Thrown weapons are not checked against the weapon-condition rules. The gun, grenade and melee violation handlers exist, but no thrown-item violation does, so in a fist-fight-only or ceremonial-only match a fighter can throw a weapon without penalty. After this fix that is also true of the previously punished case. This is outside the reported issue and was not changed. The code comment in the fix says weapon restrictions are handled by the violation procs, which is true for guns, grenades and melee but not for throws.
- Only human throwers are flubbed (`ishuman(thrown_by)`), unchanged by the fix.
