## Summary

The issue says that an AI who shapeshifts into a securitron and then dies loses the AI mob, because the AI sits inside the bot and is deleted with it. On current master the AI came back as a dead AI mob on the floor, with its mind returned to it, and was not deleted. The bot code and the shapechange status effect were both reworked after the report, which probably fixed it. No fix was committed.

## The bug

As reported (February 2023): spawn as an AI, give yourself the shapechange spell, pick securitron, and die (for example by buildmode fireball). The AI mob you possessed before is deleted. A carp form instead dropped the dead body behind, as it should. A comment traced it to the old bot death code, where the securitron was `qdel()`'d with the AI inside it.

## Reproduction

Attempted on master; the reported behaviour did not occur.

1. Using the Lua editor, spawn a human, turn it into an AI with `AIize(null, FALSE)` and give it a player's control.
2. Grant `/datum/action/cooldown/spell/shapeshift/wizard`, set `shapeshift_type` to `/mob/living/basic/bot/secbot/ed209` (the only bot among the spell's possible shapes), and trigger it.
3. Kill the bot in several ways: `death()` via Lua, `adjust_brute_loss(1000)` (via Lua and an SDQL2 `CALL` on the possessed bot), and `gib()`. An `ex_act(1)` call only damaged the bot, so it was not a kill path. A carp form was also gibbed for comparison.
4. After `death()`, the chat showed "The ED-209 Security Robot blows apart!" and "AI has died", and the player's control moved back to the dead AI. An SDQL2 query (`SELECT /mob/living/silicon/ai MAP [name, stat, loc, QDELETED(src), z]`) after the damage and gib cases showed AIs with `stat` 3 lying on the floor where the bot died, with `QDELETED` 0. The AI's dead overlays were present, and no related runtimes appeared.

## Cause (why it does not reproduce)

- Death in the shapechange effect: `/datum/status_effect/shapechange_mob/from_spell/on_shape_death` (`code/modules/spells/spell_types/shapeshift/_shape_status.dm:226`) calls `restore_caster(kill_caster_after = TRUE)` when the spell's `die_with_shapeshifted_form` and `revert_on_death` are set, which are the wizard spell's defaults (lines 229-232). `restore_caster` (line 83) moves the caster out of the shape to its former location before `qdel(former_body)` (line 114), so the AI is no longer inside the thing being deleted.
- The 2023 mechanism no longer applies. Bots are now `/mob/living/basic/bot`, and their death calls `explode()` and then the parent `death()` (`code/modules/mob/living/basic/bots/_bots.dm:310`); `explode` does not delete the bot. The old `bot.dm` code the comment pointed to is gone.

## Fix

None. No commit was made.

## Verification

Only the test-server runs above. Not checked:

- The AI was standing in a hallway (`AIize` without moving it), not on its core's turf, so a path that depends on the core location was not exercised.
- A buildmode fireball, as in the report, was not used; damage, gib and `death()` paths were.
- Other deaths, such as a shapeshift ending through the spell's revert action, were not tested.

## Notes

- The reworked shapechange effect and the basic-mob bot conversion are the likely reasons it no longer reproduces. A maintainer can probably close the issue as fixed or no longer reproducible.
