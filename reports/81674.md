## Summary

The issue reports that a basic mob (a bear, a Russian) grabbed twice with ctrl+click stops attacking and can be beaten to death. A maintainer comment on the issue said that being aggro-grabbable is fine and that the bug was the mob's AI pausing. On current master the AI does not pause. A mob grabbed above passive keeps resisting and breaks free within a few seconds, then attacks. It cannot attack while the grab holds because an aggressive grab gives the victim `TRAIT_HANDS_BLOCKED`, which stops unarmed attacks by any mob, player or AI. No fix was needed.

## The bug

As reported, once a basic mob is in an aggressive grab, it stops fighting back and the grabber can kill it at leisure. The reporter noted it applied to many mobs, and some of them could simply be strangled to death. A comment from the maintainers separated two things: AI pausing under a grab (a bug) and being aggro-grabbable (not a bug, and a feature that had been lost when combat mode was introduced).

## Reproduction

Attempted on master with the Lua editor, observing as a ghost:

1. Spawned a human and a `/mob/living/basic/bear` next to each other.
2. Made the human start pulling the bear, and set the grab state to aggressive, neck, or kill in different runs.
3. Logged the grab state and the chat every 0.5 s. In one variant the bear's AI was paused (with the `ai_paused` trait) when the grab began and unpaused after 1 s.

Observed:

- **Passive grab:** the bear kept clawing the human (health 100 to 55 in 6 s).
- **Neck or kill grab:** the bear's resist attempts repeated: "The space bear struggles as they fail to break free of X's grip!" seven times in 12 s (kill grab, with the AI paused then released). In other runs "The space bear breaks free of X's grip!" appeared within a few seconds. After breaking free it mauled the human. No claw attacks appeared while the grab held.
- `ai_status` stayed `ai_on` throughout, so the AI was not paused.

The reported behaviour (a mob that never fights back) is therefore not reproduced. The grabs were set directly with `setGrabState` rather than by ctrl+click in a real client.

## Cause

Why the bear does not attack while held:

- `code/datums/ai/basic_mobs/basic_subtrees/escape_captivity.bt.json:61`, with `code/datums/ai/generic_decorators/pawn_grabbed_by_enemy.dm:13`, makes a pawn that is grabbed above passive by an enemy run a resist leaf. That leaf succeeds, so the selector in the hostile mob's behaviour tree never reaches the combat subtree on those ticks.
- `code/game/atoms_movable.dm:1763`: upgrading a grab from passive to aggressive adds `TRAIT_IMMOBILIZED` and `TRAIT_HANDS_BLOCKED` to the victim (source `CHOKEHOLD_TRAIT`). `can_unarmed_attack()` (`code/_onclick/other_mobs.dm:18`) returns false with `TRAIT_HANDS_BLOCKED`, so no mob can make unarmed attacks while aggressively grabbed.
- The escape chance per resist is `escape_chance / effective_grab_state`, baseline 60% (`code/modules/mob/living/living.dm`, around line 1223). That is 60% for an aggressive grab, 30% for neck and 20% for kill (the report gave 30% and 20% for the last two). A failed resist also costs the victim stamina.

## Fix

None.

## Verification

Not applicable. Not checked: other basic mobs besides the bear; the effect of different grabbers' unarmed bonuses on the escape chance; a real ctrl+click grab from a player client.

## Notes

- Mobs being aggro-grabbable and unable to hit back while held matches how every mob (including players) behaves under a chokehold. If maintainers want grabbed mobs to retaliate, that would be a design change to `TRAIT_HANDS_BLOCKED`, not an AI fix.
- The old AI-pause bug described in the maintainer comment appears to have been resolved by the behavior-tree AI, whose captivity subtree resists on every tick.
- The only runtimes seen during testing were errors in the test scripts themselves.
