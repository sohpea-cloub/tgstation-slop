# #93526: Gas masks don't stop chemicals from entering your lungs

## Summary

The report says that wearing a gas mask with filters did not stop chemical smoke (grenades, blob spores) from putting reagents into the wearer. On current master, a plain gas mask and an atmospherics gas mask kept chemical smoke reagents out of the wearer in every test, with internals both on and off. The unmasked control received the reagents each time. Only the cosmetic gas-mask subtypes that carry no smoke-protection flag (clown, mime, owl, carp and similar) let smoke through, which looks intended. The reporter's own circumstances (a test-merge round, and whether internals were open) cannot be reproduced from the issue text.

## The bug

As reported (client 516.1661, a round with several test merges): the reporter wore an atmospherics gas mask with three filters, was caught in a chemical fog from a grenade or blob spores, and reagents still entered their body. Commenters noted that internals should protect, asked whether the reporter was using internals, and the reporter was not sure. The last comment hopes that PR #93864 ("Fixes internals/externals not protecting humans from inhaling...") fixed it. That PR's change is present in master: `/mob/living/carbon/human/expose_reagents` (`code/modules/mob/living/carbon/human/human_defense.dm:732`) removes the `INHALE` method when the human has active internals or externals.

## Reproduction

Attempted on master with admin tools; the bug did not occur.

1. On the test server, as an observer with the Lua editor, spawn four humans spaced apart in the same corridor:
   - no mask (control);
   - `/obj/item/clothing/mask/gas/atmos` (three filters), internals off;
   - the same atmospherics mask plus an oxygen tank, opened with `try_open_internals`;
   - a plain `/obj/item/clothing/mask/gas`, internals off.
   Before the test, `has_smoke_protection()` returned 0 for the control and 1 for each masked human, and every human held no reagents.
2. Grenade-style smoke: call `do_chem_smoke(1, nil, <the human's tile>, /datum/reagent/lube, 30)` on each human's tile. After about 16 s the control held 8.2 units of lube (total reagents 8.2). Each masked human held 0.
3. Blob-death style smoke: call `do_chem_smoke` on each tile with capsaicin, 30 units, using the medium chem smoke type (`/datum/effect_system/fluid_spread/smoke/chem/medium`). The control's total reagents rose from 21.2 to 41.9 (20.1 units of capsaicin); the masked humans stayed at 0.
4. Smoke made by a real chemical reaction: a beaker with mindbreaker, potassium, sugar and phosphorus (the smoke powder reaction) was set off near the humans. The masked humans (with and without internals) had 0 reagents; smoke tiles were present on the tiles of the two atmospherics-mask humans at the time of the checks. The control's mindbreaker amount read 0 although its total reagent volume was 32.3 and then 29.9, so this step does not show how much of the smoke reached the control; the reason was not investigated (earlier test reagents were still in its body, and it may have been metabolising).
5. The runtime log of the round had no runtime errors.

Not tried: a real player putting a mask on and walking into smoke; actual blob spores dying; a real grenade thrown by a player. The smoke came from `do_chem_smoke` and the smoke powder reaction, which are the same code paths those use.

## Cause

There is no defect in the case reported. The relevant code:

- `smoke_mob` for chem smoke (`code/game/objects/effects/effect_system/fluid_spread/effects_smoke.dm:391`) returns without transferring reagents when the target has internals or smoke protection:

  ```dm
  if(smoker.internal != null || smoker.has_smoke_protection())
  	return FALSE
  ```

- Humans report smoke protection if any worn clothing item has `BLOCK_GAS_SMOKE_EFFECT` (`code/modules/mob/living/carbon/human/life.dm:279`).
- `/obj/item/clothing/mask/gas` sets `clothing_flags = BLOCK_GAS_SMOKE_EFFECT | MASKINTERNALS` (`code/modules/clothing/masks/gasmask.dm:14`). The atmospherics mask is a subtype that does not override this, so it is protected.
- The filters in the mask only affect the air the wearer breathes (`consume_filter`); they play no part in the smoke check.
- Chemical-reaction attacks that apply reagents directly (`explode_attack_chem`, `code/modules/reagents/chemistry/recipes.dm:534`) also skip targets with smoke protection.

The cosmetic masks such as the clown (`gasmask.dm:306`), sexy clown, jonkler, mime, monkey, sexy mime, owl and carp masks (up to line 475) set `clothing_flags = MASKINTERNALS` only, so they do not have `BLOCK_GAS_SMOKE_EFFECT` and chem smoke reaches the wearer. Whether that is intended is a design question; it looks deliberate, since they are novelty masks.

## Fix

None. No change was made.

## Verification

The unmodified master behaviour was checked as described under Reproduction: control exposed, plain gas mask and atmospherics mask not exposed, with and without internals, for lube smoke, capsaicin smoke in the blob-death style, and the smoke powder reaction (the last without a clear measurement on the control). No runtimes.

Not checked: the cosmetic subtypes in game (their lack of the flag was read from the code only); other protective gear (hardsuits, MODsuits, bandanas); species that do not breathe; smoke from smoke machines; vapour or foam delivery; and how the state of the game differed in the reporter's round (the round had four test merges: changeling abilities, camera subsystem handling, a split personality revert and a crafting revert, none of which appear related to smoke).

## Notes

- The reporter may have been wearing a mask subtype without the flag, or the round may have predated PR #93864. The issue text does not say which mask other than "atmospherics gas mask with 3 filters", which has the flag in the code on master.
- The triage notes suggested checking whether a filtered mask alone blocks smoke; the answer is yes, because the protection comes from the clothing flag, not from the filters or internals.
- A possible follow-up for maintainers: players may expect the cosmetic masks (clown, mime and so on) to protect like a gas mask, since they are in the same `/obj/item/clothing/mask/gas` tree and say "gas mask" in their names. Whether they should is not clear from the inputs.
- Smoke from sources other than `do_chem_smoke` that use their own `smoke_mob` overrides (for example `smoke/bad`, `smoke/sleeping`) were not tested; the base `smoke_mob` checks (`effects_smoke.dm:118`) use the same internals / smoke protection condition.
