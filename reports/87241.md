## Summary

Security bots (Beepsky and the other secbots) and ED-209s stun through the `stun_n_cuff` component, which called `Paralyze()` directly. It never looked at `TRAIT_BATON_RESISTANCE` and never sent `COMSIG_MOB_BATONED`, so the MOD shock absorber did nothing against them. The fix makes the component skip the paralysis for baton-resistant targets, halve the stutter, and send `COMSIG_MOB_BATONED`, as batons do.

#89105 is a separate bug. The `edible` component cancels the attack chain when an edible item is used out of combat mode on a mob that cannot eat it. A deep-fried weapon is such an item, so the hit did nothing visible, but `relay_attackers` still counted it as an attack and the bot retaliated. A second commit makes `TryToEat()` leave the attack alone in that case.

## The bug

### #87241

A player wearing a MOD suit with the shock absorber module shrugs off knockdown from every baton. When a secbot (the issue names Officer Beepsky) stuns them, they are knocked down regardless.

### #89105

Hitting a bot with a deep-fried damaging item without combat mode produces no damage, no hit message, no sound and no swing animation. The bot still treats this as an attack, stuns and cuffs the attacker, and reports them.

## Reproduction

### #87241

On master `87b93338`, using admin tools:

1. Start a round and open the Lua editor.
2. Spawn a `/mob/living/basic/bot/secbot` and a `/mob/living/carbon/human`.
3. Give the human `TRAIT_BATON_RESISTANCE`. This is the trait the shock absorber module adds. The real MOD suit and module were not worn.
4. Get the bot's `/datum/component/stun_n_cuff` and call `stun_target()` on the human.

Evidence: the bot announced "The Securitron stuns ..." and `IsParalyzed()` on the human returned a `/datum/status_effect/incapacitating/paralyzed` status effect, even though the human had baton resistance.

This reproduces the bug by calling the component's proc directly, not through the bot's own pathfinding and arrest behaviour.

### #89105

Not run on master. The cause was established from the code (see Cause). The fixed behaviour was checked as described under Verification.

## Cause

### #87241

`stun_target()` in `code/datums/components/cuff_n_stun.dm` ends the stun with:

```dm
human_target.Paralyze(stun_timer)       // :91
human_target.set_stutter(40 SECONDS)    // :92
```

Batons check `TRAIT_BATON_RESISTANCE` (`code/game/objects/items/weaponry/melee/baton.dm:250`, with halved jitter and stutter at `:454-455` and `:696-698`) and send `COMSIG_MOB_BATONED` (`baton.dm:267`). The component does neither. Two things follow:

- The knockdown is applied to a baton-resistant target.
- The shock absorber module listens for `COMSIG_MOB_BATONED` (`code/modules/mod/modules/modules_general.dm:1042`) to drain power and spark (`mob_batoned`, `:1049`). That never ran for bot stuns.

ED-209 uses the same component (`code/modules/mob/living/basic/bots/ed209/ed209.dm:54`), so it had the same bug. A maintainer's comment on the issue says this is a legacy of bots predating the modern baton code, not an intentional exemption.

### #89105

`fried_item` gives the item an `edible` component. `edible` registers `UseFromHand` on `COMSIG_ITEM_ATTACK`, which calls `TryToEat()` (`code/datums/components/food/edible.dm:373`). Out of combat mode, `TryToEat()` sets the return value to `COMPONENT_CANCEL_ATTACK_CHAIN` ("Point of no return", `:382`) before `CanConsume()` (`:560`) decides whether the target can eat. A bot is not a carbon, so `CanConsume()` fails, and the attack chain has already been cancelled. The item therefore does no damage and shows no message.

`relay_attackers` is independent of this. Its `after_attackby` (`code/datums/elements/relay_attackers.dm:37`) relays an attack whenever `weapon.force` is nonzero, so the bot sees the attack and retaliates.

## Fix

Two commits on the branch.

**"Fix issue 87241: security bots respect baton resistance"**
- In `stun_target()` (`code/datums/components/cuff_n_stun.dm`), `Paralyze()` is skipped when the target has `TRAIT_BATON_RESISTANCE`.
- The stutter is halved for resistant targets, as batons do.
- `COMSIG_MOB_BATONED` is sent on the target, with the bot as the source and a null baton.
- The comment on `COMSIG_MOB_BATONED` in `code/__DEFINES/dcs/signals/signals_mob/signals_mob_living.dm` now says it also comes from `stun_target()` and that the baton is null for bots.

This reuses the existing trait and signal, so the shock absorber works without changes. ED-209 is covered because it shares the component. The alternative raised in the issue thread, changing `Paralyze()` to `stun()`, was not used: the trait check is what batons do, and the signal is needed for the module's power drain.

**"Fix issue 89105: edible weapons hit bots and animals instead of being silently ignored"**
- In `TryToEat()` (`code/datums/components/food/edible.dm`), after the combat mode check, the proc returns early when the eater is not a carbon and the item has `force`. The attack chain is then not cancelled, and the item hits as a normal weapon.
- Zero-force food used on animals is unchanged.

## Verification

All checks were on the test server after rebuilding with the fix, using the Lua editor.

### #87241

- A baton-resistant human hit by the bot's `stun_target()`: `IsParalyzed()` returned 0.
- A signal counter registered on that human for `mob_batoned` read 1.
- A normal human hit the same way was paralyzed.
- The runtime log showed no runtimes.

Not checked:
- The real MOD suit with the shock absorber module was not worn. The trait and the signal were tested, so the module's power drain and sparks were not observed.
- A full bot arrest by the bot's own AI was not run.
- ED-209 was not run, though it uses the same component.

### #89105

- A human not in combat mode attacked a secbot with a deep-fried kitchen knife (the `fried_item` element added to a kitchen knife). The chat showed "lacerates the Securitron with the lightly-fried kitchen knife!" and the bot reacted with "Level 5 infraction alert!".

Not checked:
- The behaviour before the fix was not run in game, so there is no before and after comparison.
- Other bot types and animals were not tested.
- The bot's health change was not recorded.

## Notes

- The resistant target still sees the "stuns you!" message from the bot. The bot also keeps retrying each stun cooldown (10 seconds), so a resistant target is zapped repeatedly without falling down. Whether a resistant target should be told they were stunned, or whether the bot should react differently, is a design question this fix does not answer.
- The thread on #87241 contains a view that the bot's instant stun is accepted as a counterweight to its slow AI. The maintainer comment says the omission was not intentional.
- The `edible` change is intentionally narrow: it only applies to non-carbon targets and items with force. Other cases of `TryToEat()` cancelling an attack on a non-eating target (for example a living mob that cannot consume the item) were not surveyed.
