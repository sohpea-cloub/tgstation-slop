# #69739: Cycler shotgun: switching tubes with an empty chamber leaves the gun dry-firing

## Summary

Both issues describe the cycler shotgun (reported on the bounty hunter variant) losing or deleting shells, firing only 2 of its 4 shells, and breaking when the player switches tubes. On current master none of that happens: shell counts are conserved through every tube switch and every shell can be fired. The testers attributed this to the earlier upstream PR #93261 "Cycler shotgun fixing" (their notes say it merged 2025-10-04 and is already in the tested tree; the shallow checkout could not confirm this from history). One related defect remained: when the active tube ran dry and the player switched to the full tube, `toggle_tube()` left the chamber empty, so the next trigger pull only dry-fired. The fix chambers a round when switching tubes with an empty chamber.

## The bug

### #69739

The reporter (client 514, during the multiz rework testmerge, 2022) spawned a bounty cycler shotgun, saw that examining it said it held 4 rounds, fired 2 and could not fire any more. After switching to "tube B" they could neither load more shells nor switch back to tube A. They described the gun as janky to use.

### #81315

The reporter (client 515, February 2024) said that switching tubes "deletes the ammo" and that once the chambered shot was fired the gun could never be reloaded. A comment from a maintainer on that issue (April 2024) could not reproduce it exactly but saw odd behavior and suspected the chambered round held a reference to a shell in the other tube; they had managed to delete shells entirely.

### What happens on master

On master the reported symptoms do not occur (see Reproduction). The one remaining problem is that after the active tube empties, switching to the other tube does not feed a shell into the chamber. The player has to click the gun in hand a second time (which racks it) before it can fire. Before that second click, pulling the trigger only produces a dry-fire click, which looks like "unable to fire anymore".

## Reproduction

All steps are on master `87b93338` with admin tools. The gun was fired both by calling procs from the Lua editor and by real clicks from a possessed human with godmode.

1. Spawn `/obj/item/gun/ballistic/shotgun/automatic/dual_tube/bounty` into a human's hands (Lua editor: `SS13.new(...)` then `put_in_hands`).
2. Initial state: chambered rubber shot, tube A holds 3, tube B holds 4. Examining the gun says "4 rounds remaining" (it counts only the active tube plus the chamber).
3. Original symptoms, not reproduced: with real clicks, all 4 rubber shots from tube A fired; the 5th click dry-fired, as expected for an empty gun. Alternating "fire 2, switch tube, fire 2, switch tube, ..." from Lua kept the totals consistent (tube sizes plus chamber always added up), and no shell was lost or deleted. `load_gun` worked on either tube, and a gun that had been fully emptied could be reloaded with shells and racked back into service.
4. Remaining defect, reproduced with real clicks: after the 4 rubber shots, clicking the gun in hand showed "switched to tube B", but the chamber stayed empty. The next click on a target only dry-fired. A further click on the gun in hand racked it, and then 4 incendiary slugs fired.

In a Lua run the same state showed up as the chamber being `nil` after the toggle (`ch=nil toggled=1`) while the newly selected tube still held 2 shells.

## Cause

`dual_tube/attack_self()` at `code/modules/projectiles/guns/ballistic/shotgun.dm:165` racks the gun if it has no chambered round and has ammo, and otherwise calls `toggle_tube()`. `toggle_tube()` (`shotgun.dm:171`) swaps the `magazine` and `alternate_magazine` variables and flips `toggled`, and does nothing else:

```dm
/obj/item/gun/ballistic/shotgun/automatic/dual_tube/proc/toggle_tube(mob/living/user)
	var/current_mag = magazine
	var/alt_mag = alternate_magazine
	magazine = alt_mag
	alternate_magazine = current_mag
	toggled = !toggled
	...
```

When the previously active tube has just run dry, the chamber is empty at the time of the swap. Since the swap never calls `chamber_round()` (`code/modules/projectiles/guns/ballistic.dm:447`), the chamber stays empty and the first trigger pull dry-fires. This is the point where the first click after switching tubes looks like the gun has stopped working.

The older reports (2 of 4 shots, deleted shells, wrong alt tube) are attributed to the cycler consuming two rounds per shot, broken racking, and the bounty alternate tube being the wrong type, all fixed by upstream PR #93261. That attribution was not independently checked against the old code.

## Fix

One commit on the fix branch: "Fix issue 69739: cycler shotgun chambers a round when switching tubes with an empty chamber". It adds to the end of `toggle_tube()`:

```dm
// If the chamber is empty (e.g. the old tube just ran dry), feed the new tube's shell in so the next click fires instead of dry-firing
if(!chambered)
	chamber_round()
update_appearance()
```

A round already in the chamber is left alone, so switching with a loaded chamber still fires the old tube's shell first, as before. The `update_appearance()` call refreshes the sprite after the swap.

## Verification

After rebuilding the test server with the change:

- Fired 4 rubber shots from tube A, then clicked the gun once. The chamber then held a milspec incendiary slug, tube B held 3, and the gun fired.
- Switched back to tube A (empty) while a tube B round was chambered and fired. One further click chambered the last tube B round and it fired. All 8 shells fired with no dry-fire that needed a second click.

Not checked: the other dual-tube variants (the buckshot/slug cycler type was not exercised) and the sprite after a switch. The server runtime log showed only unrelated Lua and scar-description noise.

## Notes

- #81315 is the same gun and the same current state as #69739. The "ammo goes poof" report was not reproduced; shell counts were conserved through every toggle. It can be closed as a duplicate of #69739 or as fixed upstream.
- Side finding, not fixed: `process_fire` in `code/modules/projectiles/guns/ballistic.dm:612` reads `chambered.casing_pressure` with no null check. Calling `process_fire` directly on an empty gun (as the Lua test did) runtimes with "Cannot read null.casing_pressure". Real clicks are not affected because `can_shoot` dry-fires first.
- Design choice: the fix chambers on toggle only when the chamber is empty, so switching with a loaded chamber still fires the already chambered shell first, as before.
- `attack_self` is unchanged: with an empty chamber and ammo in the current tube, the in-hand click racks instead of switching tubes.
