## Summary
In 2024 a befriended minebot showed the pet-command radial on alt-click, the same click the minebot uses to toggle its mode, so the two clashed. On current master alt-click only toggles the mode: the pet radial was moved to a hold-to-view keybind (default Shift) by an upstream change. No fix was written.

## The bug
As reported: a miner befriends a minebot, then alt-clicks it to switch between "collecting ore" and "attacking wildlife". The pet-command menu competed with the mode change.

## Reproduction
On master, using the Lua editor:
1. Spawn a human, give it the miner outfit (`/datum/outfit/job/miner`) and the `rock_stoner` trait, and spawn a `/mob/living/basic/mining_drone` four tiles away.
2. Call `befriend` on the minebot with the human as the friend, and take control of the human.
3. Alt-click the minebot twice.

Observed: the first alt-click showed "now attacking wildlife" and changed the minebot's sprite to `mining_drone_offense`; the second showed "now collecting loose ore" and changed it back to `mining_drone`. No radial menu, window or pointing cursor opened.

## Cause
At the time of the report the `obeys_commands` component opened the pet radial on `COMSIG_CLICK_ALT`, while `/mob/living/basic/mining_drone/click_alt` (`code/modules/mob/living/basic/minebots/minebot.dm:218`) uses the same click to toggle `combat_mode`. The agents' notes attribute the fix to upstream PR #88495 ("Pet Commands QOL", 2024-12-22), which moved the radial to a hold-to-view keybind (`view_pet_commands`, default Shift, `code/datums/keybinding/living.dm:239-244`; handled in `code/datums/components/pet_commands/obeys_commands.dm`). The PR was identified from the code and history, not by testing the older build.

## Fix
None; nothing to fix on master.

## Verification
Only the reproduction above: two alt-clicks gave the expected mode toggles with no pet menu. Not checked: holding Shift to open the radial, or that issuing commands from it works.

## Notes
- Leftover: `COMSIG_CLICK_ALT` is still in the `UnregisterSignal` list in `UnregisterFromParent` (`obeys_commands.dm:48`), though nothing registers it any more. It is harmless and was not changed.
- Pet commands that need a target (`requires_pointing`, which registers `COMSIG_MOB_CLICKON` in `pet_command.dm:171`) take the player's next click of any kind, including an alt-click, until a target is chosen. That looks intentional and was not tested.
